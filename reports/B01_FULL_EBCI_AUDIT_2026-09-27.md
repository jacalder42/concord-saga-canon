# B01 full EBCI audit

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Status:** EDITORIAL REPORT — NON-CANONICAL. The stop after the full B01 build required by
`decisions/B01_ACT_I_AUDIT_AND_FULL_B01_RELEASE_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-27.md` §3: ten tests and an
anti-optimisation test, on the first whole book at executable resolution.

**Method.** Claude built the 49 packets, so the judgement-heavy tests (1, 4, 7, 8, 9 and the
anti-optimisation test) were given to **an independent adversarial read** of all 49 narrative briefs
in reading order; its load-bearing claims were checked against the packets before use. Claude measured
the rest from the packets and the grids: POV, ECID, LOAD and tracking in reading order, and breadcrumb
density.

**What it changes:** only the plain defects in §4, fixed before this report (point-of-view breaches,
a name leak, editorial scaffolding in briefs). **Everything else is proposed.** B02 and B03 stay held.

---

## 1. Verdict

**The architecture passes; the briefs are overbuilt.**

- **Architecture (tests 2, 3, 6, 10): pass.** The four events escalate in kind, knowledge and
  consequence; Seraphine and Lucien change; the knowledge economy is sound with three small gaps;
  **nothing found requires reconsidering B02–B09.**
- **The briefs (tests 1, 4, 7, 8, 9 and the anti-optimisation test): the independent read's verdict
  is that B01 is overbuilt by the author's own test, and Claude agrees in large part.** *"From E15 to
  E48, no plot episode is allowed a digression, a wrong turn that stays wrong, or a character pursuing
  something unrelated"*; the mess that exists is *"scheduled mess"*. **The overbuilding lives in the
  narrative briefs, not in the architecture:** restated beats, formula phrasing, event "may show"
  lists that work as shot lists, prescribed emotional discoveries, and scheduled comedy.
- **The remedy is a compression pass on the briefs, not a redesign** (§5). It is cheap now and should
  land in the template **before B02 EBCI**, so B02 inherits the lighter brief.

## 3. The brief tests (from the independent read, checked)

### Test 1 — Narrative flow: seams

- **E10–E13** are four coping failures in a row; E12's check repeats E11's.
- **E35 → S05 → E37 → E38** delivers the same news four times (the interval shortening; the Vein's
  contradictory stories in both S05 and E37; E38's beat nearly word for word E37's).
- **The trio's roll call** (*feel / structure / verify*) recurs at E20, E21, E25, E26, E32, E39.
- **"Care is not control" as a turn** at E10, E35, E41, E44, E45, E48.
- **House voice:** nearly every exit state is a two-clause epigram (*"It passes, and it was not
  normal"*), and an *"X before Y"* formula recurs (grief before mystery, help before explanation,
  personhood before analysis, bodies first). **Prose would copy it.**

## 5. Proposed: a compression pass on the briefs (before B02 EBCI)

| # | Rule, applied to B01's 49 briefs and then written into the template |
| --- | --- |
| **C1** | **State, don't script.** Exit states are plain states, not epigrams; drop the *"X before Y"* formula and the trio roll calls; keep one beat where two restate each other |
| **C2** | **Events keep only the required observable.** The brief names the approved trace (the windows; the ripple; the amp cut-out; the shake) and the page-safe bans; **secondary images move to the control layer as options** for the writer to take or leave |
| **C3** | **Relationship beats name the rung, not the mechanism:** what is true before and after (E13, E24), never how it is felt |
| **C4** | **Scheduled comedy becomes permission:** *"room for humour"*, not *"someone laughs at the wrong time"* (E21, E34, E42) |
| **C5** | **Unassigned room.** A brief never accounts for every minute. Plot episodes from E15 on **may carry a digression, a wrong turn that stays wrong, or a character wanting something unrelated**; the brief says so in one line where it fits, and names nothing |
| **C6** | **Differentiate the E35–S05–E37–E38 run without merging:** E35 keeps the interval; S05 keeps the Vein; **E37 moves off the Vein** to the city's systems; **E38 gets a specific failure** of its own |
| **C7** | **Caro / Elisabet setup:** E28's noticing becomes firm (not `[P]`), so E30's recognition has something to recognise |
| **C8** | **Pairs that must differ:** E11/E12 (E12 domestic, no checking); E26/E43 (shared wonder against solitary, unlogged wonder); E24/E39 (E39 framed as scope, not a second fracture); E16/E17 (one admission) |
| **C9** | **Knowledge gaps:** E29's validation placed (in E29's own aftermath or E35); E47 carries the trio's reasoning for still surfaces |

**What the pass does not do:** change any episode, its order, a milestone, a breadcrumb or an approved
observable. The architecture stays; the briefs get lighter.
