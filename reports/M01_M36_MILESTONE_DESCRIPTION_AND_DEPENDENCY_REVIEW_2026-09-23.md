## Verdict and reading key

**Keep function (K):** a durable narrative commitment, still with unapproved exact wording/placement. **Rewrite (W):** keep the function but remove an inaccurate or unjustified claim. **Hold (H):** specific event's survival or cause needs source/author decision. **Retire literal (X):** present *wording* conflicts with later constraints; preserve a usable narrative function where noted. A row may be **K+W** or **H+X**. These are review dispositions in this document, **not changes to the grid's `status` field**.

A milestone can be a **human/institutional/relationship change**, not a Resonance event. Mapping an event inventory to the grid must not erase those milestones or supernaturalize them. Proposed book/act/episode fields remain subject to source and current production structure. An older Notion scene title or a note saying “ruled” in the grid is evidence to check against later explicit author direction, not an automatic canonical promotion of the whole row.
