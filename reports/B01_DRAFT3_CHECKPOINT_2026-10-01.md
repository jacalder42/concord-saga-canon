## 3. The continuity pass

**Four checkers** read draft 3 for contradictions: one per act, straight through, and one tracing the threads that run
across acts (the *None*, the renewal, the minute, the Institute, the triage, the costs, the romance, the calendar and the
recurring names). They reported about 56 findings. **None broke the book's logic.** Three fixers, one per act, verified
each and applied the smallest fix; 59 edits went in, and every one is logged.

**The ones a reader would most have noticed:**
- **Her copy of her statement.** As drafted, her own copy would have shown his line in March and spoiled E44. Her copy
  is now the witness box alone, picked up at the front desk, so E44 is the first time she sees it.
- **Guidry's window** is ten days, as the packet has it, and he no longer speaks of a burial after the funeral.
- **Lucien's room** has its front window throughout.
- **One point-of-view slip** (E29), where Seraphine recalled a scene she was not in.
- **E21's calling rule** was broken without comment at E26 and E31; it now reads as a rule the page keeps.
- **E48** put Lucien a block from the lot after E47 had sent him to the far streets.
- **E49's reprise** gave him her verb; he is still again.
- **The calendar holds** for 2026 (Easter 5 April; E44 Sunday 26 April). The one day-count slip (E31) is fixed.

**Also fixed:**
- *The card*, bare, now means only Dré's coverage card.
- The coroner's office lost a street name that would have put a neighboring parish's office in the city.
- **R.** is planted at E04 (both readers met him cold at E38).
- E48's *my letter* is now Seraphine's own inference.
- The oxygen holds now say they were extended.

**One reviser's choice was undone.** E49's reviser had made the coda's regular the pianist from E30's waltz. That breaks
the packets' guard that E30's musicians do not recur (Q-V3, `decisions/B01_COMPRESSION_GATE_AND_B02_EBCI_RELEASE_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-27.md`).
He is now planted at E16 as a Vein regular, which is the *made familiar* the change list asked for.

**What draft 3 changed, by the readers' account:**
- **E44 now rates 5.** The *None* lands as a discovery, and both readers felt it change her conduct afterwards.
- **Seraphine acts.** The target reader listed twelve decisions with costs. The editor named three, each with a lasting
  cost on the page: the paragraph and the folder, the triage, and her promise of safety in numbers.
- **The high-pressure scenes are written at the pressure of the scene** (the editor). The one lapse named is a reflective
  line during E46's shaking.
- **The costs persist.** Both readers listed the write-up, Sal's tag and the holds unprompted.
- **The frame works.** Both readers connected the reprise to the opening.

**What still holds it at 4.** Both readers named the first three independently:
1. **The *None* reckoning has no private scene.** It is settled at E48 in a brief exchange with Baz present, and both
   readers wanted one scene alone in which she asks.
2. **"Upstairs" has no face or want until the woman at E48.** Its pressure is felt, and both readers called it the right
   kind of fog, but the E48 ask introduces rather than pays off.
3. **Act II stacks its rests.** Nearly every peak is followed by a warm pause.
   - The editor would halve **E19, E27 and E35** and shrink **E43** to a page.
   - The target reader's three least-needed chapters include E19 and E43, and its list of stacked pauses includes E27
     and E35.
   - Both say each has a moment worth keeping, and none needs a whole chapter.
4. **Repeated shapes:**
   - the records negotiation (asked, refused on ownership, then terms), about ten times;
   - food as care;
   - chapters ending on a still object or a *did not say*.
5. **Line habits**, by the readers' own counts:
   - about 500 *did not* sentences;
   - about 250 *the way …* similes;
   - eighteen phones laid face down;
   - two dozen *for a long time*;
   - recurring self-quoting tags.
6. **Seraphine's presence**, not her agency (the editor). About half the chapters belong to others, and the
   investigation's cleverest moves are the men's.
7. **The ending lands twice** (the editor): the Vein coda's bell, then the presences.

**Length.** The editor would ask for about 120–125k by compression, losing no event. The target reader asked for no cut,
only less stacking.

**Confusions left on purpose:**
- The glass notice is mentioned before it is explained (F10 allowed one clause).
- One neighbor's unconfirmed account at E34 is by design.
- One Lucien thought at E10 is flagged for the line edit.
