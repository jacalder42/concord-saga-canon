## 1. What it is

- **52 pieces, P00–P49, and 150,000 words** (Act I 46k, Act II 62k, Act III 42k). The two supplements, S02 and S06,
  stay inside their packets.
- **Seraphine carries about 42% of the words;** Baz has 9 point-of-view pieces.
- **Every row carries what a brief needs:**
  - the objective, the opposition (whose will), the act and its risk, the clue, the turn, the consequence and what is
    unresolved;
  - exit conditions, a life beat and a length;
  - **the current EBCI packet it replaces or merges,** flagged *unchanged* (13), *changed* (33) or *new* (4).

  A concordance (§8) lists the packets that are retired.
- **The epistemic ladder.** Every rule and clue carries a status: observation, hypothesis, tested or warranted.
  **The three end B01 with only three warranted things:**
  - it keeps to places, within the city;
  - a practice that reduces harm;
  - the theories they disproved.

  Choosing still surfaces at the end is stated as a **bet**, and its result as evidence against chance, not proof. The
  311 trap supports only *access*: whoever places the boxes gets the city's complaint information. Not who, and not
  why.
- **Varied clue sources:** skill, curiosity, persistence, pleasure, a gift, a bargain, or an act with a cost.
  **Transgression followed by penalty is confined to about nine spine pieces.**
- **Pressure and response:** fourteen pressured choices, each with *why now* and *what else was available*. Nine of
  the responses are constructive.
- **Relationship movement:** eleven pairs, eight different movements. Only one (Lucien and Sal) runs trigger →
  escalation → break.
- **The 311 fix, reconciled.** Lucien wanting the city's 311 times (Q-OL4 #8) becomes the motive for the trap. The
  first thing he asks the policeman for, and is refused, is those times.
- **Dropped, per the answers:**
  - the Dré chart;
  - the man at E48;
  - A's second Poydras piece, A's pump stakeout, and A's TV crew.
- **All 79 obligations are carried.** The handoff (§7) records what B02 inherits:
  - E24's state;
  - the hidden-column grievance;
  - Elisabet's revoked access;
  - Lucien off Sal's job;
  - Seraphine's caseload;
  - who knows what going into B02 E08, E13 and E14.
- **About 54% of the existing prose is kept.**
- **New numbering at packet time:** the Square becomes E14, Ursulines E32, the lot E46 and the tins E49.
