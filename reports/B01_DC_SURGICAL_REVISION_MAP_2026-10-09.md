# B01 DC: surgical revision map

Status: NON-CANONICAL, REPORT, 2026-10-09. It answers a forwarded full read of `B01/dc-2026-10-09/`, made in the private
manuscript repository. It changes no ruling, card, rule, grid row, packet or guard, and nothing in the manuscript was
edited. The chapter maps and the full synthesis are private (manuscript `draft-notes/surgical-map-2026-10-09/`).

## What was asked

The forwarded read recommended a surgical revision map: chapter by chapter, what to preserve, tighten, combine or
strengthen, with a projected reduction toward about 140–150k (midpoint 145k, "not rigid") and the strongest scenes
protected.

## Method

Three readers each read one range in full: Prologue–Ch 17, Ch 18–34, and Ch 35–Epilogue. They worked against the
guards, the protected list and the approved design. A grep check confirmed that no proposed cut removes a plant a later
chapter pays.

## Findings

- **Tiers (whole book, cumulative from 163,423 words):**
  - T1 (restatement, recap, logistics): −1,168 (0.7%), about 162.3k;
  - T2 (plus the weaker repeats of a function): −2,463 (1.5%), about 161.0k;
  - T3 (plus quieter texture): −6,327 (3.9%), about 157.1k.
- **145k is not reachable by surgery.** It needs about −18.4k (11.3%). Beyond T3, the costed candidates reach only about
  153k, and they cut consequences and play that earlier rulings protect. Three revision passes have already removed
  most of the recap, and most of what remains is causal.
- **The read's claims:**
  - Repeated functions: right, and concentrated (E48's withhold-then-confront beats; Act I's five restrained endings
    in a row).
  - Records blurring: right (Seraphine's log has five names).
  - Discoveries that change nothing: mostly wrong.
  - An externally restrained climax: partly wrong (the decisive act is Seraphine's).
  - The read takes *Bastien* for an antagonist. That points to a real clarity gap: Baz's full name is first spoken by
    Caldas at E27A and is never established before it.
- **Priority 1, the two final victories:** already on the page; the remedy is audibility, about 35 words, with no
  explanation and no antagonist defeat.
- **Two continuity gaps:** Lucien's apology to Elísabet is never seen; Mr. Vidrine's oxygen tank is never confirmed.
