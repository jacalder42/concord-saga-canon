# B01: the rewrite-or-revise test — result

Status: NON-CANONICAL REPORT, 2026-10-04. The result of the three-chapter blind test approved in
`decisions/B01_REWRITE_OR_REVISE_COMPRESSION_AND_PLAY_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-10-04.md` (Q-RR3–4, ledger §400). The drafts,
the key, the judges' files and the author's verdict are private, in manuscript `draft-notes/rr-test/`. No manuscript
text is quoted here.

**It does not change** any prose, ruling or packet. It sets the scope under the approved decision rule, pending the
author's approval of the chapter list (§5).

## 1. The test

- **Chapters:** E35 (healthy on paper, heavily layered), E42 (the healthy control) and E46 (scarred).
- **Each written twice:**
  - **A**, revised in place, with the intervention map and the repaired packet's functions added;
  - **B**, rebuilt from a blank page by a different writer, from the repaired packet.
- **Blinding:** X and Y were assigned at random, independently for each chapter.
- **Judging:** the author judged first. ChatGPT's blind read came with his. Two model judges read blind. One judge's
  E46 read was refused partway and re-run by a fresh judge.

## 2. The result

| Reader | E35 | E42 | E46 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The author** | **rebuilt** | **revised** | **rebuilt** (close) |
| ChatGPT | rebuilt | revised | rebuilt |
| Model judge 1 | rebuilt | rebuilt (narrow, low) | rebuilt |
| Model judge 2 | rebuilt | rebuilt (low–medium) | revised (medium) |

- **The author preferred Y in all three.** The key shows Y was the rebuild in E35 and E46, and **the revision in E42**.
- **E35:** the rebuild is unanimous.
- **E46:** the rebuild wins three of four readers, the author's included.
- **E42:** the humans prefer the revision; the model judges prefer the rebuild, narrowly.
- **Length does not explain the result:**
  - E46's two versions differ by 13 words;
  - E42's rebuild is longer and lost;
  - E35's rebuild is longer and won.

## 3. The decision rule

The rule was approved in advance, and the author's verdict decides. **The rebuild wins E46 and E35, but not E42.** That
outcome gives:

> **rebuild the layered, function-heavy chapters; revise the healthy ones.**

It is not a whole-book rewrite.

## 4. A correction to the forwarded reading

**ChatGPT read the sweep as the rebuild winning everything,** concluding that even a healthy chapter improves when the
people are generated again. **The key does not support that.** The E42 version it praised (the institutional
supervisor, the paint-tray mishap, the uncle, the chair, Caro's knowledge of her friend's voices) is the **revision**.
That revision itself carried the repaired packet's added functions.

**What the result does show:**
- **Chapters mostly built from layered function work** (E35's friendship day; E46's aftermath and reckoning) are
  better written again from repaired packets.
- **A healthy chapter** (E42) is better revised in place with the same functions added.
- **The repaired packets work either way:** both winning E42 and E46 versions use them.

**The judges agree on why the rebuilds won.** People and the city keep interfering with the plot, objects carry
behavior, and the leads behave instead of explaining. The revisions read as accomplished but with the machinery
visible.

## 5. Next: a chapter triage for the author's approval

Which chapters count as *layered and function-heavy*, and which as *healthy*, is a list the rule does not settle. A
triage proposal classing all 51 pieces as **REBUILD** or **REVISE** follows. Each class comes with its evidence:
- the test's own discriminators;
- the archaeology's scar and health findings;
- the staging and play audits;
- the blind reads;
- pass counts.

It goes to the author before any prose work. **The three winning test versions are candidates to enter the draft** (E35
B, E42 A, E46 B), subject to a continuity check and the defects the judges named.
