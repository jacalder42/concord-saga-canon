# B01 Act I prose packets: the prose-layer sweep

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Status:** SWEEP NOTE (production). The one lightweight sweep required by
`decisions/B01_ACT_I_PROSE_PACKETS_AND_WRITER_PROFILE_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-27.md`. It is not an audit or a
score, and it checks three things only: **future knowledge**, **database leakage** and **scene theft**.

What it does not change: any EBCI packet, rule or architecture.

## Built

**21 packets** in `ebci/prose/B01/`, about 2,950 words in all:
- the prologue (`B01-E00`);
- E01–E17;
- the three supplements (`B01-S01` to `S03`).

The pilot's E31 and E33 carry the author's two edits. Shapes vary: the prologue and E14 are a few
lines, and E15 carries a short list of fixed facts because it is the Act's event.

## What the derivation left out

The packets were written from the Narrative Briefs. These were kept out of the writer-facing text:
- **E01:** that the site is linked to nothing later (the site's significance).
- **E04, E11:** where Lucien is from and who sent him. The brief's *no Virelli* becomes *the people he
  reports to stay off the page*, with no name.
- **The prologue:** Silence and Hope's names. Two presences, never named.
- **S02:** the spec's ceiling list (*no Marrakesh, no MT, no Elias or Brightbreak*). Those names are
  database, so its prose form is *no network or anyone coordinating anything*.
- **E17:** the cast-discipline phrasing becomes *an old friend and former colleague, never his
  subordinate*.
