## 2. The three outcomes, on the page

**What the crew learns:**
- **L1. The practice**, sit down and nobody up a ladder, is kept as a working rule. **At E49 it changes Jody's outcome:**
  he is seated on a shop doorstep, away from a curb's edge, when it peaks. Nobody says it worked.
- **L2. The pattern.** The cases follow the line. This is made legible at E39 and defeated at E48.
- **L3. The citywide scope** comes from Caro's pages. Seraphine now misreads them on the page first (E44).
- **L4. One wonder kept:** Lucien saves the river reading (E23).
- **L5. Two interests told apart by method.**
  - Paper and permission: holds, the caseload call, revoked access, Stroud's logs sent up as paper.
  - Instruments and collection: the cases, the collector, the badge, the turned lanyards.
  - Only the second wants the times.

**What their actions accomplish:**
- **A1. Dré's file closed with the truth.**
- **A2. The gathering:** her decision (the E44 beat), its reach, its cost and her public correction.
- **A3. The false complaint is withdrawn on the record,** under Lucien's name, on the Thursday afternoon.
  - Sal's text: the complaint is off his license, and the client stays.
  - The red tag stays until reinspection, and Tony's two weeks do not come back.
  - **It settles Lucien's action, not the opposition.**
- **A4. The refusal is a limited contest:** the woman leaves without the record she asked for.
- **A5. A watch that fails.**
  - Mrs. Carmouche's true report goes to the line on the Thursday morning, after the crew tells her what follows a call.
  - Lucien watches the lot at dawn on the Friday. Nothing comes.
  - The case is already on the Square's fence, where they had settled on the Thursday night to keep their watch.
  - His post moves, and **no cause is certified.**
- **A6. The tins:** the bet paid once.

**What changes between them:**

- **B2. The term bites at E47:** asked by the line, he asks her what of hers he may give.
- **B3. Her attraction is on the page,** separately:
