# Veil B02–B03 Episode Architecture Rhythm Audit — Pass 1

**Status:** EDITORIAL REVIEW — NONCANONICAL  
**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Reviewed:** `B02_EPISODE_ARCHITECTURE_PASS1_2026-09-26.md`, `B03_EPISODE_ARCHITECTURE_PASS1_2026-09-26.md`, saga 27-act rhythm matrix, saga pressure architecture, current milestone grid and Warehouse rulings.

## Executive finding

The reconstructed pair is structurally viable and materially cleaner than literal migration of the historical episode expansions.

The key success is that escalation changes **kind**:

- **B02:** curiosity → public language → institutional collision → directional uncertainty.
- **B03:** directional concern → evidence ethics → public consequence → Warehouse human cost.

The pair does **not** need additional major events before a second-pass episode reconciliation.

## B02 rhythm

### A1
Target W2–3 / R2–3 / C4 remains appropriate.
The Marrakesh call works because it expands scale through an ordinary human connection rather than a spectacle cutaway. Lucien's arrival gives the act a second form of arrival: distant evidence comes in; a known person comes physically closer.

**Risk:** too much investigation in slots 05–12.  
**Second-pass instruction:** make 09 and 11 lived/relational scenes first, evidence scenes second.

### A2
Target W3 / R3–4 / C3–4.
This is the densest conceptual act. Dominion, Technarc, Filament failure, cycle measurement and two relationship lines can flatten into meetings.

**Second-pass instruction:** no more than two consecutive episodes whose primary action is analysis/briefing. Convert at least three functions into:
- practical care;
- civic inconvenience;
- social disagreement;
- failed field attempt;
- relationship behavior under work.

### A3
Target W3 / R3–4 / C3.
The current architecture correctly avoids turning “global cluster → drift → threshold → no reset” into four consecutive climaxes.

**Risk:** slots 36–42 can still read as escalating charts.  
**Second-pass instruction:** preserve slot 37 as social/cultural basin and slot 43 as a genuine reward/quiet beat; do not reveal the southwest answer before B03.

## B03 rhythm

### A1
Target W3–4 / R2–4 / C3.
The act needs warmth because the reader must experience what Baz belongs to before losing him.

**Required second-pass emphasis:** Baz gets at least two scenes whose value is primarily relational/human rather than investigative competence.

### A2
Target W4 / R3–4 / C2–3.
The architecture correctly makes **publication changing behavior** the escalation. This is more useful than adding another large Resonance event.

**Risk:** institutional pressure can overtake character ownership.  
**Second-pass instruction:** Dominion/Technarc appear through consequences on people whenever possible; avoid villain-board exposition.

### A3
Target W4 / R4–5→2 / C1–3.
Warehouse is the only action peak. The epilogue is not a second climax.

The proposed slots 38–42 create enough negative space between collapse and Tahl's metaphysical brush, provided they are not filled with new crisis mechanics.

**Required:** after Baz dies, do not increase physical spectacle. Pressure changes from survival → uncertainty → consequence → remorse → uncanny quiet.

## Density / episode-count finding

The historical B02 and B03 expansions should **not** be preserved one-for-one.

Working ranges:
- **B02:** 42–46 narrative units.
- **B03:** 42–45 narrative units including main-text close, with epilogue treated separately if useful.
- supplements are reader-facing but not automatically narrative-episode slots.

These are editorial ranges, not canon counts.

## POV finding

Do not enforce 30/30/30/10 numerically at this stage.

Use it as a failure detector:
- if Baz does not feel like Veil's trilogy lead, increase ownership;
- if Seraphine disappears from lived consequence, restore her;
- if Lucien/Caro/Elisabet only react to other people's plots, give them owned decisions;
- if flex POV exists only to show spectacle, cut it;
- if anonymous MT artifacts begin functioning like named Tahl POV, pull back.

## Global-theater finding

B02/B03 should **not** be expanded into the Neon theater grammar.

That contrast is valuable:
- B02 = distant people become real through communication;
- B03 = distant/directional information becomes consequential;
- B04/B05 = direct parallel theaters become part of the storytelling grammar.

This preserves a genuine formal escalation across trilogies/books.
