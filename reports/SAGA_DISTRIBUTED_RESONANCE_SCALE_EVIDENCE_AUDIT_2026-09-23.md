## Governing distinction

**How far people hear about an event, how many places report similar events, how wide one material effect extends, and whether separate sites are physically coupled are four different questions.** A global channel, shared vocabulary, matching clock times, or emotionally similar witnesses can support investigation; none alone establishes a planet-spanning material wave. A claim of “global” should always specify its referent.

| Evidence class | Example claim | What it can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local effect | NOLA crowd reacts; windows move; local equipment degrades | Something observable happened at one site | Cause or reach outside site |
| Multi-site reports | Filaments or MT receive observations from cities | Multiple sites report phenomena | Same source, simultaneity or common physical carrier |
| Time-correlated sites | Independently dated records overlap within stated clock error | A temporal cluster worth testing | Causal direction or literal simultaneity |
| Distributed physical response | Several distant sites independently show bounded material change | Effects at several locations | Single global event rather than several local events under shared conditions |
| Planetary coupling | One initiating condition produces effects at remote sites with a specified path, latency, limits and budget | A rigorously modeled global event **if** tests survive | This level is not presently recovered as an operational rule |
| Global social consequence | People worldwide change behavior after reports, policy, transport or weather disruptions | Reach of information and human response | Direct Resonance transmission through networks or minds |

These are **diagnostic evidence classes**, not new Resonance states or scale ratings.

## Anti-handwave tests for a true cross-site event

Before describing any B2/B5/B6 effect as a **single physical event with distant reach**, the private mechanics packet must answer:

1. What changes at each site: matter, pressure, light source, acoustic source, power quality, human perception, or only reporting?
2. What common initiating condition is claimed, and can it be dated independently of characters’ later interpretation?
3. What is the proposed coupling between distant places? What material or environmental conditions enable or block it? Do not call digital communication a Resonance conduit.
4. Is there a latency or propagation pattern? How large are timing errors, upload delays, time zones and editing uncertainty?
5. Why are some sites quiet, less intense or different in kind? What would a negative observation mean?
6. What is spent, degraded or left behind locally and, if the source is common, at the source? A qualitative RP equation is not an energy budget.
7. What could a skeptical observer inspect afterward, including ordinary mechanical fault, weather, shared rumors and flawed measurement?
8. How much reaches the public through ordinary networks and institutional framing after the event, independently of the physical effect?

If the packet cannot answer the coupling and budget questions, write “multiple sites reported similar phenomena” rather than narrator-certified planetary propagation. A character may **believe** it is one event and later revise the model. Retain regional material variation and human agency.
