**Finding:** the archive supports **uncontrolled load/leakage/field behavior as a historical concept**, and current B01 requires episodes that are not under the protagonists' deliberate control. It does **not** explicitly settle an independent, self-powering ambient process in Veil B01. The cleanest reconciliation is a **two-account model for review**:

1. **Directed action:** RP is a qualitative potential for someone's chosen, bounded action; Intent selects a direction. Any genuine boost incurs immediate cost and lingering consequence.
2. **Local load and uncontrolled response:** human activity, prior pressure and site conditions may set a field state; an event might proceed without *a focal character's* new decision or Intent. That is **not emotion becoming free energy** and not an intelligent Veil. Source, persistence, trigger, material work, footprint and residue require separate rules. A crowd may be a source of social synchrony or witnesses, never automatically a homogeneous fuel tank.

“Independent of Seraphine's Intent” is defensible as a *story classification*. “Independent of all human activity/energy and able to shake matter at will” is **not established**. Veil B01 should use a bounded local precursor, not import Loom environmental saturation or Neon discharge scale.

### Case R — local load modulates an *already working* interface (PROPOSAL / incomplete)

**Possible test architecture:** a local, finite pressure state precedes Seraphine's response. A materially specified perturbation reaches an existing sound/electrical interface at the Square, causing **a small change in a system already consuming ordinary power**. The amplifier's own electrical power could supply an audible tone or transient; the amp may subsequently cut out, allowing the recovered quiet. If the hum is ambient rather than produced by the amp, identify its *separate vibrating source* and energy input. This case does **not** posit Resonance traveling through a recording or the circuit as a signal.

**Critical missing link:** what exactly varies at that interface—air pressure at a microphone, vibration of a specific structure, input impedance, supply quality, or some other physical parameter? The sources name “hum,” “pressure,” “audio pops” and technology distortion without specifying a transducer, threshold, duration or energy transfer. Merely saying “field coupling changed the amp” renames the mystery. Select **one** actual interface, then state how to discriminate it from ordinary Case O and why the audible phenomenon appears before the amp pop. If no such interface can be defended, classify the amp as ordinary and locate the pressure event elsewhere in private design.

| Audit field | Case R minimum before approval |
| --- | --- |
| Why here/now | Define local pressure history/trigger and small footprint, not “the crowd got emotional” as a sufficient event rule. |
| Input and power | Identify a physical parameter affected and where any acoustic/electrical/mechanical work comes from; an existing powered device can amplify a small input, but cannot supply a missing input explanation. |
| Amp relation | Choose private initiating fault, separate casualty, or coincidence. E15's public cause can remain disputed after this is chosen. |
| Recordability | Two ordinary files might capture pre-pop sound/pop/quiet. Their overlap would corroborate **one acoustic sequence**. Later resettling hum need not be identical and is not an exact fingerprint. |
| Limit and evidence | Bound site, duration, component response and why most nearby equipment remains normal. Name an ordinary test whose result would count against R. |
| Cost / residue | Separate material/ambient depletion or change (OPEN; no recovered equation), ordinary work disruption, and any **deliberate** Seraphine action with its own UARS cost if she actually uses one. |

**Verdict:** Case O is a coherent *rival*, not an explanation of the entire saga anomaly. Case R is a promising architecture for a private hypothesis, **not a fully worked Resonance cause**. No source permits calling it physically complete. The first rule-level decision is whether a local stored/collective load can undergo uncontrolled material response without a new actor Intent; the second is which measurable physical parameter it can affect at B01 amplitude.
