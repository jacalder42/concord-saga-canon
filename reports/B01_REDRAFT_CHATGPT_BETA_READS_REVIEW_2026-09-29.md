## 3. What they add that our panel missed

### 3.1 The telling scope between E26 and E32 (three of four readers; verified)

**The fault.**
- **E26:** Seraphine refuses the untold control, and the agreement is that *everybody on the block gets told*.
- **E32:** on Ursulines, one block of the forecast zone, only Seraphine's client on one side has been told. The trio then
  records a told side and an untold side, and Mrs. Arceneaux, untold, is hurt.
- **The decision that narrowed E26's promise is not on the page.** Was it a choice, a lack of time, or a mistake? One
  reader took a later line to mean the promise covered only the client list. The others remained unsure.

**Why it matters more than a continuity slip:**
- It is the book's central ethical line, and it is crossed off the page.
- It bears on the forecast account the author asked to be one story (Q-EB, §3.2).
- It is the kind of consequential choice the readers keep asking for (§314, L4 and L9): someone decided to leave a block
  untold, or failed to tell it, and Mrs. Arceneaux's wrist is the price.

**The smallest repair** is a decision beat at the end of E31 or the start of E32. Whoever makes the call makes it on the
page, and it costs something in E34. Q-P45-10.

### 3.2 Method stated more confidently than it is shown (two readers, echoed by our reader 7)

- **The forecast's derivation is thin on the page:** why ten days, and why a dozen Tremé blocks.
- **"Clean" is overclaimed:** the control and the uncontacted witness are called cleaner than the coverage supports.
- **Baz's own standard is broken:** his follow-up question is more leading than his standard allows.

The readers mostly read this as the characters' limitation, not the author's. It **supports L6** (showing the crew's
own data and math), and it suggests one character voice the doubt on the page. Caro, as the one with dispatch records,
could ask for a baseline: simultaneous calls happen on ordinary days too.

### 3.3 Self-aware concealment (three readers)

This is a deeper form of our panel's repeated-shapes finding (C4).
- **The pattern:** almost everyone knowingly withholds, copies or alters a record *while narrating why they know it is
  wrong*.
- **The cost:** it is meaningful as a theme, but acknowledging a concealment starts to substitute for moving it.
- **What one reader names:** unsent questions, deleted messages and rehearsed confessions *"eventually reduced
  surprise"*.

**Direction, not an edit:**
- Let some concealments be **discovered by someone else** instead of confessed to the reader. That is dramatic irony
  (§314, E-6), and it is the natural shape of the *None* scene (L1).
- Let fewer be **narrated as self-aware**.

This joins the shape pass (L7).

### 3.4 The recovery shape, precisely (the momentum and wonder readers disagree usefully)

**The two readers:**
- The momentum reader: *"stillness itself was never my problem"*. The problem is a **recurring choreography**: an order
  to sit, eat and drink, then laughter, then *not tonight*.
- The wonder reader defends the bar and river scenes as *"the life at stake"*, not pauses.

**Together they refine L7:**
- **Keep the quiet** (the author's Q-EB3 caution holds).
- **Vary the form of recovery.**
- **Stop deferring the hard conversation.** A rest chapter that ends on a decision reads as life. One that ends on
  *tomorrow* reads as delay.

The worst cases named are E33, and the openings of E16, E19, E35, E42 and E43.

### 3.5 Meaning restated after it lands (three readers)

After an image or a choice has done its work, the narration explains its significance again.
- **Examples readers name:** the pen hovering over *None*, the drowning packet, the map that would not close, the
  withheld folder, and the distinction between people and instruments.
- **The wonder reader:** the strongest chapters *"trust the physical event longer."*

**This is a line-level pattern for the author-led line edit, not a structural one.** Our panel did not isolate it. It
overlaps the Act I review's *recaps* finding (ledger §238) and the review watch-list. Q-P45-11.

## 5. On the two panels together

**Designed differently:**
- Our panel had ten personas with ratings, including fans of the board authors.
- ChatGPT's had four perspectives, with no scores and a reaction recorded before each next chapter.

**Two findings hold across both panels almost without exception:**
1. **the *None* reckoning belongs on the page;**
2. **the official thread needs one real opening by the end of B01.**

The panels also agree that the book's quiet is an asset whose form, not whose presence, is the problem. **Those three
are the strongest evidence either panel produced.** They are the ones to set first beside the author's own read.
