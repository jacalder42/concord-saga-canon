## 4. What the reads found, and what was done

**Fixed in the draft:**
- **Packet fidelity.** The performer's amplifier had worked again after the check, which breaks E14's *dead rig* and
  the rule that *the check proves nothing*. It now stays dead. The second recording is a boy's, because E17 needs him.
  The count of scarce boards in E10 now adds up.
- **Guards and credibility:**
  - E12's route landmarks and a point-of-view slip;
  - E13's clock times;
  - E15's hint of withholding from the office;
  - E15's handover to the new investigator, which both readers found took Seraphine's skill away from her (§3).
- **Continuity and line-level repeats,** listed in the private checkpoint.
- **One derived addition, with no new event.** At E15's close, Lucien sees that his two records now contradict each
  other: the coroner's file says nothing happened to him, and the police notebook says he went down. It gives the act's
  end a latent collision to carry forward.

**Not fixed, and why:**
- **The renewal and the mother go quiet after E09.** The packets reactivate them at E25; the Act II packets should keep
  them warm (**Q-AC3**).
- **The E12 meeting is the packet's fixed coincidence.** Seraphine's reasons for not seeking Lucien are on the page (E07,
  E09, E12). One reader still felt coincidence doing agency's work.
- **A chorus of elders delivering verdicts** in one shared cadence; **E09's middle**; recaps and the theme stated in
  dialogue; the remaining repeated shapes. These are revision work (**Q-AC3**).
- **E11** (**Q-AC2**).
