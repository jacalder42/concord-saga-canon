## Mechanics exposure budget — editorial proposal

Count an **explicit new concept** only when a reader is asked to remember and use it later, not when a lamp fails or a person notices a feeling. At B01 scale:

- **Prologue:** Silence/Hope may establish scale, but do not ask the reader to master Veil physics.
- **Early acts:** repeat concrete observations and evidence practices before assigning theory. Local words can vary across characters; the title “pulse” is not public agreement.
- **Midbook:** allow the first working prediction to be falsifiable, limited and useful. Show the model's errors and competing explanation.
- **Late book:** causal pattern can become stronger locally while ontology and global scope remain unconfirmed. E48 pays **prediction**, not mastery or universal explanation.

This is a qualitative reading-load rule, not a fixed number of new concepts per episode. No fresh mechanical nomenclature, color diagnoses, MT voice, meta battery, formal corridor class or Neon fracture needs introduction in B01.
