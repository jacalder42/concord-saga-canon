## 2. The momentum question

*What outcome is the reader anticipating, and what are the protagonists doing about it?* This was asked of a cold
reader after every chapter.

| After | The reader anticipates | The protagonists do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | The day the unfinished renewal reaches the boy's mother | Seraphine starts to own it and is stopped. The guilt becomes a kept secret |
| E02 | The house inspection, and whether what she felt goes on the record | She asks for her failure to be recorded (refused), volunteers as the on-site witness, and tests the road |
| E03 | Whether she will tell the mother | Caro argues that she must. Seraphine holds, which is a choice |
| E04 | The moment she finds his false *None* beside her own statement | She insists on her own words; he keeps himself off the record |

**Answer.** It is definite. It is not waiting for another strange occurrence. The first five pieces run on three things:
- **the record:** what goes on which form, and in whose words;
- **the secret:** who learns about the renewal, and when;
- **a coming collision** between her statement and his lie.

Both leads make choices in every piece, and the cold reader scored the opening **4 / 5** for wanting to turn the page.

**The gap.** The strange is **not yet a problem anyone is trying to solve.** The characters register it and keep it to
themselves. The road test is the only experiment. The reader was only waiting in two places:
- the first half of E03 (the errands);
- E04 before the site visit, where the corner post arrives and is not followed up.

**This is where the packets put it.** The next pieces carry the engine:
- **E06:** Seraphine acts on a recurrence for the first time.
- **E07:** her first wrong theory, which costs her in public.
- **E08:** her first direct ask, to Mara, refused openly.
- **E09:** Lucien's lie comes back as the mother's question.
- **E10:** Lucien renews the lie by choice.

**Watch item.** E05 is a Life piece, and it follows E03's slower half. If E06–E08 do not turn the strange into
something Seraphine pursues, the author's first-read concern returns: observers, counting in place of action.

## 3. Errors found and fixed

Two independent reads were run: a cold reader, and a checker against the packets and the profile.

1. **Canon identity.** E03 had Seraphine's mother alive. `canon/characters/SeraphineIdentity.md` §III has her parents
   and brother killed in a car wreck. Fixed; an invented relative was removed with it.
2. **The pressure's onset.** E02 dated it before the death; E01 and E04 have it arriving during the compressions. Fixed.
3. **E02's guard.** The road test drew conclusions the packet forbids (a discussion of the unnamed word, and a prayer
   used as a test). These were cut to the refusal alone. The laps were cut to two, with fewer step readouts, which also
   eases counting doing an action's work (Q-EN8).
4. **Who called emergency services.** It was Seraphine, and E02 had given the call to the grandmother. Fixed.
5. **The calendar.** The weekdays did not hold across E01–E03. Fixed:
   - he is ill from Saturday;
   - he dies Tuesday night;
   - E02 is Wednesday and E03 Thursday;
   - E04's site visit is Saturday.

   The book context's late-February window holds.
6. **Smaller continuity slips.** The renewal's signatures, who sat beside the folder, overdue library books, reused
   drive wording in E04, and metric units in an American mouth.
7. **E00** stated what the two presences are, against its packet. Cut.
8. **Line level.** A *"the way you…"* simile tic (about eight; five cut), two aphorisms, a closing chiasmus, repeated
   gestures and a refrain ending were cut or varied.

**Every other guard held**, per the checker:
- the grandmother's word about the card stays her account, never the page's;
- the strange stays felt;
- Lucien's lean has one bearing;
- every objective check finds nothing;
- nobody who sent him is named;
- Vienna is only mentioned;
- every clock time is acted on.

**Noted, not a question:** the surnames *Vael* and *Kael* rhyme, which snagged the cold reader. Canon names are final.
The prose avoids pairing the two honorifics.
