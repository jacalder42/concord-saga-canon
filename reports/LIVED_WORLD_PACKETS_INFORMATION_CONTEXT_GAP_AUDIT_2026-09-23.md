## Verdict

The four packets work as **a source-bounded review architecture**, but the Veil packet is not yet a scene-ready operational bible, and Neon/Loom remain thinner than the surviving historical development. The principal missing bridge is **specific place, date, institution, worker, resource and observer**. They frequently identify what a scene must answer without supplying answers. That is appropriate at proposal stage, but should not be confused with a completed reconciliation.

The original 164,146,176-byte author TAR is present and readable: 72 full JSON conversations plus their 72 Markdown renderings. It is fuller than the sanitized HTML for **those conversations**, not proven to contain the entire account. This pass checked late-November source passages and sampled the much larger Dec. 1 `Worldbuilding` full thread. A later line of development can reverse a Nov. 30 gap designation, but an assistant-produced long bible and its “canon” heading do not alone establish a later author ruling.

## B. Hidden source conflicts that should not be silently adopted

1. **Dec. 1 civic timeline versus current organization:** `Worldbuilding` ~39130–39230 has “FO = primary civic backbone,” “CC = proto-Concord,” military corridors and “DG gone” by Loom. This is historical scaffolding, not evidence every government has collapsed or that the later [Concord structure](../canon/factions/Concord/Concord-Structure.md) can govern settlements. Verify abbreviations and later author/editor treatment before reuse.
2. **Dec. 1 object inventories versus physics:** ~105565 lists prism cards, smoke/foil diagnostics, corridor bells and post-Mending bowls that glow in response to emotional tides. Some are physically plausible observations, others imply a universal emotional detector or reactive artifact. Do not import the list without a mechanism, range, calibration, false positives and costs.
3. **Nov. 30 power timetable versus actual books:** “Neon patchy / Loom largely dead” is a recovered gradient, not a claim that every named city or service follows a uniform calendar.
4. **Current status vocabulary:** An old file calling itself “FULL CANON” or “Tier-2 Canon” is not proof of adjudication. Conversely, calling all Dec. 1 detail OPEN would conceal genuinely developed historical text.
5. **Concord's physical means:** “No permanent infrastructure” and “low-draw hardened cells / portable relays” can coexist if sources, upkeep and hosts are named. “Always enough” cannot be a rule.
