## Gate verdict

**B01 sequence and human-scale narrative architecture: READY FOR AUTHOR/EDITOR REVIEW.**  
**EBCI mechanics specification: CONDITIONAL / HOLD.** The audits do not find a need to alter the locked narrative order, add combat or restore a missing major causal beat. They do find unresolved definition/permission questions that would be propagated into 48 packets if expansion began now. Those can be resolved by short author/editor rulings or by explicitly choosing conservative, noncommittal wording on beats whose mechanics are not yet determined.

The **meta bystander→operative transfer rule is not a B01 blocker** because B01 does not reveal or use it. It remains a Neon/Loom blocker before any successful transfer event. Likewise, the exact post-Mending LT physics need not be solved in B01, but B01 cannot leak LT motifs or mechanistic explanation through the prologue.

## Rulings needed for controlled EBCI

**Essential before drafting any packet with a Resonance event:**

1. Approve, amend or hold the [physics proposal](../proposals/MECHANICA_RESONANCE_RECONCILIATION_FOR_REVIEW_2026-09-23.md) R1/R2/R6/R7: qualitative potential vs realized action; uncontrolled load vs directed output; event evidence; regulation/attunement limits. The existing human-scale bans already remain binding.
2. Approve or amend the [VFX proposal](../proposals/VFX_SENSORY_GRAMMAR_RECONCILIATION_FOR_REVIEW_2026-09-23.md) V1–V3/V5/V7: observation classes, carrier and color interpretation, and separation of FX default from physical permission. An explicit **hold** on self-emitting VT or impossible geometry is an acceptable conservative ruling.
3. Approve/hold the [conflict proposal](../proposals/CONFLICT_SYSTEM_RECONCILIATION_FOR_REVIEW_2026-09-23.md) on quarantining conflicting C codes. An EBCI packet can describe objective/opposition/turn/consequence in plain words without any new numeric conflict code.
4. Confirm which legacy U1–U7 terms, if any, can appear in B01 metadata after the corridor ruling. Conservative path: use ordinary locations and only currently defined weather/load/FX fields; mark U category unresolved rather than inventing a class.

**Metadata/production housekeeping:** In the integrated bible the printed headings E37 precedes E36 and E44 precedes E43. Its rule says narrative order is locked. A downstream parser or editor must follow the approved reading sequence, **not silently sort by heading number**. Clarify whether the headings are labels to correct in place or the file order represents the intended interleave. This is a numbering/manifest issue, not evidence that the narrative must be rearranged.

**Can remain open beyond B01:** meta capture/transfer, exact Technarch harness, wider Neon/Loom fracture ceiling, post-Mending echo-node sensory budget, and complete C-ladder redesign (if C codes are omitted). Do not use their absence as a reason to change the B01 spine.

## EBCI entry contract — proposed, not an episode packet

Each future episode packet should identify the **story job; ordinary action and objective; opposing material condition where relevant; changed relationship/knowledge; optional Resonance event with cause, carrier, observable effect, interpretation, limit, cost and residue; FX observation class; reader reward/relief; chronology guard; exit condition**. “Optional” is real: life, pleasure, work and grief scenes may have no Resonance effect or active opposition. EBCI should not become a machine that inserts an anomaly and a C score in all 48 episodes.

No blanket numerical word count, hue-to-emotion lookup, color-coded character aura, or automatic reader-pressure score follows from this review. Older [ECG](B01_V4_THREE_LAYER_NARRATIVE_ECG_2026-09-21.md) makes its manuscript length an estimate conditional on varying episode lengths.

## Release decision

1. Obtain concise author/editor disposition on the four essential decision groups above and the two heading-order anomalies; record them in a decision ledger.
2. Apply the accepted decisions in small rule addenda or annotations with provenance. Preserve original historical sources and do not overwrite [Mechanica v4](../rules/Mechanica-v4.md) or [Resonance v1](../rules/Resonance-v1.md) merely because this report proposes wording.
3. Recheck one existing B01 event sequence privately against cause→medium→effect→interpretation→cost and one Life/Reward beat against the no-forced-conflict principle.
4. Only then release the integrated bible for controlled EBCI expansion.

**Current result: HOLD EBCI, preserve the locked B01 narrative architecture.**
