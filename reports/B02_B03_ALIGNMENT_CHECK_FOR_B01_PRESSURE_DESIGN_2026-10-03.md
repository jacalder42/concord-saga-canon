## 1. Item by item

### 1.1 The Dominion first named at B02 E08; should the ladder move?
- **Assumes:**
  - Helena's letter is the Dominion's first request.
  - One clause shows the camp report was read, which pays BC-FILE-UPSTAIRS.
  - *His posting's cover holds* (A4).
  - Lucien sends a procedural summary and *tells himself holding back is care*.
- **Verdict: holds, with a small adjustment.** B01 now shows the Dominion's people, occluded and with one face, and ends
  with Baz's account of why he left. E08 then becomes the moment the occluded *people behind the Institute* get a
  name. That is a payoff, not a spoiler. The first *partial obedience* also changes in kind. By B01's end Lucien
  suspects he was used (Q-PI4's betrayal), so his summary is a choice made **knowing** something, not a naïve one.
- **Smallest adjustment:**
  - Rewrite E08's *Want* and *Change* so he sends the summary despite what Baz told him.
  - Hand 1 of BC-FILE-UPSTAIRS is now attributable to the Dominion. Record that in the control layer only.
- **Ladder:** **do not move it.** Naming the Dominion in B01 would spend B02 E08's payoff, and the occluded B01 reveal
  is stronger. It also leaves M51 and the *no faction named in B01* note untouched. The author allows a move but
  does not need one.

### 1.3 B03 E17, Lucien's confession
- **Assumes:**
  - Lucien confesses three things: the grounds have travelled since Bywater, he withheld drift, and he answered
    Helena about Baz without asking him.
  - Baz's hurt is *being decided for*, and *the protection is the insult*.
- **Does it change now that Lucien knows about his review?** **The content holds.** All three items are still things
  Baz does not know. He conceded sending the grounds at B02 E26, but not their use elsewhere (E34) or Helena's demand
  (E14).
- **New fact: the scene mirrors B01.** In B01 Baz kept his knowledge from Lucien *for Lucien's safety*, and it landed
  as betrayal. In B03 Lucien keeps Helena's demand from Baz for Baz's safety. As written, Baz's charge is one he is
  himself guilty of. Unacknowledged, it reads as an oversight. Acknowledged, it is the scene's best material.
- **When Lucien learns about his review:** suspicion in B01 Act III (Q-PI4); confirmation at B02 E34 (1.2). By B03 E17
  he has known for a book. That sharpens his reasons for confessing (*before Baz finds it out*), and the confession
  needs no new item.
- **Smallest adjustment:** one beat. Lucien or Baz names the rhyme once, or Baz visibly hears it and sets his term
  anyway. The narrator does not certify that the two secrets are equal. The term (*the logs keep coming; Lucien does
  not decide for him again*) stands.

    Lucien's *gains nothing yet* call. Use it as a withheld fact, never as a cause.

## 2. Decisions for the author (prioritised)

1. **D1. The scope of what Baz knows.** **Recommend:** he knows *that* the Dominion watches this, and that it is real
   and recurring. He does not know their models, a mechanism, or the Veil. His B02 method is then his own, built
   apart from theirs. This keeps B02's method arc, D1/D3/D5 and Q-V3-2 intact.
2. **D2. Baz's concession at B02 E26.** **Recommend:** keep it, with a sharper, occluded objection about who reads the
   grounds. The cost is what he left for, and he still concedes for Rosette's block.
3. **D3. Lucien's knowledge timeline.** **Recommend:** suspicion at B01 Act III; the camp clause at B02 E08; confirmation
   at B02 E34. LucienID's *used, not complicit* applies to B01. From B02 on, his partial obedience is knowing.
4. **D4. The Dominion's B01 named face in Veil.** **Recommend:** one recurrence in B02, as the local bearer of the
   Dominion recommendation behind E27's order (not a *new* named official, so E27's guard holds). Then one in B03 E14,
   delivering the demand for Baz. No exposition. The alternative, leaving the face in B01, gives the pursuit no local
   body for some sixty episodes.
5. **D5. The B03 E17 mirror.** **Recommend:** name the rhyme once (Baz kept a secret from Lucien for his safety; Lucien
   kept one from Baz for his), uncertified. Also confirm the Warehouse guard: the Dominion's pursuit is never a cause
   (1.4).
6. **D6. The first Seraphine–Lucien kiss (Q-P45-2).** **Recommend:** B03 Act II, after E18's *necessity* and before
   E30, kept apart from Caro–Elisabet's E24 and not caused by the Warehouse. B02 carries line-level heat only. The
   alternative is Neon before the B04 E18 break, which keeps the delay the author objected to.
