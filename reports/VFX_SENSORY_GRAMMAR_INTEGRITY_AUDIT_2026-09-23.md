## Finding

The four established presentation pillars—**color, light, sound and geometry**—offer a recognizable visual vocabulary. The recovered visual bibles also map specific emotions and characters directly to colors, sometimes describe self-emitting VT light and literal topological folds, and sometimes permit physics-breaking movement. These claims conflict with the project's human-scale mechanics, Book 1 restraint and the rule that technology is not a metaphysical carrier. A visual grammar must say which observations are physical, which are limited attuned perception, and which are camera/prose metaphor.

## Main contradictions

1. **Mood colors vs physical carrier.** The [color file](../rules/symbols/COLOR_SEMANTICS.md) calls color “diagnostic,” with blue “truth” and green “grounding.” Notion Nov 24 instead uses green for technological strain and blue for both clarity and empathy; the Nov 26 bento uses blue for sorrow. No universal emotion-to-hue rule survives comparison. A hue cannot be reliable evidence of someone's emotion or veracity.
2. **Observable effect vs visual metaphor.** “Space folds,” “sky breathes,” “shadows tighten” and “a grief aura” can be camera/prose impressions, optical changes in available light, or literal material changes. The sources do not consistently label which. Literal geometry deformation requires a defined physical effect, measured consequence and era permission; the metaphoric version requires no new power.
3. **Self-emission and source.** Notion Hybrid Bento explicitly says VT proximity light has “no external source,” while other rules tie effects to reflections, fog, screens and environmental stress. No physical account of self-emitting light or energy budget was recovered. Quarantine as **OPEN**, especially before Mending.
4. **Physics-breaking exception.** The Hybrid Bento permits levitation/flight during a VT metaphysical moment. The [Notion unified mechanics](https://app.notion.com/p/2b6e7d902ad8802ca15de56ba3bd6b61) bans flight, phasing, object creation and broken momentum; [Mechanica](../rules/Mechanica-v4.md) bars causality bypass. Treat the VFX exception as **SUPERSEDED/INCOMPATIBLE pending explicit author ruling**, never a permission grant.
5. **Channel collision.** Older VFX references MT as a generic technological layer and VT as Veil-Touch. The [September author ruling](../recovery/CHANNEL_NAMES_RULING_2026-09-19.md) makes MT MissingThread and VT VeilThread. AR artifacts and printed text remain human technology; a violet flicker cannot automatically identify private VT contact.
6. **FX default vs physical ceiling.** The VFX ceiling file's blanket Veil “forbidden visible fractures/strobe” and the T1 FX2 default/early bang are not contradictory if FX2 is presentation intensity for a local event and geometry/shard progression remains separately constrained. Calling `default_vfx_ceiling` a hard cap **is** superseded by the later ruling. The exact code→permitted physical effects mapping is **OPEN**.
7. **“Only four expression channels” vs other evidence.** Color/light/sound/geometry are an editorial representation scheme. A trembling hand, persistent circuit fault, water displacement, bruising, smell of overheated insulation or witness testimony are ordinary physical evidence, not illicit fifth VFX channels. Interpreting the four pillars as an exhaustive sensor ontology would undermine the anti-handwave test.
8. **Automatic spectacle order.** [VFX ceilings](../rules/symbols/VFX_CEILINGS_AND_FAILURES.md) lists sound→light→color→geometry→collapse as a rule of thumb. It cannot be a necessary event sequence: a failed lamp may lead the sound, and a silent electrical dropout may carry the cost. Grounding in the carrier and event beats a universal VFX ladder.

## Proposed four-level observation label

| Label | Meaning | Example of evidence requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **OBJ — objective** | Alteration in environment/device/body that another observer could in principle record | Identify fixture, surface, frequency, before/after measurement, plausible alternative and residue. |
| **ATT — attuned** | Character senses pattern/pressure earlier than others, with limits and possible error | Range, masking, fatigue, source ambiguity; no mind reading or moral diagnosis. |
| **POV — subjective** | Perception distorted or personally meaningful without independent confirmation | Explicitly limited to POV; later witnesses may disagree. |
| **MET — metaphor/camera** | Descriptive image without a literal claim about physics | “The room folded around the news” does not bend a wall. |

These are **PROPOSAL labels**, not new canon tokens. A sentence can combine levels if tagged precisely: an objective hum may be described metaphorically as grief, while the grief inference belongs to POV.

## Material and environmental grammar — proposed tests, not settled conductivity physics

| Carrier | Plausible on-page observations to test | Common failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Humidity/water | Condensation, reflection, sound propagation, wet surfaces and persistence of ordinary traces | “Water remembers” as a stored conversation or omniscient replay. |
| Glass | Existing reflections/refraction, vibration, breakage and misregistration in a window/screen | Emotion produces free-standing colored glasslike planes. |
| Metal/electronics | Audible hum, vibration, sensor drift, power fluctuation and a documented failure | Treating data/AR as the Resonance channel itself. |
| Stone/architecture | Existing cracks, load path, acoustic reflection, alignment and measured displacement | Structural memory as direct access to past thoughts. |
| Air/acoustics/light | Sources, shadows, propagation, haze, interruption and shared witness reports | A colored aura with no source, location, cost or interpretation limit. |
| Crowds | Timing, density, imitation, blocked routes, sound, behavior and testimony | Treating every person as uniform conductive matter or assuming a shared thought. |

The table suggests what an investigation might check. It does not assert that Resonance causes any listed effect in every environment.

## Era checks

**Veil / B01:** mostly mundane light and sound; exceptional FX2 event can occur under the later ruling, but its object, witnesses, aftereffects and uncertainty must be specific. No formal micro-Shard, Neon fracture geometry, public global explanation, LT, MT reveal or Marrakesh/Elias leakage. A beautiful, harmless anomaly can stay subjective or locally observable without telegraphing metaphysics.

**Neon:** more reproducible equipment failures, civic records, fractures and meta damage; a large display still needs a causal interaction and cost. AR tears are failure of a device/overlay, not a portal or metaphysical message.

**Loom:** larger physical and sensory footprint with cost and residue. “Topological fold” needs explicit mechanical reconciliation before it can become an objective event. Post-Mending prism language belongs after the Mending and must not imply new shards or cost-free repair.
