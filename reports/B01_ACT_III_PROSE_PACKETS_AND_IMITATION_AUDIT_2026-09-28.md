## 2. The audit

An independent reviewer read:
- the twelve packets against their EBCI briefs;
- the review-side watch-list;
- the Act II checkpoint review and revision report;
- the Act II packets;
- the revised Act II prose;
- the D1–D5 ruling.

**Verdict:** *ready after the listed changes*. It proposed 45 changes, all of them wording. **All were applied.**

**Fidelity: four dropped brief elements, restored:**
- E43's *unexplained*;
- E48's *one person walks out whole*;
- E42's *friends at their worst, fond anyway*;
- E40's *not spectacle*.

**Guards now carried where they matter:**
- the lean's bearing (E45);
- Seraphine's provisional perception (E45, E48).

**Three repeats in the architecture now carry their difference in the wording:**
- **E44 against E35.** E44 is now about her people's *belief* that the practices keep it away. That belief is still
  untested when the ground begins. The packet no longer re-makes E35's discovery or states it as foreknowledge.
- **S06 against S05.** S06 is now set before opening, told as a scene. The versions are set side by side, not argued,
  and someone cares more about an ordinary problem. That element and *VIGNETTE* are **promoted from the S06
  supplement spec**, and the promotion is recorded in S06's status line.
- **The overreach ladder.** Each rung now has its own shape:
  - E41's limit is met, and *some of it goes undone*;
  - E45's limit is passed, *and some of it is him*;
  - E48 shares the burden.

**Endings now avoid worn shapes:**
- **E48** ends *in the Square, among people, with the festival still going*. The negation *not on a page* would have
  planted the image it forbade.
- **E45** loses its spoken lesson.
- **E43** loses *kept it to himself*, Baz's refrain.
- **E39** ends on the open question, not on the window being written down.
- **E38's** *the other place* would have taken E39's remote report; it is now *across town*.

**Other changes:**
- **Anchoring:** E45's anchoring is now *sooner than on the store corner, and not the way she did it there*.
- **E43** is *not music* (music is already on the page in E26, E31 and S05, and comes in E48).

- **Anyone** may be wrong in front of the others, not only Baz.
- **Working labels** that contradicted their packets are made neutral: E44 *Mara's Gathering*, E45 *The Gathering*,
  E47 *Back at the Square*, E48 *Still Water*.
- **Calendar pins:** weekdays are set in E38–E48.

**Act-level findings, which change no packet:**
