### 2.1 B03's standalone shape

**B03's own question:** *what does a rule that waits for certainty cost?*

- **Opening problem.** Standing zones become routine (E01–E02). The review adopts *"advise and
  contain"*: clearance by order only for *"imminent threat to life, as the models judge it"*. No model
  forecasts a shock (E09).
- **Escalation.**
  - The Warehouse District does not fit (E13).
  - A party moves there on the folk map's *safe* (E10, E25).
  - The zones push night work toward it (E20), and two rival models flag it (E23).
  - Baz asks for *imminent* and no model gives it (E27). The institutions are sure in their frames
    (E28), and the intervals shorten (E30).
- **Consequential choices.**
  - Seraphine's rota and finite circle (E12).
  - Lucien's refusal and confession (E16–E17).
  - Baz goes in person (E30).
  - Seraphine stays with the father (E35).
  - The rescue (E37).
- **Climax:** the Warehouse (E36–E38).
- **Changed end state.**
  - A death the cast does not yet know.
  - A rule exposed.
  - Technarc exposed.
  - A contested public record.
  - A first kiss.
  - A named stranger with a rule for himself.

**It answers its own question.** It is not a Veil epilogue.

### 2.2 Baz before his death (and the adversarial test)

**He has a life in B03:**
- **his work:** the standing map (E01), testing the MT voice and refusing to hunt its author (E05),
  refusing the oracle role (E10), restraint (E20);
- **his pleasures:** the chorba and Trip's mercilessness (E08); the second line, where he is a little
  better and Lucien is worse (E26);
- **his irritations:** teasing Lucien (E05), arguing with Harlow (E33);
- **his friendships:** the walk back with Seraphine, where each tells the other one thing Lucien does
  not know (E13); the repair with Lucien, and the invitation (E17).

**He has an unfinished future:**
- his sister's visit next month;
- the chorba on the specials board;
- *next time* at the second line;
- the map he is redrawing;
- E17's practical question, *what are they doing with it now?*

He leads **15 of 48** episodes. He reads as a protagonist of this book, which is what makes a reader
expect him in B04.

**The briefs worked against that, and this is the audit's main finding.** Ten narrative briefs
before E38, across all three books, told the writer he dies: *"no death foreshadowing"*, *"before the loss"*, *"the friendship is
worth losing"*, *"the ordinary anchor B04's grief draws on"*, *"an ordinary goodbye"*, *"no ominous framing"*. Each was written as
a guard, and together they organise his life around dying on the one layer prose sees. **Corrected
(§3.1).**

**The adversarial test.** If the reader did not know Baz was supposed to die, would they expect him
alive and important in B04? **After the correction, yes.** Nothing in the briefs now anticipates the
death.

**One prose watch item:** the future plans cluster just before the Warehouse. The repair and invitation
(E17), the kiss (E24), *next time* (E26) and an evening that ends with a plan for the morning (E30)
all fall in Act II. Each is ordinary, but prose that lingers on them together would read as the
genre's doom pattern. They are architecture (approved); how long each is held is prose's.

### 2.3 Warehouse causality

The rescue is understandable and bounded:
- **He is inside by his own reason** (E30), as an observer Rieux admits (E31).
- **He is delayed by an institution** that does not cause the event (E33), and does the useful thing
  (the stragglers).
- **He hears one child**, frees them, and takes the only open way out (E37).
- **A second fall kills him after the rescue** (E38).

**His death is not required by his choice.** He wanted out, and the entry was still falling. The
logistics in pass 5 §B make the sibling's path plausible, and the forklift operator's avoided bay shows
what the warning did do (E29 → E32 → E36).

The post → crowd → delay chain stays inference: E32 and E34 say so on the page, and E46 keeps the
counterfactual private.

### 2.4 Tahl's ladder

**The ladder:**
- **B02:** S01, S03 and S04, the voice and its mark; E41, the brush from Silence and Hope's side, *"someone
  working late"*.
- **B03:** S01 and S02 (a self-correction); S03, the coordinates.
- **E46:** his unnamed POV.
- **E47:** named, and the brush he notices.

**It reads as recognition, not a delayed introduction.** The voice has had an ethic for two books:
restraint, corrections, the work before the worker (Baz says so at B03 E05). At E47 the reader learns
a name for someone already known by their conduct. The E46 → E47 delay is one episode, short enough
not to be a trick. **Data question:** the stale W4 exception (Q-V3-3).

### 2.5 Seraphine through the climax

She leads **6 of 48** episodes (the nine-book V2 figure), and **her Act II is thin**: from E12 to E35 she
leads only E18, shared.

**Through the climax she is not a witness to a handoff.** She is absent from the Warehouse **by her own
earlier choice** (E12), and the book tracks that choice:
- E35 is her parallel climax: she stays with the father while the coordinates light her phone.
- E40 shows the rota working without her.
- E44 is led through her not-knowing.

This is protagonist agency with a cost, and it grounds B04's *"the person she could not save"*.

**Note, and an offer (Q-V3-1).** E21's POV is `[P]`, the rota's Filament at the off-registry
household's door. **The household is in Seraphine's own finite circle (E12)**, so she can be the one at
the door when Caldas Ren asks for the list she never made. That gives her Act II a scene of her own
with no new design beyond who is present.

### 2.6 Lucien: refusal, confession, listening

Three consecutive episodes, each a distinct consequence of a B02 decision:
- **E16 answers B02 E23/E26:** having sent the grounds and watched them travel, he refuses to hand over

- **E17 answers B02 E34/E47's secrets:** the truth to a friend, and a repair.
- **E18 answers B02 E21 and E31:** he listens, and she lets him.

They do not blur. **Note:** they sit back to back at Act II's opening, and prose may intercut them.

### 2.8 Veil knowledge progression

What the cast reasonably knows at the end of each book:
- **B01:** anomaly → a window model → prediction without explanation. *Systemic, not understood.*
- **B02:** a public word → not unique → signs and interval → the method travels → drift → *no reset*.
- **B03:** a standing map → a contested lean → corridor against building → **an event that exceeds every

art arriving on the page unintroduced. **Corrected** (§3.3); the question of when the word enters is
Q-V3-2.
