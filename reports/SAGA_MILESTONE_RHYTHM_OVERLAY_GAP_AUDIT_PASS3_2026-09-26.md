Scope: overlay the approved 53-row milestone grid against the 27-act rhythm waveform, geography/cast separation, soft POV targets, relationship lanes, antagonist modes and Mechanica escalation.

This report may recommend **candidate new milestones**, but does not add rows to the live grid.

## 1. Milestone admission test

A candidate milestone should normally do at least one of the following:
1. irreversibly changes a character's available choices;
2. changes a relationship's durable state;
3. changes institutional/faction authority or behavior;
4. changes a place/route/community in a way later books must remember;
5. creates or destroys durable evidence/knowledge;
6. changes who controls or can use a consequential system/channel;
7. incurs a cost or creates an obligation that carries forward;
8. materially changes the reader's model of the saga.

A beat is **not** a milestone merely because it is:
- emotionally strong;
- visually spectacular;
- a new city;
- a fight;
- a romantic scene;
- a clue;
- a condition becoming worse;
- useful pacing relief.

Life/Reward usually belongs in episode architecture unless it creates durable state.

## 4. Relationship overlay — missing durable changes

### Seraphine / Lucien
Current grid has M05 Lucien's institutional break and M33 final guide role, but **no explicit relationship-state milestone across six intervening books**.

This is a real structural blind spot if the central romance is a saga spine.

Candidate milestones should not mark kisses/heat. They should mark durable relational decisions.

**Candidate R-SL1 — Neon mutual-choice milestone**
Window: B04–B05.
Function: after Baz/Neon fracture, Seraphine and Lucien consciously choose continued partnership despite believing they may be dangerous/unreliable to each other.
Carry-forward: makes B06 near-break and B09 guide role hurt/mean more.
Status: **NEW CANDIDATE — source reconciliation needed.**

**Candidate R-SL2 — B06 fracture-with-obligation**
Window: B06 aftermath.
Function: their crisis changes how they protect/withhold/ask for help; not merely “they are strained.”
Carry-forward: defines Loom relationship behavior.
Status: **NEW CANDIDATE if recovered romance sources support a durable state change.**

### Caro / Elisabet
M07 covers B02 bond; M33 includes final goodbye/guide transition.
That leaves the primary second romance structurally invisible for B03–B08.

**Candidate R-CE1 — independent partnership choice**
Window: B04–B05.
Function: Caro and Elisabet choose a durable relationship coordinate while maintaining independent work/geography.
This should explicitly avoid “they become the stabilization couple.”
Status: **HIGH-PRIORITY NEW CANDIDATE.**

**Candidate R-CE2 — Loom commitment/payoff**
Window: B07–B08.
Function: relationship reaches a durable state that makes B09 goodbye consequential.
Status: **HIGH-PRIORITY NEW CANDIDATE.**

## 6. Geography overlay

The current grid still underrepresents recovered global theaters.

Do **not** add “Vienna appears” or “Singapore scene” as milestones.

A location earns a milestone only if something there changes.

### Candidate G1 — B05 differentiated-theater proof
Window: B05 A1/A2.
Function: one non-NOLA theater undergoes a durable change that demonstrates regional failure modes are materially different, not merely differently colored.
Possible ownership:
- Vienna: archive/institutional access changes;
- Singapore: Technarc system/accountability change;
- Reykjavík: stable comparison site loses/changes a protected assumption.
Status: **NEW CANDIDATE; choose one or at most two, not all three.**

This could also solve B05 A1's apparent milestone gap.

### Candidate G2 — B07 route/refuge decision
Rather than M25's abstract “diaspora expands,” choose a specific Filament/community route or refuge allocation whose loss/opening determines later travel.
Status: **REPLACEMENT CANDIDATE for M25**, not an extra row.

However, two books may need a **durable human-state turn**, not just relief.

### B07
The funeral/Kade sequence covers grief and succession, but the wider cast needs a durable answer to “what are we preserving while everything fails?”
This may be carried by a route/refuge/community choice rather than a separate emotional milestone.

### B09
A2 needs a durable pre-Mending commitment.

## 12. Net-density implication

Current grid: 53 rows, but not 53 equal milestones.

If:
- M21/M22 merge;
- M36 retires/folds;
- M24/M25 become condition/replacement rows;
- M15 is replaced/relocated;

then adding roughly **5–8 genuinely durable milestones** could improve structural coverage **without materially increasing actual tentpole density**.

The likely additions should disproportionately be:
- relationship decisions;
- knowledge thresholds;
- institutional consequences;
- human commitments;

not more Resonance spectacles.
