# Conflict system integrity audit

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Status:** FORENSIC / NON-CANONICAL — pre-EBCI gate 2  
**Dependency:** [Mechanica and Resonance audit](MECHANICA_RESONANCE_SYSTEMS_INTEGRITY_AUDIT_2026-09-23.md). Open physics questions remain open.  
**Scope:** conflict causes, modes, escalation, outcomes and interaction with Resonance. No rule files or B01 narrative order changed.

## Finding

The recovered Conflict Engine contains a useful **scene brief**, a distinction between *scope* and *local escalation*, and five kinds of resolution. It also overstates Resonance's role: its claim that conflict can escalate **only** when Resonance and emotion align contradicts ordinary material conflict and the low-amplitude, no-combat Book 1 architecture. Two incompatible historical C ladders carry the same code. Neither is yet a current controlled vocabulary in [canon rules](../rules/canon_rules.json). Copying either into EBCI would quietly promote development material to canon.

Recommended direction, still **PROPOSAL**: a conflict begins when agents, conditions or institutions obstruct an objective, need or obligation. Emotion changes choices and perceived stakes; Resonance sometimes changes costs, perception or physical conditions. Record objective, opposing force, available options, change and consequence before adding a Resonance overlay.

These are not alternate names for identical numbers: C2 changes from *cause/scope* to *local confrontation*, and C4 shifts existential to irreversible. The November C3 romantic quota conflicts with its own Neon note (“multiple C3s”) and should not govern the revised beat architecture. Its “post-Mending C0 only” would prohibit ordinary disagreement after the Mending, without support from current physics. **Do not use bare C0–C5 in an episode packet until one vocabulary is chosen and documented.**

## Recovered dimensions and issues

### Conflict Tier 0–5

The December tier list mixes micro-tension, interpersonal dispute, ideology, personal breaking point, systemic stakes and metaphysical convergence. Thus it is not a single ordinal measure of size: a personal break can be quiet yet irreversible, an ideological dispute can have local stakes, and a systemic conflict can start as procedural denial. **RECOVERED structure, PROPOSAL to revise as scope or tag set rather than assume increasing intensity.**

### Mode M1–M5

Internal, interpersonal, environmental, social/factional and cosmological describe useful *arenas*, but a scene often has two: a bureaucratic restriction (social) plus an argument (interpersonal), or a flood (environmental) plus a dispatch failure (institutional). M3's original examples favor Resonance storms and AR glitches and omit ordinary weather, infrastructure, terrain and exhaustion. M5's “Veil itself becomes adversarial” risks granting agency to a reactive system. The “internal is always deepest” rule is an editorial judgment, not a mechanical truth.

### C0–C5 in the December engine

This ladder can describe a **local state/turn** if reserved for scene escalation, independently of the conflict's scope. Yet C4 and C5 also inject global magnitude and metaphysics; an irrevocable private decision is not automatically an existential crisis, and an existential threat may be met with restraint rather than an acute clash. The nine-field bento's `Escalation Path (C0–C3)` does not even include its own C4/C5. Preserve as a **candidate** after a semantic cleanup, not as a compulsory numeric grid.

### Five resolution patterns

Vulnerability, Alignment, Sacrifice, Structural Shift, Grief are recovered *narrative shapes*, not guaranteed conflict solutions. A confession can leave a subpoena in force; shared goals can leave insufficient transport; sacrifice can fail; a structural change can create another dispute; grief can coexist with sustained opposition. Resolutions may also be partial, delayed, imposed or refused. “Repair beat needed” is a useful question, not a required outcome.

### “True/False conflict”

The December source uses false = fight the wrong target and true = confront a “real wound.” This is an **interpretive character-arc lens**. It must not relabel a material dispute as false because the people involved have unresolved emotions. A real eviction, staffing shortage, travel barrier or detainment is true as a material constraint even if the characters misunderstand its cause. Prefer **misattributed cause / accurate cause / unresolved cause**, each with evidence.

## Mechanical separation

**PROPOSAL:** Keep five independently describable things:

1. **Conflict substrate:** competing objectives or scarcity, authority, bad information, time, money, location, injury, access, work, care, consent or incompatible duties. Name who/what resists and what concrete outcome is at stake.
2. **Character response:** interpretation, emotion, tactic, commitment and error. Emotion can intensify a reaction without creating the original constraint.
3. **Resonance interaction, if present:** apply the [physics audit](MECHANICA_RESONANCE_SYSTEMS_INTEGRITY_AUDIT_2026-09-23.md)'s cause, coupling, observable effect, cost and residue test. “Emotion spikes → conflict automatically escalates” and “breath → C3 becomes C1” are **SUPERSEDED as universal causal laws** in the proposed model.
4. **Scene trajectory:** what changes in options, knowledge, risk, relationship or institutional position. A scene may hold tension, relieve it, defer a decision or let life happen; it need not climb two ladders.
5. **Reader and thread pressure:** keep reader intensity by cohort in `grids/reader_pressure.csv`, and milestone pressure in the specific `thread` column of `grids/milestones_payoffs.csv`. They are distinct from conflict scope, local scene tension, UARS load and W weather. The [canon rules](../rules/canon_rules.json) mark thread scores provisional and calibrated by trilogy.

Non-resonant conflict still has **ordinary cost** (time, lost trust, missed wages, injury, missed opportunity), but the Mechanica requirement for a Resonance-influenced action's immediate and lingering costs must not be indiscriminately applied to every argument or pleasant disagreement.

## Anti-overreach checks for future scene work

- Can the conflict exist if Resonance is removed? If yes, retain its material cause.
- Does each party have a concrete objective, constraint and reasonable alternative from their knowledge? An antagonist's humanity does not erase accountability or material power.
- Is escalation caused by an act, deadline, new fact or changed option, rather than by an arbitrary emotion/VFX spike?
- If a character calms down, does the physical/legal/institutional problem still exist? Track both separately.
- Does resolution change a condition, decision or relationship, or merely apply a reassuring label?
- Is the scene's reader intensity distinct from conflict magnitude? Quiet grief can be high reader pressure, a large logistical task can be low.
- Does a Life/Reward scene earn its function without retrofitted friction? The B01 architecture explicitly permits scenes that accomplish little for saga causality.
