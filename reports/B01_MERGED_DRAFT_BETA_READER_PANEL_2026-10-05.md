# B01 merged draft: the beta-reader panel

Status: NON-CANONICAL EDITORIAL REPORT, 2026-10-05. It records a panel of ten simulated beta readers run at the author's
request (*"Run a blind beta-reader panel on the merged draft"*). The panel used the same personas and brief as the 09-29
panel (`reports/B01_REDRAFT_BETA_READER_PANEL_2026-09-29.md`, ledger §313). The readers' notes (about 676,000
characters) and a fuller synthesis are private, in the manuscript's `draft-notes/beta-read-merged/`. **No manuscript
text is quoted here.**

**It does not change** any ruling, card, packet or manuscript text. **Nothing is edited before the author's read**, and
the questions in §5 wait for it.

**What it is not:**
- **Not the author's read**, which remains the primary one.
- **Not a market test.** These are model readers given personas. Their agreement shows convergence among simulated
  tastes, not demand.
- **Not the editorial lenses.** Nothing here authors the deferred fields in `canon/editorial_lenses.md`.

## 1. The panel

Every reader read the merged B01 (`B01/merged-2026-10-05/`, 173,713 words) in full, in reading order from E00 to E50, with
no design notes and no canon. Each kept a note after every chapter (with a pull score from 1 to 5), act notes and final
thoughts.

| # | Reader | Stars | Book Two? | Their one change, in brief |

## 4. Against the 09-29 panel

**Better:**
- **Stars and pull:** stars are up from 3.8 to 4.0, with no 3.5s, and pull is up in every act.
- **The *None* now lands on the page.** It was 09-29's C6 (6 / 10). Readers now name E44 and E46 as its payoffs.
- **The opposition has a face.** On 09-29 it had none (C5, 7 / 10); now the complaint is only that the face comes late.
- **The romance has a peak.** C7 (5 / 10) was "never catches"; the kiss now gives it one.
- **Act III's former troughs rise:** E43, E33 and E49.

**Unchanged:**
- the watchers (C2);
- rests placed after peaks (C3);
- the elder-feeds-her shape (C4);
- Caro and Elisabet under-used (C8);
- knowledge that never accumulates visibly (C9).

**New:**
- Lucien's repeated withholding;
- E48 as a list;
- the romance now ending cold, precisely because it now peaks.
