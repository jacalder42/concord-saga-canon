## 1. What changed, by function

| Piece | Repeated shape | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **E27** | *Refused by someone who is right*: Seraphine asked Caro for the dispatch times, was refused, and conceded that Caro was right | **She does not concede.** She grants Caro's reason and names what it costs them; Caro names what it costs her. Neither gives ground, and the scene moves on with the disagreement standing |
| **E31** | The same arrangement: Trip refuses Lucien the Vein's front window, and he accepts | **An imperfect bargain.** Trip still refuses the window but offers a lesser seat, on her terms, and Lucien takes it and names a day. He gets less than he asked for, and she is not sorry. The chapter's close no longer settles his unease: it ends on the uncertainty of the case and on the window opening |
| **E33** | A recovery chapter after the climax, repeating the shape of Miss Tavie's earlier care speech (Act I) | **Shortened, and it now changes a commitment.** Miss Tavie's order to rest is partly for her own sake, and she asks something back: that Seraphine drive her to St. Roch early the next morning. Seraphine agrees. **E34** now opens after that visit |
| **E28** | Duplicated setup and aftermath around Caro's dawn with Elisabet | **Trimmed.** The encounter's choice (Caro steps back when asked, and asks nothing back), its attraction and its consequence are kept. The aftermath is now Caro's own small act: she looks Elisabet up, and puts the phone away without reading it |
| **E26, E35** | Predictable emotional closure at the chapter's end (a gift; a shared laugh) | **Cut.** E26 ends on the shop being closed around them. E35 ends on a silence that is not resolved |
| **E36** | The Guidry letter's decision happened offstage and was reported at the bar | **On the page, in Baz's point of view** (Q-A2C3): Lucien drafts, keeps putting a reason in, and chooses to leave it out; he signs and mails the letter himself, early on the Friday. That night at the bar he says only when it will arrive and that the reason is out. Neither the letter nor the sending is replayed later. The false report to the city follows the same day, so the juxtaposition the author named is on the page: the true statement in the morning, the lie at night |

## 2. What was left alone, and why

- **E32** (the act's climax) and the E34 notebook question are unchanged: they already vary outcome (right on time and
  place, wrong on everything else; a theory questioned by its owner).
- **E35's delivery** is kept. It is the rest the answer to Q-A2C2 keeps provisionally, and its job is different: Baz
  commits to staying in the city, and the two men agree to carry the column's secret together, which costs them both.
- **The Baz guards** held through the edits: no omen, no case seen, no leaving motif.
- **One mechanical fix** found on re-reading: a quotation in E35 had been split in two.
