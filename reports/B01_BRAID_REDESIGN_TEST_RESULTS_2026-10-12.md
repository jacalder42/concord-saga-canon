## 1. The integrated outline

The two braided designs merge into one outline:
- 41 chapters, plus the frame pieces and an optional Ch 38A;
- about **121.2k** words (Acts 32.5k / 55.6k / 33.2k), or **about 119.4k without 38A**;
- about 8k of new prose.

**One merge conflict was resolved by splitting a scene.** The B-into-A chain (Baz's lie → Lucien's false report → the
hold on Mr. Vidrine's oxygen) has to start before about 70%, which is where the answers put Mr. Ledet's injury. So:
- **Ch 25 (about 58%):** Lucien's rooms are entered and the box opened, with no injury. Baz lies that it was the boxes.
- **Ch 30 (about 70%):** Caldas returns for the folder, and Ledet is hurt. That exposes the lie (*"It wasn't them on
  your stair"*) and forces the river-wall confession.

**Restored after the link correction (§524):** the lunch errand, the band night, the towel and the direct Seraphine–Baz
scenes.

## 2. The 90-day reconsideration: realism

Federal (42 CFR 435.916, high confidence) and Louisiana (manual K-0000, G-1500, H-1800, medium confidence: search
excerpts only, because the state sites were blocked):

- **The rule is real.** A renewal returned within 90 days of a procedural closure is reconsidered without a new
  application.
- **The paperwork is different from the draft's.** For a child who has died, a parent signs a *current* form that
  reports the death. Coverage is approved **through the date of death**, and the earlier months must be asked for.
- **The old signed January form cannot be mailed,** because it lists Dré as living. That gives the confession a real,
  practical reason: Seraphine cannot get the new signatures without telling the family why.
- **The clock does not bite as drafted.** A late *new* application still reaches back three months, and the hospital's
  vendor would likely file one anyway. Missing the Monday deadline would not lose the bills from the death month.
- **The fix:** put one real bill in the earliest uncovered month, such as a February sick visit or ER trip after the
  closure. A late application loses that month; the 90-day route keeps it. A late-January closure puts day 90 at about
  1–4 May, which fits calendar C.

The S1 sample already uses the corrected paperwork, and all three judges called it convincing.

## 5. Recommended adjustments, for the author (Q-ST1–7)

**Q-ST1 · Take the test results as answers.**
- Renée's kitchen moves to the final Sunday, with the corrected paperwork and br's closure line grafted in.
- Caldas breaks Ledet's wrist with his own hand.
- The stair has **no declaration**. Writer profile §11 stays as it is.
- The barrier is **folded into Seraphine's Ch 38**: no 38A, with the S4A grafts.

This makes the book about **119–120k**, inside the soft cap. **Recommended: yes.**

**Q-ST2 · Give Caldas one visible loss at the festival** (the editor). For example: the woman in cream shoes walks past
him, and his own people take the case he came near. Or Isaiah's film catches his face, and Rieux writes his name down.
The exact beat is to be chosen in packets. **Recommended: yes**, as one beat, with no explanation.

**Q-ST3 · Untangle the wrists.** Ledet's wrist is the scene the judges loved. Recommended: Mrs. Arceneaux breaks her
**hip** at Ursulines (it is serious, realistic for her age, and it raises the stakes), and Mr. Lionel's injury at the lot
becomes something other than a wrist.

**Q-ST4 · Make the chain's last step visible on the page** (both paper-test readers). One short beat should show the city
bulletin listing *Kerlerec 900*, and a delivery hold for "flagged addresses" pending inspection, reaching Mr. Vidrine's
supplier. He is named in the scene. **Recommended: yes.**

**Q-ST5 · Tell the watchers apart earlier** (both readers).
- By the midpoint, give one plain scene where Baz or Miss Tavie says what each side wants: *askers want people; boxes want
  times and places*.
- Say once, clearly, that Lucien works for *the Review* (the Institute's), and keep the festival's *"the Institute"*.
- The woman in cream shoes stays unexplained, but she is seen by then not to be with Caldas.

**Recommended: yes.**

**Q-ST6 · Make the clock bite** (realism). A real bill in the earliest uncovered month, such as a February ER visit for Dré
after the closure, which only the 90-day route covers. The closure falls in late January so that day 90 lands on the
Monday after the festival. **Recommended: yes.**

**Q-ST7 · Names.** Renita and R. still need the author's new names. The packets use placeholders until he chooses.
