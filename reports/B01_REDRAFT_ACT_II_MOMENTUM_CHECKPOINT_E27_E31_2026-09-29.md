# B01 redraft, Act II: the third momentum checkpoint (E27–E31)

**Date:** 2026-09-29
**Status:** EDITORIAL REPORT, non-canonical. It is the third Act II checkpoint, on the author's Q-AC3 test: *does the
reader want the next development?* The earlier checkpoints are recorded at ledger §302 and §303.

**What it does not change:** no ruling, card, packet, brief or grid. **It quotes no manuscript text.** The drafts are in
the private manuscript repository, `B01/redraft/act-02/`.

**Findings are bounded.** They record what one cold reader and one packet checker found, and are not an assurance that
nothing else is wrong.

## 1. What exists

**E27–E31 are drafted: 12,020 words** after revision, against the packets' 13,900. This is the stretch before the
page's window: the brunch, Caro's dawn, the Marigny, the Vein and Easter. **Act II so far (E16–E31) is about 40,800
words.**

**At the end of E31 the reader would start E32 that night.** They also said the pull belongs to Lucien rather than the
heroine, and that part of it is worry that the window's first day goes to another meal.

**On Seraphine's line:** she owns the method and the ethics (the ask, the walking, the two restraints), but not the
news. The two new pieces of evidence reach Caro and Lucien, and her Metairie thread goes quiet. **Carried forward:** in
E32–E34 the page's day and the block are hers to answer for, and the briefs give her the overreach (E32), the refusal
to be looked after (E33) and the block's questions (E34).
