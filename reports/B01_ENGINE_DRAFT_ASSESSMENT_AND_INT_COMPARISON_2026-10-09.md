Status: REPORT, NON-CANONICAL, READ-ONLY, 2026-10-09. It changes no ruling, card, rule, grid row, packet or manuscript
file. The comparison files and the consolidated decision set (`ASSESSMENT_AND_COMPARISON_RECONCILIATION.md`,

## The forwarded assessment

A whole-novel editorial assessment of the engine draft, forwarded by the author.

- **Verdict:** a character-led speculative mystery with a coherent moral engine. Its central finding is that standalone
  satisfaction lags series appeal.
- **Advice:** compare against the 10-08 integration draft before changing anything; then make targeted interventions
  rather than a rewrite.
- **Checked against the text:** its claims hold, with two small misstatements. Lucien does name *the Institute*; what he
  withholds is the people behind it, unnameable in B01 by ruling. And the tools are his own.
- **How it maps:** its priorities fall onto items already costed (Q-EC, Q-AT). New are Elísabet's independent objective
  and its *protect the texture* instruction, which withdraws the trims on three protected spans.

## The comparison

Three readers compared every chapter of the integration draft (INT) and the engine draft (ENG) in full.

- **Result:** ENG is the better base in every range.

  | Range | INT → ENG |
  | --- | --- |
  | Act I and E15 | −6.8% |
  | E17–E31 | −11.7% |
  | E32–E50 | −7.6% |

- **The one exception:** E41 is the only chapter that is net worse.
- **Where the gains are:** real momentum, conflict and consequence, concentrated from E32 on.
- **What was lost:** ENG gained its speed mostly by cutting restatement, and it also cut about 1.8k words of muscle: costs (Sal's debt, Walt, Baz's best argument and self-indictments), Lucien's foresight and avoidance beats, setups whose conclusions stayed, and some desire and humor.
- **New problems:** about a dozen seams, most from the fix round or the trim.

## Recommendation

**ENG plus targeted restorations, in the one correction pass already proposed.** The book lands at about 155–157k. The
consolidated questions Q-UD1–12 supersede Q-EC1–8 and Q-AT1–11.
