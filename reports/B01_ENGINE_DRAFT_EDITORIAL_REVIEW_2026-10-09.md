# B01 engine draft: editorial review

Status: REPORT, NON-CANONICAL, READ-ONLY, 2026-10-09. It reviews the accepted engine revision (`B01/eng-2026-10-09/`,
about 157k words, in the private manuscript repository). It changes no ruling, card, rule, grid row, packet or
manuscript file, and it does not replace the author's read. The full synthesis and the five reader reports are private,
in the manuscript's `draft-notes/eng-review-2026-10-09/`. Nothing here quotes the manuscript beyond a few words.

## Method

Five independent readers, each reading in full: Act I, the first and second halves of Act II, Act III, and the whole
book cover to cover with a pull score per chapter. They were briefed with the author's rulings and told not to repeat
the compliance checks. The lead checked the slips they reported against the text: three were confirmed, three were
withdrawn, and the rest were left as possible.

## Findings

- **Verdict: 4 / 5, with the middle at 4.5; the reader would buy Book Two.** There is no true sag. The densest run of
  strong chapters is at 54–76% of the book. The low points are E03's bar span, E08, E17 and **E48**, which closes
  threads by listing them just before the climax.
- **The engine revision worked.** The question is legible, the mystery assembles, dread precedes events, and the
  opposition is a contest.
- **Problems, ranked:**
  1. One relationship beat repeats about 15 times: a withheld thing, discovered, then answered with silence. Its line-level form is negation (about one *did not* per 310 words).
  2. The opposition is felt first at the end of Act I, named at 49% and confronted at 97%. It is polite and empty-handed, and the two-hands scheme is explained more than dramatized.
  3. The book's answer is not in Seraphine's mouth.
  4. E48 is a ledger, and E49 opens on a new loose thread.
  5. About 7–10k words of charm spans and in-book recaps are recoverable, which would give about 147–150k.
  6. Several threads are under-paid and are not in `grids/breadcrumbs.csv`.
- **Recommendations:**
  - A shape pass, with no quotas.
  - Seraphine states the answer, with no mechanism.
  - Compress E48 and move the email into it.
  - Trim the recaps.
  - Pay or register the threads.
  - **One design question for the author:** an earlier, unnamed contact from the side that asks *who*, in Act I.
