## 3. What it shows

> **Correction 2026-09-29** (`reports/CONFLICT_ENGINE_AND_PROFILE_TEST_REVIEWS_RECONCILIATION_2026-09-29.md`, after
> ChatGPT's review): item 1 below overstated the result. **What the evidence supports:** *in this small test, the
> amended profile together with character pressures generally improved judged stakes and persistent consequences
> while preserving voice; continuity fit worsened in two scenes, and removing repair instructions produced mixed
> results.*
> - Arm B changes the profile and the pressures together, so the test supports that package; it does not isolate
>   the profile.
> - C's mean (two scenes) should not be set against three-scene means. The matched-scene table is in the
>   reconciliation §2.
> - The two judges read the same eight drafts: their agreement is not a drafting replication.
>
> §4's questions are **replaced** by the reconciliation's §4. The text below is unchanged.

1. **The amendment works as intended, and costs nothing it was feared to cost.**
   - Both amended arms beat the current profile on stakes and on consequences that survive the scene.
   - The voice held: "still the author" did not fall.
   - **Neither judge found manufactured conflict.** One judge's summary: the risk in this book is over-repair and
     expansion, not hostility.
2. **The packets' resolution clauses are one driver of in-scene repair, not the only one.**
   - In E24, removing the clause won with both judges. The version that kept it repaired late, and came last.
   - In E42, the version without the clause came last with both judges, and its conflict still resolved within a page.
   - **So the profile and the packets both need the change.**
3. **A new risk: freer drafts run past their scene.**
   - Two E24 versions set their evening on the day E25 owns.
   - Two E42 versions drop Baz, whom E43 needs.
   - The current-profile drafts fit best. Part of that is a confound: the neighbouring episodes were drafted under the
     current profile.
   - **Both judges independently proposed the same remedy:** a firm time box for each scene, and the conditions the next
     piece needs, keeping the permission to leave damage unrepaired.
4. **The packets fix the events.** Every version of a scene shares its prescribed beats, and sometimes its lines. So the
   150k redraft's new packets are where the structural changes (the spine, the Q-OL4 fixes) will take effect.

**Caveats:**
- The sample is small: one draft per arm per scene, and two judges.
- Two drafters (E42-A, E24-B) hit the rate limit before echoing their stack codes. Their files are complete, but the
  codes are unverified.
- Seraphine's pressures are inferred.
- The fit criterion favours the arm the neighbours were written under.
