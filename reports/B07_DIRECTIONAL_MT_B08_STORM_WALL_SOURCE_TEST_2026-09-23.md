## Finding

Two author-pasted December 8 story functions survive: **B7 Kade's MT material helps point people toward Honey Island** and **B8 a storm wall parts, revealing an alignment chamber**. Neither author-pasted line specifies a literal electromagnetic bearing, worldwide emotional synchronization, an act of storm-control, or a newly stable pre-Mending Echo Node. An assistant-generated expansion later literalized the first as a crackling southeast-pointing signal, and assigned the second an Elisabet route-find followed by storm displacement. Those are **draft implementations**, not recovered author-approved physical laws.

Recommend **information becoming directional** in B7 (evidence, maps, instructions, decisions), while retaining an **open objective physical option** for the B8 storm-wall opening subject to medium, triggering condition, spatial extent, persistence, cost and independent traces. A real storm passage is more valuable narratively than a pure metaphor if it is earned by route knowledge and bounded weather/geometry; the evidence does not yet establish how it happens.

## B8: is the wall a thing?

The December macro requires an **entry/revelation transition**, but allows at least three manifestations; the author/editor should select one deliberately:

| Candidate | Minimum physical change and evidence | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Weather/visibility aperture** (recommended test) | A shifting band of wind, rain, spray, airborne particulate or optical distortion creates a temporary visible and traversable window. Independent sightlines, wet/dry line, debris direction, exposure and a closing interval bound the opening. | Need why the gap opens *now*, and whether characters cause it, merely predict it or exploit an ordinary pause. |
| **Structural corridor change** | A known path between two surveyed points becomes physically traversable; routes, water level, footing or built remnants change in documented ways. Distinguish storm opacity from route geometry. | “Architecture obeys destiny” without supporting substrate/coupling, load, altered materials and cost. |
| **Perceptual revelation only** | A break in sightlines, a vantage change or a mapped interpretation reveals an existing chamber/path; storm itself may remain violent. | Must still earn “wall parts” in objective scene language; not necessarily a new Resonance event. |

**Not supported by current evidence:** a painless act of Caro/Seraphine regulating a whole storm; Tahl Echo as universal remote weather control; an already stable pre-Mending Echo Node that provides a free safe path; an instantaneous global wave from the November E15 ending; automatic access because the party has “aligned emotions.” The Dec expansion's Elisabet path-finding and Rex equipment can provide human agency and measurement **without** deciding which physical aperture exists.

A worked scene later must answer: **what was blocking whom; what shifted; where and for how long; what material/atmospheric condition enabled this; who detected the window and with what fallible evidence; whether anyone actually changed the storm; what was risked/consumed; what remained after it shut or passed; and what a pursuer could document or misconstrue.** Storms can affect route visibility and services on a scale; degradation is not wholesale technological death. If the chamber itself is a pre-existing place, “revealed” is an observation, not proof a new chamber materialized.
