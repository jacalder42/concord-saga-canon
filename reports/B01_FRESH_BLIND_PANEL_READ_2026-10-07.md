# B01 fresh blind cover-to-cover read: four-reader panel — report

Status: NON-CANONICAL PRODUCTION REPORT, 2026-10-07. Authority: the author's *"1 b 2 now"* (ledger §453) and his
question on adding a romance reader. The detail is private, in the manuscript repository's
`draft-notes/blind-read-2026-10-07/`: the setup, the questions, four readers' notes and `SYNTHESIS.md`.

**What it does not change:** no canon, no manuscript text and no decision. It is evidence for the author's decisions
and for the specialist reads.

## Panel

Four blind readers each read the whole Pass 3 draft (`B01/infer-2026-10-07/`) in order:
- mystery;
- urban fantasy and serial;
- literary;
- romance (added at the author's question, with four romance questions).

They had the experiential questions only, plus a per-chapter *"Next now?"*.

## Result

- **4 / 5 from all four readers. All four would buy Book Two. No chapter got a "no"** (one *probably-no*). Every
  *probably* falls on a rest chapter.
- **They converge on four things:**
  - **The opposition needs a face, wanting something specific, by the midpoint (4 of 4).** Three name it as their
    one change.
  - **The chapter endings share one restraint-coda shape (4 of 4).** The worst run is reading chapters 30–35.
  - **The rest chapters come too often after peaks, clustered in Act II (4 of 4).** The readers' rule: every rest
    should move a secret or cost something.
  - **Withholding reads as series management by Act III (4 of 4),** above all the refusal to name the organization
    on the last page.
- **Also (3 of 4):** the penultimate chapter audits itself, and the narration decodes gestures. The latter is a small
  residue that Pass 3 did not target; it calls for no new filter pass.
- **Split:**
  - Lucien's *None* correction offstage (the mystery reader wants it on the page; the romance reader does not);
  - the watcher frame;
  - the romance reader wants Seraphine's desire on the page and one private, unhurried scene with Lucien after the
    kiss.
