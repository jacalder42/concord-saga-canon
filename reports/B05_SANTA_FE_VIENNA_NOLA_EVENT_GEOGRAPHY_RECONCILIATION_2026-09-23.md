## What cannot be inferred from three names on a map

1. **Network of reports ≠ physical wave.** Correlated city incidents, common institutional reporting, and one propagating Resonance front are three different causal claims. None is established by the recovered place list. Do not draw NOLA → Vienna → Santa Fe as a traveling storm.
2. **Multi-city pattern ≠ shared sensor.** A rig in Vienna cannot supply Santa Fe raw measurements by narrative analogy. A record of blackouts, humidity effects, AR frame loss, civilian injury, or street geometry must say *where, when, by whom, and by what method* it was observed.
3. **Institutional inference ≠ objective truth.** A Technarc model can predict Santa Fe badly or suppress its own uncertainty; the manuscript should distinguish raw physical proxy, model output, public claim, editorial interpretation, and eventual outcome.
4. **Character channel ≠ field instrument.** Tahl can receive, authenticate, contextualize, and publish reports through mortal MT. His access is subject to sources, delay and errors; MT is not remote sensing or Resonance transmission. Rex can inspect/repair an actual rig if his established route and access warrant it, not through telepresence.
5. **Site medium matters.** New Orleans' humid river/civic infrastructure, Vienna's dense archive/transport/institutional infrastructure, and Santa Fe's dry high-desert built environment offer different potential coupling conditions. These are environmental questions to test, not automatic color palettes or effects; no universal mood-color map follows.
