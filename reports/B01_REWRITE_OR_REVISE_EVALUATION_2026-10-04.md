# B01: rewrite or revise — an honest evaluation

Status: NON-CANONICAL EVALUATION, 2026-10-04. The author asked for it on 10-04 (ledger §396): *"an honest evaluation
about whether or not the manuscript should be re-written with the current rules etc vs revised."* It rests on:
- the compression archaeology audit (`reports/B01_COMPRESSION_ARCHAEOLOGY_AUDIT_2026-10-04.md`);
- the play audit (ledger §397);
- the staging pass and its two blind reads (ledger §396);
- the git history of draft 3.

**It does not change** anything. It recommends, and Q-RR1–5 await the author.

## 1. The short answer

**Neither a whole-book rewrite now, nor another round of additive passes on the whole draft.**

1. **Fix the packets first.** They are where most of the thinness came from, and B02 and B03 will be drafted from the
   same system.
2. **Then decide by experiment, not by argument.** Rewrite three chapters from the repaired packets, revise the same
   three in place, and have the author, with two blind readers, judge them without knowing which is which.
3. **Let the result set the scope:** a selective rebuild, a whole rewrite, or revision.

I hold this recommendation with moderate confidence. The evidence cuts both ways, and the deciding variable (whether a
fresh draft from better packets reads better than a patched one) is measurable but has not been measured. §5 explains
why I don't simply pick one.

## 2. The case for revising

- **Most of the text is healthy.** The audit's REBUILD spans total about 3,200 words, 2.6% of the book.
  - Both blind readers of the staging pass found distinct people, believable misreads and consequences that persist.
  - Earlier whole-book readers scored drafts of this lineage 4 / 5.
- **Much of the recent value lives in the text, not in the packets:**
  - the staging work and the eighteen approved holds;
  - the misreads that persist across chapters;
  - the pressure design;
  - the E49 reveal;
  - about 90% of play rows marked PROTECT;
  - the best scenes both blind readers name.

  A rewrite would have to re-earn all of it, and the packets do not fully encode it.
- **The last rewrite is the cautionary evidence.** The 09-29 redraft was written fresh from packets. It fixed the
  structure (agency, an antagonist driver, events). **It also dropped everything the packets did not carry as an
  obligation:** life beats, witnesses, after-scenes, bodies. That is exactly this audit's finding.
  - A new rewrite from repaired packets would carry the known functions.
  - It would also drop whatever the repaired packets still fail to name, and no packet names everything.
  - It would bring its own line habits. *I know* and *said nothing* both rose in the new lineage.
- **Cost and momentum.** The author is mid-read, and a rewrite resets that read. The recovery map is bounded: about
  +5k to +10k new words of function, five rebuild spans, and a line pass.

## 3. The case for rewriting

- **The draft is heavily layered.**
  - Acts II and III average 8–9 draft-3 passes per chapter; the range runs to 14 (E48), with up to 20 commits.
  - Only 46% of current long phrases survive from the redraft. In E31, E35, E36, E39, E47 and E48 it is 30% or less.
- **The passes optimized against audits, one dimension at a time, and each paid elsewhere.**
  - The under-120k humanism pass cut 14,790 words to a quota and took felt duration and habitual life with the
    recaps.
  - The pressure pass added beats at fixed estimates and displaced texture.
  - The staging pass then had to add reception back (+4,224).
  - The pattern is visible: a correction, a side effect, a correction of the side effect.
- **Patching has a ceiling.** About 60 additive functions across about 35 chapters, plus five rebuilds, plus a line
  pass, means another full-book pass on top of the layering. Additions inside a scene built to carry function can read
  as additions. The audit's revision-scar finding says this risk is real in the late Act III disclosure scenes.
- **The root cause is upstream.** The thinness is mostly a property of how the packets specified the book, not of
  this text. Revising the text treats the symptom for B01 and leaves the cause in place. A rewrite from repaired
  packets treats both.
- **The author's recurring concerns all trace to the drafting system:** time fixation, missing description, PCM
  repair without cause, thin play. A rewrite is the only path on which the repaired system is tested on a whole book
  before B02 and B03 depend on it.

## 4. Where the evidence actually points

| Question | Evidence | Leans |
|---|---|---|
| Is the problem in the text or the system? | Mostly the system: the Writer-options route, *Ends* truncation, unchanged estimates, quota cuts | Fix the system either way |
| Is most of the text bad? | No: 2.6% scarred, most sampled chapters healthy, blind reads positive | Revise |
| Is the text's best work encodable in packets? | Partly. The holds and pressure design are in packets now; the staging texture and the misreads' exact landing are not | Revise, or rewrite with the current text as a required reference |
| Would a rewrite repeat the last rewrite's loss? | It would lose whatever the packets still don't name. The last one did | Revise |
| Would patching produce a patched book? | Likely in the five scarred spans and the late disclosure scenes; unproven elsewhere | Selective rebuild |
| What does the author need to see next? | One coherent book to read, and a decision he can trust | The fastest path to a judged comparison |

**My judgment.**

- **Whole-book rewrite:** not justified by the evidence yet.
- **Whole-book additive revision:** risky, because of the layering.
- **Most defensible now:**
  - repair the packets;
  - rebuild the scarred spans from rebuild packets (the current text kept as a reference, not as text to preserve);
  - recover the high-value functions surgically elsewhere.
- **One untested possibility could overturn this:** a chapter written fresh from repaired packets, with the current
  chapter as a reference, may simply read better than the patched one even where the current chapter is healthy. If
  so, the whole book should be rewritten. That is why §5's test exists.

## 5. The proposed test

- **Three chapters, written two ways each:**
  - **A (revise):** the intervention map applied to the current chapter;
  - **B (rebuild):** written fresh from repaired packets, with the current chapter and the audits as references and
    no requirement to keep its wording.

| Chapter | Why |
|---|---|
| **E46** | Scarred (the reckoning table), heavily layered, a primary–primary disclosure with designed heat |
| **E35** | Healthy on paper but among the most layered (12 passes, 30% or less of the redraft's phrasing); off-duty friendship time between primaries, where the losses are largest |
| **E42** | A control: healthy, layered (10 passes) and protected, with the book's best wordless primary scene |

- **Judges:** the author first, plus two blind model readers. Each reads A and B unlabelled, in random order. They
  judge which reads more like lived fiction, which keeps the scene's obligations, and which they would rather read
  next.
- **Decision rule:**

| Result | What follows |
|---|---|
| B wins on E46 only | Selective rebuild: the five spans plus chapters like E46; revise the rest |
| B wins on E46 and E35 but not E42 | Rebuild layered, function-heavy chapters (perhaps 10–15); revise the healthy ones |
| B wins on all three, E42 included | **Rewrite B01 from the repaired packets**, the current text as reference |
| A wins, or no clear difference | Revise per the intervention map |

- **Cost:** six chapter drafts and the reads, against about 50 chapters and a restarted author read for a blind
  whole-book rewrite.

## 6. What applies whatever the result

- **Repair the packets (U1–U7)** before any prose work. That covers:
  - promoting life beats from Writer options as functions;
  - raising estimates or naming kept texture when beats are added;
  - after-scenes and witnesses allowed;
  - cost lines that name the residue;
  - line-pass exemptions;
  - a stale-packet sync check;
  - non-answers and interruptions in disclosure pieces.
- **Retire the chapter-level cut quota.** No future pass cuts to a word target. The Sudowrite import copy stays a
  separate derived artifact.
- **One more pass on the whole book, not several.** Whatever the scope, the next prose work should be a single
  integrated pass against one map, followed by the author's read.

  The last week ran fourteen passes. Each was justified alone, and together they produced the layering this
  evaluation weighs. *Stop improving architecture merely because we can* (author, 09-27) applies to prose passes too.
- **The limits of this evidence.** Every audit here was written by the same model family that drafted the text. The
  author's own read is the only independent judgment available, which is why the test puts it first.
