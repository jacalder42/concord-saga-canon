## Operational boundary to test (PROPOSAL, not an established law)

1. Separate **human stabilization** (lowering panic, improving coordinated movement, nursing, changing exposure, creating shelter and an exit path) from **Resonance/structural stabilization** (a bounded physical change in a field or material system). The first can work without a supernatural act; its proof is behavior, throughput or harm reduction. The second needs actor, directed Intent, substrate/medium, effect, finite limit, personal cost and residue.
2. In each B4 card name a controllable variable rather than “stability”: e.g. fewer people exposed for a finite interval, less movement in one assembly, or a delayed local transition. These are **illustrative possible variables**, not recovered occurrences or a new damping power.
3. Failed intervention may reflect **wrong diagnosis, no structural leverage, insufficient time, exhausted actor, growing load or adverse medium**; success may reflect different exposure, coordination, a smaller bounded target, or simply a delay. These are rival explanations to test, not assigned causes.
4. No breath, empathy or feeling repairs physics by itself. Breath/grounding can help a human regulate what they deliberately do; empathy can inform whom to aid; structural support may change ordinary load or available action. None supplies arbitrary additional energy or erases costs.
5. “Force worsens outcomes” is a tendency to evaluate in a specific overload scenario, not a prohibition on emergency physical support or an excuse to withhold it. Technology may remain functional, degrade or fail on its own scale.

## Anti-handwave tests / next evidence

For **each** of the two proposed event cards ask: (a) exact target and local conditions, (b) first observable abnormal change, (c) actor's movement/tool/choice, (d) why that action could couple here, (e) failed alternative or control, (f) duration and limit of effect, (g) immediate and lingering cost, (h) inspectable trace and witness disagreement. Record separately state, shard severity, weather, UARS load, corridor class and ordinary tech symptoms. If there is no demonstrable field change, write a human rescue or crowd-management scene instead of certifying “Resonance stabilization.”
