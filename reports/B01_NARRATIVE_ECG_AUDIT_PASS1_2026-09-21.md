## Executive finding

Veil Book 1 Migration Draft v3 is structurally healthy enough to continue reconciliation rather than restart.

The compression from recovered E00 + 42 episodes to Prologue + 36 episodes improves the book. It removes repeated interpretation/escalation beats while preserving the swamp-child wound, Seraphine/Lucien trust progression, Baz's arrival and investigative function, New Orleans recurring geography, Velvet Vein/Trip as ordinary-life infrastructure, Caro/Elisabet's B1 seed, prediction/anticipation, aftermath/care, Jackson Square recurrence, and the quiet final payoff.

The largest remaining peer-calibrated weakness is not plot or action. B1 still has more threat anticipation than pleasure anticipation.

## Key findings

### Mode rhythm
The sequence is varied. There is no long uninterrupted action run and no long exposition run.

Main dense stretches:
- E6–E13: anomaly/pattern pressure, relieved by E8–9 and E11;
- E16–22: investigation-heavy, relieved by E19 and discovery pleasure at E20;
- E28–33: systemic pressure, mitigated by aftermath beats E31–32.

### Reader rewards
Strong: CHAR, MYS/DISC, REL, WORLD, PAY.
Adequate: ACT, WON.
Comparatively light: FUN, HEAT, non-crisis pleasure.

### Character bandwidth
Act I emotional core: Seraphine + Lucien.
Act II–III emotional core: Seraphine + Lucien + Baz.
This is clean. The risk is over-seeding supporting faces, not overpopulation of the core.

### Place memory
Jackson Square is the strongest location loop.
Velvet Vein's ordinary-room → rumor-room → continuity-room progression is excellent and should be protected.
The swamp must remain materially memorable enough for Book 9's return to carry emotional specificity.

### New / Deepen / Pay
Act I appropriately introduces.
Act II increasingly deepens.
Act III increasingly pays off.
Do not solve remaining ambiguity by adding new formal terminology.

### Anticipation
Crisis anticipation is strong.
Positive/social anticipation is weak.

Highest-leverage recommendation:
Seed and pay one clearly anticipated non-crisis event—performance, meal, gathering, outing, cultural night, etc.—using existing cast/place infrastructure. Do not automatically interrupt it with a supernatural attack.

### Relationship architecture
Seraphine/Lucien is strong because it changes behavior.
Caro/Elisabet's E23 seed is good but risks feeling isolated if Caro has no independent earlier presence.

### Wonder
Wonder is still too often coupled to threat.
Protect at least one or two strange/beautiful/curious experiences that are not immediately punished.

### Recovery / aftermath
This is a major strength. Protect E26, E31, E32, E34 and E35 from later compression.

P1 — Add one positive anticipated event.
P2 — Give E16–22 distinct experiential shapes so they do not become seven evidence conversations.
P3 — Seed Caro before E23 if recovered chronology supports it.
P4 — Protect wonder without danger.
P5 — Protect recovery beats from compression.
P6 — Do not add combat.

## Overall diagnostic

GREEN / CONTINUE RECONCILIATION.

Veil Book 1 v3 does not require structural restart.

Its strongest current identity is:
urban magical mystery + relationship trust + civic place-memory + care under uncertainty.

Lean further into lived New Orleans, positive anticipation, ordinary pleasures, layered relationship beats, recurring-place memory, and micro-payoffs. Do not solve remaining issues by increasing plot density.
