## 3. Questions, with recommended answers

**Q1 to Q3 and Q5 would rewrite cards and rules against their content, so each needs a *"ruled"*
to apply** (CLAUDE.md §2). Q4 implements an existing ruling.

### Q1. Promote the transfer loop, and reconcile the card to it (findings 1 and 2)

The card cannot teach the transfer model while that model is only approved design. So the question
is whether to **rule** meta Q1–Q3 and Q5: the draw, what charges a meta, Intent, and the interface
as refined in Q2 below.

**Recommended: yes, rule them.** They are the author's own 2025 words, and the next meta work
depends on them. Then, in one pass:

- **Mechanica §48** is amended to a three-part model:
  - **The donor's stored load:** power and emotion, unintegrated.
  - **The recipient's Emotion and Intent**, which is what spends the load.
  - **Uncontrolled leakage:** what happens when nothing spends it. This is §48's *"fail
    catastrophically under pressure"*.
- **`Manufactured_Metas.md`:**
  - l. 9 becomes *"people altered to hold resonance they cannot spend, without Intent or emotional
    integration"*;
  - a **storage-and-transfer section** is added: why institutions make them, what a draw costs;
  - l. 21's thesis is kept, reworded from *"remove emotion"* to *"remove Intent"*.
- **`Manufactured_Metas_Variants.md`:** each variant's *"Design Intent"* is checked against the
  three-part model.

**Alternative:** keep the loop as approved design, and add pointer notes only. **Not recommended:**
it leaves the card teaching the old model to every downstream writer.

### Q2. The interface: what crosses, and what the apparatus does (finding 2)

**Recommended:**

- **What crosses:** the donor's stored load, **body to body, by physical contact**. That is what
  *"close"* means.
- **What the apparatus does:** a harness **positions, restrains, times and monitors.** It **never
  conducts** (Mechanica §23).
- **Distance extraction never succeeds as transfer.** A distance machine can **provoke leakage and
  instability**, and that is all its *"rarely works"* ever achieved. The transfer answer's Q5 is
  corrected to say so.

### Q5. Metas are people (finding 5)

**Recommended:** keep every mechanical limit:

- no Intent;
- no ascension;
- no restored powers;
- no stable long-term existence;
- no POV.

Then **change how the cards frame the people:**

- **Variants:** *"Not Characters"* becomes ***"People, not POV characters"***.
- **Card:** *"power without humanity"* becomes ***"power taken from people"***.
- **Card:** *"remove humanity from the equation"* keeps its question, but the answer is that
  **institutions tried, and the people were still there.**
- **Card:** *"No redemption arc"* becomes ***"No return to Intent or to what they were; their
  personhood is never in question"***.
- **Allowed:** evidence of **former identity** (a name recovered, a keepsake), **relationships**
  (someone looking for them), and **how they were recruited or coerced** (*"tricking the weak or
  poor into seeking grandeur"*, the author, 2025-11-10).
