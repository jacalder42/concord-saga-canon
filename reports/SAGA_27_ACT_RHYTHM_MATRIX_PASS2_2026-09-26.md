# Cross-act findings

## 1. B03 → B04 requires a real reset
B04 A1 should not inherit B03 A3 reader pressure. It inherits **consequences**, not tempo.
This is the first major saga-scale recovery basin.

## 2. B04 needs subtraction, not another escalation layer
If Protocol 9, Saeko/Ito politics, Tahl remorse/MT, Kade, multi-city fracture, romance movement and Riot of Light all demand climax treatment, B04 becomes a trailer for Neon rather than a book.

At episode architecture, choose a smaller set as A/B turns and let the rest be pressures/conditions.

## 3. B05 gains speed through cross-cutting
Because switching Vienna/Singapore/NOLA/Reykjavík changes systems, people and texture, B05 does not need higher average R than B04. Its density can feel larger while individual scenes breathe.

## 4. B06 should peak once
Santa Fe/Tahl is the Neon climax.
Act III aftermath is **consequence multiplication**, not a second action climax.

## 5. B07 is the crucial anti-flattening book
B07 A1 at W5/R1–3 is one of the saga's most important tonal moves.
The world is worse; the prose does not need to shout.

## 6. B08 wound sites need different genres
- Santa Fe: elegy / forensic return / repair.
- Mound City: discovery / confirmation / human history.
- Serpent Mound: threshold / pursuit / escape.
If all three are “arrive, test Resonance, pay cost, leave,” the book fails regardless of plot correctness.

## 7. B09 A2 needs protected life
The NOLA feint/preparation interval is the last major place to show ordinary human value before the Mending. Do not fill it entirely with logistics and antagonist approach.

# Provisional rhythm actions for later architecture

1. Protect B04 A1 as grief/social reset.
2. Decide which B04 functions are turns versus background pressure.
3. Build B05 around differentiated theater modes.
4. Keep B06 Tahl catastrophe singular and stagger aftermath knowledge.
5. Design B07 A1 as quiet-high-stakes.
6. Give each B08 wound a distinct narrative genre and sensory/social ecosystem.
7. Reserve B09 A2 for meaningful Life/Reward/intimacy/wonder alongside preparation.
8. Re-score live milestone pressure only after book/act architecture accepts these principles.
