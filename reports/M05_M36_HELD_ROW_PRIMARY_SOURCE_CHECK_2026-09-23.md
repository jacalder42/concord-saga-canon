## Material provenance corrections

1. **M14 is mistimed even within the older Notion scene sequence.** The [B4 bible (Nov 23)](https://app.notion.com/p/2b4e7d902ad8806ea342c63609b8d6da?pvs=204) has Saeko become the face of civic fear at E6, while the [B5 bible (Nov 23)](https://app.notion.com/p/2b4e7d902ad880ccb54ae341b81c8dc2?pvs=204) expressly moves her from local panic to the *national stage* at B5 E6. The grid's M14 “goes national” in B4 A2 fuses two rungs. **Propose** B4 emergence and a separate B5 national-scale candidate; the later macro does not require Saeko by name.
2. **M17 “first public B5 meta” has an internal historical conflict.** The same [B4 bible](https://app.notion.com/p/2b4e7d902ad8806ea342c63609b8d6da?pvs=204) shows prototypes at E7 and a malfunction setting off public panic in Act III. [B5 E7](https://app.notion.com/p/2b4e7d902ad880ccb54ae341b81c8dc2?pvs=204) has a *new* Blinking Girl field test with civilians present. A B5 first-public claim cannot be taken verbatim if B4's public malfunction survives; neither old scene is automatically retained by the later macro, which protects manufactured-meta failures beginning B6. Distinguish first prototype, first covert trial, first public witness and first disclosed evidence.
3. **M30 “Technarch collapses in B8” overstates the old bibles as well as the later structure.** [B8 E8/E13](https://app.notion.com/p/2b4e7d902ad880b69584f33efdbd15b1?pvs=204) stages active Technarch pursuit and meta assault, then calls the institution *dying*; [B9 E7](https://app.notion.com/p/2b4e7d902ad8802a87d6f7128127f583?pvs=204) stages the explicit collapse of remaining rigs/metas. The [Dec 6 Loom card](https://app.notion.com/p/2c1e7d902ad8805485e8c22a084bc21b?pvs=204) still speaks of Technarch **remnants** adding B9 pressure. A B8 *loss of reach or attrition* is a candidate, not proven organization-wide terminal failure.

## Additional mini-bible control: “living battery” is not an operative transfer

The [Nov 24 manufactured-meta mini-bible](https://app.notion.com/p/2b5e7d902ad880198b9afd6390157795?pvs=204) does use “living batteries,” but calls the metas *closed pressure systems*: they cannot direct/discharge/resolve internal Resonance and their storage becomes unstable. The metaphor supports exploited human pressure containment and dangerous field presence; it **does not on its own document a donor→relay→operative boost**. Its old equation and Veil Pressure mechanics precede Mechanica v4; compare before any transfer proposal. The [Notion targeted recovery](../proposals/concord-2026/NOTION_TARGETED_RECOVERY_PASS2.md) captures this distinction. No technology rule should turn all graded degradation into failure.
