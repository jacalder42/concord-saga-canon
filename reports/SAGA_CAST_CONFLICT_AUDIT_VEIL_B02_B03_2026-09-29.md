## 0. Short answer

**The finding is split, and the split is sharp.**

**The civic spine is a real conflict engine.** In B02 that spine is the forecast, the price, the refusal, the
submission, the order and the standing zone. In B03 it is "advise and contain", the offer, and the Warehouse.
- Decisions are made under uncertainty.
- The people and institutions opposing them have legitimate claims.
- The costs stand, land on named third parties, and compound across books.
- Lucien in particular knows the right thing and does not do it, more than once. The consequences stay.

**The ensemble is a care engine.** Between protagonists:
- Nearly every disagreement is dissolved inside its own episode, through understanding.
- The one secret between friends is confessed and repaired in the same scene.
- Every relationship ladder only climbs.
- A Veil refinement explicitly rewrote one scene as *"complementary, not people against data"* (B03 E13).
- Across the Narrative Briefs of all 95 packets, **no protagonist argues with, is angry at, or resents another
  protagonist.** The "argue" hits are all institutions or Harlow.
- The costs protagonists pay are mostly their own exhaustion: *"at a cost to herself"*, *"spent"*, *"will not go
  home"*. Lucien is the exception; his costs are standing and relational.

**Verdict:**
- **B02** is a civic-consequence engine with a care engine for its cast.
- **B03** is an event and institutional engine with a care engine for its cast. It has **zero** conflicts between
  protagonists that leave a consequence.

The prose fix cannot reach the second half of this, because the Change and Exit fields fix the resolutions:
*"Caro lets her"*, *"The repair is real"*, *"she adds a question mark"*, *"Baptiste accepts the rota"*. **About a dozen
beats need architecture revision.** Most of them touch approved design only, and none of them has to touch a ruling.

## 2. Counts

## 3. Verdict for each book

**B02: a conflict engine at the civic scale, and a care engine at the personal scale.**
- The E22–E31 block, the Lucien ladder (E08 → E23 → E26 → E34 → E47) and the standing order (E47) make a genuine
  consequence machine. A decision is made under uncertainty. It has a credible alternative (*"A list would help. That is
  the credible alternative."*, E25), a named cost (the oxygen resident, the unfiled household, the Filaments' lost
  access), and a cost that compounds (the wider line).
- That is not "perfect people providing perfect care".
- **Around that spine the cast behaves as the diagnosis describes.** Every personal friction resolves in-scene. The one
  intra-cast rupture (E25) is kept off the page. The costs to protagonists are fatigue. Only Lucien has a shadow.

**B03: an event and institutional engine, with a care engine for the cast, and it is more care-shaped than B02 where it
matters.**
- "Advise and contain" (E09, E11, E27), Harlow (E33) and S04 give the Warehouse real institutional opposition.
- But the ensemble's last act before Baz dies is almost wholly warmth:
  - the complementary walk (E13);
  - the confession-and-repair (E17);
  - the better listening (E18);
  - the kiss (E24);
  - the second line (E26);
  - *"be careful. It is ordinary."* (E30).
- **No protagonist opposes another** in the whole book.
- Seraphine's climax (E35) is a care engine, though an honest one: a want against a want, and a cost she carries.
- **The risk for B04:** grief lands on relationships that were never tested, so the loss can only be sad. It cannot be
  complicated.

## 4. The strongest places to change the beats

**Rules for every proposal:** existing people and material only; no new characters, no world rules, no physics, no

### B02 (eight, strongest first)

**B02-1. E21: kindness that intrudes, and is not forgiven on the spot.**
- **Now:** *"Lucien has reorganized her notes to help."* *"it helps a little."*
- **Proposal:**
  - Her notes hold E09's confidences: who is fragile, who lives where, who is on no list.
  - In reorganising them, **Lucien has seen the names**, two weeks before Helena asks for exactly that list (E23).
  - She is angry: her card's quiet, dense anger at *"order prioritized over care"*.
  - He stops fixing and sits with it. The listening seed is still planted, but under an accusation, not as comfort.
- **Consequence:** at E25 he could half-make the list from memory, and chooses not to (his *"ethical delay"*). She does
  not know he chose that. This sharpens E25–E26 without making anyone a villain.
- **Principles:** kindness can intrude; repair gets its own cause and time.
- **Status:** **[APPROVED DESIGN]** (E21 listening seed; E09). Not ruled.

**B02-2. E25 → E26 → E31: the refusal as a fight both sides win and lose.**
- **Now:** a clean refusal (*"she accepts that cost"*). No scene until E31, where *"neither asks the other to undo it."*
- **Proposal:**
  - E25 plays as an argument. Lucien makes his whole case; he is right that a list protects the fragile. She refuses,
    and says what she thinks of Helena's "care".
  - It ends in **withdrawal, not understanding** (his card: *"withdrawal mistaken for care"*). At E26 he does not tell
    her what he is sending.
  - At E31 she learns the order was drawn on the grounds he sent. They have it out.
  - E31's *"the refusal remains between them"* becomes **active mutual withholding**: she stops bringing him to the
    households, and he stops telling her about Helena.
- **Consequence:** it causes E34's silence, which currently has no cause beyond habit. B03 E18 then has something to
  repair (see B03-2).
- **Principles:** wants collide with intelligible motives; conflict ends in consequence.
- **Status:** **[APPROVED DESIGN]** (direction 1; Q-B2-2's E31 line, which this deepens rather than contradicts; B03's
  guardrail *"necessity acknowledged, not resolved"* holds). Not ruled.

**B02-3. The oxygen resident was on her list: Caro against Seraphine.**
- **Now:** at E09, confidences name *"who is fragile"*. At E27 *"a resident on home oxygen is moved to a shelter
  without his concentrator."* At E30 Caro has just fixed it. **The two facts never meet.**
- **Proposal:**
  - Make him one of the E09 names. **Seraphine knew.**
  - In E31 (Seraphine's civic count, *"the others, elsewhere"*), Caro, who spent days fixing it, learns a list was asked
    for and refused.
  - Caro's claim (*"prioritize survivability over protocol"*) meets Seraphine's consent line (her LOCKED moral frame).
    Caro says the list would have been right. Seraphine does not concede; E44 already says *"does not conclude the list
    would have been right."*
- **Consequence:** B03 E22's *"worst list"* becomes Caro's own list of the vulnerable, **the thing Seraphine refused to
  make**, kept by her friend.
- **Principle:** legitimate goals, incompatible methods (the cards' Caro × Seraphine collision).
- **Status:** **[APPROVED DESIGN]** (E27, E30 and E31 pass-5 and Veil-audit design; M07 and E30's romance rung are
  untouched). Not ruled.

**B02-4. E22 → E26: Baz argues to wait.**
- **Now:** *"he wants the others to understand the odds exactly"*; at E26 *"Baz agrees because it is one block"*.
- **Proposal:**
  - At E22 Baz argues for one more cycle before telling anyone. The model has failed once (E14). This is his card's
    Veil risk, *"may delay necessary escalation"*.
  - Seraphine (it is Rosette's block) and Lucien want to tell. **He is outvoted.** At E26 his agreement is a concession,
    and he says so.
  - E29 proves the model usefully right, and him wrong to have waited.
- **Consequence:** in B03 E27 he presses Rieux for "imminent" harder, overcorrecting from B02. Keep it to E27. **Do not
  link it to E30 or E31**, where his own reason must take him in and no death foreshadowing is allowed.
- **Principles:** modes, not traits; errors from fear with a cost that carries forward.
- **Status:** **[APPROVED DESIGN]** (the civic decision, option A, is unchanged; E22 and E26 are approved). Not ruled.

**B02-5. E20 → E27–E30: what Caro keeps, she drops.**
- **Now:** *"When Leila offers to take the follow-up calls, Caro keeps them."* Nothing she keeps is ever dropped.
- **Proposal:**
  - In the order-and-cluster week, a call Caro kept is not made, and the dialysis rider misses a session.
  - **Leila finds out and says so.** It was her work too, and she offered.
  - Caro still hands nothing off.

## 7. In fairness to the architecture

- **B02's civic decision is exactly what the conflict engine asks for.** It is a choice on uncertain evidence, with a
  credible alternative, legitimate opposing claims (Helena, the residents, Rosette), and costs that stand and spread:
  - to the unfiled household;
  - to the oxygen resident;
  - to the Filaments' access;
  - to the standing order;
  - to the method in Dominion hands;
  - to the wider line.
- **Lucien is a fully Companions-shaped character** already: a mode, a stress response, the right thing known and not
  done (*"knows he should"*), and costs that stand.
- **B03 E11 is a model beat.** Rosette's survival, used to justify not clearing, is kindness turned into a policy
  argument, *"shown, never commented on."*
- **Institutions are not straw.** Baptiste loses on the record; advise-and-contain has real help and real danger;
  Technarc's dataset is *"genuinely good"*.
- **Protagonists do pay costs other than fatigue:**
  - Seraphine's refusals cost third parties she knows;
  - Lucien's favour costs him;
  - Baz dies doing his job.

**The defect is narrower than B01's diagnosis.** It is not that Veil lacks stakes or opposition. It is that **the cast
never stands against each other**, never errs from appetite, and never leaves a scene unrepaired. Fixing that needs
about a dozen beat edits, not a rebuild.
