## Test question

Can ordinary technology have meaningful causal and investigative functions **without** digitally transmitting someone's Resonance, reading Intent directly, or becoming a universal failure alarm? The three cases run from direct local evidence to distant communications to regional environmental warning. Each distinguishes a device's **ordinary physical action** from any still-unproven Resonance link.

### Shared terms for these cases

- **Sample:** recorded air pressure, light, motion, electrical behavior or similar ordinary material phenomenon. A recording of a sound is a sample **of sound**, not a sample of Resonance.
- **Inference:** a model's claim based on samples, witnesses, environmental conditions and baselines. Wrong readings are possible.
- **Conditioning:** a device changes an ordinary physical or social input (an audible cue, lighting, shelter layout, communications, evacuation), with consequences for human responses.
- **Direct interaction:** a proposed specialized interface collects, buffers, suppresses, redirects or injects raw Resonance load. **No general device with this capability is currently approved.**
- **Service state:** normal → less accurate/noisy or delayed → intermittent → unavailable → damaged; recovery is possible. These are illustrative states of individual equipment, **not** a universal ladder or automatic era schedule.

## Boundary cases that would change the rule

| Proposed action | Classification and reason |
| --- | --- |
| Recording an objectively audible low thrum, comparing raw files, and warning neighbors | **Permitted ordinary technology**; truth and human behavior, not metaphysical transmission |
| A Filament speaker plays a normal rhythm that helps people breathe, orient, or coordinate | **Permitted ordinary conditioning**; any subsequent human Resonance action still requires its own Intent/cost |
| A tower automatically senses “fear” or directly classifies a person's Intent | **Not supported**; fallible behavioral/physiological proxies would be a distinct, ethically charged system |
| An AR overlay changes color in response to a calibrated ordinary sensor | **Possible conventional interface**, but risk inference must be stated; the overlay itself is not a universal emotional HUD |
| A voice from Marrakesh sends her felt state into Seraphine via a radio | **Contradicts current no-digital-transmission boundary** unless a wholly separate independently evidenced local event explains Seraphine's impression |
| A device suppresses Resonance with a tone, or a meta harness transfers charge into an operative | **OPEN direct interaction** requiring special source, material coupling, limited output, input depletion, costs and a decision about human agency |
| A backup digital recorder survives while an analog communication link loses power or breaks | **Permitted**, as actual device exposure and supply matter more than the analog/digital label alone |

## Recommendation for the rule review

The three cases support adopting the **observation / inference / ordinary conditioning / graded reliability** portion of the [technology boundary proposal](../proposals/TECH_RESONANCE_INTERACTION_BOUNDARY_AND_SAGA_FUNCTION_REVIEW_2026-09-23.md) for author/editor review. Keep no-digital-Resonance transmission and no-machine-Intent unless explicitly revised. **Do not yet ratify** direct storage, suppression or transfer. “Technology is a casualty, not a carrier” may remain a reminder against magical broadcasting, but it is too imprecise to serve as the complete governing sentence.

To finish the B05 test: recover a specific later B05 episode or technical source establishing a storm site and its observations; otherwise draft a clearly labeled new regional event case, test physical substrate and costs, and ask for author/editor approval **after** the card is concrete. No automatic changes to M19, Book 1 order, EBCI, or the rule files follow from this pass.
