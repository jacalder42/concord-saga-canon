## Part III: classification

### Phenomenon, against the five constraint types

| Type | Imposed? | How broadly | Highest authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Cannot explain it yet** | **Yes, fully.** No mechanism, no stated cause, no *Resonance*, and the narrator never settles it | Book-wide and trilogy-wide | **A** (D1, D3, D5), **S** (Mech §6, §54, §60), **B** (Q-V3-2, profile §9) |
| **2. Cannot reliably predict or control** | **Control: yes, fully.** Care and grounding never stop or repair an event. **Prediction: yes, "not reliably".** Prediction is allowed and partly works (the E32 hit; E49's bet paid once; *competence persists*), but it stays a bet, with no exact second, no complete model and no mastery | Book-wide | **A** (D6: an *imperfect* window, no guaranteed recurrence); **B** (R7, overlays P24–P26) |
| **3. Rarely perceive anything definite** | **No, not as such.** The design *requires* definite public observables at E06, E07, E14, E18, E32, E46 and E49. | **4. Cannot leave usable evidence** | **No, not as a rule.** The A rulings *mandate* evidence (D2, D4, D6).
