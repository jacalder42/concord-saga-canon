## D. Resulting B8 gap, without inventing an event

The author's diagnosis survives scrutiny: *the Mending plot can expand across Loom because the selected milestones do not yet capture enough distinct B7/B8 changes*. The ledger does not conclude that the missing changes never existed, or that each book needs equal event counts. The next recovery must locate the specific **place + people + action + consequence** for one or more B8 candidates before the team rewrites B7–B9 book purpose or shifts M31.
