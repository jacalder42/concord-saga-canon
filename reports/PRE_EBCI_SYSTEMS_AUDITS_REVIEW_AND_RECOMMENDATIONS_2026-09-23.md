## Overall judgment

The audits correctly identify the largest risks: **equation conflation, unaccounted meta load/transfer, old corridor taxonomy, color-as-emotion display, automatic emotional repairs, and conflict explained entirely by Resonance**. Their separation of recovered facts from proposals is useful. I recommend keeping the reports as evidence, then issuing a small **decision addendum** rather than replacing Mechanica v4 wholesale.

Three corrections to the audits' emphasis:

1. **Do not overcorrect into mundane-only physics.** Resonance is an active world condition; shards, weather, systemic failures and the Mending are real within the story. A lamp flicker can be an ordinary indicator, but the system cannot be reduced to lamp flickers plus improved human timing. Define *bounded external coupling and unstable field events* alongside human boosts. An effect may be objectively anomalous while remaining causal, limited and costly. The B01 bible explicitly keeps the first Square pulse a shared civic event and E33/E48 as imperfectly predictable events.
2. **Do not demand a full engineering equation or universal empirical units before B01.** The four historical formulae do different jobs. A conceptual model that distinguishes potential, available load, controlled action, environmental coupling and uncontrolled discharge is enough to police scenes. A false numerical precision would be worse than a clearly labeled qualitative relationship.
3. **Narrow the B01 hold.** The current readiness report lists four broad ruling groups. Conflict code redesign, meta transfer, post-Mending optics and the full U1–U7 reconstruction do not block B01 if packets omit those claims. The actual B01 gate is narrower: what the major local events objectively do, how observers can differ, what regulation changes, what costs/residues remain, and how FX is interpreted. The user's instruction still forbids starting EBCI before review.

## The equation I would put before the editors

**Keep the canonical shorthand `RP = Will × Emotion × Intent`, but explicitly call it *potential for directed action*, not the total energy of a scene.** Earlier [Notion unified mechanics](https://app.notion.com/p/2b6e7d902ad8802ca15de56ba3bd6b61) gives `RO = Energy × Emotion × Intent × environmental pressure modifiers`, while [Mechanica](../rules/Mechanica-v4.md) supplies an Energy on-page proxy. The [tech recovery](../recovery/TECH_GEAR_MECHANICA_RECOVERY_PASS1_2026-09-22.md) argues that Energy is a current-state proxy for available Will but cautions against collapsing them; the [character matrix](../recovery/CHARACTER_SYSTEMS_RECOVERY_MATRIX_PASS1_2026-09-22.md) also preserves a separate native-skill modifier.

**Recommended conceptual layers, not a new formula:**

- **Potential:** Will/capacity, emotional amplitude *and coherence*, Intent as a concrete chosen action and accepted consequence. Intent is neither strong wanting nor moral purity.
- **Available load:** biological/psychological reserves plus any separately established environmental or stored load. This is the missing accounting layer for meta batteries and crowd feeding. Do not assume emotions create transferable energy from nothing.
- **Coupling and ceiling:** native skill, physical means, proximity/material/environment, Veil state, fatigue and competing patterns bound the realized action.
- **Uncontrolled event:** a charged system can leak or fail without an actor's usable Intent; this is not a zero-Intent person deliberately wielding a power.
- **Cost and aftermath:** distinguish action cost, ambient field damage and human/social consequences. Small ambient tells need not incur a melodramatic personal penalty each time, while a deliberate boost or stabilization attempt must pay.

This addresses “super feelings” without making emotion irrelevant. Emotion affects pressure and coherence, not an automatically selected effect. The historical line “Emotion is energy” should be annotated as compressed or disputed, rather than deleted as if the energy economy had already been solved.

## A decisive B01 micro-audit before packet drafting

Test **three distinct sequences** against the conceptual layers:

1. **E15 Square pulse** — multiple witnesses; real shared event; competing testimony; what changes in the location/technology/people, what survives cleanup, and what nobody can yet infer.
2. **E29 restraint → E33 predicted pulse → E34 bodily recovery** — causal forecasting with error bars; a chosen retreat; preparation helps individuals while scale/expression defeat control. Ensure the episode does not present emotional alignment as a citywide stabilizer.
3. **E43 grounding failure → E45 rebound → E48 quiet pulse** — care remains valuable; Lucien's impossible-looking geometry remains a POV/perceptual problem; final quiet event pays patterned prediction with incomplete cause and local residue.

Use those to settle a compact B01 event grammar. If they pass, the other B01 beats can use the same grammar without needing the meta interface or Loom ontology. This remains a **review exercise**, not EBCI expansion.

## Recommendations on workflow and report status

- Keep all existing forensic reports immutable as dated findings. Attach this review as a later critique; do not silently “correct” the older report text or promote proposals into rules.
- Record decisions as **CURRENT / RECOVERED / SUPERSEDED / PROPOSAL / OPEN** and include the source, scope (B01 vs saga), and whether a decision changes a rule file.
- Fix the integrated bible's two heading-order anomalies only after confirming the intended reading sequence (E37 before E36, E44 before E43 in file order). This is a manifest risk for tooling, not proof of a story flaw.
- After a compact author/editor ruling on B01 physics and VFX, draft a short **Mechanica interpretation addendum** rather than Mechanica v5. Keep the meta, character combat and gear work as separate modules. No EBCI expansion until the user lifts that explicit hold.

**Recommended next editorial decision:** approve or amend the conceptual distinction among directed potential, available load, environmental coupling and uncontrolled discharge; approve a B01-level event/evidence and regulation grammar; explicitly hold the operative-transfer interface for targeted pre-Neon recovery or design.
