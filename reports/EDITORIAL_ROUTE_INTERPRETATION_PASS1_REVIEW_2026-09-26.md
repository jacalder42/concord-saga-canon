- **Elias is not routed.** The brief listed nine characters, so this is not a breach. But Elias's
  arrival (#22) and his B08–B09 movements bear on Kade's route in §9.
- **Page budget is not tested against theaters.** B04–B06 carry five or six live theaters: NOLA,
  Vienna, Chicago, Reykjavík, Singapore/Atlanta and Tahl's route. Under the soft POV targets
  (`decisions/POV_DISTRIBUTION_TARGETS_…`), Tahl holds 30 and Seraphine 30, mostly in NOLA. That
  leaves Lucien, Caro, Elisabet, Rex and Kade sharing 30 across four distant theaters. **READING:**
  several of those theaters will have to be carried by secondary cast or information (§12's RR
  column), not by principal POV. The pass's own principle, a tie earns activation *"when it gives a
  character an independent life"*, makes that trade-off real. Episode mapping should test it.

## 4. What is strong (READING)

- **B06 → B07.** The pass keeps the NOLA group in place, has Rex and Elisabet travel separately from
  their own theaters, uses staggered arrivals, and allows a funeral interval of several days. That
  solves the worst travel problem without inventing mechanics. Only Tahl's body comes from Santa Fe.
- **Lacuna owns the B09 feint.** The feint gets a carrier who belongs to the city, while Seraphine is
  ruled absent.
- **Tahl as a pattern, not stops:** Chicago gravity → distributed reporting → the Southwest → Santa Fe.
  This honours *"itinerant"* without locking an itinerary before episode mapping.
- **Caro and Elisabet apart in B05.** This matches M56's own wording (*"while each chooses separate
  necessary work … commitment does not require constant co-location"*). It corrects the flattening
  that paired them in every Neon bible.
- **"Preserve functions, not coordinates"** (§14) is a usable salvage rule for the flattened sources.
