# B01 redraft: the focused Act I revision and the forward-pull read

**Date:** 2026-09-29
**Status:** EDITORIAL REPORT, non-canonical. This records the revision pass the author approved under Q-AC3
(`decisions/REDRAFT_ACT_I_CHECKPOINT_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-29.md`, ledger §299), and the continuous read of E09–E15
that followed.

**What it does not change:** no ruling, card, packet or grid. **It quotes no manuscript text.** The drafts are in the
private manuscript repository, `B01/redraft/act-01/`, and the one reading sequence is in `B01/redraft/README.md`.

**Findings are bounded.** They record what one reader and one checker found, and are not an assurance that nothing else
is wrong.

## 1. What the revision did, by function rather than wording

| Item | Done |
| --- | --- |
| **E09** | **Cut to its turn**, the six o'clock call in which the boy's mother asks what Seraphine told the inspector and Seraphine offers to answer anything. The funeral, the boss, the unknown numbers and the night walks are reduced to a paragraph each. **The piece is about 1,550 words, down from about 2,900** |
| **The folder and the mother, kept alive by practical consequence** | **A new thread.** The boss asks her to close the family's case file before the county's month-end count. The closure form will not accept a blank date for the last action on the renewal, so she leaves it unsaved. It is still unsaved in E12, promised for Monday, and it carries into Act II |
| **The older characters** | Each now has **her own interest and incomplete knowledge**, not a verdict:<br>- the paramedic wants Seraphine's times for a report that will be blamed for the road;<br>- the card reader wants her table's spot respected and suspects the engineer is from the city or the insurance;<br>- the landlady wants her sleep, guesses the trouble is a man (wrong), and no longer speaks Seraphine's realization for her |
| **E11** | Cut to its pleasure (**about 1,040 words**): the laundromat rules, the child's correction, the ruined sweater, the laugh and the gift |
| **E13** | Ends on what Lucien has not told her, carrying the tension forward instead of resolving it into sleep |
| **E14** | **The replay is cut** (the recollected fall, the recited list, the recap of what he had hidden). Kept, because it changes behavior: the recordings, the officer, *yes*, and **Seraphine's decision to log when she feels it and when she does not**. The scale (*hundreds*) is restored. It ends on her line |
| **E15, Baz** | **What he contributes that they cannot, from his canon card:** consented testimony from strangers who owe him nothing, triangulated across dozens of accounts until what they agree on can stand on a record. Seraphine's accounts are confidences she cannot put on a record. Lucien cannot get strangers to talk. They did such a study together once, before the book |
| **E15, Seraphine** | **She keeps a distinct line, and keeps it on the page:** a log of her own perception (early, late or not at all; where; who was there). She writes in it at each corner of the route walk and does not show him. It advances alongside Baz's work and Lucien's counting |
| **E15, the two records** | **Causality checked.** The coroner's file concerns the parish room; the police notebook concerns the Square. Read side by side, they concern two places and do not contradict each other. **The threat is scrutiny:** a man who went down in front of the city may one day be asked whether it had happened before, and the one witness who saw him go down in that room has asked for his statement |
| **E15, endings** | One close (the graph paper), where there had been three |
| **Continuity** | The case-file form is consistent across E09 and E12. The clinic account is squared with her confidentiality (she passed it on without a name). The route no longer starts at a landmark |

**Act I now runs 38,739 words** (the allowance was about 46,000).

## 2. The forward-pull read (E09–E15)

A fresh reader was asked one question: **does the reader want the next development?**

| Chapter | Pull | Kind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E09 | 4 / 5 | A want: will the mother call, and what will the engineer's statement show |
| E10 | 4 / 5 | A want: his second false signature, with her request beside it |
| E11 | 1–2 / 5 | Mild interest: charming, but it lets go straight after the signing |
| E12 | 4 / 5 | A want: the matched minute, and her choice not to ask about the form |
| E13 | 2–3 / 5 | Mostly mild: the pull is the relationship and the promised walk |
| E14 | 5 / 5 | A strong want: the recording, the officer, his *yes* |
| E15 | 4 / 5 | A want: Baz's arrival, the loaded gun of his signature, her first log entry |

**At the end of E15 the reader would start the next chapter straight away.** At the Act I checkpoint the reader had said
*probably not*.

**Still weak:**
- **E11, and the middle of E13, back to back.** Two chapters of Lucien charming a child and an old woman, where the
  stakes should be tightening.
- **Seraphine's agency.** The reader judged her line real and unique but inward. The outward inquiry passes to Baz in
  Lucien's point of view, and her own threads go quiet after E12.

**What was done after the read:**
- Her log is now kept on the page.
- E13's ending carries tension forward.
- Recaps and repeated shapes are trimmed.
- The case file stays open into Act II.

**Carried to Act II as obligations:**
1. Seraphine initiates a consequential line of inquiry and keeps ownership of it (author, Q-AC3).
2. **The case file and the mother stay live by action.**
3. **E11's placement** (see §3).

## 3. Note, not a question

E11 is packet-fixed as a Life piece, and trimming it did not lift its pull. If a later read still finds E10–E13 slack, the
fold principle (a fixed beat names an obligation, not a protected scene) allows its delivery to be reconsidered. This is
recorded for the Act I line edit; it is not reopened now.
