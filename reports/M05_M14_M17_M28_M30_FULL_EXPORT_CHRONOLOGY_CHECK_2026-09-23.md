## Specific sequence controls

- **Neon meta exposure versus failure:** a B4 visible prototype and B5 public trial are *November candidates*; the December author-pasted macro specifies B6 **failures begin**, not that the first meta is seen then. Conversely, the December assistant self-audit's “B6 only” is not sufficient evidence to keep B4/B5 scenes. The word **first** in M17 must be scoped (made, fielded, seen, disclosed, malfunctioned) before timing can be adjudicated.
- **Technarc scope:** “response collapses” in B5 is an operational service claim. It does not imply no people, labs, rigs, covert assets or later failures remain. No global simultaneous tech death follows.
- **Route contradiction:** the December author-pasted B7–B9 sequence itself places a swamp approach/chamber reveal before a NOLA retreat. A reader cannot be assumed to know whether this is one group or multiple convoys. **The route ledger is the next gating artifact for M28/M31**, but no locked narrative order should be moved until James/Claude determines whose movements and which opening image survive.
- **M05 refusal:** the Dec 7 B2 “refuses again” is evidence a B2-stage break was developed; it does not prove which authority he relinquished or whether B3/B4 refusals are a progressive escalation. Separate ethics, employment/status, family succession and physical departure.
