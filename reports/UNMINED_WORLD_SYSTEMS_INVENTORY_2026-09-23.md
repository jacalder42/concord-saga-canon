## Verdict

The apparent gap is **operational everyday life**, not a lack of broad saga imagery. Locations, era aesthetics, character silhouettes, metaphysics and escalation have substantial historical development. The repository has first technology/gear and location recovery passes; the full conversation archive also contains a detailed late-November power/charging model and an author-engaged fashion framework. Ordinary wages, housing, health care, resource distribution and operational B01 wardrobe still lack comparable reconciliation. In several areas the older Notion corpus supplies abundant material but contains later-superseded physics and character details. "Unmined" therefore has two meanings: **source material still needs extraction** and **the recovered fragments have not become a usable, governed system**.

## Recommended mining sequence

1. **B01 material baseline packet:** Extract only the city/character systems that recur in B01: livelihoods and means, ordinary devices and media, wardrobe/carry, care/ER, local transit/services, Velvet Vein food/hospitality. Give each a source, precedence, a concrete scene consequence, and an OPEN marker when absent. This is preparatory recovery, not EBCI expansion.
2. **Tech/communications pass 2:** Include the now recovered Nov. 30 MT equipment and power/charging source in the Nov. 23 → Nov. 24 → Nov. 29 → Nov. 30 → Dec. 1/6 → current comparison. Resolve AR prevalence, B01 failure rarity, MT hardware/routing, diagnostics and ordinary power supply. Reject the imprinted VT transmitter absent later approval.
3. **Economy/fashion full-source pass:** Extract the Nov. 30 author-approved fashion dialogue and power economy, then reconcile with Dec. 1–15 art/style and current mechanics. Log the recovered Nov. 1–10 Tone Markets, resonance credits and synthreads as historically real but likely superseded, with exact approval and replacement evidence. Propose mundane economy minimums only where sources leave OPEN gaps.
4. **Civic and resource pass:** Follow B01 character routes and services before modeling global catastrophe. Build distinct Neon/Loom degradation overlays only after Veil ordinary operations are credible.
5. **Author/editor decisions:** Keep a short decision register: (a) era and class distribution of AR/holochat; (b) minimum B01 socioeconomic baseline and relevant jobs/homes; (c) which institutions have local authority; (d) whether any historic economy/fabric concepts survive, with provenance. Neither a Notion "canon" label nor an old assistant memory settles these.

For every operational system, use the same test as physics: **who provides it, who can access it, what it costs, what breaks, who repairs it, and what visible consequence follows**. This keeps ordinary material conflicts independent of Resonance and allows later anomalies to have specific consequences.
