## 1. How it was built

1. **Packets** (`PACKETS.md`): global rules, a 51-unit build table with a target per unit, one packet per revised or new
   unit, and the hand-offs between groups. The rules include the lifted guards, the guards that stay, the renames, the
   26-score result rule, the resonance arc, the act shape, Caldas's ladder, the Vernice realism constraints, Marius,
   Elisabet and the Vein, and the repetition map. Twelve units were copied over, 35 revised and 4 written new: the
   Interlude, Ch 37, Ch 42 and S4.
2. **Six revisers in parallel**, G1–G6. As each group finished, its continuity facts were relayed to the groups still
   writing: the verbatim lie, the score lines, the sheet's exact wording, who holds the sheet and the folder, and the
   Marius details.
3. **Four read-only checkers**, each covering the whole book:
   - continuity;
   - the causal chains and all 26 scores;
   - the guards and realism;
   - mechanical checks and length, with a trim list.
4. **One fix round.** Two fixers applied every definite and probable finding, the listed style fixes and about 3,000
   words of texture-only trims.
5. **A short blind read** (Q-RV14): a casual reader, a mystery reader and a genre critic, each reading the whole book cold.
   Two bridging lines lost to the trims (Ch 10, Ch 13) were then restored.
