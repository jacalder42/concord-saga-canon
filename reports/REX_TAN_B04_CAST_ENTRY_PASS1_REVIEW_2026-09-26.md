## 3. Tensions to weigh (READING)

1. **Rex has a Veil phase on his canon cards.**
   - RexID §X, Veil: *"systems expertise grounds early instability; limits visible before resonance
     language exists"*.
   - RexEBCI 136: *"VEIL: responsibility rewarded; systems mostly intact"*.

   The proposal's first on-page appearance is B04 A1. That is compatible only if Rex's Veil phase is
   off-page Detroit life. The author was *"unsure of fit in B2"* for the Detroit scene (Route Q8), and
   a recovered recap says *"Rex is NOT introduced in Veil"* (NS 208817, A-weak). The Veil anchor stands
   on the card either way. **The choice between off-page Veil and an on-page Veil Detroit beat is the
   author's.**
2. **The author's own Rex–Seraphine shape is fissure-timed.** His idea: *"Coming during a time after
   Seraphine and Lucien fissure … Rex answers the call and protects her"* (PC 44156). M05 (ruled) sends
   Lucien to Vienna in **B04 A2**, and the proposal puts the decisive Seraphine engagement in **early
   A2**. The proposal does not order the two. Placing the engagement **after** Lucien leaves would
   realise the author's shape directly. Placing it before would make it independent of the fissure.
   The proposal handles the unrequited undertone (*"never serious, it's unrequited"*, PC 146112)
   correctly, as optional.
3. **Elisabet at the Riot.** The proposal has Rex check routes *"with Caro, Elisabet and local
   responders"*, and gives Elisabet her civic-protection beat. That matches the approved MDR and NB
   Riot material. But ChatGPT's route interpretation (DESIGN, **not** among the approved answers) sends
   Elisabet to Reykjavík *"late"* in B04. The two proposals disagree. Neither is approved on this point.
4. **The registry's arc wording.** `cast_registry.csv` D02 (bundle D approved) reads *"loyal engineer →
   model failure → moral fracture → humane builder"*. The proposal forbids *"quietly reinstat[ing] the
   loyal employee arc"*. The two agree if the loyal-engineer → model-failure → fracture steps are
   pre-saga, as RexID's backstory has them. The registry does not say so. **READING, not a
   conflict.**
5. **Rex's Technarc relationship** (contractor, former colleague, or independent witness) is correctly
   left open. It is the question the validation flagged for the author, and it also bears on how M17
   is reached in Singapore by B06.
