## 3. Findings

**Draft 3 already passes most of the studies' tests:**
- speakers have different jobs;
- leverage moves inside conversations;
- secondary characters return with their own agendas;
- personal stakes choose the questions;
- humour is specific to the people, and the four principals are funny differently;
- disagreements outlast a change of tone;
- scenes mostly sit where the outcome is uncertain;
- chapter types vary.

**The studies' central worry is not this book's.** They worry about calculating talk and missing conflict. **Draft 3's
risks are repetition and over-articulacy:**
- characters explain their own motives in finished paragraphs;
- three speakers diagnose the protagonist in much the same words;
- house gestures recur.

**What the studies found that is real:**

| Area | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| **Action** | The stakeout's plan is not on the page before the night, so its failure is explained after the fact. The dawn watch is summarized twice rather than shown once. The finale is the hardest major event to picture |
| **Romance** | The slow burn is two-sided now, but Seraphine's outward tenderness toward Lucien is mostly the same as toward everyone. **Selective attention** is the studies' one new romance lesson. Her single bodily response in Act II goes to another character |
| **Humour** | One comic cluster sits around the book's main triage injury, against profile §6 |
| **Ensemble** | One return (Dolores) carries nothing back. Seraphine and Baz never get a quiet exchange on the page, though the ending draws on it. The boy whose death drives the file has no portrait from his family |
| **Approved design lost** | **Q-RM1 beat 6 (E41) was cut by the integrated revision's recap trim (T3-02) without being flagged.** It is listed as a restoration |

**Where the studies would harm the book if applied wholesale:**
- a learnable rule or a rings-first rule (A4 is held);
- faces for the watchers;
- banter in a crisis;
- more refusals with counteroffers;
- moral speeches;
- neighbours turned into informants;
- danger as attraction;
- stripping the everyday texture that is the stake.

## 4. Guidance

The studies support five short additions to the writer profile that it does not yet carry:
- selective romantic attention;
- the decision space in action;
- scene or summary, and several chapter shapes;
- humour belonging to a relationship;
- uneven articulacy.

**None of them sets a rate or a quota.**
