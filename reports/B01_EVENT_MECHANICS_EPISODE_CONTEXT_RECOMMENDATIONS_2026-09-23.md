## Executive recommendation

**Use a local ambient/collective pressure process as the leading *private design hypothesis* for the civic events; do not assign their onset to Seraphine's or Lucien's momentary feelings or deliberate Intent.** This fits E07–08 repeated sites under different circumstances, E15 lack of control, E25 incomplete regularity, E33 partial forecast with unforeseen expression, and E47's conclusion that no one site, crowd or institution alone is causal. It is not yet a canon mechanism. The physical source, accumulation/depletion, coupling and limits remain open. An ordinary equipment fault or witness mistake can coexist with the process.

Keep **directed Resonance action** separate: a character may attempt a bounded intervention, if author-approved, with immediate and lingering UARS cost. Helping, comforting, waiting, taking measurements and listening are ordinary human actions unless a specific influenced action is established. A civic event needs material consequences and an intelligible private budget if attributed to Resonance, but should not impose a magic price on everyone who has an emotion near it. The RP equation describes potential for directed action; it cannot, unaided, explain a self-starting public event.

### E15 in context — recommended staging contract

1. **On-page first:** a working Square and a real interruption with consequences for a performer, vendors, witnesses and responders. Sound and crowd reactions must be situated in actual activity; pigeon flight may be ordinary startle.
2. **Second trace:** a low audible thrum may precede the amp pop; the full-export beat then has a heartbeat of quiet, with ambient sound and possibly a similar hum returning later. Do not make the hum continuous through the cutout. Two recordings are a *later-investigation support*, not the shock beat's centerpiece. They document one imperfectly shared acoustic observation, not a second independent physical anomaly or a signature of Resonance.
3. **Causality:** privately test ordinary amplifier/feedback and another ordinary sound source before declaring amp and thrum linked. They can have **different causes**. E15 succeeds as a public event with disputed cause under the author ruling; no character gains new power from intense feelings.
4. **E16/E17 transition:** cleanup and inconsistent reports expand the burden beyond one Square incident. Lucien calls Baz because normal escalation and his own model are insufficient, not because Baz has a perfectly matched waveform.

### E33 in context — recommended staging contract

1. E27 must say in advance what timing/location observation would count as a miss. E33 may satisfy that limited prediction yet surprise in **expression, footprint, people affected or response**.
2. Window shudder is a tactile candidate, not a demand for objectively impossible geometry. Establish an ordinary building/site and possible wind, traffic, equipment or acoustic excitation. If the pane effect cannot be privately explained at B01 scale, downgrade/reject it rather than granting glass a free magical force.
3. **“Technology degrades” is a graded consequence.** Momentary lag, signal noise, loss of precision or another bounded quality change with devices still functioning would fit; the exact manifestation is not yet chosen. No separate lamp failure is needed. A minor symptom can matter because it delays coordination or muddies an observation, not because everything shuts off.
4. E34–35 are the event's human cost on the page: bodies first, community disagreement and practical aid second, interpretation later. An actual deliberate boost, if any, needs separate cost and lingering consequence; exhaustion alone should not be retrofitted as proof of one.

### E48 in context — revise the earlier leading-candidate ranking

The [E48 light card](../proposals/B01_E48_QUIET_EVENT_OBSERVABLE_CANDIDATE_FOR_REVIEW_2026-09-23.md) called old E41's apartment-light flicker the *leading* candidate because it was the sole recovered positive material prop for that historical event. **Episode context makes it a conditional backup.** E15 already tests an amp, E33 calls for scaled technology behavior, E38 foregrounds ambiguous hum, and E46 protects quiet. Repeating a visibly electrical symptom as E48's main image risks “one more glitch proves the model.” No other specific positive B01 E48 artifact is recovered; recommending against that repetition does **not** magically supply another effect.

**Preferred construction:** in E47, commit to a local observation window and site from plural imperfect data. In E48, depict one *quiet, ordinary-material positive change* that can be independently noted, while foregrounding changed human practice: they wait, compare without overclaiming, Lucien leaves an imperfect measurement imperfect, Baz argues for widening inquiry, and Seraphine chooses presence without imagined control. The precise physical change is **OPEN** and must be selected from approved site/material conditions; do not invent stone/water memory, an emotion color or a new channel to fill the slot. If old apartment lights prove the best grounded effect after site review, use **one bounded brightness variation without loss of service**, corroborated only to the level ordinary witnesses or records allow; keep it subordinate to the team's decision and let a mundane electrical explanation remain.

**Do not rely on a negative-only clue** (no hum, no damage, everybody feels calm) to certify that the predicted event happened. The old lights are therefore a better *fallback* than an unmeasured absence. Conversely, if the available data cannot discriminate the apparent event from routine background fluctuation, the model is not entitled to its payoff; a narrowly scoped beat amendment is preferable to a false proof.

## Minimum private decisions, ranked by effect on the book

1. **Ambient event vs actor action:** provisional ambient/local hypothesis is best narrative fit for E15/E33/E48. Author/editor must adjudicate whether an ambient process exists independently of directed Intent; this does not derive automatically from `Will × Emotion × Intent`.
2. **One physically worked case:** determine E15 sound/amp causal partition and source→medium→limit→cost/trace. If ordinary, say so. Avoid building a universal sound/glass/light law first.
3. **E33 expression:** choose a credible pane site and, separately, one modest device-quality behavior if retained; neither has to fail catastrophically.
4. **E48 positive trace:** review site and forecast data; select a quiet checkable effect. Use recovered lights only if they do not crowd the narrative with technology symptoms; otherwise explicitly develop and label a *new* physical candidate for review.
5. **Cost ledger:** separate ordinary harm, cost of any directed influenced action, and ambient process budget/residue. Without a defined physical budget, no event may be called a fully reconciled Resonance effect.

**Disposition:** author-approved story/observation direction stands. Current E48 light card remains a historical-source candidate with lower editorial priority.
