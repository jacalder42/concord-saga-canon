## 0. Bottom line

1. **No antagonist faction card gives a book or act for anything.** The five cards (Dominions,
   Technarc, Choirless, Brightbreak, Manufactured Metas) speak only in eras (Veil, Neon, Loom). Only
   **Elias** has book-level placement, from the 09-26 and 09-27 rulings. Everyone else is placed only
   where a grid row or a Warehouse working assumption touches them.
2. **Every institutional and ideological antagonist falls back to a legacy role in Loom:** Virelli,
   Han Wei, Saeko and Ito. Their cards make them *"ideological debris"*, *"legacy"* or *"doctrine
   without her"* after Neon.
   - **The only named active antagonist in B07–B09 is Elias**, with Brightbreak and Kade's splinter.
   - B09's "factions" (M50, M51) and the Choirless (*"unresolved into the cutaway"*) are **unnamed
     collectives**.
   - That fits the cards, but it leaves Loom's opposition thin. It bears on the review's C08
     (*"too wrong to be persuasive"*) and on B09 A2's *"pursuit with sparks"*. **Who pursues** is open
     (M48: *"who is escaped from"*).
3. **Every personal exit is OPEN.**
   - The cards set *end-state styles*: Virelli *"outgrown"*; Han Wei *"model failure"*; Ito
     *"exhaustion"*; Saeko *"resistance"*; Elias *"survives … may reinvent"*.
   - None is placed in a book, and none is ruled.
   - Four deaths are explicitly **NOT LOCKED**: Virelli's off-page B9 death, Caldas Ren's storm death,
     Helena Kael's dying message, and Harlow's disappearance.
4. **The Neon Rebellion has no faction card.** It is defined only by recovery material and three
   rulings. Its lineage into Brightbreak is ruled; its splinters' arc between B06 and B08 is `?`.
5. **Housekeeping defects** (§6) include:
   - `Choirless_Subtypes.md` is truncated. The missing ending is in the source export.
   - **Elias has no cast registry row.**
   - Elias's Appearance and Render cards still carry the pre-ruling *"Veil / Early Neon"* labels.
   - There are broken cross-references.

## 8. Recommended next step

This record shows that the antagonists have **function and doctrine** (well audited in 09-19/20) but
**no placement**. The natural next step mirrors what was done for the protagonists: an **editorial
antagonist arc pass**. It would propose, for each antagonist and faction:

- entrance (first pressure, first named on-page presence, first public act);
- book-by-book pressure;
- per-book turning points;
- exit and end state.

It would work against the fixed points in §3–§4, the site secret, the review's direction 4 (*"earned
opposition"*) and C08, and the cards' guardrails (institutions strongest *"off-screen"*; spectacle
only from splinters and extremists). It would put options to the author, not decisions.

**Before that, three questions shape it most:**

1. **Loom's opposition.** Is it right that the institutions and doctrinaires become legacy, leaving
   Elias, Brightbreak, Kade's splinter and a fragmented Choirless as Loom's active opposition? Or
   should one institutional face stay active into B09, for example a Technarc hardliner splinter, as
   the Technarc card itself suggests for spectacle?
2. **Ito's Loom presence:** legacy, as the cards say, or active to B09, as the registry says (§5 #1)?
3. **Choirless and Brightbreak in B09:** do some align, never unify, or simply stay separate, as
   the ruled finale implies (§5 #6)?

**And one approval for the housekeeping in §6:** the Subtypes completion, the Elias registry row, the
Elias relabels and G9 alignment, and the cross-reference fixes.
