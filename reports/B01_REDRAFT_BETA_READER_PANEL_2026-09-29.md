# B01 redraft: the first beta-reader panel

**Date:** 2026-09-29
**Status:** EDITORIAL REPORT, non-canonical. It records a panel of ten simulated beta readers that the author asked for
while he completes his own read: *"fans of each author on our editorial board, as well as archetypal readers for each of
our target audience groups"*, reading for narrative and story, not grammar, and keeping chapter, act and final notes.

**What it does not change:** no ruling, card, brief, packet, grid or manuscript text. **Nothing is edited while the
author reads.** It quotes no manuscript text. The readers' own notes (about 129,000 words in all) are in the private
manuscript repository, `draft-notes/beta-read/`, with a fuller synthesis beside them.

**What it is not:**
- **Not the author's read**, which remains the primary one.
- **Not a market test.** These are model readers given personas, and their agreement shows convergence among simulated
  tastes, not demand.
- **Not the editorial lenses.** The board-author readers are *fans of those authors' books*. Nothing here authors the
  deferred `Asks` / `Flags` / `Protects` fields in `canon/editorial_lenses.md`.

**Every reader read the whole book, in order and blind:** the manuscript only, with no design notes and no canon. Each
wrote a note after every chapter (pull 1–5, reactions, predictions), act notes, and final thoughts that include the

Several asks run into rulings or approved design. **They are recorded, not adopted.** The author decides after his read.

## 5. For the author, after his read

**No questions are put now.** When the author has finished, these are the panel's candidates to set beside his own
notes, strongest convergence first:
1. **C6 and C7 together:** one private Seraphine–Lucien scene in Act III, with the *None* told to her face. Four of the
   ten readers chose it as their one change, and it touches no ruling.
2. **C3:** the placement of the rest chapters after peaks (E11, E33, E43 especially), rather than their existence.
3. **C2:** whether, and how, the prologue's frame returns inside B01 or is held for later books.
4. **C4:** a narrow pass on the repeated shapes that survived Q-A2C4 (the elder-feeds-heroine and refusal shapes, the
   gray case *after*, the *fine* refrain).
5. **C10:** the Baz death flag. The guard work should look at the E35 stay and the room card together.
6. **C11:** plant E47's meeting-place line in E21's seven.
7. **C5 and C9:** whether B01 needs one more rung toward the watchers, and one positive rule the crew uses.
