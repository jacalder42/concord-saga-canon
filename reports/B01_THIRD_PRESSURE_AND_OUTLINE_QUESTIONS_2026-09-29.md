## 2. What it proposes

**Three pressures, ordered so care stops leading.** A drafter reads down, and what comes first leads:

| Line | Pressure | Its job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **Wants** | their own want | the engine: what they go after, and what they'll pay |
| 2. **Pressed by** | the external source in this scene: what it demands and threatens, **its legitimate claim**, and its drift at this point | the weather that has a will; it sets the terms and prices |
| 3. **Protects** | care, **phrased as a reflex with a price** | how they react when someone is hurt, and what it costs |
| 4. **Misreads** | unchanged | — |
| 5. **Under pressure** | the shadow response **and one constructive response** | Q-AC3 |

- **Rename, don't delete.** Seraphine's *"not to lose anyone else"* moves from *Wants* to *Protects*.
- **Two in three.** At least two of every three pieces take their objective from the want or the external pressure.
- **Care collides or it waits.** Care that collides with nothing is life texture.
- **Line 2 changes by act,** because the drift is the point. **It never names a faction or a later event.**

**The drift across B01:**
- **Act I:** ordinary friction: a licence, the coroner, a contract, a supervisor's discipline.
- **Act II:** a will that takes and never asks, from the reserved floor.
- **Act III:** the same signature (*only the time*) shows up in unrelated institutions, which **suggests organisation
  without naming anyone.**

It never asks, so B02 E08 and E13 stay unspent. **Every source keeps a legitimate claim, and some of its demands are
right.**

**The lines cover nine characters:** Seraphine, Lucien, Baz, Caro, Elisabet, Trip and Mara (conditional on her lines),
plus Renée and Sal as pressure sources. Each has its three-act drift, its collisions with care and want, and its
continuation into B02–B03. There is also a column for all fifty pieces.

**Seraphine's care-led pieces:** 11 of her 23 lead with care now. Re-leading four (P07, P21, P29, P37) with her want
or the external pressure brings it to 7, **with no event moved.**

**Lucien.** *"Beginning to question … as he spends more time with Baz"* **can be met in full, in behaviour,** in
eleven pieces after Baz arrives. **"Question the Dominion" cannot be met in B01 as such,** because the Dominion is
unnamed there. What he can question is "upstairs" and his own office's routing. B02 E08, where Helena's letter shows
the camp report was read, then turns that into *the Dominion* for the reader, retroactively.
- **(a) Behaviour only,** recommended. No guard changes, and nothing is spent.
- **(b) One fact and one question to Baz at P48, with no judgment** (*who asks for a copy before a file is closed?*).
  It changes obligation #52 and spends a sliver of B02 E08's recognition. It is the cheapest loosening available.

**Seraphine's second want (Q-AC4):**
- **A, recommended: her own account on the record,** to be believed as the one who was there, in her own name. It is
  a competing commitment, with pride as its ugly side. It is already acted in four spine pieces. It collides with
  Renée, with Lucien's *None* on the same form, and with the external pressure (her words are what travels upstairs).
  It turns at B02 E25 and B03 E11–E12.
- **B: pleasure,** a night of her own city. Kept as life texture.

The other wants are checked, and only Mara's (conditional) is also close to care.

## 3. Reconciled with the merged outline

The outline gained its own external-pressure column meanwhile. The two agree on the drift. **One conflict:**
- **The outline** has Lucien ask R. where his reports go, which makes R.'s *"went up in March, not by me"* read as an
  answer (Q-MO6).
- **The proposal** has him write the question and not send it. That keeps R.'s clause unprompted information, as Q-AN3
  approved.

**Recommended: the proposal's version.** At EBCI revision, the proposal's per-character lines govern, and the
outline's column is checked against them.
