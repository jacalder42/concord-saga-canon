# B01 Act III prose packets (E38–E48, S06): fidelity and imitation audit

**Date:** 2026-09-28. **Scope:** the twelve packets in `ebci/prose/B01/`, checked against their Narrative Briefs
(`ebci/B01/S1.T1.B01.A3.E38–E48.md`; S06 at the end of E48's brief), `ebci/prose/README.md`, the review-side watch-list,
the Act II checkpoint review and revision report, the Act II packets, the revised Act II prose (all of E26, E33, E35,
E37 and S05, plus the opening and ending of every other piece), Act I E09, E13 and E15, and the D1–D5 ruling. Nothing
here enters the drafting stack. This report does not change canon, packets, profile or manuscript.

## 1. Verdict

**Ready after the listed changes.** The set is faithful and lean. There are no control-layer codes or ids in it, and
none of the Writer Options leaked (E45's five and E48's four are all absent). It carries no length numbers, no
future-book pointers and no named organizations, and it has no camp or Vienna scene.

The problems are wording-level, in four groups:

1. **Six Ends lines** invite a shape the act has worn out: a record, a lesson, a double ending, or the notebook by
   negation. These are E38, E39, E43, E45, E48 and S06.
2. **Three architecture repeats need their difference written into the packet:**
   - E44 re-makes E35's discovery;
   - S06 re-stages S05, including its closing-time frame;
   - E45's overreach is E33's house-to-house again.
3. **Four brief elements dropped:**
   - E43's *unexplained*;
   - E48's *one person walks out whole*;
   - E42's *friends at their worst, fond anyway*;
   - E40's *not spectacle*.
4. **Two guards missing where they matter most:**
   - the lean's bearing (E45);
   - Seraphine's provisional perception (E45, E48).

None needs a new scene.

## 4. Act-level observations

**Rhythm.** The sequence alternates well:

- texture (E38);
- synthesis (E39);
- civic break (E40);
- aftermath (E41);
- two Life/Reward pieces back to back, exhaustion then wonder (E42, E43);
- belief (E44);
- the physical peak (E45);
- the negative-space climax (E46);
- the civic return (E47);
- the quiet payoff (E48);
- the room (S06).

**POV.** Seraphine has five points of view (E38, E41, E45 second half, E46, E48), Lucien three, Mara two, and Caro
and Baz one each. Baz falls from Act II's lead to one POV. That is fine: his method is present in E39, E47 and E48,
and the packets do not prescribe his log (it stays control-layer).

**The overextension ladder is architecture, and it repeats unless the wording differentiates it.** The rungs are
E33, then E34, E41, E45 and E48. Proposed #14, #28 and E48's existing "the others take the rest" give each rung its
own shape:

- the limit met (something left undone);
- the limit passed;
- the burden shared.

**The methods.** They converge by design at E39 and E47. The split lives in the scope question, which runs from E39
(disagreement) through E45 (it now has people in it) to E47, and physically in E48's separate posts. E47's packet
never names the scope question; Mara's people holding a post answers it implicitly (*wider in who watches, narrower in
what is claimed*). Leave that to the prose, but #5 and #7 keep E39 from re-running E27's convergence.

**Endings.** After the changes the act's cut points vary:

- mid-action (E44);
- sensory (E46);
- at a limit (E41);
- on a person or a question (E39, E40);
- among a crowd (E48).

No packet ends on writing. The remaining page risks are all in the prose's hands: Mara's steno pad, Baz's log and
Lucien's notebook exist on the page, and the checker should watch E40, E47 and E48 for them.

**Does it end as a book?** Yes, if E48 lands among people and S06 is a scene. The prediction works, the stranger walks
away whole, the model is useful and not in charge, and the bar opens. That is a whole-book shape, and it keeps the
Möbius with E01 (no mastery, no explanation).

**The three architecture repeats, judged:**

- **E44 against E35:** *not different enough as written*; #23 and #24 make it about the belief.
- **S06 against S05:** *not different enough*: same room, same argument, same closing frame. #43–#45 make it the
  morning, a scene, with versions set side by side and an ordinary problem.
- **E43 against E26:** *mostly different already* (alone, not the river, unrecorded). #19 and #20 close the notebook
  and music routes.

**Music.** It is on the page in E26, E31 and S05, and the architecture puts it in E48. Keeping E43 clear of it matters
(#19).

**Labels.** Act II's drafts carry the working labels as headings. So E45 "The Rebound", E47 "The Square Remembers" and
E48 "…Pulse" act as page cues that contradict their packets (#31, #36, #42).
