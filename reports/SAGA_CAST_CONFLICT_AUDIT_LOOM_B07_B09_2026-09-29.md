## 0. The short answer

**Partly, and in a particular shape.** Loom is not a care contest from end to end. It has:

- **one person who does wrong with lasting cost** (Kade);
- **one conflict line built exactly as the Calder principles ask** (Lacuna and Kade);
- **a ruled finale with real violence between people** (M52).

What it does is **split the cast in two**:

- **The five-person crew** (Seraphine, Lucien, Caro, Elisabet, Rex) is a **zero-conflict care collective**
  for 138 episodes. It has no incompatible wants among its members. It makes no wrong choice that costs
  anyone but the chooser. It meets no human obstruction until B08 E40.
- **All moral failure is put into Kade**, and the crew's response to it is grief and love: *"Caro weeps
  for him"* (B08 E21); *"They still love him"* (B08 E26).

This is the Loom form of the B01 diagnosis: considerate people around one designated flawed man. Across
the crew strand, **bodily cost stands in for stakes**: *"The cost lands on Seraphine's body. Caro tends
her"* (B08 E11).

**The finale's metaphysics is not the problem.** §42A makes empathy *"its medium, not its judge"*, and
the approved causal model says it is *"not a virtue gate"*. The architecture respects this: nobody earns
the Mending by being kind, and the flare is Silence's power, not a reward for Kade's goodness. The
confusion is elsewhere. **The approach to the Mending (B09 A1–A2) is a solidarity cascade**, so the crew
reaches the finale already integrated. The finale's integration then has no distance to travel.

**Finding.** Every conflict between protagonists involves **Kade**, bar one. The exception is Lucien and
Seraphine over her secret, and it is dissolved by listening (B09 E25). **Among the five crew members there
is no collision of wants anywhere in Loom.** Their decisions are unanimous:

- *"They commit to Tahl's shape"* (B07 E19);
- the particulars are confirmed by four skills converging (B08 E18);
- *"The forward group recommits"* (B09 E16).

**What is built well.** The Lacuna–Kade line obeys every principle. The argument (B07 E39) does not end.
The split (B08 E01) is a consequence. Her refusal to lie to him (B09 E24) repairs nothing. **The repair is
a separate event with its own cause and time**: his stand at the perimeter (E41), then the epilogue (E48).
This is the model for the rest of the cast.

## Counts

**How I judged.** I gave each episode one **primary engine**: the force that moves the episode from its
start to its turn, read from the "story job and turn" column.

- **Care:** someone understands, comforts, steadies, tends, guides or reconciles. Silence's and Hope's
  guidance counts as care. Mira does not, because she destabilises.
- **Opposition:** a will set against a named character's will. This covers protagonist against
  protagonist, an antagonist acting on a named person, and a legitimate claim pressed.

  go here.
- **Phenomenon / event:** metaphysics, public events, logistics, receipts, VT interludes, the Mending's

## Verdicts

**B07: mixed; the crew strand is affected.**

- **Kade's strand is sound.** The post is his, the break is a consequence, the argument does not end, and
  LX2 already stops Elias from authoring his fall.
- **The crew's strand is care and logistics.** New Orleans breaks and the crew answers with *"A Corner
  Held"* and a clean handoff. **No crew member confronts Kade about Uptown.** The crew leaves him, and
  the architecture never lets that leaving injure anyone, although his card's core wound is
  *"People leave."*

**B08: the most affected book on the crew strand; the strongest on Kade's.**

- M46 is real complicity with a real cost.
- The search strand, however, is three stabilisations resolved by tending and steadiness:
  - its costs are bodily (E11, E43);
  - its middle is *"confirmation, done well"*;
  - the crew answers a friend's authorised violence with tears and love (E21, E26);
  - Lucien's restraint appears twice (E30, E33);
  - Seraphine's secret produces no friction at all.
- LX3's human questions (preservation; acceptability; pressure elsewhere) are **the right fix, and they
  are currently notes, not beats.**

**B09: the least affected; the finale is legitimate.**

- Act III has the saga's clearest opposing wills (M52), and the Mending's integration is where
  integration belongs. **Leave E31–E45 alone (LX5).**
- The risk is **A1–A2's "sparks"**: recommitment, last intimacy, *"nobody says goodbye"*, *"he listens"*.
  They bring the crew to the swamp already whole, which flattens the finale's arc.
- Much of this is approved design (route Q8), so changes there are the author's to make.

**The finale's mechanism is not being confused with behaviour.** §42A's *"medium, not its judge"* is
honoured: nobody's kindness is a key. The confusion is **tonal and upstream**: a crew that only ever
cares makes a care-powered ending look like a reward, even though the rules say it is not.
