## 1. How it was built

1. **Packets** from the integrated outline as amended by Q-ST1–7. They include global rules, a build table with a
   target per unit, one packet per unit, and the hand-offs between groups.
2. **Six revisers in parallel** (G1–G6, about 20k words each). Each started from the br chapter or the adopted test
   sample named in its packet, and kept existing prose wherever the packet allowed. As each group finished, its continuity
   facts were relayed to the groups still writing.
3. **Four read-only checkers** read the whole book, one each on dates and names, the causal chains, the fixed guards and
   Q-ST items, and wording and length.
4. **One fix round** applied every definite and probable finding and the listed style fixes, then trimmed texture only.
