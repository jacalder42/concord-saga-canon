## 1. Method

The whole of B01 was read at manuscript `fb9e3d5`: E00–E48 and S01–S06, 55 pieces, 231,066 words, in one
reading-order file. Three independent assistant reviewers each read it all, in order:

| | Reviewer | Saw |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Developmental editor: a whole-book letter, with a length recommendation and a map of the excess | the text only |

| R3 | Voice and line editor: one novel or three passes, a residue census, point of view, the watch-list | the text + the writer profile + the watch-list |

**Proof of reading:** each reviewer quoted the last sentence of each act, and all nine quotations are exact.

**Checks:** Claude checked the specific claims against:

## 2. Findings

**B01 works as a novel and as a series opener.**
- **R2 scores it 4.4 / 5** over 55 pieces: Act I 4.3, Act II 4.4, Act III 4.6. Nothing scored below 3, and R2 would
  buy book two.
- **R1** would publish it, at a shorter length.
- **R3** finds one narrator throughout. **Act I is a separate copy pass, not a separate voice.**
- **It improves as it goes.** E42–E46 is the strongest run.
- **E46, E42, E26 and E48's payoff of E02 are named by more than one reader.**
- **No reviewer found a story problem that needs a new scene.**

**Convergent risks:**
1. **Length carried by duplicated meaning:** repeated rituals, recaps, and process scenes shown in full. R1 puts the
   right length at about 175k (170–185k) and maps 45–55k of excess, with every set piece kept. R2 found it long in
   the same places. R3 locates duplicated meaning in E16–E17, E24, E27, E41 and E48.
2. **A soft stretch in the second half of Act II.** R1 and R2 place it differently, but both name E32 and E36.
3. **The voice hardens into shared formulas.** R3 names ten. The main ones:
   - an involuntary-action disclaimer, 22–24 times;
   - one laughter shape, about 16 times, which spread after the watch-list named it;
   - everyone's *considered*, about 60 times with its partner phrase;
   - the credential phrase *a great many*, 33 times;
   - endings on a record, now fixed in Act III.

   ***As if / as though* still rises by act:** 4.6, 5.7, 6.9 per 10,000 words.
4. **The elders share one register,** the resolution template is still the default way out of distress, and the minor
   cast is large.
5. **The prologue never returns.** R1 and R2 both feel it.
   - *Checked:* **by design.** Its register returns at B02 E41 (`BC-MACRO-MOBIUS-SKY`, LOCKED).
   - Two blind readers feeling the gap makes it a real audience cost.

**Where they differ:**
- R1 finds Act III's opening trough (E38–E41) long. R2 scores E40 and E41 5/5.
- R1 would merge E18–E19. R2 does not name them.
- R1 wants more forward pull at the close. R2 accepts it.
