## 2. Findings

**It reads as the same novel, and as the right middle act.** Act I found the phenomenon. Act II turns it into a
method, then a written, falsifiable forecast, then a result right on time and place and wrong on everything else (E27 →
E33), then consequence. **Baz is the best new element.**
- **Strongest:** E20, E24, E33, E30, E26 and E36.
- **Weakest:** E28, E21 and E32.
- **It ends as an act, just barely.** E37's closing notebook line repeats a conclusion E35 reached.

**The Q-AR7–9 guards held:**
- **Agency is clearly up:** choices with costs, and trouble caused by what they think they know.
- **Casework is always present and begins to cost.** Its best form is the E24 conflict, where both are right. No
  obligation is actually missed yet.
- **The three methods disagree.** This is the act's strongest structural gain. The risk is Baz becoming the one-line
  winner.

**No guard was breached:**
- no camp scene;
- no Vienna scene;
- no bearing;
- no paperwork openings;
- no named organisations;
- nothing explained.

**What Act II learned from Act I anyway** (the review-side watch-list):
- **Endings:**
  - **8 of 20 episodes end on something written, signed or timed.** With the notebook in the closing frame it is 10 of
    20: the Act I notebook ending with a new owner.
  - **5 double endings.**
  - **One closing image repeated:** Elisabet's cable ends both E23 and E30.
- **Retired motifs return:**
  - *did you eat* (once);
  - a thinness running gag that re-skins it (six pieces);
  - the timestamped *Nothing* texts (19).
- **The shared sentence the watch-list names is back** (four speakers), and seven characters share one appraising
  look.
- **Food is on the page in 13 of 20 episodes;** the food-free episodes are among the strongest.
- **New formulas:**
  - Elisabet's scene ritual;
  - *saw him decide* (about 9; two cross into mind-reading, E29 and E36);
  - Baz's one-line verdicts.
- **Line level** (per 10,000 words, Act I revised against Act II first draft):
  - *as if / as though* 4.4 → 9.3; Act I's first draft ran about 12.7.
  - *had not known* 0.4 → 1.2.
  - **The Q-AR3 dash rule worked:** trailing ellipses in dialogue fell from 35 to 4.
  - **British usage Q-AR1 did not cover:** *round* for *around* (about 83), *had got* (26), *trouser*, *burnt*, *a
    meter* in American POVs.

**Scale** (Q-AR5: inspection, not suspicion):

- **E24 (7,191) duplicates the most:** it retells E23's meeting and mirrors its reflection. It would come near 5,500

## 4. Possible canon discoveries (flag only)

- **Lucien plans a July trip to Marseille (E36).** B02's July episodes have him in New Orleans, so the plan would have
  to be overtaken.

- **The unnamed city group** holds the coroner's form carrying Seraphine's name and paragraph.
