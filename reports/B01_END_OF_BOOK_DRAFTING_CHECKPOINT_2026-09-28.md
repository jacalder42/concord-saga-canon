## 2. Findings (the independent review)

**B01 ends as a book, and the ending is its best writing.**
- **E46** carries the negative-space climax.
- **E48** pays off E01 and E02, and meets every clause of the architecture's close:
  - the pattern is real;
  - the city knows;
  - nobody knows enough;
  - they go on;
  - prediction without explanation or control;
  - one stranger carried.
- **The Möbius holds** with E01, E02 and E15.
- **The last image is a crowd,** not a page. **S06** closes the room.

**Act III is tighter than Act II.** One of its 12 pieces ends on a written record, against 10 of 20 in Act II.
- **Strongest:** E46, E41 and E45.
- **Weakest:** E38, the middle of E47, and E43's coda.

**The guards held:**
- no camp scene;
- no Vienna scene;
- no bearing for the lean;
- nothing explained;
- the Act III packets' guards on perception.

**What remains:**
1. **E39's written window missed and was never recorded.** Lucien wrote *"I'll write that down."* The next event came
   a week late, and Baz's *"coming faster"* was falsified with it. This is the one gap in the book's own logic. The
   checker carried it through seven reports, and E47 accounted only for the corridor page. **Lesson for B02:** the
   checker needs a promises ledger.
2. **Line-level residue has returned in the first draft:**
   - *as if / as though* runs at 9.6 per 10,000 words (E48 at 19.0), against 5.6 for revised Act II;
   - new refrains;
   - the care template in E42–E45;
   - double endings in E42 and E47;
   - a point-of-view slip in E41.

   It amounts to about 3,500–4,000 words of duplicated meaning.
3. **Threads:**
   - the Wide Quiet has no public trace, though the exit state says *the city knows*;
   - Clement's phone;
   - the hum stops without comment;
   - S06's exact minutes;
   - Act II's *two months* for Lucien in E32.
4. **Twenty-three possible canon discoveries** (review §8). The central ones:
   - Seraphine's perception: *after*, *in people*, persisting for days, now including Lucien, at a stated bodily cost;
   - vertical motion inferred at the lot;
