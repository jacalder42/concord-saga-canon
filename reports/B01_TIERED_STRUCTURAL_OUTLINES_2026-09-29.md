## 2. What they found

**Both designers independently choose about 150k.**
- **120k works as a book,** but it cuts the book's pleasures, and Baz's life apart from the case. B03 needs that, because
  Baz's death must take *"a person, not a function"* (M10).
- **180k keeps most of the length problem.**

**Both build the same spine from existing material:**
- **The coroner's form is one contested object.** Four claims meet on it: Seraphine's paragraph, Lucien's *None*,
  Renée's wish for an ordinary closed record, and the city's hold on *"anything with a clock"*.
- **Renée returns twice,** in Metairie and never at the camp. Seraphine refuses her, and Renée does not forgive her.
- **Lucien's withheld true sentence goes to the coroner instead of his office.** That also leaves B02 E08, Lucien's
  first partial obedience, unspent.
- **The city's one direct act** is a policeman's question.
- **Baz signs the forecast with his dissent on the back.**
- **Mrs. Arceneaux learns there was a page, and her grievance stands.**

**Caveat: part of this convergence is inherited.** Both designers read the same conflict audit, which ranked these wills
first. The independent 120k is independent in its structure, not in its sources.

**Claude's corrections and checks:**
- **The 150k outline says it removes "75k of duplicated function". It does not.** The function map measured about 7,300
  words of duplicated function. **Most of the 75k is pace and texture: a judgement about length, not about repetition.**
  That is the trade you would be choosing.
- **The spine is sound against the obligations list:**
  - Renée never returns at the camp (Q-DR7).
  - No new person is added.
  - Nothing explains the death or the phenomenon.
  - No obligation is dropped in any tier.
- **Every tier needs architecture changes:**
  - merges (E04+E05, E18+E19, E25+E26, E34+E35 and others);
  - a changed reading order (E30 before E29);
  - a new piece count.

  These reopen the approved 48-episode architecture, Q-FR6 (no merge or cut) and Q-TP1 (about 225k).
- **Any tier is a second draft, not an edit.** At 150k, 38% of the prose goes, 10 new beats are written, and about 16
  pieces are redrafted. It would be drafted by the same pipeline under the same writer profile. **The Calder Companions
  recovery (ledger §259) shows that profile carries the author persona's own connection pattern for every character, and
  none of the conflict machinery.** Without that fixed first, a shorter B01 risks being a shorter version of the same
  contest of consideration.
