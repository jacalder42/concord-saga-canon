## 3. The conflicts

1. **Power bank against trap.** The author: *"power banks"*, *"fuel (via boosts)"*, *"give or force
   it upon others"*. Against: Notion's trap model, the assistant's *"not boosts"*, and the declined
   D14 recommendation. Under the precedence rule (CLAUDE.md §5, C3 rule 2), **the author's own
   words beat accepted assistant text.** Nothing dated rules transfer out.
2. **Human subjects against constructs.**
   - **Human:** the author (l. 118156, *"started as people"*; l. 33096, *"tricking the weak or
     poor"*; l. 155783, *"sacrificed their intent"*) and the accepted Mini-Bible (*"All metas begin
     as human test subjects (voluntary or coerced). Their names are erased upon conversion."*,
     l. 155976).
   - **Construct:** `canon/factions/Manufactured_Metas.md` (*"artificial constructs (biological,
     cybernetic, or hybrid)"*), generated on 2026-01-04 under *"Proceed with full pipeline"*, and
     a 12-10 module that builds Variants from algorithms.
3. **What feeds them.** The author: followers' extracted intent; harvested or ritualized resonance.
   The 2026 recollection: bystanders. Mechanica §22 (*"Crowds multiply patterns"*) supports
   ambient coupling, not a harvest.
4. **Intent flowing in.** *"Supplied with followers extracted intent"* conflicts with
   `canon_rules.json` (`"intent": "none_permanent"`), the card's *"No Intent, ever"* and §42A.1.
   Pass 5 §7 offers a reframe: handlers **encode directives** into the meta, which is control, not
   Intent.
5. **Who builds and uses them.** The author, 11-10: *"factions/dominions"* and *"the resonant"*,
   fighting mundane factions during an ebb. Current canon: Technarc hardliners and Dominion-adjacent
   bodies (`Manufactured_Metas_Creators.md`). The author's *resonant-faction* user has no current
   counterpart; it belongs to the superseded ontology.
6. **Mechanica.** §48 gives metas *"Amplification"*. Against that, the Notion trap model says tech
   cannot amplify, and §23 says *"Technology is a casualty, not a carrier"*.

## 4. Can a meta be drawn from without Intent? Yes

**Storing without Intent is compatible with the rules, and the author framed it that way:** *"can
not spend their own energy … without intent, so they must give or force it upon others"*.

- **§42A.1:** *"Structure can hold a shape. It cannot commit."* A meta holds load. **The
  recipient's soul supplies the Intent** that spends it. So the meta never holds Intent, and §6's
  *"No emotion-free power"* is met by the recipient.
- **§42A.2:** *"Blocked pressure is stored, not discharged."* **A meta is a small hard cap.** The
  accepted assistant text already draws the link (*"stable only because the hard-cap Veil contains
  them"*; Null *"foreshadows Silence & Hope's own failure mode"*). This gives Neon a quiet mirror of
  the Loom's premise.
- **§48:** *"They fail catastrophically under pressure"*. That is what a forced draw risks, and it
  is what M17 (B06 A1, an engineered-control failure) and M30 (B08 A3, assets that *"may fail or
  fragment"*) already stage.
- **§13.2, *Boosted*:** *"Deliberate amplification … Increased cost … Short-duration advantage …
  Common in Neon and Loom"*. A draw is a boost, and the rules already price it. The integrity audit
  adds: *"a boosted agent inherits injury and poor control"*.
- **The interface:** Mechanica says resonance *"never transmits digitally"*, and §23 makes
  technology a casualty, not a carrier. **So a draw is bodily and close**: a harness in contact, a
  handler in the room. It is never networked. Machines that try to extract at a distance fit the
  accepted *"rarely works; causes instability"* (Containment Sites, l. 238334).
- **After the Mending:** the Mini-Bible's *"Their energy folds into the Breathable Veil"* fits
  §42A.5 (*"Nothing accumulates"*).

**The one risk:** if a meta were *supplied with Intent*, it would breach `none_permanent` and
§42A.1. The handler-control reframe avoids that.

## 7. Questions for the author, with recommended answers

1. **Can factions draw on a meta's stored energy?** (The D14 core, and the integrity audit's
   question 1.)
   - **(a) Recommended: yes, and it costs.** A meta is a power bank that cannot spend itself.
     Handlers draw from it into a recipient, who supplies the Intent. It works **unreliably**, and
     every draw costs the meta, the recipient (injury, poor control, and at the extreme permanent
     harm or death) and those nearby. It follows your 11-10 and 11-21 words and fits §42A.1,
     §13.2 and §48.
   - (b) It is a Technarc goal that never reliably works: attempted, failing, a source of
     instability.
   - (c) The late-November trap model: metas hold energy and nothing draws it out.
   - This replaces the declined *"no direct boosting of operatives"*.
2. **What charges a meta?**
   - **Recommended: harvested resonance, from followers and handlers, drawn by ritual and
     harness,** as you wrote on 11-08 and 11-10. Crowds amplify it (§22) but are not harvested.
   - **"Bystander energy" has no source text.** It stays a recollection unless you adopt it. If you
     want it, the recommended form is **crowd coupling near a meta**: the crowd feeds the meta's
     instability, not the faction's battery.
3. **Intent.** **Recommended: a meta never holds Intent.** Your *"followers extracted intent"* is
   read as **handlers' encoded directives**: control, not Intent. `none_permanent`, the card and
   §42A.1 stand.
4. **Origin.** **Recommended: altered humans only, voluntary or coerced, names erased.** Your
   11-21 words and the accepted Mini-Bible agree. The card's *"artificial constructs"* wording
   would be corrected later with a dated note, which is a card edit and needs your ruling.
5. **The interface.** **Recommended: bodily and close, never networked.** It follows Mechanica's
   no-digital-transmission rule and §23. Distance-extraction machines exist, and they *"rarely
   work"*.
6. **Magnitudes.** Your 11-08 figures (10–20% individual; 3–5% collective; a hard ceiling with
   severe cost) predate the current ~20% soft threshold. **Recommended: leave them to the Mechanica
   line-by-line review**, with the integrity audit's decision 6, and treat the 11-08 figures as
   the likely origin.
7. **U1–U7.** **Recommended: unchanged**, left to the Mechanica review.
8. **What answering releases.** **Recommended:** answers 1–5 settle the transfer loop. That lifts
   D19's gate and CLAUDE.md §9's standing constraint in principle. **No Neon meta work starts** until
   the queue reaches Neon, and the B04 A1 incident card is written then.
