# Book 6 manufactured-meta failures versus Santa Fe tear — causal separation

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Status:** FORENSIC SOURCE TEST / NON-CANONICAL. Supplements [Pass 2 saga gap sweep](../recovery/SAGA_RESONANCE_EVENT_INVENTORY_PASS2_GAP_SWEEP_2026-09-23.md), especially GAP-07 and GAP-08. No episode slot, new field event, physical law, or milestone is approved here.

## Bottom line

The best-attested Book 6 order is **multi-city AR failures and rising storms → manufactured-meta failures begin → Santa Fe corridor begins tearing → Santa Fe fractures/storms make landfall → Tahl's death and aftermath**. Thus the Santa Fe tear **cannot be the initiating cause of every Book 6 meta failure**. A December 7 assistant reconstruction claims that deployed metas further destabilize corridors, but gives no operational pathway or evidence tying a particular failure to the Santa Fe tear. Treat local meta-to-corridor coupling as **RECOVERED ASSERTION / OPEN MECHANISM**, not CURRENT physics. Shared escalating pressure is plausible, not proven as an exclusive cause. Neither "meta failures cause Santa Fe" nor "Santa Fe causes all meta failures" follows.

This is a **time ordering, not a causal graph already demonstrated**. If the Dec 7 meta field trial is someday adopted, its suggested separate city would make the distinction even sharper, but it must not be counted as a confirmed additional incident. If a meta is deployed at Santa Fe, it could worsen *local* instability without having caused prior Act I failures elsewhere; that edge remains unverified.

## Candidate mechanisms to test, not canonize

1. **Shared load:** a local or regional pressure increase separately pushes weak AR services, vulnerable metas and Santa Fe structures across different thresholds. Requires distinct thresholds and geography; does not assert instantaneous global coupling.
2. **Meta contributes locally:** load accumulates in a meta; containment leaks or discharges into a physically coupled corridor medium. Requires proximity/coupling, load budget, environmental pathway, changed structural evidence and cost. The draft's "lack of Intent destabilizes corridors" is a label, not that pathway.
3. **Human intervention:** Technarch positioning/deployment/containment choice creates a local incident even if the wider storm is independent. Must identify an actual decision and operating apparatus; Protocol 9 alone is not a mechanism.
4. **Unrelated onset:** AR incidents, meta instability and Santa Fe share a crisis period but no direct event-to-event causal link. Keep viable until ruled out.

For any selected episode, test **physical change → local coupling/medium → actor action and intent (if any) → constraint and failure threshold → immediate/lingering cost → trace a second observer could measure or misread**. The meta's missing Intent prevents controlled self-direction; it does not prove zero physical output. Do not turn emotional panic, AR glitches or a character's structure-vision into objective common-cause measurements without evidence.
