# B01 E13: the street-wide disturbance, a closer read (Q-DR9)

**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Status:** EDITORIAL REVIEW (production). The closer read the author asked for before deciding Q-DR9
(`decisions/B01_ACT_I_DRAFTING_CHECKPOINT_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-28.md`).

**What it does not change:** nothing. The draft is not revised. It asks one question.

## 1. What the draft does

In E13 two things happen: Seraphine's pressure comes up slowly and Lucien falls again at the post. The draft also gives
the neighbourhood **visible public behaviour**:
- dogs barking in a chain;
- a horn held down at a four-way;
- a couple on a ladder snapping;
- a mother shouting into a house;
- two men arguing over a parking space;
- schoolchildren shoving.

Seraphine walks about six blocks and finds no edge. Nobody talks about any of it afterwards.

**Her pressure and his fall end in the same minute**, while her hands are on his shoulders and he is marking her
*same*.

Later episodes use it:
- **E16:** Seraphine's list includes *the street by your store… 6 blocks, no edge*. Lucien's table of kinds cites the
  dogs, the horns and the mother.
- **E16–E17:** Lucien's near-pattern (*close, both; far, one*) counts the store as *both*.

## 3. Findings

1. **It is more event than the architecture authorised.** The draft makes E13 a third civic behaviour event (CIV,
   about W1) where the record has an interior collapse (INT, W0). Its footprint, six blocks with no edge, is **larger
   than E08's square**. The escalation then runs: a room, a square, a neighbourhood, the Square. E15 stays *undeniable*
   because nobody talks about E13, but it is no longer the first thing that is **big**.
2. **The coincident ending risks breaching E13's keep-off.** The public signs stop at the moment the two of them are
   joined in a task. A reader can infer that their collaboration calmed the street, and *causes, calms or triggers
   nothing in the world* forbids exactly that.
   - Without the public signs, the same-minute ending is only her pressure (ATT) and his fall (POV). That is data about
     the two of them, not a world effect.
   - E16's Kerlerec counter-example already keeps it open.
3. **What is authorised and worth keeping:**
   - Seraphine failing to carry what she senses. Walking the pressure and finding no edge is ATT, and it is her
     failure.
   - Lucien's fall at the post.
   - The hands on the shoulders; the column of *same*; *S. — reads straight*; the phone numbers.
   - The same-minute times.
   - None of this needs the street to behave.
4. **The cascade is small.** E13 loses the public signs, and E16's table-of-kinds sentence loses the dogs, horns and
   mother. The store stays *both* on Lucien's map. E17 is untouched.
