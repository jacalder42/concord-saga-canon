## 2. Assessment

1. **It agrees with, and sharpens, the author's note** (*"still some re-stating, over explanation"*). The diagnosis
   (*competent, lucid, controlled — and slightly over-recorded*; *de-mechanization, not humanization*) fits the
   evidence better than the family audit of ledger §445.
2. **The §445 pass was too restrained for this problem.** It cut 12 of 220 *as if* constructions, because its
   auditors read most of them as signature. The review is right that the construction's *frequency* is itself the
   tell, whatever the quality of each instance.
3. **Two of its causes are our own rules.** *Action over statement* (ledger §440–§441) and the table-read and
   screenplay work (§443–§444) pushed feeling into withheld behavior and gaze. The rise in *did not* and *looked* is
   partly that working as designed, and partly over-applied. The fix must not swing back to stated interiority, the
   failure those rules corrected.
4. **One adjustment: no quotas.** *"Challenge perhaps half"* is a quota, and the author has repeatedly ruled out
   quotas. The test should be per instance, with the expected yield reported, not targeted.
5. **Its prohibitions are right.**
   - No synonym swapping (no *glanced* / *gazed*).
   - No generic humanizer.
   - Protect *still / again / small / quiet* as Veil's palette, thinned only where it pre-interprets.

## 3. Proposal

Five bounded tests, applied **together by one reviser per chapter**:
1. **As-if:** keep the specific, surprising, POV-true comparisons; cut or recast those that only say what something
   resembles.
2. **Look:** cut *looked* / *watched* / *eyes* where the next sentence already proves the attention.
3. **Non-action:** keep consequential refusals; cut neutral non-events.
4. **Reaction chain:** break the line → gaze/body → interpretation → reply loop.
5. **Accounting:** where a paragraph reports every small transition, keep the one detail that lets the reader infer
   the rest.

The remedies are deletion, compression, interruption, or a more consequential action; never a synonym, and never new
stated feeling.

**Calibration first,** as before (ledger §406):
- three chapters (E01, E25 and E49), revised in a copy;
- judged blind against the final draft;
- read by the author side by side.

Then the book-wide pass at the calibrated intensity.
