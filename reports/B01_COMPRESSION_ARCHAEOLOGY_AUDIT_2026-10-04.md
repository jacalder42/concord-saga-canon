## 2. Verdict

**B01 is both leaner and thinner, in different layers.**

| | Where |
|---|---|
| **Leaner, correctly** | Clock time and counting (9.4 → 1.4 notations per 10k words); procedure as investigation; repeated theories and recaps; scoring. **This should stay gone.** |
| **Thinner** | After-scenes; bodies after effort; witnesses who read the leads as a pair; off-duty time between the leads; ambient community; strangers as people; Lucien's method as delight; Seraphine's one-line interior (want, peace) |
| **Fuller** | Conflict; consequence that persists; protagonist agency; outside pressure; designed heat; secondary characters who push back |

- **By act:** Act I was cut hardest (45% of 225k survives), Act II next (50%), Act III barely (76%).
- **By character:** Lucien is the most reduced to function. Seraphine gained agency but lost her non-functional
  interior and her body after what she does. Caro gained a position but lost the friendship's history and her body
  after the crash. Baz gained a moral stake but lost pleasure on the page.

## 3. The causal hypothesis

**The hypothesis:** that the push from 200k+ toward 120k materially caused the under-staging, the reduced relational
texture and the limited play.

**Verdict: MIXED / DIFFERENT CAUSE.** The effect is confirmed. The cause it names is mostly wrong, though partly right.

1. **The main cause is the redraft's specification, not compression.**
   - The 09-29 outline named a life beat for each piece. The revised EBCI moved every one into *Writer options*.
   - The prose-packet rule excludes Writer options unless they are promoted, and none ever was. Of 107 Writer options,
     52 were the outline's named life beats; 0 were promoted; about 20 never reached the page.
   - *Ends* lines also truncated after-scenes, and location lists kept witnesses out.
   - **The system could represent ordinary life. It put it in the one field the drafter never received.**
2. **The second cause is draft 3, where the hypothesis holds as stated.**
   - The under-120k humanism pass carried a chapter-level cut quota (about 13%) and cut 14,790 words. Its cuts
     reached past their targets: felt duration went with counted duration, habitual lines and negative reception went
     as repetition, and one-line interior went as restated meaning.
   - Later passes added beats at unchanged length estimates (E43, E46, E28). The writers delivered the beats and paid
     with texture.
3. **Some problems were already in the 225k manuscript.**
   - Counting and clock fixation, at its worst there.
   - Procedure as investigation.
   - Thin primary event scenes.
   - The protagonists as observers, with no antagonist driver. These are the author's own 09-29 findings, which the
     redesign fixed.
4. **Two problems are new to this lineage:** *I know* and *said nothing* as closers. Both roughly doubled through
   the redraft and draft 3.

## 4. The author's addenda

- **Primary-cast compression: confirmed, with a mechanism.**
  - Community and secondary characters *as the scene's subject* survived or improved.
  - Community *as backdrop* to a primary scene was lost.
  - Primary–primary off-plot time fell from about 7,200 to about 1,500 words in late Act II.
  - **Three mechanisms:**
    - **Witnesses are the only lawful channel for the pair's heat.** Nothing romantic is said aloud, and the
      redraft's two-lead scenes lost their witnesses.
    - **Secondaries are vivid because each owns a want of their own.**
    - **A counter-case shows the cure.** A scene between the leads stays alive when it is wordless, carried by an
      object and watched by a third party (E42). The cure is staging and witnesses, not quips.
- **Revision scars: five REBUILD spans**, about 3,200 words, 2.6% of the book:
  - E03's folded Vein section;
  - E28's middle third (surgical first);
  - E41's sixth-trip section;
  - E46's reckoning table;
  - E48's recap.

  Two summary scars (E13's supper, E43's park) need re-staging, not rebuilding. Everything else sampled is healthy
  or protected.
