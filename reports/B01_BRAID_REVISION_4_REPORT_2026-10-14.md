The repetition the readers feel has **partly moved**. The critic still counts about twelve table-and-tally scenes and
the casual reader six, but the new question's answers fall mostly on the opposition and on deliberate echoes.

**The new question, where two or three readers agree:**
- **Caldas Ren's soft approach.** It repeats in Ch 19, 28, 30, 32, 38 and 39, in near-identical description (*gray
  hair cut close, good suit, empty hands, the second half of the sentence*). The critic counts nine appearances.
  **All three name it.**

- **Rieux reads the card twice.** He reads it to Lucien in Ch 29 and to Vernice in Ch 37 (mystery, casual).
- **Lorraine's kitchen.** Ch 41 repeats Ch 17, with the same exit line. Readers called it intentional and well done,
  and still noticed it (mystery, casual).
- **The whole crowd goes down.** The Square in Ch 10 returns at the lot in Ch 34 (mystery, casual).
- **The side stair.** It recurs at Ch 9, 33 and 42 (casual), and the readers took the reversal as working.

**Other convergences:**
- **The stolen typed summary confirms everything the crew counted (Ch 30).** All three readers say this, so the
  crew's model looks borrowed. This is the approved design (Q-RV, *"the crew's counted model, confirmed by the
  folder's sheet"*). The readers feel the confirmation as a solution handed over. The mystery reader's fix is to make
  the sheet **wrong in one line the crew has already disproved**, so that their count beats the enemy's summary.
- **The gray-case side (the "boxes") has no face or motive** (all three). By design it is unattributed (Q-EN,
  "B-lite").
- **The middle still sags in Ch 15–18** (mystery, casual).

**New, single-reader findings:**
- **Ch 25's break-in is never paid** (mystery reader). Who opened the box in Lucien's room drives the Kerlerec mistake
  and its costs, and it is never even hinted at.
- **Ch 36's *"If I falter, everything collapses"* read as jarring** (mystery reader). This is the ruled seed of
  Seraphine's false belief (`decisions/SERAPHINE_LOOM_HARD_CAP_AND_MIRA_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-27.md` Q1;
  `decisions/B01_SILENCE_CONFIRMATION_AND_RESONANCE_LIFT_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-10-12.md`). It is protected and stays.
  Only its staging is open.
