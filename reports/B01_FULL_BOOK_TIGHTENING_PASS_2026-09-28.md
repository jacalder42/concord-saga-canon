## 1. Method

- **A structural function map came first:** one whole-book read of each piece's dramatic functions, exit pull, what
  survives each crisis, and the clusters of duplicated function.
  - It answers the forwarded ChatGPT reviews' warning against *"another general polish pass"* (ledger §255), within
    Q-FR1's scope.
  - It lets the pass compress repeated functions, not only repeated wording.
- **Then 47 revisers, one per piece,** each with the brief and a list of that piece's items.
- **Untouched:** E00, E01, E43, E46, S01, S02, S04 and S06.
- **Every diff was reviewed,** and every protected line was checked by exact search.

**All protected text is intact:**

- every author protection.

## 3. What subtraction cannot reach

The function map and the forwarded reviews agree:
- **Seraphine's self-spending is repeatedly met with care, and with the relationship's biggest steps.** Nobody else
  pays for it.
- **Disagreements rarely keep a cost past their own piece.** E24 is the exception, and E39's camp argument is dropped.
- **Every major rung of the relationship is set off by one of them being incapacitated.**
- **The secondary cast shares one register.**
- **After E33 the protagonists' special perception is no longer first to the event.**
- **E37 → E43 is one long exit-pull run,** about 33,000 words.
