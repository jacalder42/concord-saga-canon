## Executive finding

The earlier **manufactured-meta battery/reactor** concept is **RECOVERED**. A complete **bystander/crowd → meta → operative boost** loop is **PARTIALLY RECOVERED as author intent but still unverified as an operational rule**. Storage, zero usable Intent, pressure accumulation, leakage, Technarc weaponization and catastrophic failure recur in independent November 2025 material. No examined source specifies a working collection law, transfer interface, recipient limits, or accounting of source and recipient costs. In fact, a November 26 tech bible calls the *Meta Harness* armor that **traps rather than controls** meta energy and says tech cannot amplify Resonance. This is evidence of a genuine unresolved design conflict, not proof that transfer is impossible or that it works.

The strongest recoverable general model is bounded human action under Will/energy, emotion, Intent, skill, pressure and environmental conditions, with visible cost. The formulation “emotion supplies pressure; Intent direction; Will capacity; environment coupling” is a **PROPOSAL** consistent with several sources, not a verbatim settled equation. Mechanica's memory-inflated synthesis must not override later explicit author rulings.

## Technarc / manufactured-meta finding

**RECOVERED:** Technarc hardliners alter human subjects into resonance-saturated biological reactors/batteries; they cannot intentionally direct or safely expend stored load, react to surrounding emotional fields, leak, disrupt technology and can catastrophically fail. A November 24 description explicitly says the human subjects can be voluntary or coerced. The later [faction file](../canon/factions/Manufactured_Metas.md) instead permits biological, cybernetic or hybrid *artificial constructs*, and says they simulate resonance “without emotion or Intent.” That ontology and the extent of remaining feeling/agency require reconciliation. The prohibition on granting them usable Intent or ascension is CURRENT in [canon rules](../rules/canon_rules.json) and in the author's December rulings.

**AUTHOR-RECALLED / PROPOSAL, NOT RECOVERED INTERFACE:** Metas collect ambient/bystander load and factions draw on them to boost personnel. Earlier conversation-context summaries use “reservoir,” “power amplifier” and “faction reliance,” but neither these summaries nor the retrieved Notion pages define a functioning handoff. A phrase such as “designed for weaponization” can mean containment, denial, overload or transfer. It cannot settle the latter.

**Possible supersession:** November 24 already calls the metas closed pressure systems unable to direct or discharge themselves. November 26 says the harness traps rather than controls their energy; the same tech bible bans tech amplification. November 27 permits tech amplification/interference and describes reactors. These texts do not show a clear dated author decision that permanently rejected an external operative interface. They show an unresolved conflict among technical descriptions and a compression gap. Do not promote passive capture, siphoning, remote relay, wearable interface, or free energy as canon.

**Required transfer test if revived:** identify source and measurable depletion (or define uncaptured excess), capture pathway, storage capacity/loss, interface/contact and failure mode, recipient's native skill and Intent, recipient/body cost, meta-subject cost, environmental/tech residue, and who can use it. Borrowed load must never buy skill, agency, safe output or exemption from the nonlinear cost curve. The observed “no digital resonance transmission” rule forbids a networked metaphysical transfer.

## Anti-handwave event contract — PROPOSAL

For each resonance-influenced event, the episode packet should record privately: **action and baseline skill → pressure/trigger → medium and coupling conditions → objective physical change → witness/attuned/POV interpretation → limit/failure → immediate cost → lingering residue**. A zero-effect beat may be ordinary emotion and needs no artificial VFX. For a claimed event, ask: what changed physically, why here and now, what did the actor do, what prevented a larger result, who paid, what remains, and what could a skeptical observer document or misread? A gap is an **audit flag**, not permission to invent an effect.

Failure-prone claims requiring evidence: “the city remembers,” feelings leaking as direct information transfer, an attuned person knowing another's secrets, breath stabilizing a building without a physical support/action, a color proving a moral truth, a meta boosting a squad without a source/interface/cost. The [B1 export audit](../recovery/ACCOUNT_EXPORT_B01_ACT1_EPISODE_FORENSIC_AUDIT_2026-09-20.md) contains old “emotional spill as direct transfer” and breath/hum phrasing; these are **RECOVERED old beats**, not a grant of new abilities.

## VFX vulnerability, pending dedicated gate 3

Distinguish **objective change** (e.g., a measurable light flicker, acoustic phase shift, condensation displacement or device desync), **attuned perception** (early but fallible sensation), **subjective POV impression**, and **prose metaphor**. The source of illumination, reflective surface, weather, architecture and electrical state should constrain color/light/sound/geometry. Mechanica's four VFX pillars are a presentation grammar, not four exhaustive physical processes; a bodily cost or damaged machine is still an ordinary observable consequence. Keep the Veil palette restrained, limit simultaneous motifs, and prevent a hue from identifying emotion, allegiance or truth by itself.

## B1 and later escalation implications

**Book 1:** retain prologue + 48 narrative episodes + six supplements, including 12 Life/Reward episodes, and the locked order. Audit existing anomaly beats for a mundane baseline, a physical trace or explicitly subjective uncertainty, a meaningful human consequence and no system lecture. Technology may fail as a local casualty; professional checking and contrary witness accounts support the mystery. No formal shard, corridor class or meta-transfer reveal is needed.

**Neon:** emerging fracture, tech failures, public crisis and meta deployment permit stronger *measurable* effects. Define whether a meta is a denial/reactor asset or demonstrably supplies an operative before staging a boost. Conventional weapons and skill remain decisive; a boosted agent inherits injury and poor control.

**Loom:** broad coupled failures and costly structural interventions may enlarge the physical footprint; do not convert Intent into arbitrary reality editing. Distinguish shard severity, weather, route viability and human costs. Post-Mending filtration prevents the specified resonance catastrophe while preserving social, biological and technological consequences.
