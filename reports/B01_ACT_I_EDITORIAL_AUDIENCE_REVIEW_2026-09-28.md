## Findings

- **Act I works as a novel's opening.** R2 averages 4.1 / 5 and would buy the book. No reviewer found a story,
  character or canon problem Act I must fix.
- **Convergent risks, which are repetition and packaging:**
  - visible refrains;
  - tidy and double endings (six appended grace notes);
  - a *wise local chorus* sharing one register;
  - food and waiting resolving most feelings;
  - recaps and stated theme (E10, E16–E17);
  - outline-style chapter titles;
  - British spelling (about 96 instances, in American POVs too).
- **The author's two open questions, answered by the reviewers:**
  - **The quiet-image ending is Calder; the appended second image is not.**
  - **The long *and… and…* close is Calder when it enacts accumulation, a tic when it is the default soft close.**
  - Both stay with the author-led line edit, as ruled.
- **Checked against the architecture:**
  - **Chapter titles** are v4.1b *working titles*. *Filament* in E09's title is canon shorthand kept off the page.
  - **S03's disconnection is by design; its length is not.** It runs 1,312 words against a 200–400 spec.
  - **All three Act I supplements overran:** S01 351 against 150–300; S02 650 against 200–350; S03 1,312 against
    200–400. The v4.1b word targets never reached the prose packets. That is a pipeline gap.
- **The readers' Act II wants are already met or declined by design:**
  - rising stakes: E33, E45;
  - Caro: E23, E28, E30, E31, E42;
  - Seraphine's agency: E22, E29, E33–E34, E48;
  - the remote-similar breadcrumb: E39, E45;
  - Mara and Trip recur.
  - **Two are declined on purpose:**
    - Dré's death stays ordinary and medical (a negative control), with no camp return in B01.
    - The prologue presences next appear at B02 E41.
- **Watch:** Baz carries 9 of Act II's 20 POVs from a thin Act I footprint.
- **Length:**
  - No target exists.
  - At Act I's rate, B01 projects to about **210,000–240,000 words**. Episode length grew across the act, from about
    3,260 to 5,160 words.
  - Earlier diagnostic ranges were 66,000–120,000.
