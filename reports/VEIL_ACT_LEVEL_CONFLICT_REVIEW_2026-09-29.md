This is the act-level review the author asked for under Q-TG1
(`decisions/REVISED_EBCI_VEIL_PASS6_AND_TRILOGY_GUIDE_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-29.md`). It reads the Veil architecture
against `proposals/TRILOGY_CONFLICT_GUIDE_2026-09-29.md`, before pass 6 is applied. It is step 1 of the approved order;

- no manuscript text. Nothing in the private manuscript repository is quoted or paraphrased;

(CLAUDE.md §4). Book numbers are two digits throughout.

## 1. Method

**What was read.**
- **The lens:** the guide (all of it).
- **The decision:** Q-RE2, Q-VB1–12, Q-FX1–2, Q-TG1.
- **The reconciliation report:** §3 (F1–F5) and its Appendix.
- **B01:** the Narrative Brief of all 50 revised packets (`ebci/B01/`, E00–E49), including every Conflict block, Change
  and Exit conditions line, plus `ebci/B01/REDRAFT_CONCORDANCE.md`.
- **B02:** pass 5, with the Veil audit amendments (`proposals/VEIL_AUDIT_AMENDMENTS_2026-09-27.md` §1, §4).
- **B03:** pass 5, pass 4 where pass 5 carries it by reference, and the same amendments (§2, §4).
- **Pass 6 over both:** `proposals/VEIL_B02_B03_BEAT_REVISION_PASS6_2026-09-29.md` (its blocks win), plus F3 and F4
  as approved.
- **Packets:** the B02 and B03 Narrative Briefs (all 95), for the episodes where the beats are thin.
- **For reference:** `proposals/RELATIONSHIP_END_STATE_REGISTER_2026-09-29.md` §1–§2.

**How each act was read.** Five questions, following the guide's §2 and §4:
- **Initiative:** the protagonist acts that change the situation.
- **Conflict among protagonists:** who, over what, in which mode, and whether it changes what someone does. A
  difference settled inside its scene does not count.
- **External opposition:** another person or institution with an objective. The phenomenon does not count, and
  neither does ordinary friction with no will behind it (Q-XP2).
- **Earned cooperation:** where people work well together, and whether the costs stay.
- **Escalation:** which options narrowed.

**What the counts are for.** Counts are descriptive only, and serve the guide's closing test: *"whether the characters'
actions make the book compelling to read"*.

**Which state is reviewed.** B01 is reviewed as its revised packets stand. B02 and B03 are reviewed **as they will
stand once pass 6 is applied with F3 and F4**, because that is the state F5 asks Claude to mark.

- No new scene, person or hostility. A recast changes what a character does in an existing episode.
- In B03, recasts lean toward **cooperation under strain**: people who disagree and still act together.
- **Keep** means the withholding earns its place.
- **Recast** gives the new mode and one line on the choice and its consequence.

## 5. B01's revised packets

**No trigger clearly fires in B01. No packet change is recommended.** What follows are observations, and two optional
items for the author in §6 (b).

**External action and the mystery.**
- **Acts II–III:** the hands drive the investigation. Their objective is stated the same way every time (they want only
  the time), and they act consequentially on the leads' circles:
  - the family's door (E25);
  - Elisabet's season (E28, via the city);
  - Denise's call (E37);
  - Mara's truck (E40);
  - Caro's logs (E42);
  - the file's copy (E48).

  These moves provoke the leads' strongest initiatives: E36's trap, E38's trade, E39's stakeout, E48's choice of post.
- **Act I (T11):** there is no will behind anything. That fits the Veil opening, and E04's lie is the lever the hands
  pull later. **Not a trigger.**

**Receive-only runs.**
- **None long.** Life and wonder pieces (E05, E11, E13, E19, E23, E30, E33, E43) are spaced among acts.
- E47's negative-space climax turns on a choice to stay.

**Modes.**
- **B01 is the most varied of the three books:**
  - open insistence (E04);
  - competing methods (E17, E29, E45);
  - bargaining (E21's seven-line page, E22, E26, E38);
  - open action against wishes (E24, E36, E39, E42);
  - blame carried (E39 → E46, E48);
  - a truth that helps (E46: widen the inquiry).
- **Withholding is frequent here too.** There are about a dozen interpersonal concealments, from Seraphine, Lucien, Baz
  and Mara.
- **All but three come out inside the book, with consequences.** The folder comes out at E03, E21 and E44; the lie at
  E36; the disconfirmer at E46; the column at E35 and E48; Sal at E39.
- **The three that stay in:** Mara's two (E08, the account; E40, the lot) and Baz's Sal (E43, out at B03 E17).
- In a concealment mystery this cycle of withholding and disclosure is the engine, and it works. **It is also why
  B02–B03 cannot add a second layer of withholding without the trilogy going uniform (T1).**

**Exits where nothing is forgiven.** Three, and all are earned; keep them:
- **E39:** the blame is fresh, and nothing said repairs it.
- **E44:** Renée's claim is the book's strongest, and the truth still buys a statement with the lapse in it.
- **E46:** neither apologises, yet the truth told helps.
