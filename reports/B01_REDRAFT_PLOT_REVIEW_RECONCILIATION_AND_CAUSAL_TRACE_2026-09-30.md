## 2. The causal trace

### 2.1 Who wants what

The trace maps each main character's objective by act, as the page shows it. **The book's plots are carried by people
who want different things:**

| Character | What they pursue | What drives it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Seraphine** | The record, then protection | Atonement for Dré |
| **Lucien** | Knowing, and being right | His exposure over the *None* raises the stakes. In Act II and Act III, finding out who reads the city's line |
| **Baz** | Honest method: consent, the control, the dissent | Protecting Seraphine from a rumor, by going behind her back |
| **Mara** | A whole book: everyone fed, everyone on the list | — |
| **Caro** | Boundaries: her data, her login | Until she bends them |

**These objectives compete.** That competition is where the book's best conflict already lives.

### 2.2 The protective line, transition by transition

| Transition | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The calls and heat checks → the page** | **Loose** | The calls shape the page's terms (told and untold, her third column). But the page itself is Lucien's prediction from his own figures; the calls do not cause it |
| **The page → the trap** | **Causal as events, sequential as protection** | Seraphine's text that the case was put there *after* leads straight to the trap. But the trap is Lucien trying to find out who reads the city's line, not an attempt to protect anyone |
| **The trap → the gathering** | **Sequential** | The gathering really comes from E40 (the false hour and the crush at Mara's lot), E41 (people calm together in the hall, and her promise), Caro's data and Mrs. Carmouche's idea. **Those links are causal**, but they run from the rumor and the crush, not from the trap |
| **The gathering → the tins** | **Causal for choosing water, loose as protection** | Rings came first. Nobody says the rings could warn people, though E46 shows it |

**The verdict:** *Can they protect people?* is **Seraphine's question and her form of atonement**. **As written it is
not the book's governing question.** The progression of questions (what is it → can we predict it → what can we safely
do) is partly forced by discoveries, but at the protective joins a reader must supply the reason.

### 2.3 The trap

The trap is **Lucien's**. On the page it is knowledge-seeking about the watchers: he wants to see who comes. Baz's
objection names the cost to Sal. The pairing of E31 and E36 suggests control and a wish to be right, though the page
never names either.

**The trap works against protection.** It costs Sal's crew their work and costs Seraphine a night.

**That divergence is a strength.** It carries Lucien's arc, and it should not be rewritten as care. A book governed by
*can they protect people* is stronger with one protagonist pursuing something else, at protection's expense.

### 2.4 The minimal gaps

These are the gaps where a reason a reader must supply could be put on the page. **Each is a line or a beat; none is new
plot.**

1. **Why predicting would protect anyone.** Nobody says why knowing where and when would help people (the page, E26).
2. **Ursulines as a lesson.** The told man sits safely while the untold woman breaks her wrist. That never becomes a
   conclusion that drives Seraphine's next choice (E32 → E34).
3. **Kerlerec to her certainty.** The night on Kerlerec (no sleep, the rift with Lucien) is left to the reader as the
   cause of her ignoring her own doubt in E41.
4. **The crush reaching her late.** Mara keeps the E40 crush from Seraphine until she is already committed to the
   gathering. It is a real causal link, but hidden from her until too late, and from the reader's sense of her choice.
5. **Rings as a warning.** It is shown in E46 and never said (the positive rule, Q-P45-8).

## 3. The disclosures, the glass timeline and two ambiguities

### 3.1 Lucien's *None*: absent

- **She learns about the lie.** Seraphine is shown learning that the *low blood pressure* was a lie (E12, dramatized).
- **She never learns about the form.** No scene shows her learning he wrote *None* on it. She does not ask (E12). He has
  not told her that the city has his (E21). Only Baz is told of the correction letter (E36). Guidry names the *None* only
  to Lucien, by phone (E48).
- **So E48 has a continuity gap.** Lucien says *my letter* and she nods once, though nothing on the page has told her
  what the letter is.

**This is the strongest single finding across every review so far,** and the one verified continuity gap in the book's
central relationship (Q-P45-1).

### 3.2 The false-report chain (E39): summarized, with the reaction dramatized

- The telling is summarized.
- Her reaction is a scene.
- The change between them is dramatized that dawn, and carries into E46.

**§319 was wrong to list this as off the page.** At most the reaction could be weighed at the line edit. **Replaying the
chain would add repetition, not drama.**

### 3.3 The Ursulines decision: absent

- **E26's agreement covers one block,** Renita's.
- **The narrowing to Seraphine's list of households is implied** (E29, E32).
- **It is reported once,** as a fact rather than a choice (E34).
- **E48 never acknowledges it.**

**The decision itself is not on the page.** Q-P45-10 stands.

### 3.4 The glass notice: explained later

**Nothing before E32 establishes the notice:**
- no glass event;
- no notice;
- no reason for one.

The notice first appears at E32, already standing.

**E36 supplies the reason afterwards.** Baz's glass-shop tickets reach back six weeks and bunch closer toward the end,
which makes a city notice plausible, but only by inference.

**It is not a slip, and it is not a planted clue.** **Recommendation:** do not turn it into enemy foreknowledge; that
would create a new plot obligation, as the review warns. At most, one clause at E32 or E36 lets a reader connect them.

### 3.5 Two ambiguities, neither a contradiction

- **E10's *closed the file*** means a physical folder. **The ambiguity** comes from E48 repeating the same words to mean
  the case. One word fixes it.
- **Renée *asking* (E09) and *the mother's not asking* (E10)** are about different things: asking Odile, and asking the
  coroner. The page never marks the difference. A clause fixes it.
