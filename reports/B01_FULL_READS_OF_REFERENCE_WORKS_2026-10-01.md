**After two syntheses and a merge, about a hundred techniques reduce to fifteen strong ones.** Each was found
independently by several readers and across works. The strongest are:
- **A speech is heard as a tactic.** The listener grades it, compresses it or misses it. People reach a fear through its
  safe version first, and confessions come out wrong.
- **Care is specific:** a practical act in one pair's own currency, often hiding its own work. One person tracks one
  tell.
- **State the number once, then follow one case.** Summarize the attrition, and dramatize the contested choice.
- **The strange gets a size without a mechanism:**
  - a working rule learned at a cost, with its cause left open;
  - a proper test that comes back empty;
  - competing accounts that nobody certifies;
  - two senses that disagree.
- **Institutions arrive as one person** with a job to protect, and procedure outranks awe.
- **Status shows in forms of address and in where people stand.**
- **Description sees through the point of view's trade or fear:** what is missing, one ordinary detail, a second look.

**Each of the seven weaknesses named in B01's reviews has at least two strong answers:**
- over-articulate motives;
- tenderness spread evenly across everyone;
- counting in place of action;
- repeated house gestures;
- rests that only recover;
- protagonists as observers;
- the strange too vague.

**What the works do worse than Calder:**
- moral speeches and fluent self-diagnosis;
- friends diagnosing the protagonist in the same words;
- house refrains;
- joke density in crisis.

**Never borrowed, because it breaks a ruled limit:**
- mind-reading in any form: auras, scent, mind-voices, possession;
- explaining the mechanism;
- numbers as imagery;
- powers;
- the strange creating desire;
- another person or tense;
- a presence deciding a human outcome.

**On romance:**
- *Bride*'s slow-burn moves split cleanly. The test question, missing tells, care misread first, and permission in place
  of a declaration fit B01's ceiling.
- Its consent grammar inside intimacy, and its protective-lie rupture and staged repair, belong to the later books.
- Its possessiveness and its scent-as-desire belong to no book.

**One flag for the author:** one reader proposed that the strange answers mood. That is a world rule, not a craft
choice.
