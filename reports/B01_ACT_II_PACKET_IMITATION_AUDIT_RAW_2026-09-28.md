# Act II prose packets: imitation audit

## Verdict

**Ready after the listed changes.** Every change is to wording. None adds a scene, a beat or content that the EBCI brief does not have. Most of them *restore* brief material that derivation dropped.

The set is lean and mostly agency-forward: E18's *"on purpose"*, E19's argument, E27's commitment and E29's choice. No guard has been turned into a scheduled beat. US spelling is clean, and titles are marked as working labels. The problems are concentrated in four areas:

1. **Derivation dropped three of the brief's built-in disagreements:** E29 BT02, E25 BT03 and E24's *"about risk"*. These are exactly where Q-AR9 wants the methods to collide.
2. **E34 lists the Act I resolution template and forbids anything that would interrupt it.**
3. **Ends lines.** Too many of them pre-land a mood or a lesson. Three Baz episodes end on a written record, after an Act I that ended five times on a notebook entry.
4. **Leakage.** A handful of control-layer and future-knowledge items (E19, E23, E25, E26).

## Across the act

**D1. Ends-line uniformity.** All twenty episodes carry an Ends line, and Act I's rhyming quiet-image endings grew from lines of this kind. Several Act II Ends are moods or lessons rather than cut points: E21, E22, E26, E30 and E36. Recast lesson-exits as cut points (E22, E26 above) or omit them where *"What this scene is for"* already carries the state (E21). Act I's E14 shows that omission is allowed. Keep hard-cut Ends as they are: E27 revised, E29, E32 and E33.

**D2. A long resolved run.** From E21 to E31, eleven consecutive pieces end warm or resolved. In Act I the liveliest moments were failure, anger left unsoothed, and an ultimatum. The architecture fixes two of these resolutions (E24, E29); elsewhere the packets should not add a landing. The lever is the D1 fixes plus the restored disagreements, not new conflict scenes.

**D3. Written-record endings.** Act I ended on a notebook entry at E06, E08, E09, E11 and E17. Act II's Baz episodes lean the same way: E27 (*"in Baz's log"*), E33 (the log, approved) and probably E37. Fixing E27's Ends leaves the log as E33's earned image, not Baz's signature cadence.

**D4. Care after the event, four pieces long.** E34 (body care), E35 (community care), S05 (the Vein cleaning up) and E36 (friends) follow E33 consecutively. That is the Act I template stretched across a sequence. The E34 and E35 fixes keep each piece's way out distinct:

- E34: care received, with the bill paid in her body.
- E35: care given, beside measurement.
- S05: a room that arbitrates nothing.
- E36: a Lucien Baz may not like.

Watch this run at the checkpoint.

**D5. Food settings.** Six packets name eating or cooking: E18, E19, E21, E22, E34 and E36. Several are optional options in [P] places, and asking whether someone has eaten was retired after its Act I payoff. Drop the optional ones (E19, E21, E36); keep E18 (Baz's want), E22 (the working setting) and E34 (architecture, but not listed).

**D6. Methods, and who owns them.** Baz holds nine of the twenty episodes, including the synthesis (E25), the prediction (E27), the event (E33) and the close (E37). Lucien has two solo POVs (E26, E32). Q-AR9's *"adds a method without replacing Lucien's"* is at risk at the level of POV assignment, and derivation also cut two Lucien/Baz collisions (E25, E29). The fixes restore the collisions; the POV question is the author's (item 1).

**D7. The Velvet Vein.** S04, E31 and S05 are three Vein pieces in eight. Both Act II supplements are the Vein, where Act I's were a Vein document, a news brief and care elsewhere. The EBCI fixes this, so it is not a packet fault. S05's vignette form is the differentiator.

**D8. Witnesses who disagree.** The engine appears in E20, E22, E35, S05 and possibly E37. It was an Act I strength, and each instance has its own purpose. No change is needed beyond S05's form.

**D9. Guards.** Agency is present or permitted almost everywhere. Casework is stated once (E22) and scheduled nowhere, which is correct; only E34 forbade it. No packet turns a guard into a beat.

**D10. Lucien loosening.** Act I's E12 and Act II's E31 and E36 all reveal a looser Lucien through someone watching. E31 is approved, so the fix goes on E36: the direction stays open.
