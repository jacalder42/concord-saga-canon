## 1. What was done

- **Revision.** Six reviser groups worked on 24 chapters, under a private handoff carrying the eighteen approved holds
  and the III-2 caution:
  - Act I: E01, E03, E06, E09, E10, E12–E15;
  - Act II: E18, E21, E22, E26, E27, E29, E32, E34–E36;
  - Act III: E39, E42, E45, E46, E48, E49.
- **Method.** Each change applied an audit row, a hold, or the act's descriptive-text deficit:
  - reception, posture, hands, faces, clothes and distance, placed at the turn of a scene rather than at its opening;
  - misreads where the audits found repair without cause.
- **No labels.** No emotion was labelled and no new mutual understanding added.
- **Length.** The working draft goes from 119,749 to 123,973 words (+3.5%), the first growth in the draft-3 series.
  The author had allowed it.
- **Over the +8% guide:** E09 (+10.7%), E26 (+14.1%), E35 (+16.8%) and E36 (+9.8%). In each case the holds needed the
  room.

## 3. Verification

- **Blind dialogue reads.** Two readers saw only the prose, split E00–E27 and E28–E50, and answered the brief's eight
  questions (private, manuscript `draft-notes/draft3/review/STAGING_BLIND_READ_A.md`, `_B.md`).
  - **Both found:** distinct people; misreads that come from character; conflicts that change behavior; consequences
    that survive scenes. Reader A singled out E15's release heard as a dismissal and E26's misreads; Reader B singled
    out the E46 table → E48 reversal.
  - **Reader B also found:** disclosures about wrongs done to people read as extracted (E44–E46). The disclosures about
    the hidden organization (E48 on the steps, the river, E49) still read as delivered: prepared, question and answer.
  - **Residue, both readers:**
    - the older New Orleans women share one register;
    - Elisabet's flirt lines repeat one device at scene ends;
    - *"I know"* closes too many exchanges;
    - epigram exits, and *opened her mouth and closed it*;
    - about twenty narration lines name a feeling the scene had already shown. Most predate this pass; the pass added
      none by design.
  - **Neither reader found anyone made artificially stupid.** The borderline cases are Lucien's E20 defense of the
    hold and his easy acceptance at E24.
- **Continuity check** (`draft-notes/draft3/continuity/STAGING_PASS_CHECK.md`).
  - **Two one-word slips fixed:**
    - E29 placed E26's drawing at a porch; it was the wall.
    - E36 left *He* ambiguous; it is now *Lucien*.
  - **One formatting fix:** E46 had two run-together quotations, each now separated by a beat.
  - **Every chain holds:**
    - the days (E46 Wednesday; E48 Friday);
    - E26 → E48;
    - E36 → E46, with no warm Baz–Lucien exchange between;
    - the E46 table → E48;
    - E39's *nothing* against its callbacks;
    - no callback to any cut line;
    - all the guards.
- **Judgment calls left for the author:**
  - E43's teasing text from Baz sits inside the cool stretch. It also works as his cover, so keep it, or trim the joke.
  - E49's new detail that her late messages were to Lucien adds romantic weight.
  - E48's PROTECT river self-analysis (the reviser's flag).
