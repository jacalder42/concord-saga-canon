## 2. Claude's self-check

The author judges; this is only for the record.

**1. Can a novelist begin without the repo?** Yes, assuming they have drafted the book so far, as the
act-by-act workflow intends. E31 has the four people and the host. E33 has what was predicted, where
they stand and what is fixed.

**2. Is what matters protected?**
- **E31:** no strangeness, no prediction talk, no threat, no declarations, Elisabet absent, the
  musician carrying nothing.
- **E33:** no cause, no spread, no light or impossible shapes, no name, no breathing or grounding
  stopping it, no whole-city helping. Lucien is seen from outside. Observation is kept apart from
  meaning.

**3. What is still unwritten?**
- **E31:** everything: what the night is like, what is said, the music, who loosens and how.
- **E33:** how the evening feels before, the order people are met in, what Seraphine does with each,
  Baz's handwriting, the sound of a house of windows.

**4. Is anything there because the database knows it?** Two lines are candidates for cutting:
- E31's *"if she is named she is Lacuna"*: it protects canon hygiene if prose names her, but the
  packet would still work without it.
- E33's *"Baz sees what Lucien does, not what he perceives"*: it restates *"How Baz sees"*.

Both are kept for the review, so the author can cut them.

**5. Would a novelist want to write it?** That is the author's question.
