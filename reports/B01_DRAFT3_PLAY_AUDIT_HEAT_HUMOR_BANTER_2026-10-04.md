## 1. Method

- **Act audits.** Three act auditors read every chapter against the author's brief: no quotas; benchmarks used for
  function only; existing successes protected. About 300 scene rows are classified on the brief's scales.
- **Synthesis.** It spot-checked the act audits against the chapters and corrected eight act-level claims. Examples:
  the shared retort occurs 9 times, not 11; E35's crawfish exchange is already on the page.

## 2. Diagnosis

- **The auditors' verdict.** B01 has social appetite, rationed rather than suppressed. About 90% of rows are PROTECT.
- **Where play lives.** Roughly 46% of banter scenes are between primary characters. The rest are primary with
  secondary characters, or the community among themselves: elders, clerks, Sal, Inez, Miss Tavie. The community is the
  book's constant comic floor.
- **The leads' arc.** Their play is built in E04–E13 and peaks at E19 and E30. By E32 it has gone to silence and
  distance. It has one last run at E39, then turns into procedure, terms and measurement. By E49 they have attention
  without play: damage B02 inherits.
- **Already working:**
  - **Heat in restraint.** It runs through the procedural scenes (the doorframe, the chalk walk, the held inch on the
    curb, the chair at the Dorseys').
  - **Failed attempts.** About a dozen already mark damage, including E29, E33, E35/E36 (a mirrored pair), E39 and
    E46.
  - **Humor under pressure.** It comes before and after each event, never during, which fits the gallows rule.
- **Set pieces.** E13, E19, E30 and E42 are protected. No new one is recommended.
- **Real defects, all small:**
  - Elisabet's flirt formula (a verdict on Lucien's argument fused with a remark on his looks) appears five times.
    The fix is cuts.
  - Lucien's attention goes unused during the E15 route walk.
  - Seraphine's own attraction has one Act I line; her behavior at E13 carries the rest.
  - One retort is shared by six speakers.
- **Scale.** 13 interventions, 4 of them HIGH (the E15 beat and the Elisabet cuts, which are one decision). The
  recommended set comes to about +100 to +250 words. Applying everything would add about +650 to +1,250.

## 3. A caution on this verdict

- **Who audited.** The auditors are the same kind of reader that wrote and revised the draft. They judged the play
  present in the current text, not the play the text no longer makes room for.
- **ChatGPT's review disagrees.** Its independent review of the 225k manuscript, forwarded 10-04 as supplementary
  evidence, argues that compression removed the runway for play:
  - the ordinary subjects (food, work, equipment, bureaucracy) that give characters something to play with;
  - care and restraint as heat;
  - scenes where a lead stops carrying the plot.
- **Where the audits agree with it.** All three act audits found that play lives mainly with the community cast, and
  that the leads' play is thinnest where plot information is delivered between them.
- **What follows.** *Rationed by design* and *thinned by compression* can both be true. The compression archaeology
  audit (ledger §395) tests which. **The author should treat Q-PA1–10 as provisional until it reports**, because its
  findings may change the size and kind of the play interventions.
