## 1. What was done

  - one per lead per act (Seraphine ×3, Lucien ×3, Baz ×2);
  - Caro for the whole book;
  - a company actor per act for the minor parts.

  Each re-performed only their own character's lines and small business against the revised draft, as proposals:
  about 160, mostly rewordings and cuts.
- **Directors (10):** merged the proposals by chapter group.
  - 139 taken, 16 adapted, 4 declined.
  - 71 gestures cut.
  - Narration that stated a feeling the scene already showed was cut.
- **Pilot results applied:**
  - E39 and E46 keep the revised draft, with the table read's moments grafted in.
  - E48's river scene is rebased on the table read, with its choreography cut.
  - E39's three continuity items are fixed.
- **Continuity:** three checkers, one per act. Four definite and seven probable findings, all fixed.
- **Length:** 174,407 → 173,341 words (−0.6%).

## 2. Blind comparison

Four judges compared six chapters (E03, E21, E29, E39, E46, E48), revised against performed, in randomised A/B order.
Two of the judges used six criteria; two were asked which version sounds less artificial.

**Result: 12 of 12 judgments prefer the performed draft.**

The judges also found that the directors had over-cut some physical beats that carried weight. Seven were restored.

## 3. Findings

1. **The screenplay model works where full actor play did not.** Re-performing against a draft kept the draft's
   argued speech, and cut the epigrams, explanatory tails and stated feelings the judges had flagged.
2. **Actors propose little.** Most of their lines already sounded like the person, and the gains came from cuts and
   rewordings, not additions.
3. **A director's gesture cap can overshoot.** Beats that carry weight (a coffee set down on the ledger, hands on a
   folder, a napkin folded before a hedge) should stay. The cap is for gestures that repeat or label.
4. **Cross-chapter echoes are the main risk.** Every definite break was a line quoted or answered elsewhere (E18 →
   E46) or a join left by a cut gesture (E09, E42). Checkers should grep every changed line book-wide.

## 4. Recommendation

- Use the screenplay model **at generation** for B02 onward, as a pass after each chapter is drafted. Keep full actor
  play for pressure scenes.
- Use it **in revision** for B01, if the author prefers the performed draft after his side-by-side read.
