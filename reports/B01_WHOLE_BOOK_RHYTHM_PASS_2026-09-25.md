## 2. What v4.1b did with the ECG's recommendations

### The headline recommendation was implemented, guardrail included

Deep matrix Pass 2 named **positive anticipation** as the book's *"missing category"* and
proposed **P1A**: seed a Velvet Vein social event early, pay it later, with a *"critical
guardrail: do not automatically attack or destroy the event. The pleasure itself is the
payoff."*

v4.1b contains exactly that chain:

| | v4.1b |
| --- | --- |
| Seed | **E06 "THE ROOM IS STILL OPEN"** — *"establish the human hearth before crisis owns every scene"*; Trip introduced through hospitality work |
| Anticipation | **E28 (LR08) "PLANS FOR AFTER"** — *"create positive anticipation and prove characters assume a future"* |
| Payoff | **E31 (LR09) "THE NIGHT THEY WERE GOING TO HAVE"** — *"actually pay the promised pleasant night"* |
| Guardrail | E31 carries **"HARD GUARDRAIL — Do not attack the event. Do not insert a plot alert to justify the scene."** |

**The guardrail survived into the architecture nearly verbatim.** That is a complete
recommendation → implementation chain, and it closes the ECG's largest structural
opportunity.

### The count correction propagated

The three-layer ECG corrected 47 → **48 narrative episodes** and said *"do not preserve the
erroneous 47 count."* v4.1b is 48. **Closed.**

### The middle-density worry is structurally mitigated

Pass 2 warned that **v3 E16–E22** must not become seven consecutive talk-shaped episodes. In
v4.1b those seven are **E20, E22, E23, E24, E25, E27, E29** — and the Life/Reward layer
interleaves **E21 (LR06), E26 (LR07), E28 (LR08)** among them. **The run the ECG feared no
longer exists as a run.** Its execution-differentiation advice still applies at prose level
and is not superseded.

## 3. The rhythm reading, with data the 09-21 reports could not have

Sources: census Pass 1 slot classifications, Pass 3 kind tags, Pass 2's order-invariant
measurements, and the v4.1b Life/Reward layer.

### Life/Reward — the layer does what it was built to do

**All 12 Life/Reward episodes are census slot-L.** Not one is candidate-bearing. The layer is
uniformly non-plot-carrying, exactly as designed, and the census reached that independently.

Their story jobs cover item 4's dimensions directly: grief-time (E03), routine (E12), trust
under ordinary conditions (E14), personhood (E18), **competence pleasure** (E21), **wonder
without punishment** (E26), **anticipation** (E28), its **payoff** (E31), **bodily recovery**
(E34), friendship (E37), **exhaustion humour** (E42), **late non-threatening strangeness**
(E44).

### Reward is not confined to the Life/Reward layer

**Eight causal-spine episodes are also slot-L** — E06, E10, E19, E22, E32, E38, E41, E46 —
and three are marked **PROTECTED** or **ABSOLUTELY PROTECTED** in the bible: E32 *"negative
space before payoff"*, E41 *"make aftermath human before attempting another solution"*, E46
*"negative-space climax beat."*

**So 20 of 48 episodes carry lived/negative-space work, and only 12 of those are the
Life/Reward layer.** Reward, recovery and quiet are load-bearing inside the spine itself.
Any future compression that treats "the LR episodes" as the book's slack would be cutting
into the wrong twelve.

### Density: every dense run is immediately relieved

Three runs of three consecutive candidate-bearing slots exist — **E07–E09, E15–E17,
E23–E25** — and each is immediately followed by lived space:

| Run | What follows |
| --- | --- |
| E07–E09 | **E10** *"Don't carry it alone"* — causal spine, slot-L, `care` |
| E15–E17 | **E18 (LR05)** *"Baz gets to arrive"* |
| E23–E25 | **E26 (LR07)** *"It is beautiful for a minute"* |

**The book never sustains a fourth consecutive candidate slot.** Combined with Pass 2's
finding that the longest run of consecutive slot-L is **2**, the alternation is tight in both
directions: no dense stretch, no dead stretch.

### The austere sequence is intentional, and the data confirms it

Pass 2's longest run without a candidate slot is **E41–E44** — *"after the breaking"*
(PROTECTED) → exhaustion humour (LR12) → grounding fails → *"something strange that doesn't
hurt"* (LR13) — landing immediately on **E45 The Rebound**.

That is precisely the three-layer ECG's austere sequence, in v3 numbering **E33→E36**, and
its stated reasoning — *"LR13 provides wonder immediately before Rebound"* — is verifiable in
the current architecture. **Confirmed as design, not drift.**

### Kind mix

Across the 28 candidate slots: **evidential 10, relational 4, civic 4, institutional 3,
physical 3, perceptual 2, decisional 1, care 1** (Pass 3).

`evidential` is the dominant register, concentrated at **E16, E20, E25, E27, E35, E39, E47**.
Pass 1's split/merge tests already question the E25/E27 and E39/E47 pairs; the tag
concentration is a second reason to look. **Not a defect** — an investigation book should be
evidential — but the one place where "restates rather than escalates" is most likely to hide.

## 4. What this pass does not measure, and why

**Romance, character load and location recurrence are not measured here.** The census tags
what principally changes per slot, not who is present or where. Measuring them properly needs
a per-episode presence-and-place table that does not exist — the same gap Pass 4 of the cast
work found, where **26 of 57 registry rows have an empty `range`**. Deep matrix Pass 2
already reports cast bandwidth and location findings **for v4**; those stand until someone
builds the table, and this pass does not restate them as if re-verified.

**Threat is not measured**, because threat level depends on the causal questions that **D5**
holds. Pass 3's `physical` tag counts **primary slot tags only** and is not a threat measure.

**No prose-position or density-along-the-body measure is attempted**, because those need the
**E36/E37 and E43/E44 order decision** that ruling D7 holds. Everything above is
order-invariant.
