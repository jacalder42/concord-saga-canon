## 3. What converged

**Strengths (all ten):**
- Ch 39, Renée's kitchen;
- Ch 1;
- Ch 36, the silence;
- Ch 10, the Square;
- the community cast;
- the Kerlerec consequence chain;
- an honest investigation;
- an earned personal ending;
- the slow romance.

**Problems:**
1. **"The Veil" arrives cold** at Ch 38 and the Epilogue (10 of 10). Nine want it set up earlier; one wants it cut.
2. **The middle repeats one shape** (10 of 10), mainly Ch 15–27 and Ch 31–37: event, triage, an argument at a table, a written line.
3. **The opposition is foggy or late** (9 of 10). The boxes have no face with a want, and six readers ask for the woman in cream shoes to want something.
4. **Prose tics** (9 of 10). Measured counts:
   - *as if* / *as though*: 84;
   - *face down*: 17;
   - *hand flat*: 11.
5. **One convenient meeting** (8 readers): Caro meeting Elisabet (Ch 20).
6. **Smaller points raised repeatedly:**
   - Mr. Ledet and Mr. Lionel are confused (5 readers).
   - Mrs. Batson's thread is unresolved (7 readers).
   - *"If I falter, everything collapses"* reads as imported (6 readers).
   - No positive answer about the phenomenon (most readers).

**The hostile case:**
- The book is plotted by confession more than by investigation.
- Caldas asks and leaves, and we never see what his people do to someone they decide "felt it first".
- The single change the hostile reader recommends: show, in one scene before the festival, what the askers do to a person.

## 5. Checked against the page

- **False:** "Caldas first speaks at about 47%" (Critic B). He speaks at Poydras in Ch 14, at about 34%, and is first named in Ch 19.
- **Partly true:**
  - **The Epilogue's "felt them take it":** it refers to the Ch 38 fence moment, but no felt event is marked there.
  - **The 24 March model "dropped":** it is never named again, though Inez's records request comes back redacted.
  - **Elisabet "absent after the midpoint":** she has a call in Ch 29 and a text in Ch 37.
- **New defects found:**
  - Mr. Lionel "raised his good hand" in Ch 34, though his injury is an ankle. That is Mr. Ledet's gesture.
  - Ch 32's "Nobody's glass went till the tenth of April" contradicts Ch 27.
  - Four chapters open on a Wednesday, and Ch 34 lacks its date.
- **A packet conflict:**
  - The packets require Baz's Ch 25 lie to say *"The boxes"* word for word.
  - But Q-ST5 has the names coined at Ch 26.
  - The page follows the packet, so the word appears a chapter early.
