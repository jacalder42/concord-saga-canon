### 2.1 Standalone novel shape

**The shape:**
- **Opening problem:** the strangeness becomes the city's shared normal. E03 gives it a word; E10 and
  E14 give Baz a method that can fail.
- **Escalation:** E15; then one block (E22); then two institutional prices (E23, E24).
- **Consequential choices:** Seraphine refuses the list (E25), and the group sends the grounds (E26).
- **Climax:** the cluster (E29), then E44, then Lucien's withholding (E47).
- **Changed end state:**
  - the folk rule is broken;
  - the model has changed category (E46);
  - the zone is standing and wider.

**What makes it a novel, not a bridge**, in the reader's words: *"three withholdings (the map at E24,
the list at E25, drift at E47) and one release (the grounds at E26). The one thing released escapes
their control (E34), and the last thing withheld costs households (E47)."*

**Notes:**
- Act III carries trilogy threads that do not pay off inside B02: drift (E40, E42, S04) and the brush
  (E41).
- **E44 is watched, not acted.** B02's Act III climax is **E47**, Lucien's decision (Veil audit
  amendments §1), not E44, so the reader's *"passive Act III peak"* overstates the case. It still
  bears on Seraphine (§2.2).

### 2.2 Seraphine's agency

**Her choices, and they are real:**
- E09: she takes on the confidences;
- E19: she will not force calm;
- **E25: she refuses the list;**
- E28–E29: she respects Rosette's refusal;

**E25's consequences are visible, and they cut both ways:**
- E27: the oxygen resident goes without his concentrator;
- E31: the costs are counted through her;

- E38: the book she kept becomes evidence.

**Note: after E33 her agency drains.** E35 is perception. E38 carries the book at Rosette's wish. At
E44 she waits. This is a design question (Q-B2-1), not a defect.

### 2.3 Knowledge economy

**Strong start:**

- E07 turns it into *"similar, not synchronized"*.

### 2.4 Relationship progression

**Caro and Elisabet: not a B01 replay.** The ladder runs:
- E05: consent;
- E30: the reversal, Caro looking after Elisabet;
- E36: an admission of wanting.

Caro's carrying is never turned, and that is by design (M54 protects her first handoff for B05).

**Seraphine and Lucien: new rungs, but one gap.** E12 (witnessing) and E21 (listening) are new rungs,
not B01's grounding. **After E25, when she refuses the request he carried, the pair has no scene
together in B02.** B03 E18 carries the next rung (*"he listens, better than in B02"*). The first test
of B01's trust goes unexamined inside B02 (Q-B2-2).

**Baz: a person, not a mentor.** He is a person throughout (E01, E04, E18, E45). **He is not a mentor
in B02**: he has no mentee, and Shirin and Elisabet are his peers. The architecture gives him none,
and **no arc should be manufactured for symmetry** (FB interpretations). This is reported to the
author because the author named it (Q-B2-4).

### 2.6 Lucien's E40 → E47 chain

**The motivation holds:**
- E08 plants the self-justification: *"holding back is care"*.
- E23 makes the grounds the price.
- E26 sends the method as grounds.
- E34 shows what that cost: the method travelled, with unknowable effect. This echoes Elisabet's
  E24 principle.
- E40 gives him a second model, and nobody has asked for it yet.
- **At E47 the withholding is earned:** *"unproven"* is his stated reason, and **E34 is the real
  one.** In the reader's words, *"coherent, not mandated."*

**Legibility, fixed (§3):** *"wider than the lean requires"* stated a counterfactual as narrator
fact, in a book built on keeping observation and inference apart.
