## 1. What was built

**All 51 pieces were drafted from a blank page** from:

- the continuity sheet;

Draft 3 was reference only. Rate limits stopped the run twice; it was finished with fewer writers in parallel.

### Echo

Early writers echoed draft 3 even when they read it only for facts. This was measured as the share of a chapter's words
that sit in six-word runs shared with draft 3's same chapter. Sixteen chapters stood at 12–44%.

A de-echo pass brought every chapter under about 8%; most are now around 1–3%. What remains is mostly fixed text.

**The pass has a cost.** The blind reads found:
- forced inversions in Act I;
- one stiff chapter, E26, which was rewritten sentence by sentence from 44%.

## 3. The blind comparison

**Method:** one experienced reader per act read both versions in full, labelled only X and Y, with no access to anything
else. In every act the experiment was X. The random draw gave the same assignment each time; each reader was independent.

| Act | Experiment, reader / editor | Draft 3, reader / editor | Chapters (exp. / d3 / even) | Recommended base |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 4.5 / 4 | 4 / 3.5 | 8 / 0 / 7 (E07 split) | **Experiment**, with draft 3's lines and set-ups imported, one line pass, trims to E03, E04, E14 |
| II | 4.5 / 4 | 4 / 4 | 13 / 6 / 1 | **Experiment**, with 12 draft-3 passages, including draft 3's E26 |
| III | 4 / 3.5 | 4 / 4 | 7 / 4 / 3 | **Draft 3**, with about ten experiment passages (3–4k words) |

**Where the experiment wins:**
- secondary characters with their own errands, who push back;
- the strange made concrete;
- place.

The readers judged most of the extra length to earn its place in Acts I–II, and about half of it in Act III.

**Where draft 3 wins:**
- a cleaner line;
- sharper key exchanges;
- the act-to-act set-ups;
- above all, the climax of Act III.

**The experiment's main defects:**
- **Act III's evidence chain is broken.** Mrs. Whitlow's *rings before legs* is set up, but the lot's event does not
  pay it, so the E48 water plan is unmotivated.
- **Artifacts of the de-echo pass:** forced inversions in Act I, and a stiff E26.
- **Recurring tics:** *flat on*, *a moment longer than it needed*.
- **A few slips.**
