**Moving the window to the Monday after Easter changed the answer:** the Marigny ring, which on the pre-redraft calendar
fell inside the window, now falls before it. **Ursulines is the first qualifying event**, and the rule's second miss
(*something outside them and nothing in*) never triggers, because something did come in.

**How forecast A survives the move.** On the pre-redraft calendar, D3 read the page as *satisfied on Saturday* (the
Marigny), with Ursulines then falsifying what it rested on. On the redraft's calendar the Marigny can no longer satisfy
it, so **the technical success and the practical inadequacy fall on the same event**: Ursulines is right on the window
and the place, and wrong on everything the page told them to watch for, on *larger*, and on the hurt it never
counted. That is still A's shape (technical success, then practical inadequacy), and the drafted E32 already scores it
this way. D3's *any* against *next* question also closes: with the Marigny outside the window, *the next* qualifying
event and *any* qualifying event are the same one.
