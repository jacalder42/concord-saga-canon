# B01 compression: the five-packet verification

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Status:** EDITORIAL REPORT (production). The Q-FB4 condition check
(`decisions/B01_FULL_AUDIT_COMPRESSION_AND_CALENDAR_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-27.md`). **Result: not cleanly
passed. B02 EBCI is not started**; the call is put to the author (§4).

What it does not change: no ruling, card, milestone, breadcrumb or architecture. The briefs changed
between rounds (§3); control layers did not, except where beat numbers moved.

## 1. Method

Three blind reads by independent agents playing an experienced novelist and editor. Each saw **only
the narrative briefs** of E13, E31, E38, E45 and E48 (control layers stripped). Rounds 1 and 2 also
saw the pre-compression briefs (`6e9f3e1`) for comparison. Each put the author's question to every
brief: *"Do these make me want to write the scenes, or do they make me feel that the scenes have
already been written badly in outline form?"* A brief also failed if it was too thin to act on or
dropped a guard a writer needs. Each round used a fresh reader who had not seen the earlier rounds.

## 3. What changed between rounds

**Round 1's findings**, accepted and fixed:
- **The compression had cut guards and carry-forwards along with the staging.** The general lesson is
  now template rule 9: *cut staging, not guards*.
- **E48's stranger.** The compression had moved *"a street musician at the Square's edge"* into
  writer options. It is approved design (Q-E48-2), so moving it was **a compression error**; it is
  restored to the brief.
- **E48's other restorations:** the off-centre onset, *never to the second*, and who takes the rest of
  the crowd.
- **E45:** *characters may wonder aloud and be disagreed with* (so the prohibition is not over-read),
  and Lucien's wound.
- **E13:** the grounding that E45 pays off.
- **E31:** who goes, and *nobody raises the predicted window*.
- **E38:** the three readings of the hum, and the cast-face guard.

**The same kind of loss was swept across the other 44 briefs.** Restored in compact form:
- E04's south-west lean
- E15's *characters may offer fault, casualty or coincidence*, and *pulse* loosely or nothing
- E17: Baz is never a subordinate
- E23: no employer named
- E28: never jealousy
- E29: the swamp wound is never named, located or linked
- E30: no magical calming or mechanics lesson
- E34: Seraphine's E33 saturation lands here
- E42: no jokes at suffering's expense
- E44: no grid
- E47: no single site, crowd or institution is causal

The recurring-face guards became one template rule (*cast discipline*) instead of a dozen repeats.

**Round 2's findings**, accepted and fixed:
- **Repetition:** each idea was stated in Want, Change, Keep and a beat. This is now part of template
  rule 1: *say each thing once*.
- **E38 had no human stake.** It now reads: *"one day that goes as planned"*, and Seraphine has started
  planning around disruption without noticing, and it comes anyway, somewhere else `[P]`.
- **S06** gets back *the bar still has to open tomorrow*.

**Round 3's findings**, recorded and **not** fixed, per the stop:
- **E13:** *"the collision"* is undefined, and the mirrored beats read as a schema. v4.1b itself says
  only *"separate coping failures collide"*.
- **E48:** it does not say whether the stranger is E31's musician (see Q-V3).
- **Across the set:** the Wants are functional, not personal. What a writer knows most clearly is what
  not to do.

Beat rows: **115** (E13 3, E31 2, E38 2, E45 4, E48 3; the ECID curves remapped).

## 4. Questions for the author

- **Q-V1: the gate.** Read the five narrative briefs yourself: `ebci/B01/S1.T1.B01.A1.E13.md`, `…A2.E31.md`,
  `…A3.E38.md`, `…A3.E45.md`, `…A3.E48.md`, down to `## Control layer`. **Recommendation:** treat the
  format as solved, on the decision's own §3 test, since all three readers found the set substantially
  more inviting. Then release B02 EBCI, fixing E13 as in Q-V2. The alternative is another compression
  round first.
- **Q-V2: E13's collision.** **Recommendation:** name it as prose's choice, e.g. *"their failures meet
  (where and how is prose's) [P]"*, and let the After line carry the episode. The alternative is to
  define it, which would be new design.
- **Q-V3: E48's stranger and E31's musician.** E31's musician is Lacuna's hidden cameo (BC-LACUNA-CAMEO,
  LOCKED). E48's stranger is ruled *not E15's performer and not a child*, and E31 is not mentioned.
  **Is the E48 stranger someone other than E31's musician?** This is the author's to answer. The
  recommendation is *yes, someone else*: the cameo carries zero portent.
- **Q-V4: functional Wants.** **Recommendation:** leave them. Want is already optional, and a personal
  want the architecture does not give would be invention. A writer finds the hunger; the brief should
  not assign it.
