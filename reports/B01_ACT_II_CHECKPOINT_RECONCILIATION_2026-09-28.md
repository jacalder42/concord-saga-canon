## 3. The forecast (analysis: `FORECAST_LOGIC.md`)

**The page's terms:**
- **Where:** a bounded area.
- **When:** ten days.
- **Size:** *"bigger than Honoré's: more than one block held at once, or longer than eight minutes"*.
- **What counts:** only what one of them sees, or an untold witness.
- **Three ways of being wrong.**
- **A promised written verdict.**

**Where it breaks:**
- E32 records that the Saturday Marigny hold met the size test.
- E33 then scores Tuesday's Ursulines event as *"the second way of being wrong"*, and credits the page with things it
  never said.
- The Marigny blocks probably lie outside the page's lines as written, but the characters treat them as inside.
- The page never says whether it predicts *any* qualifying event or *the next* one.

**Three coherent readings.** Each lists its minimal phrase changes in E27, E29, E32, E33 and E35:

| Reading | What counts | Outcome | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A: technical success, failure to prepare** (the review's preferred outcome) | The Marigny counts: the lines include it, and *bigger* is measured by her edges and time | The page is **satisfied Saturday**. Tuesday falsifies what the page rests on (*each larger than the last*) and everything it said to watch for | E33 loses its sharpest turn; the log's *hit* moves to Saturday; E29 carries a hit its brief never assigned |
| **B: the lines as written** | The Marigny is outside | Wrong by ways 2 and 3: **a near miss by a block** | Two character claims invert. The miss must be felt, or it reads as a gotcha |
| **C: a rise is not *it*** | The Marigny is a rise (nobody stopped); *held* means people held | Tuesday is the only event: **right time and place, wrong everything else**. E33 stands almost as written | No technical success |

- **Baz's expected sound stays outside every reading.** It is *"mine. It isn't on the page."*
- **All three satisfy the architecture's E33 line** (*"Time and place are right; everything else is wrong"*). Under A,
  the log must name Saturday as the hit.
- **The written verdict under the three names is still owed.** It is an Act III thread whatever the reading.

## 5. A production change (proposed)

**A continuity check between episodes.**
- **When:** after each drafted piece.
- **How:** a separate checker reads the new piece against the running continuity record, the reconciled calendar and
  the open threads (deadlines, the forecast, who knows what).
- **What it reports:** drift to Claude before the next stack is built.
- **What it never touches:** the drafting stack. It never carries the stylistic watch-list into drafting.

It would have caught Baz's arrival, the calendar and the forecast drift as they happened.

**Recommended:** adopt it from Act III on.
