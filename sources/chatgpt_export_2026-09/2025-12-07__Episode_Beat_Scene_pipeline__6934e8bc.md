# **EPISODE → BEAT → SCENE PIPELINE (Restated, 2 paragraphs)**

An **Episode** is the saga’s primary structural unit (1.3–1.8k words) and acts as a self-contained movement of narrative intent. Each Episode declares a **SID** (structural placement) and an **ECID** (POV, environment, corridor tier, weather, mode, heat, FX level, resonance state, audience). It includes 3–6 **Beat Packets**, each tagged with a **BID** and built to produce ≥3 changes (character, plot, relationship, veil, symbol), with ≥1 visible on the page. Episodes define their pressure map, Melody/Harmony/Rhythm/Jazz/Intent, and exit logic.

A **Beat** is the actionable craft layer—what actually *happens*—and carries symbolic, emotional, and mechanical weight. Beats route through corridor/weather constraints, respect UARS cost, world logic, emotional engines, romance/heat rules (when applicable), and use only 1–2 motifs from the Symbol & Motif System. **Scenes** are the smallest container and inherit ECID unless overridden; they express the beat through close/mid/wide camera, POV voice rules, blocking, and micro-changes. Scenes must deliver visible change while never violating mechanics or metaphysics.
