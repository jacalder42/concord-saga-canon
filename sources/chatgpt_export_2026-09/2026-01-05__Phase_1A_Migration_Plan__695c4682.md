## 1. Authority Statement

**Mechanica v4** is the authoritative mechanical framework governing **resonance physics, action cost, escalation limits, and environmental interaction** across the Concord Saga.

It supersedes and replaces all prior drafts, partial systems, and experimental mechanics, including but not limited to:

- Mechanica v1–v3
- Early Veil Mechanics drafts
- Shard Mechanics v1
- Breathable Veil v1
- Any scene- or character-specific “power logic” not aligned with v4

If a contradiction exists between Mechanica v4 and any other document, **Mechanica v4 prevails**.

---

## 2. What Mechanica Governs

Mechanica defines **how the world works under emotional pressure**.

Specifically, it governs:

- Resonance as a **human-scale emotional physics**, not magic
- The **cost of action** under resonance pressure (UARS)
- Environmental modifiers (materials, humidity, crowds, cities)
- Corridor ecology (U1–U7)
- Resonance weather (W0–W4)
- Resonance states (CALM → BLOOM → SHARD → RUPTURE → NODE)
- Shard progression and collapse behavior
- Channel separation rules (MT / VT / LT)
- Post-Mending filtration and Echo Node behavior
- Constraints on ascension, Intent, and manufactured entities
- Symbol → action permissions (VFX ceilings)

Mechanica exists to **prevent power creep, narrative cheating, and metaphysical hand-waving**.

---

## 3. What Mechanica Explicitly Does *Not* Govern

Mechanica does **not** govern:

- Character psychology or motivation (that is narrative)
- Moral outcomes or “rightness”
- Plot choices or story direction
- Dialogue content
- Romance dynamics beyond cost/pressure constraints
- Authorial symbolism not tied to action or consequence

Mechanica constrains **possibility and cost**, not meaning.

---

## 4. Core Design Principles

### 4.1 Resonance Is Not Magic
- Resonance is **emotional physics**, shaped by:
  - Will
  - Emotion
  - Intent
- It does not grant wish fulfillment.
- It always exacts a cost.

### 4.2 Cost Is Mandatory
- Every resonance-influenced action carries:
  - physical cost
  - psychological cost
  - relational cost
  - environmental or technological fallout
- Costs must be **visible on the page**.

### 4.3 Escalation Is Earned
- Power escalation follows:
  - pressure accumulation
  - emotional stakes
  - environmental conditions
- No character may “jump tiers” without consequence.

### 4.4 Environment Matters
- Where something happens is as important as:
  - who is acting
  - what they intend
- Cities, corridors, weather, materials, and crowds materially affect outcomes.

### 4.5 Channels Are Separate
- MT ≠ VT ≠ LT
- Resonance **never transmits digitally**
- Technology may only distort, degrade, or fail under pressure

---

## 5. Trilogy Escalation Model (Mechanical)

Mechanica behaves differently across eras:

### Veil Trilogy
- Subtle resonance effects
- Low amplitude
- High suppression
- Minimal visible geometry
- Early shard precursors only

### Neon Trilogy
- Fracture and instability
- Shard emergence
- Tech disruption becomes common
- Emotional amplification visible
- Extremist misuse escalates consequences

### Loom Trilogy
- Systemic collapse
- Resonance storms
