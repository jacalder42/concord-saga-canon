# Book 1, Episode 44 (working label: When Grounding Fails)

Mara's point of view. A community gathering her people have organized. Late April.

**What this scene is for.** Testing the hope that the practices that steady people can hold the thing itself off.

**Going in.** Mara wants to keep people steady with what has worked, and some of her people believe it keeps it away.

**What changes.** The practices help the people in front of her. They are not going to stop what is coming.

**Keep off the page.** A grid, a ritual or magic. Anyone explaining the difference; it is shown, not said.

**Ends.** As the ground begins, before anyone knows what it is.
