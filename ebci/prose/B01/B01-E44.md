# Book 1, Episode 44 (working label: Mara's Gathering)

Mara's point of view. A community gathering her people have organized, the Wednesday of the next week. Seraphine and Lucien are somewhere in it; it is not their scene.

**What this scene is for.** Testing the hope that the practices that steady people can hold the thing itself off. Care that stays care while things are unsteady.

**Going in.** Mara wants to keep people steady with what the city has already learned to do, and some of her people have come to believe it keeps it away.

**What changes.** The practices do real good for the people in the room, and Mara can see what good. Whether they do what her people hope is still untested when the scene ends.

**Keep off the page.** A grid, a ritual or magic. Anyone explaining the difference; it is shown, not said.

**Ends.** As the ground begins, before anyone knows what it is.
