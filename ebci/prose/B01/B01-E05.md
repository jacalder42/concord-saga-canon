# Book 1, Episode 5 (working label: The Room Is Still Open)

Seraphine's point of view. The Velvet Vein, a working bar in the Marigny. Late February, the Saturday night after the site visit. About 2,300 words.

**What this scene is for.** The human hearth, before crisis owns every scene: a real nightlife room, hosted by Trip, that the reader wants to go back to. A pleasure Seraphine chooses.

**What she wants here.** A room that doesn't need her; an evening that asks nothing of her.

**Who pushes back.** Trip's house rules, lightly. The room has its own ways, and its host keeps them.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- The Vein at work, Trip hosting.
- She stops watching the door.
- She pays for one more song than the house allows, and Trip lets it stand.

**What it costs or changes.** She is looked after without asking. The room holds her and asks nothing back.

**Still open.** Monday's beans: she means to come back.

**Keep off the page.** Any talk of the strange; the scene works without a line of it. Trip explains nothing, and the room is nobody's headquarters. The musicians are incidental, and none is set up to matter. No penalty for her small rule-breaking. No clock time in the narration unless someone acts on it.

**Ends.** Saturday night. Seraphine goes home late. The Vein is a room she will come back to.
