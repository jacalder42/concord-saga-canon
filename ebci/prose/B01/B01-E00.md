# Book 1, Episode 0 (working label: The Conversation in the Sky)

The prologue. Two presences, high above everything, never named. No place, no calendar. About 500 words.

**What this scene is for.** To open the widest horizon without explaining it. Brief, abstract, beautiful: a question the reader carries down into the swamp.

**What they want here.** To reach a strain they perceive far below, and to reach each other across it.

**Who pushes back.** No one. The distance itself.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- They register a strain and cannot act on it.
- They barely reach each other.
- They perceive, and they do not act.

**What it costs or changes.** The distance is set.

**Still open.** A fevered child, far below.

**Keep off the page.** Names of anyone or anything. What they are, how the world works, any prophecy, any preview, any promise that they will reach it. A thread may appear, as an image only.

**Ends.** A hard cut to a sick child at a camp on the swamp's edge, at night.
