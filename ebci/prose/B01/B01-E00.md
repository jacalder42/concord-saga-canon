# Book 1, Prologue — The Conversation in the Sky

Two presences, high above everything, who are never named. No place, no calendar.

Brief, abstract, beautiful. They feel a strain in the world below and cannot reach it, and they can barely reach each other.

**Keep off the page.** Names of any kind. Any explanation of what they are or what the world is. Any future.

**Ends.** Hard cut to a sick child in the swamp.
