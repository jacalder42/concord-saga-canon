# Book 1, Episode 31 (working label: Easter, and a Case on the Chalk)

Lucien's point of view. The Marigny, along the chalked curbs off Esplanade; Mr. Ledet's house. Easter Sunday, early April; the window opens the next day. About 2,600 words.

**What this scene is for.** Someone else has been at Lucien's evidence, and he chooses the Easter table over waiting for them. A small, cold find on a holiday morning, then a warm, ordinary table.

**What he wants here.** To be right first, on paper: to re-walk the edges he chalked on Saturday before the window, and be ready.

**Who pushes back.** Seraphine, who tells him to let it be. Trip, who refuses him the Vein's front window as a watch post: it is her room and her customers' window, and she is right to keep it. Mr. Ledet, who is expecting him for Easter. Then his own want to catch whoever it was.

**Pressing on him from outside.** Someone else's will, inside his evidence: an unmarked grey case, zip-tied low on one of his chalk marks, with a cable running into the drain. It arrived after the event there, from nobody he knows. It states no authority and no reason, and whose interests it serves is unknown. It takes, and asks nothing.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- The chalk re-walked, and a grey case on his Dauphine mark.
- He wants to wait for its owner, and doesn't. He leaves it untouched and goes to Mr. Ledet's.
- Mr. Ledet's Easter.

**What it costs or changes.** Without saying it, he sees that someone else has put out instruments of their own, the approach he once considered recommending. That is recognition, not evidence that anyone read his letter. In his own head, an ugly want: that the next one comes as the page says, even if it lands on someone, so the page will be right. His evidence is no longer only his: someone else measures where it has been, and arrives after.

**Still open.** The window is close. The case stays where it is.

**Keep off the page.** The case shows no reading, does nothing and bears no label or name. Nobody says whose it is, and nobody says the three of them are watched. Lucien voices no distrust of his office and tells it nothing; he registers the case as a route, never as anyone's motive. No setting of watches or counting of fences in place of acting. The Vein does not become a headquarters.

**Ends.** Easter Sunday. The case is untouched on the chalk mark. Lucien is at Mr. Ledet's. The window opens tomorrow.
