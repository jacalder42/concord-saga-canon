# Book 1, Episode 35 (working label: Baz and Lucien, Off the Clock)

Baz's point of view. Across the river: the ferry, Algiers, and back. The Sunday. About 2,900 words.

**What this scene is for.** The friendship apart from the case, and one thing Baz keeps from Seraphine. Friendship, on a day off.

**What he wants here.** To belong here: a day off with his friend, in a city he is choosing.

**Who pushes back.** Lucien, who asks whether he has told her.

**Pressing on him from outside.** On the step before they go, Mr. Ledet tells Baz that a man asked after the car with the Texas plate, saying he was from the rental company; Mr. Ledet told him he knew no Frenchman. Nothing more is given, and nobody says who he was.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- Mr. Ledet's report, on the step.
- A day off, across the river.
- Baz chooses to stay in New Orleans anyway, with plans that carry no dates.
- Lucien asks whether he has told her. Baz says no, and tells Lucien about his column, briefly: the moved sheet, Lucien's question, and the silence they then share.

**What it costs or changes.** The concealment is now held by the two of them. Before, old friends working a case; after, friends with a life apart from it, and a thing they are both keeping from her. Baz keeps something from Lucien too, and the page does not narrate it.

**Still open.** Their plans, undated.

**Keep off the page.** The case stays off the day, except the column and Mr. Ledet's report. Baz restating the column's terms. Baz never sees the man who asked, and nobody names who sent him. Nothing foreshadows how Baz's story ends, and that someone may be looking for him can be felt; his plans have no dates and take nobody out of the city. Lucien may say he is from Vienna; there is no Vienna scene.

**Ends.** Sunday evening, back across the river. Baz is staying, knowing someone has asked after his car. Lucien knows about the column; Seraphine does not.
