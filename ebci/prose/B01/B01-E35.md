# Book 1, Episode 35 (working label: Baz and Lucien, Off the Clock)

Baz's point of view. Across the river: the ferry, Algiers, and back. The Sunday. About 2,900 words.

**What this scene is for.** The friendship apart from the case, and one thing Baz keeps from Seraphine. Friendship, on a day off.

**What he wants here.** To belong here: a day off with his friend, in a city he is choosing.

**Who pushes back.** Lucien, who asks whether he has told her.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- A day off, across the river.
- Baz chooses to stay in New Orleans, with plans that carry no dates.
- Lucien asks whether he has told her. Baz says no, and tells Lucien about his column.

**What it costs or changes.** The concealment is now held by the two of them. Before, old friends working a case; after, friends with a life apart from it, and a thing they are both keeping from her.

**Still open.** Their plans, undated.

**Keep off the page.** The case stays off the day, except the column. Nothing ominous in his plans or his pleasures; his plans have no dates and take nobody out of the city. Lucien may say he is from Vienna; there is no Vienna scene.

**Ends.** Sunday evening, back across the river. Baz is staying. Lucien knows about the column; Seraphine does not.
