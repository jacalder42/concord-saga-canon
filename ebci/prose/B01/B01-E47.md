# Book 1, Episode 47 (working label: The Square Remembers)

Lucien's point of view. Jackson Square. Late April, a festival weekend, the Quarter full of visitors.

**What this scene is for.** A return to the first public place, where people's memory matters.

**Going in.** Lucien wants to name the conclusion carefully and choose the next thing to watch. The corridor page is answered, and the verdict under their names can be written here or can keep waiting.

**What changes.** The Square goes on with its own life around them. They call it systemic, which does not mean understood. Glass and ground have shown different faces, so they choose to watch still surfaces at a narrow window, and ask Mara's people to hold one post.

**Keep off the page.** The stone or the water remembering; people remember. Any single site, crowd or institution as the cause. Any claim about the world.

**Ends.** A narrow window, and a way to watch it, agreed and not yet tried.
