# Book 1, Episode 47 (working label: Back at the Square)

Lucien's point of view. Jackson Square. Friday, the start of a festival weekend at the end of April, the Quarter full of visitors.

**What this scene is for.** A return to the first public place, where people's memory matters.

**Going in.** Lucien wants to name the conclusion carefully and choose the next thing to watch. The corridor page is answered, and the verdict under their names can be written here or can keep waiting; if it is written, the scene goes on past it.

**What changes.** The Square goes on with its own life around them. They call it systemic, which does not mean understood. Glass and ground have shown different faces, so they choose to watch still surfaces at a narrow window, and ask Mara's people to hold one post.

**Keep off the page.** The stone or the water remembering; people remember. Any single site, crowd or institution as the cause. Any claim about the world. A summing-up of everything so far.

**Ends.** A narrow window, and a way to watch it, agreed and not yet tried.
