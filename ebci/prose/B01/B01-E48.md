# Book 1, Episode 48 (working label: Still Water)

Seraphine's point of view. Jackson Square, with the watch spread across separate posts. Saturday, the festival weekend.

**What this scene is for.** A prediction that works, without explanation or control.

**Going in.** She wants to be there in time. They are early because of the work they did together, not because anyone was fast.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- Inside the window, still water at two posts ripples finely with no wind. It is caught on video. The onset is off to one side and never timed to the second.
- People nearby feel a faint unease. One stranger is caught in it and frightened: a street musician at the Square's edge whom none of them knows. Not Clement, not anyone from the Vein's band, not a child.
- Seraphine helps that one person, not the whole Square; the others take the rest of the crowd. It costs her body. He walks away whole, and nobody can say why.
- The model was useful. It was not in charge of anything.

**Keep off the page.** Sound, damage, an outage or light. The stranger's recovery as proof of anything. A mechanism. Mastery. A claim about the world. A tidy name for it; the words for it stay scattered. Any rule about when or how she feels it. If anyone says what the result means, it is evidence against the chance model they tested, not proof.

**Ends.** In the Square, among people, with the festival still going: it is real, the city knows something is happening, nobody knows enough, and they go on.
