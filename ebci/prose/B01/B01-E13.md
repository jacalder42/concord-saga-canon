# Book 1, Episode 13 (working label: After Is Awkward)

Lucien's point of view. A hardware store, then Miss Tavie's house and laundry room. Mid-March, the next day. About 2,400 words.

**What this scene is for.** New trust under ordinary conditions. Awkwardness, and humor against it.

**What he wants here.** One thing to go right in a way he can show: clearing Miss Tavie's dryer vent.

**Who pushes back.** Miss Tavie, whose dryer it is (Ferdinand put it in) and whose house has its own law.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- A practical errand keeps him and Seraphine together.
- He wins Miss Tavie round before he touches anything.
- The vent is choked, and clearing it works.

**What it costs or changes.** His first practical success in the city, won by patience and a joke rather than by taking over. Trust a day old has survived a mundane afternoon.

**Still open.** Seraphine's short, easy reply when he tells her he is home.

**Keep off the page.** Any declaration of romance. Any clue. Competence, not trespass.

**Ends.** Evening. Miss Tavie's vent is clear. Lucien walks home alone and tells Seraphine he is in; her answer is looser than her messages that morning.
