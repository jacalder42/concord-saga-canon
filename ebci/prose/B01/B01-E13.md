# Book 1, Episode 13 — The Dual Collapse

Seraphine's and Lucien's points of view. Wherever they are when it breaks. Mid-March.

**Where they start.** Two uncertain people, each failing alone: she can't carry what she senses, he can't control what he sees.

**Where they end.** Real trust. Each has steadied the other with something concrete, and each has let the other's way of knowing count.

**Keep off the page.** Either one fixing the other. Their coming together changing anything in the world.

**Ends.** Neither has said so yet.
