# Book 1, Episode 13 (working label: After Is Awkward)

Lucien's point of view. A hardware store, with its own staff and customers; then Miss Tavie's house, her laundry room and back step. Mid-March, the next day. About 2,900 words.

**What this scene is for.** New trust under ordinary conditions. Awkwardness, and humor against it.

**What he wants here.** One thing to go right in a way he can show: clearing Miss Tavie's dryer vent.

**Who pushes back.** Miss Tavie, whose dryer it is (Ferdinand put it in) and whose house has its own law.

**Life in the scene.** Miss Tavie's thanks are on her own terms: feeding a man who has done right by her husband's work, and keeping him at her table long enough to tease him about Seraphine.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- A practical errand keeps him and Seraphine together. The awkwardness is in person, not only in their messages.
- At the hardware store, someone else reads the two of them as a couple; she corrects the stranger and keeps the stranger's point. Or, instead, he chooses not to measure something he would always measure. One of the two, not both, and a joke about husbands is not made twice.
- He wins Miss Tavie round before he touches anything.
- The vent is choked, and clearing it works. Some of the work is on the page as work they do together: the machine moved between them, and their hands side by side in the warm air from the new vent, the small wonder of a house breathing again. It does not take Miss Tavie's moments or the hand-off of the tape.
- After it works, supper, and an unhurried rest (*Re-stage*, below).

*Everything else is yours, including what people say to each other, how often, and in what order.*

**What it costs or changes.** His first practical success in the city, won by patience and a joke rather than by taking over. Trust a day old has survived a mundane afternoon.

**Still open.** Seraphine's short, easy reply when he tells her he is home.

**Keep off the page.** Any declaration of romance; a stranger's assumption is not one. Any clue. Competence, not trespass. No second set piece: the laundry room is the act's one.

**Ends.** Evening. Miss Tavie's vent is clear. Lucien walks home alone and tells Seraphine he is in; her answer is looser than her messages that morning.

**Re-stage (the supper, at present one summarizing sentence).** Re-stage this block as a short scene, under about 300 words; the chapter is not rebuilt.
- *Keep:* supper given as Miss Tavie's thanks; her stories about her husband; her hand on his sleeve, asking him back to check on it; Seraphine's thanks at the corner and his deflection; the texts home, hers in lowercase, with his name typed.
- *Gain:* an unhurried rest after a success, the first the two of them have had; Miss Tavie teasing about one of Seraphine's habits, to him, in front of her, as a warning or a recommendation.
- *Guards:* nothing romantic said aloud; no laugh that stops on grief (E03 has it); Miss Tavie no oracle; no clock.
