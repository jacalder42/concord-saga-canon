# Book 1, Episode 15 — A Pulse Over Jackson Square

Seraphine's and Lucien's points of view. Jackson Square on a working day. Mid-March.

**What this scene is for.** The first thing the whole city can't dismiss, and can't agree on.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- The Square at work, with a performer on a small amplified rig.
- A disturbance that some people hear as a low thrum. The performer's amp fails.
- The crowd reacts with their bodies. There are minor injuries.
- Two ordinary recordings exist afterwards. Witnesses disagree.
- Seraphine reaches individuals. Lucien can't make what he perceives resolve.

**Keep off the page.** Any cause or mechanism, any frequency. The crowd, or the two of them, causing it. The Square remembering. Light, colour, or anything changing shape. Any link to anything larger. People may argue about fault, casualty or coincidence; nobody wins. *Pulse* loosely, or no word at all.

**Ends.** Lucien's private problem is now the city's.
