# Book 1, Episode 5 — The Misalignment

Lucien's point of view. The place his assignment brings him to interview Seraphine. Late February.

**What this scene is for.** His second strange moment is harder to dismiss, and two ways of knowing meet badly.

**Going in.** He's here to interview her as someone directly involved.

**What changes.** His categories and her account don't fit. Then a misalignment takes him bodily, and he doesn't tell her how bad it was.

**Keep off the page.** Seraphine as a mystic or expert; she is a witness and a person. Anything he sees actually bending.

**Ends.** His own perception is becoming unreliable, and no one knows.
