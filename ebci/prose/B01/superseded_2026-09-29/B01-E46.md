# Book 1, Episode 46 (working label: The Wide Quiet)

Seraphine's point of view, wherever she is when it comes; the rest of the city reaches her only as far as she can hear. Thursday, the next day.

**What this scene is for.** The quiet as the climax.

**What happens.** The city goes quiet, and the absence frightens. Then sound comes back.

**Keep off the page.** Hurrying it, a joke about it, or an explanation. Exposition filling the silence. Any rule about how she feels it.

**Ends.** When sound is back.
