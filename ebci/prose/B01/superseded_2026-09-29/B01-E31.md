# Book 1, Episode 31 (working label: The Night They Were Going to Have)

Seraphine's point of view. The Velvet Vein, a bar in the Marigny, New Orleans. A night in early April.

**What this scene is for.** The night the four of them planned, had. A good bar night is enough.

**At the table.** Seraphine; Lucien, precise and private, in the city on assignment; Baz, his old friend, who came to help and stayed; Caro, Seraphine's longtime friend, a paramedic. Trip is hosting.

**Going in.** They go because they want to. The city has had a strange few weeks, and there is a window they predicted a few days from now. Tonight nobody brings it up.

**What changes.** Something in the night shows Seraphine one of them differently, or shows her where she stands among them. Probably Lucien, loosening. Let the night decide.

**Keep off the page.** Anything strange. Research, instruments, the prediction. Any threat. A local musician plays. Tonight she is only the music. Elisabet is not there. No declarations.

**Ends.** They leave late.
