# Book 1, Episode 45 (working label: The Rebound)

Lucien's point of view, then Seraphine's. The same gathering and its immediate blocks, within the hour. Late April.

**What this scene is for.** Hitting Lucien where he most needs control, and paying the trust between him and Seraphine.

**Going in.** They want the gathering kept safe. Lucien wants to hold a model of where everything is.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- The ground shakes across the site, and people's bodies with it.
- Lucien's own view won't resolve, while something objective holds steady: his instrument, or a photograph against a survey line.
- He goes under into the need to solve it, and checking again makes it worse. Seraphine anchors him, and he lets her, sooner than the first time.
- Ordinary injuries; people separated from each other. Seraphine takes on more than she can.

**Keep off the page.** The gathering or the practices causing it. *It learned.* Impossible shapes. Buildings coming down. Retelling what Mara's piece already showed. Anyone may wonder aloud about a cause and be disagreed with.

**Ends.** A broken gathering, people hurt and needing care, and a decision to widen the inquiry, not the claim. How far to look is now a question of what they owe people.
