# Book 1, Episode 45 (working label: The Gathering)

Lucien's point of view, then Seraphine's. The same gathering and the blocks around it, from the moment the ground begins through the hour after. That Wednesday.

**What this scene is for.** Hitting Lucien where he most needs control, and paying the trust between him and Seraphine.

**Going in.** They want the gathering kept safe. Lucien wants to hold a model of where everything is.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- The ground shakes across the site, and people's bodies with it.
- Lucien's own view won't resolve, while something objective holds steady: his instrument, or a photograph against a survey line.
- He goes under into the need to solve it, and checking again makes it worse. Seraphine anchors him, and he lets her, sooner than on the store corner, and not the way she did it there.
- Ordinary injuries; people separated from each other. Seraphine takes on more than she can, past the limit she has already met, and some of it is him.
- Anyone may wonder aloud about a cause and be disagreed with.
- The question of how far to look now has these people in it.

**Keep off the page.** The gathering or the practices causing it. *It learned.* Impossible shapes. Buildings coming down. Retelling what Mara's piece already showed. A landmark or compass direction for which way he goes. Any rule about when or how she feels it.

**Ends.** Before everyone has been found: people hurt and being cared for, and an agreement to look further without claiming more.
