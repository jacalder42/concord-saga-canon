# Book 1, Episode 10 (working label: The Form, Re-signed)

Lucien's point of view. Sal's job, a gallery on jacks and its side alley; the corner store with the gallery post; the coroner's paperwork. Early to mid-March, some days after the funeral week. About 2,800 words.

**What this scene is for.** His coping fails in the open while he guards his standing, and he chooses the lie on the record a second time. A careful man making himself smaller and more precise.

**What he wants here.** To keep his place on Sal's crew, keep his daily look at the store's gallery post, and get the coroner's confirmation done with his statement as it stands.

**Who pushes back.** Sal, whose rule is plain (come on time or don't come at all) and who has noticed Lucien going grey. The store's owner, whose corner is a shop, not a place to stand. Guidry, who needs the statement confirmed within a set period. Under all three, Lucien's own eye, which gives him a wrong measurement.

**Pressing on him from outside.** Ordinary friction, with no will behind it. Sal's contract: his authority over his crew and his duty to his client; the gallery has to come off the jacks on schedule, in his business's interest. The coroner's procedure: Guidry's authority, statements confirmed within a set period, the record's interest; a reasonable demand. A shop owner's say over her own property.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- On Sal's job, a measurement of Lucien's cuts one of a handful of scarce old boards short.
- He keeps his daily look at the store's post, on the owner's terms. The lean again.
- Guidry asks him to confirm his statement, and mentions that the boy's mother wants the file closed. At the counter, with his *None* on the observation line beside her paragraph, Lucien chooses the lie again. As he confirms it, the reader sees what he is uncomfortable with: it denies what happened to him in that room, beside her account that it happened.
- Guidry closes the folder.

**What it costs or changes.** The false line on a legal record is renewed, now by choice and with the family's wish beside it; nothing has come of it yet. Sal is a scarce board down. Lucien grows more exact as his certainty drops, and the lie is now something he has done twice.

**Still open.** The signed confirmation, and what it hides.

**Keep off the page.** A shortened name for the observation line: it is referred to by what it asks. The case declared closed: Guidry closes the folder, not the case. The lean is faint and unnamed, with no landmark and no direction. He tells no one how bad it was and asks no one for help. Sal's worry is Sal's own. Nobody asks Lucien to report or contain anything, and nobody from the Institute appears. He may be wrong, embarrassed and stubborn on the page.

**Ends.** Some days later. The form is re-signed and back with Guidry, his folder closed on it. Lucien is still on Sal's crew, one salvaged board short. He still keeps his daily look at the post, on the owner's terms.
