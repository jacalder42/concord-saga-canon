# Book 1, Episode 38 (working label: The Long Hum)

Seraphine's point of view. Her rounds, across several neighborhoods, and the systems they run on. Late April, the day after the city started taping its windows.

**What this scene is for.** The changed city, lived from inside one day rather than reported.

**Going in.** She wants one day that goes as planned.

**What changes.** She has started planning around disruption before it comes, without noticing she does it. It comes anyway, somewhere else. Routes, schedules, closures and delays are the texture. Her casework may slip; nothing requires it.

**Keep off the page.** A centre, a source or an explanation. What people call *the hum* stays ambiguous: the pipes, their nerves, or the pressure. Any rule about when or how she feels it. A new recurring face from the city's services; use one already on the page, if any.

**Ends.** Nothing is isolated any more: on the news from the other place, before she has decided what to do about it.
