# Book 1, Episode 38 (working label: The Long Hum)

Seraphine's point of view. Her rounds, across several neighborhoods, and the systems they run on. Tuesday, the day after the city started taping its windows.

**What this scene is for.** The changed city, lived from inside one day rather than reported.

**Going in.** She wants one day that goes as planned.

**What changes.** She has started planning around disruption before it comes, without noticing she does it: in the routes she takes and the order she does things. It comes anyway, in another part of the city. People have started talking about a hum. Routes, schedules, closures and delays are what her day is made of as they reach her; this is not a survey of how the city is coping. By evening nothing feels isolated any more. Her casework may slip; nothing requires it.

**Keep off the page.** A centre, a source or an explanation. What people call *the hum* stays ambiguous: the pipes, their nerves, or the pressure. Any rule about when or how she feels it. A new recurring face from the city's services; use one already on the page, if any.

**Ends.** On word of it from across town, before she has decided what to do about it.
