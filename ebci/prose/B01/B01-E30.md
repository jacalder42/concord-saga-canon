**What this scene is for.** The night they planned, paid. A good night out with friends, which is enough.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**

- A local musician plays a waltz, and Seraphine dances.

**What it costs or changes.** She chose pleasure and had it. For one night she sees Lucien differently, and it costs nothing on this page. Nothing is declared.

**Still open.** What she saw in him. Nobody says anything about it.

**Keep off the page.** Any plot alert or threat. Nobody raises the window, and no instrument or notebook comes out. The musician is unnamed and belongs to this night only. Elisabet is not here. No romance payoff; nothing declared.
