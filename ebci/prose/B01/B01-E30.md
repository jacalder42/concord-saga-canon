# Book 1, Episode 30 (working label: The Night They Were Going to Have)

Seraphine's point of view. The Velvet Vein, Marigny/Bywater. Early April, that Saturday night. About 3,000 words.

**What this scene is for.** The night they planned, paid. A good night out with friends, which is enough.

**What she wants here.** A night for herself, in a room that doesn't need her. She goes because she wants to, and she dances.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- A night at the Vein, Trip hosting. The room is whole and at its best: its own people, its own stories and rules, the one room in this book that no institution runs.
- Caro carries the morning's crash into the night in her body, as Seraphine sees it and Caro tells it: her arms ache from holding a man still at dawn, and then, dancing, she stops feeling them, gladly.
- Caro meets Lucien and Baz the way she meets anyone: physically, plainly and funny, sizing them up. Lucien answers her literally, never with a quip. Seraphine finds that she does not have to manage her friends meeting each other, and it frees her.
- A local musician plays a waltz, and Seraphine dances. The waltz is kept whole.
- Under the streetlight at the end, she says one thing to him that is not good night. The night's contentment does not erase wanting.

**What it costs or changes.** She chose pleasure and had it. The waltz brings physical awareness and touch between her and Lucien, sparks they both feel. For one night she sees him differently, and feels him close, and it costs nothing on this page. No kiss, and nothing is declared.

**Still open.** What she saw in him. Nobody says anything about it.

**Keep off the page.** Any plot alert or threat. Nobody raises the window, and no instrument or notebook comes out. The musician is unnamed and belongs to this night only. A fast dance that shows Lucien failing: the waltz stays the only dance he does. Elisabet is not here. Any kiss; anything declared.

**Ends.** Late Saturday night; they leave the Vein. Seraphine, Lucien, Baz and Caro were there, with Trip hosting. The window is still ahead.
