# Book 1, Episode 50 (working label: What They Hold)

The book's last piece, after the Velvet Vein vignette. The two presences from the prologue. No place, no calendar. About 500 words.

**What this scene is for.** Close the book where it opened, with the two presences, a little closer than before. Brief, abstract, beautiful: the book's question carried up and handed back. Below, someone has just spoken aloud the word for what they hold, as a guess.

**What they want here.** To know whether anyone below knows about what they hold.

**Who they are, by what they do.** One still acts toward silence and stillness (he). The other toward hope: reaching, trying (she). They may speak to each other, and may be named if a name arrives naturally.

**Who pushes back.** No one. The distance itself, and a question nothing answers.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- They perceive a knowing pass between people far below, and hear the word for what they hold spoken there.
- They react to it together, and ask whether he, or the people he left, know about what they hold.
- Nothing answers. They perceive, and they do not act.

**What it costs or changes.** They are slightly more engaged than at the start: intrigued by something that happened, not only watching.

**Still open.** Whether he, or those he left, know about what they hold.

**Keep off the page.** What they are, how the world works, any prophecy, any preview, any promise that they will reach anyone. What they hold stays an image, never explained. The word *the Veil* has been spoken below, as one man's guess; here they react to it, and may use it as their own word too. Nothing says whether the guess is right. The people below are not named, described or placed, and nothing says what was told beyond the word. A thread may appear, as an image only. If water trembles in their seeing, it is not tied to the place Seraphine left, and its beginning is unseen. A few short paragraphs.

**Ends.** The end of the book. The question stands, unanswered.
