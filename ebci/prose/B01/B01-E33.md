# Book 1, Episode 33 (working label: Water, Food, Shower, Sleep)

Seraphine's point of view. Miss Tavie's house. The night of the block. About 2,200 words. The kitchen keeps its room, and the piece still ends on her choice.

**What this scene is for.** Bodies first after the block: water, food, a shower, sleep. Being looked after.

**What she wants here.** To get back to the block tonight. Under it, a room that doesn't need her.

**Who pushes back.** Miss Tavie, who gives orders in her own house and means them.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- Bodies, and Miss Tavie's orders.
- Miss Tavie's care is plain and practical: one pan, one fork between them, nothing made a fuss of.
- Watching Miss Tavie work at her own stove, competently, without asking anything of her, is a pleasure, and the pleasure is Seraphine's own: a room that does not need her, felt from inside, not handed to her as anyone's verdict.
- Her claim that she's fine is refused.
- She lets Miss Tavie win, and stays down.
- Alone on the stair up, the afternoon arrives in her body, one place at a time: what she knelt on, what she lifted, what she held. Nobody helps her up.
- In the shower the body lets go: arms she can hardly lift, the water running colored and then clear. One release; no crying.
- When someone asks by text whether she is home, she notices it was Baz who asked, and not Lucien.
- From below, Miss Tavie checks on her once, without words. Seraphine means to answer it.

*Everything else is yours, including what people say to each other, how often, and in what order.*

**What it costs or changes.** What she spent on the block lands in her body (knees, arms, hands that keep shaking and then stop), and she is looked after completely. Staying down has a cost she knows: the block will see that she did not come back.

**Still open.** Whether she goes back.

**Keep off the page.** A debrief, a clue or any scoring of the page; nothing interrupts the body. Staying down is her choice, not a collapse. The looking-after takes its own form, unlike the book's other rests, and the piece ends on her choice.

**Ends.** That night. Seraphine is at Miss Tavie's and has not gone back to the block.
