# Book 1, Episode 33 (working label: Water, Food, Shower, Sleep)

Seraphine's point of view. Miss Tavie's house. The night of the block. About 2,200 words.

**What this scene is for.** Bodies first after the block: water, food, a shower, sleep. Being looked after.

**What she wants here.** To get back to the block tonight. Under it, a room that doesn't need her.

**Who pushes back.** Miss Tavie, who gives orders in her own house and means them.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- Bodies, and Miss Tavie's orders.
- Her claim that she's fine is refused.
- She lets Miss Tavie win, and stays down.

**What it costs or changes.** What she spent on the block lands in her body, and she is looked after completely. Staying down has a cost she knows: the block will see that she did not come back.

**Still open.** Whether she goes back.

**Keep off the page.** A debrief, a clue or any scoring of the page; nothing interrupts the body. Staying down is her choice, not a collapse. The looking-after takes its own form, unlike the book's other rests, and the piece ends on her choice.

**Ends.** That night. Seraphine is at Miss Tavie's and has not gone back to the block.
