# Book 1, Episode 39 (working label: Tracing the Scatter)

Lucien's point of view. Working on the move, with food and waiting visible. Wednesday, the next day.

**What this scene is for.** The best working-out they have done, and a disagreement about how far to look.

**Going in.** Lucien wants a likely window for the next one. The signed page from the corridor has its answer, Saturday's. The office has his three pages, not the sentence he kept back, and may or may not have answered.

**What changes.** Their three ways of knowing reach the same window by different roads. A report from far off joins the pile; it only suggests. Lucien wants to widen the comparison; Seraphine asks what that costs the people here who are relying on them. Any of them may be wrong in front of the others.

**Keep off the page.** Any claim about the world beyond what they have in front of them. Anyone going back over everything so far; the reader has lived it. Anyone settling the disagreement for them.

**Ends.** On the question of how far to look, still open between them, with the window already agreed.
