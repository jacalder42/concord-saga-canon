# Book 1, Episode 39 (working label: Tracing the Scatter)

Lucien's point of view. Working on the move, with food and waiting visible. Late April, the next day.

**What this scene is for.** The strongest piece of working-out in the book, and a disagreement about how far to look.

**Going in.** Lucien wants a likely window for the next one. The signed page from the corridor has its answer, Saturday's, and the verdict they promised to write under their three names is still unwritten. The office has his three pages and not the sentence; anything it does about them is permitted, not required.

**What changes.** Their three ways of knowing converge on a window. A report from somewhere else joins the pile, suggestive only. Lucien wants to widen the comparison; Seraphine asks what that costs the people here who are relying on them. Baz may be wrong in front of the other two.

**Keep off the page.** Any claim about the world beyond what they have in front of them.

**Ends.** With the window found and the question of how far to look still open between them.
