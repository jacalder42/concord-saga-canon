# Book 1, Episode 43 (working label: Something Strange That Doesn't Hurt)

Baz's point of view. Somewhere ordinary, and not the river: City Park, or a place of the prose's choosing. Late April, a Sunday.

**What this scene is for.** Wonder that belongs to one person.

**What happens.** Something small and strange happens to Baz alone. He doesn't write it down and doesn't tell it as evidence. What it is belongs to the prose; it need not be a sound.

**Keep off the page.** Any mechanic, danger or report. Anyone else sharing it.

**Ends.** It has passed, and he has kept it to himself.
