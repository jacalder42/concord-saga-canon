# Book 1, Episode 9 (working label: Don't Carry It Alone)

Seraphine's point of view. Miss Tavie's house, where Seraphine rents the upstairs, and the neighborhood around it. Early to mid-March: the week of Dré's funeral, the week after the benefits office. About 2,600 words.

**What this scene is for.** The funeral week turns inward, and the family's claim on her is felt. A house that talks back; guidance that comes from a person, not a prophecy.

**What she wants here.** To get through the funeral week on her feet: working, walking at night, keeping her evening call to Odile.

**Who pushes back.** Miss Tavie, her elderly landlady downstairs, whose sleep the night pacing breaks. Odile, who tells her she need not keep calling.

**Pressing on her from outside.** Ordinary friction, with no will behind it. The family's expectations and the funeral week's customs: Odile speaks with a grandmother's authority over her family's grief, to protect Renée, in the family's interest. Renée's own claim, as Dré's mother, stands behind it.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- The funeral week, and the pacing that keeps the house awake.
- In one of the evening calls, Seraphine tells Odile that the renewal sat unfinished on her desk. Odile's response is her own.
- On the evening call, Seraphine presses Odile for what Renée has said, against Odile's wish. Odile tells her: Renée has asked Odile, not the coroner, what Seraphine told the man who came about the house.
- Over the pacing, Miss Tavie tells her plainly that she cannot carry every room. Seraphine understands it and can't do it.

**What it costs or changes.** Odile knows about the renewal now. Seraphine has pushed past the call's distance, and knows Renée has a question with her name on it, about the form and about Lucien.

**Still open.** What Renée wants.

**Keep off the page.** Miss Tavie is not an oracle, and her advice solves nothing. Odile's wish is her own, and right. Seraphine doesn't learn what Lucien wrote; she knows only that Renée asked. No return to the camp. Caseload numbers reported upward go to the state, never a county. The evening call is a time she keeps; otherwise no clock time in the narration unless someone acts on it.

**Ends.** The funeral week. Seraphine is upstairs at Miss Tavie's. Odile knows about the renewal. Seraphine knows Renée asked Odile what she told Lucien. She is still carrying it.
