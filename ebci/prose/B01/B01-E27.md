# Book 1, Episode 27 (working label: Plans for After)

Seraphine's point of view. A busy breakfast place on a wet Sunday. Late March, the Sunday after the page; the window is still a week off. About 1,900 words.

**What this scene is for.** Anticipation: they assume a future beyond the problem. Seraphine asks her friend for what Caro's job won't let her give, is refused, and they plan Saturday instead. Something to look forward to, and a friendship that can say no.

**What she wants here.** The dispatch times for the page. Under it, a night that doesn't need her.

**Who pushes back.** Caro, who says no: the data belongs to her job and its patients, not to the page.

**Pressing on her from outside.** Caro's employer's confidentiality: the emergency service's authority, the patients as the reason, the patients' and the service's interests. It is reasonable. Caro upholds it against her friend, and she is right.

**Already true.** Caro is the one friend Seraphine has told about the folder.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- The ask, and Caro's no.
- Elisabet on the television, and Caro notices. Asked when she will know, Elisabet answers October.
- Refused, Caro changes the subject to Saturday, and a Saturday night out is planned, as if the future can be assumed.

**What it costs or changes.** A night is planned. Caro takes a small, attentive notice of Elisabet's public self.

**Still open.** Saturday.

**Keep off the page.** Anything like *if we survive*, and any ominous wink. Keep the plan specific. Caro's notice of Elisabet stays tiny: interest or amusement, never jealousy or a declaration. Elisabet appears only on the television; October is the only time she gives, and only as an answer. Caro's no is a no; this piece does not reopen or soften it. No clock time in the narration unless someone acts on it. No named mechanism. No return to the camp. Plans stay undated beyond this Saturday.

**Ends.** Sunday, late March; the window has not opened. Seraphine has no dispatch times. Next Saturday night is planned at the Vein, and Caro is off that night.
