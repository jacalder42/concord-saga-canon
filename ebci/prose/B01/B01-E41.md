# Book 1, Episode 41 (working label: After the Breaking)

Seraphine's point of view. The aftermath of Thursday's broken line, in the places people sit down, the Velvet Vein among them. That night and Friday.

**What this scene is for.** The aftermath made human before anyone tries another answer.

**Going in.** She wants to be useful to everyone.

**What changes.** She cannot be. Ordinary care dominates, there is more of it needed than she has, and some of it goes undone.

**Keep off the page.** Her being looked after back into shape; this is her limit, met, not mended. Any rule about how she feels it. The Vein as a room arguing about what happened; here it is only somewhere to sit.

**Ends.** At the limit, not after it.
