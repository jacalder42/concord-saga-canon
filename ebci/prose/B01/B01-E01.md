# Book 1, Episode 1 — The Sick Child

Seraphine's point of view. A small wetland community at the swamp's edge, outside New Orleans. Late February.

**What this scene is for.** The mystery costs a human life before it explains anything.

**Going in.** A family's child is very sick, and Seraphine comes to help.

**What happens.** She does what can be done, less sure than usual of what she's sensing. The child dies. She stays with the family.

**Keep off the page.** Anything she senses being confirmed or named. The swamp as myth; it is a place where people live.

**Ends.** The child is dead, and Seraphine is still there.
