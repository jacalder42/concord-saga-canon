# Book 1, Episode 21 (working label: Heat Checks)

Lucien's and Seraphine's points of view, handed over at breaks. Lucien's job site and his room; the care center; Seraphine's phone. The same Thursday as Poydras, afternoon into night. About 4,200 words.

**What this scene is for.** Seraphine and Lucien's first real fight. Fear drives a real disagreement over method, risk and responsibility. Seraphine starts warning her households before anyone is sure, under a name that is not the truth. Two frightened people, each right about something, hurting each other more than they meant to.

**What they want here.** Seraphine: to be the one who warns them first, needed before the fact. She means to warn her oldest clients who live alone before the next one comes. Lucien: a clean sample, and her safe inside his procedure. He means to keep the record clean enough to test.

**Who pushes back.** Denise, whose liability rules will not let the center frighten clients with a warning it cannot stand behind. Lucien, who speaks for Baz's need for untold witnesses, and who turns her own written admission from the first Square, that she made it worse, against her. Under both, each one's fear for the other.

**Pressing on them from outside.** On Seraphine, Denise's liability: an employer's authority; forty-three households and the center's exposure as the reason; the center's interests, and Seraphine's as Denise sees them. It is reasonable, and it protects Denise too. On Lucien, his office's want for a clean sample: his employer's authority, method as the reason, the office's report as the interest. Beside this ordinary friction, something with a will: Lucien brings word that someone at the city's table has her name.

**Already true.**
- The Batiste case file is still open at the center. Denise wants it closed before the county's month-end count, which is days away. Seraphine promised it for Monday and missed. The closure form will not take a blank date for the last action on the renewal. It stays live here by what she does or avoids doing, never by recollection.
- Denise has already banned center business in the Square and said she is watching.
- The blue folder has been on Seraphine's kitchen table since her call with Odile. Only Caro and Odile have heard what is in it.
- Her admission from the first Square is in her own notebook, and Lucien has not seen it. How he comes to have it happens on the page.
- The heat checks are her line of inquiry: she starts them, she logs them, and she reads what they show.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- Seraphine and Denise: the heat checks, under a cover name.
- Lucien brings the city's table home. The fight over method and risk, and her Square line used against her.
- She tells him she is not a post for him to check morning and night, and walks out.
- She comes back and tells him the folder: the boy's health coverage had lapsed, and the renewal paperwork was sitting on her desk while she worked on it. His procedure is rewritten as seven lines that carry her rule as well as his.
- The calls themselves, logged, telling by telling. The mornings go quiet.

**What it costs or changes.** The heat checks run under a cover name: a lie to Denise and to forty-three households, and every call is also a warning. The fight costs: she walks out. The repair is changed behavior, not talk: the working page now carries both methods. The morning texts stop for two days, then start again. Before, real trust, with care expressed as control on both sides; after, trust that has come through a real disagreement.

**Still open.** Whether the lie holds with Denise. Whether the calls, logged before and after telling, stay clean enough to count.

**Keep off the page.** Misunderstanding as the driver: fear drives it. Any declaration; hand on hand is the ceiling. The warning names nothing: no days, no hour, no word for what it is. Lucien reports the table as he saw it and says nothing against his office. Each half carries only what its point-of-view holder perceives or is told. Seraphine's casework is real work, and the lie is hers to carry. The narrator certifies no cause and no blame; the two of them may blame each other. No clock time in the narration unless someone acts on it. No named mechanism. No return to the camp. No bearing for Lucien's lean.

**Ends.** Late March, night. Seraphine has the forty-three-name pad and the seven-line page. The calls are under way and logged, telling by telling. Denise believes they are ordinary heat checks. Lucien knows about the folder. No morning text passes between them for the next two days; then the texts resume.
