# Book 1, Episode 19 (working label: Three Smart People Having Fun)

Seraphine's point of view. Her car, across the city; then a shop. The same Wednesday, into the evening, after the block. About 2,000 words.

**What this scene is for.** Competence as pleasure, before the investigation becomes a burden: three smart people enjoying each other.

**What she wants here.** A stretch of time that doesn't need her, and to win: the argument about how the city's street names are said.

**Who pushes back.** Lucien's rule that the names are said as if read in English, against her view that a name is said the way the city says it.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- A small question, and the three of them at it. She corrects Baz first, and enjoys it.
- They arrive at the point that the names can only be learned by hearing them and saying them back.

**What it costs or changes.** Something small gets solved, for the fun of it. She is at ease with them for the first time since the boy died.

**Still open.** Nothing pressing. The teasing between her and Lucien, which does not stop here.

**Keep off the page.** Any clue or case. Anything romantic said aloud between her and Lucien, and any kiss: the teasing stays light.

**Ends.** Evening. The three together, lighter than they were.
