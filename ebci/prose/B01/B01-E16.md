# Book 1, Episode 16 (working label: Baz Gets to Arrive)

Baz's point of view. The last of his drive in from Houston; the Velvet Vein on a Monday night. Late March, the Monday after the Square, at night. About 2,700 words. The added beats do not displace the drive in, the city coming in slowly, or the walk to the Vein.

**What this scene is for.** Baz as a person before he is a method: the city's pleasures through a new pair of eyes.

**What he wants here.** To arrive as himself: one of them here, not a method sent for.

**Who pushes back.** Lucien, whose hand keeps going to the notebook because he wants to begin.

**Pressing on him from outside.** Nothing with a will. He is a visitor without standing: nobody here has asked him for anything, and he holds no role in the city.

**Already true.** Baz drove in from Houston, where he was finishing a documentation project for a housing group. He and Lucien once did a tenants' study together in Marseille. Seraphine asked for him to be brought to the Vein on its beans night. He is sleeping on Lucien's floor. Before all of this, Baz and Lucien worked together in Vienna, and Baz left that work; Houston and the Marseille study belong to his life since. Why he left is his own: unspoken here, and never narrated in full from inside him.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- Baz arrives, the long way in.
- Lucien and Baz at the Vein, with Seraphine there.
- He puts the case off until the morning: the friend before the case.
- Baz meets a place by collecting its versions: somebody tells him a small local thing, someone else tells it differently, and he is delighted that they disagree. It is his trade at rest, never a clue.
- The Vein's welcome is food first: the beans arrive before anyone asks him anything.
- The room has noticed the Mondays Seraphine did not come after Trip first asked her. Trip lets her know it, briefly, without asking why, and lets it go.
- Seraphine and Lucien play together in front of Baz, at least once: a story from their own earlier weeks that one starts and the other corrects, or Lucien telling something against himself and checking whether she laughs. Baz sees it. Nobody names it. Nothing about Vienna in it.
- When Baz says Seraphine is why Lucien said *we*, how it lands in her is shown in what she does, not named.
- Alone with Baz, Lucien gives his friend one honest answer about how he is: not all right, and better than he was.
- At the gate, Baz takes the case back on Lucien's terms: he asks for the summary, tomorrow. The friend first, and then the case honored.

*Everything else is yours, including what people say to each other, how often, and in what order.*

**What it costs or changes.** He arrives as a person, and the case waits a night.

**Still open.** The start they agree for the next morning.

**Keep off the page.** Any clue in the meal, and any shadow over him or his plans. He is not Lucien's mentor, professor or subordinate. Trip welcomes him on the Vein's own terms. Seraphine is there, and meets him as a person, not as a case.

**Ends.** Monday night. Baz is staying at Lucien's. They have agreed to start the next morning. Seraphine has met him. The walk home and Baz settling on the sloping floor may run on as a short after-scene.
