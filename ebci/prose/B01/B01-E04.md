# Book 1, Episode 4 — A Line Out of Place

Lucien's point of view. The city, on an ordinary working day of his assignment. Late February.

**What this scene is for.** Meet Lucien through the way he sees: a precise, private man looking at a city built on other rules.

**What happens.** A line looks wrong, with a faint lean toward the south-west. He checks, and it's fine. He doesn't name the lean or make anything of its direction.

**Keep off the page.** Where he's from, who sent him and why. Anything actually changing in the world.

**Ends.** He has noticed something he can't account for.
