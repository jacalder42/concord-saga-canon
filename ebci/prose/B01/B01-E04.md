# Book 1, Episode 4 (working label: The Inspector's Line)

Lucien's point of view. His working days in the city: his rented room, Sal's restoration job on Kerlerec, a corner store near it. Then the family's camp at the swamp's edge, for a dwelling hazard check, and the city again. Late February, from the days after the death to the Saturday of the check. About 3,600 words.

**What this scene is for.** Lucien, introduced by how he sees: New Orleans through a precise, private man. Then his own perception turns unreliable, and he puts a lie on a legal record. Two ways of knowing meet badly.

**What he wants here.** His standing: a clean, bounded job, and a box on the form for what he is told. On the hazard check the coroner has asked for (carbon monoxide, ventilation, electrical), done for the parish's contracted firm, he tries to fit Seraphine's account into the form's boxes and keep his own finding clean.

**Who pushes back.** Seraphine, who insists her account goes on the form in her own words.

**Pressing on him from outside.** Ordinary friction, with no will behind it. His position, in one line on the page: the review that brought him to the city placed him with the firm that does the parish's inspections, and Sal's job is how he learns the city. The coroner's form and its fixed lines: the office's authority, an accurate record as its reason, the record's interest. Sal's schedule: a contractor's authority over his own job and crew, in his own interest.

**Life in the scene.** His own order is how he keeps himself in a strange city: the room measured and put right before he goes out, the small rituals of a morning alone. Sal's house on its jacks is a job with a crew and a schedule, not a backdrop, and the street may notice him back.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- His working days. Once, a corner post reads out of true, toward the low sun. He checks it, and it is plumb.
- The hazard check. His readings are normal. Her account doesn't fit his categories; he writes it in as she says it. At most one line, private, of his regard for how she told it: her words did more than his form will.
- In the room where the boy died, while he is taking her account, something takes him bodily and he goes down. He tells her less than happened.
- He fills in the form. On the employer line he writes the Institute. His instrument and appliance findings (the readings, the ventilation, the fuel-burning appliances) he records honestly as normal. The last line, separate from them, asks, under its full heading, for conditions observed during the inspection affecting the health or safety of occupants, including the inspector; the reader should follow what it asks. He writes *None*, and his breath hitches, or he sighs.

**What it costs or changes.** Both are on the form now: her paragraph in her own words, and his *None*, a false statement on a legal record that conceals only his own experience in that room. The hitch tells the reader only that he is uncomfortable with something. Before, they were strangers. Now she is his witness, and her words sit beside his lie, which she doesn't know about.

**Still open.** The lie on the form.

**Keep off the page.** Seraphine is a witness and a person, not a mystic or an expert. Nothing objective bends: every check he makes finds nothing wrong. The post's lean is faint and unnamed; *toward the low sun* is the only bearing he gets, and he doesn't map, name or interpret it. The Institute, the Hollen Institute for Civic Continuity, may be named: he knows it is a cover for the people who sent him, and may think of it as one. He does not know that the review's work is meant to turn up events like the one in that room. The people behind it are never named or described, and no other sponsor, family history or superior appears. His weekly note goes to R., his contact at the Institute; nothing more of R. is shown. He may be from Vienna; no scene there. The camp is a house where a boy died, and nobody infers anything about the place. No clock time in the narration unless someone acts on it.

**Ends.** After the Saturday check. The form, with the Institute on its employer line, his normal instrument findings, her paragraph and his *None*, is with Guidry. Lucien is back in the city; his weekly note to the Institute is sent. He has told no one how bad it was.
