# Book 1, Episode 3 (working label: The Day Still Happens)

Seraphine's point of view. Errands and ordinary routes in her New Orleans, around Tremé. Late February, the same week: the first of two days off. About 2,800 words.

**What this scene is for.** Grief in ordinary time. She had a life, and a friend, before any of this.

**What she wants here.** To get through the day in the calm, capable voice she uses on everyone, and to be let off with it.

**Who pushes back.** Caro, who needs the money from the double shift she is working, wants the real answer instead of the voice, and wants to call out of the shift to stay.

**Pressing on her from outside.** Ordinary friction, with no will behind it. Caro's shifts: the double is money she needs. Seraphine's boss has given her two days, and they end.

**What happens. Only this is fixed:**
- Errands that have to be done, with Caro.
- Caro refuses the voice.
- Seraphine chooses to tell her about the folder: the card's renewal, left unfinished on her desk.

**What it costs or changes.** Caro knows now, and goes to her shift. Nothing between them is fixed by her knowing.

**Still open.** The daily evening call to Odile.

**Keep off the page.** Any clue. Caro needs no introduction and is not on standby: she has her own day and her own shift to get to. No clock time in the narration unless someone acts on it.

**Ends.** The same week. Caro knows about the card and has gone to work. Seraphine is keeping her evening call to Odile.
