## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Close Act II on a city adapting and on Baz's bet that it is coming faster, while Lucien, over Baz's refusal, sets a trap on the city's complaint line.
Want:                               To copy the glazier's spike: every order for broken glass in the city, as blocks rather than addresses, and how close together they come (10-03, §391: was *with its date and time*). Underneath, to belong here, by being good at a morning's work.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Copy Mr. Lacoste's order tickets.
  Opposition:                       Mr. Lacoste, whose tickets stay on his spike: his shop, his records, his business. A trade opens it: Baz works the counter for the morning. Then Lucien, who wants Baz in on a false report to the city's line; Baz won't do it.
  Pressed by:                       The city's complaint notice asks residents for the date and time of what they saw, and tape is going up on windows citywide. That is the city's authority, with public safety as its stated reason, in interests of its own. Mr. Lacoste tells it first, before any trade: a man from the city has already been through his tickets and photographed them, addresses and all; his card hung on a string, turned so nobody in the shop could read it. Since then he has come back and offered to buy the whole spike, and Mr. Lacoste refused (10-03, §391, C1, N12). What he wants can be inferred: the record of where and when, and who it happened to (10-03, map §2). Once, near the shop, Baz may glimpse the man he recognised in the Poydras lobby; no contact follows, and Baz tells nobody (10-03, map §2, optional).
  Turn:                             The tickets show glass going on blocks nobody reported, closer and closer together. Baz's conclusion, held as a bet: it is coming faster.
  Consequence:                      Baz works the counter, sells glass well, and copies the spike, blocks not addresses, and hands it back intact (10-03, §391, C1). He is in the shop when a new sheet cracks while the old glass in the crate makes no sound, and the old-glass idea dies for him. He takes the tickets to people nobody warned, asks whether everyone stopped at once, and finds hits nobody reported. He declines Lucien's trap without a fight. Lucien, who has just written Guidry a correction letter, the true sentence his form left out, which reopens the environmental question the coroner might have closed (10-03, map §2), and has sent the Institute his overdue report, which says nothing beyond his timber work (10-03, §391, N7), files a false report to the line anyway, naming Sal's job, where he knows nothing happened: a report of that kind brings an inspector (10-03, map §2).
  Unresolved:                       Whether a case will come to Sal's job, and what the inspector will find there. What the coroner does with the reopened question (10-03, map §2).
Change:                             Before: two friends who agree on method. After: two friends who disagree about a lie, and stay friends. Baz holds a bet about the pace.
Reader experience:                  A morning of work Baz is good at, and an act closing on a city, and a friend, each doing something he wouldn't.
