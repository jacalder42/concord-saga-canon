## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Give the mystery a human cost before anything explains it.
Want:                               Keep a feverish boy alive until the ambulance comes.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Seraphine drives out at night, off-shift, on the grandmother's call, to keep Dré (seven, a child she knows through her work) alive until the unit arrives.
  Opposition:                       Odile, the grandmother, who will not let Seraphine raise the lapsed card tonight; then a road the ambulance needs half an hour to cover.
  Pressed by:                       Ordinary friction, no will behind it. The parish road and dispatch: the emergency system's authority and protocol, serving the public, which cannot shorten the distance. The lapsed card: Dré's coverage card lapsed in January when the notice went to a former address, and the renewal sits unfinished in Seraphine's blue folder and the unfinished part was hers. Working off-shift: her employer's rules about acting outside her hours.
  Turn:                             The ambulance comes too late.
  Consequence:                      Dré dies. Seraphine chose to run the resuscitation herself on the dispatcher's count, and she chose to leave her part in the lapsed card unsaid when Odile stopped her; it stays unsaid, at a risk to her professional standing and to Renée's trust.
  Unresolved:                       A pressure in that room that does not behave like grief; the card, unsaid.
Change:                             The child dies, and Seraphine stays with the family.
