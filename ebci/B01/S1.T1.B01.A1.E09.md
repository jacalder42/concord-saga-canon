## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Turn the funeral week inward, and let the family's claim on her be felt.
Want:                               To get through the funeral week on her feet.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Seraphine tries to keep going through the week of the funeral: working, walking at night, keeping her evening call to the boy's grandmother.
  Opposition:                       Miss Tavie, her elderly landlady downstairs, whose sleep her night pacing breaks; Odile, who tells her she need not keep calling.
  Pressed by:                       Ordinary friction, no will behind it. Family expectation and the funeral week's customs: Odile's authority is a grandmother's over her family's grief, her justification is to protect Renée, and her interest is the family's; Renée's own claim, as the boy's mother, stands behind it.
  Turn:                             On the evening call, Seraphine presses Odile for what Renée has said, against Odile's wish, and Odile tells her: Renée wants to know what Seraphine told the man who came about the house.
  Consequence:                      Seraphine knows Renée is asking about the form, and about Lucien. Miss Tavie, over the pacing, tells her plainly that she cannot carry every room; she understands it and cannot do it.
  Unresolved:                       What Renée wants.
Change:                             Before: she has kept the family at a daily call's distance. After: she has pushed past it, and knows Renée has a question with her name on it.
Reader experience:                  A house that talks back, and guidance that is relational, not prophetic.
Keep / don't spend:                 Miss Tavie is not an oracle and her advice solves nothing. Odile's wish is her own, and right. Seraphine does not learn what Lucien wrote; she knows only that Renée asked. No return to the camp. The evening call is a channel Seraphine uses; no other clock time in narration unless a character does something with it.
Exit conditions:                    The funeral week. Seraphine is at Miss Tavie's, upstairs. She knows Renée asked what she told Lucien. She is still carrying it.
