## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Let grief occupy ordinary time; show that Seraphine had a life and a friend before the mystery.
Want:                               To get through the day, and to be let off with her steady voice.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Seraphine spends the first of her two days off doing errands with Caro, and tries to get through it in the calm, capable voice she uses on everyone.
  Opposition:                       Caro, who needs the money from the double shift she is working, wants the real answer instead of the voice, and wants to call out of the shift to stay.
  Pressed by:                       Ordinary friction, no will behind it. Caro's shift economy: the double is money she needs. The care center's ordinary week: Seraphine's boss has given her two days, and they end.
  Turn:                             Caro refuses the voice.
  Consequence:                      Seraphine chooses to tell her the folder: the lapsed card's renewal, left unfinished on her desk. Caro now knows, and Caro goes to her shift.
  Unresolved:                       The daily evening call to Odile.
Change:                             Before: Caro can see she is carrying it badly and doesn't know why. After: Caro knows about the card, and nothing between them is fixed by knowing.
Reader experience:                  An ordinary day with a friend in it.
Keep / don't spend:                 No clue. Caro needs no introduction and is not on standby: she has her own day and her own shift to get to. No clock time in narration unless a character does something with it.
Exit conditions:                    The same week. Caro knows about the card and has gone to work. Seraphine keeps six o'clock for her call to Odile.
