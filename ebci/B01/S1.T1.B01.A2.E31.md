## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Someone else has been at Lucien's evidence, and he chooses the Easter table over waiting for them. That evening the three face a limit: they cannot warn every household in the window's blocks, so they choose whom to tell.
Want:                               To be right first, on paper: to re-walk his chalk edges before the window and be ready.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Re-walk the edges he chalked on Saturday.
  Opposition:                       Seraphine, who tells him to let it be. Trip, who refuses him the Vein's front window as a watch post: it is her room and her customers' window, and she is right to keep it. Mr. Ledet, who is expecting him for Easter. Then his own want to catch whoever it was. In the evening, the window's blocks themselves: more doors than three people can reach before it opens.
  Pressed by:                       Someone else's will, inside his evidence: a grey case, unmarked but for a small printed asset tag whose code begins *TD-* and a number (10-03, map §2), zip-tied low on one of his chalk marks, a cable running into the drain. It arrived after the event there, from nobody he knows. It states no authority and no reason, and whose interests it serves is unknown. It takes and asks nothing. Before any of it, a message from R., his contact at the Hollen Institute: the March report is overdue, and is there anything of note in the city? It asks, mildly, for what he has noticed. Lucien drafts a reply, deletes it, and sends nothing (10-03, §391, N7).
  Turn:                             He finds the case on his Dauphine mark.
  Consequence:                      He wants to wait for its owner, and doesn't. He leaves it untouched and goes to Mr. Ledet's. Without saying it, he sees that someone else has put out instruments of their own, the approach he once considered recommending. That is recognition, not evidence that anyone read his letter. In his own head, an ugly want: that the next one comes as the page says, even if it lands on someone, so the page will be right. To want it, he sets aside what he saw at the Marigny crossing and Elisabet's flat readings, and tells no one (10-03, §391, N6 carried). That evening they choose whom to tell, because they cannot tell everyone: Seraphine's households. It is triage, a choice about who counts, and all three know someone on those blocks is left out.
  Unresolved:                       The window is close. The case stays where it is. The households on the window's blocks who have not been told.
Change:                             His evidence is no longer only his: someone else measures where it has been, and arrives after. The waltz is still with him: in his own point of view, the night before has changed how he is with her, and their teasing goes on. The three have chosen who counts.
Reader experience:                  A small, cold find on a holiday morning, a warm, ordinary table, then an evening's hard arithmetic about who gets told.
Keep / don't spend:                 The case shows no reading, does nothing and bears only the clue, unexplained: the small asset tag, its code beginning *TD-* (10-03, map §2). Lucien may notice it; nobody reads it as anyone's. Nobody says whose it is, and nobody says the three are watched. Lucien voices no distrust of the Institute aloud and tells it nothing; he may think about what it covers, its senders unnamed; his thoughts register the case as a route, never as anyone's motive. The waltz's aftermath may carry teasing and touch; the heat menu is available (touch, proximity, attraction bending a practical choice), as a menu, not a quota; nothing romantic is declared aloud, and no kiss (10-03, map §0.3 G3). If the Marigny's score comes up, one clause says why it is neither hit nor miss: it came before the window opened, so the page's terms could not count it. The choice of whom to tell is a limit on reach, never a test: nobody is left untold to keep a sample clean, and Lucien's ugly want stays in his own head. No setting of watches or counting of fences in place of acting. The re-walk itself is a few lines; Trip's refusal of the window, a bargain, is given whole (10-03, §391, C2). R.'s message names no one beyond R. and asks for nothing specific; Lucien's unsent draft stays unsent and its content is his; he says nothing against the Institute (10-03, §391, N7). The Vein does not become a headquarters.
Exit conditions:                    Easter Sunday evening. The case is untouched on the chalk mark, and Lucien has seen its tag (10-03, map §2). Lucien has been to Mr. Ledet's. The three have chosen to tell Seraphine's households in the window's blocks, and know the rest go untold. The window opens tomorrow, against the page's one stated claim (10-03, map §2). R.'s message is unanswered (10-03, §391, N7).
