## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Lucien acts on his wrong theory. He and Elisabet go into a city drainage pump station at night and photograph a week of the held paper charts. The supervisor catches them and takes his name.
Want:                               To be right before the fact: the charts that will prove the ground.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Get the city's held pump charts himself, tonight.
  Opposition:                       The shift supervisor, whose rule is that nobody is inside at night without leave; it is his station and his rule. Behind him, the hold on the records. Beside Lucien, Elisabet's caution about what charts can show, which he does not hear.
  Pressed by:                       The drainage board's procedure. Its authority is the board's; its stated justification is that nobody unauthorised is inside at night; the interest it serves is the public's infrastructure. It is reasonable, and Lucien breaks it. Trespass is the law's. The hold on the records has no stated authority, justification or interest. The supervisor answers to his own rules, not to the hold.
  Turn:                             The supervisor finds them, and takes Lucien's name and the name of his office.
  Consequence:                      Lucien has photographs of a week of charts and believes they confirm the ground. Elisabet tells him the pumps run most of the day in a wet spring, and he doesn't hear it. The access letter that got them in is now known to the supervisor. Lucien does not tell his office about the night.
  Unresolved:                       The supervisor has his name.
Change:                             Lucien breaks a reasonable rule for his theory, on his own account. His name is now on someone's record, and Elisabet's access is exposed.
Reader experience:                  A night raid on paper: the thrill of taking, the cold of being caught, and a man too sure to listen.
Keep / don't spend:                 The supervisor keeps his own motive, his station and his rule. He is nobody's proxy, and the narrator never says where the name goes next. The charts show pumps running and prove nothing about the Square; the page never lets them. Lucien gives his office's name because it is true; he voices no distrust of it and tells it nothing. Elisabet goes in for her own work and her own want, by her own choice. Not downtown, and nothing near the Warehouse District. The piece is not only its penalty: the station's ordinary night life stays in view. Standing guards: no clock time in narration unless someone does something with it (the charts' times are the thing taken); no named mechanism; the narrator certifies no cause and no blame; no bearing for Lucien's lean.
Exit conditions:                    The same night, late March, after they leave. Lucien and Elisabet each hold the photographs. The supervisor has Lucien's name and his office's, and has seen Elisabet's access letter. Lucien's office has not been told.
