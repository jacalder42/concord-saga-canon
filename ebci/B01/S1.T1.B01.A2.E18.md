## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Baz sees one himself, as the control on everything he has been told, and asks each of his friends what would prove them wrong.
Want:                               To see it with his own eyes, and to be one of them here.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Witness one himself, and set his friends' readings against what he saw.
  Opposition:                       Renita, who did not ask for a stranger in her kitchen. His friends' readings, which pull apart: Lucien reads the ground, Seraphine reads the people.
  Pressed by:                       A block's suspicion of strangers, earned: the street doesn't know him, and its interests are its own. Ordinary.
  Turn:                             He has seen it himself.
  Consequence:                      Baz is a witness now. Seraphine states her theory, that it finds people weighed down past what they can hold and spares those who are looked after, and names what would prove her wrong (someone having it with nothing weighing on them) without saying she has already seen something like it.
  Unresolved:                       Which of them is right.
Change:                             The three work as three for the first time, and one of them adopts a theory while keeping its disconfirmer to herself.
Reader experience:                  Three people reading the same minutes three ways, and the reader knowing one thing Baz doesn't.
Keep / don't spend:                 Baz perceives only what he sees and is told: he writes down her answer, never that she is holding anything back. What she keeps back is never narrated from inside her; the reader already has it. No formal name for it. No lore and no council of specialists: what the block knows is useful and is not the answer. Nobody states a rule: what Lucien finds at the corner is what he saw. Ordinary consequences only. Nobody asks Baz for anything.
Exit conditions:                    Late afternoon, the same day. The three are together at the corner by the barrels. Renita's fire is off, and her pot is scorched. Baz's notebook holds his own sighting, the block's accounts and each friend's answer to his question.
Required observable:                Along one block it comes up slowly and holds: everyone in reach stops what their hands are doing at once and starts again at once, a stove is left burning, and afterwards people don't talk about it. At the corner it is there on one side of the barrels and not the other.
Required consequence:               Seraphine turns Renita's fire off without being asked; Lucien, down at the corner, works his way back on his knees until it thins; Baz has his own sighting and three readings of it; Seraphine's theory and what would prove it wrong are on the record.
Prohibited on the page:             A mechanism; feeling, grief or care as cause; the narrator confirming Seraphine's theory or Lucien's edge; community knowledge as the hidden answer; any link to the camp or to a site; Baz perceiving her concealment.
Carry from Act I:                   Seraphine keeps a log of her own perception: whether she feels it early, late or not at all, where, and who was there. She began it after the Square and shows it to no one; it is hers. This one comes up slowly, and she logs it.
