## Narrative brief
Story job:                          The first undeniable civic-scale event: many people share it at once, and nobody can say what it was.
Want:                               Seraphine: to be the one who reaches the hurt. Lucien: to find what failed, with what he has on him.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Seraphine: reach three hurt strangers and get them seen to. Lucien: find what failed.
  Opposition:                       Clement, the performer, against a man in a Saints jersey who says Clement's amplifier did it; the city's new painted line on the stone and the order it stands for; then the thing itself, which nobody controls.
  Pressed by:                       The city's ordinary response: a young policeman taking names and accounts, who writes with more care than the moment seems to need, and a city that will want an official cause. Authority: the police. Justification: an incident with injuries needs a record. Interests: the city's. His own part is reasonable.
  Turn:                             Hundreds of people had it at the same moment.
  Consequence:                      Private becomes public. Lucien went down in front of the Square and gave his name, and, asked what he does, the Institute's; the policeman has both. Seraphine or Lucien may state, as their own limited finding, that everyone on the steady recording went down at once, as near as one recording can show: a finding about that recording, not a law (10-03, map §1; Q-SF3).
  Unresolved:                       What the city will make of it.
Change:                             What Lucien has hidden since the camp has now happened to the city.
Reader experience:                  An event undeniable in fact and disputed in cause.
Keep / don't spend:                 Nobody controls it; the page never resolves the cause, though characters may argue fault, casualty or coincidence. No word for it is needed: *pulse* loosely, or nothing. Seraphine feels nothing coming beforehand this time. Care reaches individuals; it does not calm the Square. Lucien's reading of the fallen gives him no direction, and he draws no rule from it. Whatever Lucien thinks of how the policeman writes stays Lucien's impression; nobody on the page says who will read it.
Exit conditions:                    Midday into afternoon, the Square. Clement has his own recording; a boy's phone has another. The amp is silent, and Lucien's check found no fault. The policeman has Lucien's name, the Institute's name and his account. The three strangers Seraphine reached have been seen to. Seraphine has begun logging her own perception every time (early, late or not at all).
Required observable:                During a disturbance some people hear as a low sound, a performer's amp quits, and people across the Square go down where they stand, at once and for seconds, facing every way; Clement's phone, clipped to his rig's stand, keeps recording, and its picture holds steady while the people in it fall.
Required consequence:               Minor injuries and a dead rig; two ordinary recordings exist; testimony disagrees; Seraphine reaches three hurt strangers; Lucien's check of the amp on the stone finds no fault, and he cannot make what he perceives resolve.
Prohibited on the page:             A mechanism or frequency; the crowd, the performer or Seraphine and Lucien's closeness as cause; the Square remembering; light, colour or changed geometry; any link to anything larger or anywhere else; the amp check as proof of cause either way.
