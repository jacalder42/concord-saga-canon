  Opposition:                       The post she promised to keep (tins borrowed from Miss Tavie, filled to the brim and watched); a frightened stranger at the Square's edge; her own pull to carry more than one.
  Pressed by:                       The grey case is gone from the fence: someone took it away. Whether anyone else measured the window is not known. No stated authority; whoever it is wanted only the time. Yesterday's refusal at the table stands, and the holds are still on. Nobody on the page today asks the three for anything.
  Turn:                             Unease rises and the stranger is caught in it, frightened. She leaves her post before her tin moves. Miss Tavie, who lent the tins and stays by them watching her go, sees the water move and cannot say when. Mrs. Carmouche's post on Royal shivers too, and she logs it herself and insists she touched nothing; Baz's and the middle stay still.
  Consequence:                      Seraphine waits; when the stranger is caught and frightened, she leaves her post for him, carries that one person, and declines the rest of the Square. It costs her body. Because she left first, her post records a movement and no onset; Lucien says aloud what her going cost the record, and she accepts it. For once, she got there in time. If anyone says what the result means, it is evidence against the chance model they tested, not proof.
