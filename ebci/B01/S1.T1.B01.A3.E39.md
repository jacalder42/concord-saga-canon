  Pressed by:                       Sal's contract, stopped by a city red tag issued on the complaint as it was reported. Sal's interests are his business and his crew. A collector on a schedule, faceless, working the dead hour, who takes and asks nothing. The drift: a method, above the city.
  Turn:                             At dawn a small car pulls in facing them with its lights on, so the plate sits behind the glare and the figure is lit from behind. Someone in a rain jacket, hood up, back turned, lanyard turned in, kneels, cuts the ties clean and takes the case. Whoever places the cases gets the city's complaint information.
  Consequence:                      Lucien confesses to Sal, and is off the job. His photograph is glare. He goes for the car door by stepping into the road in front of a car that is already moving, and Seraphine holds his arm; the car leaves. He blames her. She is late for work. He now holds evidence of access (whoever places the cases gets the city's complaint information) that he can't use without confessing how he got it.
  Unresolved:                       Who, and why.
Change:                             Lucien and Sal: working together → Lucien off the job, unforgiven. Seraphine and Lucien: Before: trust that has come through a fight. After: the trust still works, and he blames her for the arm; nothing said repairs it.
Reader experience:                  A long night in a car, one laugh, and a dawn that comes to nothing he can use.
Keep / don't spend:                 The failure has a practical cause: the lights in their faces, the glare in the photograph, and a moving car. The collector has no face, no name, no plate read and no logo; the car leaves and nobody follows it. The case shows no reading and does nothing. The trap shows access only, not who and not why; Lucien may infer more, and the page certifies nothing; his thoughts register the route, never a motive. The narrator doesn't take sides in the blame: Lucien blames Seraphine, and the page neither agrees nor disagrees. Sal's anger is his own. Nobody says the three are watched, and nobody asks them for anything. Baz is not there. No clock time unless someone acts on it.
Exit conditions:                    The next morning. The case is gone from the jack post. Lucien is off Sal's job, and Sal knows what he did. Seraphine knows why the case was there, and is late for work. Lucien holds what he saw. Inside the window, nothing has come yet.
Carry from Act II:                  Seraphine learns of the false report here, for the first time. The Robertson corner case is hers: she found it, wrote it down, and texted the others about it, and it is what gave Lucien the idea. Lucien's own Dauphine case is still untold. Since St. Roch, Miss Tavie asks things of Seraphine as well as giving them. Her work the next morning is the care center, where Denise is watching.
