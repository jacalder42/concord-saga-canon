## Narrative brief
Story job:                          The trap proves one thing, access, and costs Lucien his place on Sal's crew, his phone, and something with Seraphine; a kiss neither of them will admit to.
Want:                               To know who reads the city's complaint line.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        See who comes for the case his false report brought.
  Opposition:                       Sal, whose job the city stopped on Lucien's lie. The collector, who keeps a schedule, comes for the case, faces Lucien at the post and will not leave without it (10-03, map §3). Seraphine, whose hand holds his arm as the car goes, against his want.
  Pressed by:                       Sal's contract, stopped by a city red tag: the complaint brought the inspector, and the inspector's own finding (a two-story cottage on bottle jacks, its shoring under way) stopped the job (10-03, map §3, Q-SF7). Sal's interests are his business and his crew. A collector on a schedule, working the dead hour, who wants the case and the record it holds of where and when, and takes it; seen and heard, unnamed, naming no employer and recruiting no one (10-03, map §0.1, §3). The drift: a method, above the city.
  Turn:                             Toward dawn the collector walks toward the case, past their parked car, and they pose as a couple: a cover kiss, which runs a beat too long. When it ends they have lost the moment, and the collector is already at the post. Lucien gets out. At the post words are exchanged, and the collector is seen: a face, a voice, the recurring kit clue on the case: a small printed asset tag, its code beginning *TD-* and a different number (as fixed at E31). The collector cuts the ties and takes the case; in the struggle Lucien's phone, with the night's photographs on it, goes with it (10-03, map §3, Q-PI6a, H1). Whoever places the cases gets the city's complaint information.
  Consequence:                      Lucien confesses to Sal, and is off the job. Sal's loss is concrete on the page and lasts: men of his crew out of work who need the wages, a client who now doubts him, and the job stopped for weeks, with his name on the tag. The collector leaves by car; Lucien steps into the road in front of a car that is already moving, and Seraphine holds his arm; the car leaves, its plate behind its lights. His photographs are gone with his phone. He blames her for the arm. Both of them say the kiss was nothing, and it did not count (10-03, map §3, H1). She is late for work. He now holds evidence of access (whoever places the cases gets the city's complaint information) that he can't use without confessing how he got it, and a face he could know again.
  Unresolved:                       Who, and why.
Change:                             Lucien and Sal: working together → Lucien off the job, unforgiven. Seraphine and Lucien: Before: trust that has come through a fight. After: the trust still works, he blames her for the arm, and nothing said repairs it; between them is a kiss both have called nothing (10-03, H1).
Reader experience:                  A long night in a car, one laugh, a kiss that was only cover and went on too long, and a dawn that costs him what he came to keep.

Lucien is off Sal's job, and Sal knows what he did; Sal's job is stopped, and his crew is without work. Seraphine knows why the case was there, and is late for work. Lucien holds what he saw, and the collector's face. The kiss is between them, called nothing by both. Inside the window, nothing has come yet.
