## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Move the strange from private perception into public behaviour.
Want:                               Get Keon, eighteen, through the office with the card he needs to start a job on Monday.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Seraphine is there on her own casework with Keon, a young client who cannot start his job without the card.
  Opposition:                       A man in the room who is on a warning at his own job and cannot be late again, pushing at the clerk; the guard by the door, deciding who in the room is the problem, with his eyes on Keon.
  Pressed by:                       Ordinary friction, no will behind it. The office's discipline: the guard's authority is the office's, his justification order in a public room, and his interest the office's and his own long tenure. The man's employer, whose warning presses on him. Her own boss's doubt about whether she is ready to be back.
  Turn:                             Everyone in the room stops at the same moment, hands and all, and then lets go together.
  Consequence:                      When it stops, Keon moves on his own, from his grandmother's training, to pick up an old man's scattered papers under the guard's eye, and Seraphine fails to stop him. Her doubt, and her fear of what the guard saw, come after. It risks Keon's appointment and her own standing on her third day back; it holds, and Keon keeps his place for now. Afterwards nobody mentions it, and she writes down that everyone in the room went together (10-03, map §1; Q-SF9).
  Unresolved:                       It will happen again.
Change:                             A room full of strangers does something together, and afterwards behaves as if it hadn't.
Reader experience:                  Competence under pressure, in a room with its own life.
Keep / don't spend:                 Everyone has an ordinary reason to be there; nobody is a puppet; she calms no one by any aura, only by what she does and says. Keon's move is his own, not hers to arrange; she reaches for him too late, and does not decide for him (10-03, map §1). The guard and the man on a warning keep their own motives. Nothing is explained. No clock time in narration unless a character does something with it.
Exit conditions:                    The same morning. Keon has his receipt and keeps his start date; the card follows by post. Seraphine has a line in her notebook about everyone going together. She goes back to the center.
Required observable:                Everyone in reach stops what their hands are doing at the same moment, and starts again at the same moment; afterwards almost nobody mentions it.
Required consequence:               The brittle moment passes without anyone hurt, and she records it (10-03, map §1: *by what she did* cut, since the move is Keon's).
Prohibited on the page:             A stated cause; anyone's thoughts read; a law stated by the narrator; the event linked to the camp by anyone.
