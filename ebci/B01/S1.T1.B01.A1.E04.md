## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Introduce Lucien through how he sees, then make his own perception unreliable, and put a lie on a legal record.
Want:                               His standing: a clean, bounded job, and a place on the form for what he is told.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        On a structural site visit for the coroner's file, Lucien tries to fit Seraphine's account into the form's boxes and to keep his own finding clean.
  Opposition:                       Seraphine, who insists her account goes on the form in her own words.
  Pressed by:                       Ordinary friction, no will behind it. His posting: he is in New Orleans for the Hollen Institute for Civic Continuity (Vienna), on its Regional Review of Unexpected Public Costs for the parishes around the city, which is why he is on the coroner's contract list; a weekly note and a report are owed to the Institute. He knows the Institute is a cover for the people who sent him. Structural timber is his expertise, and his work with Sal is how he learns the city's building stock. The coroner's form and its fixed lines: the office's authority, an accurate record as its justification, the record's interest. Sal's schedule: a contractor's authority over his own job and crew, in his own interest.
  Turn:                             In the room where the boy died, while he is taking her account, something takes him bodily and he goes down.
  Consequence:                      He chose to write her words in; after going down, he chooses to write *None* on the observation line, a false statement on a legal record, and his breath hitches, or he sighs, as he writes it. Both are on the form now.
  Unresolved:                       The lie on the form.
Change:                             A line looks wrong once, and he checks it and finds nothing; then the room takes him, and he hides how bad it was. Before: strangers. After: she is his witness, and her words sit on the record beside his lie, which she does not know.
Reader experience:                  New Orleans seen by a precise, private man, and two ways of knowing meeting badly.
Keep / don't spend:                 Seraphine is a witness and a person, not a mystic or an expert. Nothing objective bends: every check he makes finds nothing wrong. The lean appears once, faint and unnamed: a corner post reads out of true, toward the low sun, and that is the only bearing he is ever given; he does not map, name or interpret it. The form is described so that a listener follows it: an employer line, where he writes the Institute, and an observation line whose full heading asks for conditions observed during the inspection affecting the health or safety of occupants, including the inspector. The hitch says only that he is uncomfortable with something; Seraphine is not there and learns nothing of the *None*. The Institute may be named and he may think of it as a cover; the people behind it are never named or described, and no other sponsor, family history or superior appears. He may be from Vienna, with no scene there. The camp is a house where a boy died, nothing more, and nobody infers anything about the place. No clock time in narration unless a character does something with it.
Exit conditions:                    After the Saturday site visit. The form, with the Institute on its employer line, her paragraph and his *None*, is with Guidry. Lucien is back in the city; his weekly note to the Institute is sent. He has told no one how bad it was.
