## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Reward restraint. A slow one comes up in the Marigny and holds still, and its middle is a house holding a repast. Seraphine tests the edges and holds back from the house she wants to enter.
Want:                               To go into the grieving house and be the one who holds it; and to test what she believes about who it comes to.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Reach the grieving house; meanwhile, walk the edges in and out so Lucien can mark them.
  Opposition:                       Baz, who argues that nobody on these blocks has been told anything, so going in spends the only clean witnesses they have. A news crew, which climbs the mourners' steps. Her own fear of being too late.
  Pressed by:                       A news crew on a mourner's steps. It has no authority but the camera's; its justification is the story; the interest served is the station's. It is ordinary, and self-serving.
  Turn:                             The house laughs, and the pressure falls.
  Consequence:                      Restraint is rewarded. The edges held all day, and the house got through its day without her. Seraphine takes the fall as proof that people who are cared for are spared it.
  Unresolved:                       Whether it counts on the page.
Change:                             Seraphine chooses restraint against her fear of being too late, and it is shown sound before the day is out. Her theory feels proved to her.
Reader experience:                  A choice against instinct, proved sound, and a proof the reader can see is one coincidence.
Keep / don't spend:                 Pressure zone, never drift. What she feels stays in the Marigny: nothing places it at the camp or the swamp or links it to anything later. Lucien's lean on his feet stays his, with no direction, landmark or bearing. Baz argues for withdrawal; Lucien resists, wanting the middle, then accepts. Her one move toward the house stops at the road. Standing guards: no clock time in narration unless someone does something with it (the chalk's times are Lucien's test); no named mechanism; no rule stated as a law; the narrator certifies no cause and no blame; nothing foreshadows harm to Baz.
Exit conditions:                    The same Saturday, early evening, the Marigny. Lucien's chalk marks the edge on eleven curbs; the pressure never moved off it, and it has gone. None of the three went into the house. The house has sent them plates. The three are due at the Vein tonight.
