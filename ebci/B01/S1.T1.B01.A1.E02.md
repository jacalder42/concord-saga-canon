## Narrative brief
Story job:                          Separate grief from anomaly, then show that it persists.
Want:                               To have someone say she did it wrong.
Conflict:
  Objective:                        Seraphine wants her failure on the record: she asks the parish coroner's investigator, Guidry, to write down that she did it wrong.
  Opposition:                       Guidry's procedure, which records what happened and not blame; Odile, who will not let any of it land on Renée, and who spoke of the card in the night: Seraphine is waiting for her to say it.
  Pressed by:                       Ordinary friction, no will behind it. The parish coroner's procedure: its authority is the office's, its justification an accurate record, its interest the file's. The generator, and a carboxyhemoglobin result left equivocal by the oxygen given in the ambulance and at the hospital, lead Guidry to request a dwelling hazard check (carbon monoxide, ventilation, electrical) from the parish's contracted firm. The death certificate will issue pending on the labs (10-03, map §1).
  Turn:                             With the boy gone, the pressure is still in the room, with nobody in it carrying anything (10-03, map §1: he was taken in the night).
  Consequence:                      She is refused the record she asked for. She tests the pressure herself, walking the road's steps twice: it thins about forty steps out and is gone by about sixty. She keeps that to herself, and leaves with a private rule, not yet in words: next time, arrive sooner.
  Unresolved:                       A hazard check will come for the house; the certificate is pending (10-03, map §1).
Change:                             Something lingers that does not behave like grief, and she will not call it a ghost.
Reader experience:                  Care that costs, and a thing that stays when it should not.
Keep / don't spend:                 The coroner's office, and Guidry's windbreaker, are the parish's; nothing here is a county's. The residue stays unnamed: no ghost, no explanation, no site. Her walk is her own test and she draws no conclusion from it. Odile's protectiveness is her own, and right. No clock time in narration unless a character does something with it.
Exit conditions:                    The next morning. Guidry has said a hazard check will come for the house, and that the certificate will issue pending (10-03, map §1); Renée's sister has taken Renée to Metairie, and Renée gave Seraphine one long look and said nothing; Odile stays. Seraphine leaves the camp, alone, with what she found in the road unsaid.
