Want:                               A model that says it first: to be right before the fact, on paper.
Conflict:

  Opposition:                       Elisabet, whose objection is that his pattern may only record where he and Seraphine happened to be standing. Behind her, the hold on the pump records she was promised.
  Pressed by:                       The records are held for "upstairs": no authority, justification or interest is given. It is the will from Poydras, now standing between him and the data. There is also Elisabet's own permit, which is hers to risk.

  Consequence:                      He is committed to the ground: the pumps pull the water out, and the city settles. He has a pact with Elisabet for the charts, made outside his office.
  Unresolved:                       The charts.
Change:                             Lucien goes from a pattern to a cause of his own, and from his office's channels to a private trade.
Reader experience:                  Discovery, briefly a pleasure; then the itch of wanting to be right.

He holds his theory of the ground. He and Elisabet have agreed to get the pump charts together. His office has not been asked. Baz has his copies of the two recordings; the originals stay with their owners.
