Conflict:

  Opposition:                       Renita, who would like the stranger to stop sitting on her neighbour's step so the block can get on with its afternoon. Seraphine, who refuses his split-street design because it would leave households like Renita's untold.
  Pressed by:                       The block's own rules about who gets told and who sits where. The authority and interests are the neighbours' own, and the justification is their own day. It is reasonable. Nothing from the city is in this piece.
  Turn:                             The page is signed. What people are told carries no days and no hour.
  Consequence:                      A bet exists in writing. Baz signs, with his dissent written on the back. He tells Mr. Benoit himself, his last untold witness, so his clean sample is spent.
  Unresolved:                       Ten days.
Change:                             The pattern becomes a committed, checkable bet with three names under it. Baz chooses belonging over a cleaner test, and records his dissent instead of refusing.
Reader experience:                  Method with stakes: a page that will change how people behave, and a friend who loses an argument well.
Keep / don't spend:                 The page is a bet, not a finding. Its watch items are the characters' own: Lucien's (the street, the ground, an edge) come from his theory of the ground, and Seraphine's (it coming up slowly) from her ears. The page expects each event to be bigger than the last. The narrator certifies none of it. Prepare people; never try to stop anything; no exact-second prophecy. Renita keeps her own reasons: she is tired of it, not hostile. The window's dates stay off the page, though its length may be said. Standing guards: no clock time in narration unless someone does something with it (the page is a decision, not a tally); no named mechanism; the narrator certifies no cause and no blame; nothing Baz plans or enjoys foreshadows harm to him.
Exit conditions:                    Saturday evening, late March. The page is signed by the three. Lucien keeps it; Baz has a copy with his dissent on the back. Mr. Benoit has been told. The ten-day window has been written, to open in nine days, the Monday after Easter; and telling has begun inside its lines, each telling recorded.
