No explanation reaches her, from him or from the page. It changes how she judges him and what she does with him in the days that follow. At six, Odile's call shows that Odile knows she told Renée.
  Unresolved:                       The family asks whether the man will come back, and nobody can say. The certificate is still pending, and whether Guidry holds the question open is his to decide. What she will do with what she now knows about Lucien.
