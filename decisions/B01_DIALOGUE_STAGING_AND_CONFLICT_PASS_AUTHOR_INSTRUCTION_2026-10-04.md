## What the pass does

1. **Staging.** Important lines get enough reception, physical behavior, silence, POV interpretation and change for the
   reader to know how they land. Physical description returns where it was thinned. Emotions are not explained by
   labels. The rule: dramatize change, compress information, and don't explain a change that is already dramatized.
2. **Conflict and PCM.** PCM and the Companions are modes, not a compatibility system; they stay behind the page, and
   integration is rare (`decisions/CALDER_COMPANIONS_AND_B01_SHAPE_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-29.md`).
   - **Misreading:** for each consequential conversation, map intent → line → reception → interpretation → reaction →
     the speaker's reading of that reaction. Allow believable, character-specific misreads, escalation, breaks, and
     unrepaired consequences that persist across scenes.
   - **Repair** needs its own cause and time.
   - **No manufactured hostility,** and nobody is made artificially stupid or artificially wise.
3. **Priority scenes:**
   - E21 (Seraphine and Denise);
   - E26 (Baz, Seraphine and Lucien);
   - E30, kept as the positive control;
   - the major Seraphine–Lucien confrontations;
   - E39 and its aftermath;
   - E46, Baz's failed attempt, protected;
   - E49, the disclosure: it should feel extracted, not delivered.

## Order

1. An audit table of consequential conversations: participants, wants, pressure, the failure mode the sources support,
   the staging quality, the degree of mutual understanding, whether repair is earned, the proposed intervention, and the
   downstream consequence.
2. Each scene is classed PROTECT / STAGE MORE / MISREAD-ESCALATE / DELAY REPAIR / COMBINATION.
3. **Any change to plot, canon, established character behavior, an episode outcome or the relationship architecture is
   held for the author.**
4. Revision of everything else.
5. A blind dialogue read with no PCM terms.
6. A continuity check, then reading copies and a fresh import copy. The working draft may grow; the import copy stays
   under 120k.

**It does not change** any ruling, card or packet. Held items come back as questions.
