Status: CURRENT AUTHOR INSTRUCTION (production), 2026-10-05. It authorises two pieces of drafting. It is **not** a ruling
on canon and approves no content (CLAUDE.md §4).

## What is accepted

1. **Dialogue voice sheets are drafted** as a proposal, `proposals/DIALOGUE_VOICE_SHEETS_2026-10-05.md`. They are
   **review-side**, like `ebci/prose/REVIEW_WATCHLIST.md`: for revisers, checkpoints and the author's line edit.
2. **The deferral on authoring the editorial-lens fields is lifted.** The 2026-09-19 ruling deferred the 23
   `Asks` / `Flags` / `Protects` fields until recovery and distillation were complete (`canon/editorial_lenses.md`; CLAUDE.md
   §4.1). The author judges that too exclusive for this phase. The fields are drafted as a proposal,
   `proposals/EDITORIAL_LENSES_AUTHORING_PROPOSAL_2026-10-05.md`.

## What is not accepted or changed

- **Q-WP3 stands** (`decisions/WRITER_PROFILE_APPROVAL_AND_PROSE_CALIBRATION_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-27.md`): no permanent
  per-character voice layer in the drafting input. The sheets are not part of the drafting stack. Putting any of them
  into a prose packet needs its own decision (Q-DV1).
- **Nothing is promoted to canon.** Both files are Tier D proposals. The sheets' speech habits are inferences marked
  `[I]` and have not been tested against the manuscript, which Claude cannot read.
- **`canon/editorial_lenses.md` is not edited.** The `TODO` fields stay until the author says *proceed* on the proposal
  (Q-EL2). `CLAUDE.md` §4.1's deferral note is therefore unchanged for now, and goes stale on application.
- **The 2026-09-19 ruling's second concern is kept:** no lens criteria are inferred from board members' published work or
  attributed to them. The proposal says how it was written to respect that.
- No card, rule, packet, grid, profile line or manuscript text changes.
