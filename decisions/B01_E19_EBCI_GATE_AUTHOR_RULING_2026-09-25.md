## What this changes in practice — nothing yet

**Supersession is not release.** Before this ruling a session faced two prohibitions on
building B01 episodes; it now faces one, **and that one is still closed**. The set of
episodes that may be generated today is **unchanged: none.**

The gate that remains is `CLAUDE.md` §9 item 5 — **"Release B01 to EBCI — author gate"** —
and it has not been opened. Work continues at item 1–4: the event census, the whole-book
rhythm pass, cast reconciliation.

**A reading of "E19 is unblocked" is wrong** and is the specific misreading this document
exists to prevent. `E19` has no privileged status now; it is one of 48 episodes behind a
single hold, rather than the first episode behind two.

## Scope

separately by `CLAUDE.md` §9 item 6 — *"No B02–B09 episode expansion yet"* — which this
ruling does not touch.
