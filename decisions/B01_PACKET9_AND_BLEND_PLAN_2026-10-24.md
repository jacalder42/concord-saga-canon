## New rules for B
1. Set-pieces are never cut or compressed.
2. Signature voice lines and jokes are kept, and reworded only if they get stronger.
3. **Texture floor,** per scene: similes at about 1.5 per 1,000 words and inner thought at about 2.8 per 1,000, the reference-novel rates. Don't cut below either unless the scene starts above it.
4. Rebuild rather than just cut, so the share of short sentences doesn't rise.
5. The standing rules still apply: Lucien uncontracted; no narrator rhetorical questions; numbers only where the plot needs them; protected and echo lines; no editing instructions in the text; open threads protected.
6. Per-scene counts are logged for similes, inner thought, short-sentence share and blocking.
