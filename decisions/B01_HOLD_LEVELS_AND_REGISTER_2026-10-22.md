## Ruling
1. **Every B01 hold carries one of five levels:**
   - **Exact:** the words are fixed.
   - **Precise:** the fact is fixed; the wording is free.
   - **General:** the intent is fixed; the execution is free.
   - **Subtle:** present and never confirmed, with a visibility of 1st, re or later.
   - **Withheld:** must not appear yet.
2. **Level is separate from status** (ruled, approved, direction).
3. **Defaults:**
   - A quoted line is Exact only if the author said "verbatim", it sits in a locked passage, or a later chapter calls it back. Otherwise it is Precise.
   - When the level is unclear, the hold is treated as General and flagged, never frozen.
4. **The register.** One B01 holds register, on the manuscript branch at `draft-notes/final-2026-10-18/B01_HOLDS_REGISTER.md`, lists the active holds with their level and source. Drafting briefs and check passes work against it.
5. **Maintenance.** Each new ruling that touches B01 updates the register in the same commit.

## The Prologue
- Only "He kept the record. She kept the stories.", the screen-door line, and "There's a dog in the road" are Exact. Silence and Hope's names are Withheld.
- "Tie the watchers to events on earth" and the presences' fewest-words rule are General.
- **Revising the Prologue's prose is permitted** as long as these intents hold.
