## 2. The author's read of B01 (the human read)

> *"In general, I find the prose enjoyable to read, it is approachable. There are still some negative confirmations, and
> and and, and other generative tendencies, but overall it is good. I like the quality of life given to the everyday
> people they work with, but sometimes it feels like the protagonists are just replying to them, and not actively
> participating in the life of the book, like they are just third party observers. I think there is a large amount of
> focus on time and counting. My gut instinct is that happens because there isn't much action to be done instead, that
> is what we need to work on. I also recognize that resonance is not necessarily a known commodity yet, but everything
> seems super vague and inferred. I think the mystery could hold for awhile, but something is going to need to be
> driving the mystery. There could be wrong conspiracy theories, or subtle clues to other actors, etc. A whole book
> about running around a neighborhood and talking to people, even if well written, is going to lose reader
> interest."*

**What Claude takes from it:**

| Finding | Where it goes |
| --- | --- |
| **The prose works.** It is enjoyable and approachable, and the everyday people's lives are a strength | Kept. The voice is not the problem |
| **Generative residue:** negative confirmations, *"and … and … and"* chains | Added to the review watch-list, review-side (§3). A profile line is a question for the author |
| **The protagonists are observers:** they reply instead of participating | **B01 architecture.** A mystery and action engine for the ~150k redraft (§4) |
| **Time and counting substitute for action** | The same design pass |
| **The strange is too vague;** the mystery needs a driver (wrong theories, clues to other actors) | The same design pass. The private causal cards (approved 09-27) give concrete, consistent effects without explaining them; D3 (ruled) already keeps competing explanations in characters' mouths |
| **A book of walking and talking will lose readers** | The same design pass, together with the antagonist options (`proposals/B01_ANTAGONIST_PRESENCE_OPTIONS_2026-09-29.md`, Q-AN1–4, still open) |

**It likely applies beyond B01.** The Veil beat revision (Q-CE2 step 2) checks B02–B03 for the same shape.
