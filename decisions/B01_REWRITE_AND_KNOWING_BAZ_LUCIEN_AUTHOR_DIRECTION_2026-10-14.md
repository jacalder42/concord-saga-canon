## 2. What it means

1. **Rewrite, not polish.** B01 is drafted fresh from a new outline. Braids 2–4 stay as reference; scenes are compared with
   them afterward and merged or blended where the old one is better. The old text is not the base.
2. **Fewer confessions; realistic behavior first.** Confession-shaped scenes are limited and costed (proposal §5).
3. **Baz knows the *why*; Lucien knows the *what*,** from before page one. The "when and how to tell" becomes the human
   engine: the strife is inside the characters as well as outside them. Dominion and Caldas can act more overtly, with backstory.
4. **The investigation still has something to find.** The crew's discoveries test, correct and extend what Baz and Lucien
   already hold. (Proposal §3.)
