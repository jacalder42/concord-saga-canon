# Nyla's B03 retaking: the in-town boarding school (author ruling, 2026-10-28)

**Status:** current author ruling (a lean, adopted as the working design). Ledger §629. It answers §625 §3's open coercion mechanism.

**The author:** *"For Nyla, I lean that her family is struggling to adjust to her life with resonance and are convinced to let someone else help, like an intown boarding school for Nyla."*

## Ruled (working design)
- **The coercion is persuasion, not force.**
  - After the B02 escape, Nyla's family is struggling to live with what she is, and with what keeps happening around her.
  - They are **convinced to let someone else help**: an **in-town boarding school** that offers care, structure and safety.
  - Lanette agrees, under strain. The coercion is the exhaustion and the offer, not a threat.
- **The school is a front of the funder faction's program** (§626). From it Nyla is moved to the conversion site (B03 E37, the Warehouse's older section).
- **The page stays off the procedure** (§625 §1.5). The taking is felt through Lanette, as a choice she made for her child that turns out to be the one thing she had refused.

## The writer thread's design notes (DESIGN; the author may override)
- **The B01 refusal still holds its shape.** Lanette refused the Hollen Foundation by name (B01 C43). The school must **not** present as the Foundation: it carries another name, and it comes to her through someone or something she trusts. Her consent is to a school, not to Hollen.
- **The echo of Vienna.** In B01, Vienna's Lena was "taken to the school" and keeps "a glass of water by the bed. Every night" (C37). A reader who remembers that can feel the dread when New Orleans gets a school, with nothing stated.
- **What Lanette knows.** At B03 E12 she knows where Nyla is (the school) but not what it is. She is not yet afraid. The dread is the reader's.
