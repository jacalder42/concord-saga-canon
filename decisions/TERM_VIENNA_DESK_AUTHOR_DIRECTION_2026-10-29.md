# Term change: "the Review" → "the Vienna desk" (author direction, 2026-10-29)

**Status:** author-directed canon-term change. Ledger §632. B02/B03 consistency is the writer thread's decision.

1. **The term.** In narration and speech, Lucien's Vienna office is **"the Vienna desk"**, or **"the desk"** where it cannot be mistaken for a literal desk. It is no longer called "the Review".
2. **Paper only.** The formal name **"Regional Review"** survives on paper only: forms, letterheads and quoted letters (for example *HOLLEN INSTITUTE FOR CIVIC CONTINUITY — REGIONAL REVIEW, VIENNA*).
3. **Definition.** Defined once in B01 at C04:179: *The Vienna desk was the Hollen office its field observers reported to. It read what they sent and filed it…*
4. **Canon fact unchanged.** It is the Hollen Institute's Regional Review in Vienna, a Dominion front (§617): Lucien's desk (Marius, N.), the body that suspends him (C43), and the body that invites Elisabet to Vienna (§620 §6).
5. **C34 post-office line (B01 I7.2, C34:178):** "Vienna writes it and the City paints it. The Vienna desk is their post office." This supersedes "The Review is their post office." (§630).
6. **Scope.** B02 and B03 follow the change (writer thread). Living documents are swept; historical decision files and ledger entries are records and are not edited.
7. **Not affected.** "Review C01", "Review C04" and similar (references to review passes), and supplement titles such as *Mandatory Safety Review*, are not this office.
