# Table-read pilot and "action over statement" — author instruction

Status: CURRENT AUTHOR INSTRUCTION, 2026-10-06. Approved design.

**The author's question:** whether assigning an *actor* to each character, one that receives only the information
needed to write that character's speech and actions, would avoid mechanical, artificial-sounding interactions. He
supplied an example of artificial speech corrected by action.

**Claude's proposal:**
- **A table read.**
  - A director sets the scene.
  - Each actor holds a private card: only what that character knows at that moment, what they want, what they won't
    say, what they fear, their voice and their habits. Actors play in turns, each turn an action, a line or silence.
  - A writer renders the transcript in the POV character's prose under the profile, cutting hard.
  - A checker checks continuity and the guards.
- **A pilot first**, on three scenes: E39 (the confession and the kiss), E46's night scene, and E48's river argument
  between Baz and Lucien. Then a blind comparison against the revised draft (`B01/revised-2026-10-06/`).
- **The example** goes into profile §5 as an illustration.

**The answer, in chat:** *"Proceed."*

## What it approves

- The pilot.
- The method document `ebci/prose/TABLE_READ_METHOD.md`, a production method kept outside the drafting stack.
- The §5 illustration, added 2026-10-06.

## What it does not approve
