- **"Unrestricted" means that nothing is off the table for proposing.** Each proposal is tagged with what it would
  reopen: ruled canon, an author ruling on the profile, approved design, an assistant guard, or a protected line.
  Nothing is applied without the author's answer. Anything that touches ruled canon still needs a specific ruling
  (CLAUDE.md §4).
- **The Q-SM8 blind reads after the pass** take the book-critic perspective too.
- All work is read-only, in the private manuscript repository (`draft-notes/brutal-round-2026-10-09/`).
