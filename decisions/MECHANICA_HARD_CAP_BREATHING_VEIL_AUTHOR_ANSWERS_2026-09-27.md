### 1.1 The approved model (answer 1)

**The seven answers:**

1. **What the soul contributes:** Intent, a lasting commitment. *"Hold the line"* makes a cap;
   *"let it breathe"* makes a membrane.
2. **What the old Loom regulated:** a single fixed ceiling on resonance crossing the boundary, not
   people. Mira made no choices about who was safe.
3. **Why the hard cap is brittle:**
   - blocked pressure is stored, not discharged;
   - it concentrates at the thinnest points;
   - one soul is a single point of failure;
   - shielded humans never adapt.
4. **What changes when Silence and Hope disperse:** regulation moves from their tending from outside
   to the veil's own laws. Laws apply themselves.
5. **What "filter" means:** energy passes over time, and only its peaks are limited. The breath
   (expansion and contraction) gives way under a surge and recovers.
6. **What Seraphine continues:** Intent and rhythm. She makes no decisions about people.
7. **Why no successor:** each cause of brittleness is removed (no accumulation, no concentration, no
   single point), and humans adapt.

**The D5 table in the specification (§3) is approved with the model:**

- wound stabilisation under the cap is **temporary**, costly and leaves a scar;
- mortals may attribute every effect to other causes.
