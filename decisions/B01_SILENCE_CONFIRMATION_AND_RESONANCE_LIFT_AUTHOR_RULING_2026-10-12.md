## 2. What is lifted

1. **Keeping the strange silent.**
   - The page may say what the phenomenon is, as far as the characters have learned it.
   - Every withholding guard written for that purpose is retired:
     - *Veil* only twice, and never explained;
     - Baz knows nothing;
     - the empty folder;
     - the felt net never tied to the events;
     - "*Resonance* stays out of Veil's page language" (2026-09-27).
2. **Knowing and confirmation.**
   - Characters may know causes, not only effects.
   - The page and the narrator may confirm a finding that the story has established.
   - This amends the profile §9 *Not the mechanism (ruled)* line and its Q-DQ4 scope sentence, and the related "the page does not settle the cause" clause.
   - This applies to the phenomenon. The separate *no blame, just inference* rule for blame between people is a different matter and is not changed here.
3. **Vocabulary.**
   - *Resonance* may appear on the page in Veil.
   - This amends the profile §9 line *"In the first three books, the city's words are ordinary… No technical or metaphysical vocabulary"*, as far as *resonance* and the Veil's own terms go.
   - *No numbers used as imagery* is a craft line and stays as written.
4. **The "first one" flag is withdrawn.**
   - The proposal's flag tied Caldas's "the first one" to Tahl's B06 line "the first one has to be last".
   - The author judged it overreaching: the association is not clear.
   - Caldas may say "the first one". No guard is placed on the phrase.
