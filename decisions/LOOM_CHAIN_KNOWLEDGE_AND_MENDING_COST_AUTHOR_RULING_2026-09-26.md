## 3. The locked Finale Phase Map against the 12-07 ascension order

**The author asked for the differences, not a choice.** Two sources:

- **The map**, locked 2025-11-15 (`PC` 93542–93903, *"Lock"* at 93904). It is a structure, set out
  in phases.
- **The 12-07 recollection** (`BBR` 8713, 9535, 9751). It is a sequence:
  - *"Seraphine > Hope > Silence (after helping power Tahl's echo) > Lucien > goodbye > Caro."*
  - *"Rex and Elisabet are trying to hold off the antagonists, Rex tells Elisabet to go warn the
    others, Elias attacks, Rex knocks him down, Kade fears Rex will kill Elias, and attacks Rex,
    then the Tahl echo."*
  - Elisabet *"made it there after Silence and Hope had become part of the veil but before Caro had
    ascended."*

### Where they agree

- Seraphine goes first: map 1.1 has her initiate the Loom interface, and 12-07 has her first.
- Rex sends Elisabet ahead (map 2.2; 12-07).
- Kade moves on Rex, the flare stops him, and Tahl is a VT presence (map 3.1–3.3; 12-07).
- Silence and Hope give themselves up, and Lucien and Caro take their places: map 4.3–5.3 (*"Lucien
  becomes Silence," "Caro becomes Hope"*); 12-07; and **the author today**.
- Elisabet arrives to witness (map 4.5; 12-07).

### Where they differ

| # | Point | The map (11-15) | 12-07 and later | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a | **Who is the "Guide"** | **Tahl / VT** is *"A Guide"*; Lucien and Caro are *"Two Anchors"* | **Today: Lucien and Caro *"become the guides."*** The 09-26 rulings make Tahl's echo the **LT conduit** | **A word clash, not a plot clash.** One reading: Lucien and Caro are the guides (the new Silence and Hope), and Tahl is the conduit. Terminology is the author's to settle |

Silence goes **after helping power Tahl's echo** | 12-07 adds the **Silence boost** (the author's own idea, 11-15 and 11-27) and **staggers** the two. The map has no boost |
| c | **When the flare happens** | **Mid-Mending** (Phase 3), **before** Silence and Hope understand (Phase 4) | The flare is the **penultimate chapter** and the Mending the **last** (author, 11-28). Silence powers the echo **before** unravelling | Compatible if the chapters intercut. The **causal link** differs: in 12-07 Silence's boost **causes** the flare's strength; in the map the flare is independent |
| d | **The order of the transformations** | Silence and Hope dissolve, then Lucien and Caro move into place, then Elisabet arrives, then Seraphine, Lucien and Caro transform **together** (Phase 5) | **Strictly sequential:** Seraphine, Hope, Silence, **Lucien**, **the goodbye**, **Caro** | 12-07 **separates Lucien and Caro** and puts **Elisabet's goodbye to Caro between them** |

| f | **The attack on Rex** | Kade approaches Rex's blind spot *"driven by desperation"*. **No Elias**: the map predates him, and Kade leads his own splinter faction | **Elias attacks first; Rex knocks him down; Kade, fearing Rex will kill Elias, attacks Rex** (ruled 09-26) | **Superseded** by the later author choreography and the 09-26 motive ruling |

Echo appearances are penultimate-chapter only (Tier-1). In the epilogue he is the **LT conduit**, implied (ruled) | Compatible: the map's *"link"* is the ruled conduit |

**In short:** the **map is the structure** (who becomes what) and **12-07 is the sequence** (who goes
when, and the human beats). They mostly nest. **The real differences** are three:

1. **Silence's boost of Tahl's echo** (12-07) makes the flare and the unravelling one causal chain.
2. Silence and Hope go **one at a time**; Lucien and Caro go **one at a time**, with **Elisabet's
   goodbye** between.
3. **Elias's** place in the attack, which the map predates.

The **"Guide"** word is used in different senses.

**For the author, only if he wants to settle it:**

- Use the 12-07 order inside the map's structure?
- Keep "Guide" for Lucien and Caro, with Tahl as the conduit?
