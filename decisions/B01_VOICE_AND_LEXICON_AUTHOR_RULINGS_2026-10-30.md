## 2. Elisabet: register-dependent contraction

- This supersedes the I8 brief's "no contractions" and the register's "verdicts uncontracted". ElisabetID gets a note.

## 3. The lexicon: "loading", "storage" and "scar" are allowed in every sense
- **The resonance sense is now allowed too.** This closes the §637 open item and supersedes the §620/§621 page bar on "storage" and "loading", and the bar on "scar" as a term.
  - "Wound" (capitalised, as the reserved term for the four) was not part of this ruling, and stays reserved.
  - "Breathing" stays reserved.
- **Condition:** every resonance-sense use is **flagged for review** (the editing thread's guard script reports it). The writer gate checks each flagged use against:
  - **the cap (§584):** nothing says the Veil, the net or the world was built, holds something back, or stores pressure toward a release;
  - **the meta-storage line (Mechanica §48; Nyla path 1, §625):** "storing" or "holding" what cannot be spent is B03's conversion vocabulary. A B01 use must not prefigure it as a mechanism;
  - **the site rule:** nothing counts, orders or links sites;
  - **S-well:** nothing out-forecasts Dominion.
- **I9.1:** 38 uses, all ordinary (eyebrow and forearm scars; the corner and Hampson stores; the loading dock, zone and boxes; structural load). None is flagged.
