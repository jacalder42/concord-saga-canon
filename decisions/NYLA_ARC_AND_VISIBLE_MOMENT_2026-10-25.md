# Nyla's arc (B01–B03) and the Epilogue's visible moment: author rulings, 2026-10-25

The source is manuscript `draft-notes/final-2026-10-18/NYLA_CAPTURE_OPTIONS_2026-10-25.md`. The author accepted the recommendation with the changes below.

## 1. Nyla's arc: Option 3, adopted
- **B01:** Nyla is kept safe at the end. "Nothing had come for her from across the water" stays, and on a reread it becomes irony.
- **B02:** Dominion takes Nyla. Lanette leads the recovery. Nyla gets out on the night of the big B02 pulse (E44), so no new catastrophe is needed. P12, the 15 May place, becomes an early-B02 beat: the place lapses and the relief was false.
- **B03:** the second loss of Nyla is what gives Caldas free rein. His lethal intent is aimed at **Baz**, never at Nyla. Nyla stays offstage in B03.
- **Rejected:** a permanent capture or meta conversion. Metas stay rumour until B04, and Lanette's C43 win stands.
- **Superseded:** "Dominion loses Nyla for good" now means lost **at the Square** (B01). The memo lists the other affected rows, with file:line, to update when B02 is drafted.

### The author's amendment: paper is the justification, not the vehicle
The taking may be *justified* by paper: a welfare referral or a placement. On the page, though, it is carried by **people, places and action**, not by forms, filings or document handling. **Less paperwork, not more.** Any documents stay offstage, or are mentioned in passing at most.

## 2. The Epilogue: one visible sense moment, adopted
- **What's seen:** the watchers (Silence and Hope) see the light on the boards under the sleeping Nyla bend like heat-shimmer over a summer road. Her hand taps once or twice. No human sees it.
- **B02:** the Pontalba camera's footage of C42 shows the same bending of light, and Dominion takes that as its grounds. This makes Lucien's cut page tragic.

## 3. Gates: deliberately light (the author's ruling)
The author ruled that the proposed light and rhythm gates were too strong: "they'll just become the next paperwork nightmare." **One rule only:**
- Neither the narration nor a character confirms that the Veil answers rhythm. That is the reserved X1 answer, Mechanica-v4 l.913.

Everything else is free. Rhythm, music, tapping and light may sit near the phenomenon, and the prose need not avoid timing, patterns or co-occurrence. Do not build checklists around this.
