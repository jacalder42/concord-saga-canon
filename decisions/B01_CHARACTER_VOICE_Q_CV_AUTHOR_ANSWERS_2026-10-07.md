## Binding execution constraint

**Do not increase explicitness while increasing differentiation.**
- Caro's material reveals.
- Baz notices.
- Seraphine experiences.
- **None explains their arc.** No polished explanatory line that solves the audit but makes the novel worse.

**No rhythm pass:** an authorial narrator is allowed. The differentiation that matters is rooms, measurement,
attribution, triage and counting.

## Sequence

1. The CV revision (about 25–30 lines, surgical).
2. A changed-zone audit.
3. **A fresh whole-book banter, heat and humor *diagnostic* read** (not a revision pass), with three questions kept
   separate:
   - **Banter:** do these people enjoy, needle, challenge and surprise one another enough, and does the humor arise
     from who they are rather than from inserted jokes?
   - **Heat:** is attraction present in attention, physical reception, avoidance, misreading and choice, not merely
     in flirting or explicit interior declaration?
   - **Humor:** does the book provide enough pressure release, and are different characters funny in different ways?
     This tests the HWFWM / Guy Ritchie aspiration without forcing the manuscript toward quippiness.

   **Its framing:** where is B01 already alive, and where does it temporarily stop being alive?
4. The author's decisions.
5. Only then, targeted banter, heat and humor changes.
