## Answers

- **Q-MS1: approved.** The E24 thread look moves earlier, and *What went to Vienna now was timber* is restored as the
  exit. This corrects a regression from the banter, heat and humor pass; it is not further optimization.
- **Q-MS2: partly approved.**
  - **E27 and E28, approved, cautiously.** One small reminder in each gives the waiting week a clock. The lines must
    arise from something already happening, not from narrator reminders that the plot is waiting. **They do not
    make more things happen; they make *not happening yet* stay active.**
  - **E35, approved.** Lucien receives and carries Seraphine's TD-4462 message and chooses not to answer. It is a
    relationship and continuity omission, so not romantic commentary.
  - **E08 and E16: compression *tests*, not quotas.**
    - Find where each chapter's engine actually starts, and whether the material before it is redundant.
    - If 80 words solve it, cut 80; if everything earns its place, leave it.
    - The reviser recommends KEEP or COMPRESS, with an exact proposed diff. **Nothing is cut automatically.**
  - **The gray cases and the runner, approved, through progression rather than synonyms.** A later appearance changes
    only where the observer's understanding, behavior or stakes have changed. **If nothing has changed, no cosmetic
    variation.**
  - **E19 and E41: declined.** There is no stall, and not every clue needs an acknowledgement.
- **Q-MS3: watch only**, carried forward as B02 and saga craft warnings, not B01 corrections. In particular, *"not
  today"* is a diagnosis: **withholding needs different dramatic shapes**: silence, lying, distraction, partial
  disclosure, competing obligations, mistaken assumptions, interruption and deliberate refusal. Not every one should
  read as the author saying *later*.
- **Q-MS4: the core verdict is approved and frozen.**
  - **Packaging:** the Prologue, the Supplement, the Interlude and the Epilogue attach to neighboring releases.
  - **Not locked; publishing hypotheses only:** launching Prologue–E04 together, and pairing E16 with E17 and E23
    with E24. Cadence, platform, subscriber expectations, free versus read-ahead, and launch volume decide those.

## Frozen findings

1. **Serialization modules.** *B01's existing conventional chapters are validated as the default serialization
   modules. Future serialization planning packages those chapters; it does not subdivide the manuscript unless later
   real-world reader data gives a reason to reconsider.* This closes the long-open serialization question; the older
   1,200–1,800-word daily-episode model is retired for B01.
2. **The waiting week (E27–E30) is a known structural characteristic, not merely a patched problem.**
   - The pattern is *claim → waiting → window*.
   - Waiting can generate great tension. The danger is not that the characters wait, but that **the reader stops
     waiting**, because the anticipated event has faded from working memory.
   - The principle is carried into later books.
