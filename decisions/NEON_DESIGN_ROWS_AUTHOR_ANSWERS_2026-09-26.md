## 1. The delegated timing for M57 (a PROPOSAL, adopted as the working assumption)

**B05 Act III**, where the approved Book 5 E12 has it (`NB` 103710: *"After being emotionally distant
for most of Book 4 and early Book 5, they admit:"*).

**Why it fits the saga:**

- **It follows M05 in order.** Lucien withdraws in B04, grieving, and has to be back in New Orleans
  before they reconnect. Act III leaves B05's first two acts for that return.
- **It separates two relationship turns.** M56 (Caro and Elisabet) sits in B05 Act II. Putting both
  recommitments in the same act would repeat a beat.
- **It restores the bond before B06.** The pair go into Santa Fe and Tahl's death together.
- **It keeps approved material** rather than moving it.

**The author can override this.** Nothing else depends on it yet.
