   - **Preserve:** negative evidence, consequential refusals, withheld actions, meaningful silence, investigation logic,
     uncertainty about the strange, and character-specific avoidance.
   - **Challenge:** redundant explanation, stacked non-actions performing one response, doubled closing sentences and
     waiting choreography.

8. **After Pass 3, search-term-driven prose passes stop.** Work returns to blind reading, voice, heat and humor,
