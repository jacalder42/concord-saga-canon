## 4. How the Lucien-witness logic is met

**E07.** When Seraphine decides she needs other witnesses, she thinks of the engineer who went down on one knee in that
front room. **She decides against him first, for reasons of her own:**
- he told her *low blood pressure* and held that door shut;
- she has no way to reach him;
- a man who writes things on legal forms is the last person she can ask whether a room stopped.

**E09.** Her step toward him is the request to the coroner's investigator for a copy of *both* statements. What he wrote
will tell her whether he told the file what happened.

**E10.** The investigator refuses her his statement. She may have her own, and his is the family's to request. Resistance
is met, and E09's guard (she does not learn what he wrote) holds.

**The guard's reading, applied and flagged for confirmation.** E01's brief says *"the camp is linked to nothing later
and nobody calls it special."* It is read here as: **the page never makes the camp a cause, a source or a special place.
Seraphine may privately count the man who fell there as a possible witness.** The author's instruction requires that
much, and it goes no further.
