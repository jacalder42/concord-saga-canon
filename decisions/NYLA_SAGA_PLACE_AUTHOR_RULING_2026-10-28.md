## 1. Ruled
1. **Nyla is the source of the Warehouse Rupture (M08, B03).**
   - Dominion misreads a converter as an emitter (*Emitter (prov.)*, B01) and tries to manufacture one.
   - A manufactured meta holds what it cannot spend (Mechanica §48), and the forced conversion fails by leakage. The first Rupture is that failure.
   - Baz dies pulling her free (M10: "the child" is Nyla).
   - Tahl's coordinates draw the crowd that amplifies the leak (§22; M11).
   - Caldas's revenge (§624 §4) runs through this operation.
2. **She survives, converted** (path 3). Under §48 she remains a person: personhood is never in question, she cannot ascend, and she is unstable and prone to leakage. Conversion erases names on paper.
3. **Her arc ends in Neon, as part of the fall of Dominion** (path 5).
   - Once resonance is public, a child converted and erased by a Dominion-adjacent body is the scandal that cannot be contained.
   - It **causes** the Neon wane that canon already calls for (`canon/factions/Dominions.md:108–111`, §365).
   - Her and Lanette's choice to go public is theirs; MT is the natural channel.
   - **Loom carries no Nyla load.**
4. **§615 is amended, not replaced.** The B02 capture, the Lanette-led recovery and the escape on the E44 pulse **stand**. In **B03 she is taken again, by coercion** (author: "perhaps"): someone close is leveraged, or a threat is made. The mechanism is open.
5. **The procedure stays vague or off the page.** It is felt through Lanette, Seraphine and Baz, never shown as procedure on a child.
6. **The page keeps the reserves.** The Warehouse reads as a store of pressure breaking out, which pays off at Veil level how the cap fails, without naming the cap, Mira or the cycle.

## 2. Leaning (not yet ruled): the funder runs the conversion
- If the Houston funder faction runs it, **the B03 funder reveal (§624 §3) and Nyla's conversion become one revelation.** The reader learns both in B03, before Baz dies; the cast learns in B04.
- **A tension to settle when this is ruled.** The writer thread's path-5 sketch had the scandal break *Virelli's* secrecy and the funder faction step into the vacuum as "Concord". If the faction itself ran the program, there are two readings:
  - **(a)** The public sees only "Dominion". Virelli's leadership falls for the faction's crime, and the faction rebrands as "Concord" and escapes the blame. **The Loom collapse of the co-opter** (BC9, unplaced) **becomes the day the faction's authorship of the Nyla program comes out.** This places that collapse and keeps Neon's payoff, Dominion's fall, inside Neon.
  - **(b)** The faction is exposed in Neon, and Virelli's leadership survives longer. That inverts the canon wane.
  - **Writer-thread recommendation: (a).** Under the trilogy contract, Neon's partial payoff (the fall) lands in Neon, and the faction's culpability is a fair Loom thread rather than a dependency.
- The faction's canon profile ("funds care, consent forms and goodwill… seeks firsts… the help is real; the motive is self-interest", Dominions.md:251) fits a program that seeks the ultimate "first". The conversion is where the self-interest stops being benign.
