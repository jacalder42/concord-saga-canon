## 2. Answers

**(a), (b), (d) and (e) are approved as recommended:**

- **(a)** Fix the five continuity and consequence items. In particular:
  - Lucien's realisation at E48 rests only on routes the book has already shown;
  - the phone pays off: the E48 asker knows about his page and Seraphine's log because of his phone;
  - Renée means no *final* certificate;
  - E20 shows what the researchers asked for, not "only times".
- **(b)** Give Baz's E26 rationalisation and recognition real weight. Add a failed attempt to tell Lucien soon after E43,
  for a concrete reason, so that his silence until E48 is a choice with a cost.
- **(d)** Trim repeated processing in Act II. The dryer, the waltz, Renée's scenes, the roll call, the silent gate and
  Jody's sequence are protected.
- **(e)** Make a separate Sudowrite import copy well under 120k (about 116–117k), so that the cap never dictates a
  scene's room.

**(c), Claude's "records collected by two routes" ending, is declined.** In its place:

> *"Option B, Seraphine forces it. Dominion doesn't have to be revealed, but what they are seeking, the Veil, is. That's
> Baz's bombshell suspicion at the very end, which we see Silence and Hope reacting to in the Epilogue."*

- **E49:**
  - **Seraphine forces the telling.** She confronts Lucien and Baz from what she has seen herself (Lucien's changed face,
    Caldas, the file sent upstairs).
  - **They tell her who these people are, without naming them:** Lucien works for them through the Institute, Baz used
    to, and Caldas is theirs.
  - **They tell her what these people want:** the record of where and when it happens, the people who can feel it, and
    Baz.
  - **The researchers stay unnamed.** Baz may say that the people with the cases are not the same people.
- **The bombshell, at the very end of E49:** Baz's *suspicion* that what they are seeking is **the Veil**.
  - It is a suspicion, not certified by the narrator.
  - It is not explained: nothing says what the Veil is or how it works.
- **E50:** Silence and Hope react to the word spoken below. *Veil* therefore appears at E49 (Baz) and in E50. This
  supersedes §385's *once, in E50*.
- **The Dominion is not named in B01.** B02 E08 keeps the first naming.
