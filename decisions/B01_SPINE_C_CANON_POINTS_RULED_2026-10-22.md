## Rulings
1. **Lucien learns about the form in C26, not at the Mass.** Seraphine asks "Who's Doucet?" at her car outside Lacoste Glass (Wed 15 April), after fitting the Mass card together in C25. He knows the name from the household boxes on his own form, and he lies: "I do not know." A short paragraph in his point of view, right after the lie, shows the cost. **Amends** §601's "Lucien realizes at the Mass" and the C40 document-match timing.
2. **Nyla's first sighting is in Act I, at the C10 Square fall, and it is partial.** Tamika says "That little girl didn't fall." Seraphine can't be sure what she saw. **Held back for C42:** the mother down on the stone, holding the girl's arm pulled out straight.
3. **C44: Lucien gives Vienna his notebook without Nyla's page.** The reader sees him take it out. The B02 packets that assume Vienna holds her name need a note.
4. **Baz tells Seraphine about the form in C37,** at the Vein, with Lucien absent ("How long?" / "Since the Kia."). He has known since C30, and he holds it back at C34 ("That one is not mine to tell"). Seraphine's proof moves to C38§1, and the C38 Causeway becomes Lucien's Confession about the boy.
5. **What counts as a Confession.** A capital-C Confession is the full account, given at a chosen cost. Three earlier scenes are admissions, not Confessions: C16 (the river steps, "I sent your name"), C28§4 (the Kerlerec report) and C30§4 (Baz in the Kia). The count stays at three: 34.3, 38 and 40.
