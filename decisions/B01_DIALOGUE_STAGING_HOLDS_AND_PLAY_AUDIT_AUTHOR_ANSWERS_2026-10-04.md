## 2. Sequence

The forwarded prompt's order is adopted:

1. the dialogue and PCM revision;
2. the blind dialogue read;
3. the continuity check;
4. rebuilt reading and import copies;
5. **then a separate, read-only audit of HEAT, FLIRTING, INNUENDO, HUMOR and BANTER**, with the revised manuscript as its
   baseline. The completed dialogue/PCM audit is not reopened.

The audit's full instruction is kept privately in the manuscript repository
(`draft-notes/draft3/PLAY_AUDIT_BRIEF.md`). It must deliver:

- an overall diagnosis;
- a scene table with classes, value and invasiveness ratings;
- the relational play matrix;
- the strongest existing examples;
- the best places for banter, for heat/flirting/innuendo and for humor under pressure;
- places where an attempt should fail, and places where the absence of former play should signal damage;
- a short list of banter set pieces;
- the items that need author approval.

**There are no quotas. Prose is not revised until the author approves the interventions.** The author edits anything
that goes too far.

## 3. What it does not change

No ruling, card or packet. The standing B01 guards hold: nothing romantic is declared aloud, the E39 kiss is called
nothing, consent is ruled, and nothing strange creates desire.
