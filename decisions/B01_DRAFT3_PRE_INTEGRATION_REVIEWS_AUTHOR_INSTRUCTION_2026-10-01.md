> *"I would add having a reader assigned to each major character, where they focus solely on the characters words,

## How it is run

- **One reader per character.** Each reads only that character's dialogue, thought and action across the book and judges
  them against the character's sources:
  - the Tier-1 cards in `canon/characters/` and `canon/pov/`;
  - the registry;
  - the pressure cards, wants lines and relationship register;
  - the language notes in `ebci/prose/README.md`;
  - the dated rulings that bear on the character.

  Where a source is silent or conflicts with the page, the reader records it. **It does not decide.**
- **Narration readers.** The narrated portions are judged against `ebci/prose/WRITER_PROFILE_JA_CALDER.md` (including
  §3A and §12A) and its recovered sources.
- **The five suggested reviews** run alongside.
- **All of it is read-only** on the current draft 3 (manuscript commit `0031847`, 135,115 words).
- **Outputs are private** (the manuscript's `draft-notes/draft3/review/`). This repository gets one synthesis report
  that quotes no manuscript text, and the integration list.
