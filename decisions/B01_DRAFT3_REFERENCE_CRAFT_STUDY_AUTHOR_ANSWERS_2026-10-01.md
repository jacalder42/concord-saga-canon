## 2. The writer profile: no always, no never

**Instruction (author, 2026-10-01).** The Calder profile carries no *always* or *never* craft rules. Anything can be
done if its cost can be surmounted.

**Applied to `ebci/prose/WRITER_PROFILE_JA_CALDER.md`:**
- **A governing paragraph** follows the opening: every line is a default with a price, and a broken default must pay its
  way on the page.
- **Every craft absolute is restated as a costed default.** For example:
  - *never sermonise* becomes *a sermon costs the reader*;
  - *no head hopping* becomes *hopping heads mid-scene costs the reader's footing*;
  - *never call it beautiful* becomes *calling it beautiful spends it*;
  - *no intimacy during active danger* becomes *it costs the danger or the intimacy, and usually belongs to the
    aftermath*;
  - the line-level tells become tells that cost more than they carry.
- **The catastrophe rule.** The flattening is confirmed:
  - The source is `rules/Emotion/HUMOR_AND_RELIEF_SYSTEM.md`.
  - Its own §4.3 permits **gallows humour under extreme pressure: brief and sharp, followed by silence or cost**, and its
    §6 calls good humour under pressure *rare and costly*.
  - The author's 2025-11-17 Calder humour modes list *quips during stress* as tension release.
  - The profile had taken only the system's §5 forbidden list.
  - §6 now reads: humour inside an active catastrophe is gallows humour (rare, brief, sharp, followed by silence or
    cost), and badly timed it damages trust.
  - The humour system gets an additive dated note. Its own text is unchanged.
- **Ruled limits keep their force, marked *(ruled)*.** They are author rulings or canon rules, not craft defaults:
  - third person past (Veil);
  - not the mechanism;
  - nobody reads minds;
  - Veil's ordinary vocabulary and no numbers as imagery;
  - no powers or impossible geometry, and nobody breaks physics (the world);
  - consent, and nothing strange creates desire;
  - the R ceiling and the explicit level;
  - US spelling;
  - no quotas.

  This instruction does not reword them. **If the author wants any of these treated as costed too, that needs its own
  ruling.**
- **§8's consent line becomes a definition, not a ban:** a moment that breaks a person is harm, and the book treats it
  as harm.
