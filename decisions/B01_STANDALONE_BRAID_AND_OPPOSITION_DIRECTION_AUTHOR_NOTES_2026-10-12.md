# B01 standalone review: the braid, Ren's violence, the cream-shoes woman, length (author direction, 2026-10-12)

Status: CURRENT AUTHOR DIRECTION (design direction for the B01 standalone redesign). It answers no Q-SN question
directly and rules no canon; it reshapes the proposal the questions come from. Ledger §521.

Given after reading `proposals/B01_STANDALONE_REVIEW_AND_REDESIGN_PROPOSAL_2026-10-12.md` (ledger §520), *"a few thoughts
to consider before proceeding"*:

1. **The split novel is a braid, not a defect.** *"I find the split novel comment intriguing. We have danced around this
   notion for a long time. The Jazz Framework and braiding principles in particular set this up."*
   - **Seraphine** is the consistent thread that weaves the social and literary functions through the saga, which fits
     her ultimate fate.
   - **The trilogy leads (Baz, Tahl, Kade)** drive the investigation arc of the saga: suspense, mystery, action.
   - **Lucien is the link** that connects the two melodies in Veil: *"he would be the only reason Seraphine and Baz ever
     interact."*
   - The mechanism helps the POV and plot baton passes, and sets up others to carry the link in Neon and Loom (*"Rex,
     Elisabet, Lacuna, etc."*).
2. **Ren's lack of violence was the assistant's, not the author's.** *"I am open to more open conflict when it is
   productive to drive pace, reader payoffs, etc."*
3. **The woman in cream shoes** *"was interesting"*, and the author still likes burying hints of a future Technarc
   presence. *"Perhaps she is still seeded as an unexplained observer."*
4. **Length.** 120k is a soft cap; the hard cap is about 135–140k, *"similar to our other rules. Nothing is impossible, it
   has to be able to pay an escalating cost. If 150k makes it the best book ever written, that's fine."*

5. **Romance (follow-up, same day).** *"The romantic holds and limitations have also been tool proposed, they are free
   to change as best serves the saga and reader payoff."* This opens the no-second-kiss and no-declaration holds, the
   cover kiss that "didn't count", B03 E21 as *the first kiss that counts*, the heat limits and the chosen-distance
   ending, each costed like everything else.

6. **Clarification of item 1 (same day).** *"You took my intent with 'The only reason Seraphine and Baz interact' too
   far. My intention is Lucien is the only reason they meet, otherwise their paths would not have crossed. (ie Baz would
   not come to New Orleans) They obviously can interact once meeting."* The link is causal, not a routing rule.

## What this changes in the proposal

- The §2–§4 recommendation fused the two novels (the opposition pressing on the Batiste file). Under this direction the
  two melodies stay separate strands with their own spines, intersecting at chosen points, chiefly through Lucien.
- *"Never violent"* and *"carries nothing"* for Caldas are no longer assumed guards. Violence is available where it
  pays; the prop rule is a separate, earlier approved item and is not reopened by this note.
- The cream-shoes woman is not merged into Caldas. She stays, unexplained, as a seed.
- Length: soft cap 120k, hard cap about 135–140k, either exceedable at an escalating, stated cost.

## What it does not change

The set items (§519): Baz, Tahl and Kade's arcs, and the B09 direction. No ruling, card or manuscript file. The saga-level
consequences (a "link" role in the POV baton pass for Neon and Loom) are recorded here as direction only.
