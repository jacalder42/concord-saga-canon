## 3. The identity failure: missing, not ignored

The E00–E04 drafting log records the minimum identity context actually given for Seraphine: *care coordinator at the
Tremé center*. Her family history (`canon/characters/SeraphineIdentity.md` §III: parents and brother killed in a car
wreck; raised in Tremé by Tante Colette Vael) was **not in it.**

The rule it followed (`ebci/prose/README.md`, Q-CAL1) gives only:
- name and pronouns;
- a scene-relevant role;
- at most one physical or presence fact.

That leaves **no slot for foundational biography** that ordinary prose touches without warning: a pharmacist's small
talk, a holiday, a phone call.

**The fix** (applied to the README): **for a point-of-view character, the minimum identity context also carries the
foundational family facts** (who is living, who is dead, and who raised them) **and the home they live in.** These are
facts, not history or wound. **For everyone else the rule is unchanged.**

*"There is enough structure now. The next useful evidence is the redraft itself"* (§293) still stands.
