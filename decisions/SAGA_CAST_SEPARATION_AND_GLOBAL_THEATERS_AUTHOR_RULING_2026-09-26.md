# Saga Cast Separation and Global Theaters — Author Ruling — 2026-09-26

Status: CURRENT AUTHOR RULING
Scope: saga-wide cast geography and scene-planning assumptions. This ruling changes no book, act, episode, milestone row, travel itinerary, POV assignment, relationship outcome, or EBCI status.

## Author words

> "The primary cast is not intended to be around each other for the full saga etc"

This followed the author's agreement that the recent structural reconciliation had compressed several global locations and that the next pass should recover narrative geography before pressure recalibration.

## Accepted

1. **Primary-cast separation is intentional saga architecture.** No planning layer may assume that Seraphine, Lucien, Caro, Elisabet, Rex, Tahl, Kade, Lacuna or other principal characters should remain co-located merely because they share a book or saga-level event.

2. **The main-cast itinerary is not the world map.** A city or regional theater may carry meaningful narrative work through:
   - a principal character physically present there;
   - a recurring local/place-anchor character;
   - factional or institutional action;
   - independent local consequences;
   - ordinary communications, records, MT, reports, visitors or later carry-forward.

3. **Absence is meaningful.** Future architecture should track who is *not* present at an event/site when that separation affects knowledge, relationships, logistics, responsibility or reunion.

4. **Global theaters should remain distinct.** Recovering Vienna, Singapore, Reykjavík, Marrakesh and other established geographies does not require sending the full ensemble to each place.

5. **Reunion and convergence must be earned.** When separated characters meet again, the architecture should preserve the asymmetric knowledge, changed relationships, costs and consequences accumulated while apart.

## Not accepted / still open

This ruling does **not** decide:
- which books or acts any character spends in Vienna, Singapore, Reykjavík, Marrakesh, Bristol, coastal Japan, Santa Fe, Mound City, Serpent Mound, Honey Island or any other site;
- which recovered historical travel beats survive;
- whether a city receives a principal POV, local POV, supplement, remote report or no on-page scene;
- whether any specific historical city-bible event is current canon;
- exact travel methods, dates, routes or team rosters;
- the number of global locations required per book;
- any change to the live 53-row milestone grid;
- thread-pressure recalibration;
- B01 EBCI release.

## Immediate consequence

Before saga pressure/rhythm recalibration, perform a geography/cast reconciliation that distinguishes:
- primary lived setting;
- secondary lived theater;
- character-origin/home;
- institutional theater;
- remote world receipt;
- travel/transition;
- wound/Mending geography.

For each retained theater, track physical presence, local recurring cast, remote observers, information exit, carry-forward and explicit absences.

The resulting matrix is a proposal/reconciliation artifact, not canon.
