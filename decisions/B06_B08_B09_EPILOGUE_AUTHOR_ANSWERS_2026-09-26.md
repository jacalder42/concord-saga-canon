### 1.1 Warnings from VT

The author-pasted master says *"Filament warnings go unheeded"* (B6 A1) and *"Filaments warn
Santa Fe is reaching fracture threshold"* (B5 A3). The author's intent is that **the warnings come
from VT**. The two fit if the warning **originates** in VT and **reaches** people through mortal
hands. The card does not choose among these candidates:

- **Tahl**, whose VT access is *"partial, accidental"*, relays what he perceives. B5 A3's
  *"hardest MT truth message yet"* is then the warning.
- **Filaments** relay what Tahl or others report.

The constraint is Mechanica §38: VT *"never broadcasts; never travels digitally."* A VT warning
reaches the public **only once a person has turned it into mortal information.** That is a
consequence of the rule and the answer, not an addition.

### 1.2 One identifiable Echo appearance

This answers the B6 card's finding D. It reads closest to that finding's reading 1: TahlEBCI's
restriction governs **identifiable** appearances, and the grid's other Echo moments (M22 B6,
M27 B7) become echoes that **may or may not be Tahl's** to the cast. Two items remain open:

- **Which appearance is the one?** `TahlEBCI.md` and the author-pasted Loom master both put an
  **Echo flare in B09's penultimate chapter**, stopping Kade's collapse. That is the natural
  candidate.
- **The epilogue triangle.** In the author's locked epilogue, the LT request shows **Tahl's
  triangle**, which Kade recognises. If the flare is the one identifiable appearance, the
  triangle needs to count as something else, such as a conduit signature (answer 2 below) rather
  than an appearance. **M36** (*"Tahl's Echo dissolves"*, B9 EP, with a quoted line) would be a
  second identifiable appearance. **Flagged for the author; not resolved.**

M27's *"an agent again"* is now doubly held. The cast cannot confirm it is Tahl, and
`TahlEBCI.md` says the Echo *"has no agency, only guidance."*

### 1.4 What the name answer settles

### 2.1 Elias claims it

This fits `EliasID.md`: Elias *"possesses no metaphysical access, authority, or sensitivity of any
kind"* and is dangerous through *"people, timing, and narrative pressure."* A claim is his
instrument. It gives POOL-018 (*"Kade's credibility appropriated"*) a concrete incident, and gives
B9's *"Kade reaches Brightbreak zenith"* a cause, as the card proposed.

**The author leaves open whether Kade actually caused it.** If he did, the card's guardrails
still apply: the packet's *"no emotional alignment key granting free passage"*, and KadeID's
*"power is human-scale"* and *"does not ascend."* A Kade-caused opening would need a mechanism
that respects both. That is flagged, not ruled out.

### 2.2 "Act III … the very end of Act II" — which book?

The answer names acts but not a book. Two readings. **This document does not choose:**

1. **Book 9.** Mending-site material moves to B9 Act III, where the Mending is, and the opening
   comes at the very end of **B9 Act II**. B8 would then not end on the opening. This is close to
   the B8 card's reading 3 ("move the opening"). It removes the B8/B9 overlap the card found, but
   B8 needs a new end image.
2. **Book 8.** Mending-site material stays in B8 Act III, and the opening moves earlier, to the end
   of **B8 Act II**, so B8 Act III takes place at the site. This does not match the author-pasted
   B8 (*"ACT II CLOSE — swamp confirmed as only viable Mending site"*), and it pushes B8 further
   into B9's ground.

Reading 1 fits the answer's wording better (the Mending site belongs with the Mending) and fits
the author's direction that Loom is thin and needs rebuilding. **It is put to the author, with
reading 1 marked as the better textual fit.** The 09-26 lean toward an **actual** opening
(decision 4) is unaffected: that lean was about the opening's nature, not its book.

### 3.1 The `LT_RULES` §6 amendment

**Applied in this commit.** `rules/Channels/LT_RULES_POST_MENDING.md` §6 (*"LT never interacts
directly with MT"*) keeps its general rule and gains one named exception. **The B09 epilogue
handshake reaches Kade's device as a message request, and Tahl's echo enables it.** The
exception is the echo's doing. It does not mean LT generally rides MT hardware. The rest of the
file is unchanged: LT still does not coerce, override agency or act as a guide.

**Read with, not amended:**

- **Mechanica §37** (*"MT cannot … access VT or LT"*) is about MT, the channel, reaching into LT.
  The handshake runs the other way, from LT through Tahl's echo.
- **`TahlEBCI.md`** already says *"Echo may interface lightly post-death only (handled
  elsewhere)"* under LT. This ruling is the "elsewhere."
- **`LT_RULES` §2** (*"never presents as a voice, command, or instruction"*): a message request
  that shows a symbol and a blank prompt is none of those. The author's locked design is
  consistent with §2 as written.

**"More tangible than VT was"** is recorded as the author's intent for LT, via Tahl. It sits
against `LT_RULES`' "soft, prismatic, felt as clarity" language, and against §4 (*"LT cannot
transmit information"*). **Not reconciled here**: it bears on the held Post-Mending era file and
LT `res_states` (CLAUDE.md §4.1).

### 3.2 Kade's role, and the name

This answers the milestone ruling's addendum: *"a new role … for Kade"*. **Kade reintegrates the
rebellion's people**, working alongside Elisabet and Rex, who handle society more broadly. It
also confirms the 2025-11-15 "Accord" idea as the author's.

**An observation on the name, not a ruling:** *Concord* is already used in-world for the
protagonists' group (`LacunaID.md`: *"stabilizing force for Concord"*) and is the saga's own
title. Naming what grows out of the reconstruction *Concord* would make the saga's name the
thing its survivors build. *Accord* avoids the collision. **The author's choice.**
