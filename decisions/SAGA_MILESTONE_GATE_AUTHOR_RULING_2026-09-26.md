## What each ruling does and does not settle

### 1 — Tahl's naming

The live question was *"the B3 epilogue, or earlier in Book 3?"* (saga pass 1 §4). **Answered.**

**This gives one of the "four locks living only in proposals" (ledger §26.6) an author
source.** The handoff document's unquoted *"may not even be named before the B03 epilogue"*
now has the author's own words behind it: **B3 epilogue.** The other half of that lock,
*"not a primary character in B01–B03,"* is still a paraphrase.

### 2 — Tahl's exposure

**What is ruled is narrow.** Tahl **posted coordinates**, and they were the coordinates of **the
event Baz was investigating**. That fits the protected ruling that Tahl *"accidentally exposes
the incident that Baz gets killed at"*. It also matches option A in the decision packet: an
accurate report disclosing a site, which changes how people respond. **It rules out option B:
Tahl's post does not produce the Rupture itself.**

**The causal chain carries the author's "perhaps."** More people arriving and a slowed
evacuation are **not ruled**. They are the leading hypothesis for the Warehouse causal card to
test.

**Nothing here says Tahl knew Baz was there,** or that Baz and Tahl worked together. The
existing ruling that *"Baz does not work directly with Tahl"* stands.

### 3 and 4 — leans

**Recorded so the direction is not lost, and so nobody promotes them.** A lean tells later
work what to look for first. It does not approve a scene, a mechanism or a placement.

- **Lean 3** keeps the Riot, the first public meta and the Colorstorm *as events* in the search.
  **It does not revive "first global Colorstorm" or "first public meta" as wording.** Those
  claims still need the evidence the review asked for.
- **Lean 4** favours an **objective** opening over a discovered path. The decision packet
  requires a minimum scene card for any literal opening: site, medium, initial state, timing,
  change, actor contribution, footprint, cost, traces, and how pursuers misread it. That card
  is still owed. **The chamber reveal is kept, and no pre-Mending formal Echo Node** is
  implied.

### 5 — LT

**This answer differs from both options the packet offered, and it matches Mechanica.**

`rules/Mechanica-v4.md` §39: *"LT exists only post-Mending … Is perceivable only by
ascendants … Does not override human agency … Cannot be used coercively. Civilians perceive
only calm or clarity."* Seraphine is one of the trio who ascend (`CLAUDE.md` §4.1). **An
ascended Seraphine reaching out to survivors fits the Mechanica definition. A renamed mortal
public channel does not.**

**So M35's wording, "MT is renamed LT; Kade's stewardship completes," conflicts with this
ruling on both counts.** LT is not MT renamed, and LT is not Kade's. M35's own note, *"A rename,
not a channel conversion,"* cites Notion B9 E20 and the Post-Mending bible 7.3. That older
source is now **superseded** on LT's identity.

**Explicitly not ruled:**

- **What survivors perceive.** Mechanica says *"only calm or clarity"*, not a readable message.
  Whether the scene keeps that constraint or tests it is open.
- **What becomes of MT, and of Kade's stewardship,** after the Mending. MT does not *become* LT
  under this ruling. Whether MT continues, ends or changes is a separate question.
- **The Book 9 epilogue timeskip** (ledger §27.3).
- **The post-Mending `res_states` list** (ledger §18). The ruling bears on it but does not
  settle it.
