## 2. The derivation rule (new)

**Prose packets derive from the Narrative Brief, not the Control Layer.**

- **Writer Options are excluded by default.** They enter only by explicit, episode-specific promotion.
  E38's *"child's hand, notebook, light at the dock door"* are exactly the images prose should
  discover. The notebook could become a sentimental death prop simply because a packet suggested it.
- **Never passed:**
  - ECID and U/W/FX codes;
  - breadcrumb and milestone ids;
  - causal-card terminology;
  - tracking;
  - provenance;
  - future-book pointers;
  - validator language;
  - editorial explanations.
- **Shapes may differ.** A packet omits fields that do not help its scene: *"If the production system
  insists that E31 and E33 contain the same fields, we've rebuilt the problem."*

## 3. The pilot

**Two prose packets, B01 E31 and E33. No canonical prose yet.** The test has changed from EBCI's. It is
now: *"does the prose-production packet give a novelist enough without smuggling the planning system
back in?"*

**E31 is extremely light:**
- Seraphine's POV;
- friends go to the Velvet Vein because they want to;
- no research;
- the night succeeds;
- someone, provisionally Lucien, opens up;
- Lacuna is an unnoticed musician with zero portent;
- no clue or wonder obligation.

**E33 carries only the page-safe causal and epistemic limits:**
- Baz's POV;
- the predicted window, and the observable phenomenon;
- Seraphine's overreach;
- Lucien's failed reading, **visible only through what Baz can observe**;
- Baz records the contradiction and does not explain it;
- observation stays separate from interpretation.

**The five questions:**
1. Can a competent novelist begin without the repo?
2. Is everything protected whose violation would damage canon?
3. Are the dialogue, imagery, blocking, humour and emotional discovery still unwritten?
4. Is anything present because the database knows it rather than because the novelist needs it?
   Remove it.
5. Does the packet make the novelist want to write the scene?

**Stop after the two packets for review.**
