**What this settles:**

- **The circuit is the old way's work.** Stabilising the wounds, with Honey Island last, is the
  **hard-cap plan** that Silence and Hope are guiding. Honey Island is where the cap would be
  reinstated, and where Seraphine instead begins the breathing veil. This fits the author's 11-13
  engine: Silence and Hope coach *"the old way, a hard cap"*, and *"at the final moment, Seraphine
  truely understands the answer."*
- **Seraphine's doubt runs the length of the circuit.** *("It grows wound by wound" was an inference with no source, flagged in the [hard-cap recovery](../recovery/HARD_CAP_BREATHING_VEIL_SOURCE_RECOVERY_2026-09-26.md) §3. The sources tie her doubt to intuition and to being "drawn into becoming the Loom", not to the node visits. **Ruled later the same day: her doubt grows from becoming the Loom** ([ruling](LOOM_CHAIN_KNOWLEDGE_AND_MENDING_COST_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-26.md)).)* The **answer**
  stays reserved for the end; the **potential** begins earlier. The B08 card's F1 ("the old way
  fails") becomes one stage of that growing doubt.
- **Mechanica fit:** stabilisation is §35 (*"Empathy, Intent clarity, Structural support,
  Grounding rituals"*). It does not conflict with §34's *"permanent scar."* A stabilised scar is
  still a scar. **D5 still holds the physics.**
