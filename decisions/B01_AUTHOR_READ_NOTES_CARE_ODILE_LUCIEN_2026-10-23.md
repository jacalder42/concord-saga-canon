4. **Lucien reads houses against their own history, not against "true".**
   - Old New Orleans stock always leans: alluvial soils, decades of settling. Settlement has a consistent story, in the doors, the cracks and the floors.
   - **The anomaly is what doesn't fit that story:** the wrong direction (the bob draws against the house's settled fall), the wrong speed (a post he checks daily changed overnight), or the wrong kind (water shivering, which no settling causes).
   - Lucien trusts things to be *honest*, to lean the way they were pushed. Sal: "Nothing in this city is plumb but the drunks."
   - **Chapters touched:** C04 and C09, plus a light pass on "plumb"/"true" lines elsewhere.
