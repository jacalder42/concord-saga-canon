# Trip's gears and her POV rule — author ruling

Status: CURRENT AUTHOR RULING. Approved by the author on 2026-11-17, as the six rulings in his proposal *"Trip's gears, for
the writer thread (on DP5.1)"* (Claude Docs). Held unrecorded at his instruction until 2026-11-19, when he said **"Adopt
and record"** (recording ledger §678; the trial they govern, DP5.2, is gated at §677 and adopted at §678).

**What this does not change:** Trip's leadership ceiling, her influence through conversation and presence rather than
instruction, the other leads' arcs and romances, who knows what when (apart from Baz, by the page, §4), the Book One
bans, or any B02/B03 card. The innuendo retired on 2026-10-05 (Q-VI7) stays retired.

## 1. The six rulings, as approved
1. **TripID IX, the POV rule.** Trip may name a cause about the person in front of her, from inside one of her gears.
   She never explains the plot or how the world works, never leads the cast, and never addresses the reader.
2. **TripID I, the hard rule.** "Truth" narrows to "never claims the final truth about events". Leadership and narrative
   control stay as they are.
3. **"Dry and kind" (Q-VI8) becomes:** "dry at the door, warm at the bar, playful on the floor, and kind throughout."
4. **The tease gear:** banter and nicknames, never innuendo, never romantic.
5. **The October 5 voice pass** gets a dated note: the POV rule was loosened at the author's word on 2026-11-17.
6. **Unchanged:** she cannot resolve plot conflicts or replace the leads' emotional work.

## 2. The author's reasons, in his words
- *"I have some concerns that the Trip in the manuscript does not have the gravity originally intended. She become dry,
  when a more effective comparison is that she's more like a female Calder."*
- *"Trip is intended to be like Calder in that she has gears: hostess, bartender, performer, etc."*
- On loosening "may name tension but never diagnose cause": *"Yes loosen it."*

## 3. The gears (approved design, not ruled)
Host, bartender, performer, tease, gossip keeper, bouncer and auntie. What carries over from Calder is the movement
(initiate, sustain, accelerate, redirect, brake, let it ride) and the habit of meeting each person from the angle that
will reach them, never anything biographical. The gears are design for drafting and review; rulings 1–4 are the canon
limits they work inside.
