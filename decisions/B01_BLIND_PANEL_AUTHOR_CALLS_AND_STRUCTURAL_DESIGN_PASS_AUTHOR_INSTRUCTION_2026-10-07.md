## The author's reading

The manuscript is viable. The remaining problem is **structural sameness, not prose artificiality**. B01 has enough
events but sometimes converts them into consequences too slowly. Its pattern is *experience → rest → record →
withhold → experience*. The strong chapters run *event → choice → cost/change → new question*.

**The new momentum criterion, for every chapter:** *What is different at the end because this chapter happened?* A
rest chapter passes if a relationship changed, someone decided something, a secret moved, someone incurred a cost, a
theory died, or a new obligation appeared.
