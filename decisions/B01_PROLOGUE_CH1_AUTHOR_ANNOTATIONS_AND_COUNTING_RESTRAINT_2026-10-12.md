# B01: the author's Prologue and Chapter 1 annotations; counting restraint

Status: CURRENT AUTHOR DIRECTION, 2026-10-12. **Approved design** for B01's prose. It changes no ruled canon, card or
grid row.

**The source.** The author made 58 markups in the annotated reading copy `B01_ENG_ANNOTATED_READING_e01.html` (the
Prologue and early Chapter 1, made on the 10-09 engine draft), discussed them with ChatGPT, and gave these answers.

## Decisions

1. **The annotations are applied** to the current draft (`B01/br-2026-10-09/` E00, E01), mapped from the engine draft.
   Two were skipped because their passages had already been cut (the handbook clause; *"called me first"*).
2. **Counting restraint (book-wide).** Counting and timekeeping must earn their presence through character, causality
   or a specific payoff. Measurement as a theme gives them no automatic protection.
3. **The watchers:** *"He kept the record. She kept the stories."*
4. **The screen door:** *"It was still holding, but the wire beneath it was broken."* *Mended* is kept: the door is
   mended with tape, though the wire cannot be fixed.
5. **Gail's sidebar warning is cut.** It was not credible in an emergency. Its information moves to her radio handoff,
   overheard in E01, so E25 and the E44 confession (*"Gail said he was probably—"*) still pay.
6. **Odile's car line is cut** from the emergency scene.
7. **Renée's hand anomaly is dropped.** Her hands now tremble, and her shock is shown in what she does.
8. **The kitchen light replaces the iron cord** as Seraphine's leaving ritual.

## What this does not change

No other chapter, and no ruling. The annotation data is kept in the private manuscript at
`draft-notes/author-annotations-2026-10-12/`.
