### What the Tahl rulings settle and leave open

- **The crew has a ruled source for the wound pattern:** Tahl's posthumous message. The earlier
  chats may give more (search running). The message goes to **"the group"**. That is consistent with
  the site secret if it is private rather than public (READING).
- **It is not an Echo appearance.** A message composed before death and delivered after is mortal
  communication. `TahlEBCI.md`'s *"Cannot return post-death except as echo"* and the single
  identifiable Echo (the B9 flare) are untouched. **Its delivery must be mortal** (MT, a device, a
  scheduled or found message), not VT.
- **Open:**
  - how it is delivered (scheduled, found in his archive, sent by someone; POOL-031 *archive
    review / custody* is a candidate);
  - who receives it;
  - what it says;
  - whether Kade, who never met Tahl, is among "the group."
- **The B6 epilogue** now holds two recorded items: this message, and M23's MT handoff to Kade.
  Their order is open.
