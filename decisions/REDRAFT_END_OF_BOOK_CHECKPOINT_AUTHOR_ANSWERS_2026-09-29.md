## 3. The two design changes, recorded

### 3.1 Calendar C, as the redraft realises it

- **Was (calendar C, D1, applied at Q-OL4 #10):** B01 late February to late April. Anchors: E15 mid-March, E33 mid-April,
  E45 and E48 late April. Act III about 18–30 April.
- **Now (the finished redraft):** B01 runs late February to **about 2 May**. The Square (redraft E14) is about 17 March.
  Ursulines (E32) is Friday about 10 April. Act III runs Monday about 20 April to Saturday about 2 May: the lot (E45–E46)
  is Wednesday about 29 April, and the Square's festival days (E48–E49) are Friday and Saturday, about 1–2 May.
- **Why:** Act I's weekdays fix Easter at about 5 April. The page's window opens the Monday after Easter, and Act II's E36
  ends on a Friday, about 17 April. The briefs' old late-April weekdays could not all be true at once (ledger §301, §308).
  **The endpoint moves about three days past *late April*.** The weekdays are consistent throughout; no approximate date
  hides an incompatible one.
- **B02's opening interval checked:** B02 runs May through August (Q-B2-6). Its Act I briefs are dated *May* (E01–E09)
  and then *late May* (E10–E14). E01 opens with Baz already known in the city, so B02 E01 is read as **mid-May or later,
  at least about ten days after B01's close**. That leaves a short but workable May for E01–E09. **No B02 brief
  changes now;** the Veil continuity reconciliation, which waits on the author's read, confirms it.

### 3.2 Forecast A, as the redraft realises it

- **Was (D3, forecast A, for the pre-redraft draft):** the page's lines included the Marigny. *Bigger* was measured by
  edges and duration, so the page was **satisfied on the Saturday** (the Marigny). A **later event** (Ursulines)
  exposed how little they understood. The two faces fell on two events.
- **Now (the finished redraft):** the Marigny ring (about 4 April) falls **before** the window. It is neither hit nor
  miss (E31), and it is the one event that came up slowly, as her watch line said. **Ursulines is the first and only
  qualifying event.** At Ursulines the page is right on window and place, and wrong on every watch line, on *larger*, and
  on the hurt it had no line for. **Technical success and practical failure fall on the same event.** The central idea
  (right technically, inadequate in practice) is kept; **the dramatic form has changed**, and this record says so.
  *(The Act III reconciliation report's §2.2 had called this A's shape "still"; that sentence is corrected there, ledger
  §312.)*
