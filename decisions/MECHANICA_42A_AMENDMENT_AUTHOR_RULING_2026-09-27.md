## 1. What is ruled

**§42A, *The Hard Cap and the Loom*, is added to `rules/Mechanica-v4.md`, as drafted**, after §42
*Filtration mechanics*. The only additions are the dated amendment note that heads the section and
the reflowing of that note. No existing section is edited, moved or renumbered.

**The whole section is now Mechanica rule text.** The draft's *"Notes for the ruling"* said so before
the ruling: applying it would make the section rule text. The author applied it without changes.

This makes the following **rule, not only approved design**:

- the soul supplies Intent;
- the hard cap sets one fixed ceiling, not a regulation of people, and is brittle for four reasons;
- breaks become wounds, and stabilising a wound under a hard cap is temporary;
- the Breathable Veil is held jointly;
- its laws apply themselves;
- empathy is its medium, not its judge;
- after the Mending the Loom does not decide for people, change the laws or command;
- the cycle ends;
- humans adapt.

**The source decisions keep their record and wording.** Where they and §42A differ, **§42A governs**,
as Mechanica does over other documents (§1).

## 2. What it settles, and what it does not

- **Settled:**
  - Mechanica now says what powers the Loom. The Mira ruling's *"Mechanica has no statement on what
    powers the Loom"* is answered.
  - The mechanism behind §40–§42 is in the rules.
- **Consistent with §1:** Mechanica v4 already supersedes *"Breathable Veil v1"*, the Bible #5 that
  the specification cited. §42A uses it only where a ruling or approved design took it up. Its
  virtue-gate wording stays unused.
- **Not in §42A, and still open:**
  - whether Silence and Hope have Intent;
  - what Seraphine experiences after the Mending;
  - why Honey Island must be repaired last;
  - Mira's schedule and her Veil seed;
  - all numbers.
- **Mira is not named in Mechanica.** Her role stays in `canon/cast_registry.csv` (`L01`) and the
  rulings.
- **Not added:** the optional pointer from §7.4 or §41, and a glossary entry for "hard cap"
  (Appendix B). Both need an instruction.
- **Still governs how any of this reaches the page:** §60, *"No mechanical explanation
  in-dialogue"*. The finale's answer stays reserved for the very end.
