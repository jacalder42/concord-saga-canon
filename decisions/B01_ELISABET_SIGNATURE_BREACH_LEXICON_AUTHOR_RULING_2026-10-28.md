## 1. Lexicon: breach, scar, Wound (author: "Wound sounds current and scar sounds old or permanent. I'm fine with wound lowercase or an alternate term that makes rational sense")
The writer thread chose, under that latitude:

| Term | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **breach** | An active local point where pressure has broken through. It stays weak, and each later break there is worse (the §619 escalation). Many exist. | The camp; Ursulines; the Square |
| **scar** | An old breach that has gone quiet but is still marked, and can wake. | Elisabet's northern site |
| **Wound** (capital W) | One of the **four** failure points of the cap: Santa Fe → Mound City → Serpent Mound → Honey Island. Reserved. | — |

- **Why "breach":** it is the word New Orleans already uses for the levee failure. It is current, it means a structure gave way where it was weakest, and a breached place takes water again.
- **Amends:** §619 ("a break leaves a local wound") now reads *breach*. Mechanica §42A.2's "each break becomes a wound" is read as a breach (lower case), distinct from the four Wounds. A dated note is added there.
- **The author may swap in lowercase "wound"** at any time; only the word changes.
- **Page use:** characters may say "breach" in its ordinary sense. They never learn the four Wounds in B01.

## 2. The water signature (observed): enters B01
- **What it is:**
  - Confined-aquifer wells normally rise and fall with the earth tides; they breathe.
  - During some intervals, several independent wells lose most of that tidal response at the same time. The tide is suppressed, not zeroed, and a faint, faster variation remains.
  - The public records show the suppression. Elisabet's per-second loggers show the grain inside it and the lead of nine seconds or more.
- **The physics is sound:** tidal response of well levels is a real, well-documented effect. The suppression intervals last hours to days.
- **Two exclusions:**
  - The ground still tides; only the water's response changes, so "Does not move the ground" stands.
  - A shallow well point (Ms. Dorsey's) can't show tides. Her home loggers show the grain and the lead, not the suppression.
- **I4 C16:65 ("my wells were flat… I have nothing"):** reading (a), no conflict. E7 is a local event of about 14 blocks with no citywide envelope. Her line answers the ground-motion question she was asked. The C16 +60 makes the answer exact rather than a denial of everything.
- **Mechanics ledger §10:** a new row (see that file).

## 3. The mechanism (tentative): adopted with conditions
- **Private physics, in the ledger only.** Loading changes how water responds before a breakthrough. The water couples to the load more strongly than to the much weaker tidal strain, so the tide is overridden. The faint, faster variation is the load's own expression. This is Mechanica §21's "strong amplification", read as preferential coupling. §31 is an analogy only, never cited as cause.
- **Conditions:**
  - The signature shows coarsely *that a window is open*. It never counts pulses and never says where. It must not out-forecast Dominion or spoil the Square's third pulse (§619).
  - The page never says "storage", "loading", or that something is *holding it back*; that is the cap (X1).
  - It has no bearing on Line 8.
