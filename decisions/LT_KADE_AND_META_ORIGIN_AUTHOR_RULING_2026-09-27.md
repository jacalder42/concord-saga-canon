## 1. VT closes at the Mending, and LT succeeds it (LT Q1)

**RULED.**

- **VT closes at the Mending.** Silence and Hope, its two parties, disperse into the veil's laws
  (Mechanica §42A.3).
- **LT succeeds it as a separate channel.** It is not VT converted: the channels never merge
  (Mechanica §36), and they never run at once.
- **The source is the author's own words:** *"post Mending VT converted to LT (LuminousThread)"*
  (2025-11-29) and *"LT = LuminousThread the successor to VT"* (2025-12-11).

their names erased. The source is the author's own words: *"My memory says they started as people
who get modified to become metas. If that is correct, they should have previous names before the
conversion"* (2025-11-21).
