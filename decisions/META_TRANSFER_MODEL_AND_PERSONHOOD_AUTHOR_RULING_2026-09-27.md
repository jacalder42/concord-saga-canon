## 1. The transfer loop is ruled, on a three-part model (Q1)

**RULED.** Meta Q1–Q3 of `decisions/MANUFACTURED_META_TRANSFER_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-27.md` are
promoted to ruled canon. Its Q5 is ruled as refined in §2 below.

- **Factions draw on a meta's stored energy.** The draw is unreliable and costly: to the meta, to the
  recipient (injury, poor control, and at the extreme permanent harm or death), and to those nearby.
- **What charges a meta:** harvested resonance, from followers and handlers. Crowds amplify it but are
  not harvested.
- **A meta never holds Intent.** Handlers' encoded directives are control, not Intent.
- **The three-part model:**
  1. **The donor's load:** power and emotion, stored and unintegrated. The meta cannot spend it.
  2. **The recipient's Emotion and Intent:** a person who receives a drawn load spends it. The
     recipient supplies the Intent.
  3. **Leakage:** a load that nothing spends discharges uncontrolled. That is §48's *"fail
     catastrophically under pressure"*.

**Applied:**

- **Mechanica §48** is rewritten to the model.
- **`canon/factions/Manufactured_Metas.md`:**
  - l. 9 now reads *"without Intent or emotional integration"*; it was *"without emotion or
    Intent"*;
  - a **storage-and-transfer** section is added;
  - the thesis now reads *"remove Intent"*; it was *"remove emotion"*;
  - *"output"* is framed as leakage.
- **`canon/factions/Manufactured_Metas_Variants.md`:** a section maps each variant to the model.
  Each variant's *"Design Intent"* is **the institution's aim**, not the meta's, and its *"resonance
  output"* is leakage.
- `rules/canon_rules.json` `manufactured_metas`: a `_model_note` is added.

## 2. The interface (Q2)

**RULED.**

- **What crosses:** the donor's stored load, **body to body, by physical contact.**
- **The apparatus** (a harness) **positions, restrains, times and monitors. It never conducts**
  (Mechanica §23).
- **Distance extraction never succeeds as transfer.** A distance machine can provoke leakage and
  instability, and nothing more. This corrects the transfer answer's *"rarely work"*.

## 4. Metas are people (Q5)

**RULED.** Every mechanical limit stands:

- no Intent;
- no ascension;
- no restored powers;
- no stable long-term existence;
- no POV.

**The framing changes:**

- **Variants:** *"Not Characters"* → ***"People, Not POV Characters"***.
- **Card:** *"power without humanity"* → ***"power taken from people"***.
- **Card:** the question *"what happens when we try to remove humanity from the equation?"* is kept.
  Its answer is added: **institutions tried, and the people were still there.**
- **Card:** *"No redemption arc"* → ***"No return to Intent or to what they were; their personhood
  is never in question"***.
- **Allowed:**
  - evidence of **former identity** (a name recovered, a keepsake);
  - **relationships** (someone looking for them);
  - **how they were recruited or coerced**. The author: *"tricking the weak or poor into seeking
    grandeur"* (2025-11-10).
