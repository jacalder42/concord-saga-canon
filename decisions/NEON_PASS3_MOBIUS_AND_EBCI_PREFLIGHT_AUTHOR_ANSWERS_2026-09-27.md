## 4. The author's direction on Seraphine

> *"I think we will need more Seraphine before we are done."*

**READING:** the provisional Neon share stands for now, but **the saga must give Seraphine more
point-of-view weight before the architecture is final.** Applied as follows:

- **Loom's architecture** (the next step) is built with Seraphine's share **at or above the ~30%
  band**. She is the saga lead, and Loom is where she becomes the Loom.
- **The nine-book audit** measures her share across all nine books and proposes where Neon (and
  Veil, if needed) should give her more, without cutting the separated theaters that Neon's design
  relies on.
- **Not decided here:** a target number, or which Neon episodes change.
