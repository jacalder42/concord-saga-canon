# B01 E01 realism: the transport, and the story's period

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Status:** CURRENT AUTHOR ANSWERS. Ledger §362. The period is a standing setting answer (approved design); the E01
changes are manuscript direction.

**What it does not change:** no guard, ruled card or *(ruled)* profile line. Guidry, the coroner's form and the *None*
are unchanged.

## 1. The E01 realism review

A check of E01 against current procedure (Louisiana Medicaid renewal, 911 routing, dispatcher CPR for a child, EMS,
the death investigation). Most of the chapter is accurate. The author approved four fixes:
1. no lay pulse check;
2. the call transferred across the parish line, and the house tried;
3. (superseded by §2 below) a medical-control call before a field pronouncement;
4. Odile will not renew by phone: *"stubborn enough to want to deal with a person (Seraphine) instead of a phone or a
   computer."*

Fixes 1, 2 and 4 are applied (manuscript `bcdcc8e`).

## 2. The transport (the author's choice)

> *"If the paramedics take him to the hospital, Renee goes with them. Seraphine could stay and deal with any paperwork
> or reports needed. Likely it wouldn't be a coroner but some matter of police."*

- The paramedics transport Dré; Renée rides with him; Seraphine stays with Odile, and the deputies take the reports.
- The coffee confrontation is cut. The author: it *"doesn't feel right."*
- **Checked against the law:** a child's sudden, unexpected death, including one in an emergency room, is still a
  parish coroner's case in Louisiana (R.S. 13:5713). The coroner therefore stays, as the author's *"matter of police"*
  is carried by the deputies. Guidry still comes to the house for the scene, because the arrest happened there.
- E01 and E02 are revised to match (ledger §362).

## 3. The story's period

> *"Agreed present day, leave the year unstated."*

The saga is set in the present day, and **no year is stated on the page**. Modern technology is part of the world;
characters work around it by choice. The B01 calendar already matches the present.
