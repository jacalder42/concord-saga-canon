# B01 music: rules and range (2026-10-24)

## Author rulings, given through the research thread and confirmed in the writer thread
- **Songs are never named.** Use the title phrase only, and quote no other lyric (licensing). Each book may carry an optional "Heard in this book" end page.
- **One song per person;** a returning song changes meaning.
- **Music sits beside the phenomenon** and never explains it.
- **No song for the Lacuna cameo** (the plum-gauze singer).
- **Avoid** "What a Wonderful World" and "When the Saints".
