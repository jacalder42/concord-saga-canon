## 3. Readings (not ruled)

- **Why Silence and Hope think only Seraphine must ascend.** Last cycle, one Seraphine-type, Mira,
  was enough to make a hard cap. That fits the author's 11-15 *"they only understood their own hard
  cap solution"* (`PC` 91822), and it makes their error a learned one, not a blind one.
- **Where Mira is now.** The author's 11-13 *"I think Mira merges back with architecture"* (`PC`
  41513) and 09-26 *"integrates with the loom"* suggest she is **part of the current cap's
  structure**. From there she guides the one who will replace it. That matches the saga's Möbius
  shape.
- **Her guidance and Seraphine's doubt.** Seraphine's doubt grows from becoming the Loom (ruled). A
  guide who **made** the old cap could pull either way:
  - toward the hard cap, as the way it was done before;
  - toward the breathing veil, as the one who knows its cost.

  The author's 11-13 *"through Mira Seraphine believes D"* (her withdrawal is inevitable) leans
  toward the first. This is not decided.
