# B01 compression archaeology (muscle vs fat): author instruction

Status: CURRENT AUTHOR INSTRUCTION, 2026-10-04. It queues a read-only audit. It is not a ruling on canon, and it does
not authorize any prose revision.

## The instruction

After the heat, humor, banter, flirting and innuendo audit (ledger §394) is complete and pushed, run a separate
**read-only comparative audit of the original 200k+ B01 manuscript against the current revised (~120k–124k) B01**. The
author's full text is kept privately in the manuscript repository
(`draft-notes/draft3/COMPRESSION_ARCHAEOLOGY_BRIEF.md`).

**Purpose:** *"Did compression make B01 leaner, or did it make B01 thinner? Identify where it did each."*

### What it requires

- **A textual baseline.**
  - Locate the best surviving 200k+ manuscript, with its date, commit and word count. If several are plausible, list
    them and justify the choice; never silently pick an intermediate revision.
  - Identify the current manuscript after the staging pass.
  - Reconstruct episode ancestry where the numbering or structure changed, and document the uncertainty.
- **Four classes for removed or compressed material:**
  - FAT (correctly removed);
  - MUSCLE (a valuable function lost);
  - MIXED (a fat scene with muscle inside it);
  - SUPERSEDED (no longer valid against current canon, mechanics, guards, reveal order or arcs).
- **The old manuscript is evidence, not authority.** The default recommendation is *function worth recovering*, not
  *restore this passage*. Reusable prose is only flagged, never restored.
- **Matched units.** Compare matched units across Act I, early, middle and late Act II, early and late Act III, and the
  ending.
- **The prose ecology,** compared across:
  - plot;
  - repetition;
  - dialogue and its staging;
  - interiority;
  - differentiation;
  - place and the senses;
  - ordinary life;
  - humor, banter, heat, flirting and innuendo;
  - relationship rhythms;
  - conflict aftermath;
  - transitions;
  - wonder and dread;
  - narrative voice.
- **Function, not word count.** For each finding, give the old and current footprints and the likely new prose needed.
- **Systematic cut bias,** characterized or quantified.
- **No word target.** 120k is not a target or a ceiling for this audit, and fat can still be cut.
- **Cross-check against the play audit.** Contradictions are evidence.
- **A verdict on the causal hypothesis:** CONFIRMED, PARTLY CONFIRMED, NOT CONFIRMED, or MIXED / DIFFERENT CAUSE. It
  separates problems caused by compression from problems already present in the 200k+ manuscript.
- **The outputs:**
  - a comparative table;
  - a Muscle Recovery Ledger;
  - aggregate proportions, as ranges where sampling can't support precision;
  - ranked lists of the highest-value lost material;
  - the old material not to romanticize;
  - a natural-length finding, without a target.
- **Push it, and return the findings and the author decisions needed before any recovery work.**

## What it does not change

It changes no ruling, card, packet or manuscript text. The Muscle Recovery Ledger is for review, not authorization.
The play audit is not interrupted.
