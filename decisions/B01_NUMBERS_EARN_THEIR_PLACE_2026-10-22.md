# B01: numbers must earn their place (2026-10-22)

## Author direction
- *"Portions of B1 is starting to feel like a procedural thriller about counting. It's a boring plot with some interesting people in an interesting place. I feel like the plot was thin to start and numbers, counting, and measuring were the only thing inflated."*
- *"Consider if we essentially ban counting or numbers. Much like romance, kisses, etc they have to earn their place."*
- On the proposed steps (adopt the rule, draft a spine proposal, then sweep): *"1 yes 2 yes consider conversation below 3 tbd"*.

## Ruling
- **Default.** Numbers, counting, measuring and timestamps are out by default.
- **What earns a number.** A number earns its place only if it:
  - changes what someone does;
  - is a clue the reader must hold that pays off (a handful in the book, each shown once);
  - carries feeling;
  - or is Lucien under pressure, from an allowance that shrinks across the book toward "I was afraid".
- **Replacements.** Images replace clock times. Rough sizes replace counts. What happens in between replaces intervals. Narration never counts for a character.
- **What stays.** The datelines stay as the book's calendar. The 90-day deadline is named rarely and never counted down.
- **Rule text.** The full text is in the manuscript style sheet, §10.

## Context
- A measure on 2026-10-21 found about one number or measuring word per 32 words of B01.
- The author's parallel reference-novel thread found that every chapter except P, C02, C13, C19 and E runs above the most number-heavy reference novel. Recited figures run at 16× the reference rate.
- **Diagnosis, which both threads share:**
  - The plot's engine is proof: heroes gather readings, and opposition seeks the folder, method and phone.
  - Measurement became the only way to advance.

## Sequence
1. Adopt the rule. Done.
2. A read-only scene-function audit of all 201 sections, plus two or three one-page plot spines built from material already in B01. In progress.
3. The manuscript sweep. Timing is TBD: after the spine is chosen, so that numbers are not thinned in scenes that will be cut.

Line-level passes are paused until the spine is chosen.
