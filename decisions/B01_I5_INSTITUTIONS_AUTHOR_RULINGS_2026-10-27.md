1. **The Dominion naming lock is OFF.** This amends `canon/factions/Dominions.md`, dated note 2026-10-18, later (ledger §592): "named once in B01, at C34 … No further naming in B01 is licensed." 2. **Hollen is a front for Dominion.** Both the Hollen Institute (Vienna, the Regional Review) and the Hollen Foundation (the tent, the intake) are Dominion fronts. Legal ownership and funding mechanics stay off the page ("as best serves the story").
   - **Canon correction:** Dominions.md cites Baz's "post office" line at C34. That line is not in I4 (it was cut before H2). Treat it as absent until I5 decides whether to restore it.
3. **Baz's knowledge** (delegated by the author to the editing thread, "what serves the story best"). The ruling adopted:

   - He does **not** learn in B01 that his Houston funder is a faction inside Dominion. That stays a B03 payoff (the split at his death), and nothing in B01 hints it to him. This is CANON_COLLISIONS row B2, unchanged.
   - The canon basis, "believes he left Dominion but is still being paid by it", stands.
