> *"I lean 3 or 4, potential tie in to Baz's demise."*

### 2. Concord's nature (direction; bears on §2 items 3 and 4)

> *"it feels more nuanced than this. Concord starts as a loose collection of people trying to help. The term is then
> co-opted by an organization that eventually begins to behave insincerely or following their own best interest."*

- **Accepted (direction):** **the protagonists' Concord is a loose collection of people trying to help.** The term
  starts with them. **An organization later co-opts the term** and, over time, **begins to behave insincerely or in
  its own interest.**
- **This refines** the 2026-10-18 approved recommendation *"the ethic emerges; the organization is founded after the
  split"* (`B01_POLISH_RECOMMENDATIONS_APPROVED_2026-10-18.md`, Concord 1). Where that wording implies that the
  protagonists' circle founds a formal organization, it **overstates the formality** and is superseded: what the
  circle has after the split is a loose collection, not an organization. The author's 2025-12-12 constraint *"Concord
  is NOT founded — it EMERGES"* (`recovery/ACCOUNT_EXPORT_CONCORD_TIER1_PAIR_AUDIT_2026-09-20.md` §2) **largely stands
  again** for the protagonists' Concord.
- **Consistent with** the 2025-12-10 two-branch model (`sources/chatgpt_export_2026-09/2025-12-10__Character_Vault_Chat__6938daf6.md`,
  "Communal Concord" and "Institutional Concord"; *"The institutional Concord drifts. The communal Concord never
  dies."*): the loose collection is the communal branch; the co-opting organization is the institutional branch. **One
  difference is recorded, not resolved:** the Dec 10 text has the institutional drift *"quietly, not villainously"*
  and *"not maliciously subverted"* in Neon; the author now says the organization *"eventually begins to behave
  insincerely or following their own best interest"*. The author's 2026-10-18 words govern; the Dec 10 text stays as
  history.
- **Reading of item 1 under item 2 (Claude's reconciliation, not an author ruling):** "the founding" at the B03→B04
  boundary is read as the point where the loose collection, free of the funder, comes to be Concord (the term in use
  for it), not the founding of a formal body. If the author means a formal step, that is a new question.
- **Who the co-opting organization is: resolved at 09:59 (see "The funder" below) as funder lineage.** The options put
  were (a) funder lineage, (b) a separate institution, (c) both (the sequence approved earlier the same day, *"the
  former funder first, then an institutional Concord that grows from it"*, Concord 2). The ruling below makes the
  co-opting organization **the former funder**, the internal Dominion faction. The institutional Concord of the Dec
  10 model is therefore that faction's organization under the borrowed name, not a separate body; (b) is not used.
- **Not accepted:** no card is reclassified. The Tier-1 Concord files are annotated with which parts describe the loose
  collection and which the co-opting organization (applied 2026-10-18, ledger §592); the classification line and
  §VII of Concord-Mandate are not rewritten (see the remaining OPEN items).

## The funder: an internal faction within Dominion (author ruling, 2026-10-18, 09:59, "Agreed as recommended")

Relayed by the coordinator, replacing an earlier interim instruction of the same morning (record the funder as "a
Dominion splinter or internal faction (author considering)", relationship to leadership OPEN), which is withdrawn.

**Accepted (ruled):**
- **Baz's funder is an internal faction within Dominion, not a clean splinter that has left.** Virelli's leadership
  wants resonance hidden and contained. This faction has concluded the secrecy will not hold (**B02 is when it becomes
  unsustainable**) and wants to **own the public face of resonance when it breaks**. It funds care, consent forms and
  goodwill, and seeks *firsts*, because whoever holds them holds the evidence and the story. This explains the B01 page:
  the Dominion form word *first* (C11) and the appetite for consents (C12).
- **The help is real; the motive is self-interest.** It is **the organization that later co-opts the name "Concord"**,
  when resonance goes public in Neon, and drifts into insincerity. **The loose network the protagonists belong to keeps
  the word honestly; the two collide.** It **collapses in Loom, with Dominion's legacy** (agrees with item 4).
- **Baz believes he left Dominion but is still being paid by it.** The circle learning this **around his death (end of
  B03)** is **the cause of the split** (agrees with item 1's B03→B04 boundary).

**Accepted, sub-points:**
1. **Virelli does not know at first**: a hedge inside the institution, not open revolt. **By B04**, when he demands
   Lucien's return (M05), **he suspects**.
2. **Partly sincere at the start:** some members believe in openness; the leadership wants control of the story. Not a
   plain villain. *"No redemption arc"* applies to **the faction's leadership, provisionally** (refines item 3).
3. **Known characters:** **Helena Kael is a possible member** (her B02 request to Lucien becomes double-edged).
   Recorded as a **possibility, not locked**. Marius is not proposed.
4. **Baz does not suspect in B01.** **Lucien may notice the shared word *first* and say nothing**; no page change is
   needed.

**Not accepted / not changed:** **no B01 page change**. The faction has no name. Its size, its members beyond the
Helena possibility, the book in which Virelli first suspects (by B04, not earlier fixed), and the book and manner of its
collapse in Loom are not ruled.

**Supersedes** the *"Dominion splinter"* wording of §588 (`B01_POLISH_CANON_AUDIT_RULINGS_2026-10-18.md` #1), §590
(`B01_POLISH_RECOMMENDATIONS_APPROVED_2026-10-18.md`, Concord and the funder) and the §591 canon notes, by dated
pointers; those texts are kept as written. **Resolves** item 2's OPEN question (the co-opting organization is the
former funder: funder lineage), and answers §2 item 7 in part (Baz does not suspect in B01; the circle learns around
his death; the name is co-opted when resonance goes public in Neon).

## After the split: funding (author, 2026-10-18, 10:02, "Yes") — PROVISIONAL DIRECTION, nothing locked

Relayed by the coordinator.

**Accepted (provisional direction):**
- **After the split from the faction funder** (B03→B04, around Baz's death), the loose network's money comes mainly
  from two sources:
  - **(a) Elisabet brings in open, public money she can sign for**: for example a university research grant or Nordic
    civil-protection funding tied to her environmental crisis-data work, **with written conditions and data owned by the
    people it is about**. It is the honest mirror of the faction's secret consent-and-*firsts* money.
  - **(b) Local mutual aid carries the day-to-day** (church halls, plate sales, neighbours), so **the network never
    depends on one patron again**.
- **It gives Elisabet a reason to stay in New Orleans.** At B01's end her month is nearly up and her car is packed
  (C20, *"I gave it a month"*); *"Ask me in a month"* (C16) has a place to land.
- **Elisabet is not personally wealthy** (card: middle to upper-middle academic class). The money is institutional and
  public, not hers.
- **Rex is not the funder.** He is a **possible later contributor of infrastructure** (systems engineering) in Neon or
  Loom.

**Option, not locked:** a later pressure point: if her grant comes with strings, she recognizes the pattern fastest.

**Not accepted / not changed:** which funder, the grant's size or terms, the book in which the money arrives (after the
split; B04 by the leaning), whether Elisabet formally signs for the network or for a project it uses, and any Rex
contribution are not ruled. No B01 page change. Applied as dated notes: `canon/characters/ElisabetID.md`,
`canon/characters/RexID.md`, `canon/factions/Concord/Concord-Structure.md` (§VI), `Concord-EraFunction.md`, and the
B03/B04 book contexts.
