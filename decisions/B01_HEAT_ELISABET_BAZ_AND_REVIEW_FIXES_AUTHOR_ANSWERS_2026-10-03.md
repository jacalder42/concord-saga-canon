## 1. What was put to the author

There were two inputs: ChatGPT's review of canon commit 282fc0c and manuscript commit dc796c2, and the author's own
note. ChatGPT made five findings, and Claude verified each one against the files. The author's note was: *"B01 needs
significant heat moment or we'll lose the Romantasy readers … What would Ali Hazelwood do?"*

Claude's recommendation was:

- **The kiss:** a pretext "cover" kiss during the E39 overnight stakeout in Seraphine's car. They pose as a couple while
  whoever collects the sensor walks past, and the kiss goes on a beat too long. Afterwards both insist it did not count,
  which makes B03 E21 the first kiss that counts.
- **Elisabet:** her flirting with both Seraphine and Lucien, raised by the author, is restored as Act II's romance
  engine. It is recovered design: `proposals/B01_CHARACTER_LIFE_ENTRY_EXIT_AUDIT_PASS1_2026-09-22.md` recorded her
  "flirt texture toward both Lucien and Seraphine", and it never reached the 09-29 packets.
