## 3. The full-B01 audit: its tests

After A2 and A3 are built, the audit is **broader than Act I's two questions**. It is the first
inspection of an entire book at executable resolution:

1. **Narrative flow:** does B01 still feel like a novel rather than 48 individually optimised episodes?
2. **Causal progression:** do E15 → E33 → E45 → E48 escalate in **kind, knowledge and consequence**,
   not just intensity?
3. **Character movement:** plot Seraphine's, Lucien's, Baz's and Caro's beginning, middle and end
   states from the packets; does each actually change?
4. **Relationship rhythm:** Seraphine/Lucien and Caro/Elisabet especially: progression, breathing
   room, accidental repetition.
5. **Ordinary-life ecology:** fun, slice of life and wonder as a **book-level waveform**, not quotas;
   any long stretch where the human world disappears.
6. **Knowledge economy:** for each major conclusion, who observed it, who inferred it, who was told
   it, and what remains uncertain. Especially for Resonance.
7. **Breadcrumb visibility:** has the ledger made B01 suspiciously full of meaningful objects and
   phrases? *"Some material must remain gloriously useless."*
8. **Episode necessity:** which episodes could be removed with nothing changing? **Do not delete them
   automatically**: Life/Reward episodes may be necessary without moving plot. Understand why each
   exists.
9. **Prose freedom:** sample packets across event, relationship, investigation, Life/Reward and
   supplement types: **does EBCI prescribe the best part of the scene?**
10. **Downstream discoveries:** explicitly, anything that would require reconsidering B02–B09. *"This
    is the last cheap point to catch it."*

**The anti-optimisation test:** *"Where is B01 allowed to be messy, surprising, funny, inefficient, or
merely alive? If the answer is hard to find after full EBCI, we've overbuilt it even if every validator
passes."*

## 4. The production order: an expected path

*"Act I moves me further toward Veil EBCI before Veil prose, but not Neon/Loom EBCI yet."* EBCI protects
the cross-book knowledge, relationships, causality and breadcrumbs B02/B03 need to inherit; it does not
generate enough new saga architecture to justify hardening Neon and Loom now, whose provisional
architecture is *"doing its job perfectly"*.

**Assuming full B01 passes:** B01 EBCI → full-book audit → B02 EBCI → audit → B03 EBCI → Veil EBCI /
trilogy audit → **Veil prose**. Then Veil prose talks back to provisional Neon **before** Neon EBCI.
*"That is now supported by evidence rather than just planning theory."* This refines, and does not yet
rule, item 8 of the progressive-resolution instruction and §5 of the post-audit instruction.
