## 2. The author's new direction: varied pressures, and a third, external one

> *"I feel like we need varied pressures. The care one is currently overstated but overly focused on. We've also added
> the secondary pressures for characters. I feel like we still need a third antagonistic pressure even if it shifts be
> the system/governance/society. This third pressure could shift over B1 to increasingly point toward factions. Perhaps
> this shows with Lucien beginning to question Dominion more as he spends more time with Baz etc"*

**Taken as:**
1. **Care is de-emphasised,** not removed. It stops leading.
2. **The wants stay** as the second pressure.
3. **A third, external pressure is added:** what the system, governance, institutions or society press on each
   character.
4. **The external pressure drifts across B01,** from diffuse friction toward organised actors. **The factions stay
   unnamed in B01** (Q-AN), and B02 names them.
5. **Lucien begins to question his office, and through it the Dominion,** as he spends time with Baz.

**A constraint to resolve.** Approved guards say Lucien's distrust of his institution is **not stated** in B01 (the
E17 packet; obligation #52). His questioning can be shown in behaviour inside that guard. If the author wants it
voiced, the loosening is his call, and it is set out in the proposal.

**Being drafted:** a proposal for the three-pressure model, the third pressure for each Veil-era character with its
B01 arc and B02–B03 continuation, Lucien's arc in detail, an external-pressure column for the merged outline's 50
pieces, and Seraphine's second want.
