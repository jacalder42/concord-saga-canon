## 2. Also decided: web serialisation stays out of the packets

**No web-serialisation concern enters prose packets or drafting.** The novel keeps its natural scene and turn
boundaries. Web installments of about 1,500–1,800 words are **derived later from the finished manuscript**. The author:
*"Otherwise we're giving the drafting system another optimization target just when we've learned to give it fewer."*

## 3. The production sequence

**Q-AR1–9 → Act I stays frozen as the calibration corpus → derive E18–E37 + S04/S05 → audit the Act II packets for
Act I-shape imitation → draft sequentially against revised Act I.**
