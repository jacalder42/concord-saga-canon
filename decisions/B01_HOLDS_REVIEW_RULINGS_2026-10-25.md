## Directions
1. Characters develop and contest theories. Lucien, Elisabet, Baz and Caro are not confined to observing and withholding; wrong theories are productive material.
2. More than one positive finding is allowed. "The water goes first" stays, and the book may discover the phenomenon's geometry, its selectivity and what it doesn't follow. The finale's deeper mechanism stays protected.
3. Dominion becomes a usable name after C34.
4. Clarify what Dominion believes an *Emitter* is. Characters can understand a dangerous organization's theory without the narrator endorsing it.
5. The Nyla anchor restriction is removed. She can demonstrably interact with the phenomenon without being classified as an anchor, emitter or lens.

## General
1. Protect actual future reveals, not old drafting habits.
2. Allow observations and wrong theories, but don't reveal the finale's collective-rhythm mechanism.
3. Keep Nyla's Epilogue interaction concrete, without assigning her a cosmic classification.

**Note:** the B02 packets' old guard, "physics observed, never explained by the page", predates this ruling and should be updated to the loosened W2 when B02 is drafted.
