## 1. The trilogy contract (adopted)
For every trilogy:
1. **Its own question.** Asked in book 1 and answered in book 3. The answer may be "no".
2. **An entry point.** Its first book (B01, B04, B07) re-delivers on its own page everything its climax needs.
3. **A partial payoff for every setup** inside its trilogy. A setup paid only in a later trilogy is a million-word dependency, to be fixed or cut.
4. **Its own discovery ladder.** It never resets, and each trilogy's ceiling is the next one's floor.
5. **An ending that lands.** Book 3 resolves the lead's arc and the trilogy's antagonist face. Only the saga's reserved reveals may stay unanswered.
