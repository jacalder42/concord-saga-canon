# Progressive resolution sequence — author instruction

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Status:** CURRENT AUTHOR INSTRUCTION. It governs the **work order and method**. It rules no canon.

**Context:** Claude recommended building provisional Neon and Loom architecture before Veil prose,
with a live breadcrumb ledger and a B01 EBCI preflight in parallel (in chat, answering the author's
question on breadcrumbs and the Möbius). The author agreed, with refinements.

**The author's words, verbatim:**

> Agreed with the overall direction, with several refinements.
>
> 1. Proceed with Neon B04–B06 to provisional episode-architecture / Pass-3 resolution, beginning with reconciliation of existing material rather than a fresh draft. The target is sufficient episode resolution to establish owner, function, turn, milestone, relationship/system obligation and downstream payoff—not matching Veil's Pass-5 depth for its own sake. If an individual book requires another pass to become structurally sound, do it.
> 2. Then bring Loom B07–B09 to the same provisional episode-architecture resolution and perform a Loom trilogy audit. Preserve the existing B08 end-sequence deferral until episode architecture gives us enough information to resolve it honestly.
> 3. Activate grids/breadcrumbs.csv as a live ledger during this work rather than waiting until both trilogies are finished. Begin with existing Veil plants and known saga payoffs, classifying the payoff dependency:
>     * LOCKED/RULED: precise planting is safe.
>     * SOFT/APPROVED DESIGN: plant polyvalently so more than one execution can satisfy it.
>     * PROVISIONAL/EPISODE ARCHITECTURE: plant directionally and do not make upstream continuity depend on exact episode placement.
>
> Episode numbers should be locators, not the identity of a breadcrumb; the underlying narrative function/payoff is what survives a downstream move.
>
> 4. Distinguish the already-defined macro-Möbius from the broader Möbius audit. The B01 prologue ↔ B09 epilogue structure is established enough to design now. Nested character, relationship, place, system and image Möbius structures should remain open to discovery during B04–B09 episode development and receive a dedicated nine-book audit afterward.
> 5. After Neon and Loom reach episode architecture, perform a full nine-book Breadcrumb / Möbius / Promise-Payoff audit. Use it to identify any surgical Veil episode-architecture amendments before EBCI. Do not add plants merely to fill the ledger; ordinary details and Life/Reward material must remain ordinary where appropriate.
> 6. In parallel, proceed with a B01 EBCI preflight only: template/schema, causal-card requirements, physics/effects/conflict-code questions, envelope requirements and production/validation workflow. Do not release the B01 EBCI hold or generate B01 EBCI yet.
> 7. After the nine-book episode audit, return for the B01 EBCI release decision. If released, build Veil EBCI B01–B03 and audit the trilogy again.
> 8. Do not yet decide that Veil prose must precede Neon/Loom EBCI. Use Veil EBCI as the diagnostic. If Veil EBCI mostly resolves already-known architecture into executable beats, consider continuing into Neon—and potentially Loom—EBCI before prose. If Veil EBCI creates material new saga obligations, stop, reconcile downstream architecture, and consider beginning Veil prose before hardening later EBCI.
>
> The governing principle is progressive resolution:
>
> Saga architecture → all-nine episode architecture → saga breadcrumb/Möbius audit → Veil EBCI → reassess → deeper downstream EBCI as justified → prose.
>
> Neon and Loom episode architecture is provisional by design and must remain revisable when Veil EBCI or prose discovers a better answer.
>
> Proceed with Neon reconciliation / Pass 3 and the non-narrative B01 EBCI preflight under these constraints. Do not delete or replace existing material without specific direction.
