## What it directs

- **Scope:** a restrained line-level pass on the current authoritative B01, the performed draft. **It is not:**
  - a developmental rewrite;
  - a compression pass;
  - a voice rewrite;
  - a humanizer pass;
  - a word-count exercise.
- **Governing rules:**
  - Do not remove the behavioral language. Remove redundant interpretation, and vary repeated behavioral syntax.
  - Dramatize change, compress information, and never explain a change after it has been dramatized.
- **Watch families, which are search families, not bans:**
  - going still;
  - looking from X to Y;
  - hands on, around or against things;
  - objects held against the body;
  - faces doing things;
  - waiting for the rest;
  - studying someone before speaking;
  - *plain* / *plainly*;
  - *the way someone…*;
  - an action followed by its explanation;
  - a reception beat that restates the dialogue;
  - recurring micro-blocking.
- **A five-question decision test.** It protects character signatures, named strong behavioral moments, and
  metaphors that reveal POV, setting, profession, culture or the book's physical logic.
- **Classification A–F:**
  - A: redundant interpretation;
  - B: repeated syntax;
  - C: generic blocking;
  - D: over-explained comparison;
  - E: dialogue-reception redundancy;
  - F: protect.
- **Order of work:**
  1. Before editing, a recurrence audit: counts, the examples to protect, the weaker examples, the uncertain
     patterns, and the intervention level by act.
  2. Then the pass, made conservatively.
  3. Then a report: edits by class, net words, chapters substantially touched, matches deliberately left alone, and
     flags for anything touching characterization, continuity, canon, attraction, mystery logic or emotional
     consequence.

## How it is carried out (Claude's implementation choices, reversible)

- **A new private folder,** `B01/final-2026-10-06/`, copied from the performed draft. The performed draft stays
  untouched, so the author can compare the two side by side again.
- **A regex recurrence script** (`draft-notes/final-pass-2026-10-06/recurrence.py`) gives the whole-book baseline.
  Readers then refine it, because counts alone cannot tell a signature from a tic.

## What it does not change

- No canon text or writer-profile rule.
- No guard.
- No plot beat or chapter boundary.
- None of:
  - the mystery architecture;
  - the E48/E49 disclosure;
  - the St. Ann result;
  - the Veil.
