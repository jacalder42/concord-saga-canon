# POV Distribution Targets and Trilogy Baton — Author Ruling — 2026-09-26

Status: CURRENT AUTHOR RULING / RECOVERED FRAMEWORK CLARIFIED
Scope: saga-wide POV planning and later EBCI allocation. This ruling assigns no episode POV and changes no existing episode architecture.

The author recalled the established POV framework as approximately:

## Current interpretation

### 1. The percentages are diagnostic bands, not quotas

They are a composition target used to notice imbalance. They do not require:
- exact percentages;
- equal shares per act;
- mechanical rotation;
- compensating episodes merely to restore a ratio;
- forcing a POV onto an event that belongs to another character.

A book can deviate when its actual story requires it. Trilogy-scale balance may also explain an individual book's deviation.

### 2. Story ownership outranks arithmetic

Choose POV based on:
1. who has the most consequential experience or decision in the scene;
2. whose knowledge/ignorance creates the strongest narrative effect;
3. whose arc owns the consequence;
4. geographic presence and information access;
5. voice/rhythm variety;
6. only then, whether the broader POV balance needs correction.

### 3. Baton lead is not exclusive protagonist

Baz, Tahl and Kade carry the trilogy-level truth/protagonist baton. That does not require them to:
- be present in every major event;
- dominate every book equally;
- displace Seraphine's saga-lead function;
- absorb scenes that belong to Lucien, Caro, Elisabet, Rex or local characters.

### 4. Seraphine's saga-lead share can breathe

Approximately 30% protects her continuous saga centrality without requiring every plotline to route through her. Her absence from a theater can be structurally useful.

### 5. Secondary-protagonist share is a pool, not equal slices

The ~30% support/secondary band is distributed according to story need among eligible POVs. It does not mean Lucien, Caro, Elisabet, Rex, Lacuna or others receive equal mathematical allocations.

### 6. Flex is genuinely flexible

The ~10% flex band may be used for:
- civic/local POV;
- a character whose book-specific arc temporarily needs more room;
- antagonist or institutional perspective if separately permitted;
- a rare special-purpose perspective;
- redistribution to the three major bands when that produces the better book.

It is not a requirement to introduce novelty POVs.

## Geography interaction

This framework now operates together with `SAGA_CAST_SEPARATION_AND_GLOBAL_THEATERS_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-26.md`.

Distributed POV is one tool for making separated global theaters lived rather than merely reported. But geography does not create a POV entitlement: Vienna, Singapore, Reykjavík, Marrakesh or another theater may be experienced through a principal POV, a local recurring POV, remote receipt, supplement or no direct scene depending on story need.

Likewise, the 30/30/30/10 target must never be used to reassemble the principal cast simply to simplify POV allocation.

## Later implementation

When book architecture is stable enough, track:
- POV episode count;
- approximate narrative-word share if useful;
- act clustering;
- theater/location;
- major decision ownership;
- consecutive-POV runs;
- absence windows.

Use those measurements as **editorial diagnostics**, not automatic validators.

A healthy deviation requires a narrative reason, not a waiver. A mathematically perfect distribution with weak scene ownership is a failure.
