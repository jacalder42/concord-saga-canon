# Standing rule: rewording Exact and required lines for clarity (author ruling, 2026-10-29)

**Status:** current author ruling, relayed by the editing thread in `01_GATE_REQUEST_I72.md` item 3. Ledger §633.

## The rule
An Exact or required line may be reworded for clarity **with author approval**, provided three things survive:
1. **its function**: what the line does in its scene and in the book;
2. **its key phrase**: the words a later callback or reader memory hangs on;
3. **the callbacks it serves**: every echo elsewhere still lands.

The writer gate checks all three for each reworded line and records the old and new wording in the register and the callbacks list.

## Why
Several I7.1 fixes were workarounds placed beside a locked line. This rule lets the line itself be fixed instead.
