# B01 rewrite: opposition redesign (author direction and answers, 2026-10-15)

Status: AUTHOR DECISION. Ledger §565. Proposal: manuscript branch `claude/rewrite-2026-10-14`, `draft-notes/rewrite-2026-10-14/PROPOSAL_OPPOSITION_REDESIGN.md`, with its addendum. Prior rulings are compiled in `OPPOSITION_PRIOR_RULINGS.md`.

## The author's direction (verbatim)
> "We had them corrected for a bit and it feels like their success is backtracking. 1 Caldas is tracking Baz because Dominion wants to know what Baz knows and why he left/defected etc 2 Caldas should threaten, intimidate, and appear capable of violence. 3 … Act 2 introduces the notion that some folks already know and are trying to control it … Does Act II close with a confrontation against Caldas that the protagonists lose … this sets Act 3 up to resolve the first rung of the mystery, the defeat of Caldas, Baz's confession etc."

> "Act 2 portion is fine, act 3 should be the Veil file and what he thinks Dominion is trying to do. Setting up the B2 hook etc"

## Answers (all as recommended)
- **Q1. The VEIL file is "what you took".**
  - Caldas recovers the paper at Ch 30.
  - Baz's Act III confession gives the file from memory, together with what he thinks Dominion is trying to do.
  - The B02 mystery becomes "what he saw".
  - This reverses Q-H3 (2026-10-15).
- **Q2. "Why he left" reaches the page as one clause** (they did not warn a street, people were hurt, he left that week). Detail stays in B02. This amends Q-H4.
- **Q3. At Ch 30 Caldas takes the folder and Baz's notebook.** His man carries both. Caldas leaves empty-handed, so the prop rule holds.
- **Q4. Force on a protagonist at Ch 30.** Caldas leans on Lucien's torn arm for the keys: pain, no new injury. This is in addition to Mr. Ledet's wrist.
- **Q5. At Ch 13 Caldas names "Bastien" to Lucien, and Lucien lies** ("not since Vienna").
- **Q6. The S4 memo is cut.**
- **Q7. Caldas's parting line at 41.4:** "Nobody first. All right. Then it's everybody, Ms. Vael. That's a longer list."
- **Q8. Ch 44 becomes a short final intake.** It covers the Cases, the rest of the Review, and "if I say no". It ends on "What I saw … Not on a step" and on "they will call it care."

## Design summary
- **Act I:** the phenomenon looks new.
- **Act II:** people who already know try to control it. Caldas threatens and intimidates in person, and is capable of violence by the midpoint. Every Caldas scene has a demand, a refusal and a consequence. Act II ends with a loss to Caldas in person (Ch 30): he exposes Baz, takes the file, and Lucien walks.
- **Act III:**
  - Baz's Confession C-2 moves to 34.3: the VEIL file, and his partly wrong model of Dominion's aim (own it; pool the first feelers as the lever; disperse the rest).
  - The 2 May trap at 41.4 publicly disproves "who felt it first" ("nobody is first"), which is the first rung of the mystery. Caldas withdraws because of what they did, with no memo.
- **Exactly three capital-C Confessions:** 34.3 (Baz), 38 (Lucien), 40 (Seraphine).
- **B02+ limits on Caldas hold.** No arrest that sticks, no death, no maiming, no permanent burning. He recurs.
