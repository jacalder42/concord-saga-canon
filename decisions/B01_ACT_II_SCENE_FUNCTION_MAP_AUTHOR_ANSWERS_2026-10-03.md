## The caution, applied as a binding constraint on this revision

The author's note on times (ledger §347; Q-TM1–3, `decisions/B01_DRAFT3_UNDER_120K_HUMANISM_PASS_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-10-02.md`)
still holds, and the new beats must not reintroduce it.

- **The new beats show what the outside groups want without dwelling on clock times:**
  - **N9:** the young man with the turned badge pays for what each household saw and kept (photos, video, a
    written note), not for "the minute each pane broke".
  - **N3:** Isaiah's clip is a seen event (every hand stops). Its timing is not a data point to discuss.
  - **N4, N5, N8, N12** carry no minute- or time-stamp detail beyond what the scene needs.
- **Wherever the revision touches an Act II scene,** it cuts clock times, minute counts and time bookkeeping that the
  plot does not turn on. Keep only a time the plot turns on, such as a window or an appointment.
- **The watchers' want is the record of where and when, not only times.** Their requests read as wanting people's
  accounts, images and records, with time as one field among several.
