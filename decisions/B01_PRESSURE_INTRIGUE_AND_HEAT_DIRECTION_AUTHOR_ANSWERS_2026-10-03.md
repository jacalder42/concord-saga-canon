# B01: pressure, intrigue, heat, and Lucien and Baz's Dominion fit. Author direction and answers

Status: CURRENT AUTHOR ANSWERS (approved design), 2026-10-03. This is not ruled canon.

## The author's direction

> *I think resonance and antagonist factions need to be more visible and generate pressure to advance plot. I think we
> should play up the mystery and intrigue. I think more romantic heat would help. I think some action or a confrontation
> would help.*

On Lucien and Baz, he recalled that both work, or worked, for the Dominion, and that Baz left. He then proposed:
- Lucien is still with the Dominion **but does not know they have him searching for instances of resonance**.
- Baz **left the Dominion when he learned about resonance**. Once Lucien calls him in, he is caught between telling his
  friend and keeping him in the dark for his safety.
- This perhaps leads to Baz revealing his knowledge at the end of B01, with **Silence and Hope reprising in the
  epilogue, reacting to the knowledge spreading**.
- **Two shadowy pressures:** one group researching resonance on its own (Technarc?), and one looking for Baz and
  threatened by him (the Dominion).

## What is accepted (approved design)

- **Faction pressure.** The antagonist factions become more visible in B01 and drive the plot. Resonance becomes more
  visible as pressure, though not as a word on the page.
- **Mystery, intrigue, action.** More mystery and intrigue, and at least some action or confrontation.
- **Occlusion and the ladder.** References to the Dominion may be occluded ("the people behind the Institute"). The
  Dominion ladder may move if that works better.
- **Resonance off the page (Q-V3-2) stands.** Baz's way of revealing what he knows may make Silence and Hope question
  whether he, or his old employer, knows about the Veil.
- **Technarc stays unnamed in B01.** Clues may be planted that are understood only in later books: a symbol, part of a
  name.
- **Q-EN2's "they want only the time" is retired.** The watchers get motives a reader can infer.
- **The B01 romance ceiling is raised without a kiss:**
  - touch and brushes of skin;
  - pulling someone out of danger;
  - collisions around corners.

  The heat ladder's B01 pacing gives way to line-level development. The heat lineage's outer limits (no H4, R ceiling)
  are not addressed here and stand.
