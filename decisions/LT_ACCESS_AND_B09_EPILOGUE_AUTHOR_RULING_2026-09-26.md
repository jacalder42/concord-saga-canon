## 1. The §39 amendment

**Applied in this commit.** In `rules/Mechanica-v4.md` §39, *"Is perceivable only by
ascendants"* becomes *"Is accessible to ascendants and, by named exception, to Kade,"* with a
dated note citing this ruling. Nothing else in §39 changes: LT does not override agency, cannot be
used coercively, and *"civilians perceive only calm or clarity."*

**Why the amendment was needed and "widening ascendant" was not an option.** `KadeID.md` lists
*"does not ascend"* among Kade's canon constraints. Reading 3 (widen "ascendant") would have
contradicted a Tier-1 card. The amendment makes Kade an exception to the ascendant rule and
leaves the question of who ascends where it was (ledger §27.2).

**Consequential edits, made so the substrate does not contradict itself.** Three other rule
lines and one Tier-1 card line restated the old §39. Each now points to the amendment and says no more than the ruling:

| File | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Mechanica-v4.md` §33 (channel language) | *"Ascendant-only perception"* | Limited to ascendants and the §39 exception |
| `rules/Channels/CHANNELS_OVERVIEW.md` §5, §6 | *"perceivable only by ascendants"*; *"ascendant-only prismatic channel"* | Ascendants, and Kade by named exception |
| `rules/Channels/LT_RULES_POST_MENDING.md` §2 | ascendants; post-Mending anchors | Kade added by named exception. What "post-Mending anchors" covers was already undefined and stays open |
| `canon/characters/KadeEBCI.md`, LT block *(added in a follow-up commit the same day; the first commit missed it)* | *"Not accessible"*; *"Receives prismatic handshake only in epilogue (no agency)"* | Accessible by named exception. The handshake line is kept; it is the LT reach that closes the epilogue scene. *"(no agency)"* is retained until what access consists of is ruled |

**Not changed:** `Mechanica-v4.md` §37, *"MT cannot … access VT or LT."* Kade's access is
personal. Nothing in the ruling routes it through MT, so §37 does not conflict.

### Constraints the amendment must be read with

These are existing rules. The ruling does not lift them, and scene development has to satisfy
them:

- **`KadeID.md`:** Kade *"does not ascend; cannot replace Tahl; must learn restraint; power is
  human-scale."* LT access must not become ascension by another name or a successor to Tahl's
  role.
- **`LT_RULES_POST_MENDING.md`:** LT *"never presents as a voice, command, or instruction"*; it
  *"cannot … transmit information"*; *"LT is not a guide."* See §3 on the word "prompt."
- **§39:** LT does not override agency and cannot be used coercively.

## 2. The epilogue scene

### What is fixed

- **Characters:** Lacuna and Kade.
- **Action:** they talk while looking at the night sky.
- **Close:** a prompt from LT reaching out ends the scene.
- **Function:** Möbius with the B01 prologue.

### How it sits with the earlier rulings

- **Answer 5 (09-26):** LT is *"the final thing in the epilogue where Seraphine reaches out to
  the survivors."* This scene closes on LT reaching out. **The two fit if the prompt is
  Seraphine's reach, received through Kade's access.** That is the natural reading, but this
  message does not say it, and it is not recorded as ruled.
- **Addendum (09-26):** MT continues under a new MT-initialled name, with a new role for MT and
  for Kade. Kade is *"MT successor"* on his card. After this ruling, **Kade holds both MT's
  continuation and LT access.** How those two roles relate is not specified.
- **The author's analogy** was *"much like Tahl and VT."* Tahl, a mortal, reaches a channel not
  built for him. Kade, a mortal, is given access to LT. `BazID.md` records the truth lineage
  **Baz → Tahl → Kade**; the epilogue gives its last member the channel contact his predecessor
  had. That connection is an observation from the cards, not an addition to the ruling.
- **The scene was already implied by Kade's card.** `KadeEBCI.md` said Kade *"receives
  prismatic handshake only in epilogue."* The ruled scene gives that handshake a setting and a
  witness. The 09-26 ruling adds access; the card's epilogue handshake predates it.
- **Lacuna's own card answers part of "does she perceive it":** `LacunaEBCI.md` gives her LT as
  *"not accessible; post-Mending clarity hum only; no agency."* The ruling does not change that.
  In the scene she would feel the clarity civilians feel, not the reach itself, unless the
  author later extends access past Kade.
- **Lacuna and Kade:** `LacunaID.md` makes her a *"stabilizing force for Concord, especially for
  Kade, during collapse."* `LacunaEBCI.md` records *"romantic potential [that] increases emotional
  charge but does not resolve it."* Whether the epilogue resolves that potential is **not
  ruled**, and this document does not assume it does.

### The Möbius, as a reading

The B01 prologue's beats (v4.1b, `PROTECTED`): Silence and Hope register strain in the Veil;
they perceive pressure *"but cannot simply intervene or dictate mortal outcomes"*; *"wider
perception does not equal control."* Its Möbius seed: *"Silence/Hope begin as distance,
constraint and incomplete communication."*

Read against that, the epilogue can close the loop in at least three ways. **Each is a reading
for scene development, not a ruling:**

1. **Direction.** The prologue is a conversation in the sky, above mortals. The epilogue is a
   conversation under the sky, by mortals looking up.
2. **Communication.** The prologue begins with incomplete communication. The epilogue ends with
   a reach across, which is received.
3. **Constraint.** The prologue's point is that perception does not equal control. LT's rules
   keep that true afterwards: it reaches but does not direct.

The prologue's execution note (*"brief, abstract and beautiful; no cosmology lecture; no
prophecy montage"*) is a plausible standard for its mirror. That is a suggestion.

## 3. Open, and flagged for development

| Item | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **What "a prompt from LT" is** | `LT_RULES_POST_MENDING.md` says LT never presents as a voice, command or instruction and cannot transmit information. A **prompt** read as a **reaching-out felt as presence or invitation** fits those rules. A prompt read as a **message or instruction** does not. The author's word is kept; which sense is meant is open |
| **Whether the prompt arrives through a device** | `LT_RULES_POST_MENDING.md` §6: *"LT never interacts directly with MT."* VT *"never travels digitally."* If the scene shows the prompt on a screen, it conflicts with both. If it is perceived directly, it does not. Open |
| **Other survivors' access** | "At least Kade." Lacuna's card currently says *"not accessible; clarity hum only"* (§2); extending access to her would need a ruling |
| **Timeskip** | Still open (ledger §27.3) |
| **Slot** | Epilogue episode number, and its order relative to the MT-continuation material, are not placed |
