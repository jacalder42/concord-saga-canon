## Two guards added to the book-wide brief (the author's wording, in the private brief)

1. **REACTION / INFORMATION GUARD.** Keep a negation, explanation or retrospective statement when it is the only
   carrier of a character's meaningful reaction, decision, intention, prior choice, uncertainty, refusal, concealment
   or knowledge state, or of a fact the reader cannot reasonably infer.
2. **POV GUARD.** Interiority that the current POV could not know is not preserved for its usefulness. Cut it or flag
   it, and never repair it by inventing evidence.
