# B01: narrative shape, a solved mystery, and what Veil teaches (author direction, 2026-10-14)

Status: CURRENT AUTHOR DIRECTION for the next B01 proposal (approved design direction, not ruled canon). Ledger §545.

It answers no numbered question. It sets the direction the next proposal must follow. It changes no ruling, card, rule or
manuscript file by itself.

## 1. The author's words

> *"I think we need to:*
> *1 remove the repetitive shape of the investigation and interactions*
> *2 give the reader a solved mystery within book 1. Perhaps along the lines of "what is happening and why" with the answer of
> resonance anomalies and the thinning Veil. I imagine that by the end of the Veil trilogy the characters know what resonance is
> and how it works, that sets Neon up to be the emplacement of resonance, and Loom to be the aftermath, etc.*
> *3 we've cut 30k+ words out and pace/momentum is still suffering. So it's not the word count, it's the shape of the narrative
> action. Everything needs modulate its shape, otherwise we'll continue to bleed reader attention."*

The author referred to an attached analysis. It was not received in this session, so it is not recorded here.

## 2. The direction

1. **Shape, not length.** The repetition to remove is in the shape of the investigation and of the interactions, not in the word
   count. Every scene and chapter should vary its shape, meaning how its action moves: what drives it, where it turns, how it
   ends. Cutting words is not the remedy.
2. **A solved mystery inside B01.**
   - The question is *what is happening, and why*.
   - The answer is *resonance anomalies* and *the thinning Veil*.
   - The direction is the Veil **thinning**: getting thinner, and getting worse. The page today says only that it is *thinner
     in some places*.
3. **The trilogy knowledge curve** (the author's sketch, "etc."):
   - by the end of the Veil trilogy, the characters know what resonance is and how it works;
   - Neon becomes *the emplacement of resonance*;
   - Loom is *the aftermath*.
