## Governing rule

**Every title stays open until that book's prose is written.** Nothing on this page is locked. It supersedes nothing, and the per-book `title` fields in `book_context/*.json` remain TODO.

## Trilogy objects

- **Veil (B01–B03):** the trilogy object is **the door**, specifically the screen door. The image is the taped screen at Odile's camp: "It was still holding, but the wire beneath it was broken" (Prologue, Ch 1, Ch 33, Epilogue).
- **"Screen" as a deliberate double meaning, which the author likes:**
  - the screen door;
  - the Veil as a world-wide mesh, from the Prologue's "a net thin as frost and as wide as the world";
  - device screens and the electronic web, which are the Neon trilogy's terrain.

  The double meaning is a rhyme, not an equation. The Veil is not the internet. M09 still stands: mortal devices do not reach the VeilThread. Devices may be affected, and they may mirror the Veil.
