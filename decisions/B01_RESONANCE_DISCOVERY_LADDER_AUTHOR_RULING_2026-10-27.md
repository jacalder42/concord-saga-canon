# B01: resonance is discoverable. The reveal ladder, the forecast engine and Nyla as converter (author ruling, 2026-10-27)

**Status:** current author ruling. Ledger §619.

**Basis:** the writer-thread proposal (2026-10-27) and the editing thread's evaluation, which the author attached as "good additional thoughts and suggestions". The author's words: *"I lean canon 1 yes 2 yes questions 1 yes 2 yes 3 yes."* The refinements in the evaluation are adopted where they sharpen a yes, and each one is marked below.

## 0. The diagnosis
In the author's words: *"all the previous efforts to be coy and vague about resonance started the problems we have been circling around… people aren't allowed to have any insight about what's happening, why it's happening, or what to do about them."* The 2026-10-12 lift (§533) allowed knowing and confirmation, but Mechanica §7.1 ("subtle, low amplitude") and §60 ("characters do not explain") kept pulling the page back to vagueness. This ruling amends both.

## 1. The governing principle
> **The physical behaviour of resonance is discoverable, even when its ultimate metaphysical origin is not.**
> **Book 1 can reveal how resonance weather works without revealing why the Veil exists.**

## 2. Canon amendments (ruled)
1. **Mechanica §7.1 (Veil trilogy):** "subtle, low amplitude" is replaced by **escalating, physically consequential, increasingly measurable.** Institutional suppression stays important, but it cannot prevent public observation. Mechanica §6 still holds: the *public* does not acknowledge metaphysics. People can see effects, and they misname them as weather, pipes, hysteria or ground movement.
2. **Mechanica §60, for the Veil books:** characters may **describe, hypothesise, test, teach and explain** what they have discovered, in their own words. Two limits remain:
   - Mechanica's own vocabulary stays off the page: W-levels, RP, corridor tiers, "shard", "Loom".
   - Nobody may know a reserved truth without evidence (§4 below).
3. **§615 / X1, clarified:** the one gate stays. Nothing confirms that the Veil answers rhythm. That gate does **not** bar showing that coordinated human behaviour reduces harm (seated, together, away from glass, a signal everyone trusts).

## 3. What is actually happening (canon already ruled, now usable as the story's ground truth)
- **The Veil is a hard cap.** It stores blocked pressure, which concentrates at its thinnest points and breaks through (§42A.2). New Orleans is such a point, and the pressure there is rising.
- **Three separate scales,** confirmed against the Mechanica text:
  - **weather** (W0–W4, §30): conditions and event severity;
  - **the shard ladder** (flicker → ghostwave → fracture → rupture, §34): damage to the Veil;
  - **materials** (§21) and **crowds** (§22): how a breakthrough shows locally.

  A severe event does not automatically mean an equally severe fracture.
- **Emotion governs coupling, not origin** (the Ursulines refinement, adopted). The incoming event has its own energy and course, and fear does not create it. People's states change how they couple to it, and crowds multiply whatever state they are in (§22). Love and connection stabilise when they are mutual (Resonance-v1 §7). Stabilisation needs empathy, structure and grounding; force makes it worse (§35).
- **The expression channels are light, sound, geometry and colour** (Resonance-v1 §9). B01's water and pressure effects read as sound and geometry (infrasound-like pressure). **Light is unused in B01 until the Epilogue,** which marks a new category of event.

## 4. Reserved: not discoverable in B01
- what the Veil is (a cap powered by a soul), Mira, Silence and Hope, the cycle, the Loom;
- the breathing-Veil / collective-rhythm answer;
- the four-wound chain and the Mending site.

Characters may form wrong theories about any of these.

## 5. Story rulings
1. **Dominion's forecast is the plot engine (Q1: yes).** Dominion can predict *where and when* a high-severity breakthrough is likely. It has a plan: the observation positions, the cameras, the time window, the count. Our people find evidence of that plan before they understand it ("why are these people positioning themselves around a disaster that hasn't happened yet?"). They reverse-engineer enough to act before the Square.
   - **Refinement, adopted:** Dominion's model is **incomplete.** It predicts the event. It does not know how people survive it, because it treats people as subjects, emitters and counts. The protagonists' indispensable discovery is human: preparation, position, a trusted signal, and finally **mutual connection.** It is shown working, never explained (§2.3).
2. **Ursulines: "warning changes outcomes, unevenly" (Q2: yes, refined).**
   - Some warned people were frightened and fared worse.
   - Some warned people prepared and fared better.
   - Some unwarned people were surprised and came through.

   The finding is not "fear is fuel". It is the question **"what makes a warning useful?"** That gives Seraphine her evolution: delivering information is not the same as helping someone use it.
3. **Nyla converts; she does not emit (Q3: yes, provisional).**
   - She does not generate the incoming resonance. She changes how it shows, **without conscious control.**
   - At the Square that shows as an absence of displacement around her. In the Epilogue it becomes visible as light.
   - Dominion's *Emitter* label is consequentially wrong.
   - **Not frozen:** conversion, grounding and refraction stay open, to be tested against the later saga (sensitivity, filaments, the Loom). There is no directed power in B01.
4. **The Square must be a victory the reader can see.**
   - The page has to make plain what would have happened without them: falls, panic, crush injuries near glass and stone.
   - It has to show their protocol working: seated, away from glass, water indicators, a signal for repeat pulses, Caro's medical team. The crowd doesn't trust the first calm, because the C35 church lunch taught them not to.
   - At least one thing exceeds the forecast and the protocol. Nyla is one; at least one other is needed.
5. **Discovery comes through failure, accident and argument,** not scheduled lessons (Mechanica §59: failure is story and must leave residue).

## 6. Open questions put to the author
- **Wounds and feedback.** Do breakthroughs progressively weaken the places where they happen?
  - Canon *supports* it but does not state it. §42A.2 says "each break becomes a wound"; §31 says weather escalates with "shard proximity"; §34 says a fracture is a "persistent break".
  - **Recommended:** yes. A break leaves a local wound that raises that spot's future severity ("the city remembers where it broke"). This gives B01 its clock: a weakening system nearing a threshold at the Square.
- **The Square's second surprise,** alongside Nyla. Options: the intensity exceeds the forecast; the boundary shifts mid-event; the seated crowd changes the manifestation in a way the instruments can't account for.
