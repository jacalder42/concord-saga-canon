## 3. Interpretations

- **Baz's positional B01 movement and Caro's single step are acceptable.** B02/B03 own Baz's deeper
  movement and his death; Caro's larger function develops later. **Do not manufacture arcs for
  symmetry.** The compression should help both read as people before functions.
- **The event example (E45):** required observable, *localised ground and body instability; Lucien's
  perception conflicts with an objective reference that stays stable*; required consequence, *ordinary
  injuries and separation; Seraphine overextends; Lucien accepts anchoring*; prohibited, *causal
  attribution to the gathering or practice, "it learned", impossible geometry, structural collapse*.
  The rest is prose's.
- **The relationship example (E24):** before, *both express care as control or method*; after, *they can
  disagree, hurt each other somewhat, repair, and continue with better boundaries*. The scene skeleton
  is not the brief's to give.
- **If the compressed sample is substantially more inviting, the EBCI format is essentially solved**,
  and B02 can proceed with much less procedural caution.
