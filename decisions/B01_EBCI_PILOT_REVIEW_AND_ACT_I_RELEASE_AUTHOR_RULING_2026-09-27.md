# B01 EBCI pilot review, and the Act I release — author ruling

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Status:** CURRENT AUTHOR RULING (production). **The E31/E33 pilot passes the novelist test, with
revisions before scaling. After the revisions, the B01 EBCI hold is released for Act I only.**
Approvals of packet choices are approved design (Tier B). No canon ruling, milestone, card or
Mechanica rule text changes.

## 1. The author's verdict (summary, with his words where they carry the ruling)

- **E31 passes; it is the more important test**, and *"it proves EBCI can leave an episode loose enough
  to live."* The best lines are *"almost anti-outline"*: *"They go because they want to"* and *"The night
  succeeds."* **Do not add wonder just because we're tracking it. A good bar night is enough.**
- **E33 passes strongly:** *"the system can constrain causality without dictating prose."* **Baz POV,
  decisively:** *"Baz gives us observation rather than interpretation"*; he is *"one of the people
  actually building the knowledge the saga later inherits."*
- **EBCI itself is not the over-engineering problem.** *"The control system is heavy, but the narrative
  layer can be kept light. If we preserve that separation, EBCI looks like it can do exactly what we
  wanted: protect causality, continuity and long-range obligations while leaving the actual novel to be
  discovered in prose."*

Prose-facing (Sudowrite) packets consume primarily: **story job; want/objective where applicable; turn/change; reader experience/reward; must-preserve / must-not-spend constraints; exit state; loose beats.** Opposition, consequence and unresolved stay optional. **Event episodes additionally receive only the page-safe causal constraints they actually need**, never the whole causal card. **ECID, full event records, breadcrumb administration, tracking, provenance and validation belong to the control layer and are not passed to the prose generator automatically** |
| **R4** | **Do not register Silence and Hope as ordinary cast to satisfy the validator.** Generalise the POV concept to **an authorised POV-capable narrative entity**, so metaphysical entities and approved VT interludes validate without collapsing their ontology into the human cast registry. **The least disruptive implementation** consistent with the existing registries and validators |

## 4. The Act I release, and what follows

- **After R1–R4, the B01 EBCI hold is released for Act I only** (the prologue `S1.T1.B01.PR.E00` and
  E01–E17, with the supplements that fall inside the act). It is **the next stress test**: ordinary
  life, discovery, causal events, relationship development, the prologue's metaphysical material, and
  transitions between episode types.
- **Not released:** A2 and A3 beyond the approved E31/E33 pilot; **B02 and B03**.
- **After Act I, stop for an audit** with two primary questions:
  1. **Did EBCI discover anything that materially changes downstream book or saga architecture?**
  2. **Do the packets still leave enough room for prose discovery rather than prescribing execution?**
- **If Act I passes both, return for authorisation to release the remainder of B01.**
- *"Preserve existing material and provenance; no destructive replacement without direction."*
