## Trilogy Question (RULED §624)
- **Mystery:** *What is breaking in the Southwest, and is it linked to the other breaks?*
- **Dramatic:** *Once the truth is public and exploited, can the people who know it make the world act in time?* (Answer: **no**.)

**Trilogy contract** (ruled §624, `decisions/TRILOGY_CONTRACT_AND_QUESTIONS_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-10-28.md`):
1. Its own question, asked in book 1 and answered in book 3. The answer may be "no".
2. Book 1 is an entry point.
3. Every setup gets a partial payoff inside the trilogy.
4. The discovery ladder never resets.
5. Book 3 closes the lead's arc and the antagonist face. Only the saga's reserved reveals may stay unanswered.

## Book-3 Answers (DESIGN, from the matrix §3, except where marked)
- **Mystery:** the breaks are linked, several, closed in order, the first one last (Tahl's fragment, B06 EP; M39). Santa Fe is real and ignored; the Rupture stays open into Loom as the "no".
- **Dramatic:**
  - No: Tahl dies carrying the warning (M20). The fragment reaches five people.
  - The truth topples an institution but cannot stop the weather: **Nyla's conversion, made public, causes Dominion's wane. Virelli's leadership falls, and the funder faction escapes by co-opting "Concord"** (**RULED** §625/§626).
- **Lead's arc:** Tahl, from remorse to confession to the last clear act to death.
