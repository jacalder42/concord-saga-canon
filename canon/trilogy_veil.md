## Trilogy Question (RULED §624)
- **Mystery:** *What is this weather, how does it behave, and where is it going?*
- **Dramatic:** *Who controls what is known about it, and what does telling the truth cost?*

**Trilogy contract** (ruled §624, `decisions/TRILOGY_CONTRACT_AND_QUESTIONS_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-10-28.md`):
1. Its own question, asked in book 1 and answered in book 3. The answer may be "no".
2. Book 1 is an entry point.
3. Every setup gets a partial payoff inside the trilogy.
4. The discovery ladder never resets.
5. Book 3 closes the lead's arc and the antagonist face. Only the saga's reserved reveals may stay unanswered.

## Book-3 Answers (DESIGN, from `proposals/TRILOGY_RESOLUTION_MATRIX_2026-10-28.md` §3, except where marked)
- **Mystery:**
  - It breaks through at weak places.
  - **Breaches** worsen where it has broken before (§619/§620).
  - It can tear a district permanently: the Warehouse Rupture, M08.
  - It leans southwest.
  - Why the Veil exists stays reserved.
- **Dramatic:**
  - Truth costs Baz his life (M10).
  - The Warehouse is the leak from Dominion's forced conversion of Nyla, which the funder faction runs (**RULED** §625/§626).
  - Tahl's coordinates draw the crowd (M11).
  - Caldas is defeated in B03, but his revenge lands (**RULED** §624).
  - The **reader** learns in B03 that Baz's funder is a Dominion faction; the cast learns in B04 (**RULED** §624).
- **Lead's arc:** Baz believes he left Dominion while still being paid by it, and dies pulling Nyla free.
