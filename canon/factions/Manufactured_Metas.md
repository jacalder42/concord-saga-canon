# MANUFACTURED METAS  
Faction Canon • Engineered Antagonist Class  
Human-Made Resonance Failures • Intent-Absent Altered Humans

---

## FACTION FUNCTION

Manufactured Metas are **people altered to hold resonance they cannot spend, without Intent or emotional integration**.

They are not villains.
They are **evidence**.

Their role in the saga is to demonstrate that:
- resonance without Intent is unstable
- power without empathy collapses
- systems built to bypass humanity fail catastrophically
- human fear, not metaphysics, is the true antagonist

Manufactured Metas exist to prove a negative:
> *You cannot remove Intent from resonance and expect coherence.*

---

## WHAT MANUFACTURED METAS ARE

Manufactured Metas are:
- altered humans: people modified into metas, voluntary or coerced, their names erased
- designed by human institutions under pressure
- created to store, control, isolate, or weaponize resonance
- holding a stored load of energy and emotion, unintegrated, that they cannot spend
- permanently **incapable of Intent**

> **Amended 2026-09-27 by author ruling** (`decisions/LT_KADE_AND_META_ORIGIN_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-27.md` §3): metas are **altered humans only**. Was: the subtitle *"Intent-Absent Constructs"*; *"engineered beings created to simulate or replace resonance without emotion or Intent"*; and *"artificial constructs (biological, cybernetic, or hybrid)"*. The author: *"My memory says they started as people who get modified to become metas"* (2025-11-21).

They may:
- mimic emotional behavior
- approximate resonance output
- display pattern recognition or learning

They can **never**:
- develop Intent
- ascend
- stabilize long-term
- integrate into the Breathable Veil

---

## WHAT MANUFACTURED METAS ARE NOT

They are not:
- a culture
- a belief system
- a population
- a movement
- a metaphysical species

They do not organize.
They do not recruit.
They do not evolve ideologically.

They **fail mechanically**.

---

## STORAGE AND TRANSFER (CANON LOCK)

This is why institutions make them (Mechanica §48).

The model has three parts:
- **The meta's load:** energy and emotion, stored and unintegrated. The meta cannot spend it.
- **The recipient's Emotion and Intent:** handlers draw the load into a person, who spends it.
- **Leakage:** a load that nothing spends discharges uncontrolled.

The draw:
- is **body to body, by physical contact**. A harness positions, restrains, times and monitors; it
  never conducts
- **never works at a distance**. Distance machines only provoke leakage and instability
- is charged by resonance harvested from followers and handlers
- is **unreliable and costly**: to the meta, to the recipient (injury, poor control, at the extreme
  permanent harm or death) and to those nearby

A meta never holds Intent. Handlers' encoded directives are control, not Intent.

---

## ORIGIN CONDITIONS

Manufactured Metas emerge when:

- Dominions suppress hybrid knowledge  
- Technarc seeks predictive control  
- Hardliners attempt resonance sterilization  
- Institutions refuse emotional unpredictability  

They are born from:
- fear of hybrids
- distrust of empathy
- belief that resonance can be engineered like software
- the need for power when resonance ebbs

The people converted are volunteers, the tricked (the weak or poor promised grandeur) and the
coerced.

Their creation is always framed as **necessity**.

---

## CORE DESIGN ERROR (CANON LOCK)

**Resonance requires Intent.**

Intent is not:
- willpower
- intelligence
- obedience
- programming

Intent is:
- emotional alignment + agency + choice

Manufactured Metas lack this permanently.

This is not a bug.
It is the **inevitable outcome** of their design premise.

---

## BEHAVIORAL PROFILE (GENERAL)

Manufactured Metas typically exhibit:
- a high stored load; what shows as output is leakage
- unstable pattern feedback
- emotional mimicry without grounding
- escalating failure under pressure
- unpredictable collapse behaviors

They often appear:
- eerily calm
- mechanically precise
- emotionally “off”
- increasingly erratic over time

The more power they display, the closer they are to failure.

## THEMATIC FUNCTION

Manufactured Metas embody:
- intelligence without wisdom
- power taken from people
- control without consent
- fear mistaken for innovation

They force the saga to answer:
> *If resonance requires being human, what happens when we try to remove humanity from the equation?*

Institutions tried. The people were still there.

---

## CANON LOCKS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

- Entirely human-made
- No supernatural origin
- No Intent, ever
- No ascension
- No return to Intent or to what they were; their personhood is never in question
- No stable long-term existence

Individual Metas may evoke sympathy.  
The system that created them is the antagonist.

---

## USAGE GUIDELINES

Use Manufactured Metas when:
- you need visible proof of institutional failure
- resonance power must feel wrong or brittle
- antagonism should be tragic, not malicious
- consequences of fear-driven design must be undeniable

Do **not**:
- give them ideology
- give them POV
- frame them as the “real villains”

**Do** allow:
- evidence of former identity: a name recovered, a keepsake
- relationships: someone looking for them
- how they were recruited or coerced

They are the warning, not the cause.
