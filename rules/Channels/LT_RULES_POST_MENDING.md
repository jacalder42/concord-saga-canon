# LT — LUMINOUS THREAD RULES (POST-MENDING)
Status: Authoritative Canon
Source: Project Memory + Mechanica v4 + Resonance v1
Phase: Phase 1A Migration
Applies To: Post-Mending World Only

---

## 1. WHAT LT IS

LT (Luminous Thread) is the **post-Mending metaphysical channel** formed when the Veil becomes breathable.

LT represents:
- filtered resonance flow
- stabilized boundary ecology
- gentle connective awareness

LT is not an upgrade to VT.
LT is a different channel entirely.

---

## 2. ACCESS & PERCEPTION

LT is perceivable only by:
- ascendants
- post-Mending anchors
- Kade, by named exception (Mechanica §39, amended 2026-09-26 by author ruling —
  `decisions/LT_ACCESS_AND_B09_EPILOGUE_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-26.md`). Whether other
  protagonist survivors have access, and what "post-Mending anchors" covers, is open.

Civilians do not perceive LT directly.

Civilians experience LT indirectly as:
- calmer emotional environments
- reduced catastrophic escalation
- clearer emotional ecology

LT never presents as a voice, command, or instruction.

---

## 3. WHAT LT CAN DO

LT can:
- provide gentle alignment cues
- support emotional regulation
- enable quiet recognition between ascendants
- reflect global stabilization

LT operates through:
- filtration
- presence
- resonance smoothing

LT does not provide answers.
LT does not solve problems.

---

## 4. WHAT LT CANNOT DO

LT cannot:
- coerce behavior
- override agency
- transmit information
- function as surveillance
- act independently of humans

LT is not a system.
LT is not a guide.
LT is not an authority.

---

## 5. LT & LANGUAGE CONSTRAINTS

LT scenes must use:
- soft, prismatic imagery
- gentle sensory language
- minimal metaphor layering

LT scenes must avoid:
- declarations
- prophecy
- omniscient tone
- system voice phrasing

LT is felt as clarity, not instruction.

---

## 6. LT & TECHNOLOGY

LT never interacts directly with MT.

MT may appear:
- quieter
- more stable
- less intrusive

This is a byproduct of environmental filtration,
not LT influence on technology.

**Named exception (author ruling 2026-09-26,
`decisions/B06_B08_B09_EPILOGUE_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-26.md` §3.1):** the Book 9 epilogue
LT handshake reaches Kade's device as a message request. **Tahl's echo enables it**, acting as
the conduit on Kade's side. The exception belongs to the echo. It does not let LT ride MT
hardware in general, and the rule above stands for every other case. The author: *"the LT rule
was generalized. The intent is that Tahl's echo/soul is what enables the handshake to occur."*

---

## 7. FAILURE & MISUSE

LT failure does not look like collapse.

Misuse appears as:
- misinterpretation
- projection
- emotional overreach

LT does not punish misuse.
Consequences remain human.

---

## 8. RELATIONSHIP TO VT

VT and LT coexist but do not merge.

- VT = boundary contact
- LT = filtered connective ecology

VT remains sharper.
LT remains softer.

Neither replaces the other.

---

## 9. POST-MENDING SAFEGUARDS

LT enforces:
- no new shards
- no rupture events
- no catastrophic escalation

LT does not prevent:
- conflict
- grief
- human error

The world remains imperfect.

---

## 10. CANON GUARDRAILS

If LT is depicted as:
- directive
- omnipotent
- corrective
- narratively convenient

The scene violates canon.
