# SUPPLEMENTS — PERMISSION RULES
Status: Authoritative Canon
Source: Project Memory + Mechanica v4 + Resonance v1
Phase: Phase 1A Migration
Applies To: Veil, Neon, Loom, Post-Mending

---

## 1. PURPOSE

This document defines when **supplementary material** is permitted and how it must respect channel boundaries.

Supplements exist to:
- deepen emotional context
- clarify off-page consequences
- provide reflective or aftermath perspective

Supplements do not:
- ~~advance plot~~ — **superseded 2026-09-18, see §1.1**
- introduce new mechanics
- override on-page outcomes

### 1.1 The "do not advance plot" clause is superseded

`recovery/CANON_DECISIONS_2026-09-18.md` §3.5 supersedes the `advance plot` line
above. The remaining two prohibitions stand unchanged, as does everything in §2–§4.

**What changed.** The `LINK` supplement function (decisions §3.2) exists precisely to
convey off-page events — Chronicle leaks, MT civic updates, route changes, hazard
warnings. That is plot linkage, which the original clause forbade outright.

**Why.** The clause and the function cannot both hold. The recovered Supplement Text
Architecture Bible defines `LINK` as one of four emotional functions in the reader
cycle, and the worked example in decisions §3.3 — a Chronicle leak about political
suppression, vehicle `CHRON`, function `LINK`, type `POL` — is a supplement whose
entire purpose is carrying plot the narrative did not show.

**What replaces it.** A supplement may carry plot linkage under the `LINK` function.
It still may not:

- replace narrative, break POV, or spoil future beats
- deliver metaphysical exposition directly
- feel like bonus content
- introduce new mechanics or override on-page outcomes (unchanged, above)
- appear in the §4 absolute-exception windows (unchanged, below)

So the boundary moved from *"supplements never carry plot"* to *"supplements may carry
plot that happened off-page, and never plot that belongs on-page."*

**Constraints from the same bible**, recorded in
`canon/supplements/SUPPLEMENT_VEHICLES.md` and machine-readable in
`rules/canon_rules.json` under `supplement_system.constraints`: supplements run
150–600 words, sit between episodes, and `VT` Glimpses are capped at 10–12 across all
nine books.

## 2. WHAT COUNTS AS A SUPPLEMENT

A supplement may include:
- letters
- recorded messages
- journals
- memory fragments
- reflective vignettes
- aftermath scenes

Supplements are:
- optional
- secondary
- emotionally grounded

They are not primary narrative engines.

---

## 3. GENERAL PERMISSION RULE

Supplements are allowed:
- after major events
- after character deaths
- after arc resolutions

As long as they:
- do not contradict canon
- respect channel separation
- do not alter outcomes

---

## 4. ABSOLUTE EXCEPTIONS (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Supplements are **not permitted** after:

1. **Tahl’s death** (Neon Book 6)
2. **Ascension deaths** (Book 9, Act III)

These moments must remain:
- singular
- final
- unsoftened

Any supplement in these windows violates canon.

---

## 5. CHANNEL CONSTRAINTS WITHIN SUPPLEMENTS

### MT Supplements
- May include recordings, messages, archives
- Must obey MT failure and degradation rules
- Cannot carry VT or LT content

---

### VT Supplements
- Must remain intimate and bounded
- No broadcast framing
- No system voice or prophecy

---

### LT Supplements (Post-Mending Only)
- Ascendant perception only
- Gentle, non-directive
- No instruction or revelation

---

## 6. WHAT SUPPLEMENTS CANNOT DO

Supplements cannot:
- resurrect characters
- revise causality
- introduce new metaphysical rules
- bypass cost or consequence
- explain the system

If a supplement explains mechanics, it is invalid.

---

## 7. TONE & PLACEMENT GUIDANCE

Supplements should:
- be quieter than primary scenes
- focus on emotional residue
- deepen meaning, not clarify plot

Placement should:
- follow emotional peaks
- never interrupt escalation
- never reduce stakes retroactively

---

## 8. AUTHORIAL DISCRETION CLAUSE

There are no absolute prohibitions beyond Section 4.

Any supplement is valid **if narratively justified** and if it:
- respects channel boundaries
- preserves finality
- does not undermine consequence

This clause allows flexibility without breaking canon.
