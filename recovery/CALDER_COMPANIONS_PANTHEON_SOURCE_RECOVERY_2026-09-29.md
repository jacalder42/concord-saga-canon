## 0. Summary

- **What it is.** A six-guide "Calder Pantheon" (the author named it *"Calder's Companions"*), built on
  **2025-11-20** in the **Gladiatrix** chat (not the Concord project) as part of the author's cross-project Calder
  OS: Chaos Squirrel, Shadow Crow, Insight Owl, Boundary Bear, Cunning Fox, Heart Deer, each with a light and a shadow
  mode; a six-mode **Character Map** (Primary, Secondary, Stress, Shadow Collapse, Growth, Integrated); a **Temporal
  Map**; and a ten-section **Relationship Dynamics Map** whose §7 *Conflict Loops* run Trigger → Reaction → Escalation
  → Break point → Consequences.
- **Whose words.** The seed ideas are the author's (the Anti-Calder as a lens; the squirrel; asking for guides that
  capture *"personalities very dissimilar from ours"*; the name *Calder's Companions*; the integration moment as
  *"perfect"*). **Almost all defining text is ChatGPT's (gpt-5-1)**, carried forward by *"Proceed"*. A **compressed,
  altered** version (1.17–1.20) was pasted by the author into his Notion *Calder OS Manual* on 2025-11-21: adopted
  assistant text. **No author ruling ever touched it. Everything is Tier D.**
- **Character mappings.** Only **Gladiatrix** characters (Amazon, Achillia; another project). **No Concord character was
  ever given a guide.** Concord's Notion page *00.07 Pantheon* reuses the name for a list of Concord characters'
  "frequencies": no animals, modes, shadows or loops.
- **Where it dropped out.** In three steps: (1) the Notion export of 11-21 kept the guides but dropped the shadow names,
  the six-mode map's detail, the rarity rule and every conflict loop, and added *"Conflict resolves"*; (2) the
  Concord handoff of 11-21 loaded it *"for emotional and tonal calibration only"*, and the Concord blueprint (11-23)
  replaced it with the character "frequencies" page; (3) on 11-30 the author asked for *"author voice and pantheon as a
  base layer"*; the reply built voice cards and never used the guides. It is **absent from the repository** outside
  `sources/`, and the 09-27 recovery register quotes the 11-30 line without tracing what *pantheon* referred to.
- **What reaches a drafter now:** nothing of it. What does reach the drafter is a dialogue and empathy rule taken from
  the Compendium's description of **Calder's own** connection pattern, applied to *"People"* in general (§6).

### 1.5 The Relationship Dynamics Map (assistant, Step F.3; sections 1–5 l.64586–64876, 11-20 ~15:13; 6–10 l.65030–65291, 11-20 15:17)

| Pair | Loop |
| --- | --- |

| Bear ↔ Crow | *"Control + paralysis → frozen relationship."* |

| Fox ↔ Crow | *"Scheming + suspicion → paranoia spiral."* |
| Bear ↔ Deer | *"Smothering + collapse → codependent loop."* |

*"Shadow entanglements create tragic romance, villain/hero parallels, toxic friendships, and misunderstandings."*

**§3 Growth reciprocity** (l.64769–64792): Squirrel↔Crow (move / think), Bear↔Deer (stand / soften), Owl↔Fox (see truth
/ take action), Squirrel↔Deer (bravery / gentleness), Bear↔Owl (steadies / guides restraint), Fox↔Crow (nuance /

**§4 Axis oppositions** (l.64796–64822): Squirrel↔Crow (*"the foundational dramatic axis"*), Fox↔Owl (*"tension, mind
games, political intrigue"*), Bear↔Deer (*"romance, guardianship, emotional arcs"*). *"Every story lives somewhere on
this triangle."*

**§5 Narrative tension dynamics** (l.64826–64852): Complementary, Mirror, Opposition, Compression (Bear, Crow),
Expansion (Deer, Squirrel), Subversion (Fox, Owl).

**§6 Rapport cycles** (l.65036–65067): Squirrel→Deer *"Play → Softening → Trust → Intimacy"*; Bear→Owl *"Safety → Truth →
Respect → Loyalty"*; Fox→Crow *"Understanding → Nuance → Alliance → Mutual Respect"*; Owl→Deer *"Clarity → Vulnerability
→ Connection → Peace"*; Fox→Squirrel *"Chaos → Strategy → Mischief → Joy"*; Bear→Deer *"Protection → Softness →
Belonging → Devotion"*.

**§7 Conflict loops** — *"how two archetypes annoy, threaten, frustrate, or wound each other in patterned ways … 1.

## 6. Relevance to B01: findings about instructions (not about the manuscript)

The questions: few interpersonal conflicts, integration as the default, disagreements dissolved by understanding.

1. **The recovered system had explicit machinery for patterned, recurring interpersonal friction; none of it is in the
   drafting instructions.** The Conflict Loops end in a *consequence*, not a repair (*"Strange distance; frustrated
   affection"*, *"Coldness; strategic withdrawal"*, *"Emotional ache; misalignment"*, *"Broken alliance"*, *"Quiet
   heartbreak"*). Repair is a **separate** section (§10). Break states are *"essential for building: mid-book tension,
   arguments with payoff … cliffhangers"*.
2. **The system made integration rare and defined everyday behaviour as partial and pressured.** Its rules are *"modes,
   not traits"*, stress shifts to another guide (*"Owl → Crow under stress → paralyzing doubt"*, *"Deer → Squirrel …
   frantic appeasing"*), and integration is to be used *"sparingly"* (*"You do not write this often"*). The drafter's
   instructions have no default-versus-rare distinction for emotional competence.
3. **The drafter's dialogue and empathy rules come from the author persona's own connection pattern, applied to
   everyone.** The Compendium describes how *Calder* connects (*"Mirroring … Curiosity … Anecdote"*) and how *Calder's*
   empathy works (*"it solves, doesn't soothe"*). Profile §4–§5 give these to *"People"* and to *"Empathy"* in general.
   The Pantheon was requested for the opposite purpose, in the author's words to *"capture the perspective of
   personalities very dissimilar from ours"*. Of the Compendium's conflict section, only *"redesign the argument"*
   reached the profile. *"Each party has legitimate goals but incompatible methods"* and *"True villains are the ones who
   refuse revision"* did not.
4. **The adopted Notion version leans toward resolution.** 1.17 lists *"Conflict resolves"* under Voltron mode; 1.19 has
   *"Resolution = momentum alignment"* and uses the Crow to cut melodrama. Restoring 1.17 alone would restore that bias,
   not the conflict loops.
5. **Packet-level scoping.** The packets that name conflict mostly scope it to resolve inside the episode:
   - E24: *"They hurt each other somewhat, repair it, and go on, on better terms"*; *"Ends. Working together again,
     differently."*
   - E19: *"an argument … They enjoy it."*
   - E29: *"Lucien … comes round."*
   - E42: *"A tiny conflict resolves."*

   E39 is the exception: *"still open between them"*, and *"Anyone settling the disagreement for them"* is kept off the
   page. README Q-AR7/Q-AR9 permit disagreement (*"disagree about what things mean"*; *"most interesting where the
   methods disagree"*). None of this gives a character a stress or shadow pattern, or a loop that carries across
   episodes.
6. **The cards stress regulation.** Seraphine *"naturally de-escalates conflict"* and her Identity Loop is *"Others
   escalate → Seraphine regulates → calm returns"*. Caro is *"Kinetic Regulator"*; Baz *"low-drama … connects through:
   listening"*; Elisabet lowers noise; Lucien stabilises *"by reducing amplitude"*. Their *Failure modes* and *Stress
   behaviors* (for example Lucien *"withdrawal mistaken for care"*, Elisabet *"may choose correctness over humanity"*)
   do not reach the drafter.
7. **Other conflict systems exist, and are held as Tier D.** The Conflict Engine and the bentos were audited 09-23 and
   kept out of EBCI; *1.7 Relational Physics* (with *Rupture* and *Suspension* end states) was never recovered.
