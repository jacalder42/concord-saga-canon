## 1. Reading this inventory

An **anchor** is a recoverable narrative change that may need a Resonance event. An older name for its effect is **not** proof that the named physics is still valid. `CURRENT` means supported by a present author ruling or working architecture; `RECOVERED` means attested historical development but not promoted; `PROPOSAL` is a suggested position or mechanism; `SUPERSEDED` is retained only to document a conflict; `OPEN` means the evidence or causal link is insufficient. A row can carry multiple labels because the story event and its historical explanation have different authority.

**Evidence priority:** current explicit author rulings and current integrated Book 1 constraints → later reconciled recovery with evidence → full author export and historical Notion → older nine-book synthesis → proposed milestone grid. A file's self-description as “authoritative” does not override later author rulings. “First,” “global,” “permanent,” and “only” require special checking against sources and the later architecture.

## 5. Collision register

1. **B1 micro-Shard / prismatic M01/M03:** older grid language conflicts with low-amplitude B1 limits. Preserve the story function of early observable disturbance, not the obsolete literal category.
2. **B2 “global” reports versus B5 “first global” Colorstorm:** define local event count, cross-city reporting, synchronized propagation and actual planetary weather separately. A worldwide communication graph is not automatically one worldwide Resonance event.
3. **B3 first Rupture and “permanent scar”:** compelling proposal in the grid; verify against the primary B3 packet and later rules before calling it durable.
4. **Baz death:** B3 current author ruling overrides B4 placement in older syntheses. Physical cause and Tahl attribution still require a source-backed incident chain.
5. **Pre-Mending Echo Node:** M29 conflicts with Mechanica's post-Mending language; hold the name and state classification pending reconciliation.
6. **Meta battery versus operative boost:** storage is recovered; transfer into personnel remains unverified. Neither a boost event nor suppression of its cost can be inferred.
7. **Technology:** classify interference by scale and actual symptom: noise, distortion, reduced fidelity, intermittent behavior, damage, then possible failure. Avoid converting every Resonance event into failed equipment.
8. **Finale target:** later ruling and repeated endgame evidence have Kade attack Rex. The old Kade-attacks-Elias summary is superseded.
9. **Channels:** MT (MissingThread), VT (VeilThread), and LT (LuminousThread after Mending) are identities and boundaries, not interchangeable names for an energy conduit. The grid's “rename” shorthand must not imply that the Mending runs through LT.
10. **Axes:** do not translate an event's milestone/reader pressure into UARS load, shard stage, weather W, corridor class, FX intensity or conflict tier. Each needs independent evidence.
