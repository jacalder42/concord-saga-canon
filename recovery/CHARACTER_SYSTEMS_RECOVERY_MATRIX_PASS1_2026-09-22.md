## System-level recovered laws

### Core equation strata
Recovered early/Layer-1:
**Resonance = Energy × Emotion × Intent**

Recovered UARS long-form:
**Resonance Output (RO) = Energy × Emotion × Intent × Environmental Pressure Modifiers**

Recovered boost expression:
**Boost = Native Skill × (Emotion Alignment + Intent Focus)**

Definitions:
- Energy = biological + psychological stamina/capacity.
- Emotion = amplitude + coherence, not merely intensity.
- Intent = conscious direction/vector; older sources also add moral direction/alignment.
- Environmental Pressure = Veil density/resistance, shard stress, crowd emotion, location instability, etc.
- Native Skill/Baseline Talent constrains what RO can actually accomplish.

Hard physics:
- No new capabilities from Resonance.
- No telekinesis, flight, invisibility, healing, energy blasts, bullet-curving, superhuman durability.
- Human biomechanics remain binding.
- Resonance primarily improves timing, precision, perception, balance, leverage, composure, endurance, coordination, and social/emotional regulation.
- Firearms remain lethal.
- Tech may detect/interfere/be disrupted, but cannot become a magical Resonance wielder.

### Recovered action sequence
Historical combat/action bento:
**Trigger → Intent Lock → Capability Filter → Environmental Pressure → Cost → Story Outcome**

This is provisionally superior to any prose pattern where emotion directly creates an effect.

### Classification for recovered ability claims
- **KEEP** — mechanically compatible as written or with trivial wording cleanup.
- **TRANSLATE** — preserve function, rewrite effect into grounded UARS physics.
- **RETIRE** — violates hard-system promise / super-feelings drift.
- **VT-ONLY** — metaphysical exception, not normal Resonance.
- **REVIEW** — potentially valid but needs cross-system ruling.

# Cross-Character Mechanical Conclusions

## 1. Preserve asymmetry
Characters should not all have a “Resonance attack.”

Their advantages should arise from different human baselines:
- Seraphine — balance, breath, relational regulation.
- Lucien — structure, restraint, close-range control.
- Caro — emergency action, explosive close-range protection.
- Elisabet — prediction through pattern/timing.
- Baz — witness/documentation; not fighter.
- Tahl — investigation/communication; no mortal output.
- Rex — engineering, weapons, tools, logistics.
- Kade — athletic volatility + environmental receptivity.
- Lacuna — music/ritual/social synchronization; not sonic magic.

## 2. Resonance should modify verbs, not invent nouns
Good:
- steadies;
- times;
- notices;
- redirects;
- braces;
- endures;
- coordinates;
- resists;
- recovers.

Dangerous:
- blasts;
- compels;
- absorbs;
- projects truth;
- creates gravity;
- forces confession;
- generates lightning;
- magically tunes people.

## 3. Baseline provenance is mandatory
Before any combat skill is promoted, answer:
**Where did this person learn it? How long? How often do they practice?**

Immediate provenance gaps:
- Lucien martial training;
- Caro Muay Thai/close-quarters training;
- Elisabet Aikido/defensive training;
- Rex weapons proficiency.

Kade's lack of formal training is already part of the point.
Baz/Tahl should remain noncombatants.

## 4. Gear must remain a separate axis
Do not conflate:
- Character skill;
- Resonance modification;
- Technology;
- Faction equipment.

Next recovery should inventory:
- personal gear by character;
- Dominion field gear;
- Technarc sensors/weapons/drones;
- Filament survival/comms tools;
- Tahl's equipment;
- ordinary analog fallbacks;
- what tech can measure vs merely glitch around.

## 5. VFX is downstream
Do not restore character colors/auras until the physical effect is defined.

Required future order:
**mechanical event → carrier/medium → observable artifact → optional symbolic VFX association.**

Never:
**emotion → signature color → inferred power.**
