## 1. Short answer

**Partly, and only the world-level half.**

- **Ingested.** The world grammar was saved to ChatGPT project memory as *Symbol & Motif System (Memory Edition v2)*, on the author's *"Save it"* (Dec 5–6, 2025). ChatGPT later inflated it, with the *Symbolic Action Engine*, into the five `rules/symbols/` files (2026-01-05, Phase 1A). That covers the four channels, colour, geometry, light and sound, era drift and VFX ceilings. It is a sensory and VFX grammar, not a literary motif bible.
- **Not ingested.** Most of the development: the Dec 5 per-character motif sets (two versions), the POV metaphor domains, the city and environment dialects and the Bible v2 prose integration layer; the Nov 10 personal-glyph system, which grew from the author's own ideas; the Nov 1 literary motif layer; the Nov 30 human-made tech symbology; and the "Symbolic Motifs" lines in the Tier-1 backstory modules the author pasted on Dec 10.
- **The author's own symbols that are captured:** Tahl's triangle (`grids/breadcrumbs.csv`, `BC-TAHL-SIGNATURE-TRIANGLE`) and the stars / night sky (Möbius design and the B09 epilogue rulings).
- **Not in the drafting stack.** No motif material reaches the writer profile, `ebci/prose/README.md` or any B01 packet. The motifs on B01's page grew from the story (notebook, glass, pencil, still water), not from the bible.

## 3. What the repository already holds

- **`rules/symbols/`** (~3,000 words, "Authoritative Canon"). It is the Memory Edition's world grammar plus the action engine's ceilings. It has:
  - the four channels, *"No additional symbolic channels are permitted"*, and 1–2 channels per beat;
  - colour as diagnostic (Green = Grounding);
  - geometry families, light and sound motifs, era drift and VFX ceilings.

  Its own TODOs ask for city palettes and a colour lookup. It contains **no character, place, POV or prose material**. The Memory Edition's environment motifs (humidity, water, stone, glass/metal, crowds), weather/corridor iconography and §VI prose layer did not survive the inflation.
- **`rules/Emotion/`** cites the symbol system but adds no motifs.
- **`canon/pov/`**: approved and forbidden **metaphor domains** for the four antagonists. For example, Virelli has *pruning / cultivation* and *thresholds, gates*; Saeko has *quiet / stillness*. The protagonists' cards describe how they think, not their metaphor domains.
- **`canon/factions/filaments/Filaments-Aesthetics.md`**: emergent, "never talismanic" motifs.
- **Render and Appearance cards forbid symbolism on bodies**, for example LacunaRender's *"No symbols; no glyphs"* and HanWeiRender's *"No symbolic props or metaphors"*. Seraphine's prismatic accents are **post-Mending only**, which fits the author's Loom-reveal note.
- **Tahl's triangle**: `BC-TAHL-SIGNATURE-TRIANGLE` (approved design; planted at B02 E07, paid through B03, B04–B06, M53). **Stars / night sky**: `proposals/MACRO_MOBIUS_PROLOGUE_EPILOGUE_DESIGN_2026-09-27.md` and the B09 epilogue rulings.
- **`grids/episode_beats.csv` `motif_1`/`motif_2`**: 35 of 373 rows filled, all emergent objects (*the case*, *the page*, *chalk*, *the waltz*). The ledger already notes that no vocabulary binds them to `rules/symbols/`.
- **Prior audits.** `reports/VFX_SENSORY_GRAMMAR_INTEGRITY_AUDIT_2026-09-23.md` and `proposals/VFX_SENSORY_GRAMMAR_RECONCILIATION_FOR_REVIEW_2026-09-23.md` (V2: *colour is a contextual motif, not a universal detector*) surveyed the Notion VFX pages, not the Dec 5 chat. ## 6. Questions for the author

**Q-SY1. Recover the missing material into a proposal?**
*Recommended: yes, as one Tier D proposal, not into `rules/`.* It would hold the two character-motif versions side by side, the protagonist metaphor domains, the backstory object motifs, and the city and environment dialects, with K1–K8 marked. It is evidence for later card work, not a ruleset.

**Q-SY2. Should a compact prose-facing motif sheet join the drafting stack?**
*Recommended: no standing sheet.* Use the README's existing route: at a character's first substantial POV appearance, **one card line** on how they notice may be promoted. Beyond that, a motif enters a prose packet only as a concrete object or image in that episode's Narrative Brief, where the episode needs it (Tahl's triangle at B02 E07 is the model).

**Q-SY3. How does any motif material relate to *"never demonstrate the voice guide"*?**
*Recommended: the profile rule governs.* Motif material sits in the control layer, consulted when packets are derived. It is never sent whole to the drafting model. Motifs are permissions, not quotas. The watch-list's refrain test applies to them as it does to the notebook and the pencil.

**Q-SY4. Colour in prose.**
*Recommended:* adopt the 09-23 proposal's V2 for prose: colour is a contextual observation, never a reading of emotion or truth. Leave `rules/symbols/COLOR_SEMANTICS.md` unchanged until the Mechanica line-by-line review.

**Q-SY5. The personal glyphs (your Nov 10 ideas).**
*Recommended: retire them for bodies, and record them as Tier D provenance.* The Render canon bans them, and Veil's restraint already holds. Keep **Tahl's triangle as the saga's one personal sign**; its uniqueness strengthens the breadcrumb. If you want glyphs for Neon or Loom, that is a new design question.

**Q-SY6. Lucien's eyes conveying intent (your Nov 1 words).**
*Recommended: yes,* as one physical, non-glowing identity-context line for his POV. The garnet/silver-pin version belongs to the retired vampire model and stays out.

**Q-SY7. `motif_1`/`motif_2` in `grids/episode_beats.csv`.**
