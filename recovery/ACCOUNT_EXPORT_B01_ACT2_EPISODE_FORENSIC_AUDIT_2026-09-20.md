## Executive finding

Book 1 Act II is also **highly salvageable**.

Its durable causal spine is:

**Baz arrives → trio synchronizes knowledge → neighborhood spike → Filament warning → institutions notice → Seraphine/Lucien fracture & repair → pattern mapping → next-pulse warning → active pulse hunt → Marigny failure → compression stillness → pulse strike → grounding aftermath → Filament interpretation → citywide behavioral consequences.**

As with Act I, the main problems are not story architecture. They are:

1. old literalized resonance/VFX language;
2. overpopulation of Filament utility roles;
3. institutional shorthand that needs current Dominion/Technarc differentiation;
4. repeated “Filament council” exposition that may be compressible;
5. insufficient lived-in NOLA social infrastructure between pressure events.

There are **no episodes that require wholesale deletion on current evidence**, but several should be merged/tuned during beat welding.

# I. E16 — “Baz Arrives in NOLA”

**Disposition: KEEP.**

### Preserve
- Baz POV entrance;
- shift from reacting to investigating;
- Baz reads people/civic behavior, not metaphysics;
- concern for Lucien;
- Jackson Square aftermath as ordinary-person “this place feels wrong” memory;
- final “tell me everything” pivot.

### Strong narrative value
This is Baz's first active on-page entrance and should feel **human-scale**.

### Reconcile
- Baz “feeling the square's memory” must remain ordinary embodied unease unless Mechanica supports more;
- indigo tint / stable three-beat hum = cinematic motif, not literal sensory power.

### Place/culture opportunity
The ride into NOLA should carry:
- humidity;
- street life;
- neighborhood specificity;
- ordinary local commentary;
without making the city only an anomaly map.

# IV. E19 — “The Filament Circle: The City Is Breathing Wrong”

**Disposition: KEEP FUNCTION / CAST CONSOLIDATION REQUIRED.**

The episode's **function is excellent**:
- local care network has independent observations;
- trio learns the instability has ecological/social rhythm;
- “city is breathing wrong” becomes a memorable human metaphor;
- pulse intervals are shortening.

### Problem: old Filament role inflation
The scene uses:
- Rootkeeper;
- Sparkbroker;
- Witness;
as quasi-elder archetypes with distinct exposition functions.

This conflicts with the current supporting-cast goal:
**recognizable recurring humans, not a named utility specialist for each explanatory beat.**

### Current recommendation
Do not lock Sparkbroker/Witness as separate recurring characters yet.

Potential function routing to test later:
- **Rootkeeper** — local/place-bound elder texture only;
- **Mara/M** — recurring community gravity / practical care;
- **Samir “Pivot” Choksi** — probabilistic/logistical interpretation if source supports it;
- **Arianna / another protected Filament** — witness/emotional-history function if source supports it.

### Important
Do **not execute merges yet** until the deeper Filament-source pass confirms whether Sparkbroker/Witness have trajectories elsewhere.

### Mechanics
Basin water reacting, ember flicks, green stability, indigo waves = likely cinematic ritual language, not literal powers.
