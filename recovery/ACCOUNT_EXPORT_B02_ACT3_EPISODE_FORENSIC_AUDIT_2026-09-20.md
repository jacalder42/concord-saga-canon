## Executive finding

Book 2 Act III is the **most compressed-system-heavy material recovered so far**.

The narrative spine is sound:

**new citywide baseline → people begin anticipating events → Seraphine overload worsens →
Lucien's third major misalignment → Filaments withdraw/reorganize → global cluster →
ordinary institutions fail → trio discovers directional drift → drift moves through NOLA →
people begin moving with it → city-scale threshold → aftermath → global confirmation →
Book 3 handoff.**

But the old implementation repeatedly literalizes the city as a single organism and
ordinary people as involuntary synchronized components.

That is the major repair task.

The act should preserve:
- anticipation;
- crowd-flow anomalies;
- social contagion;
- infrastructure stress;
- directional propagation;
- global simultaneity;
without making civilians behave like puppets or NOLA itself a conscious magical entity.

There is still **no meaningful named-Tahl contamination** in this B2 Act III run.

No episode is narratively worthless, but several are likely compression candidates.

---

# I. E01 — “The New Baseline”

**Disposition: KEEP.**

Preserve:
- the city has adapted to repeated instability;
- ordinary people continue routines;
- protagonists recognize that “normal” has moved;
- minor anomalies no longer stop everyone;
- fatigue replaces novelty.

This is an excellent act opener because escalation is measured through **habituation**.

Social-world opportunity:
this episode should strongly feature ordinary recurring places/faces rather than generic
crowds.

Velvet Vein is a natural candidate for showing people joking, arguing, adapting and
misunderstanding the new normal.

---

# II. E02 — “People Feel It Coming”

**Disposition: KEEP FUNCTION / HUMAN-AGENCY REBUILD.**

Preserve:
- ordinary people begin anticipating instability before official warnings;
- behavior shifts seconds/minutes before events;
- protagonists realize sensitivity is spreading socially.

Old version risks:
- crowds stopping together;
- strangers looking in same direction;
- synchronized breathing/motion.

Current implementation should favor:
- people leaving early without knowing why;
- conversations trailing off;
- service workers recognizing a “bad minute”;
- local folk heuristics;
- uneven, probabilistic anticipation.

This is more human and more interesting than hive behavior.

---

# III. E03 — “Seraphine Overload #3”

**Disposition: KEEP / MAJOR CHARACTER ANCHOR.**

Preserve:
- Seraphine cannot filter the accumulated human pressure;
- competence fails;
- she needs active help;
- overload is consequence of care, not proof of special destiny;
- recovery is incomplete.

Critical guardrail:
do not frame Seraphine as literally absorbing an entire city's emotions unless current
Mechanica supports that scale.

Execution grammar:
presence/warmth becomes liability when she cannot maintain boundaries.

Relationship opportunity:
support should come through established relationships rather than anonymous Filament
healers.

---

# IV. E04 — “Lucien Misalignment #3”

**Disposition: KEEP / MAJOR CHARACTER ANCHOR.**

Preserve:
- structural perception becomes unreliable;
- Lucien cannot tell which alignment is trustworthy;
- control strategy fails;
- someone else must help him choose action despite incomplete certainty.

This should parallel E03 without duplicating it:
Seraphine is overwhelmed by too much human signal;
Lucien is overwhelmed by too many possible structures.

That symmetry is strong.

Reconcile:
duplicated streets / impossible angles / literal geometry splits through current Mechanica.

---

# V. E05 — “The Filaments Pull Back”

**Disposition: KEEP / REBUILD ORGANIZATIONAL LOGIC.**

Preserve:
- local care network recognizes limits;
- some practices are suspended;
- resources shift toward ordinary shelter/care;
- protagonists lose an informal support layer;
- withdrawal is responsible, not cowardly.

This is important:
**restraint is competence.**

Avoid:
a centralized council ordering all Filaments to retreat.

Better:
distributed groups independently reach similar limits and coordinate practical care.

Supporting cast:
Mara/M and Arianna are strong candidates for the human face of this decision if source
provenance/timing supports.

---

# VI. E06 — “The Global Cluster”

**Disposition: KEEP / GLOBAL-ANCHOR CONSOLIDATION.**

Preserve:
- multiple cities report related events in a narrow time window;
- local forms differ;
- simultaneity becomes undeniable;
- protagonists realize Book 2's problem is planetary in scope.

Use protected global anchors selectively.

Marrakesh / Naima el-Faris is the clearest recovered candidate.

Do not build a roll-call of exotic cities merely to show scale.

The event should feel global because **independent human reports converge**, not because
every skyline performs the same VFX.

---

# VII. E07 — “When Systems Miss”

**Disposition: KEEP.**

Preserve:
- transit, emergency response, communications and scheduling begin failing in small
  interacting ways;
- no single failure is catastrophic;
- combined failures expose fragility;
- Baz sees human consequences before institutional models do.

Excellent theme:
systems optimized for normal variance fail when timing itself becomes unreliable.

Recurring civic faces can make this much stronger:
- Ramon;
- Leila;
- Daniel Rieux;
- Shirin;
as appropriate.

Use 1–2, not all.

# VIII. E08 — “Drift”

**Disposition: KEEP / TERMINOLOGY MILESTONE / SYSTEM REVIEW.**

This is a major recovery point.

The old source begins using **drift** as a directional propagation concept:
events do not simply recur; their center/pressure appears to move.

Preserve:
- trio notices directional migration;
- Baz tests against reports;
- Lucien finds structural correspondence;
- Seraphine recognizes human effects arriving in sequence;
- “drift” becomes a useful working term.

### Do not over-lock ontology

Current system reconciliation must determine whether drift is:
- literal movement of a Veil instability;
- propagation through linked conditions;
- changing probability/pressure;
- obsolete early model later disproven.

Treat “drift” initially as **character working vocabulary**, not narrator truth.

# IX. E09 — “Following the Drift”

**Disposition: KEEP / STRONG FIELD EPISODE.**

Preserve:
- trio follows predicted path;
- gets ahead of an event;
- sees ordinary places change in sequence;
- prediction partially works;
- intervention remains limited.

This is a satisfying payoff to the investigative work from B1 through B2.

Social-world opportunity:

threat to lived geography.

# X. E10 — “People Move With It”

**Disposition: KEEP FUNCTION / MAJOR HUMAN-AGENCY REBUILD.**

This is the riskiest civilian-synchronization episode.

Durable idea:
public movement begins statistically correlating with the directional pressure.

Old implementation risks turning people into:
- flocking bodies;
- involuntary coordinated walkers;
- a citywide choreography.

Current version should preserve agency:
- people independently choose routes that feel safer;
- crowds thin before an event;
- businesses close early;
- drivers avoid a block;
- rumor and embodied discomfort amplify one another;
- aggregate behavior produces a visible flow pattern no individual intended.

That is both more plausible and thematically richer:
**emergent social pattern, not possession.**

---

# XI. E11 — “The City Tilts”

**Disposition: KEEP FUNCTION / HEAVY DE-LITERALIZATION.**

Preserve:
- directional drift reaches city-scale perception;
- Lucien hits another structural threshold;
- Seraphine experiences mass unease;
- Baz sees civic flow reorienting;
- protagonists realize the phenomenon can reorganize urban behavior.

Old “city tilts” imagery should mostly remain metaphor/cinematic language unless current
Mechanica explicitly supports physical displacement.

This may be a good place to let architecture, transit and crowd flow create the *feeling*
of a tilted city without streets literally rotating.

# XII. E12 — “The Threshold Event”

**Disposition: KEEP / ACT CLIMAX.**

Preserve:
- multiple modes coincide;
- protagonists cannot fully stabilize;
- public/institutional systems strain;
- threshold passes;
- event resolves into a changed baseline rather than reset.

This should be Book 2's major payoff:
the world has moved from isolated anomalies to **persistent systemic instability**.

Mechanics should be reconciled before final choreography.

# XV. E15 — “No Reset”

# XVI. E16 — “What the City Feels”

**Disposition: KEEP FUNCTION / HUMANIZE.**

The title captures the old act's tendency to personify NOLA.

Preserve the thematic question:
what does a city become when millions of individual adaptations create a collective
pattern?

Do not answer by making the city literally sentient.

Better:
- Baz sees aggregate behavior;
- Seraphine sees individual distress inside it;
- Lucien sees built systems responding;
- no single perspective equals “the city's feelings.”

This can become a sophisticated civic-scale synthesis rather than magical anthropomorphism.

# XX. CIVILIAN AGENCY RULE

Across B2 Acts II–III, the old expansion repeatedly makes civilians synchronize too
perfectly.

Current migration rule:

**Prefer emergent coordination over involuntary choreography.**

Use:
- clustered decisions;
- crowd-flow shifts;
- folk heuristics;
- rumor;
- anticipatory discomfort;
- shared environmental cues;
- institutional messaging;
- social imitation.

Use literal synchronized behavior only where Mechanica explicitly requires it.

This keeps ordinary people as people.
