# 2. Chicago

## What is actually established

Tier-1 Caro canon:
- born in Chicago;
- Pilsen emotional home;
- Rosa/Miguel/Mateo family ecology;
- frontline EMT/paramedic work in Chicago;
- responsibility/self-neglect imprint.

Historical distributed-report architecture also names Chicago among outside-city observations in Veil-era material.

These are separate facts.

**A report from Chicago is not evidence that Caro returned to Chicago.**

## Search result

Targeted repository searches found no B04, B05 or B06 scene/beat that reliably places Caro in Chicago.

The family names likewise currently resolve primarily to Caro's identity canon, not a Neon scene schedule.

## Recommendation

Keep **Chicago-Neon return** as a deliberate design candidate.

It earns inclusion only if it changes:
- Caro's family relationship;
- her responsibility belief;
- her work/care decision;
- or her later ability to delegate.

Do not add Chicago merely because it is an unused origin city.

- **receiving camp/location OPEN**;
- **named responders OPEN**.

Assistant episode expansions assigning episode numbers are not authority.

## Why this is a strong Caro carrier

The recovered event naturally tests Caro's Tier-1 competence:
- finite time;
- too many people;
- triage;
- transport;
- formal/informal handoffs;
- unequal ability to leave;
- unfinished care;
- institutional capacity.

It can produce a durable Caro turn **without adding another catastrophe**.

### Candidate causal chain

1. Existing crisis overloads local care/transport.
2. Evacuation is ordered/called for reasons established by the event architecture.
3. Caro initially behaves according to her old rule: personally cover the next unmet need.
4. The scale makes this impossible.
5. A trusted responder/community network can take one responsibility, but only if Caro relinquishes direct control.
6. Caro hands it off and moves to the higher-leverage task / next group.
7. Someone is still unhappy, frightened or underserved; delegation does not magically solve scarcity.
8. The response succeeds better than if Caro had remained the bottleneck.

This is a credible implementation of **CARO-N1 — handoff is not abandonment**.

## Important limit

Nothing recovered yet proves Caro is in the evacuation city.

Therefore:
**B05 evacuation is the preferred carrier to test, not a locked Caro scene.**
