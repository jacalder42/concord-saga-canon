**Status:** EDITORIAL RECOVERY / NON-CANONICAL

**Purpose:** identify already-developed characters capable of carrying B04–B06 global theaters, Technarc fracture, Tahl/public consequences, and independent Caro/Elisabet material without inventing a new supporting cast.

No character is newly canonized and no book attendance is assigned by this report.

# 1. Executive finding

The recovered supporting cast is sufficient to build a distributed Neon.

The strongest result is Singapore: it already contains a deliberately differentiated human ecosystem around Rex. The saga does **not** need a new “Technarc defector” invented to carry the institutional fracture.

Reykjavík likewise has enough local structure to support Elisabet without turning every local into a named recurring character.

Vienna has a smaller but useful institutional/family layer around Lucien.

Marrakesh has one strong recurring human anchor, Naima el-Faris, and should resist cast proliferation.

MT/public consequences already have a media/commenter chorus capable of showing verification, distortion and correction without turning commenters into staff.

# 3. Recommended Technarc milestone synthesis

The previous docket offered:
- A1-A public operational failure fractures compliance;
- A1-B internal refusal exposes cost of control.

Recovery now suggests these should be **one causal chain**, not competing alternatives:

1. Kasumi supplies ignored evidence.
2. Shun refuses/escalates dissent.
3. Hardliners proceed.
4. A specific intervention/system fails in public or operationally undeniable fashion.
5. Tamsin/local operators cannot sustain the fiction of control.
6. Rex chooses whether to defend, expose, redirect or abandon Technarc protocol.
7. A meaningful local authority/community stops granting automatic compliance.

Milestone-worthy state change = **loss of presumptive Technarc authority in one consequential theater**.

The internal refusal is setup/cause; the public compliance fracture is the durable turn.

This can likely **revise M17** rather than add a new row.

Exact manufactured-meta involvement remains open because M17's B05 public-meta chronology is contested.

Instead define Caro's **job/function first** during Neon:
- care logistics?
- civilian movement?
- Filament coordination?

This requires a targeted Caro Neon function recovery before assigning her Marrakesh/Reykjavík travel.
