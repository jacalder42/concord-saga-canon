## 3. What the sources establish, by topic

Marks: **A** author · **L** strongly accepted · **W** weakly accepted · **U** unaccepted.

### 3.1 Who Elias is and what he wants

- **A:** from the Mississippi Gulf Coast (East Beach, Ocean Springs); the poor white kid in a Black
  neighbourhood (chosen). Tier 1.
- **A:** *"wants reach, fame, influence."* His self-justification: *"I'm getting the hard stuff
  done, even if it isn't pretty."*
- **A:** his core imprint: *"People need something to believe in more than they need the truth."*
  Stories are his vehicle.
- **A:** *"not clearly a bad guy for a long time"*; he *"operates between protag and antag for
  awhile."*
- **L:** *"predatory empathy"*; no villain self-awareness.
- **Ideologies across versions:** agency and loss of resonance (U); *"pain is misalignment"* (U);
  anti-chaos delay (W); power (U). **Only the imprint and "reach, fame, influence" are the
  author's.**

### 3.2 How he enters

- **A:** he posted as the handle *Brightbreak* in Neon, gained influence and lost it *"just as it
  started growing"*. He went to New Orleans for MT, then saw Kade at the funeral and switched
  target. His followers became *The Brightbreak*.
- **A:** face to face he is always *Elias*.
- **A:** *"no presence in Veil, no named presence in Neon"* (12-10). This conflicts with the
  author's own *"Perhaps named in 6"* (11-29) and with the accepted Visibility Rule's B6
  face-to-face (§6).

### 3.3 How he works on Kade

- **A:** he needs *"the livewire version of Kade"*; Lacuna is the *"settling influence"* he must
  remove.
- **A (question) → L (answer):** not possessive, but he trades on Kade's wish to feel wanted. He
  *"weaponizes"* attention, lets Kade's romantic misreading stand, and gives him *"the illusion of
  being valued, not the reality of being loved."*
- **L:** **authorisation.** He wants *"Kade to authorize his story without realizing it."*
- **A (question):** *"Does Kade see himself as Elias's Tahl?"*. **A (design):** Kade attacks Rex to
  *"save" Elias (representation of himself)*. The mirror: Kade protects Elias as he could not
  protect Tahl (W, `NS` 5316ff).
- **U, detailed stage-by-stage lines** (not adopted, useful for drafting):
  - B7: *"You speak like someone trying to outrun silence"*; *"Stillness isn't death. Unused
    momentum is."*
  - B8: *"Lacuna helps you rest. I want to help you move."*; *"Has she ever asked you what you
    want?"*
  - Anchor-undermining lines: of Lacuna, *"She's afraid of what you might become"*; of Rex, *"He
    wants you smaller."*

### 3.4 Brightbreak

- **A:** a pseudo-faction, not a persona; it grows from a handle into a following and then into
  "The Brightbreak".
- **L:** decentralised cells; shadow symbiosis with the Choirless, but never unified with them;
  tightens in B9; dissolves after the Mending.
- **Lineage — A, unrecorded until now:** the author's November design gave the endgame splinter to
  the **Neon Rebellion**. Kade was *"an accidental leader"* of a splinter that *"chase[s] our
  protagonists in the end game"*, and at the close the splinters become *"something new, like the
  black light brigade."* **Brightbreak fills that slot.** Two variants since:
  - the Neon Rebellion splinter itself (W, `NS` 3562ff);
  - a civilian mutual-aid network, *"not anti-resonance"* (W, `NS` 145699ff).

  These are incompatible. **The author's own design supports the splinter reading.**

### 3.5 Lacuna and Elias

- **A:** Lacuna *"keeps him grounded until Elias fractures that"*. She is *"a right hand … she
  represents movements not her self."* She is his *"settling influence."* In the November draft
  her schism with Kade *"could signal Kade falling in with the one of the dark splinters."*
- **A:** readers link Elias to Brightbreak *"when he starts to undermine Lacuna."*
- **A → L:** *"an argument between them and Elias"* (`CSR` 1748), which became Lab 6. The conflict
  is **who gets to aim Kade.**
- **U, Lacuna's lines across versions:**
  - *"He's not wrong. He's just wrong for you."*
  - *"His eyes move like he's counting exits."*
  - *"like a predator watching another predator."*

  Lacuna uses only "Elias" and *"never dignifies the myth"*.
- **Conflict:** the repo cards say Elias *"does not clock her as influential"* and *"Lacuna barely
  registers"*. The author's words make her the obstacle he works to remove. See §5.

### 3.6 How Kade gets wound up

- **A:** *"whip smart but broken street kid … incapable of stillness … momentum is a dual edged
  sword … doubling down on things he knows are foolish."* A memorable punk kid seen at
  gatherings. Before the funeral he hero-worships Tahl from afar: they *"never met."*
- **A:** his grief diary in MT comments becomes a platform. In one version **Lacuna nudges him to
  post** (*"Kade thinks it's private because of the Veil static, but Lacuna knows it is the only
  worldwide channel left"*). **Conflict:** `MDR` 11347 says *"Kade does not think MT is
  semi-private."*
- **A:** a runaway train: *"lean into his nature and just - keep - escalating."*
- **A:** after the break with Lacuna he follows *"the loudest voices, the angry ones,"* and tells
  himself he is keeping people from getting hurt.
- **Saved to memory (arc line):** *"grief → influence → misinterpretation → exploitation → fracture
  → redemption."*

### 3.7 When Kade starts to question Brightbreak

**In every author statement, Kade breaks only at the echo.** Before it, the author gives **one**
doubt beat, placed just before the attack: Kade *"try's to tell him to stop, wait, and let him try
to talk with Rex"* (`NS` 8601).

- **U, earlier doubts** (none adopted):
  - B8: *"Kade's stomach turns when they arm themselves"*; *"What if Elias is wrong?"*;
  - Brightbreak *"quoting him out of context"*;
  - B8 as *"Kade rejects Elias — fully and finally"*. That one contradicts the echo as the break.
- **Consistent with the rulings:** the 09-19 recovery downgrades the B09 A2 "realises" beat to
  doubt, with recognition at the echo.
- **READING:** Kade's B08 complicity (ruled; authorisation, per today's card) is the natural
  **first doubt**. He sees what his words licensed. It has to stay a doubt, not a break. The
  author's *"He tells himself …"* is the mechanism that keeps it one.

### 3.8 The finale — the author's choreography, consistent November 2025 to September 2026

1. Rex and Elisabet hold off the antagonists. Rex sends Elisabet to warn the others (A:
   `PC` 92334ff, `NS` 8111, `BBR` 8713).
2. **Elias attacks first** (A: `BBR` 8713, 9749 *"1 A"*). Rex knocks him down or incapacitates him
   (A: `NS` 8346, `BBR` 8713).
3. Just before this, Kade tries to stop Elias and talk to Rex (A, proposed: `NS` 8601).
4. **Kade fears Rex will kill Elias, and attacks Rex**, to *"save" Elias (representation of
   himself)* (A).
5. **Tahl's echo**, with Silence's boost (A: `NS` 8111), stops him. Rex recognises it (ruled
   09-26).
6. Kade talks down the Brightbreakers and stands with Rex against the Choirless. **The Choirless
   stay unresolved** into the Mending cutaway (A + L: `NS` 139944, 140139).
7. *"Tahl then aids Rex"* (A: `RPS` 2761, 2026).

**Two motives for the attack, recorded, not reconciled:**

- **A:** Kade fears Rex will kill Elias.
- **Assistant** (`NS`, quoted as "canon text" in `BBR` 9377): Elias whispers *"If Rex reaches her,
  the world ends,"* and Kade believes it *"because it mirrors his deepest fear: failing the world
  like he failed Tahl."*

They can coexist: Elias's lie, and then Elias on the ground.

### 3.9 Elias's fate and "Solace"

- **A:** he may return in a later expansion, *"he'll liely spin up some new following."* **L:**
  never publicly unmasked; Brightbreak dissolves.
- **Fate variants** (all U): flees; subdued; *"sitting alone on a curb"*; *"I was trying to
  help"*.
- **"Solace":** first his nickname (A, 11-27). Dropped because it *"was originally a Choirless
  persona"* (A, 11-30). Then *"Choirless could still be led by a Solace character"* (A). **Open:**
  a Solace as a Choirless leader.
