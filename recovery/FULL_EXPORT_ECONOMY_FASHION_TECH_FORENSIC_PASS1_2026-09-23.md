## A. Older economy concepts are now RECOVERED, but mostly SUPERSEDED candidates

**Nov. 1 “Resonant Exchange”:** an early model explicitly treats magic as fueled by offered/consumed emotion and later describes measurable, tradable tones sold by artists/healers. **Nov. 1 “Tone Markets”:** commodified or bottled emotion, later outlawed in a Concord ethics code. **Nov. 8 “synthreads”:** mood reactive fabrics changing color with wearer resonance. **Nov. 10 “resonance credits”:** one line proposes emotion based energy trade, alongside chorus engines/ley reactors and memory glass.

These terms **are genuinely in the full export**. They were missed by sanitized subset searches and the Notion root search. The Nov. 1 branch also contains magic tiers, prophecy/deathwork, resonance carrying text messages, resurrection rites, a Concord ethics polity and other apparatus incompatible with later no-wishes/no-causality-bypass/no-digital-transmission and the human-scale current world. Nov. 8 also imagines orbital/aether routes and peripheral colonies. The Nov. 10 energy hardware suggests devices that store emotional amplitude. None can be restored just because the wording was once explicit.

**Disposition:** RECOVERED HISTORICAL DEVELOPMENT, **SUPERSEDED/CONTRADICTED as literal current economy and materials**, subject to line-by-line later ruling review. The ideas can still inform faction rhetoric or failed historical experiments only as a clearly marked **PROPOSAL**. “Resonant Exchange” in later combat documents also means an action sequence; do not conflate the two usages. A search hit for “economy” often means **UARS action economy**, not wages and markets.

## B. Late-November power and material economics are far more grounded

In the Nov. 30 conversation, the author says “Proceed” immediately before the assistant builds a **Power + Charging Ecology Bible (light)** (~4783–4980). It gives a central progression: power largely invisible in Veil, negotiable in Neon, and a scarce exchange good in Loom. Devices downshift because both power availability and resonance conditions matter.

This is **RECOVERED APPROVED DEVELOPMENT**, with a later-canon check pending. It is a more coherent near-term economic substrate than the early emotion-credit model. It does **not** imply that electricity literally becomes the sole legal tender. Some attached setting examples (airships, Blue-Lines, ranked weather terminology, tech clothing interactions) require cross-checking.

The same conversation **explicitly flags** the wider “Resource & barter economy” as **not yet built** (~5452): money as systems fail; which necessities acquire exchange value; and prices/access across Veil, Neon and Loom. It also flags shelter governance, food/water/public health logistics, governance and daily media (~5461–5493). Therefore “no material economy work” was too strong; “no complete reconciled material economy” remains accurate. Current [Trip identity](../canon/characters/TripID.md) also supplies a concrete B01 class anchor.

**Disposition:** Power/charging = RECOVERED, pending chronology and mechanical reconciliation. Wages, housing, prices and broad resource distribution = OPEN.
