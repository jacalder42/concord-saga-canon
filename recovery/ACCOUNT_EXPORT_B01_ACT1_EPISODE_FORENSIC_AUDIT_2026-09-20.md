Unlike the later Veil material with named-Tahl chronology corruption, E00–E15 contains no
Tahl problem. Its core sequence still matches current recovered B1 architecture:

1. Silence/Hope prologue.
2. Seraphine fails to save swamp child.

4. Lucien first geometry flickers.

This is not material that should be restarted from scratch.

The main required transformation is **mechanics/art-language cleanup**, not narrative
replacement.

The old EBCI often literalizes:
- colored glows;
- fields colliding;
- geometry visibly bending;
- hums syncing to decisions;
- visible “resonance” on people.

The Dec. 9 Art Canon source explicitly says light/geometry/sound are cinematic metaphors,
not supernatural phenomena unless Mechanica independently permits them.

Therefore the episode skeleton is strong, but every literal sensory effect must pass a
Mechanica test.
