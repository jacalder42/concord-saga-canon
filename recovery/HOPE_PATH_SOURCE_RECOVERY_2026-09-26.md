## Bottom line

1. **There is an approved Hope Bible v1.** It is `MDR` 14802–15181, approved by *"Approve and save
   Hope Bible"* (`MDR` 15189, USER, 11-18). **The repo had not recovered it.** A matching **Silence
   Bible** sits just before it (`MDR` ~14644).
2. **Hope is the emotional, lyrical half; Silence is the analytic half.** These are the author's own
   words (below): *"silence = facts, history, etc, hope = emotion, love, humor, etc"*.
3. **The approved split at Tahl's death fits today's ruling.** *"Silence shelters the Echo; / Hope
   **nurtures** it."* The Bible continues: *"This is the first time Hope shows emotional agency — not
   Intent, but a form of emotional selfhood"* (`MDR` 15035–15038). **Silence collects; Hope nurtures,
   after.**
4. **Hope breaks before Silence does** (approved twice). That fits the 12-07 order, in which Hope goes
   before Silence. **No source says why** she goes first.
5. **Several sources give Hope earlier "proto-agency"** toward Tahl, in late Veil. That sits against
   today's ruling that **Silence** makes the first act of agency. It reconciles only if Hope's early
   acts count as involuntary (§4).
