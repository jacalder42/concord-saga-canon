## Executive finding

Baz and Tahl demonstrate why **scene grammar and temporal implementation must be audited
separately**.

Both early Vault bundles contain excellent durable execution material, but both also carry
major obsolete chronology:

- Baz's early bundle assumes Neon/Loom/Post-Mending survival;
- current ruling locks Baz's death in Book 3.
- Tahl's bundle treats him as an ordinary named participant through Veil/early Neon;
- current ruling keeps him MissingThread / anonymous Threadnaut through B1–B3, with the
  Tahl Morgan reveal in the B3 epilogue.

Therefore these bundles are **not safe temporal canon**. Their body-language,
relationship-function and human-recognition material is still highly valuable.

## 12. High-value editorial lesson

Baz/Tahl show four separable layers inside a single old character bundle:

This should become the standard treatment for all old character bundles.
