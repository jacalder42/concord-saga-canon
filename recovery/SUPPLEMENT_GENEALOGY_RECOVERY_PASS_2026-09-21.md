## Reconstructed evolutionary model

The following is editorial reconstruction, not recovered verbatim history.

### Stage 1 — PRIVATE
Correspondence between people.
Likely forms: LETTER, RECORDING, JOURNAL.
Jobs: intimacy, interiority, asynchronous information, emotional residue, distance.
Author-recalled origin: Seraphine / Lucien letters.

### Stage 2 — PUBLIC / CIVIC
News briefings, reports, public descriptions.
Likely forms: RECORDING, VIGNETTE, LETTER/document.
Jobs: outside-protagonist perspective, events elsewhere, public misunderstanding, widened geography, consequences without briefing-room scenes.
Author-recalled transition: news briefings.

### Stage 3 — CULTURAL / LOCAL
Recovered descendants: VEIN, VELVET, RITUAL.
Jobs: joy, texture, local memory, humor, social temperature, ordinary life.

### Stage 4 — INVESTIGATIVE / NETWORKED
Recovered descendants: CHRON, FIELD, later MT.
Jobs: LINK, breadcrumb, off-page plot movement, pattern recognition, competing interpretations, public awareness.

### Stage 5 — SAGA CHANNEL
Recovered descendant: MT, with Tahl→Kade voice arc.
Jobs: civic awareness, global public reaction, emotional windows, eventually a global mortal voice under collapse.

VT/LT are not simply later news vehicles; they remain bounded by separate metaphysical/channel rules.

## Proposed editorial job vocabulary

Do NOT add these to controlled vocabulary yet.

HEARTH — makes a place/community feel lived in.
RESIDUE — emotional/social consequence.
BREADCRUMB — clue, echo or pattern before protagonists can fully use it.
WORLDLINE — advances an off-camera saga/faction/global thread.
COUNTERPOINT — another interpretation of a known event.
REWARD — delight, humor, culture, sensory pleasure.

These can overlap. They are editorial diagnostics, not ontology.

## WORLDLINE rule

Proposed working rule:

**A WORLDLINE supplement may advance the global story, but may not advance protagonist knowledge unless the protagonists later encounter that information through ordinary narrative causality.**

The reader may know something similar happened elsewhere, an institution changed policy, a faction acted, someone began collecting data, or public narratives are changing while Seraphine/Lucien/Baz remain unaware.

## Structural analogue

The transferable lesson from recap shows/leaderboards/god chats is:

**after sustained local/social/protagonist material, briefly reveal that the larger game kept moving.**

For Concord, the artifact should be native to its world: correspondence, reporting, institutional notices, field records, cultural fragments, later MT, and later saga-appropriate channels.

Avoid a single generic global-news format unless the world itself develops one.
