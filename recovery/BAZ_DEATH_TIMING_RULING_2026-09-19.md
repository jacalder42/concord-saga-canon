# Baz Death Timing — Recovery Correction

**Status:** AUTHOR RULING / RECOVERY CORRECTION
**Date:** 2026-09-19

## Locked chronology

- **Baz dies at the end of Book 3.**
- **The rest of the cast does not learn of his death until the start of Book 4.**

This resolves the earlier recovery mistake that treated the sanitized Veil→Neon bridge as proof Baz survived Book 3.

## Source support

Notion independently preserves the Book 3 placement in several sources, including:
- `BOOK 3 — VEIL III (Final Beat Bible)`, whose Act III explicitly kills Baz in the Warehouse Incident;
- Book 3 character material stating Baz dies at the end of Book 3;
- Veil Trilogy overview and environmental/city canon placing the death in the B3 Warehouse arc;
- `BOOK 4 — NEON I (Final Beat Bible)`, which begins the post-Baz grief trajectory.

## Superseded staging

Older Notion B3 material stages Lucien, Seraphine, and others as direct witnesses to or immediately aware of Baz's death. That **knowledge timing is superseded** by the current author ruling.

Therefore distinguish:
1. **event chronology:** Baz dies at end of B3;
2. **reader knowledge:** may include the death at B3 ending, depending final POV staging;
3. **cast knowledge:** rest of cast learns at opening of B4.

The exact witness configuration, death mechanics, and B4 notification sequence remain subject to reconciliation. Do not reopen the B3 death placement.
