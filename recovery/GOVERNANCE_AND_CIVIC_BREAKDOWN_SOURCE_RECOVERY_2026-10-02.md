## 3. The recovered model by era

Weight key:

- **AW**: the author's own words;
- **AS**: author-saved, strongly accepted (the Track-1 block and the text it compiles);
- **AC**: accepted by an explicit choice (*"Proceed as described"* on a named option, *"Integrate the realist
  recommendations"*);
- **WK**: weak (*Proceed*, a menu letter, or nothing).

### 3.1 Saga-wide frame

- **The iceberg** (AS; WB 11518–11521; PRG 13842–14073):
  - layer 1 is local institutions on the page;
  - layer 2 is pressure felt through media and rumour;
  - layer 3 is a world *"never denied, rarely mapped"*;
  - use *policy moments, not political systems*, and name one or two new factions per book at most.
- **Mundane governments** (AW G3, AS): in place, competent, unaware of resonance. Their normal frameworks fail in
  public. *"Behavior > ideology"*: the four response modes are Denial-stability, Grid-control, Adaptive coalition and
  Fracture/fail state. Two cities can be in different modes.
- **Legitimacy transfer** (AS; PRG 14083–14207):
  1. anomalies;
  2. normal frameworks fail publicly;
  3. trust debt;
  4. Dominions, hardliners and Choirless offer coherent frames;
  5. people choose *"who keeps them alive"*;
  6. soft takeovers in some regions, violent fractures in others.
- **Perimeter governance rhythm** (AS, asked for in G4): one glimpse every two to three chapters in Veil, one per
  chapter or act turn in Neon, one per movement in Loom. The forms run from official bulletin and Chronicle insert to
  MT clip, secondary-POV anecdote, checkpoint residue and background broadcast. *"One scope signal per glimpse unless
  crescendo."*
- **Leak taxonomy** (AS): existential, data, narrative, structural.
  - **Seraphine is the first leak** (G5): she leaks *"by existing"*, and others carry the public consequences.
  - The Veil playbook (PRG 14332–14479) is contain, discredit, compartmentalize, recruit or neutralize.
- **The comms crackdown leads to MT** (AW G4, AS): soft censorship and framing in late Veil; hard blackouts and
  overrides in Neon; MT forms as folk survival media.
- **Official cover story** (the author's memory paste, PM 1374–1378): governments attribute events to electrical
  faults, solar anomalies or *"mass hysteria"*.
- **Weak elaborations, not adopted:** *"Governance always reacts late"* (WB 147234); two incompatible agency-name
  sets (WB 14751–14775; 22336–22360); a US regional bloc map (WB 22270–22320); an official lexicon (WB 40595–40628).

### 3.2 Veil (B01–B03)

- **Configuration** (AS):
  - MG strong and resonance-blind;
  - the Dominions *"strong/hidden; rules the narrative frame"*;
  - TA strong black-ops, compartmentalized;
  - folk order weak;
  - Concord a nascent rumour network;
  - late Veil, soft censorship and framing.
- **Dominion mechanism** (WK; CV 52270–52278, 55057–55082): *Containment Recommendations* are advisories. Local
  governments carry them out *"voluntarily to appear 'responsible'"*. This is the cleanest jurisdiction model in the
  export: **the Dominion advises, a public body signs**.
- **B01–B03 civic ladder** (WK; BBR 12519–14822, 28176–30350; EXP 9377–37153):
  - council fear of attention and funding loss;
  - information requests that read like surveillance;
  - gathering-notice rules;
  - Seraphine *"allowed to care, but not to name"*;
  - containment orders, movement clearance and identity checks;
  - a cross-jurisdictional directive in B03.
- **Dominion arc** (WK; BBR): *"Dominions MUST break politically, not violently"* in B03. Superseded; see §5.
- **Military** (AW G10): visible in passive control and peacekeeping. The assistant's competing claims are both weak:
  secrecy and deference to the Dominions and Technarc (WB 36140), against *"High trust from civilians"* (WB 40639).

### 3.3 Neon (B04–B06)

- **Configuration** (AS):
  - MG of *"mixed credibility; clampdowns/curfews in some zones"*;
  - the Dominions surface in pockets and fracture elsewhere;
  - TA legitimacy *"erodes on tech failure"*;
  - folk order thickens into corridors, Knots and MT mutual aid;
  - Concord emerges as a *"route-truth & fairness federation"*;
  - *"hard blackouts/overrides"*.
- **The Neon Rebellion as the governments' label** (AW G8; the assistant adopts it at WB 14148–14190, 19163–19205
  and 28922–28945): a frame, *"what a collapsing institution calls people telling the truth"*. Kade inherits the
  term *"against his will"* and becomes *"its accidental voice"* (WB 28942).
- **Military and ordnance** (AW G9–G11): explosives are kept away from node and shard events, and the explanation
  goes into a news report or supplement. Four rival mechanisms (WK) are unresolved.
- **Collapse model: A + light B** (AC; WB 93644–93980). Option A, *Slow Slide, Sudden Break*, with regional
  variation from B:
  - **Slide:** NOLA, Santa Fe and Singapore break early; London and Tokyo mid; Vienna and Reykjavík late.
  - **Sudden break:** B06 Act III at Tahl's death, when *"institutions lose legitimacy in minutes"*.
  - **Loom:** the military retreats into humanitarian roles.
- **Protocol 9** (Notion, pasted; NB 111539–112290; RPS 2746–2855): Technarc's programme. Prepared in B03, launched
  in B04 as monitoring and containment, and expanded in B05 into checkpoints and detention. Its later versions (9.1,
  9.3) are open.
- **NOLA in B05–B06** (WK; NS 188961; WB 94095–94125): a citywide resonance curfew; local governments contradicting
  federal alerts; police retreat and the Guard *"goes humanitarian"* at B06.

### 3.4 Loom (B07–B09)

- **Configuration** (AS): folk order, Knots and convoys dominate local order; Concord is a stabilizer spine; the
  remnants of MG, the Dominions and TA are patchy.
- **Accepted realism notes** (AC):
  - NS 42678–42925 (*"Proceed as you recommend"*): in B07–B08, show *"one competent local official + one terrible
    one"*; at the B09 feint, *"a small, confused coalition of security forces"*.
  - NS 13909–14183 (*"Integrate the realist recommendations"*): a B07 civic-confusion moment, and a B09 A1 scene where
    institutions try to keep order and fail.
- **Panic governments reanimate old doctrine** (WK; CV 57703–57712): Dominion policies return as emergency laws, and
  *"No one remembers why these laws existed"*.
- **Weak:** Choirless *"Emotion Fines"* and quiet curfews (NS 182691–183140); Technarc remnants' martial law (NB 10212).

### 3.5 Post-Mending

- **Direction** (AW G13): not utopian, *"perfectly imperfect"*. Post-Mending detail is not needed beyond the
  epilogue.
- **Final compression** (WK; WB 147275–147290): *"Local-first procedural councils … No global authority … No
  ascendant-run system."*
- Long geopolitical passages (WB 99870–103680) carry no weight against G13.
