## 1. The system — `HYBRID RESONANCE GEOGRAPHY SYSTEM`

Core principle, verbatim:

> Resonance geography = emotional topography + civic stress + metaphysical pressure.

**Geography evolves per era**, which is why no static location list was ever going to work:

| Era | Form |
| --- | --- |
| Veil | shards as Ghostwaves, Flickers, micro-fractures |
| Neon | **Neon Zones** — urban resonance bloom districts |
| Loom | **Loom Corridors** — semi-stable travel paths; *"NOT magical roads — safe-ish paths through unsafe resonance"* |
| Post-Mending | **Echo Nodes** — gentle pools of stabilized resonance at former shard fractures |

### 1.1 Five Neon Zone types

| Type | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Blue Pulse Districts | clarity hum, calm-before-chaos |
| Amber Drift Zones | emotional panic amplification |
| Violet Bloom Stripes | near-shard rupture conditions |
| **Red Flare Intersections** | **violence flashpoints** |
| Silver Static Blocks | metas disrupt resonance signal |

Formation causes: shard density plus emotional density ("people pressure"), urban design
stressors, nearby Filament activity, Technarch interference. Naming convention: capitalized
and treated like neighbourhoods.

### 1.2 Four Loom Corridor classes

`Humanitarian` (Filament-built; lantern signals, quiet boats, grounded rituals) ·
`Concord` (protagonist routes; emotional stabilizers required — Elisabet, Seraphine, Rex) ·
`Rupture` (extremely dangerous; lead toward shard-cluster fail zones; used in the final
Loom approach) · `Ghostline` (almost silent; emotional emptiness; *"hint at Hope's fraying
modulation"*).

### 1.3 The evolution mapping — Neon Zone → Loom form

Blue Pulse → stable pockets within Corridors · Amber Drift → corridor bottlenecks ·
Violet Bloom → **rupture origin points** · Red Flare → collapse accelerants ·
Silver Static → corridor fragmentation.

Visual trigger for the transition: ambient sky showing prismatic shear lines (Veil-thread
fray).

### 1.4 City-by-city map, as the system gives it

- **New Orleans** — Blue Pulse: Tremé, Marigny · Amber Drift: Bywater, Central City ·
  Violet Bloom: **Warehouse District (Baz's death site)** · Red Flare: Canal Street ·
  Silver Static: Port/Industrial.
  Loom corridors: The Atchafalaya Pass · The Three-Lantern Corridor (Tremé → River) ·
  The Honey Island Approach.
- **Vienna** — Neon: Ringstrasse Amber Drift, Museum Quarter Blue Pulse. Loom: Archive
  Faultline Corridor.
- **Singapore** — Neon: Silver Static around Technarch towers. Loom: Neon Spine Transit
  Corridor.
- **Reykjavík** — Neon: minimal, mostly Blue Pulse. Loom: Clarity Channel (geothermal).
- **Marrakesh** — Neon: Prism Convergence (market). Loom: Luminous Diagonal Corridor.

### 1.5 The narrative/character split is built in

This is the distinction James raised, and the source already draws it:

- **§V City-by-City Geo-Map** — the **place** layer. Places with resonance properties,
  naming conventions, narrative function.
- **§VIII Character Interaction Rules** — the **character** layer. What each person does
  *in* geography: Seraphine senses harmonic distortion before others · Lucien identifies
  structural weakness in corridor geometry · Caro's emotional bleed amplifies or stabilizes
  fields · Elisabet's clarity-breath can temporarily smooth a Drift Zone · Rex calculates
  safest routes inside corridor edges · **Kade destabilizes Amber Drift zones (dangerous)**
  · Lacuna hums into resonance to calm a corridor · Silence/Hope imprint the collapse
  pattern indirectly · Tahl's Echo flickers at corridor pinch points.

So the shape question is answered by the source: **one place layer, plus a
character-interaction layer that references it.** Not two parallel location systems.

### 1.6 Reader accessibility layer, as given

Zones = chaotic pockets · Corridors = safe routes · Nodes = healing places.

## 2. CONFLICT — three location taxonomies — **RULED 2026-09-19, Ruling 1**

> **Ruled:** `recovery/GATE_RULINGS_2026-09-19.md` Ruling 1 — **Option C, qualified.**
> The three taxonomies are **not rivals**; they are three different kinds of thing:
>
> - the **geography system** holds the **controlled vocabulary** of zone types and
>   corridor classes — and `ENV` derives from that layer **and only that layer**;
> - the **city bibles** hold **named places**, proper nouns for specific ground, each
>   mapping onto a type. *"Red Lantern Faultline is a name, not a type."*
> - the **Reykjavík scheme** is the **shard progression** — a severity scale that layers
>   over places. **Not a spatial taxonomy, and not to be treated as one.**
>
> **The qualification governs everything:** *"All specific type assignments are
> PRELIMINARY until vetted against narrative and milestones."* That includes the contested
> ground below **and** the assignments both source documents already carry. **The
> structure is ruled; the contents are provisional.**

The conflict as originally recorded is kept below, because it is the evidence the ruling
was made against.

| Source | Taxonomy used |
| --- | --- |
| Geography system (11-27) | five Neon Zone types; four Loom Corridor classes |
| New Orleans bible (12-01) | **Blue Pulse Corridor · Red Lantern Faultline · Violet Spiral** |
| Reykjavík bible (11-27) | **Tier 1 Flickers · Tier 2 Ghostwaves · Tier 3 Fractures · Tier 4 Rupture Threat** |

The Reykjavík scheme is the **shard progression** (Flicker → Ghostwave → Fracture →
Rupture) used as a spatial tier, not the zone-type scheme at all.

**And the two New Orleans maps disagree on specific neighbourhoods:**

| Neighbourhood | Geography system | New Orleans bible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tremé | **Blue Pulse** | **Red Lantern Faultline** (with Esplanade, Marigny) |
| Marigny | **Blue Pulse** | **Red Lantern Faultline** |
| Bywater | **Amber Drift** | early-shard Flickers → Ghostwaves (Veil) |
| French Quarter | *unassigned* | **Blue Pulse Corridor** (with Convention Center, Riverwalk) |
| Warehouse District | **Violet Bloom** | **Violet Spiral** — agrees in colour, differs in term |

Only the Warehouse District agrees, and even there the type name differs. The New Orleans
bible is **four days later** and is stamped `FINAL CANON`; the geography system is stamped
`CANON` and is the only document that defines the type vocabulary the city bible partly
departs from.

~~**Both readings recorded; neither wins.**~~ **Closed by Ruling 1.** The disagreement was
never between two vocabularies — it was between a vocabulary and a set of place names. The
`ENV` vocabulary comes from the geography system alone, so the city bible's terms do not
compete with it.

**What stays open is narrower and is not a blocker:** which *type* each named place maps
onto. Tremé, Marigny, the French Quarter and Bywater are **contested ground** and are
**left unassigned**, per Ruling 1 and the §4 instruction to derive `ENV` from the type
layer only. The Warehouse District's two entries — `Violet Bloom` and `Violet Spiral` —
are now readable as one type and one name for the same place, which is exactly the shape
the ruling describes; it is still marked provisional like every other assignment.

**Scope note from the ruling:** the zone-type vocabulary appears in five files, all under
`recovery/` and `proposals/`. **The canon substrate contains none of it.** There is
nothing to migrate and nothing to unpick.

## 3. CONFLICT — `Corridor` — **RULED 2026-09-19, Ruling 2**

> **Ruled:** `recovery/GATE_RULINGS_2026-09-19.md` Ruling 2 — **Option B**, with the
> definition supplied by the author:
>
> > **A corridor is a linear path or area between two known points. Context determines
> > whether it is safe, dangerous, or otherwise.**
>
> **The four senses are not four meanings.** They are one definition, one measurement
> scale applied to it, and two instance classes — see the amended table below.

Ledger §29 recorded two senses. This pass found four, and the ruling resolves all four:

| Recorded sense | Where | **Under Ruling 2** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `rules/canon_rules.json`, Mechanica §24–25 | **Intensity measured on corridors.** `U1`–`U7` is a scale, not a rival sense |
| 2 | Geography system §IV | **A named class of corridors.** Instance layer |
| 3 | New Orleans bible §III — "Blue Pulse Corridor" | **Conditional — see the geometry test below** |
| 4 | Reykjavík §2C, NOLA §II — "Laugavegur Corridor", "River Corridor" | **Ordinary instances. Correct usage.** |

~~Sense 3 is the worst of the four, because it applies the word to the thing sense 2
explicitly contrasts with (*zones are chaotic pockets, corridors are safe routes*).~~
**That objection no longer holds.** Safety is not part of the definition, so a bloom zone
being dangerous does not disqualify it from being a corridor. The New Orleans bible's
"diaspora corridors" for evacuation routes is straightforwardly sense 2.

### The geometry test for "Blue Pulse Corridor"

The term now stands or falls on **geometry alone**:

- **linear and connective**, running between two known points → the name is correct and
  stays;
- **a pocket or area without that connective geometry** → it is a zone, and "Corridor"
  comes off the name.

This is a question **about the referent**, to be answered when the New Orleans geography is
vetted under Ruling 1. **It is not a naming decision to be made now.**

### Consequential amendment, carried out separately

The geography system's framing that *corridors are safe routes and zones are chaotic
pockets* is **superseded** — the distinction is geometric, not one of safety. That line is
amended wherever it appears; see the ledger entry for the pass.

**Still: do not rename anything.** The ruling's scope note is explicit — bare `Corridor`
appears in **13 places across the canon substrate** (the EBCI header, Concord's Corridor
Preservation, corridor viability, corridor instability, corridor ecology `U1`–`U7`,
corridor shifts) and **all of them are correct under this definition.** No mass rename, no
prose rewriting.

- **§VII Sudowrite location keys** — seven sensory generation tags. **Not transcribed**;
  they are setting phrases for a generation tool and sit closest to the §1 prose line.
