## Finding A — B3 Warehouse: event and human stakes recovered

**Classification:** CURRENT B3 death and Warehouse arc under author ruling; RECOVERED direct Rupture/collapse and child rescue; OPEN upstream trigger and institutional contribution; SUPERSEDED old group-at-death staging.

The November primary page says: A3 E11 Rupture forms in the Warehouse District, sound and color change; E12 Baz enters for trapped civilians; E13 a resonance shockwave collapses part of the building, crushing Baz as he frees a child; E15 leaves a lasting scar. It therefore provides a physical **claimed** death chain rather than an unspecified “super feeling.” The September recovery independently documents the child rescue and emphasizes that Han Wei fails to predict the Rupture. The revised Book 3 map retains Baz as investigator and moves the named Tahl revelation and first/only Veil VT brush to the epilogue. The old Notion page's direct Lucien witness, immediate group grief, and Act II Tahl near-slip do not govern the present staging.

## Finding B — B5 Colorstorm: named event recovered, global mechanism unresolved

**Classification:** RECOVERED November B5 finale; PROPOSAL present placement/retention; OPEN planetary coupling and sensory physics.

The original A3 E15 identifies a **multi-spectrum Bloom above New Orleans**, alleged fractured light and chord pulses, overwhelming local responders, Kade unconscious and Tahl near-fatal VT proximity. The same line states that “emotional bleedthrough becomes global.” Those words name a story scope; they do not supply the mechanism by which a NOLA event affects distant populations. Book 6's older page says the B5 Colorstorm “never truly ended,” supporting a persistence motif within the same early version. The later structural backup and reconciliation do not give it the same protected endpoint status.

**Recommendation:** Split the possible anchor into three testable assertions: (1) a NOLA event occurred; (2) other locations experienced similar effects; (3) the NOLA event **caused** those effects. The page supports (1) and narratively asserts (2) and (3), but the physics does not yet establish (3). Review later B5/B6 primary packets before reinstating “first global Colorstorm.” Do not use technology outage as a universal intensity meter: distortion, degraded quality and intermittent operation are valid scaled outcomes.

## Finding C — B8 “first Echo Node”: version conflict, not a settled precursor

**Classification:** RECOVERED November scene; SUPERSEDED as a formal pre-Mending Echo Node under later geography and Mechanica; OPEN whether a distinct precursor event survives.

The November B8 page E1 hints at a calm place, E11 describes a shimmering pool called an Echo Node and says Seraphine stabilizes it effortlessly; E13 calls such places safe routes; E15 combines the pool, storm and Seraphine's flare into an outward world pulse. Four days later the geography canon specifically places **Echo Nodes after the Breathable Veil forms**. The December backup has a different B8 movement: route and “node alignment” visits, convergence pressure, revelation of the Mending chamber. Its use of the unqualified word **node** does not establish a formal Echo Node state; it needs its own vocabulary audit. The old E11 “effortlessly” also conflicts with visible-cost discipline if treated as a Resonance action.
