**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Status:** RECOVERY / NON-CANONICAL. It records the echo-guide Mira's history in the export,
separates her from the other Miras, and tests the author's two origin hypotheses. **It rules
nothing and edits no card.** The author ruled on 09-26 that she is **not** Mira Tremeaux: she was
*"intended to be a echo that guides Seraphine and integrates with the loom,"* and *"Unsure if she
still fits the saga"*

*mirror*). The author's quotations and the flattening were checked in this session.

## Bottom line

1. **"Mira" names at least five figures.** The two that matter are **the echo guide** (the author's
   idea) and a **Filament elder** that became "Mira Tremeaux" and then "Mira Janelle Batiste". The
   others:
   - Mira Quon, a Sydney rave founder and Tahl's mentor, in early assistant rosters;
   - one-off reuses: an ER nurse, the Blinking Girl's former name, a barista, a coven member, and
     Kade's sister.
2. **The echo guide is the author's own idea, 11-13-2025:** *"What if Mira becomes Seraphine's
   guide. Is she a ghost in the machine time character that begins to seed Seraphine's Luminous
   Thread transition."* (`PC` 39413)
3. **Her role was locked on 11-13** (*"Lock it. Highest of fives"*, `PC` 51477): Seraphine's *"soft
   guide and confidant through the transformation."* **Her nature was never settled.** The assistant
   offered five options (`PC` 39587–39599), and no author choice among them was found.
4. **The author himself accepted her flattening on 11-27.** The assistant proposed *"Retcon Mira into
   a memory-anchor rather than an actual echo"* (`NS` 18595). The author answered *"Integrate
   proposals and proceed"* (`NS` 18632). That was right after he had asked *"Did we lose Mira as an
   echo that functions as Seraphine's guide in Loom?"* (`NS` 18482). **The 09-26 ruling reverses
   that acceptance.**
5. **Neither of the author's new origin hypotheses has a source** (§4). Both are new ideas, and both
   are open.

## 3. How she was flattened, and conflated

1. **11-14/15:** the assistant merged the guide with Mira Quon, the loud mentor
   (`PC` ~80191–80213, *"The Compass"*).
2. **11-18:** she was split in two: a human "sensitive witness", and a separate *"Echo-Guide … Not
   metaphysical — human"* (`MDR` ~18126–18138).
3. **11-22/24:** she was demoted to a **Filament elder**. **"Mira Tremeaux" first appears on 11-24**
   (`NB` ~125399) as an early Filament organiser, later a militant recruiter. The Reykjavík *"Echo-guide
   (QZ)"* survives separately (`NB` ~152773). *(Inference: "Tremeaux" from her Tremé placement.)*
4. **11-27, the flattening:** *"Retcon Mira into a memory-anchor rather than an actual echo"* (`NS`
   18595). The author accepted (*"Integrate proposals and proceed"*). She became **Mira Janelle
   Batiste**, a Filament elder who is *"not an Echo."*
5. **It did not hold.** Later assistant passages bring back the echo guide in Loom (`NS` ~79384) and
   count *"2 echoes (Tahl, Mira)"* (`NS` ~45429).
6. **09-19, repo:** `proposals/concord-2026/EDITORIAL_CASTING_RESOLUTION_FILAMENT_2026-09-19.md`
   treated all of these as **one** contradictory Mira, retired "Mira Tremeaux", and split her among
   Mara, Arianna, Ren and a Reykjavík role. **The 11-13 lock was never cited. This is where the
   conflation reached canon** (`canon/cast_retired_aliases.csv`). The Reykjavík echo guide was demoted
   separately (`EDITORIAL_CASTING_RESOLUTION_REYKJAVIK_2026-09-19.md` §VII).

**The rule used to flatten her was the assistant's, not the author's.** Its "one echo only" argument
has **no author source**. Today's ruled rule is narrower: **Tahl's echo appears identifiably to the
cast only once.** A guide perceived **only by Seraphine** (the source design, `PC` ~43870, ~84330)
does not breach that.

**Both hypotheses are new author ideas, not recoveries.** Each fits something already on file:

- **A** uses the B01 E03 echo, which exists. It would turn **a boy** into Mira, or need the child
  re-gendered. The book-1 draft is prose, not canon, and the v4.1b beat bible keeps the child
  unnamed.
- **B** fits the author's 11-13 *"Mira merges back with architecture"* and the ruled *"cycle of hard
  cap veils."* A predecessor who helped build the last cap, guiding the one who will end the cycle,
  matches the saga's Möbius shape. **That is a reading, not a finding.**
