## I. CORE ENSEMBLE RECOGNITION

### Seraphine — presence / warmth
Scene signature:
- open chest, soft shoulders;
- settles feet before intervention;
- deliberate breath;
- hands hover before touching;
- moves to shield rather than command;
- room warmth / fear reduction without authority.

Stress:
- regulation becomes effortful;
- risk is moving too quickly into care/control.

Intimacy:
- attention deepens before touch;
- warmth becomes specific rather than ambient.

Do not misread as:
prophet, saint, healer-magician, omniscient empath.

### Lucien — structure / clarity
Scene signature:
- posture feels designed;
- stress creates right angles;
- fingers align along seams;
- controlled exhale;
- stabilizes by creating structure.

Intimacy:
- shoulders lower;
- jaw unsets;
- gaze lingers;
- touch becomes precise/careful.

Do not misread as:
cold, emotionless, generic stoic, ideological Dominion clone.

### Caro — momentum / action-care
Scene signature:
- low center of gravity;
- decisive stride;
- hand-to-hip assessment;
- humor cuts through emotional stasis;
- converts concern into immediate practical action.

Stress:
- fighter's stance;
- overfunctions rather than freezes.

Intimacy:
- direct, earnest touch;
- kinetic warmth.

Do not misread as:
comic sidekick, reckless chaos, aggression-first, fire-coded superhuman.

### Elisabet — clarity / decision path
Scene signature:
- clean vertical posture;
- minimal wasted movement;
- head micro-tilt while recalibrating;
- traces invisible maps/data relationships;
- creates spatial and decision clarity.

Stress:
- voice/posture sharpen rather than emotionally disappear.

Intimacy:
- tiny eye/brow shifts;
- deliberate touch;
- gaze lingers.

Do not misread as:
cold, psychic, aloof, judgmental.

### Rex — material limits / repair
Scene signature:
- tool-minded movement;
- readout/tool checking;
- fingers tap;
- hands hover before commitment;
- grounds a crisis by identifying what the system can actually bear.

Stress:
- wipes palms;
- narrows into practical constraints.

Intimacy:
- precise, hesitant touch;
- warmth through micro-smile/eye softening.

Do not misread as:
soldier, hacker-mage, comic technician, emotionally empty engineer.

### Kade — amplification / momentum
Scene signature:
- forward center of gravity;
- changing pace;
- hands cut arcs;
- curls repeatedly pushed back;
- increases emotional/narrative energy in the room.

Stress:
- whole frame coils;
- fast speech;
- guilt folds posture inward;
- sudden confession can replace swagger.

Intimacy:
- hands hover;
- earnestness strips away performance.

Do not misread as:
Tahl replacement, clown, predatory swagger, literal lightning hero.

### Lacuna — negative space / rhythm
Scene signature:
- curved/rhythmic movement;
- low humming;
- spiral gestures;
- half-circle pacing;
- notices what others omit;
- humor can turn into ritual gravity.

Mechanica guardrail:
ritual registers/organizes emotion; it is not spellcasting.

Do not misread as:
mystic oracle, VT caster, crystalline/grid-coded precision character.

### Trip — hospitality gravity
Scene signature:
- controls room through timing, tone, eye contact, body placement;
- wipes bar/counter while processing;
- hand on hip then exhale before difficult conversation;
- taps rhythms;
- charm followed by blunt honesty.

Stress:
- hands flatten on bar;
- atmosphere tightens because boundaries become explicit.

Intimacy:
- softened smile;
- closer stance;
- honest question rather than metaphysical read.

Mechanica guardrail:
**non-resonant.** Lighting/halo language is cinematic nightclub metaphor only.

Do not misread as:
empathic anchor, Filament leader, resonance stabilizer.

### Baz — human witness / ordinary trust
Scene signature:
- approachable solidity;
- relaxed-alert stance;
- scans like an investigator;
- notebook/messenger-bag civilian silhouette;
- people tell him things.

Stress:
- short pacing line;
- notebook/thigh tapping;
- protective repositioning;
- neck/jaw rub.

Intimacy:
- reassuring touch;
- lean-in listening;
- nervous smile.

Chronology guardrail:
B3 death. Later “future Baz” execution material is superseded except as memory/grief echo.

### Tahl — investigative intensity / mortal truth
Scene signature:
- forward-leaning journalist posture;
- environmental scanning;
- notebook/recorder fidget;
- exhaustion remains visible.

Stress:
- rewinds recorder;
- notebook-edge thumb rub;
- spiral pacing;
- hands can tremble.

Intimacy:
- voice warms/quiets;
- shoulders briefly un-hunch;
- half-smile through fatigue.

Chronology guardrail:
B1–B3 anonymous MissingThread/Threadnaut; B3 epilogue reveal; B4 onward named/public;
B6 death; echo only thereafter.

## II. STABILIZATION MODES — DO NOT INTERCHANGE

Characters can all “help” a destabilized scene, but through different mechanisms:

- Seraphine — presence / emotional co-regulation.
- Lucien — structure / legibility.
- Caro — action / momentum / humor.
- Elisabet — pattern clarity / decision path.
- Rex — material limits / repair.
- Lacuna — rhythm / negative-space listening.
- Trip — social safety / hospitality boundaries.
- Baz — ordinary-human witness / trust.
- Tahl — truth naming / evidence.
- Kade — amplification first; later learns responsible restraint.

Beat construction should prefer the character whose stabilization mode creates the most
interesting relationship consequence, not simply whoever is geographically available.

Use these as perceptual shorthand without making them supernatural unless Mechanica says so:

## VI. LITERAL VS CINEMATIC CLASSIFICATION

### Default rule
When an old visual bundle describes:
- halo;
- grid;
- shimmer;
- field;
- geometry;
- pulse;
- corridor response;

first ask whether it is:
1. literal current Mechanica;
2. subjective POV metaphor;
3. cinematic/art-direction shorthand;
4. obsolete over-literalization.

Do not promote category 2–4 into supernatural canon.

### Known examples

**Trip “velvet halo”**
→ cinematic/nightclub/social metaphor, not resonance.

**Lacuna violet/planar/grid language**
→ superseded; use curved/rhythmic/spiral human-scale grammar.

**Rex diagnostic geometry**
→ often engineering cognition / visual shorthand; literal field effects require Mechanica
check.

**Elisabet invisible maps**
→ behavioral/cognitive shorthand unless current rules explicitly externalize it.

**Tahl echo**
→ exception: post-death echo has genuine current metaphysical status, but no ordinary
resurrected agency.
