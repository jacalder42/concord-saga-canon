## Precedence principle

A source's title (`Final Canon`, `Locked`, etc.) is evidence of its authority at the time it was created, but not immunity from later revision. Prefer later explicit author-approved material and developed GitHub canon over earlier Notion when they conflict. Incomplete GitHub TODO/scaffold content does not erase richer approved material that simply failed to migrate.

## Future-source handling rule

Whenever a newly recovered artifact conflicts with this ledger:
1. identify its date/sequence if possible;
2. identify whether it records author decision, assistant proposal, archive copy, or later reconstruction;
3. preserve both versions;
4. mark supersession explicitly rather than deleting history;
5. ask for author decision only where chronology/evidence cannot settle it.
