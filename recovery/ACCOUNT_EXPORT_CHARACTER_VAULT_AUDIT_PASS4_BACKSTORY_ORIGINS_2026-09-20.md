story.

him is not metaphysics but **truth delivered without spectacle**.

He initially misreads MT as:
- a global truth movement;
- a journalistic network;
- a moral project;
- a resistance identity.

But MT is actually a mortal communications survival strategy. Kade projects a larger
identity onto it.

His relationship to Tahl begins **parasocially**:
- Tahl is the journalist Kade wishes he could be;
- Tahl becomes a symbolic north star;
- Kade loves Tahl's work/symbol before he knows Tahl as a person.

The source gives the attachment real cost:
- local credibility erodes;
- freelance/editor relationships suffer;
- a mentor warns him away;
- family conflict increases;
- friendships fray;
- Kade places hope and self-redemption into Tahl/MT.

This is excellent causal architecture for:
**admiration → over-identification → Tahl's death → destabilization → Elias exploitation.**

The durable insight:
> The hole Tahl filled becomes the crack Elias exploits.

## IX. Character-layer architecture result

This pass reinforces the need to keep five layers distinct:

1. **Identity canon** — origin, family, role, psychological invariants.
2. **Relationship canon** — pairwise bonds and durable interpersonal functions.
3. **Temporal implementation** — when identities/reveals/deaths actually occur.
4. **Execution grammar** — posture, stress, intimacy, room effects, scene behavior.
5. **Narrative assets** — causal chains, costs, scene premises, setups/payoffs.

The old Vault often stored all five together. Compression moved mostly layer 1 into Tier-1
and lost substantial value from layers 2–5.

Recovery should restore those layers without bloating ID files.
