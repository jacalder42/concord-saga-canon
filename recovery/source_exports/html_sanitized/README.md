## Why sanitized copies instead of raw browser exports

The recovery copies therefore preserve the rendered conversation turns while removing scripts, application chrome, session/auth material, and unrelated assets.

Do **not** treat sanitization as editorial revision of the conversation. It is a security/archival cleanup step.
