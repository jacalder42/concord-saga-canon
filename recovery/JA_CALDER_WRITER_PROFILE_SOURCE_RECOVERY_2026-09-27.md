## 2. The core sources

### 2.1 The two voice files (AD)

**Uploaded 2025-11-17** as *"JA Calder voice test.docx"* and *"JA Calder.docx"*: upload message at
`EXP/2025-11-17__Initial_editorial_board__691b69e3.md` l.21205. **Their full text survives only in the
`.json`**, in `tool` messages, because the markdown export drops tool messages.

**Provenance.** Both are ChatGPT output (*"Awesome—here's a tight, copy-pasteable Calder Voice Integration
Brief…"*; *"Proceed?"*), made in a chat outside the Concord project and adopted by the author as his
voice files.

**The Integration Brief (voice test):**
- *"Essence: Adaptive precision, humor as honesty, function over rule. Core maxim: Engagement > certainty.
  Authenticity > decorum. Consequences > dogma."*
- *"Cadence: alternate long "architectural" sentences with clipped, conversational rejoinders.
  Overload → Release: one dense paragraph of spiraling thought → one blunt, decisive line/action. Awe:
  slow, geometric imagery; verbs of transformation > adjectives."*
- *"Never sermonize—stage reasoned dialogue or consequence."*
- *"Permit one surgical expletive where it's true."*
- *"After any sharp line, add a humanizing beat (breath, glance, touch, wry thought)."*
- *"Don't pad with abstract moralizing, empty aphorisms, or pretty-but-pointless metaphors."*
- *"Shock: short sentence + white space + grounding line."*
- *"Anger: tighten syntax; let precision do the shouting."*
- *"Joy: quick beats, imperfect timing, motion."*

**The Compendium (JA Calder.docx), sections I–V:**
- *"Humor must feel earned, spontaneous, and local to the emotional pressure in the scene. When in doubt,
  one precise expletive is better than three clever quips."*
- *"Dialogue and narration should balance energy, not mirror emotion. When one character burns hot,
  Calder's voice cools; when someone withdraws, it leans forward."*
- *"Show feeling through rhythm, image, and choice; never through declaration."*
- *"Avoid melodrama. Let one true sensory detail carry the emotional load."*
- *"Never call it beautiful—show how it functions or resonates."*
- *"Treat paralysis as the inhale; "fuck it" is the exhale that restarts the scene's heartbeat."*
- *"The "fuck it" line is never nihilism; it's confidence in adaptability. Keep it brief, blunt, and
  earned."*
- *"Shock to wake, not to wound."*
- *"Avoid omniscience; favor discovery."*
- *"Let opposing tones co-exist in close proximity: sarcasm followed by sincerity, data beside
  metaphor."*
- *"No decoration without structure."*
- *"Treat every reader like a collaborator, not an audience."*
- *"Design until it works; then write until it feels."*
- The Anti-Calder profile, *"Strip humor, contractions, and conditional language. Replace "maybe" with
  "must.""*, is **the register of rigid antagonists**, not the narrator's.
