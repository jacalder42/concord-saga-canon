## 4. Flattening, and ties little used

**Where old development collapsed the geography:**

1. **The approved Notion Book 6 is set in NOLA** (*"The entire city becomes unstable"*; *"NOLA stages
   a massive jazz funeral"*, `NB` 103879, 103966). Layer 5 moved it to Santa Fe, but the character
   beats were never re-derived.
2. **The MDR Neon act bibles place every Neon scene in NOLA.** That includes Rex (Technarc runs
   Protocol 9 locally), Tahl and Kade.
3. **BB's Veil Books 2–3 turn Honey Island into the default site.** They send the group through
   Baton Rouge and the Pearl River, and Baz dies in *"The Rupture at Honey Island"*. The word
   "Warehouse" never appears in BB.
4. **The assistant's *"Tahl: Home base New Orleans … Never leaves Louisiana"***, which the author did
   not accept.
5. **The NS C4 "Final Canon Card" chain** moves "the group", Rex included, together: Atlanta in B04
   A3, then NOLA, then a rupture site, then Santa Fe, then a Santa Fe collapse in B07. The same
   chat's later Layer 5 and Layer 6 maps put Rex back in Singapore. **Flattening and reinstatement
   happened inside one chat.**
6. **Caro's identity collapsed twice:** into Louisiana (the Lafourche sheet), and into Elisabet
   (*"almost reads like Elisabet got double applied"*, author, `V` 9160).
7. **Every approved Neon bible pairs Caro and Elisabet in one place**, against the cast-separation
   ruling.

**Author caveat, 2026-09-26:** *"Previous chats had flatten the saga location environments."* The
approved Layer 5 and Layer 6 routes may themselves reflect it.
