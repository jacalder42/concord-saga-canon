## Executive finding

The author recollection is strongly supported.

The recovered antagonist architecture explicitly describes early Dominions as old geopolitical blocs organized around bloodline/heritage resonance and explicitly lists:
- Witchlight hierarchies (Seraphine);
- **Vampiric lineages (Lucien)**;
- Technarc-adjacent diplomats.

Current canon has removed supernatural species. The surviving institutional descendant of Lucien's vampire-era architecture is the **Dominion / Dominions ecosystem**.

Marcellus Virelli is the strongest recovered antagonist attached to that ecosystem.

This corrects the generic-employer drift in B01 Character Life / Entry / Exit Audit Pass 2.

## 5. Proposed B01 causal architecture

This is the minimum architecture supported by recovery + current author ruling:

1. Dominion or a Dominion-linked research/civic apparatus monitors anomalous Resonance-type signatures/events.
2. Lucien has a legitimate professional function in that apparatus or an affiliated bureau.
3. NOLA / the swamp event creates a defensible reason for Lucien to be dispatched or tasked.
4. Lucien investigates the event and contacts/interviews Seraphine because of her direct involvement.
5. Lucien initially approaches the problem through structure, classification and containment.
6. As events exceed his model and mandate, he personally calls Baz.
7. Baz is not a random outside friend: he has prior professional history with Lucien and enough institutional literacy/access to help.
8. Baz's arrival broadens the inquiry from structural anomaly toward witness, context, information flow and institutional contradiction.
9. Dominion pressure can therefore enter B01 organically through the protagonists' own reporting/authorization chain rather than through an unrelated villain arrival.
10. Virelli can remain distant/approaching authority in B01. His institution reaches the story before he needs to physically enter NOLA.

## 6. Why this improves Virelli

Virelli becomes more than “Faction Villain #1.”

He represents:
- the institution that helped form Lucien;
- the inherited worldview Lucien partially shares but must ethically outgrow;
- the authority that can plausibly constrain Lucien's work;
- the purity/lineage logic descended from the old vampire architecture;
- the temptation to treat Resonance instability as a classification/control problem.

Current Virelli EBCI already says he views Lucien as a **corrupted intellect** and attempts rhetorical containment.

Thus the antagonism is partly:
**“You belong to what made you.”**

## 7. B01 reveal discipline

Do not explain Dominion history in B01.

Reader-facing progression should be approximately:

EARLY:
Lucien has credentials, a mandate and institutional habits.

MIDDLE:
Baz's arrival shows this is an existing professional relationship, not improvised amateur investigation.

LATER:
requests, redactions, reporting requirements, coded warnings or access friction suggest that the institution cares about controlling information as much as understanding events.

VEIL TRILOGY:
the reader gradually understands that Dominion's concern with order, heritage and legitimacy is ideological rather than merely bureaucratic.

Do not frontload:
- vampire ancestry;
- bloodline exposition;
- global Dominion history;
- Virelli biography;
- anti-hybrid doctrine in lecture form.

The vampire layer is archaeological provenance, not current-world lore.

### Baz

His mundane professional life should preserve lower-status / human-scale contrast:

Their comedy can therefore be partly occupational:
**Lucien knows the official process; Baz knows how the process actually works.**
