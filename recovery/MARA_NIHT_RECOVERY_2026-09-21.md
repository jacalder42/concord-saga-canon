## 2. Recovered — artist identity

- **Name.** `Mara Niht` is a deliberate selection, not a spelling accident. The source
  weighs `Mara of the Niht`, `Mara of Night`, `Niht Mara`, `MaraNIHT`, `Mara and the NIHT`
  and `NihtMara` on mystique against searchability, and lands on **`Mara Niht`** —
  *"short, spellable, searchable, keeps the archaic myth edge."* This independently
  corroborates `SECONDARY_TERTIARY_CHARACTER_AUDIT_PASS2`'s *"Author ruling fixes
  spelling/name: Mara Niht."*
- **Derivation.** The name descends from *Night-Mare / Night-Rider* — the folkloric
  night-mare, not the horse. Earlier persona headings read `MARA (Nightweight)` and
  `MARA (Night-Rider)`.
- **Genre.** Dark bubble grunge × Nordic club electronica. 90–115 BPM.
- **Voice.** *"Feral, confident, predatory-cute, emotionally awake. Power is assumed. No
  apology. No explanation. No victim posture."*
- **Emotional engine.** Curiosity, desire, defiance, connection, wonder. Failure is
  feedback, not shame.
- **Enemy states.** Apathy, fake sweetness, irony-without-heart, performative angst,
  empty nihilism.
- **Iceland coding.** Substantial and deliberate — Icelandic slang hooks, Nordic folklore
  motifs, *"Reykjavík harbor at night, fog, sodium-gold streetlight."* The source also
  says *"content palette (urban, not Iceland)"*, so the coding is **aesthetic**, not a
  stated nationality.

## 5. The source body is NOT in this repository, deliberately

`CLAUDE.md` §1: *"No prose, scene text, or dialogue is stored here. If a task would put
narrative prose in this repo, stop and say so instead."*

**513 lyric blocks across 108,378 words is exactly that.** The `.md` is 680 KB and the
`.json` 1.7 MB. Neither is committed.

**This collides with §7**, which says recovered source material is irreplaceable and
should be committed before transforming, so the raw form survives in history. Both rules
are standing and both apply to this file. **§1 resolves the tie by instructing a stop, so
the stop is what happened** — but the §7 concern is real and unmet: the only copies are
the author's.

**For the author**, three options, none taken here:
1. Keep the raw files in the music project's own repository and cite them from here.
2. Rule a narrow exception admitting recovered source bodies under `recovery/` even when
   they are prose, on the §7 argument.
3. Store elsewhere and record the location.

**The `.json` also carries a `workspace_account_id`.** It is deliberately not reproduced
anywhere in this repository.

**Worth noting for work-queue item 6:** this export **retains its conversation id**, which
the 21 sanitized exports do not — §8 item 6 records that *"sanitization stripped every
conversation ID, so the exports cannot be mined for more."* This file demonstrates an
export path that preserves them.
