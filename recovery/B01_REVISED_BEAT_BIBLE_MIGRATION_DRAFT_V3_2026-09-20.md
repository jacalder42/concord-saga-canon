## Governing decisions

1. Preserve the recovered causal spine.
2. Preserve all protected B1 anchors.
3. Compress repeated interpretation/escalation beats, not relationship or causal changes.
4. Retire formal “micro-Shard” language from B1 pending author/rules amendment.
5. Use Flicker/Ghostwave/residue/pressure-zone language only where behavior warrants it.
6. Restore Caro/Elisabet's B1 relationship state: **recognition / instinctive safety**.
7. Establish Velvet Vein/Trip as ordinary-life infrastructure.
8. Prefer recurring NOLA faces where old utility roles duplicate them.
9. Keep Filaments human-scale.
10. Keep institutions mostly as footprints, not front-line villain parade.
11. Apply the recovered Möbius mechanic: recurring elements return transformed or inverted, not merely repeated.
12. Protect the B1 miniature of Seraphine's saga loop: **too late → temptation to act too early → timing/restraint**.
13. Modulate pressure by changing the *kind* of pressure, not merely its intensity.
14. Hand B2 social conditions and questions, not B2 answers: fragmented public language, changed habits, institutional collection, and no confirmed global extent.

# PROLOGUE — THE CONVERSATION IN THE SKY
Source: old E00

### Story job
Open the saga's metaphysical horizon without explaining it.

### Beats
- Silence and Hope register strain in the Veil.
- They can perceive pressure but cannot simply intervene or dictate mortal outcomes.
- Images/sensations foreshadow human-scale instability without prophetic scene previews.
- The emphasis is constraint: something larger is changing, and even entities with a wider
  view do not possess clean control.

### Execution
Keep abstract, brief and beautiful.
No cosmology lecture.
No literal future-vision montage.

### Möbius seed
Silence/Hope begin as distance, constraint and incomplete communication. Do not explain enough to prevent later saga returns from changing the meaning of this scene.

### Exit
Cut hard from metaphysical distance to a sick child in the swamp.

# ACT I — DISCOVERY BECOMES PATTERN

## 1. THE SICK CHILD
Source: E01

### Story job
Give the mystery a human cost before explanation.

### Beats
- Seraphine reaches a family in the swamp during a medical/care crisis.
- She does everything she reasonably can.
- Subtle wrongness interferes with her confidence in what she is perceiving.
- The child dies.
- Seraphine remains with the family rather than fleeing into investigation.
- Keep the swamp materially specific—people, weather, ground, sounds, practical details—without mythologizing it. Book 9 must be able to return to a remembered human place, not a generic sacred site.

### Character
Seraphine's foundational wound:
**failure to save → responsibility to understand.**

Her dangerous private answer begins here: **next time, arrive sooner / move faster / carry more.** B1 must challenge that answer without curing it.

### Mechanics
No Shard terminology.
Any anomaly remains low-amplitude/ambiguous.

## 36. THE PREDICTED QUIET PULSE
Source: E41 + E42 — PROTECTED PAYOFF / compressed close

### Story job
Prove prediction is possible without pretending explanation/control is complete.

### Möbius inversion
Jackson Square's first major pulse was **shock without understanding**. This final event should be **understanding without control**—quieter, more legible and more unsettling because the team was substantially right.

### Beats
- The predicted event arrives close enough to expectation to falsify “random,” while timing/expression retains enough uncertainty to prevent prediction from becoming authority.
- It is quieter/stranger than prior pulses rather than simply larger.
- The team observes more than intervenes.
- The city bears marks through changed routines, records, damage and memory—not magical
  scar VFX unless Mechanica supports a specific effect.
- Baz argues the next step must widen the investigation.
- Seraphine accepts that presence is not control, but has **not** mastered boundaries; under pressure she will still default toward over-responsibility.
- Lucien accepts that a usable model can remain incomplete, but still believes a better structure may eventually resolve the uncertainty.
- Give Lucien a tiny behavioral callback to his opening line-checking: he notices an imperfect line/measurement and does **not** need to correct or re-check it immediately.
- Ordinary NOLA life resumes around a changed baseline.
- Make that baseline concrete through one or two changed habits rather than exposition: a route avoided, altered closing practice, neighbor check-in ritual, backup supplies, or another partly-right folk adaptation.
- Public language remains fragmented. Different people call events different things; B1 does **not** achieve consensus around “pulse.”
- Institutional traces show watching/collecting—requests, records access, quiet observation or classification—but no open Dominion/Technarc confrontation.
- The cast may suspect NOLA is not unique, but no outside-city report is credible enough to confirm it. Marrakesh/Naima retains B2's first credible global-confirmation function.
- Velvet Vein/Trip may provide a brief social afterimage: people are already telling the
  story differently. This is **Velvet Vein Möbius state 3 — continuity room**: the room remains itself while the city's stories have changed.
- The trio chooses to continue.

### Book-end state
**The system is patterned.  
The city knows something is happening.  
Nobody knows enough.**

### B2 handoff
Public language is ready to emerge but remains unresolved.
Institutional attention is already present as observation/collection, not open confrontation.
Ordinary people have begun adapting their habits.
Global confirmation remains unverified, so Marrakesh can genuinely change the model.
Seraphine and Lucien carry partial gains rather than completed lessons.

# CHARACTER STATE AT BOOK END

## Seraphine
Begins:
**If I care enough, I should be able to save/fix this.**

Ends:
