## Executive finding

Book 1 Act III is structurally strong but is the first section where **compression is
probably necessary**.

Recovered sequence:

**Long Hum → scattered disturbances → harmonic mapping → civic systems fracture → public
gathering loses cohesion → shaken aftermath → Filament grounding fails → rebound pulse →
Wide Quiet → Square Remembers / synchronization → predicted quiet pulse → aftermath /
Book 2 decision.**

All twelve episodes contain useful material. However, unlike Acts I–II, several consecutive
episodes perform overlapping escalation or aftermath functions.

Recommended eventual shape:
- preserve all major events;
- consider combining some episode-scale units during welding;
- do not preserve twelve episodes merely because the old expansion generated twelve.

The strongest candidates for compression are:
- E34 + E35 as one civic-fracture sequence if pacing needs tightening;
- E36 as aftermath bridge folded partly into E37;
- E41 + E42 as prediction/payoff + book-close sequence if separate episodes feel too
  attenuated.

Do **not** merge automatically. First preserve all recovered beats.

# I. E31 — “The Long Hum”

**Disposition: KEEP.**

### Function
Act III opens with the city no longer experiencing isolated anomalies. A persistent hum
becomes the connective tissue among multiple disturbances.

### Preserve
- morning wrongness;
- trio checking one another;
- Quarter / residential / riverfront movement;
- multiple small incidents rather than one spectacle;
- realization that the city itself is joining the pattern.

### Strength
This is a clean act-opening thesis:
**the anomaly is becoming environmental/social rather than episodic.**

### Reconcile
“Emotional bloom,” visible color, riverfront distortion and group micro-stabilization must
be separated into:
- current literal Mechanica;
- subjective/cinematic shorthand;
- ordinary crowd behavior.

# VII. E37 — “Filament Grounding Fails”

**Disposition: KEEP FUNCTION / REBUILD FILAMENT IMPLEMENTATION.**

### Major durable beat
The care network's familiar stabilization practices stop being sufficient.

That is an important trilogy escalation.

### Old implementation problem
The source describes:
- coordinated three-block “grounding ritual”;
- Filaments at cross-streets forming an alignment grid;
- colored arcs;
- synchronized breath;
- shockwaves;
- Rootkeeper as quasi-commanding elder.

This risks making Filaments feel like a fantasy magical order, directly against current
architecture.

### Current rebuild principle
Filaments are:
**decentralized human-scale resonance stewards — Care Through Presence.**

Their failure should therefore feel like:
- trained human practices stop working reliably;
- people cannot co-regulate under new pressure;
- local networks become overwhelmed;
- coordination itself begins amplifying distress.

Not:
a magical shield spell breaking.

### Rootkeeper
Keep local elder presence if useful.
Do not turn Rootkeeper into the commander of NOLA Filaments.

### Supporting cast
This is where Mara/M, Arianna, Pivot or other protected Filament characters may carry
specific human functions once provenance is checked.

# VIII. E38 — “The Rebound Pulse”

**Disposition: KEEP / MAJOR MECHANICS RECONCILIATION.**

### Preserve
- failed stabilization contributes to an unexpected rebound;
- event is short, dangerous and qualitatively new;
- Filaments overwhelmed;
- public panic;
- Seraphine anchors Lucien;
- pulse ends abruptly;
- city has crossed a threshold.

### Important causal caution
The old text can read as though **Filament grounding causes the pulse**.

Current Mechanica review should decide whether:
- their attempt actually amplifies an unstable system;
- they merely fail at the same moment a rebound emerges;
- “rebound” is later taxonomy for a system event independent of their intervention.

Do not assign moral/causal blame to Filaments accidentally.

### Relationship asset
Seraphine anchoring Lucien is a useful payoff to their Act I–II mutual-grounding arc.

# IX. E39 — “The Wide Quiet”

**Disposition: KEEP / PROTECT.**

This is one of Book 1's strongest atmospheric episodes.

### Preserve
- city becomes deeply quiet after the rebound;
- ordinary voices/noise disappear;
- trio walks rather than fights;
- each tries to understand absence;
- small disturbance proves pressure remains;
- quiet feels anticipatory rather than peaceful.

### Why protect it
After multiple crisis episodes, the book needs negative space.

Do **not** compress this merely because it is quiet.

### Art-direction rule
Minimalism is stronger than VFX.
Let ordinary city silence carry the uncanny effect.

- E35 = public cohesion fails;

- E38 = system rebounds.

If those distinctions are vivid, all five can survive.
If not, compress.
