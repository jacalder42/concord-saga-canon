## 8. Contradictions between passages

1. **Protocol 9: owner and timing.** Technarch in B03 A1 (NB 4995), *Dominion* in B03 A1 (NS 211499); launched in B04 E7 (NB 111539) and again in B05 (NS 23913); B04 has "Protocol 7" (NS 25086); 9.1 in B03 A2 (NB 5223) and in Loom (NS 2982).

3. **Loom's de facto governance.** Concord federation plus Knots (PRG 12632, 14485), the "Firewall Team" (NS 185160), or Concord "fractures" in B08 (NS 182594).
4. **Who imposes curfews and martial law in Loom.** Local government (NS 134334), Technarch remnants (NS 150792; NB 10428), Choirless militias (NS 182699) or "failed government clusters" (NS 323).
5. **Curfew onset.** Civic curfews already in B03 (NB 4995) against curfews only "proposed informally" in B05 (NS 25465). PRG 12209 puts them "in Veil/early Neon".

8. **The epilogue's interval.** The "Six Months After the Mending" sidebar (NB 113900s) conflicts with the ruled few-day timeskip (D10).
9. **Technarch after the Mending.** "Reduced/regulated" (PRG 14485), "technocracy reborn" (NS 185240) and "Collapsed" (NB 113600s).
10. **B08 geography.** The global B08 civic clusters (NS 44318–44600) predate the ruled Santa Fe → Mound City B08.
