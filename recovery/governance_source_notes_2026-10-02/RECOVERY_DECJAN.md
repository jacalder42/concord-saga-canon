**F6. Dominion institutional mechanics** (CV; assistant; *Proceed* or letters).
- **How power is exercised:**
  - *Containment Recommendations* are advisories, not orders. "Local governments implement these recommendations voluntarily to appear 'responsible'" (52270–52278).
  - The surface layer is ministries, ethics boards and "stability commissions" that never use the name (52161–52190).
  - In Veil they "direct governments to minimize emotional knowledge" and "pressure local authorities in NOLA" (26490–26500).
- **Enforcement bodies:**
  - The **Regulatory Arm** ("not soldiers, not police. Gatekeepers") issues containment orders, restricts the movement of resonance-active people and monitors hybrid births. In Veil it blocks Seraphine's care and flags Baz as "risk-amplifying". In Neon it "lose[s] jurisdiction to Technarc" (55057–55082).
  - **Quietwardens** are "civil administrators with extreme authority" (26460–26470).
- **Field protocols** (57460–57545):
  - *Courtesy Veil*, *Containment Circle* and *Stabilization Narrative* ("The situation is under review").
  - **Directive 14**: relocation to "evaluation facilities", redacted public reports, "legal silence on witnesses".
  - *Quiet Removal*.
- **Regions:**
  - Provincial Regencies, including the **U.S. Northeast**, implement Dominion-aligned law without naming its origin. A Regent is a "governor equivalent" (55474–55530).
  - An **Americas Dominion** in the Northeast and D.C. "quietly influences Chicago, early Santa Fe policy" (35295–35300).
- **Factions and fates** (52663–52930):
  - **Pragmatics**, the "Civic Stewards", are the backbone of bureaucratic governance in Veil. In Loom many defect to Concord-adjacent civic survival groups, and post-Mending they join local recovery councils.
  - The **Technocrats** are, post-Mending, "some prosecuted".
