## Executive finding
Rex is already migrated into Tier-1 GitHub canon, and the migration is a deliberate narrowing/refinement of the older “Rational Thread / Technarch defector / post-Mending leader” package.

**physical constraint anchor; systems reality enforcer; limits-as-care.**

### VEIL — constraint voice at margins
- systems mostly work;
- Rex's warnings often seem overly cautious because redundancy still exists;
- former Technarc proximity gives him access/context;
- Warehouse becomes proof that optimization + secrecy can kill.
- supporting role, not core lead.

### NEON — repair loses the race
- infrastructure failures accelerate faster than repair;
- manufactured metas / containment systems expose certainty failure;
- Rex increasingly chooses people over system preservation;
- mechanical/civic triage becomes more important;
- cost = exhaustion and guilt, not “logic learns emotions exist.”

### LOOM — protect people, not systems
- collapse exceeds engineering solution;
- Rex stops trying to save every system;
- protects survivable corridors, equipment, evacuation, rear guard;
- limits become strategic reality for others without making Rex strategist.
- B9 rear guard with Lacuna + Filaments is an ideal expression.

### Post-Mending
- repair culture;
- resilient, human-scale infrastructure;
- training and standards with margin;
- reliability as hope.
- no automatic governance office or global leadership role.
