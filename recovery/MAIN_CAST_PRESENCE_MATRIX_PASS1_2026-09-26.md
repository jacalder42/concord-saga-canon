# Main cast physical presence matrix — Pass 1

**Date:** 2026-09-26
**Status:** RECOVERY / NON-CANONICAL. A **source-first forensic record** of where the principal cast
is **physically** located across Books 1–9: first by book, then by act where the evidence allows.
**It designs no itinerary, assigns no wound teams, invents no travel, and changes no canon, rule,
grid row or book context.** Unknown cells stay `?`; that is the finding, not a gap to fill.

**Asked:** the author, *"Proceed with location reconciliation"*, supplying ChatGPT's brief *"CONCORD
SAGA — MAIN CAST PHYSICAL PRESENCE FORENSIC MATRIX"*. Its governing principle: **follow the people,
not the cities.** The next step (a separate one, for ChatGPT) is editorial: where the characters
*should* be.

**Companions:**

- [`MAIN_CAST_ROUTE_LEDGER_PASS1_2026-09-26.md`](MAIN_CAST_ROUTE_LEDGER_PASS1_2026-09-26.md): each
  character's route, with the evidence.

  contradictions, unknowns, absences, flattening, and the handoff summary.

**Method:** four read-only research passes, grouped by character, worked from one brief. Claude
re-checked each key quotation and speaker against the export; several line references were
corrected by one to three lines.
