## 2. Which continuations are promising without inventing a new manifestation?

| Carry-forward track | Specific old fact | Candidate human/civic change later | What would falsify the connection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Evidence custody** | B1/B2 recordings or reports; B3 mapping; B5 warning | Records gain/lose credibility or access; decisions about evacuation and MT verification. | Earlier records never survive, cannot be compared or were not seen by the later decision maker. |
| **Institutional receipts** | Dominion/Technarc restrictions; B5 response loss; B6 meta failure | A named official or technician exposes, withholds, misreads or repairs a specific system. | No direct organizational continuity, no authority left, or the purported device never existed at that site. |
| **Place and repair** | B3 Warehouse damage; B5 displaced site; B6 Santa Fe fracture | Return, occupation, partial repair, departure or stranded survivors alter a local community's options. | Site no longer damaged or inaccessible in the relevant period; route and people do not overlap. |
| **People as carriers** | Baz's survivors; Tahl's readers; Santa Fe evacuees; manufactured-meta person | A survivor's testimony or changed loyalty produces later stakes without a new storm. | Person's location, custody, agency or timeline makes the meeting impossible. |
| **Practices under load** | B4 two different stabilizations; B6 finite field rescue | Filament method is modified, rejected or taught, with a bounded outcome. | The earlier targets/techniques were unlike the later case or never recorded. |

This supports the author's request for **a web rather than evenly spread spectacle**: a quiet records dispute or refugee decision can inherit consequence from a huge previous event while occurring in a different city. No link requires all roads to reach Honey Island.

## 3. Highest-value source tasks before adding slots

1. **B3 Warehouse aftermath:** recover who knew of Baz's death when; actual Warehouse structural condition after B3; any B4/B7 independent repair/occupation records. This adjudicates FUP-06 and prevents a duplicate catastrophe.
2. **B5 displaced people into Loom:** find an identifiable site, household, camp or crew in November Notion/December export and see whether its fate continues into B6/B7. Without a named carrier FUP-11 is thematic, not causal.
3. **B5 warning reception:** source-check a local Santa Fe recipient/decision against the [empty observation schema](B05_ELISABET_TAHL_WARNING_EVIDENCE_LEDGER_PASS1_2026-09-23.md). Keep Tahl's message honest about uncertainty.
4. **B6→B7 custody:** recover what happens to MT credentials/archives and Tahl's records, which Filament units handle survivors, and who first uses the succession vacuum. This grounds both Kade and Elias without an instant global influence effect.
5. **Meta individual chain:** distinguish an exploited person's first visibility, first failure, rescue/containment and later consequence. Search custody and record holders before staging another assault.
6. **Independent local service cases:** demand outage date, severity, still-operating comparator, source and reporting lag. Technology degradation is scaled; different cities need not have identical effects.

**Disposition:** FUP-06, -10, -12, -14 and -15 have the strongest *existing* later-book junctions; their detailed followup actions are still open. FUP-01 is valuable as a human memory and negative control, not as a hidden supernatural seed. FUP-04/-08/-09 need an actual person, document or site to prove the crossing. Prioritize recoverable continuations over extra milestones; promote only when a later action changes a state.
