1. Presence does not require introduction.
2. More book must not mean more cast.
3. Outside the core trio, generally one primary recurring support face per narrative unit unless the actual relationship requires two.
4. Unnamed locals remain essential.
5. A name appearing only in a supplement does not become recurring cast by default.
6. Life/Reward units may advance relationship or character state but should not become hidden plot-delivery scenes.
7. At least some reward units must accomplish almost nothing for saga causality.
8. Supplements may carry off-page LINK plot but may not steal plot that belongs on-page.
9. WORLDLINE is an editorial job, not a new formal vehicle.
10. No B1 MT under current chronology.
11. Do not interrupt protected pressure windows with supplements.
12. Velvet Vein is hearth/social infrastructure, not anomaly HQ.
13. Lacuna remains human-only, minor/atmospheric in Veil.
