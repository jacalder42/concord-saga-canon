## Executive finding

Book 2 Act I is again structurally recoverable and, importantly, **does not yet contain the
named-Tahl chronology corruption that becomes severe later in Veil**.

Recovered Act-I spine:

**NOLA micro-events accelerate → public names “the pulse” → rumor feeds fear → Marrakesh
confirms global scale → map will not stay local → Dominion warns → Technarc probes →
Seraphine/Lucien encounter stronger localized failures → mobile residue appears →
Technarc parameters surface → institutions go quiet → pressure builds → second major pulse
→ simultaneous global reports → acceleration curve confirmed.**

The main reconciliation needs are:
1. public-rumor episodes E03–E04 may be compressible;
2. Marrakesh should use the recovered **Naima el-Faris** place-anchor architecture rather
   than a generic “elder” if provenance/timing fits;
3. Dominion/Technarc contact should use current institutional logic and avoid premature
   named-villain exposure;
4. Seraphine's old “distance-empathy” and Technarc caller “emotional signature from miles
   away” are over-literalized and need current Mechanica;
5. “echo” vocabulary is overloaded and should not create Tahl-Echo confusion;
6. Witness Elder / generic Filament specialists remain consolidation candidates.

No episode requires mandatory deletion.

# I. E01 — “Hairline Cracks”

**Disposition: KEEP.**

Book 2 correctly begins smaller than Book 1 ended:
the important change is **frequency**, not immediate spectacle.

Preserve:
- morning-after unease;
- many tiny events;
- trio recognizes shorter recovery interval;
- ordinary people beginning to adapt behavior.

Reconcile:
light-shifts / emotional fields / geometric flickers through current Mechanica.

Narrative value:
Book 1 taught them to predict an event. Book 2 immediately shows prediction did not solve
the problem.

Preserve:

# III. E03 — “Someone Said ‘Pulse’”

**Disposition: KEEP / HIGH-VALUE SOCIAL BREADCRUMB.**

This is an excellent public-language milestone.

Preserve:
- protagonists overhear ordinary citizens naming the phenomenon themselves;
- language escapes specialist control;
- fear acquires a shared label;
- public interpretation becomes a new force.

This is an early ancestor of the saga's later concern with narrative ownership.

Trip / Velvet Vein opportunity:
the function is especially compatible with recurring social spaces where language spreads
organically. Do not relocate automatically, but compare against a generic café.

# IV. E04 — “Rumors in the Cafeteria”

**Disposition: KEEP FUNCTION / POSSIBLE MERGE WITH E03.**

The source deliberately escalates:
E03 = word appears;
E04 = rumor ecology forms.

That distinction is useful, but two full episodes may be more than the beat requires.

Preserve:
- contradictory explanations;
- rumor changes behavior;
- Baz cannot separate false claims from real micro-events cleanly;
- Seraphine becomes overloaded by crowd fear;
- small anomaly occurs amid social tension.

Critical mechanics correction:
the old text says Seraphine “feels each rumor sharpen the emotional air” and treats rumor
as a quasi-field event. Current implementation should preserve social contagion without
assuming rumor literally generates resonance.

Potential merge:
one longer public-awareness episode can move:
**first use of pulse → competing explanations → crowd tension → minor event → protagonists
realize narrative itself now matters.**

# V. E05 — “A Call from Marrakesh”

**Disposition: KEEP / PLACE-ANCHOR REBUILD.**

This is the first major cross-city expansion of Book 2.

Preserve:
- a city outside NOLA reports analogous but locally distinct symptoms;
- trio learns global instability is not culturally/geographically uniform;
- local experience is no longer sufficient.

### Important current recovery

The older episode uses a generic **Marrakesh elder**.

Current reconciliation has **Naima el-Faris** as a protected/promoted Marrakesh
place-anchor.

Before prose migration, test whether Naima can naturally own this call or whether the
generic elder serves a different function.

Preference:
if Naima's recovered role fits, reuse her rather than inventing a one-scene Marrakesh
oracle/elder.

### Guardrail

Avoid “regional resonance dialect” becoming mystical ethnic essentialism.
The durable concept is:
**same systemic pressure expressed through different built/social/cultural conditions.**

# VI. E06 — “Threads in the Map”

**Disposition: KEEP / MAJOR MECHANICS CLEANUP.**

Excellent investigative function:
- NOLA + Marrakesh data;
- Baz maps;
- Seraphine contributes human reports;
- Lucien sees structural relationships;
- global pattern emerges;
- map refuses a simple stable explanation.

Problematic old implementation:
- Seraphine “feels distance resonance”;
- paper curls in response to data;
- map physically distorts;
- world responds to realization.

Those are prime Art-Canon-metaphor candidates.

Keep the **cognitive discovery**, not necessarily supernatural office supplies.

# VII. E07 — “The Letter with No Seal”

**Disposition: KEEP / STRONG DOMINION FOOTPRINT.**

One of the cleaner institutional beats.

Preserve:

- no sender;
- implication of old authority;
- Baz understands enough to know it is not casual;
- threat is restraint/classification rather than attack.

This fits current Dominion:
**Order as Mercy / institutional preservation / authority.**

Do not name Virelli merely to make the threat bigger.
An impersonal institution may be more effective here.

# VIII. E08 — “Formal Dissonance”

**Disposition: KEEP FUNCTION / REBUILD TECHNARC CONTACT.**

The structural contrast with E07 is excellent:

Dominion sends a warning.
Technarc requests data.

That cleanly differentiates:
- authority/containment;
- prediction/classification.

### Major old-mechanics problem

The source says Seraphine can feel the remote caller's emotional texture and even a “flick
of irritation from miles away.”

That should **not** migrate without explicit current Mechanica support.

Better current pressure:
- cadence too measured;
- questions too informed;
- bureaucratic euphemism;
- caller knows data they should not possess;
- social/linguistic absence of ordinary concern.

Seraphine can read the conversation as a person without supernatural telephone empathy.

### Duplicate-source note

The export contains two Episode 8 headers/versions.
Treat as same development slot, not two episodes.

# XI. E11 — “A House Full of Echo”

**Disposition: KEEP FUNCTION / TERMINOLOGY REVIEW.**

Preserve:
- ordinary house contains accumulated emotional residue;
- protagonists enter carefully;
- multiple past emotional events seem layered;
- Seraphine is overwhelmed;
- Lucien finds spatial pattern;
- Baz asks whether human spaces retain consequences.

This is a good expansion from public pulses into **domestic human cost**.

### Terminology danger

“Echo” is doing too many jobs in the saga:
- residue/imprint;
- echo cluster;
- later formal Tahl Echo.

Recommend reserving capital-E **Echo** for current formal ontology and using:
- residue;
- imprint;
- afterimage;
for B2 phenomena if Mechanica supports them.

### Cultural guardrail

Do not make the house haunted.
This is not ghost fiction.
