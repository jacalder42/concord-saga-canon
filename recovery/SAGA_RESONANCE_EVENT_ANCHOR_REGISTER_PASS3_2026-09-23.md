## Reading discipline

- **Protected function** means a currently supported story occurrence/relationship, not proof of every physical explanation in an older source.
- **Historical candidate** means an earlier scene named in sources, with survival or placement unresolved.
- **Event test** asks whether something objective changes through Resonance. Human decisions, death, migration, grief, publication, investigations and institutional collapse may be consequential **without themselves being Resonance events**.
- Use `EV-` handles as Pass 1 aliases, `GAP-` as Pass 2 triage aliases. Suffixes such as `EV-B06-01 / meta` below are *review subdivisions*, not minted canonical IDs.
- Authority: explicit author rulings/current B1 architecture > later reconciled evidence > dated author-pasted full export > historical Notion and assistant expansions > proposed M01–M36 grid. The full export was the intact 72-MD/72-JSON archive, not merely sanitized HTML. Macro beats do not constitute measurements.

## Non-events and retired literalizations

**Do not count as objective Resonance merely by wording:** child death/illness; panic, grief, romance, care or moral recognition; MT post; an institution losing credibility; maps narrowing a hypothesis; evacuation or diaspora; Filament ground care without measured field change; attuned perception of a pull; poetic sky, breath or geometric imagery; equipment artifact without an identified exposure and ordinary rival. Such facts can cause or follow real physical events without themselves becoming an event.

**Do not silently restore:** B1 micro-Shards/Neon geometry; B5 first-global NOLA Colorstorm; B6 first failure caused by later Santa Fe tear; B7 supernatural MT bearing; B8 pre-Mending formal Echo Node/effortless repair/worldwide pulse; B9 Kade→Elias attack; LT as the Mending mechanism; globally instantaneous technology death; a zero-cost meta-to-operative boost.
