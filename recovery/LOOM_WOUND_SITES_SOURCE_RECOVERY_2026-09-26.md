## Bottom line

1. **The author saved a four-node "Alignment Chain"**: Santa Fe Scar → Mound City (St. Louis) →
   Serpent Mound → Honey Island. He answered it *"Proceed with save"* (`WB` 128752). **Its order
   fits the 09-26 rulings** (§4).
2. **Honey Island is last because it is the original Tear.** This rests on an approved 11-22
   option (`NB` ~42495–42530, *"First the swamp ending is approved"*): *"The original Tear occurred
   in the swamp"*, and the antagonists *"all assume … NOLA. They are wrong."*
3. **Two 09-26 rulings are new material, not recovered.** First, the crew **stabilises** each site.
   Second, Silence and Hope's hard cap is the reason for the circuit. **No source has either.** In
   every source the crew travels to **investigate and measure**. That is not a conflict: it is the
   author adding to his own design. It is recorded so the source weight is clear.
4. **In the sources, the crew discovers the swamp endpoint late.** The 09-26 rulings have them
   know the pattern, and Tahl's B06-epilogue message gives it to them. **The two reconcile** (§5).

## 5. What the crew knows, and when

**In the sources, the crew discovers the swamp at the end of the chain.** In the 09-26 rulings the
primary protagonists know the pattern, with Honey Island last, and Tahl's B06-epilogue message
tells them.

**READING (reconciliation, not adopted):**

- **Tahl's message gives the pattern:** that there are wounds, and that the swamp comes last.
- **The chain gives the particulars.** Elisabet's mapping, Rex's proof, Seraphine's perception and
  Caro's felt readings locate and verify each site as the crew goes.
- So the crew **knows the shape** from B07 but **finds each node's place and nature** on the road.
  **The sources' discovery beats become confirmation beats**, and B08's *"may be back in
  Louisiana"* remains the **factions'** conclusion (ruled).

**Site-secret risks in the accepted material:** the 11-30 card's B08 *"all factions converging on
the swamp"*, and Serpent Mound's *"multiple factions converge."* **Framing that fits the rulings:**
the factions follow the crew to Serpent Mound and read the vectors as pointing to **Louisiana /
New Orleans**. That is exactly what the feint exploits. They do not learn "the swamp" until B09 A3.
