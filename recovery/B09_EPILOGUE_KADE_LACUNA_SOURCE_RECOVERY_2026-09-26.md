## 2. The recovered choreography, by weight

**The locked sequence (2025-11-29, *Narrative Structure* ~87941–88180, ASSISTANT; locked by the
author at ~88183).** Built from the author's 87933 message:

- Lacuna presses **POST** for Kade.
- She remarks that the stars look chattier. She does not name the Thread.
- His screen flickers; a **"new message request, unknown source"** appears.
- Kade: *"That's odd… what the fuck?"*
- A single **△**, Tahl's MT signature, renders.
- The interface shifts to a **blank `LuminousThread://_` prompt**: *"No instructions. Just
  possibility."*

The assistant framed Tahl's part as a **one-time, will-less echo ping**, *"because Kade is the last
human to share a resonance imprint with him,"* and explicitly **not** as Tahl entering LT
communication with the trio. **The prose is the assistant's; the beats and the lock are the
author's.**

**The days-after skeleton (2025-11-30, ~113472–113670, ASSISTANT; accepted by the author at
~113672, "good with this version too").** This is *"After the Breath"*, set days after the
Mending, in five scenes:

1. Elisabet at the swamp.
2. Elisabet calls Kade, with Lacuna in the background.
3. Rex and Elisabet.
4. Kade and Lacuna on the **Velvet Vein rooftop at night**. She laces her fingers with his and
   taps post; the stars *"sound like they're talking."*
5. *"LuminousThread: 1 Pending Connection"*, with the note **"Tahl is the conduit on Kade's
   side; Seraphine/Lucien/Caro on the other."** Kade accepts: *"Okay. One more thread."*

The author chose closing-line option 2 (~113645). It is prose and is not reproduced here.
