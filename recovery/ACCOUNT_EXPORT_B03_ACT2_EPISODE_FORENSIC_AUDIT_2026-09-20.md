## Executive finding

Book 3 Act II contains the strongest **truth-line / institutional-pressure / pre-Baz-death**
material yet, but its old Tahl implementation must remain transformed throughout.

The durable act function is:

**MissingThread becomes consequential → Baz increasingly trusts the work → institutional
pressure targets information channels → public interpretation fractures → the primary
cast follows converging evidence → danger becomes physical → Baz's human-investigator
role reaches maximum importance → the act drives toward the conditions that make his B3
death consequential rather than arbitrary.**

The old architecture frequently tries to solve this by bringing named Tahl physically into
the investigation.

Current architecture should instead let **Baz and the reader know the work before knowing
the worker**.

This also protects Baz from being narratively displaced before his death.

---

# I. Baz must remain the embodied investigator

This is the most important Act-II correction.

Historical named-Tahl material can accidentally turn B3 into:
**Baz hands investigation to the newer, more interesting investigator, then dies.**

That weakens Baz.

Current preferred structure:
- MissingThread expands the evidence horizon;
- Baz evaluates, challenges and validates it;
- Baz remains the embodied primary investigator inside the core cast;
- the anonymous reporter becomes an external truth-line counterpart;
- Baz's death removes a person the reader has watched doing the human work.

Tahl does not replace Baz before Baz dies.

The later truth lineage works because something is **lost**, not because a baton pass was
already administratively complete.

---

# II. MissingThread escalation

**Disposition: KEEP / TRANSFORM.**

Act II should increase the channel's importance through:
- better sourcing;
- corrections;
- pattern comparisons;
- institutional documents;
- civilian testimony;
- time-stamped predictions or observations;
- publication despite pressure.

Do not increase importance by making MissingThread omniscient.

### Ethical signature to protect
The channel should distinguish:
- observed;
- inferred;
- unverified;
- corrected.

That makes the later Kade fixation understandable:
the authority comes from method, not mystique.

---

# III. Baz ↔ MissingThread relationship

**Disposition: KEEP FUNCTION / PROTECT ANONYMITY.**

Historical direct named-Tahl exchanges should become:
- anonymous correspondence;
- source packets;
- disputed interpretations;
- one side withholding identity;
- Baz testing claims rather than accepting them.

### Relationship progression
1. Baz notices quality.
2. Baz checks a claim.
3. Baz finds an error or overreach.
4. MissingThread corrects publicly or privately.
5. Baz's trust increases because correction occurs.
6. Baz begins sharing limited non-identifying corroboration.
7. Both independently approach the same dangerous truth.

This creates earned professional respect without premature friendship.

### Guardrail
Do not make Baz know Tahl's legal identity unless current epilogue reveal is explicitly
designed as a reveal to the reader but not to Baz. Current default should preserve the
identity reveal broadly.

---

# IV. Institutional pressure becomes narrative pressure

Book 2:
institutions controlled spaces, data and response.

Book 3 Act II:
they increasingly control:
- access;
- records;
- source credibility;
- public explanations;
- platform visibility;
- what counts as “verified.”

### Dominion
Frames restriction as responsibility:
unverified reporting causes panic; sensitive knowledge requires stewardship.

### Technarc
Frames restriction as information hygiene:
bad data contaminates models; unauthorized disclosure reduces predictive reliability.

Both can oppose MissingThread for different reasons.

This is stronger than generic agents chasing a reporter.

- reliable reporter;
- panic account;

This is an ideal **Velvet Vein / Trip** function.

- Shirin checks data;
- Ramon compares infrastructure reports;

This makes public narrative a cast event rather than anonymous social-media montage.

# VI. Anonymous source network

Historical Tahl scenes often perform source-gathering functions.

Transform those into a **network effect without creating a formal organization**.

MissingThread can receive:
- screenshots;
- transit logs;
- medical observations;
- photographs;
- eyewitness notes;
- institutional documents.

Important:
the source network is not automatically “the Threadnauts” as a formal faction.

The public may call followers/contributors something before any organization exists.

This preserves the later ambiguity Kade inherits.

# VII. Physical danger without identity reveal

Old Act II material increases danger by putting named Tahl near events.

Current options:

### If physical body is unnecessary
Use artifact/evidence delivery.

### If physical proximity matters
Show:
- a source packet from somewhere dangerous;
- a photograph taken from a risky position;
- abrupt posting silence;
- damaged/partial recording;
- institutional reaction suggesting someone was physically present.

### If an embodied anonymous encounter is essential
Keep identifying detail minimal and natural.

Do not use costume gimmicks.

The reader should think:
**someone risked themselves for this evidence**
without yet knowing who.

# VIII. Baz danger escalation

Act II should make Baz's eventual death feel causally connected to who he is.

Protect beats where Baz:
- refuses to treat civilians as data;
- goes in person when a model is insufficient;
- checks testimony;
- challenges institutional explanations;
- chooses presence over safe abstraction;
- understands risk but does not become reckless.

His death should not be punishment for stupidity.

The tragedy is:
**the virtues that make Baz trustworthy also keep him close to human consequence.**

---

# IX. Baz death setup — what Act II should NOT do

Do not:
- repeatedly fake Baz deaths;
- give him obvious “one last job” dialogue;
- suddenly make him sentimental;
- have everyone discuss how indispensable he is;
- telegraph sacrifice as destiny;
- turn Tahl into his designated successor before the death.

Better setup:
- ordinary competence;
- future-oriented plans;
- unresolved conversations;
- professional respect with MissingThread still incomplete;
- relational intersections beyond Lucien.

The death should feel structurally earned but emotionally interruptive.

# XVII. MissingThread must remain fallible

This is a crucial editorial amendment.

A hidden-identity investigator becomes boring if anonymity is paired with omniscience.

Preserve/recover:
- wrong hypothesis;
- incomplete source;
- cautious retraction;
- correction;
- unanswered question;
- disagreement with Baz.

The reader should trust MissingThread because the work **earns correction**, not because
the plot guarantees every post is right.

# XIX. Act-II compression logic

The old expansion likely over-serializes:
- anonymous investigation;
- institutional response;
- another clue;
- another warning;
- another convergence.

During later welding, compress any sequence where only the **amount** of evidence changes.

Keep separate episodes when the **kind** of story changes:
- evidence becomes public;
- institution changes tactics;
- Baz changes trust;
- physical danger appears;
- a relationship changes;
- model is falsified;
- public behavior changes.

# XXI. Editorial conclusion

Book 3 Act II becomes stronger when the Tahl chronology correction is treated as a design
opportunity.

The reader should increasingly feel:
**MissingThread is a person-shaped absence.**

There is:
- judgment;
- ethics;
- risk;
- humor or dryness in phrasing;
- fallibility;
- persistence;
behind the work.

But the saga withholds embodiment.

Meanwhile Baz remains fully embodied:
eating, arguing, walking into rooms, checking on people, making plans.

That contrast makes Baz's coming death and Tahl's eventual reveal operate in opposite
directions:
- Baz goes from person → absence/legacy.
- Tahl goes from work/voice/absence → person.

That is a powerful structural mirror and should be protected.
