- Tremé neighborhood rhythms;
- Acadiana empathy;
- second-line grief/joy duality;
- hurricane-season grounding;
- Aunt Colette's presence model.

- forward lean as attention/care;
- deliberate breath under stress;
- feet settle before intervention;
- gentle sway under emotional load;
- hands hover rather than grab;

### Social effect

- reduces fear without commanding;
- emotional filter rather than commander;
- children calm quickly;

- Caro: mutual grounding / emotional triage;

### Protected misreads

- not healer-magician;
- not saint/angel coded;
- warmth is not weakness;
- empathy is not omniscience.

### Disposition

**NARRATIVE ASSET / CURRENT CHARACTER GRAMMAR**, subject to later system terminology.

The strongest recovery target is not SeraphineIdentity expansion. It is a durable
**character execution / relationship grammar** layer.
