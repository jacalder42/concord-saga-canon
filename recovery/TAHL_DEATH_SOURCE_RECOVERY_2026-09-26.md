## The best-attested staging: `NS` 149826–150210, answered "This direction is good"

A Book 6 **Act III** sequence. The author's reply (`NS` 150222) accepts its **direction**, with
the correction that Kade and Tahl never met:

1. Tahl, away from Santa Fe, reads Elisabet's map: Santa Fe is the rupture's epicentre, with
   sympathetic waves elsewhere.
2. Against her warning he goes: *"I can't keep reporting from the edges if the center is about to
   fail."* (149890)
3. Kade is left keeping MT running, but they never meet (author correction).
4. His last broadcast: *"I am not here as an expert. I'm here as a witness."* (150005)
5. The VT slip, then Silence's Intent spark, then the moment of death: *"heart arrhythmia,
   neurological overload … MT goes black. Tahl dies in Santa Fe."* (~150078–150095)
6. Kade learns through an offline feed, then confirmation.
7. **Elisabet rebuilds her maps around a post-Santa-Fe topology, *"seeing that all roads now
   converge on Louisiana's swamp"*** (150160).
8. *"No farewell post."* (150174)

**Also accepted, for the cause only:** `NS` 131417–131575, *"TAHL DIES FROM A RESONANCE-INDUCED
NEUROLOGICAL COLLAPSE TRIGGERED BY A VT-SLIP DURING THE SANTA FE RUPTURE EVENT"* (131442). The
