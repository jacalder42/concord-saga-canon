## Executive finding

The antagonist layer resolves cleanly once the sources are ordered correctly.

Current Tier-1 and the Jan. 4 adjudicated source agree on the durable four-part human
antagonist architecture:

- **Marcellus Virelli — containment / Order as Mercy**
- **Han Wei — prediction / control / model certainty**
- **Ito Masayuki — public fear amplifier / populist ignition**
- **Saeko Morita — ideological engineer / serenity coercion**

The September material that assigns Saeko the public-facing protection/populist role and
Ito the doctrinal-engineer role is therefore **inverted and must not migrate**.

The Character Vault remains useful primarily for execution grammar:
how each antagonist occupies a room, frames care, applies pressure, and misreads the
protagonists.

The most important differentiation rule is:

> Virelli controls because he believes authority must protect.  
> Han controls because he believes systems must predict.  
> Ito radicalizes because he believes fear is truth.  
> Saeko erases because she believes calm ends suffering.

Do not flatten them into four versions of “emotion is dangerous.”

### Disposition

**CURRENT / PROTECTED.**

- moral objection treated as incomplete data;
- harm delegated to models, protocols and authorization chains.

### Disposition

**CURRENT / HIGH-VALUE SCENE GRAMMAR.**

statistically inconvenient.
