## What the older field rig can actually contribute

A rig plus corrupted drive is a useful story mechanism: an institution collects observations, has incentives to hide bad forecasts, and leaves a recoverable custody trail. It could give Tahl evidence of **what the institution believed** or **what its instruments output**. It cannot, by its mere existence, certify a direct reading of Resonance as a substance.

Forensic separation:

- **Observed fact in older beat:** a field rig was deployed after a bloom; a drive was damaged or data corrupted; Tahl takes it; the dossier contains institutional calculations and meta tests.
- **Claim by institutional researchers:** their algorithm estimates “density” and systemic risk.
- **OPEN physics:** what the rig measures, sensor error, calibration, local versus remote applicability, why the drive corrupts, whether corruption is ordinary fault, sabotage or event effect.
- **OPEN story survival:** whether Tahl still retrieves a drive in the later Santa Fe-oriented Book 5, where that happens, and what he may publish without exposing sources.
- **TECH rule implication:** sensors can provide **fallible proxies and evidence of institutional decisions** without magically detecting emotion, VT or Intent.

A source phrase such as “AR freezes citywide for 6–12 seconds” is not a validated service log. If retained, distinguish displays losing frames, the network stalling, blackout of power, uniform user perception, and a later editorial shorthand. Nonaffected devices and fast recovery should remain possible. Likewise a camera filming a meta records behavior and material effects—not a metaphysical payload.
