## 2. Equation reconciliation

Later Dec. 6 memory source explicitly states:
- Resonance Potential (RP) = Will × Emotion × Intent
- on-page proxy uses Energy × Emotion × Intent
- UARS governs action costs with realistic boosts
- boosts >20% impose steep physical/psychological consequences

Disposition: KEEP.

Interpretation:
- Will = latent total capacity to sustain directed action (stamina + focus + resolve + persistence).
- Energy = observable current-state proxy for available Will in a scene.
- Emotion = amplitude/coherence input.
- Intent = directional commitment.
- Environment modifies cost/effect, but does not replace RP.

Do NOT collapse Will and Energy into a single variable yet.

## 3. Technology hard laws — strongest recovered layer

KEEP:
1. Technology cannot wield Resonance because it lacks human Emotion + Intent + Will.
2. Resonance cannot be digitally transmitted.
3. Technology may observe indirect effects, infer conditions, or fail under interference.
4. High-complexity/high-frequency systems fail earlier than simple/analog systems.
5. Mechanical tools remain highly reliable.
6. Guns remain lethal.
7. Resonance does not bend bullets, accelerate projectiles, create force blasts, or grant bulletproof durability.
8. Resonance may improve a human user's grip, timing, stance, composure, accuracy, or reaction within UARS limits.
9. Veil-era technology should be overwhelmingly reliable; resonance glitches are rare and alarming.

- create safe physical geometry;

It may NOT:

- cancel Intent;
- project a magical null field;
- suppress Resonance as if it were RF.

### G. Manufactured metas — MAJOR SYSTEM REVIEW
Historical architecture:

- described as high-output with no Intent;
- catastrophic instability;
- central Technarch experiment.

Current Mechanica says:
- possess power/capability/amplification;
- lack Intent/emotional integration;
- cannot ascend;
- fail catastrophically.

Problem:
RP = Will × Emotion × Intent implies zero Intent should produce zero controlled Resonance Potential, not “high output.”

This is not necessarily fatal, but requires explicit distinction between:
- stored/environmentally forced Resonance energy;
- Resonance Potential generated/wielded by an agent;
- uncontrolled discharge.

Candidate interpretation:
manufactured metas do not WIELD Resonance. They are artificially loaded pressure vessels whose failure releases accumulated environmental/biological energy without Intent.

Do not lock until deeper meta recovery.

## 8. Tech failure model

Preferred causal model:
**Resonance event → physical/interference correlates → device vulnerability → observable malfunction**

NOT:
**emotion → device feels emotion → symbolic glitch**

Examples:
Good:
- high local pressure correlates with broadband RF noise; drone loses navigation precision.
- conflicting sensors exceed confidence threshold; autonomous system enters safe mode.
- smart optic loses stabilization; iron sights still work.
- digital mapping unavailable; paper map remains usable.

Bad:
- grief turns screen indigo because grief.
- hope stabilizes a radio because hope.
- Seraphine's empathy makes a grid work.
- Kade's anger magically blackouts microphones absent a physical interference mechanism.

## 10. Environmental shaping inconsistency

Mechanica currently lists “Environmental (field shaping, stabilization)” as a boost type.

This risks violating:
Resonance amplifies existing human capability; it does not create new forces.

Candidate correction:
Environmental action means **acting within / exploiting / stabilizing an already-active Resonance environment**, not projecting a new field into inert space.

Examples:
- choosing position/material/path;
- grounding a crowd;
- interrupting feedback;
- reducing one's own leakage;
- using environmental structure;
- helping an existing unstable system return toward equilibrium.

Not:
- creating barriers;
- projecting force;
- generating weather;
- telekinetically reshaping surroundings.
