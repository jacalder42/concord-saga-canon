## 1. Governing conclusion

Veil's recovered causal spine is strong but its Dec. 8 expansion is socially thin.

The correction is **not more characters**.

The correction is to assign recurring human faces to functions already present in the
episodes, and to restore places where ordinary life can recur between anomaly beats.

Target cast feeling:

**B1 neighborhood → B2 city → B3 city under institutional/public pressure.**

Recognition should increase as scale increases.

---

# 2. Core overlay rule

Before retaining an anonymous/named utility character, ask:

1. Is this a one-scene human whose individuality matters?
2. Or is this a recurring civic function already owned by someone?

Prefer recurrence when function matches.

Do not consolidate merely to shrink the cast.
One-scene civilians remain valuable when the point is breadth, surprise, loss or a life
outside the protagonist network.

# 27. Place recurrence guardrail

A place should recur because its function changes.

### Velvet Vein
ordinary life → rumor hub → truth-pressure/community support.

### ER / medical system
odd cases → triage pressure → overwhelmed system.

### transit/infrastructure
minor anomalies → route adaptation → system unreliability.

### neighborhood care spaces
grounding confidence → limits → shelter/triage.

This lets the reader measure escalation spatially.
