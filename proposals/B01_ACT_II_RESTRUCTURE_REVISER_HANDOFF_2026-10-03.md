# B01 Act II restructure: reviser handoff

**What it is.** These are the per-chapter instructions for the manuscript revision of draft 3's Act II. It carries out
`decisions/B01_ACT_II_SCENE_FUNCTION_MAP_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-10-03.md`: Q-AM1–13 accepted as recommended, N1 omitted, the
saving banked, and the author's caution on times binding. The map it applies is
`proposals/B01_ACT_II_SCENE_FUNCTION_MAP_2026-10-03.md`, which gives the scene numbers (E17.1 and so on) and the
protected list (§4).

**Source of truth for each chapter.** The chapter's updated EBCI packet (`ebci/B01/S1.T1.B01.A2.EXX.md`) and its prose
packet (`ebci/prose/B01/B01-EXX.md`) are the source. Both were updated in place on 10-03 and tagged *(10-03, §391)*. If
this handoff and a packet disagree, the packet wins and the disagreement is recorded.

**What it does not change.**
- No ruling, card, overlay or guard.
- No protected scene in the map's §4.
- No causal step: every fact a later chapter cites is kept or carried.
- D1/D3/D5 still apply: no explanation and no certified cause.
- No *Resonance*, and no *Veil* before E49/E50.
- Baz never sees a case, a collector or a tag (C3).
- Hand 2 asks no protagonist for anything before B02 E13 (T5/C4).
- A4 stays held.
- It quotes no manuscript text.

**Word counts.** The *now* figures are draft 3 as it stands (manuscript `B01/draft3/act-02/`). Each target is the map's
estimate, net of cuts and new beats. Cuts made under the time caution (§1) come on top of these and are **banked**
(Q-AM12); they are not spent elsewhere. A chapter may land within about ±100 words of its target. A larger overrun
needs a reason, recorded by the checker.

## 1. Time bookkeeping to cut (the author's caution, binding)

Cut these wherever the revision touches an Act II scene. **Keep a time only where the plot turns on it**: the window,
an appointment, the order on the two recordings (E17), the lagging 911 line (E32), and the call-time lag and the notice
confound behind Baz's bet (E36).

1. **Clock times in narration that nobody acts on.** That covers a time of day on an arrival, a visit or a report,
   including the map's *at eight* (N8) and *at seven* (N9), which are not written.
2. **Minute counts and durations recited:** how long something held, how long someone waited, or how long Caro held
   the patient's head.
3. **Recaps of established times:** restating when the Square was, when the window opens, or when a call came.
4. **Interval tallies, recited.** The neighbour's two-day count (E34) is said once, as her claim. The shortening gaps
   (E36) are Baz's bet. Nobody else recites intervals.
5. **Logging that decides nothing:** a character writing or reading out times while nothing is chosen, tested or
   paid for. Seraphine's log is kept as *who was told, and when it was said*, not as a column of clock times.
6. **Timestamp comparisons that change no choice,** across phones, charts, tickets or notes. One exception: the order on
   the two recordings, by the amp's pop, at E17.
7. **Calendar bookkeeping around the window.** Its length and its opening (the Monday after Easter) stay; dates and
   day-counting go.
8. **The watchers' want reduced to *the times*.** Both outside hands want the record: who it happened to, where, what
   was seen and kept (photos, video, a written note), with time as one field. N9's buyer pays for what households saw
   and kept, never for the minute each pane broke. N5's email offers to buy Elisabet's well records; *with timestamps*
   is not carried.
9. **Ethics arguments framed through the clock.** Where the crew argue about recording people, the stake is the
   person: privacy, consent, the record and a duty (Q-TM1 option C). E27's refusal is about the patient's record.
10. **A watch or phone checked for the time** in place of an action.

## 2. Reviser groups

The chapters are grouped so that most cross-chapter moves stay inside one group:
- **Group A:** E17, E20, E21, E22, E24 (Act II's first stretch, the earned record and the charts).
- **Group B:** E26, E27, E28, E29, E31 (from the page to the night before the window).
- **Group C:** E32, E34, E35, E36 (Ursulines to the act's close).

No move had to change group: every cut-and-carry in C1–C5 starts and ends inside one group. The new beats' later
payoffs do cross groups. §6 lists each dependency.

**Order.** A, then B, then C, or all three in parallel with §6 checked at the end. A reviser in a later group reads the
earlier groups' revised chapters for the facts in §6 before signing off.
