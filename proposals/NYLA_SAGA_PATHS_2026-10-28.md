# Nyla's place in the saga: four paths against canon (2026-10-28)

**Status:** DESIGN analysis for the author. Nothing is ruled. §615 (taken in B02, Lanette-led recovery, escape on the E44 pulse) stands until the author rules. Ledger §624 §6.

## The author's paths
1. **No role outside Veil.** She becomes a Dominion attempt to make a meta and is killed in the explosion that kills Baz, or is its source.
2. **She is merged into the Mira echo** in Loom.
3. **As in 1, but she survives the meta conversion** in a diminished state.
4. **She extends into Loom, entangled with or used by a faction.**
5. **She ends in Neon, as part of the fall of Dominion** (the author, 07:46).

## The canon these paths touch
- **Manufactured metas** (Mechanica §48; META_TRANSFER ruling, 09-27):
  - they are **altered humans only**, voluntary or coerced, and **their names are erased at conversion**;
  - they **hold resonance they cannot spend**;
  - they are permanently unstable, cannot ascend, and **fail catastrophically under pressure: leakage**;
  - **personhood is never in question**;
  - **their creators are the true antagonists**.
- **"Dominion-adjacent research bodies"** are named meta creators (`canon/factions/Manufactured_Metas_Creators.md:42`). The Hollen Institute, Dominion's front (§617), fits.
- **Nyla converts; she does not emit** (§619, provisional). She changes how resonance shows: the fall went round her at the Square, and the light bent under her in the Epilogue. Dominion's *Emitter (prov.)* label is wrong in a way that matters.
- **Suppression stores pressure and increases the blast radius** (Resonance-v1 §23). The hard cap fails the same way (§42A.2).
- **The Warehouse is the first Rupture** (M08, B03), and **Baz dies rescuing a child** there (M10).
- **Mira** is the previous Seraphine-type. Her soul powers the current cap, only Seraphine perceives her, and she is released at the Mending (MIRA rulings, 09-26 and 09-27). **Tahl's echo is the only one identifiable to the cast.**
- **Loom's factions:**
  - **the Choirless** ("Emotion is entropy; silence is survival") are Loom's named pressure, but absent from the B07–B08 architecture;
  - **Brightbreak** is a fear-fed certainty engine that turns people into stories;
  - **the Dominion co-opter's collapse** is placed nowhere.

## The strongest single move, whichever ending is chosen
**Path 1's "she is the source" fits canon better than anything else on the table.**
- Dominion misreads a converter as an emitter, and tries to manufacture what it thinks she is.
- A meta *holds* what it cannot spend. Forced conversion turns a child who **let it pass** into a child who **stores** it, and stored load fails by **leakage**.
- So the Warehouse Rupture is not weather. It is **Dominion's own conversion failing.**
- **What this pays off:**
  - **Veil's dramatic question** ("what does telling the truth cost?"): the truth about her, mislabelled and misused, is what kills Baz.
  - **Caldas's revenge:** the program is his operation, possibly set in motion before his defeat, and Tahl's coordinates draw the crowd that amplifies the leak (§22).
  - **M10** becomes specific: the child Baz pulls free is Nyla.
  - **The saga's hidden mechanism gets a partial payoff inside Veil.** The Warehouse is a small hard cap failing: stored, not discharged, breaking out. A reader learns how the Veil fails before learning that the Veil *is* a cap. That is trilogy-contract rule 3 doing its job.
  - **It rhymes with B01.** Lucien cut Nyla's page so the desk would never have her name, and conversion erases names.

## The writer thread's lean (not a ruling, revised after path 5)
- **Take 1-as-source whatever the ending.** It gives Veil's climax a cause, Caldas his revenge, Baz's rescue a face, and the saga's cap mechanism a partial payoff.
- **Then D, the fall of Dominion.** It is the only combination that satisfies the contract in **both** trilogies she touches:
  - Veil asks and closes the event;
  - Neon answers with a consequence canon already needs, the cause of Dominion's wane, and stages the split and the "Concord" co-option that the Neon architecture is missing.

  Loom stays clean, and its Choirless gap is fixed on its own terms.
- **A** stays the clean fallback. **B** is rich, but it loads Loom and keeps her offstage for a whole trilogy. **C** is the weakest.

## What any ruling must also settle
1. **§615:** does the B02 escape on the E44 pulse stand, so that she is retaken in B03, or does the B02 capture lead straight into the conversion?
2. **Who runs the conversion:** Hollen (the Institute or the Foundation) under Caldas, or the Houston funder faction. If the funder is involved, the B03 funder reveal (§624 §3) and Nyla's conversion become one revelation.
3. **Her age across the saga:** she is about eight in B01. The time span between trilogies is a **GAP**, and it decides what a Loom Nyla can be.
4. **The page's handling:** the conversion is never shown as procedure on a child. It is felt through Lanette, Seraphine and Baz. That keeps paper the justification, not the vehicle (§615).
