## 2. Questions

### Q1. Why is Tahl at Santa Fe?

No source gives a reason (B06 card, link 11). The best-attested staging, whose **direction** was
accepted: he reads Elisabet's map, and **against her warning** he goes: *"I can't keep reporting from
the edges if the center is about to fail."* (`NS` 149890).

| Option | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| **A (recommended)** | **Against Elisabet's bounded warning (M55, now B06 A1)**, because after the Warehouse he will not send others where he will not go himself. This is the Warehouse mirror: a **consequence at a distance** in B03 becomes a **consequence in person** in B06 (B06 card, finding B). It gives M55 a human cost and ties his remorse arc (M12) to his death, without making it a chosen sacrifice |
| B | To warn and evacuate, after the Filament warnings go unheeded (link 6) |
| C | To report only: the journalist's pull, with no remorse link |

### Q2. His last public act at the site

This was ruled: his last *message* is the private one to the group.

| Option | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| **A (recommended)** | A **live witness broadcast** from the rupture's edge. The accepted-direction line is *"I am not here as an expert. I'm here as a witness."* (`NS` 150005). Then **MT goes black**. There is no farewell post; his last words to anyone are the group message |
| B | No public broadcast at all. MT simply goes silent |

### Q3. The slip, the glimpse, and Silence

| Option | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| **A (recommended)** | Cumulative pressure plus proximity trigger a **VT slip** (TahlEBCI: *"partial, accidental"*). In it he **glimpses the wound pattern**: the shape, not the particulars. He gets it into his device by mortal means (Q4). **The contact kills him.** **After death, Silence collects his echo**, which is Silence's first agency (M21, and the author: *"Silence gains Intent after collecting Tahl's echo after his death"*, `PC` 158951). **No pre-death "Intent spark"**: the assistant chain's slip → spark → death is not used |
| B | Keep a pre-death spark (Silence reaches for him and fails), with agency proper at the collection |

### Q6. What it says (the "shape", not the particulars)

Ruled: *"Tahl's message = the shape, the chain = the particulars"*. The protagonists know there are
**multiple wounds** and that **Honey Island must be repaired last**. The factions believe
"Louisiana".

| Option | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| **A (recommended)** | **The shape:** the tears are **linked**, there are **more than one**, they must be closed **in order**, and **"the first one has to be last."** It names **no place** except where he stands. **Seraphine recognises "the first one"** as the swamp, the original Tear near her home. So the Honey-Island-last knowledge is the protagonists' interpretation, private and never broadcast, and the site secret holds. Particulars (Mound City, Serpent Mound) come from the chain in B08 |
| B | The shape plus the order of the sites, stated outright |
| C | The shape only; Honey Island-last comes later, from Mira or from Silence and Hope |
