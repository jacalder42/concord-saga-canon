## 3. What the 09-27 metaphysics rulings mean for Veil

- **What the reader sees is the cap beginning to fail** (Mechanica §42A, R): flickers and ghostwaves
  are stored pressure starting to break through at weak points (§7.1, §34).
  - **On the page they stay unexplained.** No one in Veil knows there is a cap (§60; the answer is
    reserved).
  - They are observed with bounded evidence and competing explanations (D5).
- **P, an optional thematic rhyme:** Veil's *"heavy institutional suppression"* (Mechanica §7.1) can
  echo the cap's logic at human scale: protection by blocking, and pressure stored elsewhere. It is a
  rhyme, never a statement.
- **The Mira seed** (D: subtle; no named appearance):
  - perceived only by Seraphine (R);
  - compatible with *"she knows the cost, not the answer"* (R);
  - **distinguishable from the B01 child's E03 imprint**, which stays a fading imprint and not a
    guide (R, hypothesis A not taken);
  - carries nothing about the cap or the guides.

  Its book, place and form are **open**.
- **People adapt** (§42A.5, R), in the future. **P:** Veil can show ordinary people's coping mattering
  (review C12). It must not suggest that adaptation has already solved anything.
