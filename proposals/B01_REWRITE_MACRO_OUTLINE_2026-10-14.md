## 2. What changed from braid 4 (and why)

1. **Baz arrives on page in Ch 3** and is in every act's turn. In braid 4 he first appears in Ch 12.
2. **The two men recognise each other at the end of Act I,** the first time the reader sees both sets of knowledge in contact.
3. **One Caldas interview, one Caldas offer.** The repeated "before the others" meetings (five) become one interview (Ch 14),
   one overt temptation aimed at Seraphine's blue folder (Ch 16), one confrontation of Baz (Ch 20), one act of violence (Ch 31),
   and one public failure (Ch 42). Each is a different kind of act.
4. **The stolen sheet becomes Baz's folder** (Ch 20 hidden; Ch 31 handed over; Ch 35 tested and partly disproved).
5. **The camp returns** (Ch 34, at night), as the first place the crew proves on the ground.
6. **Fewer tally rooms.** Investigation scenes happen in places: a pump station at night, a repast, a church lot, a laundromat,
   a courtroom corridor, the camp, the river wall. Each scene has a different shape (see the shape tags in §3).
7. **Confessions** are C-1 (Ch 41), C-2 (Ch 31) and C-3 (Ch 40). Nothing else is a capital-C Confession.
8. **The cause of every character turn is on the page.** Lucien's turn is Ch 39–40, after Seraphine finds his form; Caro's data
   reversal is Ch 33; Elisabet's thaw is Ch 39.

## 4. Checks run on this outline (to be re-run on the cards)
- **Confession budget:** three capital-C scenes (31, 40, 41), each with a cost carried forward.
- **Knowledge continuity:** the engine's knowledge map holds (Baz hides to Ch 31; Lucien's omissions Ch 5, 10, 13, 24; Lucien's C-3 follows Seraphine's find).
- **Shape variety:** no two consecutive chapters share a shape; no more than two table/debrief scenes in any act.
- **Payoffs:** each thread in the engine's ledger has a plant, an advance and a payoff on the table above.
