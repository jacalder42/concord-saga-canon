## 0. Revision of the same day: Baz arrives by summons (author, Q-MO1)

The author answered Q-MO2–5 *agreed* and, on Q-MO1, asked that **Baz arrive in or near Act II**, as originally intended, to
help set up the Seraphine/Lucien chemistry: *"Perhaps Baz only appears in eventual communication with Lucien before being
summoned? Perhaps the summon happens at the close of Act I and is told from Baz's POV?"* This revision takes that. It removes
Baz's arrival (old Ch 3), the sideways warning (old Ch 8), his presence at the funeral and "Bastien" in the Square. Act I closes
on **Baz's summons, in his POV (Ch 11)**; he reaches the city on 1 April. The book is **43 chapters**.

**Refinement (author, same day, on Lucien and "B"):** *"I can see Lucien originally only reporting to Vienna, then gradually becoming less and
less comfortable with their instructions, and he starts reaching out to Baz for guidance. Perhaps Baz is left unnamed until his POV, maybe
the communication is with 'B' and Lucien can internally register it going to 'B' or 'him' or his 'mentor'."* Taken as follows:
- **Lucien's reporting arc:** Ch 4 he reports **only to Vienna** (a curated note to the Institute desk). By Ch 8 the instructions sit badly
  (the instrument failure, the Institute's questions) and he makes his **first unsent, then sent, message to "B"**, whom his POV calls
  *B*, *him* or *his mentor* and never by name. Ch 10 (the Square) he writes "Come." The reader learns the name **Baz** in Ch 11, his POV.
- **Baz is Lucien's mentor** (the senior hand at the Vienna table, who left). This fits canon's *same table, for the same people* and
  leaves the ages unstated. Lucien thinks of Baz as a trusted former mentor who went into community work; he does not know Baz holds the *why*.
- **What the reader sees in Act I:** Lucien's pull toward an outside voice and Baz's replies (what he asks, what he refuses to put in
  writing), without a name or a face. Seraphine does not meet "B" until Ch 12–14.

What it changes:
- **Act I Baz is a voice at a distance.** Lucien writes and phones an old friend in Houston (Ch 4, 8, 10) beside his curated notes to
  the Institute. The reader sees **what Baz does not say** in his replies ("don't put it in writing; tell me where"), so his
  knowledge shows by deed before he is on the page.
- **Ch 11 (Baz) is the Act I turn.** He holds Lucien's message ("It's here. Come.") and what he knows about the force, the
  aim and what the people who sent Lucien will do. He decides whether to go, and what he can tell Lucien when he gets there.
- **Baz is the chemistry catalyst in Act II.** He watches Seraphine and Lucien, sees what each hides, and engineers small
  occasions to put them together (the canvass pairing, the bar, the waltz). The waltz (Ch 22) can be told from his POV.
- **The "one who knew" warning** moves from Baz's old Ch 8 to Baz's first scene with Seraphine (Ch 13–15), after Caldas's offer.
- **Reunion** at Ch 12 replaces "Bastien" in the Square.

## 2. What changed from braid 4 (and why)

1. **Baz is a voice in Act I and on the page by summons** (Ch 11), in the city from Ch 12. In braid 4 he first appears in Ch 12 by chance.
2. **One Caldas interview, one Caldas offer.** The repeated "before the others" meetings (five) become one interview (Ch 13),
   one overt temptation aimed at Seraphine's blue folder (Ch 15), one confrontation of Baz (Ch 19), one act of violence (Ch 30), and one
   public failure (Ch 41). Each is a different kind of act.
3. **The stolen sheet becomes Baz's folder,** taken as his final straw with Dominion (Ch 19 hidden; Ch 30 handed over; Ch 34 tested and partly disproved).
4. **The camp returns** (Ch 33, at night), as the first place the crew proves on the ground.
5. **Fewer tally rooms.** Investigation scenes happen in places: a pump station at night, a repast, a church lot, a laundromat, a courtroom corridor, the camp, the river wall.
6. **Confessions** are C-3 (Ch 38), C-2 (Ch 30) and C-1 (Ch 40). Nothing else is a capital-C Confession.
7. **The cause of every character turn is on the page.** Lucien's turn is Ch 38, after Seraphine reads his form in Guidry's release; Caro's data reversal is Ch 32; Elisabet's thaw is Ch 39.

## 4. Checks run on this outline (to be re-run on the cards)
- **Confession budget:** three capital-C scenes (30, 38, 40), each with a cost carried forward.
- **Knowledge continuity:** Baz hides to Ch 30; Lucien's omissions Ch 4, 8, 12, 23; Lucien's C-3 follows Seraphine's reading of the form.
- **Shape variety:** no two consecutive chapters share a shape; no more than two table/debrief scenes in any act.
- **Payoffs:** each thread in the engine's ledger has a plant, an advance and a payoff above.
