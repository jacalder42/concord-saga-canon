# Comparative-series event-density recode — pass 1

**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Status:** PROPOSAL / DERIVED DIAGNOSTIC, NOT A BOOK-EVENT CENSUS OR CANON.  
**Scope:** The five ten-part pacing maps from the 2026-09-21/22 “Compare Series Structure” conversation. No novel text, authorized chapter-by-chapter event ledger, or exact per-book word-position data was available in this pass. This document recodes that *earlier analysis*, not the books themselves.

## Finding and scope correction

The comparison chat considered ten books in a broad first pass: *Dungeon Crawler Carl* (books 1 and 5), *He Who Fights with Monsters* 1, *Hounded*, *Rivers of London*, *Goblet of Fire*, *A Court of Mist and Fury*, *Fourth Wing*, *A Darker Shade of Magic*, and *Bride*. Only five received approximately ten-part pacing maps (the five below). The other five have **no window data to recode here**. The first pass offered scale, format, cast, geography, action and activity bands, not a measured event inventory.

**What we can count:** 50 approximate book-windows and the number of windows carrying a given activity tag. **What we cannot count from this source:** how many plot events each novel actually has; events per 10,000 words; maximum event-free run; a true word-percentile cluster statistic; physical action or set-piece totals; number of causal state changes; cross-book relative density. Even an apparently “empty” tag can mean the earlier summary omitted it. Chapter counts are publishing units, not event counts.

This packet uses the [narrative audit framework](narrative-audit-framework-v1.md) for reward/mode context and the [saga event levels](SAGA_EVENT_TIERS_AND_UNSLOTTED_POOL_PASS1_2026-09-23.md) for the eventual A/B/C/D distinction. It does not revise either framework or apply a quota to Concord.

## Coding protocol for this recode

One row = roughly a tenth of **narrative progression as summarized in the prior chat**, not a mathematically measured word decile. Assign 1–3 non-exclusive tags to activity explicitly mentioned in that row:

| Tag | Activity visible in the coarse summary; not a discrete-event definition |
| --- | --- |
| A | physical danger/action |
| I | investigation/disclosure |
| W | world/system discovery |
| S | relationship/social change |
| P | public/institutional/political |
| T | structured trial/training |
| D | ordinary/rest |
| R | aftermath/recovery |

An “A” tag denotes danger or action in that window; it does **not** mean one fight. “S” can indicate relationship life without a reversible/irreversible event; “D” is not filler. A window can contain several events, no state-changing events, or one event spanning adjacent windows. Coding is an **interpretation of the prior chat's short descriptions**, not independently checked novel data.

## Clustering observations at this resolution

- **DCC 1:** Immediate world rupture and learning/survival; sustained external-danger visibility at 20–40%, then a 70–100% threat/spectacle run, with a community/ally stretch around 40–70%. The map cannot tell how many distinct fights occur.
- **Rivers of London:** Investigation/education and geographic/institutional expansion alternate through approximately 20–80%, then crisis/resolution at 80–100%. Its middle is active without constant physical peril; the 12 large chapters should not be equated to Concord episodes.
- **Goblet of Fire:** Public occasion around 10–30%, tournament/test around 40–50% and 60–70%, social respite around 50–60%, information gathering near 60–80%, final danger at 80–90%, and explicit mourning/explanation at 90–100%. Anticipation and aftermath matter as much as action spikes.
- **ACOMAF:** Trauma/escape at 0–20%; substantial social/relational occupancy across 20–90%, including ordinary/training and a 70–80% emotional climax; public catastrophe at 90–100%. The emotional peak and external climax are separated.
- **Fourth Wing:** Structured entry/tests and danger roughly 0–40%; aftermath and power/relationship development 40–80%; military revelation and battle 80–100%. Its A/T co-occurrence is a feature of this training-and-war premise, not a target for Veil's no-combat opening.

**Cross-sample observation:** the coarse maps show heterogeneous long middle-book carriers (community, investigation, scheduled public events, relationship life, training) and different kinds of late cluster. They do not yield an “optimal events per book” mean. This supports varying B07–B08 functions and locations rather than dragging every thread toward the B09 end site, but it **does not establish that Loom currently has too few events**.

## Why the historical numeric rule is not comparator evidence

The 2025-11-08 full-export “Event Density & World Texture” discussion offered putative targets of 15–20, then 20–24 named events per trilogy, and shifted seven primary events from saga-wide to “per trilogy.” It was an advisory/simulated board discussion, not measured data from these books; purported statements in real authors' voices cannot validate numeric frequencies. Do not import its ~15k-word incident cadence, counts, or 1:2:3 ratio as empirically derived rules.

## Next measurement pass — distinct from this recode

For a **true** sample, start with three unlike comparators: DCC 1 (high external incident), *Rivers of London* (investigative/place accumulation), and ACOMAF (relational/aftermath). Extend to *Goblet* (anticipated occasion) and *Fourth Wing* (training/war) only after calibrating the coding protocol. Use an authorized full text or verified, sufficiently granular chapter notes; secondary web recaps may omit events and must be flagged lower confidence. Do not silently mix source types.

For each chapter/scene make one row per **distinct before → action/discovery → changed state**, including non-Resonance money/access/relationship/information changes. Required fields: book, chapter/page or word-position anchor, event ID, event tier A/B/C (D receipts nested, never standalone), primary kind plus optional secondary kind, initiating constraint, state change, beneficiary/cost, locale/people, causal dependency, aftermath start/end, source and uncertainty. Two stages:

1. **Blind double-code 10% per book**, adjudicate disagreement on event boundary versus detail/receipt; keep one continuous sequence split across chapters as one event.
2. **Complete book-level census**, report events per 10k actual words (if words available), per 10% measured prose, composition by kind and tier, zero-event gaps, runs of related events, clustering around set pieces, and lag to consequence. Show ranges where text position or source quality is approximate.

Then compare Concord at **the same observation granularity**, preserving B01's locked 48 narrative episodes, six supplements and no-combat design, and keeping the saga's 36-row milestone grid separate from a complete event census. A B07/B08 deficit should be diagnosed as missing *causal changes, people, locations, and carried costs*, not as failure to hit a borrowed quota.
