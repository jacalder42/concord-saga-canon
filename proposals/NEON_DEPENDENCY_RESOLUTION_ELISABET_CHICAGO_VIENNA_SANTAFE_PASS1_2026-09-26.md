# 1. Elisabet — source test

## Tier-1 identity already contains the turn

Elisabet's canon establishes:
- environmental systems / crisis analysis;
- pattern recognition and decision-path illumination under uncertainty;
- formative disillusionment: correct analysis is insufficient when bureaucratic/institutional action arrives too late;
- core wound: confusion or delay leads to irreversible harm;
- Neon: clarity is strained by acceleration and **delayed action proves costly**;
- authority ceiling: advisor/mapper/stabilizer, not commander.

Therefore a Neon milestone does not need to invent a new trait.

The missing durable action is the moment she stops treating further certainty as the only responsible response.

## Recovered B05 arena

The strongest recovered B05 information spine is:
- distributed reports;
- Elisabet makes a fallible geographic inference;
- Tahl distinguishes verified facts from interpretation;
- Santa Fe becomes a bounded warning/threshold;
- B06 later tests the warning through actual catastrophe.

Current author ruling additionally says:
- the decisive warning **originates in Tahl's VT perception**;
- it reaches people through mortal communication / MT.

Therefore:
**Elisabet does not discover Santa Fe supernaturally.**
Her work can independently corroborate, constrain, challenge, operationalize or fail to fully confirm Tahl's warning.

## Candidate milestone ELIS-N1

**Working wording:**
> With the available evidence still incomplete and plausible ordinary rivals unresolved, Elisabet recommends a bounded protective action before certainty. She states what is known, what is inferred, what could falsify the model, and the cost of acting too early versus too late. The recommendation changes a real route/access/response decision.

### Why it qualifies
Before:
- clarity tends toward further analysis;
- institutional delay is an old wound.

After:
- she accepts that responsible clarity can include acting under declared uncertainty;
- she owns consequences without pretending certainty;
- this directly supports Loom's shared/relational clarity.

### Guardrails
- she does not command a city;
- she does not predict exact rupture time;
- Tahl's VT warning remains distinct;
- no magical detector;
- if the action helps, it is not proof her entire causal model was correct;
- if it costs people unnecessarily, that cost remains visible.

### Recommendation
**ADD as a strong candidate milestone**, likely B05 A3.

## Important constraint

## Best setup class: ordinary family return

If Chicago is selected as the B05 evacuation city, the cleanest setup is:
**Caro goes home for an ordinary family reason before the crisis is known to be there.**

Do not make the reason melodramatic or load-bearing.

Candidate forms:
- long-promised visit after repeatedly postponing;
- helping Rosa/Miguel/Mateo with an ordinary household transition;
- family gathering;
- checking in because Caro herself is exhausted and her family insists she come home.

### Best thematic fit
**A long-postponed ordinary visit.**

Why:
Caro's problem is that crisis always outranks ordinary life.
Choosing to go home when nothing is on fire is itself character movement.

Then the city crisis converts a protected ordinary interval back into response.

This creates an important irony:
Caro finally chooses family/ordinary life → crisis arrives anyway → she must learn that even in crisis she cannot personally carry everyone.

## Recommendation
Advance this as **design**, not recovered canon:
> B05 Caro takes a long-postponed ordinary trip home to Pilsen before the major-city crisis escalates.

Keep exact family occasion open until beat architecture.
