# Veil Book 1 — Life / Reward Companion Pass v1

Status: PROPOSED STRUCTURAL COMPANION — NON-CANONICAL
Date: 2026-09-21
Pairs with: B01 Revised Beat Bible Migration Draft v3
Purpose: restore the lived-life / reader-reward architecture that was compressed out of the narrative spine.

## 1. Governing ruling for this pass

The current Prologue + 36-episode B1 v3 is treated as the **narrative spine**, not the complete target manuscript architecture.

Do not expand the book by:
- undoing useful compression;
- making existing plot episodes longer and more expository;
- adding fights;
- onboarding the full saga cast;
- forcing every life beat to advance the macroplot.

Instead, add/select companion dramatic units whose primary job is reader experience:
- character;
- relationship;
- humor;
- food/music/nightlife;
- ordinary work;
- local culture;
- wonder;
- anticipation;
- recovery;
- small satisfactions;
- life continuing around the saga.

Recovered control intent supports this approach:
- humor/wonder/ordinary-life beats were part of the Episode Expansion system;
- FUN pacing was expected recurrently;
- later uniform LOW fun/slice/wonder overlays were ruled skeleton state, not design.

## 2. Target architecture

Protected narrative spine:
**P + 36 episodes.**

Provisional companion target:
**18–26 additional dramatic units**, producing approximately **55–63 total narrative units plus Prologue**.

This is not a quota.

A companion unit may be:
- a full ~1,200–1,800 word serial episode;
- a shorter ~700–1,200 word breather;
- paired with a spine unit inside a later conventional chapter;
- occasionally folded into a spine episode if folding does not erase its breathing-room function.

Do not return to 60–80 plot episodes.
Return to roughly 55–65 **reader-experience units**.

## 3. Cast restraint rule

### Presence does not require introduction.

A person may:
- serve a drink;
- play music;
- answer a call;
- appear at a scene;
- be recognized later;
without receiving biography, POV, backstory, or a narrative flag telling the reader to memorize them.

### Companion-pass cast preference

Priority 1:
Seraphine, Lucien, Baz.

Priority 2:
already-essential B1 relationships / hearth:
Trip, Caro, Elisabet where chronology/function earns them.

Priority 3:
one recurring local/support face only when recurrence itself creates payoff.

Priority 4:
unnamed civilians, workers, neighbors, musicians, EMTs, bartenders, customers, drivers, tourists, etc.

Do not use the Life Layer as a cast-introduction delivery system.

## 4. Companion-unit classes

**LIFE** — ordinary work, errands, food, home, transit, neighborhood routines.
**FUN** — humor, banter, ridiculousness, social pleasure.
**REL** — relationship movement without requiring plot movement.
**HEARTH** — recurring social place; especially Velvet Vein.
**WONDER** — beauty/strangeness/curiosity without immediate threat.
**CARE** — recovery, practical help, embodied aftermath.
**ANTICIPATE** — establish something pleasant to look forward to.
**DISCOVERY-PLAY** — investigation/learning that is enjoyable rather than pressured.
**PRIVATE** — interiority, solitude, habit, secret want.
**CITY** — New Orleans exists independently of protagonists.

A unit may carry multiple classes but should have one dominant reason to exist.

## 6. Provisional count

Spine:
36 episodes + Prologue.

Companion candidates:
26.

Likely after selection/merging:
**19–24 companion units.**

Expected working architecture:
**55–60 episodes + Prologue**, with room to rise toward low 60s if recovered material earns it.

At an average near 1,500–1,800 words per unit, with some short breathers and some longer spine episodes, this returns B1 to a plausible full-novel/serial scale without manufacturing more plot.

## 7. Reader-reward spacing

The companion pass should prevent long droughts in:
- FUN;
- REL;
- WON;
- LIFE/CITY;
- HEARTH;
- CARE.

Target principle:
Every 3–5 narrative-spine episodes should contain or be adjacent to at least one unit whose primary purpose is not macroplot escalation.

Recovered FUN guidance of roughly every 5–8 episodes is a floor, not a requirement that every reward be comedy.

## 8. Cast-bloat check

Companion units should make the book feel **more populated while requiring fewer introductions**.

Preferred effect:
the reader repeatedly sees a city full of people but can easily name only the people who matter.

Do not promote a background face merely because they recur.

A future-book promotion can create the useful feeling:
"I've seen her before."

## 9. What this changes about v4

Do not create B1 v4 yet.

First:
1. review these 26 candidate companion units;
2. mine recovered EBCI/Jazz material for compatible life/reward beats;
3. mark each candidate RECOVERED / COMPATIBLE / NEW;
4. select approximately 19–24;
5. interleave them with the 36 spine episodes;
6. run Narrative ECG again;
7. only then issue a v4 beat bible.

This prevents the v4 process from stuffing life material inside already-dense plot episodes.

## 10. Success test

B1 is ready for detailed EBCI expansion when:
- the plot can be summarized cleanly from the 36 spine units;
- the reader experience cannot be summarized without the companion units;
- New Orleans feels worth living in;
- Baz feels loved before B3 makes that costly;
- Seraphine and Lucien exist when they are not solving Resonance;
- Velvet Vein feels like a place people actually choose to go;
- Caro exists as a person before romance asks the reader to care;
- the reader has laughed, wanted, eaten, listened, rested, wandered and wondered;
- not every episode appears to know it belongs to a nine-book saga.
