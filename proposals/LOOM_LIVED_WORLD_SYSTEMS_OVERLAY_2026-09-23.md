## Three Loom falsification checks

1. Two settlements have the same weather index but different food and power conditions. Show their supply routes, fuel, labor and shelter rules; do not make W-level stand for all scarcity.
2. A convoy chooses between charging a medical device and a transmitter. Establish source capacity, duty cycle, replacement options, who decides and the lasting human cost; keep equipment power separate from UARS load.
3. A marked coat or route symbol gains someone entry. Identify the social authority that recognizes it, whether it can be copied, and what happens if the gatekeeper disputes it. A stitched sign does not itself stabilize a corridor.
