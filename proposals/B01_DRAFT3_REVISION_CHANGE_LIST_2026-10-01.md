## 1. Global rules (every chapter)

**G1. Names:**
- **Inez** replaces Mara (A01).
- **The Beaulieu Center for Family Services** is introduced once, in the chapter where the center first matters (E01 or
  E03), and is *the center* after that.
- **Lucien's cover employer is the Hollen Institute for Civic Continuity** (Vienna). It is running **the Regional Review
  of Unexpected Public Costs** for the parishes around the city.

**G2. Parish, not county.** Louisiana has parishes.
- The coroner's office and Guidry's windbreaker are **the parish's**.
- The caseload numbers reported upward go to **the state**, which funds the center's contract **[execution: the funder
  is the state]**.

**G3. Fewer contrast constructions** (profile §12, Q-HR1). Cut *she X, not Y*, *not X but Y*, *Not X.* on its own,
*not because* and *not quite* wherever the contrast is not the news. Do not swap in another repeated pattern.

**G4. Say less after the image lands** (P3). Cut the sentence that explains what a gesture, object or silence has
already shown. **Keep narration that adds something the image does not, within the point of view.**

**G5. Tempo follows the scene's temperature** (P5, new profile line).
- **In peaks:** shorter sentences and paragraphs, more white space, fewer *and … and … and* chains, fewer asides, more
  action and spoken lines.
- **In quiet and thinking:** keep the long sentences.
- **First priority:** E14, E29, E32, E39, E46, E47.

**G6. Language and register** (profile §12A):
- non-English words in plain type, with no italics;
- meaning from context;
- no phonetic accents;
- honorifics and nicknames as New Orleans uses them.

**The language tells are permissions, used sparingly**
(`proposals/LANGUAGE_AND_REGISTER_CHARACTER_NOTES_2026-10-01.md`):
- Lucien: German under stress, French in intimacy;
- Baz: Marseille French under pressure, and translating for others;
- Caro: Spanish;
- Seraphine: her elders' Louisiana French, mostly as forms of address.

**G7. Repeated shapes** (P2). Where a chapter repeats a shape (the elder who feeds her, the dignified refusal, the gray
case found *after*, *I'm fine*, concealment narrated as it happens), vary it. **Let some concealments be discovered by
someone else, and some refusals collide.**

**G8. Rests are judged by function** (P1). Keep every quiet chapter. Vary how recovery looks, and end a rest on a choice
where that serves it. **E11, E33 and E43 get the closest look.**

**G9. Length.** No target. Most chapters should get shorter under G3–G5. Added beats go where this list puts them.

**G10. Do not smooth the edges.** Keep:
- disagreements that stay unresolved;
- Baz's dissent;
- the trap as Lucien's own want;
- Seraphine choosing one person;
- Renée's refusal to forgive.

**Do not:**
- name the Dominion or Technarc;
- explain the phenomenon;
- use *Resonance* on the page.
