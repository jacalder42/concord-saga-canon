# 2. Lucien / Vienna B04 adjudication

## Source status

No recovered source explicitly says **Lucien physically returns to Vienna in B04**.

Recovered support around it is nevertheless unusually strong:
- Vienna = Lucien's birthplace, emotional home and Dominion/Baz ecosystem.
- Baz's death is Lucien's Neon fracture point.
- B04 opens the post-Baz grief trajectory.
- relationship chronology says B04 tests whether Lucien will let Seraphine stay present with him.
- B03 has Lucien refusing a return directive.
- B04 has Dominion emissaries/reassignment pressure and Lucien rejecting it again.
- Vienna has Jan/Baz family, Marius/archive, Helena/family-Dominion pressure, ordinary Baz-haunted places.
- saga timeline audit identifies B04–B07 Lucien as at risk of repeating one grief/Silence note.

## Editorial ruling recommendation

**ADVANCE B04 VIENNA RETURN AS THE PREFERRED DESIGN.**

But:
**Lucien returns to Vienna by choice, not because he accepts Dominion reassignment.**

This is essential.

### Narrative function

Immediately after Baz:
1. Lucien and Seraphine are strained.
2. Seraphine cannot regulate grief for him and should not chase him into emotional submission.
3. Lucien falls back toward the architecture that taught him how to contain himself.
4. He returns physically to Vienna—part retreat, part practical obligation, part need to understand what Baz left behind.
5. Dominion interprets his return as recoverability/reabsorption.
6. Baz's ordinary Vienna makes institutional retreat impossible: cafés/routes/people/family preserve a version of Baz that Dominion cannot classify.
7. Lucien must distinguish **home from institution** and **structure from submission**.
8. His eventual refusal of reassignment is therefore made from inside the old system's geography, not safely from NOLA.

### Why B04 is superior to B05

B04:
- externalizes fresh grief;
- gives Seraphine/Lucien separation real geography;
- establishes Neon parallel theaters immediately;
- gives Vienna lived continuity before later collapse;
- makes B04 Dominion pressure personal;
- changes Lucien rather than simply illustrating him.

B05:
- competes with Caro/Chicago, Elisabet/Reykjavík and Rex/Singapore;
- risks “everyone goes home” symmetry;
- arrives after the sharpest Baz wound.

### What the Vienna sequence must NOT become
- a nostalgia tour;
- Lucien returning to obey Dominion;
- another Warehouse investigation;
- Baz appearing as Echo;
- Seraphine following immediately to fix him;
- Jan forgiving him;
- a full second ensemble.

### Preferred duration
A **substantial short B04 theater**, not the entire book:
- arrival/ordinary Baz absence;
- family/archive/institutional pressure;
- one irreversible Lucien choice;
- departure or changed relationship to Vienna.

Exact act placement remains open, but A1→A2 is strongest.

## Milestone consequence

Do not add “Lucien goes to Vienna” as a milestone.

Instead, **REVISE M05** so its irreversible break can culminate in B04 Vienna:
- B02: Lucien learns/admitting containment intent;
- B03: refuses return directive;
- B04 Vienna: returns on his own terms, then refuses reassignment/withholds cooperation or makes another concrete irreversible break.

Travel is staging. The milestone is **ownership of structure without institutional obedience**.
