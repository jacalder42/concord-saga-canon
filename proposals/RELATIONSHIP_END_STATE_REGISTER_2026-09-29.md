## 0. How to read an entry

- **Current design:** the relationship's course as the architecture has it now, with sources.
- **Ruled constraints:** what cannot move. **[RULED]** content is used, never altered.
- **Conflict loop** (Companions §7): **Trigger → Reaction → Escalation → Break point → Consequences.** Repair is a
  separate field (Companions §9–§10; the author's *"Repair requires its own cause and time"*, Q3–Q4 of 09-29).
- **End state** (1.7.19): **Convergence** (toward union, shared momentum); **Divergence** (paths split, respect
  remains); **Rupture** (break under pressure, hard stop); **Evolution** (becomes something new, flips dynamic);
  **Suspension** (unresolved, holds tension for future return).
- **Repair:** where, and its own cause. Or **none**.
- **Uses:** audit proposal IDs. **F1–F11** = foundations §7 changes; **B02-n, B03-n** = Veil §4; **B04-n, B05-n,
  B06-n** = Neon §9; **B7-n, B8-n, B9-n** = Loom.
- **Touches:** **RULED** (used, content unchanged), **AD** (approved design, Tier B: the author's to change),
  **PROPOSED ROW** (a `proposed` grid row), or **neither**.

Locators are B02–B03 pass 5 (pass 4 where pass 5 carries it by reference) and B04–B09 pass 3 as amended by Neon
pass 4. Those rows are **story jobs, not beats**. Book numbers are always two digits.

   and meets his complicity with Seraphine's anger, not tears. The next contact is the ruled LT reach, and **the saga

(Rupture), Caro – Leila (Divergence), Seraphine – Mara (Suspension, conditional).

shadow, and neither retracts. The commitment (M56) is made with the words standing, and the ruled goodbye repairs a
later fight, not those words.
