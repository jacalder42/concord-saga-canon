## Decision rule

Choose *one ordinary-material anchor per event*, then validate it against available site, equipment, witnesses and physics. The anchor is **evidence something happened**, not automatic proof Resonance caused it. A source's cinematic image can remain POV/metaphor without becoming an OBJ measurement. A beat may retain unsettled origin. Do not pick a color or “wave” first and infer the physics backward.
