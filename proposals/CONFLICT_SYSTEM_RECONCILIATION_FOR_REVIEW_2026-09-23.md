# Conflict system reconciliation for author and Claude/editor review

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Status:** PROPOSAL / NOT CANON  
**Evidence:** [conflict forensic audit](../reports/CONFLICT_SYSTEMS_INTEGRITY_AUDIT_2026-09-23.md); [Mechanica and Resonance audit](../reports/MECHANICA_RESONANCE_SYSTEMS_INTEGRITY_AUDIT_2026-09-23.md).

## Working model

A conflict is a **constraint on a goal, obligation or need**. Record the party or condition applying the constraint and how a decision changes options. Emotional meaning belongs to character response. A Resonance event is a conditional overlay with its own causality and cost. This model preserves the recovered engine's interest in human choices while allowing an institutional denial, a missed ferry, an exhausted caregiver or a funding decision to remain material.

## B01 trial before any schema amendment

Sample the existing E01–02 care crisis, E19/23 investigation and authority, E24 method/risk argument, E27/29/33 prediction and pulse, E31 chosen night out, and E40/41/43 civic capacity and grounding failure. On each, answer objective, constraint, changed choice, consequence and whether Resonance is actually causally active. Leave the episode sequence and content intact. If the five-field brief cannot describe a Life/Reward episode, allow it to remain a Life/Reward episode instead of forcing an antagonist or anomaly into it.

Success would show distinct causes and consequences across personal, professional, civic and environmental scenes, with Resonance as one limited interaction rather than the master explanation. Failure would identify a precise missing field or an event whose physical mechanism is still open. Do not infer a new mechanic from a narrative need.
