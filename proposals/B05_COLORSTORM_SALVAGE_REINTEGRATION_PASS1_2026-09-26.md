# B05 Colorstorm Salvage and Reintegration — Pass 1

**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Status:** EDITORIAL SALVAGE PROPOSAL / NON-CANONICAL  
**Scope:** recover the useful B05 New Orleans Colorstorm material without restoring superseded global physics or mandatory-finale status.

## 1. Source finding

The Colorstorm is not a newly invented event.

Recovered historical material establishes:
- November B05 beat bible A3 E15 explicitly staged a New Orleans overhead Bloom / Colorstorm;
- associated older material included citywide/graded technology disruption, MT warning and a multi-spectrum visual event;
- the old version asserted emotional bleedthrough/global propagation;
- later December B05 architecture instead ends through regional storms + Santa Fe threshold;
- current author gate is a **soft lean toward retaining Colorstorm as an event**, not a ruling;
- current evidence audits explicitly permit salvaging a **local occurrence** while separating remote reports and causal linkage.

Therefore:

**Keep the event candidate. Retire the obsolete claim that Colorstorm is the first global Resonance event or the causal engine of distant phenomena.**

## 2. Proposed narrative identity

### THE COLORSTORM — working retained name

A sustained, bounded New Orleans atmospheric/optical anomaly becomes visible enough and persistent enough to enter ordinary city life.

Its strongest story function is not:
- attack;
- explosion;
- final boss;
- proof of planetary propagation.

Its strongest function is:
**beautiful anomaly → cultural absorption → normalization → inconvenience/unease → evidentiary value without causal certainty.**

The city lives under it.

## 3. Why it belongs in B05

B05 is increasingly about **different systems adapting differently**.

Parallel theaters:
- Singapore — engineered control / Technarc legitimacy;
- Reykjavík — evidence / uncertainty / Elisabet;
- Chicago — care / evacuation / Caro;
- New Orleans — public culture living with persistent anomaly;
- Santa Fe — warning / threshold.

Colorstorm gives NOLA a distinct job without requiring another plot climax.

It also preserves wonder in Neon.

Escalation should not mean every manifestation becomes uglier, louder or more destructive.
A phenomenon can be:
- objectively abnormal;
- visually extraordinary;
- initially pleasurable or culturally generative;
- later burdensome;
- still not malicious.

## 4. Preferred B05 recurrence

### Act I — apparition
A bounded color/optical phenomenon appears over or through part of New Orleans.

Initial reader experience:
- beautiful;
- uncertain;
- photographable;
- publicly visible;
- not immediately dangerous.

People name it **the Colorstorm** before anyone understands it.

The colloquial name does not establish a Mechanica category.

### Act II — normalization
The phenomenon persists or recurs enough to become part of city life.

Possible human-scale consequences:
- photographers and tourists chase vantage points;
- local art/music responds;
- vendors make bad merchandise almost immediately;
- bars invent Colorstorm drinks;
- social feeds circulate confident false explanations;
- MT distinguishes observation from interpretation;
- Filaments develop practical habits around observed local conditions without claiming causal mastery;
- Technarc instruments it;
- civic actors use the same event to argue incompatible policies.

Velvet Vein remains a social hearth under altered conditions, not anomaly HQ.

Mara Niht can plausibly perform during a Colorstorm interval if her NOLA visit survives later placement review. This is an optional cultural bridge, not an event requirement.

### Act III — changed meaning
The Colorstorm has lasted long enough that beauty becomes:
- inconvenient;
- eerie;
- operationally relevant;
- evidence of persistence rather than novelty.

It can contribute one dataset to Elisabet's distributed comparison.

It does **not**:
- point magically toward Santa Fe;
- transmit emotion to Santa Fe;
- cause the Santa Fe rupture;
- prove every distant report shares one mechanism.

By the B05 close, the reader can understand:
**something once treated as spectacle has become environmental context.**

## 5. Mechanica / D5 event-card gate

Before episode-level use, the Colorstorm still needs a concrete physical event card.

Required fields:

### Initial conditions
OPEN.
Possible inputs must come from existing approved conditions rather than a new “Colorstorm power.”

### Medium / coupling
OPEN.

Candidate physical substrates to test, not approve:
- cloud/fog/humidity;
- suspended particulates;
- water films / wet surfaces;
- glass;
- river haze;
- ordinary light interacting with an altered local physical condition.

Do not make “emotion” itself the optical medium.

### Objective observations
Need a bounded set such as:
- anomalous spectral/color behavior visible to ordinary observers;
- photographs/video record at least some ordinary optical effect;
- footprint has edges/gradients or otherwise measurable locality;
- persistence/recurrence can be timed.

### Interpretation
Competing hypotheses should survive:
- atmospheric/meteorological;
- infrastructure/light pollution/industrial;
- Resonance-related;
- mixed ordinary conditioning + Resonance;
- public misinformation.

### Limits
Must establish:
- footprint;
- duration/recurrence;
- what it does **not** affect;
- whether ordinary weather changes appearance;
- whether the phenomenon is visible from outside the footprint.

### Cost
Do not force lethal cost.

Possible costs worth testing:
- navigation/visibility;
- sleep/disorientation;
- traffic/event management;
- tourism/crowd pressure;
- sensor reliability;
- social panic or normalization;
- operational distraction.

### Residue/evidence
Ordinary recordings and environmental observations may persist.
No recording contains or transmits Resonance.

## 6. Technology boundary

Historical versions included AR/holochat outages and exact-duration freezes.

Do not restore those automatically.

Preferred approach:
- Colorstorm and technology effects are separately observable;
- degraded technology may correlate spatially/temporally;
- service interruption can be graded;
- some ordinary devices continue working;
- recordings can document visible effects;
- no machine directly reads Intent/emotion/VT;
- no digital Resonance transmission.

A later event card can decide whether a specific local technology degradation belongs to the Colorstorm conditions or is merely concurrent.

## 7. Cultural afterlife

If retained, Colorstorm should leave memory beyond B05.

Possible later references:
- “before/after the Colorstorm” as local shorthand;
- photographs/art/music survive;
- public explanations remain contested;
- a later character remembers when the worsening world still looked beautiful;
- post-Mending people reinterpret the event with more information but still cannot retroactively prove every old theory.

This gives Neon history rather than disposable spectacle.

## 9. Classification

Colorstorm should eventually live in:
- **event inventory** as a named B05 local event-condition;
- B05 act/episode architecture as recurring environmental presence;
- location/place-memory architecture for NOLA;
- possibly VFX grammar after physical medium is approved.

It should **not** become:
- a Resonance taxonomy category;
- a global propagation law;
- a mandatory saga milestone;
- a substitute for Santa Fe;
- proof that emotions become weather.

## 10. Editorial payoff

This salvage preserves four things the old design did well:
1. a memorable name;
2. a distinctive visual identity;
3. New Orleans as a lived Resonance environment;
4. wonder becoming culturally normalized.

It discards four things that caused later structural/mechanical trouble:
1. “first global” inflation;
2. emotion-as-weather handwave;
3. automatic planetary causal coupling;
4. mandatory B05-climax status.

The result is stronger because **Colorstorm becomes part of the world instead of an announcement about the plot.**
