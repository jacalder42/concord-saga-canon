## 1. How the tells work

**Each tell is a permission, not a quota** (Q-FL5). It follows writer profile §12A:
- plain type;
- meaning from context;
- no phonetic accent;
- never kitsch.

A tell appears at moments of stress, tenderness or habit. A whole book may use it only a few times.

## 3. Baz, derived from his canon card

**What his card holds** (`canon/characters/BazID.md`):
- born in Marseille, in the northern districts;
- his father, Arnaud, is a dockworker;
- his mother, Samira, is a French-Algerian nurse who ran a bilingual household;
- his identity line: *"language and translation as power and responsibility."*

**The derived tell:**
- **Marseille French for precision and for swearing,** at work and in anger. It is the language he thinks in when things
  go wrong fast.
- **A few words of his mother's Algerian Arabic only in tenderness or grief:** a blessing, a family endearment. Never for
  show.
- **His real tell is that he translates for other people.** He switches language to help someone else understand,
  choosing what to carry across and what to leave. That is his card's *power and responsibility*, and it suits a man
  whose method is honest record.

**Before use:** a native-speaker check of any Arabic or Marseille French (Q-FL6, Q-FL8).

## 4. Seraphine (answered 2026-10-01: her elders, both registers, mostly forms of address)

**Approved (Q-FL4):** a few words of her family's Louisiana French, heard more under pressure and carried by cadence.
**Honorifics and nicknames carry much of it.** The author: *"Honorifics and nicknames are common and important in that
culture."*

**Still open: which family strand the words come from.** The recovery records two versions, neither restated for her
current origin:
- V1, Cajun French inflections;
- V2, Louisiana Creole phrases from elders.

**Recommended:** her family's elders, in both registers, as forms of address rather than full sentences. To be confirmed
at the revision's packet build.

## 5. The naming system (Q-FL6)

**The 2025-12-05 naming bible stays as recovered design:** native forms of a name signal vulnerability, and there are
intimacy names (Lucien's French for Seraphine).

**The rule:** use a name only when a scene needs it, after a native-speaker check. Its surnames are stale; current canon
names win.
