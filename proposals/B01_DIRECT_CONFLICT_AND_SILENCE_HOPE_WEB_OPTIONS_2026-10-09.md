## A direct conflict (Q-DC1–4)

The book has one direct contest, the phone at the jack post, and the crew loses it. Every other run-in is a demand the
crew refuses. Caldas stays at surveillance in B01 (approved progression), so the contest is fought with his side's
instrument.

- **A (recommended): *Medical exempt*.** A new chapter at about 80%. Seraphine takes the delivery hold to the issuing
  office with her own evidence. Lucien pays by putting his name on a city record as the false caller.
  - **The win:** medical gases and supplies are exempted from the holds.
  - **For the opposition:** the woman in cream shoes loses her lever.
  - **For the reader:** the holds turn out to come off the same line the cases come to.
  - **Cost and score:** about +2.0–2.6k words; it needs one relaxation of approved design (Q-SD3, *the holds thin*). The
    hostile readers' "contest they can win or lose" test moves from about 1.5 to about 4.
- **B, *The plate*:** a rematch with the collectors. It wins little.
- **C, *Bastien*:** Caldas's side shuts Baz out of housing. It is the most face-to-face option, but it pulls Caldas's B02
  intimidation forward and needs G15 relaxed.
- **D, *The gallery's names*:** a human strand in the climax. It is cheap, but it is still a refusal; best as an add-on.

## Silence, Hope and the web (Q-SH1–6)

Built on the author's direction: **the web is where the Veil still works; the events are its failures**; revelation
runs as a sine curve; her feeling draws the watchers in E50.

- **Seraphine's ladder, which rises and falls:**
  - a flicker at E03;
  - a stronger moment at E30 (the waltz hall);
  - doubt and loss of it at E41;
  - the peak at E49, after Baz's word.

  It is felt in the hands, never logged, named or tied to the events.
- **Four separation rules:** the web is never felt at or before an onset, is never stronger where events happen, predicts
  nothing, and never makes an event smaller. These protect *it keeps to places*.
- **The rest:**
  - the prologue's held thing becomes a net strung among people, worn thin at one place;
  - at E49 she infers the folder tab's word and does not ask;
  - E50 reacts to someone below feeling the net hold, and *the line moved* goes, protecting Tahl's ruled VT contacts;
  - one sentence in I01;
  - a lean yes for an Act I interlude after E14, so the watchers appear once per act;
  - a provisional breadcrumb row;
  - a B02 E41 note.
- **Cost:** about +260 to +400 words, or +560 to +700 with the interlude.
- **Flag:** check the web direction at the next Mechanica §42A review.
