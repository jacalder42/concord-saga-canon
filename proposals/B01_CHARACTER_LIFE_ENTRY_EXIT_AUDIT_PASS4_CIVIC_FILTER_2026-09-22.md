# B01 Character Life / Entry / Exit Audit — Pass 4: Civic Name Filter

Status: PROPOSAL / RECOVERY RECONCILIATION — NON-CANONICAL
Date: 2026-09-22
Scope: optional New Orleans civic/cultural faces against actual B01 v4.1a demand.
Decision vocabulary: NAME / UNNAMED / DEFER / CUT FROM B01.
Purpose: prevent “approved saga cast” from becoming “everyone must debut in Book 1.”

## Governing rule

A historical B1 appearance is evidence, not an obligation.

Name a civic face in B01 only when at least one is true:
1. recognition itself creates a later payoff;
2. the person already has a real job/life that naturally intersects the scene;
3. reusing them prevents a recurring utility-role duplicate;
4. their B01 state is necessary to understand a later transformation.

Otherwise use an anonymous civilian/professional or defer the character.

# 1. LEILA BROUSSARD — NAME, SELECTIVELY

## Provenance

Historical Leila explicitly has:
- B1 ER nurse handling odd cases;
- B2 triage during shard flickers;
- B3 overwhelmed medical-system pressure;
- B4 collapse/surge triage.

Current consolidation merges Nurse Camille Dupré into Leila.

This is a genuine B1 recurrence root, not a retroactive cameo.

## Why B01 earns the name

v4.1a already needs:
- Caro independent medical/civic competence;
- Caro/Elisabet recognition/safety;
- medical/civic aftermath;
- later bodily consequence.

Leila can provide **medical-system continuity without creating another nurse**.

## Guardrail

Do not make Leila:
- Caro's exposition partner;
- anomaly expert;
- instant member of the protagonist circle;
- present in every medical scene.

Her first job is to be an ER nurse doing her job.

## Entry

Natural coordinate:
the medical/civic environment where Caro and Elisabet's B1 intersection is finally welded.

Leila should already be busy before protagonists arrive.

## Ordinary life requirement

B01 only needs one or two civilian indicators:
- shift pressure;
- colleague/patient obligation;
- practical preference/routine;
- what she wants to happen after shift.

Do not build a subplot.

## Exit

She finishes or survives a bad shift and returns to the medical system.

That is enough to make B2 recognition pay.

# 2. MME ROSETTE — NAME, LIGHT

## Provenance

Recovered progression:
- B1 — senses “pressure drops”;
- B2 — recognizes resonance “weather”;
- B3 — warns children about “thin air”;
- B4 — warns of “thin air days.”

This is unusually clean low-bandwidth recurrence.

## Why B01 earns the name

Her value is precisely that she is **not an expert**.

She gives the city a folk vocabulary that can evolve before formal explanation catches up.

A quiet B1 appearance creates recognition payoff in B2/B3 without requiring plot involvement.

## Guardrail

No secret knowledge.
No prophecy.
No Filament leadership.
No magical sensing taxonomy.

Her language is ordinary observation, analogy, experience and neighborhood memory.

## Entry

Best around an ordinary neighborhood/crowd episode where she would plausibly already be present.

Do not stop the narrative to explain her.

## Exit

She goes back to whatever she was doing.

No closure required.

# 3. RAMON ESPINA — DEFER BY DEFAULT

## Provenance

Historical source is real:
- Jeremiah Knox B1–B3 transit technician;
- Ramon visual-canon transit worker/early anomaly witness;
- identities consolidated to Ramon.

His eventual function is strong:
physical transit/infrastructure improvisation.

## Why not automatically name him in B01

v4.1a can show:
- transit delay;
- signal failure;
- route change;
- ordinary infrastructure consequence

without introducing the person who will eventually embody that system.

The reader already has many names to learn.

## Exception

NAME Ramon in B01 only if an infrastructure scene becomes sufficiently human that:
- he acts rather than reports;
- his decision affects people;
- we see him doing his job;
- later recognition will matter.

Otherwise:
**anonymous transit worker / public works crew in B01; Ramon enters B2.**

This does not erase his historical B1 provenance. It is a deliberate migration choice.

# 4. SHIRIN PETROV — DEFER FROM FOREGROUND

## Provenance

Strong recovered long arc:
B1 student anomaly spreadsheet → B2 district mapping → later public/global data infrastructure.

## Problem

A named B1 “student with anomaly spreadsheet” risks making the world feel prematurely organized and creates another investigator adjacent to Lucien/Baz.

B1's boundary is:
global/systemic extent suspected, not established.

## Best B01 treatment

Do not introduce Shirin as an active protagonist contact.

Optional:
- a crude crowdsourced sheet/link is visible;
- a supplement/public artifact contains incomplete amateur aggregation;
- no emphasis on author/creator identity.

If later review determines recognition is valuable, her name can exist in tiny print/background without a character entrance.

## Entry target

B2 is cleaner:
the data has grown enough that the person maintaining it becomes narratively meaningful.

This makes:
**artifact first → person later**
a recognition payoff.

# 12. Important principle: unnamed does not mean disposable

Anonymous B01 people should still have:
- specific behavior;
- wants;
- inconvenience;
- humor;
- fear;
- competence;
- relationships visible at the edge.

The purpose of pruning names is not to turn civilians into wallpaper.

It is to preserve the distinction between:
**a vivid human being in one scene**
and
**a character the reader is being asked to remember.**

# 15. Recommendation for consolidated cast map

Build the B01 cast-entry/exit map using three statuses:

**ON-PAGE NAMED**
reader is expected to remember.

**ON-PAGE UNNAMED / WORLD POPULATION**
specific humans, no recurrence contract.

**OFF-PAGE / DEFERRED**
exists in saga canon but B01 makes no demand on reader memory.

Then map every v4.1a episode against those statuses.

Do not reopen the beat spine unless the map reveals:
- a character enters before causality supports them;
- a relationship advances without prior contact;
- a support face is overloaded;
- a major character disappears implausibly;
- a named character has no B01 exit/continuity state.
