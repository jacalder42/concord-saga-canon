# B01 Act II: scene-function map, condensation and pressure fill

**What it is.** A map of every Act II scene in draft 3 (E16–E36, about 47,600 words, manuscript after the ending-reveal
revision). It marks the repeats, proposes where the procedural investigation can be condensed, and proposes what fills
the space: outside pressure and mystery. Every merge or cut waits for the author (§5).

**What it does not change.** No packet, card, grid row, overlay, ruling or manuscript text. It does not change the
approved pressure design (`proposals/B01_PRESSURE_DESIGN_PACKET_PLACEMENT_MAP_2026-10-03.md` §0: Hand 1, the people
behind the Institute, works through paper, permission and people, with Caldas Ren seen only; Hand 2, the researchers,
works through instruments and collection, with TD- tags, and buys, takes or files for the record of where and when). It
does not change D1/D3/D5 (no explanation), Q-V3-2 (no *Resonance*), the rule that *Veil* appears only at E49/E50, C3
(Baz never sees a case, the collector or the tag), T5/C4 (Hand 2's first direct request to the crew is B02 E13), or A4
(*ripples first* held). It quotes no manuscript text.

**Sources read.** E14–E15 and E16–E36 in full; E37–E49 openings and every Act III reference to Act II people and objects;
`draft-notes/draft3/CONTINUITY_FACTS.md`; prose packets B01-E16…E36; the engine's surface rules R1–R8 (Q-EN5).
