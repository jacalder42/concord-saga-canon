## 4. Proposed institutional model

Do not make Dominion a monolithic employer where every character has “Dominion Agent” on a badge.

Better model:
**a transnational heritage-derived institutional network with chartered/aligned civic, research, archival and regulatory bodies.**

At B01 altitude, ordinary professionals can work inside Dominion-aligned entities without thinking of themselves primarily as faction operatives.

This explains:
- Lucien's civic/cultural systems bureau;
- Baz's possible later civic-facing entity;
- Marius's archive role;
- Helena's family/institution overlap;
- Virelli's authority to pressure/reassign;
- why Dominion can become politically dangerous without every employee being villainous.
