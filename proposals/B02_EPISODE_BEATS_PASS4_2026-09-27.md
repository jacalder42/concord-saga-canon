## 1. THE BASELINE IS WORK NOW
pass 2 · slot 01 | new · pass 3

### Story job
Show that Baz has chosen to stay and do this work. The strange baseline is now his job.

### Beats
- A working morning in NOLA: calls, a map, a notebook that **keeps observation and inference in separate columns**.
- He checks a small reported anomaly and finds it ordinary. He records that too.
- An ordinary exchange shows he is becoming known locally: a coffee order, a nod.
- He files what is still unexplained without forcing it into a pattern.

Baz's ethic in one gesture: **revise, don't defend.**

## 3. THE FIRST PULSE
pass 2 · slot 03 | recovered · A1 E03

### Story job
Give the city its first shared public event, and its first word for it.

### Beats
- Several mundane witnesses in different places at once: a driver, a bartender, a kid, a nurse on break.
- Secondary effects only: lights stutter, sound warps, emotions feel "thick", devices glitch.

### Guardrail

separate. No explanation.
