## Demonstration of the proposed scene check

**Low-amplitude Book 1 example — test only, not an episode amendment.** A tired witness hears a light fitting buzz while standing in humid air; a second person hears it too; the fitting later fails inspection. The resonant interpretation remains uncertain until ordinary electrical causes are checked. Record the fixture, humidity, location, timing, competing explanation, physical residue and cost to the person investigating. A character's fear alone does not certify the cause.

**Meta transfer example — deliberately incomplete.** “Crowd fear charges meta; operative draws a boost” fails the gate: what is captured, how close, what changed in the crowd, what stores it, how it leaves the meta, what couples it to the operative, what skill it enhances, and who suffers remain unanswered. It must stay an unresolved design brief.
