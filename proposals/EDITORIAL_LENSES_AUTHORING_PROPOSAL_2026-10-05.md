# Editorial lenses: authored fields

## 1. How the fields were written

The 2026-09-19 ruling barred two things: authoring before recovery and distillation were done, and filling the fields
*"by inference from the board members' published work, which would put invented lens criteria into canon under real
people's names."* The author has now judged the first bar too exclusive for this phase. **The second concern is kept.**
So:

- **Each lens is defined by its saved focus line, not by the person.** The focus comes from the Codex wording the project
  already holds (§2).
- **The criteria come from the saga's own instruments:** the writer profile, the author's rulings, the Jazz reading test,
  the review watch-list, the reader-archetype panel and the beta-panel metrics. They are what each focus means *here*.
- **Nothing is attributed to a named person.** The lenses do not quote, paraphrase or claim to represent Neil Gaiman,
  Matt Dinniman, Ali Hazelwood, Travis Deverell, SenLinYu, Nate Regier, Steve Jobs, Hannah Fry or Questlove. A lens is
  shorthand for a craft focus. Invoke it as *"through the Dinniman lens"*, never *"Dinniman would say"*.
- **The export contains simulated board debates with quotes attributed to these people** (for example in
  `2025-11-08__Cast_and_characters_request`). They are assistant text, not statements by those people. **They were not
  used.**
- **This lens does not type characters.** The Regier lens uses the PCM vocabulary of conditions and distress. It does not
  assign types to any character, which would be an inference the author has not made.

## 3. The lenses

**When they run** (unchanged): immediately before prose generation (Gate Review), and during post-prose editing
(Editorial Audit). Findings and flags only.

### Primary board

**Neil Gaiman: mythic weave and humanity**
- **Asks:** Does the strange stay observable while the people stay specific? Whose motif is this, and is it on the page
  with the right person (Q-MS4: the one already on the page wins)? Does a return carry a change?
- **Flags:** a mechanism explained or named *(ruled against)*; a mythic register in an ordinary scene; one motif owned by
  two characters; a collective voice replacing a human one; recurrence that only re-describes.
- **Protects:** the unexplained cause; one or two sensory channels at a time; small, ordinary wonder; the motif that
  pays on the page.

**Matt Dinniman: web-serial momentum and chaos craft**
- **Asks:** What does the reader get to enjoy here: a reversal, a rule used cleverly, a joke that costs something? Does
  humor stay local to the pressure in the scene? Does the pace turn?
- **Flags:** scheduled comedy; three quips where one precise line would do; a joke that makes a consequence disappear
  (profile §6); chaos without cost; runs of episodes ending the same way.
- **Protects:** gallows humor rare, brief and sharp; the rhythm section's hooks; fun tracked for presence, not capped
  (ruled 2026-09-27).

**Ali Hazelwood: romance architecture and intimacy logic**
- **Asks:** What does each person have to gain by moving toward the other, and what does this scene change between them?
  Is attention selective, different from how they attend to everyone? Is consent present?
- **Flags:** tenderness given equally to everyone; heat without emotional cause; chemistry stated, not shown; romance
  declared aloud where B01 rules it out; a charge that is absent where the ladder expects one.
- **Protects:** the B01 heat design (sparks, the cover kiss, charge at the end); consent as ruled; nothing strange
  creating desire *(ruled)*; heat at the pace the relationship has earned on the page.

**Travis Deverell: fun, heart and reader flow**
- **Asks:** Would a reader start the next episode tonight, and why? Does the episode deliver one reward? Does the
  character-first energy survive the plot?
- **Flags:** restated meaning; recaps; procedural repetition; slow starts; a closing hook that is a formula.
- **Protects:** the episode as a unit; momentum from people, not mechanism; the beta panels' *pull* measure.

**SenLinYu: tension, emotional honesty and slow-burn precision**
- **Asks:** Is the feeling carried by choice and behaviour more than by naming? Is the vulnerability legible at the
  moment of the line? What does restraint cost this person here?
- **Flags:** emotion named where behaviour would carry it; two characters diagnosing a third in the same words;
  melodrama spinning up; a stock gesture doing the writing.
- **Protects:** stripped syntax at vulnerability; one true sensory detail; subtext in dialogue; the slow burn.

**Nate Regier: PCM alignment, conflict and repair**
- **Asks:** What does this person need, how do they get worse under pressure, and what trap do they fall into when
  overwhelmed (profile §5A)? Whose will pushes back? Is any repair caused and given its own time?
- **Flags:** a repair that closes a scene without a cause; everyone communicating well at once; a misunderstanding kept
  with nothing sustaining it; one conflict style in every mouth; manufactured friction.
- **Protects:** wants that collide; kindness that intrudes; disagreements that leave something changed; repair that comes
  late, partly or never.

**Steve Jobs: clarity, minimalism and narrative UX**
- **Asks:** What does a first-time reader need at this moment, and what is the least that gives it? Is the way through
  the scene legible? What can be cut without losing a promise?
- **Flags:** explanation before it is needed; terms used before they are earned; an overbuilt packet; a feature with no
  payoff; packaging snags (working titles, a spelling lapse).
- **Protects:** the reader's orientation; reduction; the delight curve; the reader as collaborator (profile §1).

### Secondary consultants

**Hannah Fry: systems clarity and narrative mathematics**
- **Asks:** If a reader checked, would the logic hold: who knew what, when? Is a number on the page because it changes a
  decision (profile §3A)? Is the complexity readable?

**Questlove: cadence, rhythm and improvisational structure**
- **Asks:** Where is the accent, and does an expected beat arrive late or turned? Who carries the melody and who the
  rhythm section? Does a rest change something, or only restore breath? (`ebci/prose/JAZZ_READING_TEST.md`)
