## 2. The first tear: what the sources and the page already give

**The original design** (`seraphine_full.md` §IV–§VI, historical; `SeraphineIdentity.md` §XII, the distilled card) has
these parts:
- *Care + urgency + helplessness + human grief without release* exceed the Veil's buffering.
- The first fissure forms: *"Localized, unstable, unnoticed by the wider world."*
- *"The swamp becomes imprinted as a resonance sink."*
- *"Seraphine does not perceive this as power. She only knows something answered when it shouldn't have."*
- The Möbius: *"The saga closes where it began: the swamp."*

**Reconciled with §42A** (Version B, the conversation's recommendation):
- The cap's stored pressure supplies the load.
- The camp is a point near threshold.
- Seraphine's involuntary convergence is the last disturbance, and it gives.
- She is causally involved, not culpable.
- Ordinary grief does not tear the Veil.

**The page already carries the empirical traces.** The 10-13 proposal (§4) said the pressure *decays*. It does not: it is bound
to the place.

| Where | Trace |
| --- | --- |
| Prologue | *"Is it giving?" "Not yet."*: the give is pending |
| Ch 1 | The fullness is in the whole room, on everything equally, before the notification. At *"He's gone,"* the coffee goes to fine ripples (revision 3) |
| **Ch 2** | Walking away the next morning, the fullness *thins around forty* paces. **Walking back, around forty it shuts over her again**: bounded, and still there |
| **Ch 4** | **Four days later**, at the camp, Lucien's verticals lean while she reads her statement; the CO meter reads zero; the level is true |
| Ch 29, Ch 30 | Marius: *"the report on the camp went up in March. Not by me."* The Institute treats the camp as significant |
| Ch 35 | The empty-room test: *"Empty room, morning after. Had it already."* |
| EP | Water trembling *"at the edge of the low wet country… where it began, neither of them saw"* |

So the page has a persistent, bounded local disturbance at the camp, measured twice, and the Institute has noticed. **What it
lacks is anyone saying so.** The crew never counts the camp as a *place* under their own rule (*it keeps to places*), and nobody
asks *why there*. That is the missing B01 receipt the brief describes. It can be added without the chain, without Seraphine
learning she triggered it, and without returning to the camp (Q-DR7).

## 4. Proposed revision 3 change list, if the author approves §5

About 400–700 words, in four places, all additive.

1. **Ch 2 (Q-R3-3):** one sentence of Seraphine's perception, from the source: something answered that shouldn't have. It is her
   feeling, not a cause, and she does not take it as anything she did.
2. **Ch 29 or Ch 35 (Q-R3-2):** when the crew lays out *it keeps to places*, the camp becomes place one, from her own Ch 2 paces and
   Lucien's Ch 4 lean. Someone asks *why there*, and nobody answers. It is stated as the first place they know of. It is not
   stated as the origin, and no cause is given.
3. **Ch 40 or Ch 43 (Q-R3-2):** one report from the camp, without anyone returning. Mrs. Doucet tells Seraphine that the water in
   the dog's dish on the porch still won't lie flat. Still water stays the instrument; no animal reacts.
4. **Ch 30:** no change. The sheet already says *where it is thin, things come through*, and Elisabet's *unknown* (Ch 38) stays
   *why it is thinner where it is thin*.
