**B1-38A/B (hold).** The old Bywater/Tremé details are recoverable *design possibilities* embedded in one episode, not new canon. **B1-40A (hold).** Actor: one accountable public worker or existing recurring face only if legitimately in that service. Need actual service change, duration, consequence and mundane alternative before claiming a separate event or assigning Resonance as cause.
