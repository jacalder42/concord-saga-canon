## 0. Governing identity

**B03 = CONSEQUENCE / TRUTH HAS A COST.** Information changes behaviour, and institutions contest
evidence. **Every reading of the southwest lean converges on the Warehouse District, and each reads
it differently:**

| Reader | How it reads the Warehouse District |
| --- | --- |
| **Baz's** standing-conditions map | *"not standing: building"* |
| **The Dominion's** Bywater rule | *"a zone: contain it"* |
| **Technarc's** model | *"stable: nothing building"* |
| **Anonymous MT** | a warning, with coordinates |
| **The city's folk map** | *"safe, because it isn't standing"* |

**Nobody owns the whole model** (pass 2, slot 14).

**Baz is the Veil lead through his death.** Tahl is named only in the epilogue (A10).

**Scale:** **45 narrative episodes** (A1: 15, A2: 15, A3: 15), plus **a three-episode epilogue**
(E46–E48, the `EP` position). There are **6 LR units** and **4 supplements**. Pass 2 said ~42–45,
and B01 has 48, B02 47.

**The causal paragraph from B02** (direction 2):

> B02 ended with a standing restricted-zone protocol, a Dominion that had acted on Baz's grounds and
> now applies *"the Bywater rule"* elsewhere, a stretch that does not reset, drift pointing southwest,
> Lucien's withheld drift and pending refusal, and Technarc outside the circle. **So B03 begins with
> zones multiplying as care, Baz redrawing his model around what stays, and a Dominion ready to read
> the next anomaly as a zone to contain.** When the lean reaches the Warehouse District, the Dominion
> contains rather than evacuates (the B02 protocol, A9: expected), and Technarc arrives on its own
> reading (outside the circle, A9: unexpected).

## 5. The Warehouse — bounded event model and counterfactual (direction 3; D5)

**Status:** [P]. This is an **observational** model. It says what is seen, where, for how long, and
what survives as evidence. **It supplies no mechanism.** D5 holds causal physics, and the Rupture is
never explained (Mechanica §60). It is the *"bounded event model before causal staging is locked"*
that direction 3 asks for.

| Field | Model [P] |
| --- | --- |
| **Precursors** (E13, E21, E27–E30) | **Intermittent** episodes in one building, **each a little larger**. It is **unlike the Bywater stretch, which stands.** Observed: alarms, workers' complaints, hums, lights, devices failing briefly |
| **Onset** (E31–E34) | A sustained hum. Light shifts and devices fail within **about a block**. **It can be felt a few blocks out.** The crowd and the perimeter are both present |
| **The Rupture** (E36) | **A shock, felt as pressure and sound.** Glass fails. **The building's older section partially collapses**, and people inside are trapped |
| **Limits** | **No citywide effect, no synchronization, no second event.** Injuries in the crowd and among responders. **One death: Baz.** The *"first responder"* in the reports is Baz (A7) [P: no second death] |
| **Rival readings** | **An old building's structural failure, triggered or worsened**, against **a Resonance event**. Both stay live in the public record (E42). The narrator certifies neither |
| **Durable evidence** | Footage; MT's pre-event post; Shirin's logs; responder records; **the child**. The site stays affected, but **its duration and behaviour are measured later** (M08: *"any durable New Orleans scar is measured separately"*) |
| **Models** | **It exceeds all three:** Baz's, the Dominion's and Technarc's (link 8). None predicted a shock |

**The responsibility chain, kept separate** (direction 3; *"no blame, just inference"*):

| Strand | What it did | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The post (S03)** | Warned accurately. It helped the worker (E32), and it may have drawn others | **Inference only.** Never narrator-certified |
| **The institutions** | The Dominion contained instead of evacuating (E34). Technarc held a door and withheld what it knew (E33) | Shown; neither caused the Rupture |
| **Baz's rescue** | He chose to go in, and freed the child | Shown |
| **The physical failure** | The Rupture and the collapse | Observed, not explained |

**No one murders him** (the 09-19 recovery).

**The counterfactual is private** (E46). Only Tahl asks it, and the book never answers it. The
reader holds every piece and no verdict.
