# 7. MATERIAL REPAIR A — CARO

## Current v4.1a problem
E6 says Caro “may be glimpsed.”
E30 is her first required meaningful beat.

That conflicts with current author ruling:
**Caro and Seraphine are friends before page one.**

If Caro is Seraphine's buddy/human foil, a mere glimpse makes her feel retroactively installed for the Elisabet romance.

## Minimal repair

### E3 — The Day Still Happens
Make Caro one element of the ordinary life that still happens.

Not a “Caro scene.”
Not a biography.
Not romance setup.

Function:
- Seraphine has someone who knows how she normally behaves;
- Caro can detect that Seraphine is carrying the swamp death badly;
- wit/sarcasm creates ordinary relational texture;
- Caro has her own work/errand/time pressure and leaves for it.

This establishes:
**preexisting friendship + independent life.**

### E6 — Room Is Still Open
Caro may be there because she and Seraphine sometimes go there.
If present, Trip/Caro/Seraphine should feel like existing social geometry, not a roll call.

### E28 — Plans for After
Caro can be part of the pleasant future plan, further proving she exists outside C/E.

### E30
Now functions correctly:
the reader already knows Caro.
The new event is **Elisabet becoming personally important to her**, not “meet Caro.”
