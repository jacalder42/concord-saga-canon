## 0. Identity

**B04 = FIRST FRACTURE: GRIEF BECOMES PUBLIC ARGUMENT.** Truth acquires a public cost. The world does
not simply learn that Resonance is real; it learns that **institutions and communities respond
differently, and that the responses themselves have consequences.**

**Entry state** (the B03 exit, as amended): the body is not yet recovered; the cast fears it is Baz;
Rieux is injured and his log sealed in a Dominion file; *"advise and contain"* is the protocol's rule;
Technarc's presence is exposed in footage; Lucien has asked Helena for a favour; Tahl, named for the
reader, has a private rule; Rex is in Detroit, having lost a contract; Caro and Elisabet have kissed.

**Scale:** **45 episodes** (15/15/15), **5 LR units**, **4 supplements**. No epilogue.

**POV:** Tahl 10 · Seraphine 12 · Lucien 6 · Rex 6 · Caro 3 · Elisabet 3 · Kade 2 · flex 3 (E05 and E31 re-owned to Seraphine (Neon pass 4, 2026-09-27; `proposals/NEON_PASS4_SURGICAL_2026-09-27.md`)).

## 5. Exit state (B04 → B05)

- **The world:** the Filament split is irreversible; Protocol 9 is in force; the quieting movement
  has a caring face and a loud amplifier; the Riot is claimed by every camp.
- **Seraphine:** publicly visible and listed; she has learned **too early** and **constraint as
  care**, and holds neither yet.
- **Lucien:** in Vienna, holding Rieux's log, owing Janvier a meeting, meaning to come home.
- **Tahl:** remorseful, overloaded, trusted for an hour; **he has asked to meet them.**
- **Rex:** in NOLA, in the cast, MT's verified source.
- **Caro and Elisabet:** openly together; each with her own work.
- **Kade:** in NOLA, a listener, who has seen the triangle.
