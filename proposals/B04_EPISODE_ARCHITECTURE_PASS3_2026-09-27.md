## 0. Identity

**B04 = FIRST FRACTURE: GRIEF BECOMES PUBLIC ARGUMENT.** Truth acquires a public cost. The world does
not simply learn that Resonance is real; it learns that **institutions and communities respond
differently, and that the responses themselves have consequences.**

**Entry state** (the B03 exit, as amended): the body is not yet recovered; the cast fears it is Baz;
Rieux is injured and his log sealed in a Dominion file; *"advise and contain"* is the protocol's rule;
Technarc's presence is exposed in footage; Lucien has asked Helena for a favour; Tahl, named for the
reader, has a private rule; Rex is in Detroit, having lost a contract; Caro and Elisabet have kissed.
**Also (propagated 2026-10-28, §627, N2/N3):** the child Baz died rescuing is **Nyla** (§625); the reader knows what Baz found before he died (B03 D3–D4), and **the cast does not yet know that his funder was a faction inside Dominion**.

**Trilogy questions (§624; propagated 2026-10-28, §627, N1):**
- **Mystery:** *What is breaking in the Southwest, and is it linked to the other breaks?* Asked on the B04 page at E27 (N1b).
- **Dramatic:** *Once the truth is public and exploited, can the people who know it make the world act in time?* Asked at E30 (unchanged). Answered in B06.

**Entry point (contract test 2; propagated 2026-10-28, §627, N11):** the split, Nyla, and resonance's behaviour are re-delivered at E02–E15 (E02, E04, E06, E08, E15).

**Scale:** **45 episodes** (15/15/15), **5 LR units**, **4 supplements**. No epilogue.

**POV:** Tahl 10 · Seraphine 12 · Lucien 6 · Rex 5 · Caro 3 · Elisabet 4 · Kade 2 · flex 3 (E05 and E31 re-owned to Seraphine (Neon pass 4, 2026-09-27; `proposals/NEON_PASS4_SURGICAL_2026-09-27.md`); E06 re-owned Rex → Elisabet (propagated 2026-10-28, §627, N2)).
