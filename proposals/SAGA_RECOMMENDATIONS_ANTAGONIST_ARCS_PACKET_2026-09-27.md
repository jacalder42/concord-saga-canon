## 1. Antagonists: Loom's opposition and the causal chain

### A1. Loom's active opposition: three operational axes, plus Kade's splinter

**Recommended.** The institutions and doctrinaires stay legacy, as their cards say. Loom's **active**
opposition runs on three distinct axes. This matches the cards' own rule that spectacle comes from
splinters and extremists, not from the institutions:

| Axis | Who | What they offer (C08: a real offer) | What it costs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Narrative** | **Elias / Brightbreak** (ruled placement) | Certainty and coordination in chaos | Authorization, without truth |
| **Operational** | **A Technarc hardliner remnant**: the splinter left when Han Wei's model fails (A5) | Measurement, sensors, services and protection that actually work | Dependence and containment. They **track** Resonance and pursue anomalies |
| **Relief** | **The Choirless remnant**: fragmented, with an optional masked Solace (POOL-033) | An end to pain | The erasure of feeling and choice |
| *(protagonist-adjacent)* | **Kade's splinter** (M44, M46, ruled) | Agency and voice | Violence it authorizes |

**Why:**

- It gives every ruled Loom row an actor:
  - the remnant **pursues** at Serpent Mound (M48, *"who is escaped from"*);
  - it **searches** NOLA (M50);
  - it **fragments** under M30.
- The three axes stay **operationally distinct**: story, services, relief.
- It gives **Rex** his Loom mirror: the engineer who left, against the systems that stayed.

**Guardrails:**

- No named Technarc leader on the page. Han Wei's card forbids his confronting the protagonists.
- The remnant is people doing a job, not a villain parade.
- *"Total collapse needs evidence"* (M30).

### A2. Why the factions believe "Louisiana", and how they learn the site in B09 A3

This is open under M31 and M51.

- **Option A (recommended): they read it off their instruments.**
  - The remnant's instruments register the B08 storm-wall opening (M31). The reading points
    **generally to Louisiana**.
  - Elias frames the opening publicly as Kade's.
  - The NOLA feint (M50) **exploits that measured inference**. That is the review's C12: a feint
    should exploit an adversary's inference, not expose civilians.
  - The site becomes detectable **only when the Mending begins**: the Mending is itself the signal.
    That puts the convergence in A3 and nowhere earlier, which is M51 and the site secret.
  - It fits Q5 (the ordinary witness or record that reaches Elias at a distance in B08).
- **B:** a human leak or witness in B09 A3.
- **C:** Elias deduces it.

A is recommended because it is causal, it needs no traitor, and it makes the siege begin only in A3
**by mechanism, not by fiat**.

### A3. Ito: the man becomes legacy, the movement stays active

**Recommended:** the cards govern the man. Ito peaks with the Choirless's **first overt violence
(B06)**, then fades **through exhaustion** in B07 (*"the Choirless radicalize beyond him"*). The
registry's *"spread B7; peak B8; Mending opposition B9"* is read as **the movement's curve, not the
man's**. The registry row gets a clarifying note. This resolves matrix §5 #1 without discarding
either source.

### A4. Choirless and Brightbreak: separate movements, individual drift, collision at the perimeter

**Recommended:**

- The movements **never unify** (accepted Vault).
- **Individuals drift between them.** That is the Dec 12 *"some align"* read at the level of members.
- They **collide at the B09 perimeter** (A3.1).
- Kade talks down Brightbreak and stands with Rex against the Choirless (ruled finale).

This reconciles all three sources (matrix §5 #6).
