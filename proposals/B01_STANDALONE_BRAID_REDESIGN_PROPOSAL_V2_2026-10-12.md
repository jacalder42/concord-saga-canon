# B01 as a standalone novel: the braided redesign (proposal, version 2)

Status: PROPOSAL, non-canonical (Tier D), 2026-10-12. It changes no ruling, card, grid row, packet or manuscript file.
Every change below is a question for the author (§6). Ledger §523.

**It supersedes** the recommendation (§4) and questions Q-SN1–13 of
`proposals/B01_STANDALONE_REVIEW_AND_REDESIGN_PROPOSAL_2026-10-12.md`. That proposal's diagnosis (§1) still stands.
Q-SN0 and Q-SN14 carry over as Q-SB0 and Q-SB13.

**Corrected 2026-10-12 (ledger §524).** The first version read the author's *"the only reason Seraphine and Baz ever
interact"* as a routing rule: every Seraphine–Baz contact had to pass through Lucien. That overreached. **The author's
intent is causal: Lucien is the only reason their paths cross.** Without him Baz would never come to New Orleans. Once
they have met, they interact freely. §1, §2 item 6, §3, §4, §5 and Q-SB6 are corrected below. The two designs' routing
of Seraphine–Baz scenes through Lucien is withdrawn, and the scenes they rerouted or cut on that ground are restored as
available: the dance, the towel visit and the lunch errand.

**Built on the author's direction of 10-12**
(`decisions/B01_STANDALONE_BRAID_AND_OPPOSITION_DIRECTION_AUTHOR_NOTES_2026-10-12.md`, ledger §521–§522):
- the two novels are **two melodies, braided**;
- Seraphine carries the social and literary melody, and the trilogy leads carry the investigation;
- **Lucien is the Veil link**;
- Caldas may be violent where it pays;
- the woman in cream shoes stays as an unexplained observer;
- length has a soft cap of 120k and a hard cap of about 135–140k;
- the romantic holds are open.

**Evidence:** two independent braided redesigns, written blind to each other: **1, the Conductor** (tempo and
counterpoint) and **2, the Composer** (theme and harmony). They are private, in the manuscript's
`draft-notes/standalone-review-2026-10-12/BRAID_DESIGN_*.md`.

## 1. The braid

| | **Melody A: the record** | **Melody B: the watching** | **The link** |

What he joined (Seraphine and Baz) holds without him |

**Tempo.**
- **Act I** is Seraphine's melody, with Lucien's investigation under it. **The Act I turn is Lucien bringing Baz**
  (*"You said* we*"*; current Ch 11 moves into Act I).
- **Act II** alternates, and tension is redistributed: when one strand tightens, the other breathes or carries the
  harm.
- **Act III** tightens into two climaxes on consecutive days (B on Saturday, A on Sunday). It then unbraids for the
  final units, so the book ends on people, not on a word.

**The shared theme** (the Composer's Hand, never stated on the page): *telling your own truth against being told.*
- Seraphine tells Renée herself, before anyone can use it.
- Baz's truths are dragged out by cost.
- Lucien's truths are told *for* him: by Caldas, by Baz, and by Renée's copy of his form. Only at the stair does he
  tell one himself.

## 2. Where the two designs agree (the core)

1. **Two strands, not fused.** Caldas never presses on the Batiste file. Seraphine's melody has its own pressures, a
   clock and a legitimate opponent.
2. **Lucien leads the investigation in Act I and hands it to Baz.** Bringing Baz is the act turn.
3. **Caldas is the one asker.** He absorbs the man "with the review", the city caller and the asker role of the woman
   in cream shoes.
   - **His wants, stated:** Bastien back, and *the first one* (whoever felt it first). Design 1 adds the folder Baz took.
   - **He is seen at Dré's funeral** at about 15%.
   - **Poydras becomes an in-scene interview** at about 33%.
4. **Violence where it pays, inside Baz's fixed ladder:**
   - **Mr. Ledet is hurt** (Baz's landlord);
   - **Caldas lays hands on Baz** at the festival, and the gallery's response stops him.

   No lead dies or is maimed in B01, and B03 keeps the lethal turn. B02's intimidation rung must now escalate *in kind*.
5. **The woman in cream shoes is an unexplained observer** on the boxes' side.
   - She is drawn to times, glass and water.
   - She appears about five times, never in a scene of her own.
   - She leaves a Technarc seed that the book never explains.
6. **The link is causal (corrected, §524).** Lucien is the reason Seraphine and Baz ever meet: he asks Baz to New
   Orleans and brings him into her room. After that they interact on their own terms. The designs' routing of every
   contact through Lucien is withdrawn.
7. **The final weekend:**
   - **Friday:** Caldas exposes Lucien to her.
   - **Saturday:** the festival (B), where Baz names the Institute.
   - **Sunday:** Renée's kitchen (A), and she finds *None* in Renée's copy.
   - **Sunday night:** Lucien's stair (the link), where he gives her his written account, the inverse of *None*.
   - **Monday:** a coda with Miss Hazel, then the Epilogue.
8. **Romance holds opened.**
   - The stakeout kiss counts.
   - The romance escalates further in late April.
   - The ending is distance by her choice.
   - B03 E21 becomes the **return** kiss, not the first that counts.
9. **About 121k words**, roughly 1.2–1.6k over the soft cap, paid for by the climactic weekend and well inside the hard
   cap. Cast is about 50–55 named people: Renita and R. renamed, and Mrs. Whitlow merged into Miss Hazel.
10. **The repeating middle is cured by the braid.**
    - Tending and food stay in A chapters; the debrief and the board go to B chapters, and only at intersections.
    - The event cycle drops from about 11 runs to about 5.
    - No three units in a row share a shape.
11. **The FIXED items hold in both designs.**
    - **Baz:** arrives in Act II as a POV; former Dominion; *that*, not *how*; no case touched; no metaphysical contact
      (the mistral is cut); no death foreshadowing; alive and staying.
    - **Tahl:** unnamed. **Kade:** absent.
    - **B09:** the prologue and the watchers' non-action; the camp unconnected; no control; *Veil* unexplained; the trio
      formed and Caro–Elisabet seeded.

## 4. What it costs

- **Lost:**
  - the fused design's tight single rope (the opposition pressing on her guilt);
  - some of the cream-shoes woman's asker texture;
  - about 40 named neighbors;
  - the goose afternoon, Palm Sunday and the glass shop, mostly;
  - a degree of the book's quiet: there is now violence, and an openly menacing Caldas.
- **Prose survival:** about 75–80% of the new book is existing prose. About 7k is new: the stair, the funeral sighting,
  Lucien's note, the Ledet scene and the Sunday kitchen bridge.
- **What B02/B03 must absorb (packet notes; no chain is rewritten):**
  1. Seraphine has met Caldas and saw him take Baz's arm.
  2. Ledet's injury, and B02's intimidation escalating in kind.
  3. The cream-shoes woman is not Caldas's. B02 E13 may recognise her with the Technarc kit.
  4. The kiss counted and Lucien declared. B03 E21 becomes the return.
  5. Seraphine and Baz have a direct thread (the address).
  6. Lucien is openly the Institute's man.
  7. The reconsideration's outcome and Denise's reading of the closure.
  8. The existing 10-04→10-12 packet debt, still owed.
- **Saga note (direction only).** Both designs reach the same grammar for the link role: **it belongs to one melody
  institutionally and to the other by love; everything it carries leaks; it breaks at the book's end, and the break is
  the baton pass.** The candidates are Elisabet or Rex in Neon (between Seraphine and Tahl) and Lacuna in Loom (between
  Seraphine and Kade). Nothing beyond B01 is proposed here.

## 5. Tests before any rewrite

1. **Paper test.** A two-page synopsis goes to two fresh blind readers (the casual and urban-fantasy profiles). At the
   Ch 22 line, ask: what are the two stories, who opposes each, and how did Seraphine and Baz come to know each other?
   **Pass:** they name Caldas and Bastien or *the first one*, Renée's certificate, and Lucien, unprompted.
2. **The kitchen's placement.** Compare br Ch 37 in place against the moved Sunday kitchen, with a one-page bridge.
   Ask which hits harder and whether she chooses it. **If it fails:** the kitchen stays before the lot, and the rest
   stands.
3. **The violence.** Draft the Ledet scene and the river wall against br Ch 33. Ask: does Caldas frighten, is the
   violence earned, does he stay a person?
4. **The stair**, drafted with and without the declaration.
5. **The link's cost**, run on the outline: does every intersection Lucien carries cost him something on the page?
6. **Realism:** the 90-day reconsideration against Louisiana practice and br Ch 18's wording.
7. **If the tests pass:** an integrated outline, then packets (the only redraft source), then the revision into a new
   folder, with the br draft kept as the checkpoint. Then the standing checks and the author's read.
