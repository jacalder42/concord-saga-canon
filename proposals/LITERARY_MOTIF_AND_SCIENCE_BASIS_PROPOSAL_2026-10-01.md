## 2. The science basis (Q-SY4)

### 2.1 The precedent: the force equation

In the author's words, the equation went from *Resonance = energy × emotion × intent* to *"Resonance (energy) = Will x Emotion x Intent"*, *"similar in structure to Force (unbalanced) = Mass x Acceleration (for our purposes consider acceleration as defined by intensity and vector)"* (`Prompt_crafting_types`, begun 2025-11-10; recapped in `Project_review_and_guidance`, 2025-11-30). The public side was relabelled *energy* so readers would not confuse Will and Intent.

**Where it lives.** `Resonance-v1.md` holds **no equation**; its *"Emotion is energy"* (§2) is a slogan. The equation is Mechanica §8 and `canon_rules.json` (`RP = Will × Emotion × Intent`), with the on-page proxy *Energy × Emotion × Intent* (§10).

| Force | Resonance | Mechanica §9 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mass *m* | Will | finite capacity |
| magnitude of *a* | Emotion | amplitude: more power, more risk |
| direction of *a* | Intent | the vector; its absence *"produces instability"* |

The 09-23 systems audit keeps RP **a qualitative potential, not a conservation equation**; so does this proposal. The author also floated environmental forces as *"friction"* that cancels or deflects, or, *"when aligned"*, gives *"unintended acceleration or amplification"* (`Concord_Saga_Notion_Blueprint`, begun 2025-11-21). That is how a driven oscillator behaves: a push in phase with the motion adds energy; out of phase, it removes it. A backstage analogy, not rule text.

### 2.2 Ground rules

Downstream of the source, everything is ordinary physics. Figures below describe the real world, not the phenomenon.

The author's ladder, *"Veil = felt, Neon Rebellion = quantified, Concord = understood"* (2025-11-08, framed as a question; `reports/B01_REDRAFT_PATH_TO_4_5_ANALYSIS_2026-09-29.md` §4), puts the numbers in Neon's instruments.

## 3. Light and colour, less pedantically (Q-SY4)

**Principle (the 09-23 reconciliation's V2):** colour is what light does in a place, seen by someone, never a code the reader decodes. Near the phenomenon, light does only what surfaces allow: caustic rings from rippled water; reflections broken by a shaking surface; a hanging lamp trembling. In humid air a streetlight wears a corona (droplet diffraction, ordinary New Orleans weather). Fringes and stress patterns wait for Neon's instruments.

**`rules/symbols/COLOR_SEMANTICS.md`** reads colour as emotional pressure (red fear, indigo grief, green grounding). **Recommended:** leave it unchanged until the Mechanica line-by-line review, as backstage art and VFX vocabulary. In prose a colour is what someone sees, with its ordinary cause.

**The green conflict (K1):**

| Reading | Source | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green = **strain** (structural, or tech) | v1.0 #413; the Notion-origin *Hybrid VFX Engine Sheet* | Earlier; never saved |
| Green = **grounding / regulation** | Memory Edition v2 (author: *"Save it"*); `rules/symbols/`; Mechanica §51 | Later, author-saved, Tier C |

Under C3 (rules 3–4) the later, saved reading weighs more. **Recommended for prose:** neither. Green appears when something is green: a Gulf storm sky before hail, oxidised copper, the aurora's oxygen line (Q-MS1).

**Prismatic, iridescent, opal** are physically **structural colour**: made by layers or ordered structure rather than pigment, and changing with where you stand. Films interfere; opal diffracts from ordered silica spheres; a rainbow disperses. That gives the trio's reveal a non-glowing basis: colour that belongs to no single viewer suits a Veil *"held jointly"* (§42A.3). The timing conflicts:

- the author: *"perhaps just in Loom as they approach ascension"* (`Visual_and_VFX` #513);
- `rules/symbols/`: prismatic effects post-Mending only, as on Seraphine's, Kade's and Lacuna's cards;
- `ElisabetAppearance.md`: *"faint prismatic edges"*, with no era gate.

See Q-MS3.
