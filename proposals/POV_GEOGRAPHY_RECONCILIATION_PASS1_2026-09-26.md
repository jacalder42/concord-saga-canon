## 1. Recovered framework

Editorial target, approximately:
- **30% trilogy lead:** Baz → Tahl → Kade;
- **30% Seraphine:** saga lead;
- **30% secondary protagonists:** Lucien/Caro/Elisabet/Rex/etc. according to story ownership;
- **10% flex:** rare local/civic/special POV or redistribution.

The percentages are diagnostic bands, not quotas.

## 2. Why geography changes the POV audit

The old percentage framework and recovered global environment program complement each other.

A compressed ensemble tends to create two distortions:
1. Seraphine or the trilogy lead becomes the camera for events that belong to another character;
2. distant cities become reports because no POV-bearing character is allowed to live there.

Intentional cast separation permits the secondary-protagonist band to carry genuine parallel plots while Seraphine and the trilogy lead remain central at saga/trilogy scale.

## 4. Anti-quota tests

Do not add a Baz/Tahl/Kade episode merely because the baton lead is below 30%.
Do not add a Seraphine episode merely because a spreadsheet says 28%.
Do not divide the secondary band equally among named protagonists.
Do not reserve exactly 10% for novelty POVs.
Do not switch POV inside a scene merely to improve totals.
Do not send a POV character to a city merely because that city needs a camera.

Instead ask whether an apparent imbalance reveals:
- a missing character arc;
- a compressed theater;
- a protagonist who has become passive;
- too many remote reports;
- a relationship happening mostly off-page;
- an event owned by the wrong character;
- or simply a book whose story naturally deviates.

## 6. Recommended measurement later

For each book, a future POV ledger should record:
`episode | POV | approximate words | theater | direct/remote | decision owned | consequence owned | baton/saga/secondary/flex band`.

Then summarize percentages **after architecture exists**.

Review deviations qualitatively:
- <20 or >40 in a nominal 30 band is a prompt to inspect, not automatically an error;
- long act-level absence can be excellent if deliberate;
- word share may matter more than episode count when episode lengths vary;
- a 10% flex share of zero can be entirely healthy.

No hard tolerance band is canonized here; the examples above are editorial diagnostics only.

## 7. Immediate implication

The next B02–B06 geography recovery should extract **POV evidence at the same time as physical presence**. That lets us distinguish:
- character physically present + POV;
- character present but not POV;
- local POV;
- remote report/communication;
- supplement/information vehicle;
- event only referenced later.

This combined audit is more useful than separate geography and POV passes and should precede saga pressure recalibration.
