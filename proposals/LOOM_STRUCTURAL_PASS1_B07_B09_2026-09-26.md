## 4. Each book on the author's plan, and what is still empty

### B07: inheritance

**The author's beats:** the funeral parade (at it or just after) → Lacuna frames it and Kade is
elevated → the world destabilizes → Lacuna settles Kade → Seraphine is drawn toward *"becoming
the Loom"* → Kade leans into MT → **Elias arrives, seeking MT, attaches to Kade; Brightbreak

**Recovered support:** Kade's comment-diary takeover of MT (11-18, author); Elias's route to NOLA

*"Technarc remnant attempted containment"*, Filament shelters Uptown; carry-forward check);
POOL-009/010 (funeral, MT succession); M24/M25 as world conditions.

### B08: the search

**The author's beats:** it opens with Lacuna and Kade arguing about embracing violence →
**two strands:**

- **(a) the protagonists search for the tear**, plausibly at the *"thin/clear places"* (Santa Fe,
  St. Louis / Mound City, Serpent Mound), under Silence and Hope's hard-cap coaching;
- **(b) Kade follows the angry splinter** and tells himself he is restraining it, while Elias
  works on his voice.

→ it ends with **learning the tear may be back in Louisiana.**

**Empty:** B08's **anchor event**, the one irreversible thing that happens in B08 and belongs
to it.

**Already ruled for B08:** the storm-wall opening may happen here and may be fleeting. Elias
claims it for Kade. **READING, not adopted:** a fleeting opening glimpsed or reported from the
swamp is a natural form for *"learning it may be back in Louisiana."* Elias's claim turns the same
event into a Kade miracle for everyone else, so the factions hear of a miracle, not a site.

### B09: escape, feint, and two chapter clusters

**The author's beats:** Acts I–II are endgame setup. They include an escape from another location
(the B08 search's last site?), pursuit, and **a feint in NOLA while the crew heads to Honey
Island.** Act III: the antagonists learn the site; Elisabet and Rex hang back. In the last
chapter or two come the Elias / Kade / Rex / Tahl scene, then the Mending, then the epilogue.

**This fixes the B09 Act II siege problem without new invention.** The pasted "Siege of the
Swamp" becomes the NOLA feint and the pursuit. The siege exists only in Act III, once the
antagonists learn the site. That matches the author's 11-22 *"They all thought the protagonist
group was in New Orleans."*
