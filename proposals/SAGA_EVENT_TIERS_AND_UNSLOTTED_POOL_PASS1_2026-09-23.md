## Finding

The [36-row milestone grid](../grids/milestones_payoffs.csv) is a useful list of high-visibility promises, but it is neither the complete plot nor the inventory of physical Resonance incidents. Its rows mix deaths, discoveries, physical thresholds, publications, collective decisions, and end states. In particular, its B8 rows are a superseded formal pre-Mending Echo Node, an unverified total Technarc collapse, and an approach to the B9 final site. That selection makes B7–B8 appear to be an extended approach to the Mending. The [B7–B8 presence ledger](../reports/B07_B08_PLACE_EVENT_PRESENCE_LEDGER_PASS1_2026-09-23.md) has many source-rooted *possible* human, local, and institutional changes, but no fully recovered distinct B8 non-finale irreversible turn. This is a **representation and recovery gap**, not proof of an empty book.

The next unit of work should be a **web of event candidates**, including events with no approved time. A candidate may earn a slot because it changes a later choice, destroys an option, moves information between people, or incurs a carried cost. A scene can be vivid without qualifying as an event; a funeral, a service outage, and an argument can qualify without being Resonance manifestations.

## 1. Four levels of narrative organization

These are *scope and causal-role levels*, not Resonance power, reader-pressure, weather, or required quotas.

| Level | Test | Examples and treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A — saga anchor** | Would removing it change the nine-book premise, era transition, or protected end state? | B3 Warehouse death; B6 Tahl death; B9 Mending. Reference existing M/EV aliases; the grid's *wording and proposed placement* can still be contested. Do not mint a second anchor for each consequence. |
| **B — book turn** | Does an action or discovery irreversibly change the book's human, civic, spatial, or institutional options and create obligations for later events? | A specific Filament split that changes access to aid; a loss of trustworthy publication authority; a locally consequential evacuation. **B8's non-finale turn remains unverified.** A book can contain multiple turns with different scales. |
| **C — local event** | Is there a before/after in a particular decision, service, relationship, site, piece of evidence, or person's capacity? | A report is verified, a bounded route is lost, someone refuses a splinter demand, a meta is rescued or exploited. It may cause a B turn, remain a quiet consequence, or be rejected as redundant. |
| **D — receipt / scene observation** | Does a witness, record, physical trace, ordinary action, or later testimony let the reader test a higher-level claim? | A working comparator device, camp inventory, two independent local timestamps, one person's mistaken reading of a light shift. Keep the receipt attached to its event; do not inflate each into a milestone. |

**Promotion is earned, not automatic:** several C events can jointly produce one B turn; one C event might itself be a B turn. A Level A anchor can contain separate C events (Warehouse Rupture, Baz's rescue, delayed notification), which must not be collapsed into a single supernatural cause. There is no target number of events per book or uniform spacing. Reader pressure and thread pressure remain separate annotations.

## 2. Candidate card and causal-link grammar

Give an *unslotted* candidate a stable `POOL-###` handle with no book number. Maintain separate fields:

`event_kind` (physical / informational / social / institutional / logistical / relational / hybrid); `source_status` (CURRENT protected function / RECOVERED later macro / HISTORICAL candidate / SUPERSEDED literal / PROPOSAL / OPEN); `source_window` (where a document placed it); `placement_window` (**UNSET** until editorial review); `source + exact old alias`; `site candidates and certainty`; `people actually on-site / remote receiver / unknown`; `starting constraint → action → changed state`; `cost / residue / beneficiary or harmed person`; `antagonist actor, asset, doctrine or institutional pressure`; `dependencies`; `duplicate_or_alternate`; `question_to_resolve`.

For physical Resonance cards additionally require initial conditions → medium/coupling → objective change → interpretation and rival reading → limits → cost → durable evidence. For nonphysical cards, require an equally concrete decision and consequence; do not pretend that grief or an MT post is a field event.

Typed edges, each with evidence and certainty:

- **requires**: later event is impossible unless a specific earlier condition exists. This is a genuine ordering constraint.
- **informs**: a person learns a report; the same person need not visit the reporting site. Specify sender, receiver, channel and lag.
- **enables / blocks**: access, resources, authority, or a safe route changes; specify whose options change.
- **motivates / contests**: belief or doctrine shapes a *person's choice*, without proving a physical Resonance link.
- **consequence**: a cost or institutional response carries forward; identify the carrier.
- **alternate / duplicate-risk**: mutually exclusive old stagings or possibly the same incident; these are **not** successive plot beats.
- **echoes**: meaningful comparison only, with **no causal ordering claim**. Avoid using this edge to pad a web.

A shared theme, place name, weather label or character does **not** establish a causal edge. Record independent happenings as independent. A book window in an old source is evidence about that version, not an instruction to slot the candidate there.

## 5. Recovery and selection order

1. **Freeze the meaning of the 36 M rows as proposal-level anchors for review.** Keep original aliases; mark rows that are too coarse or literally superseded. No grid overwrite now.
2. **Mine C and B candidates across the saga, especially B7/B8, with exact passages and dates.** The full unsanitized 2025-11-01 72-MD/72-JSON archive, Dec 8 user-pasted macro, historical Notion B7/B8 pages, later author rulings, and current B1 architecture are distinct authority layers. Search ordinary civic, work, money, migration, care, rivalry, investigation and faction decisions as well as Resonance. This seed is **not** the complete nine-book event census.
3. **Normalize alternatives and duplicates before counting.** For each repeated trip/report/meta encounter/near-break, identify whether it is one event with two versions, a consequence, or genuinely two differently costly actions.
4. **Make causal cards and a sparse typed graph.** Separate place, actual bodies, remote evidence, antagonist person/asset/doctrine, and measurable physical effect. List unknowns openly.
5. **Choose book turns from the causal material, then place the unslotted local events.** Ask what irreversible non-finale choice B8 actually owns *before* solving it by importing the B9 approach. Book assignment, route/time and character availability are simultaneous constraints; an old source window does not decide them.
6. **Only then revise milestone descriptions, map by location/character/antagonist, and stress-test density.** No even distribution target; follow cost, changed options and local continuity. B1 integrated order stays locked. Run physics/conflict/VFX/exposure and integrated B1 gates before EBCI.

## Immediate review decisions

- **Adopt the tier distinction and unslotted pool as working editorial method?** Recommended **yes**, without canonizing any pool row.
- **Retain the exact 36 M rows as the only level-A roster?** Recommended **no**: they are proposed and mixed-level. Preserve IDs for cross-reference; classify function and authority before deciding promotions or retirements.
- **Assign B8 a climax now?** Recommended **no**. First recover one or more *distinct, situated, non-finale changes* and test whether any truly earn a B turn. POOL-016–019 are avenues to investigate, not instructions to add all of them.
- **Advance Honey Island wall/chamber into B8 by default?** Recommended **hold**. Its source window is real but duplication with B9 access remains unresolved.
