# B. Tahl middle-Neon agency — two design options

Recovered constraint: Tahl's line is anonymous/then named truth voice → public consequence → unsustainable burden → hardest truth → Santa Fe death. His ethical contribution is **publish responsibly**; he must remain fallible.

## T1-A — The correction that costs him authority

**Window:** B04 A3 or B05 A1.

Tahl publishes a strong interpretation from incomplete distributed evidence. New evidence materially weakens one part of it. Rather than quietly editing or defending the post, he issues a visible correction/retraction that:
- costs him followers/credibility with one audience;
- increases trust with another;
- frustrates a faction/institution trying to use his first version;
- establishes the standard that MT's authority comes from correction rather than omniscience.

**Durable state change:** MT becomes trusted by some precisely because it can admit error; opponents learn that controlling the narrative requires attacking the process, not just disproving one claim.

**Strengths**
- embodies the recovered “publish responsibly” rule;
- preserves Tahl fallibility;
- gives him agency without a Resonance feat;
- creates public/institutional consequence;
- differentiates him from Kade's later grief-driven voice.

**Risk**
Requires a specific wrong-but-reasonable claim. Do not make Tahl dangerously incompetent or let the correction magically stop panic.

## T1-B — The warning he refuses to sensationalize

**Window:** B05 A2/A3.

Tahl has evidence pointing toward a dangerous development but not enough to support the most alarming public interpretation. Under pressure to publish the maximal claim, he separates what is observed from what is inferred, publishes the bounded warning, and withholds the unsupported conclusion.

The bounded post still changes behavior:
- some civilians alter routes/plans;
- a local/institutional actor responds;
- sensationalist rivals/factions accuse MT of hiding the truth;
- later confirmation vindicates the method, not every prediction.

**Durable state change:** MT becomes a public information infrastructure with explicit evidence standards, and Tahl accepts that responsible truth-telling can make him less satisfying to his own audience.

**Strengths**
- directly uses evidence-tier architecture;
- avoids “hero blogger is always right”;
- can point toward Santa Fe without prematurely solving it;
- establishes the burden that eventually burns him out.

**Risk**
If later confirmation is too neat, it becomes “Tahl was secretly right all along.” Preserve residual uncertainty/error.

### Editorial preference
**T1-A is the stronger character milestone.**  
T1-B is the stronger plot/evidence milestone.

A hybrid is possible later, but do not combine them by default; two ethical crises are more useful than one overstuffed post if episode architecture has room.

# C. Technarc authority transition — two design options

Recovered constraint:
- B04 failures/secrecy/control pressure;
- B05 response capability collapses while hardliners continue pushing;
- B06 manufactured-meta failures begin;
- Loom Technarc no longer commands, though systems/remnants persist.
Do not equate response failure, asset failure, organizational collapse and ideological defeat.

## A1-A — Public operational failure fractures compliance

**Window:** B05 A1/A2.

A Technarc-controlled response to a regional crisis fails in a way ordinary people/local authorities can verify. The important consequence is not spectacle: a city/service/community stops accepting Technarc direction as presumptively authoritative and adopts a different response protocol.

**Durable state change:** Technarc retains assets and hardliners, but loses automatic compliance in at least one meaningful theater.

**Best geography:** Singapore is a strong candidate because it makes Technarc's home theater consequential, but city assignment remains open.

**Dependencies:** M13; could use M17 if manufactured-meta deployment is the intervention.

**Strengths**
- clean bridge from B04 control to B05 weakened response;
- gives global geography durable consequence;
- distinguishes legitimacy from destruction;
- can merge G1 geography candidate into this row.

**Risk**
Must not make one local failure equal global Technarc collapse.

## A1-B — Internal refusal exposes the cost of control

**Window:** B05 A2/B06 A1.

A Technarc scientist/operator/local command element refuses or exposes a hardliner action because the protocol treats people—including manufactured metas—as expendable assets. The refusal/exposure compromises a specific Technarc capability, dataset, site or chain of command.

**Durable state change:** the institution's monopoly on expertise/control fractures from inside; later remnants and defectors become causally plausible.

**Dependencies:** M13/M17; can seed Reykjavík scientist-splinter material if source reconciliation supports it.

**Strengths**
- humanizes institutional collapse;
- creates an independent secondary/global theater;
- gives manufactured metas personhood/accountability consequences rather than using them only as VFX;
- provides a causal ancestor for later Technarc remnants.

**Risk**
More invention is required. Do not create a heroic scientist solely to solve the faction arc; recover existing candidates first.

### Editorial preference
**A1-A is safer and structurally cleaner now.**  
It can likely be built by revising/strengthening M17 rather than adding a wholly separate row.

**A1-B is richer if an existing recovered Singapore/Reykjavík character can carry it.**  
Source-recover before adopting.
