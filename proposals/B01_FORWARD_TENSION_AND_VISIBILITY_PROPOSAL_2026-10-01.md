## 2. The questions

**Q-VZ1. Working findings the crew keeps and uses.**
- Characters may hold, and act on, **a local, provisional working rule that visibly helps at least once.**
  - The narrator still never states a law.
  - The rule stays a character's, labelled by its scope ("on these blocks", "so far").
- **Q-MO1 already allows it**: *a practice that reduces harm* is one of the three warranted things. The page has
  withdrawn more than the design requires.
- **In practice:** the precaution already given in the calls (sit down; the stove off; nobody up a ladder) is retained,
  used, and seen to help someone once. The **E48 honest account** gains a short *what held* line beside *what failed*.
  **It excludes *rings first*, which stays held (A4).**
- The packets' C-level *no rule stated as a law* is clarified to *no law stated by the narrator.*
- **Recommendation: yes.**

**Q-VZ2. Two interests, told apart by method.**
- Q-EN2's single signature becomes two, with no names and no agenda:
  - **Hand 1, authority,** works through paper and permission: holds, caseload calls, revoked access, the file going up.
  - **Hand 2, measurement,** works through instruments and collection: the cases, the collector, times taken.
- On the page, Hand 1's acts stop being described as *wanting only the times*. A reader can then suspect two interests
  without being told there are two.
- **Recommendation: yes.**

**Q-VZ3. A response the reader can feel.**
- **Hand 1.** **The E48 refusal may cost something before the tins.** E37 already starts it: removing Seraphine's name
  from Mr. Vidrine's oxygen account follows Denise's decision there and *takes a week* as a form. The form lands on the
  Saturday morning, the morning after her refusal, and she learns of it before the window. She watches her tin not knowing
  whether *no* cost it.
  - **It is honestly ambiguous:** a process already in motion, or a response. The page lets the reader wonder, and
    nothing confirms either.
  - **It is not a conspiracy proof.** Not every setback is theirs.
  - The oxygen is not resolved, there is no rescue, and he is not in danger on the page.
  - It carries to B02 with the holds.
- **Hand 2, optional.** After the dawn stakeout (E39), the cases stop following the city's line, and the crew loses its
  only way to anticipate them. The response is inferred from an absence.
- **Recommendation: yes to Hand 1, as an ambiguous landing. Hand 2 is the author's choice.** If the author wants a response that is unmistakable rather than ambiguous, Hand 2's absence is the clearer one. The *it takes and never asks* guard is retired, and
  A6 is no longer its only exception.

**Q-VZ4. One wonder the crew keeps.**
- **At E23, Lucien keeps the river-note reading.** This reverses draft 3's change that had him discard it.
- He tells nobody. It becomes the one record he holds that is not harm, and he comes back to it later. It is a reason to
  pursue the phenomenon beyond preventing the next emergency.
- No mechanism is attached, and E43's dry wind stays unlogged.
- **Recommendation: yes, E23 only.** It is light, inside W6's *no new Mechanica*.
