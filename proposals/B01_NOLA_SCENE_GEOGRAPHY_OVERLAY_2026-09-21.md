## Executive finding

**GREEN — Book 1 can support the expanded Life/Reward architecture without inventing a new city or a larger cast.**

The strongest correction is geographic.

The current v3 causal spine uses New Orleans primarily as:
- crisis site;
- investigation field;
- Jackson Square recurrence;
- Marigny pressure zone;
- Velvet Vein social hearth.

Historical environment work gives us a much larger lived city.

The v4 Life/Reward layer should make Book 1 feel as if the protagonists have ordinary routes through New Orleans before the saga turns those routes into memory.

Governing principle:

> **The reader should know where these people go when nothing important is happening.**

That is the missing geography layer.

Historical NOLA sources assign different emotional/narrative functions to different parts of the city.

These functions are useful even where old Resonance terminology is obsolete.

Recovered function:
- public visibility;
- tourism + culture;
- crowd energy;
- creation/chaos;
- lantern/public-facing city image.

B1 use:
- public recurrence;
- Jackson Square;
- mixed witness testimony;

Recovered function:
- music;

- Seraphine/Lacuna/Baz route associations.

## Marigny / Bywater

Recovered function:
- expressive/social fringe;
- youth/community overlap;

# 8. City-memory ladder

The city should accumulate reader memory in layers.

## Layer 1 — orientation

Reader learns:
- Seraphine has routes;
- Lucien has routes;
- people eat/work/commute/go out;
- the Vein is fun;
- the Square is public;
- neighborhoods feel different.

## Layer 2 — recurrence

Same places acquire:
- anomaly gossip;
- tension;
- friendship;
- care;
- anticipation.

## Layer 3 — consequence

Civic events alter:
- behavior;
- stories;
- routes;
- ordinary expectations.

## Layer 4 — memory

By E34–36, the city itself has narrative memory.

This is the desired B1 environmental arc:

**home → disturbance → investigation → shared experience → remembered home.**

Not:
**cool location → cool location → cool location.**

# 9. Cast-control rules for geography

The geography layer must NOT become a supporting-cast onboarding mechanism.

## Rule G1 — Places can recur without proprietors becoming cast

A deli worker, nurse, driver, musician, neighbor, vendor or bartender can exist unnamed.

## Rule G2 — Presence does not require introduction

A future recurring face may appear at low resolution without narrative spotlight.

## Rule G3 — Named location does not require named local

Real or invented places may carry social texture through anonymous people.

## Rule G4 — Core characters should generate much of the Life layer themselves

Use:
Seraphine;
Lucien;
Baz;
Caro where recovered;
Elisabet where chronology permits;
Trip only where Vein function requires.

Do not solve “make NOLA alive” by adding ten NOLA characters.

## Rule G5 — Different geography can replace different cast

Changing neighborhood, transit mode, meal, time of day or social environment can create freshness without a new person.

This is a major v4 tool.

# 12. Geographic ECG

Opening:
swamp → ordinary Seraphine NOLA → ordered Lucien NOLA → nightlife.

Effect:
the novel moves from intimate Louisiana wound into two different lived urban grammars before civic escalation.

Act I:
neighborhood → Square → Tremé care → private routine → Square.

Effect:
public and private NOLA alternate.

Act II:
arrival/food → neighborhood fieldwork → community → institution → City Park/river wonder → Marigny → medical/civic → Vein pleasure → predicted zone → home → community → city reaction.

Effect:
**strongest geographic movement.**

Act III:
friendship/river → distributed city → civic systems → home → care → wonder → event → citywide quiet → Square → Vein.

Effect:
the city contracts under pressure, then reopens socially.

Verdict:
**GREEN.**

# 13. Effect on manuscript scale

This overlay reinforces the conclusion that 36 episodes were a causal spine, not a full experiential novel.

The additional length can come from:
- travel between meaningful places;
- food;
- waiting;
- entering/leaving;
- neighborhood observation;
- weather;
- ordinary transactions;
- private routines;
- social anticipation;
- recovery;
- embodied environment.

These are not padding if they:
1. produce reader reward;
2. build city memory;
3. reveal character behavior;
4. alter the meaning of later recurrence.

A 95k–120k Book 1 remains plausible without restoring redundant plot.
