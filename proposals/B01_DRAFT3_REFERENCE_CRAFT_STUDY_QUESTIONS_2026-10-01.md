## Q-CS3 — The writer profile

Add these lines, approved design:

- **§3A, *Scene or summary*:**
  - Dramatize where the outcome is uncertain or a choice is contested. Summarize routine, travel and repeated
    collection.
  - Never summarize the choice that makes a consequence intelligible.
- **§3A, *Chapters have several shapes*:**
  - Planning, action, argument, discovery, recovery, work, reunion.
  - Not every chapter closes on a kept secret or a hook. Ask what changed.
- **§5, *Articulacy is uneven*:**
  - Some people say their reasons in one sentence, some cannot say them, and some say the wrong thing.
  - Two characters do not diagnose a third in the same words.
- **§6, *Humour belongs to a relationship*:**
  - Who this person teases safely, whom they provoke, whom they placate, and whom they cannot answer honestly.
  - Someone can be tired of the joke, or resist it.
- **§10, *The decision space is legible*:**
  - What someone is trying to reach, protect or prevent; what is in the way; what the choice changes.
  - What a decision needs is planted before it is needed. A plan is on the page before it fails.
- **§11, *Romantic attention is selective*:**
  - It differs from how the person attends to everyone else, and it changes a response.
  - Tenderness given equally to everyone does not signal desire.

**Recommended: yes.** The book's guards and its no-quota rule stand. The studies' premise-specific material stays out:
danger as attraction, system menus, pop-culture banter, and a learnable rule for the phenomenon.
