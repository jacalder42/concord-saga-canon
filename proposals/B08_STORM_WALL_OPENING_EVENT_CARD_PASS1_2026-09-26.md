## 2. Findings

### A. Mechanica already constrains an opening at Landfall, and makes it more interesting

Mechanica §31: *"Weather escalates due to: accumulated emotional pressure; shard proximity;
**crowd density**; environmental conductivity. Weather de-escalates slowly and unevenly.
**Sudden calm is suspicious.**"*

Two consequences, both **RULE**-derived and neither a mechanism:

1. **The siege makes the storm worse.** A siege is crowd density. Under the rules, the factions
   gathering at the swamp in A3.1 feed the W4 state that blocks them. An opening happens
   **against** that pressure, so it cannot be a free lull.
2. **An opening is a "sudden calm", and the rules call that suspicious.** An aperture is by
   definition a local, abrupt de-escalation. The rule does not forbid it. It says the world
   should treat it as suspect. That favours a **bounded, costly, short** opening over a clean
   parting, and it gives the pursuers a reason to read it wrongly (finding B).

**Of the source test's three forms**, the first two, a **weather/visibility aperture** and a
**structural corridor change**, satisfy "actual opening" (the author's lean). The third,
**perceptual revelation only**, is what the lean leans away from. It stays on the list until
ruled.

### B. Kade's threshold is in the same act, and pursuers could attribute the opening to him

B8 A3.4: *"Kade approaches breakthrough/misinterpretation threshold."* A2.3: *"Kade misinterprets
resonance; Brightbreak rhetoric sharpens."* A1.5: *"Kade becomes unwilling beacon; Brightbreak
shadows him."* **READING:** an abrupt opening that the rules call suspicious, arriving as Kade
reaches his misinterpretation threshold, is **exactly what Brightbreak could claim as his doing**.
That is a candidate answer to the packet's last field: **the pursuers misread the opening as
Kade's power**, when its cause is something else.

This also connects to two things already on file, and invents neither:

- **POOL-018** (proposal-only): *"Kade's published credibility is appropriated."* A misattributed
  opening would be a concrete incident of it, which the pool says it lacks.
- **B9's arc:** *"Kade reaches Brightbreak zenith; rhetoric near-weaponized"* (B9 A1.6), then
  *"realizes Brightbreak is using him"* (B9 A2.6). A false miracle credited to him at the end of
  B8 would give that zenith a cause.

**Not proposed:** that Kade causes the opening. That would be the *"emotional alignment key"* the
packet forbids, and KadeID's *"power is human-scale."*

### C. B8 Act III and B9 Acts I–II restage the same three beats

Read side by side, the two author-pasted books repeat three beats. This is POOL-020's
duplicate-risk made specific:

| Beat | B08 | B09 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Convoy splits** | A1.2 *"splits into forward team + rear guard"*; A3.2 *"forward and rear teams split under pressure"* | A1.2 *"convoy splits under pressure"* |
| **Siege forms** | A3.1 *"siege lines form around swamp"* | A2.1 *"multi-faction siege forms around swamp"* |
| **Approach to the chamber** | A3.7 *"prepare final entry into swamp alignment chamber"*; close: *"chamber revealed"* | A1.4 *"Mending team enters swamp"*; A2.2 *"progresses toward alignment chamber"* |

B9 also **opens** with *"retreat from NOLA; corridor collapse spreading"*, after B8 has ended at
the swamp. That works only if B9's opening strand is **someone else**, the rear guard or the
city, or a return. It is not stated.

**Three readings, for the author. None is chosen:**

1. **Seen, not entered.** The B8 opening reveals the chamber and closes, as §31's "suspicious"
   calm would. B9 must earn entry. B8's siege *forms*; B9's *closes in*. B9's NOLA retreat is
   the rear or civilian strand. Keeps the lean and B9's ending, but makes B9 A1 a second
   approach.
2. **Forward team through.** The forward team passes in B8. B9 A1–A2 follow the rear guard and
   the siege outside while the Mending team moves inside. Uses the recovered split. Risks B8
   consuming B9's entry.
3. **Move the opening.** B9 A1's close, *"directional resonance locks path forward,"* becomes the
   opening. B8 needs a different end image. **Goes against the author's lean** (actual opening at
   B8's end), so it is listed for completeness only.

Whichever is chosen, **the convoy should split once**, or each split should be a different kind
of split. As pasted, it splits three times.

### D. "Opens" versus "forms": the author's text supersedes the Dec 7 draft

The Dec 7 assistant reconstruction had the storm wall **forming** at the B8 close and
**circling** through B9 Act I. The Dec 8 author-pasted master, in both pastes, has it
**opening** at the B8 close. The later, author-pasted text governs the macro. The Dec 7 version
is recorded because reading 1 of finding C partly recovers it: the wall is still there in B9,
because the B8 opening closed.

## 3. What would complete the card

**The form** (finding A): aperture, route change, or, if the lean is not taken, revelation only.

**The agent:** who **detects** the opening, and with what fallible evidence (the Dec 8 expansion's
Elisabet is a lead, not a choice). Who, if anyone, **acts** on the storm, and at what cost.

**The interval:** how long it stays open, and what closes it.

**The cost:** what is spent or lost to reach or use it. With the siege and W4 conditions, the
rear guard is the obvious exposure. That is an observation, not a placement.

**The traces and the misreading:** what a second observer can find afterwards, and what the siege
factions conclude (finding B is a candidate).

**The B9 boundary:** which of finding C's readings, and what that does to B9 Act I.
