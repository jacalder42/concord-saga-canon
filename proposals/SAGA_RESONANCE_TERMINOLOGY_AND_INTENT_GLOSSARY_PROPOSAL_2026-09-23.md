# Saga Resonance terminology and intent — working glossary proposal

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Status:** PROPOSAL FOR J.A. CALDER + CLAUDE/EDITOR REVIEW; **NOT CANON**. Companion to [Pass 3 anchor register](../recovery/SAGA_RESONANCE_EVENT_ANCHOR_REGISTER_PASS3_2026-09-23.md). This does not overwrite the existing [Mechanica Appendix B](../rules/Mechanica-v4.md) or the [Veil migration glossary](../recovery/VEIL_MECHANICA_TERMINOLOGY_RECONCILIATION_2026-09-20.md). Those have useful entries but the former still calls MT “Mortal Technology,” contrary to [author ruling](../recovery/CHANNEL_NAMES_RULING_2026-09-19.md); the latter only protects Veil uses.

## Why this exists

A term can denote a **controlled engine token**, physical process, institutional/public label, attuned sensation, editorial shorthand or prose metaphor. Copying the word into an event card does not transfer its physics. This ledger pairs **recovered wording** with **what the source was trying to accomplish narratively**, then distinguishes an approved boundary from a recommended safer gloss. Historical titles saying “CANON” confer no current authority. Do not use this as a find-and-replace script; retain character voice, uncertainty and deliberate misinterpretation.

**Annotation rule for future cards:** `term | referent/axis | source phrase and date | speaker or POV | objective / attuned / subjective / metaphor / editorial / unknown | story function | approved boundary | open mechanism | recommended wording | signoff`. Keep the original phrase for provenance, even when the display term is retired.

## Editorial example: same word, four non-equivalent uses

“**The wall opened**” in B8 could describe: (a) objective wind/rain/optical aperture with a timed trace; (b) objective traversable ground or corridor condition; (c) line of sight suddenly revealing a chamber that was already there; or (d) Seraphine's attuned impression. The December author-pasted beat protects **revelation/access**, not a specific one of (a)–(d). A scene card must choose which one the narrator is asserting and allow witnesses to disagree. [Source test](../reports/B07_DIRECTIONAL_MT_B08_STORM_WALL_SOURCE_TEST_2026-09-23.md).

Similarly “**Kade's signal points**” can mean his messages now name a destination, **not** that a receiver points southeast. The assistant expansion proposed the latter, but no author-approved carrier or compass mechanism is recovered. [Source test](../reports/B07_DIRECTIONAL_MT_B08_STORM_WALL_SOURCE_TEST_2026-09-23.md).

## Author/editor choices and adoption sequence

1. **Approve the ledger structure, not every provisional definition.** Terms with explicit author rulings (Thread names, corridor geometry, no B1 micro-Shard, tech degradation spectrum) are recorded as constraints; physical interpretations with OPEN labels remain questions.
2. **Resolve 5 highest-cost collisions:** MT infrastructure versus channel; B8 pre-Mending site versus post-Mending Echo Node; B7 directional phrase; B8 wall physical status; meta input/output and ontology. The glossary can still be used while these stay OPEN if the status column remains visible.
3. **Add a one-row glossary citation to each causal card** (especially “stabilize,” “pressure,” “echo,” “global”) and mark objective/attuned/subjective/metaphor. Put no new magic in a definition.
4. **After milestone-description review and author decisions**, promote only approved definitions into rules, channel files or structured vocabulary; retain obsolete forms in this ledger for provenance. Do not silently rewrite historical exports, Notion source pages or the 36-row proposed milestone grid.
5. This ledger covers Resonance/event wording, not the separate **MODE / supplement / economic / wardrobe** controlled vocabularies. They need their own scoped terminology pass if those systems enter production.

**Use in review:** if replacing a phrase changes what happened, it is a **story/physics ruling**, not copyediting. If it merely clarifies what the source already meant without adding an event, record the safe gloss and keep the original wording alongside it.
