## Compact event-card acceptance test

Before claiming the E15 thrum or E33 window movement is Resonance-caused, supply one private row per **physical effect**:

`source/trigger | coupling medium | exact bounded observable | why here/now | why this character's action matters (or none) | ceiling/failure | ordinary alternative | cost payer and kind | residue and independent check | OBS class (OBJ/ATT/POV/MET)`.

“Emotion is energy,” “the city remembers,” an FX color, or two copies of a clip cannot fill a missing field. The recording chain must distinguish **two independent observations of one sound** from **two different anomalies**. A device can record sound/light/material behavior; it cannot transmit VT or certify metaphysics. Costs of repair/work stoppage are not automatically UARS costs.
