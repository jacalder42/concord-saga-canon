point-of-view carrier. Seraphine was already New Orleans's anchor in Neon; pass 4 thickens the NOLA
theater's point of view, it does not move her into anyone else's. **Pass.**

- **For EBCI, not now:** introduce each new supporting face **by function, not biography**, one per
  episode. **Pass, no change.**
