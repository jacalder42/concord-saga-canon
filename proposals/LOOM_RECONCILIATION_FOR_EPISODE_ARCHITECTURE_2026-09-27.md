## 4. Two conflicts the survey surfaced, for the author

### 4.1 Book size: the author's *"60–80 episodes"* against 45

- **The author, twice:** *"1. Episodes are ~1500 words 2. 60-80 total episodes per book … Each act will
  be decided into clusters of 3-4 episodes"* (`NB` 106496); *"Each book will need 60-80 episodes of
  ~1500 words… The acts can vary in number of episodes"* (`SBEP` 1117, 12-08). Also: *"There are no set
  number of chapters or episodes in an act. Acts are narrative driven"* (`NS` 60903).
- **The 2026 architecture:** B01 has 48 (v4.1b), B02 47, B03 48 with its epilogue (all approved design);
  **Neon 45 per book (N12, approved 09-27)**; Loom 45 per book here.
- **What the numbers mean:** 45 episodes of about 1,500 words is roughly 68,000 words; 60–80 is roughly
  90,000–120,000.
- **Two readings:**
  1. **Architecture units are not prose units.** An architecture episode may become one to two prose
     episodes at EBCI, when beats are split for length. The 45 are the **turns**, and the 60–80 is the
     **delivered count**.
  2. **The architecture should carry 60–80 now.**
- **Recommended: reading 1**, decided at the Veil EBCI diagnostic (the author's own item 8): Veil EBCI
  shows how many prose episodes each architecture episode needs.

### 4.2 *"Originally Tahl and Kade never met"* against N9

- **The author, 11-30:** *"Originally Tahl and Kade never met."* (`NS` 150220)
- **N9, approved 09-27:** Kade asks a tired stranger at Trip's what he thinks of MT (B05 E07), and
  recognises him at Tahl's death.
- **Two readings:**
  1. **They never met as Tahl and Kade.** In N9 Kade meets a stranger; he never knows he met MT until
     the stranger is dead. The 11-30 line is kept in spirit.
  2. **They never meet at all.** B05 E07 is cut; `BC-KADE-MEETS-TAHL-UNKNOWING` is retired.
- **Recommended: reading 1**, with the author's confirmation, since N9 was approved on a recommendation
  that did not cite the 11-30 line.
