## 1. The ruled frame
- **Stabilisation under the hard cap is temporary, costly, and leaves a mark** (Mechanica §42A.2, read as a breach per the §620 lexicon).
- **Stabilisation requires empathy, clear intent, structural support and grounding rituals; force makes it worse** (§35).
- **Crowds multiply whatever state they are in; love and connection stabilise when they are mutual** (§22; Resonance-v1 §7).
- **The rhythm gate (X1) and the saga reserve stand:** nothing confirms that the Veil answers rhythm, and the breathing-Veil answer belongs to B09. The corridor is **people keeping people safe**, not an intervention on the Veil.
- **On the Neon page Santa Fe is never called a Wound**, and no count is given (§627 N14).
- **Neon's dramatic answer is "no"** (§624). The corridor is a partial yes at human scale that cannot touch the Rupture.

## 3. What each person does
- **Tahl** posts routes and timings live, and helps people through. He records the corridor as observed. He does not explain it, and he does not confirm what it means. Turn: **the technique scales to people, not to the Rupture.**
- **The elders** hold it by keeping a group calm, together and coordinated: the B01 Square's human protocol, scaled. Nobody claims to steer the event.
- **Nobody** uses collective behaviour to change the Veil. They are keeping people safe (§619 caution; X1).

## 4. What it sets up
- **B08 E10, *Holding It*:** the crew's first stabilisation of the Santa Fe Wound. Temporary, costly, still marked. It is the next rung up, and the first place the technique meets the thing itself.
- **Neon's "no":** the world does not act in time. The people who know save some people.
