## 5. Neon-wide rules for these files

- **Physics:** every Resonance effect is **observed**, not explained, until its D5 card exists. The
  cards Neon owes are listed in each book file.
- **Metas are people** (ruled). No successful transfer is staged in public. Contact only; apparatus
  never conducts; distance extraction never transfers.
- **MT is mortal infrastructure.** Tahl's *"never again the coordinates of a live event"* (B03 E48)
  governs what he publishes.
- **Separation is the design.** A city named in a report is not a lived theater. Remote receipt
  (RR) is not presence.
- **Life/Reward stays ordinary.** Each book keeps 4–5 LR units.
- **Supplements** are MT posts, official notices and records, 3–4 per book.
