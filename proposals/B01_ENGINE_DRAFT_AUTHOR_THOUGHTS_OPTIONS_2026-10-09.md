## Findings and recommendations

1. **Narrowing.** It is real but back-loaded: it is flat from E34 to E39A and drops in a cliff at E46–E49. Recommended: status marks as the data land (E22, E32, E34, E39, E48), net shorter. No exception needed.
2. **Lucien's cost.** None of his first nine withholdings costs anything on the page. Recommended: Seraphine leans on his statement as her second witness at E27, which the reader knows says *None*.
3. **Seraphine's want.** Her own want, *to be the one who feels it first*, is only recorded in Act II. Recommended: she pursues it (E29) and minds losing it (E34).
4. **False suspicion.** Inez's POV chapters already clear her. Recommended instead: Elísabet, suspected by Lucien, folded into his E31 misread. It costs him her data and is disproved at E39.
5. **Early sighting.** The opposition never appears in Act I. Recommended: an empty-handed woman at the E14 fall asks a bystander Seraphine's name; she is matched later by manner only. This replaces the merged list's C3.
6. **Lucien learning.** He does learn through action from E36, but repeats the E36 move at E48, when he sends R. the posts. Recommended: he has her yes and does not send. This changes approved E48 design.
7. **Payoff.** All three kinds are on the page but unregistered. Recommended: E49 registers one payoff three ways: an outside count of harm reduced by a warning, which is inside the approved *carried, not prevented*; the precursor's second use marked; *It keeps to places* marked tried.
8. **Closure.** Recommended: three short E49 beats. She keeps the film from R.; her new log is tied to having been read; Baz's new address is kept from Lucien.
9. **A concrete answer.** Bounded by the author's 10-03 and 10-07 calls: the Veil is not explained, and the word snaps onto experienced pattern. Recommended: the place-and-edge limitation made confident, plus one beat in Seraphine's own thought setting the word against it. *It has happened before, elsewhere* awaits a canon check. A stated purpose would need an exception to approved design.
10. **The withheld name.** The people behind the Institute are unnameable on B01's page by ruling (G1). Recommended: he turns his secrecy toward R. rather than his friends (items 6 and 8), and she answers his refusal with an act.
11. **Antagonist positions.** Item 8 removes a lever from each side. The optional oxygen-hold loss at about 68% gives a standalone win.
