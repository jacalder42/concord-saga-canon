**READING, recommended for testing:** make B07's break **civic, not structural**. The post and
Elias's amplification turn grief into a mobilisation that meets an institutional response in a
district. The district becomes untenable through conflict, fear and withdrawn services, not
because a building falls. The carry-forward check already says a second Warehouse collapse *"is
not required."*

### C. The order: two readings

1. **The post causes the break.** Kade's post (grief, blame or a call to act), amplified by
   Elias, mobilises people, and the district breaks. **The accountability is direct.**
2. **The break is already happening, and the post turns it.** A district is failing; Kade's post,
   amplified, turns evacuation into blame or mobilisation, and the failure becomes a break.
   **The accountability is shared.** This fits *"information becomes power as it becomes
   scarce"* (author, hedged).

Reading 1 gives Kade the sharper cost. Reading 2 keeps him more redeemable. **The author's to
choose.**
