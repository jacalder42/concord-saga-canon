## 2. The mirror: five axes

**[P]** throughout. Each axis names what the prologue does and what the epilogue answers.

| # | Axis | Prologue (B01 PR) | Epilogue (B09 EP) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Vantage** | A conversation **in** the sky: from above, looking down | A conversation **under** the sky: from below, looking up |
| 2 | **The pair** | **Two** who perceive and cannot touch the world: Silence and Hope | **Two** who can touch the world and are all potential: Lacuna and Kade. Both pairs are defined by what is not yet said |
| 3 | **Communication** | Incomplete. The two can barely reach each other, and cannot reach anyone below | A reach that **arrives** as an invitation. The prompt is open, and it is an **offer**, not an instruction |
| 4 | **The law** | *Wider perception does not equal control* | **Still true.** The veil is held jointly; Seraphine tethers and does not regulate (Mechanica §42A). The reach does not direct anyone |
| 5 | **The cut** | Ends on a hard cut to **the swamp**: a child in distress, and nobody arrives in time | Ends on **an unanswered prompt**. The swamp is mended, and **not shown**. Kade can answer, and the book closes first. **Both ends are open, so the last page leads back to the first** |

**Who is talking in the stars at the end** stays a reading, not a design decision. The author's
11-16 statement makes the prologue's talkers Silence and Hope. They have given themselves to the
Loom, so the epilogue's sky is **the new structure**, with Seraphine as its tether. Lacuna's
*"the stars look like they're talking"* works under any reading.

## 3. Execution standard for both ends

- **Brief, abstract and beautiful** at the prologue; **brief, human and plain** at the epilogue.
- **No explanation at either end.** No cosmology in the prologue; no account of how LT works in
  the epilogue.
- **No montage.** Not a prophecy montage at the start, and not a recap montage at the close.
- **No preview, no appearance.** The prologue does not preview the Mending. The epilogue does not
  show Tahl: the triangle **implies** him.

## 4. Guardrails

- **The prologue carries no LT motif.** No triangle, no *LuminousThread*, and no promise of a
  later reach.
- **The prologue names nothing.** Silence and Hope are not introduced by name to the reader
  unless B01 EBCI decides otherwise.
- **Lacuna does not name the Thread.**
- **Kade's answer is never shown.**
- **Nothing in between spends the mirror.** The sky may recur (B02 E41 echoes the prologue's
  register; see §5), but **no intermediate book stages a received reach from the sky.** That would
  pre-spend axis 3.
