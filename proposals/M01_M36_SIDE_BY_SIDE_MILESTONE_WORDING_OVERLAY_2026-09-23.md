## Decisions that remain genuinely conditional

1. **M11 exposure mechanism:** the [author ruling](../recovery/VEIL_STRUCTURE_2026-09-19.md) protects Tahl *accidentally exposing the incident* at which Baz is killed. It does not prove he creates the Rupture; exact exposure, recipient, response and causal contribution remain open. Do not reduce his remorse to a mistaken belief without an explicit author amendment.
2. **M12 identity timing:** the earlier [Veil reveal ruling](../recovery/VEIL_STRUCTURE_2026-09-19.md) allows B4 first naming, while the later [B3→B4 handoff](concord-2026/B03_B04_HANDOFF_RECONCILIATION_2026-09-19.md) moves the name/MT linkage into the B3 epilogue. Proposed text deliberately carries both options. B4 remorse remains either way.
3. **M16–M19 named scenes:** Riot of Light, first public B5 meta and NOLA Colorstorm are historical candidates. The later author-pasted macro protects other B4–B6 functions; do not aggregate those older events or promote an alleged first-global event without ruling/evidence.
4. **M20:** B6 Santa Fe death appears in Act II in the later author-pasted December macro. The original `A3` target and New Orleans child-shielding note are older staging. Propose A2 correction on the *review copy* only after confirming exact story sequence.
5. **M29/M31:** formal Echo Nodes remain post-Mending. The older B8 E11/15 pool/world pulse are not automatically retained alongside the later B8 storm-wall/chamber reveal. Decide whether wall opening is physical environmental change or route discovery.
6. **M26/M35:** MT is a public mortal information channel with differentiated technology reliability; LT is post-Mending identity. The old “last intact channel” and automatic `MT renamed LT` shorthand require separate communication and channel decisions.
7. **M33/M34/M36:** Mending is protected, but Silence/Hope exact dissolution, instant worldwide cessation and a quoted Tahl farewell remain story/physics questions, not grid facts.

## Dependency-type check before any CSV update

For each retained setup arrow, mark one of **chronological**, **informational**, **character/relationship**, **institutional**, **material cause**, **physical enabling condition**, or **thematic echo**. A later event does not require an earlier one merely because both appear in the same trilogy. The current high-risk arrows are `M01→M02/M03/M04` (retired micro-Shard), `M09→M10` (VT versus structural death), `M11→M12` (unrecovered exposure mechanics), `M19→M24` (old global Colorstorm), `M03/M29→M33` (premature B1 filter/formal B8 Node) and `M29→M31` (Node versus storm-wall).
