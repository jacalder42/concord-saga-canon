## 4. The contextual line pass (Q-D3-4, approved as method; run last)

**No quota.** In order of priority:
1. **Conspicuous clusters.** The same construction two or three times on a page, such as *the way …* similes, *did not*
   stacks or *for a long time*.
2. **Repeated endings.** Chapters closing on someone not saying something, a still object, or a phone face down.
3. **Explanations that duplicate an action:**
   - meaning stated after the image already showed it;
   - the ten duplications the ending test found in E49.

**Keep distinctive observations, even in a common shape.** Vary rhythm rather than shorten for its own sake. The
before-and-after counts are recorded **as description, not as a score.**
