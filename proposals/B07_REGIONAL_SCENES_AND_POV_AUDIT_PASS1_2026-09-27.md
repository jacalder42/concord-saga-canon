**Rules for receipts (REC):**

- **At most two foreign receipts per act.** Each has a **named or registered carrier**.
- Each shows a **different kind** of failure or survival: grid, institutions, community, weather.
- **None implies the swamp.** None gives an identifiable echo.

### 4.3 Risks

1. **Seraphine thins out.** In B07 she is placed and grieving, and her Loom pull is internal. Without
   the **A2 decision beat** and the **departure close**, she falls below band. She should own both.
2. **Kade peaks too early.** A 35% Kade book about his rise risks making B07 his fall's peak. The
   B07 card says it must not be. His A3 cost should be real but **redeemable**: the break is already
   happening and his post turns it (the card's reading 2).
3. **Elias at the funeral.** His identification of Kade is an emotional-climax setting, and his card
   forbids POV there. Carry it through **Lacuna's edge-awareness** or Kade's notice. **No Elias POV in
   B07.**
4. **Lacuna in the pool.** Her ruled prominence needs POV at the funeral and at the break. The
   recovered "sparingly, to ground Kade" rule fits A2. Two to three strong units, not a running
   strand.
5. **Receipts delivered only into Seraphine's POV** would collapse the world back into NOLA through
   her (pressure pass §5.1). Deliver them through their **owners**: Rex, Elisabet, Caro.
6. **The four-city list.** Keep Marrakesh and Vienna out of B07 (§3).
