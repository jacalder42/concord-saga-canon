## 0. Identity

**B08 = THE CIRCUIT AND THE FALL.** Two strands, one per ruled anchor. **The search strand:** the crew
works Tahl's shape into particulars, wound by wound, under Silence and Hope's hard-cap plan, and
Seraphine's doubt grows from what she is becoming. **Kade's strand:** estranged from Lacuna, he
follows the angry voices and **authorises harm**, telling himself he is keeping anyone from getting
hurt. **The strands meet only through MT.**

**Ruled anchors:** the Lacuna–Kade split opens the book (M44). **Santa Fe, the first wound: something
real is found, at a cost** (the A1 anchor). **Mound City after Santa Fe: the particulars confirmed**
(M47). **Kade's first complicity** (M46). **Serpent Mound, the third wound, at the book's end; the
escape opens B09** (M48). **The factions conclude "Louisiana"; the swamp stays secret** (M31's ruled
part; M51).

**Approved design used:** Seraphine and Lucien at every wound; Caro at Santa Fe and Mound City, then a
named task with a rejoin window (route Q2); Rex as Tahl's mourner at Santa Fe, carrying a recovered
artefact; Elisabet on the US network, no Reykjavík round trip; Lacuna in NOLA and the Gulf, building
the feint's capacity; **Kade authorises and does not strike; the victims are people at a Filament or
mutual-aid site, in a city that is neither NOLA nor a wound site** (D5); **Kade did not cause the
opening; the cap fails at a weak point; Elias claims it from a distance** (D2); the remnant's
instruments read the opening as **"Louisiana"** (A2); **no antagonist witnesses the site in B08**.

**Scale:** 45 episodes (15/15/15), 4 LR units, 2 supplements, 1 VT interlude.
**POV:** Seraphine 14 (31%) · Kade 10 (22%) · Lucien 6 · Rex 4 · Caro 4 · Lacuna 3 · Elisabet 2 ·
local 1 · VT 1 (Clock A; E33 re-owned Rex → Lucien, L10, 2026-09-27).

### Which clock, and why (for the author)

| Test | Clock A: one interval | Clock B: staggered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Crowding** (the reading in the chain ruling) | One act carries all of it | **Less crowded** |
| **The site secret** | Holds: the remnant is in Ohio, and the public hears "Louisiana" | Holds, but **the remnant spends days in Louisiana in B08**, close to the B09 A2 search. **It pre-spends the feint's ground** |

| **B09's start** | **One continuous chase** from Ohio | A chase that restarts |

**Recommended: Clock A.** Its crowding is real, but the episode count carries it (nine episodes after
the common six), and **the remnant never sets foot in Louisiana in B08**. **The deferral stands until
the author chooses.** **Chosen 2026-09-27: Clock A** (L3, approved design; `decisions/LOOM_PASS3_AND_B01_PRERELEASE_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-27.md`).
