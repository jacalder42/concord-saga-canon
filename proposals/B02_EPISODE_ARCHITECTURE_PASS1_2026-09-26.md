## Governing book identity

**B02 = PATTERN / PUBLIC LANGUAGE.**

The book globalizes **information before it globalizes the principal cast**. New Orleans remains the primary lived theater. Marrakesh becomes the first unmistakable distant human case through ordinary communication. Other cities may enter as checked reports rather than travel montage.

Working scale: **~42–46 narrative episodes + supplements**, subject to rhythm pass. Historical E-numbers are source coordinates, not retained numbering.

POV target remains diagnostic rather than arithmetic: roughly 30% Baz / 30% Seraphine / 30% secondary protagonists led by Lucien, Caro and Elisabet / 10% flex. No quota overrides scene ownership.

## B02 hard safeguards

- Lucien is physically in NOLA in Act I. **No B02 Vienna trip.**
- Marrakesh is a lived distant place, not a mystical signal source.
- Naima may carry recurring local continuity, but the exact A1 caller remains open.
- No B02 Tahl POV or named Tahl.
- No B02 VT contact for Tahl. Old “Brush” material becomes subjective/evidence discrepancy only.
- MT is mortal media.
- No perfect synchronized global pulse unless later Mechanica evidence earns it.
- “Drift” remains a working model, not formal ontology.
- Filaments do not become a magical order.
- Caro/Elisabet attraction can progress without consuming a dedicated subplot.
- Preserve at least 3–4 ordinary-life/reward beats across the book.
