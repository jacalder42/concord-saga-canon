## Book-level hypotheses to carry into saga rhythm

### B01 → B02: expansion of epistemic horizon
B01's locality is a feature. B02 should not simply add spectacle; it should introduce **independent observers and places with their own lives**. Marrakesh is the strongest recovered early theater. Vienna/Singapore can become legible through institutional/character ownership rather than anonymous world-map flashes.

### B03 → B04: separation should increase
The Neon transition is a natural point to **split the ensemble more, not less**. Baz's death and institutional consequences create reasons for Lucien/Vienna, Rex/Singapore, Elisabet/field work and NOLA's public sphere to diverge.

### B04–B06: likely geographic heart of the saga
This trilogy should test whether parallel theaters can carry distinct conflict systems:
- NOLA: public life, MT, rebellion, relationships;
- Vienna: Dominion/archive/family/Choirless intellectual pressure;
- Singapore: Technarc/meta ethics/tech failure;
- Reykjavík + field network: research, refuge, Caro/Elisabet and Filament work;
- Santa Fe: approaching threshold and eventual catastrophe.

This is a **hypothesis from recovered functions**, not a locked five-track outline.

### B07–B09: preserve places without requiring visits
Loom's scale should come from **known places changing**, not merely a longer travel list. Local anchors can carry Vienna/Singapore/Reykjavík/Marrakesh while selected principals work the wound chain, NOLA fracture, Kade/Brightbreak and final Mending.

## Rhythm implication

Do **not** recalibrate a single saga pressure curve before this ledger is developed one level deeper. Parallel theaters permit:
- one location escalating while another recovers;
- one relationship separating while another forms;
- world pressure rising while local reader pressure falls;
- information lag creating suspense without another physical event.

The next rhythm model should therefore distinguish at minimum **world/background pressure** from **active-thread/reader pressure** and should be able to represent different simultaneous theaters.
