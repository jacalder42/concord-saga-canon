Status: PROPOSAL / RECOVERY RECONCILIATION — NON-CANONICAL

Scope: Seraphine, Caro, Lucien, Baz, Elisabet; Lacuna disposition note.
Purpose: establish defensible pre-plot lives, entry causality, connections, mundane texture, recurring social/comic material, and B01 exit states before EBCI.

## Governing test

If Resonance vanished from the novel, would this person still have a reason to be in this place, know these people, and have something they were trying to do that day?

Every named B01 character must eventually receive:
1. pre-B01 state / residence;
2. livelihood and money/class reality;
3. reason to be in New Orleans;
4. reason to be at first on-page coordinate;
5. preexisting relationships;
6. ordinary objective independent of plot;
7. mundane life / obligations;
8. interests, hobbies, tastes and petty preferences;
9. recurring places/routes;
10. comic/social texture and possible callbacks;
11. B01 state change;
12. B01 exit vector;
13. recurrence expectation;
14. REMOVE / UNNAMED / CAMEO / B01 RECURRING / SAGA-SEED classification.

Do not invent missing biography merely to complete fields. Mark OPEN and recover first.

## Current-author rulings received 2026-09-22

These are current author decisions and supersede incompatible older temporal scaffolds:

- Lucien and Baz work for the same organization/group, historically engaged in research into Resonance-type signatures/events.
- Lucien is sent to New Orleans to investigate.

- When Lucien feels in over his head, he calls Baz for support.

- Caro watches this dynamic with interest; Caro/Elisabet remains the eventual primary romance.
- Lacuna's B01 fit is open to reassessment; recovered B01 performance material is evidence, not a requirement.

## Running-joke / callback policy

B01 should seed comic continuity, not a joke quota.

Target:
roughly 5-8 recurring social/comic motifs across the whole book, mostly relationship-based.

Best current engines:
1. Caro <-> Seraphine established-friend shorthand.
2. Baz <-> Lucien old-colleague/friend knowledge.
3. Caro <-> Lucien speed mismatch.
4. Trip's house/room rules and regular-customer ecology.
5. Seraphine being cared for when she reflexively tries to become caretaker.
6. One mundane object/errand/problem that survives multiple anomaly episodes.
7. Elisabet's social directness producing reactions without making flirtation her personality.
8. Optional Vein music/bar disagreement if Trip/Lacuna survives.

Rules:
- recurrence must change meaning;
- do not make every character witty;
- jokes cannot erase grief, competence or class/cultural reality;
- do not telegraph Baz's death by making his callbacks prematurely sentimental;
- a joke may become a relationship diagnostic when someone stops making it.

## Exit policy

Include B01 exits in the same character-life pass.

Exit means the last defensible B01 state, not necessarily leaving town or leaving the saga.

Each exit should answer:
- where are they physically/socially after the book?
- what ordinary obligation resumes?
- what changed?
- why will they recur, or why should the reader not expect them immediately?
- is the exit active (choice/departure) or ambient (life continues)?

This is especially important for supporting characters so they do not disappear when their plot utility ends.
