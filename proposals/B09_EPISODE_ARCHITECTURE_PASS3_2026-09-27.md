## 0. Identity

**B09 = THE MENDING.** Acts I–II are the author's *"end game setup"*: **escape, pursuit with sparks, and
a feint in New Orleans while the crew goes to the swamp.** Act III is the finale in the author's shape:
the antagonists learn the site; Elisabet and Rex hang back; **in the last two chapter clusters, the
Elias / Kade / Rex / Tahl scene, then the Mending** (the author: *"two clusters / chapters, not two
episodes"*). Then the epilogue.

**Ruled:** the escape from Serpent Mound opens the book (M48). The NOLA feint while the crew is
elsewhere (M50). **The factions learn the swamp is the Mending site in Act III and not before** (M51).
Elias attacks Rex; Rex knocks him down; Kade, fearing Rex will kill Elias, attacks Rex; **Tahl's one
identifiable flare stops him, known by Rex** (M52). **The Mending:** Seraphine → Hope → Silence (after
powering Tahl's echo) → Lucien → Elisabet's goodbye → Caro; Mira's echo released (M33). **The veil is
held jointly** (§42A). The epilogue, a few days later: MT continues; Lacuna and Kade under the night
sky; the LT prompt through Tahl's echo; Kade can answer, and the saga ends before he does (M35, M53).

**Approved design used:** the remnant pursues from Serpent Mound (M59); pursuit with sparks, including
the forward group's recommitment at the feint split, the guide relationships and a last intimacy, **no
price previewed, no farewell staged** (09-27 Q8); Lacuna carries the feint with the local network; the
Choirless are not in the NOLA search (D18); **the Mending is the signal** that exposes the site (A2);
Rex and Elisabet hold a separate rear approach; Elisabet warns in person, under a real constraint
(route Q8); the Choirless and Brightbreak collide at the perimeter; Kade talks down Brightbreak and
stands with Rex against the Choirless, unresolved (A4); Caro rejoins before Act III (route Q2).

**Scale:** 45 episodes (15/15/15) and **a three-episode epilogue (E46–E48, `EP`)**; 3 LR units; 2
supplements; 3 VT interludes.
**POV:** Seraphine 13 (27%) · Kade 9 · Elisabet 6 · Caro 5 · Rex 4 · Lucien 4 · Lacuna 3 · VT 3 ·
local 1 (E02 re-owned Rex → Lucien, L10, 2026-09-27). **Across Loom, Seraphine is 41 of 138 (30%);
Lucien 12 (9%).**
