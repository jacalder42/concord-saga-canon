### The one clarification (proposed text, about 100 words, for profile §5A or the prose README)

> *Mystery is not stasis.* The cause stays unexplained; progress does not. A scene that tests something should leave the
> characters able to believe or do something they could not before. That can be a door closed, a practice that helped,
> or a finding limited to what was measured. A point-of-view character may be confidently wrong, and the page may let it
> stand. A supporting character may care and still misread what someone needs. Attraction may bend a practical decision
> without a word said. Nobody has to state what a scene meant. Lucien may conceal where he comes from while openly
> pursuing what he wants to know.

### 3.1 The inspection

- **Who investigates.** A previously well child who dies unexpectedly is a coroner's case. The statutory child
  death-review protocol applies.
  - The autopsy is effectively certain. From 1 August 2026, SB 29 makes it mandatory for unexplained deaths of children
    under 15.
  - The investigator documents the scene himself.
- **Carbon monoxide** is settled first by **blood carboxyhemoglobin**. Oxygen given in the ambulance and at the hospital
  lowers it, so a late draw can leave the result **equivocal**.
- **The hazard check.** At a scene, the fire department, the utility, the sheriff or the fire marshal checks for hazards.
  Coroners and sheriffs do sometimes bring in **outside specialists**, as in reported furnace cases.
  - **Plausible:** with a generator present and an equivocal result, the coroner asks for a dwelling hazard check (CO,
    ventilation, electrical), and the parish's contracted firm does it.
  - **Implausible:** a routine structural inspection, or a check after a clear blood result.
- **The weak source.** An outdoor generator 130 ft downwind is a weak CO source; fatal cases are indoor or attached.
  That suits a check whose purpose is to **rule it out**.
- **Keeping the *None* distinct.** The form has two separate things:
  - **Instrument and appliance findings** (CO readings, ventilation, fuel-burning appliances): Lucien records them
    honestly as normal.
  - **The broad line**, *conditions observed affecting the health or safety of occupants, including the inspector*: his
    *None* conceals **his own experience** there, exactly as now. It is not a technical finding.
- **What the change adds.** Had he reported his episode in that room, a dizzy inspector in a house with a generator
  history, the coroner could not close the environmental question. **His lie speeds closure. Seraphine's truth
  slows it.**

### 3.2 Case closure

- **Pending.** The death certificate can issue as **"pending"**, so burial proceeds. It is amended when toxicology,
  histology and cultures return, typically **6–12 weeks**. B01 runs from late February to about 2 May, which fits.
- **The coroner's authority.**
  - The coroner's cause and manner stand unless a court orders otherwise.
  - The family may request the reports and may ask for an amendment.
  - A single statement cannot hold a case open indefinitely. Whether the manner is finalised or left undetermined is the
    coroner's judgment.
- **Why the family wants closure.** A pending certificate delays life insurance, benefits and the paperwork.
- **The repaired lever.** Seraphine's statement describes an unexplained pressure in the room where the child died,
  something a reader of the file cannot distinguish from an environmental symptom. While it stands, Guidry **can**, at his
  discretion, keep the environmental question open or record the manner as undetermined, and he chooses to. Nothing
  obliges him (amended 10-03, F2).
- **Renée's agency:**
  - She has the right to the reports.
  - She asks Seraphine to amend or withdraw her paragraph.
  - She can ask the coroner directly.
  - She can refuse Seraphine entirely.

  Her objective (a final, natural certificate so she can be done with offices) and her power over Seraphine both stay.
- **Medicaid.** Fear of an ambulance bill delaying a call is well documented, and EMS transports regardless of coverage.
  **Odile and the card stay.** One realistic addition, optional: investigators note the delay, which can bring DCFS
  questions.
