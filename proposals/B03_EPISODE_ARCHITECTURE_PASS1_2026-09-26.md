**Purpose:** Reconcile B03 into episode-level architecture that protects Baz as Veil lead, the Warehouse human climax, anonymous MT influence, delayed B04 death confirmation, and the Tahl epilogue reveal.

## Governing book identity

**B03 = CONSEQUENCE / TRUTH HAS A COST.**

B03 should not become a Tahl book before the epilogue and should not become a world tour. Its scale expands because **information changes behavior** and because institutions contest evidence.

Working scale: **~42–45 narrative episodes + supplements/epilogue**, subject to rhythm pass.

POV diagnostic: Baz and Seraphine remain dominant; Lucien and other secondary protagonists carry meaningful ownership; anonymous MT artifacts can have high presence without becoming named Tahl POV. Tahl gets the epilogue reveal, not repeated main-book cutaways.

## Epilogue — The anonymous voice becomes a person
**1–3 epilogue units, not necessarily standard narrative episodes**

1. **Aftermath:** Threadnaut/MT reviews reports: unexpected bystanders, hampered response, a dead responder/rescuer; he knows his accurate post changed behavior but did not know Baz was there.
2. **Reveal:** the narrative names him for the first time: **Tahl Morgan**. MT/Threadnaut becomes retrospectively legible.
3. **First/only Veil VT brush:** after disengaging from feeds/screens and looking outward/upward, Tahl experiences the bounded accidental contact. No device carries VT, no dialogue/power/proof.
4. **Ethical return:** he publishes only what mortal evidence supports. Remorse, humility and responsibility become the engine of B04 Tahl.
5. **Optional final receipt:** southwest/Santa Fe remains a direction/problem, not an answer.

## B03 hard safeguards

- Baz remains Veil trilogy lead through his death.
- Tahl is not named and does not receive ordinary main-text POV before the epilogue.
- Anonymous MT presence may increase substantially without violating that rule.
- Tahl did **not** know Baz was at Warehouse.
- The coordinates post is accurate; any crowd/delay consequence remains bounded by the event-card status.
- Dominion may be expected at the perimeter; Technarc may be the unexpected intervention under delegated working assumption. Neither causes the Rupture or murders Baz.
- Reader witnessing Baz's death while the cast remains uncertain is the current strongest architecture; still a working assumption until explicitly ruled.
- The rest of the cast learns Baz died at the **start of B04**.
- Tahl's B03 epilogue contact is his **first and only Veil-era VT brush**.
- No Baz Echo. His mortality is the point.
- Do not add a second B03 spectacle after Warehouse. The epilogue changes **meaning**, not event scale.
