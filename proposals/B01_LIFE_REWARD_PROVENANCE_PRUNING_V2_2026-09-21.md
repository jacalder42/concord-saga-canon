## Executive finding

The concern that Prologue + 36 episodes feels underweight is supported, but the solution is not to restore 60–80 plot-bearing episodes.

Recovered B1 material shows two simultaneous truths:
1. Compression from the old E00+42 substrate to P+36 removed useful repetition.
2. Recovered episode/reconciliation material repeatedly identifies missing joy, humor, ordinary wonder, food/coffee/music, nightlife, social life, Velvet Vein/Trip, ordinary city reaction, Baz learning NOLA through people, and Seraphine/Lucien existing in the city for reasons other than anomaly.

Use a hybrid restoration:
- keep the 36 narrative-spine episodes;
- restore lived texture inside spine episodes where recovered EBCI supports it;
- add a limited set of standalone Life/Reward units where compression otherwise destroys breathing room, anticipation, relationship consequence, or recovery.

## Provenance labels

RECOVERED — function directly attested in recovered B1/Veil source or forensic audit.
COMPATIBLE — not recovered as an exact old episode, but directly implements recovered architecture/relationship/place guidance without changing causal canon.
NEW — genuinely fresh scene proposal; lowest preservation priority.

## Disposition labels

STANDALONE — preserve as own dramatic unit unless later prose proves it combines naturally.
EMBED — restore as breathing-room material inside an existing spine episode.
OPTIONAL — reserve; use only if ECG shows a gap.
DROP — redundant or insufficiently supported.

## Revised B1 size target

Base:
- Prologue
- 36 narrative-spine episodes

Add:
- 14 strong standalone companions
- 1 short closing companion
- 0–2 optional reserve units if ECG requires

Working total:
**51–53 episodes + Prologue.**

This is intentionally lower than the historical 60–80 target but materially fuller than P+36.

The old 60–80 was a planning envelope, not a canon requirement. The current 36 spine has already compressed repetitive interpretation/escalation. Embedded texture will increase prose mass inside many of the 36. New standalone units are selected for breathing room, not to manufacture plot.

At historical ~1.3–1.8k words per unit, 51–53 units suggests a raw serial-planning range of roughly 66k–95k words before chapter combination and prose expansion. Several spine episodes can naturally run longer. Do not use word count as the primary reason to add a scene.
