## 1. B02 amendments

### E03 — THE FIRST PULSE *(T1)*
**Replaces the story job:**
- **Was:** *"The city's first shared public event, and its first word for it."*
- **Now:** **the first pulse the whole city names.** B01's events were real, and the words for them
  were scattered (B01 L1278). This time **one word, *pulse*, sticks**, city-wide, by nightfall.

**Turn change:** only *"Changes"*. The word that spreads is no longer the city's first reaction; it is
the first that lasts. *"B2 owns broader public 'pulse' naming"* (B01 L1478).

### E15 — THE LARGER PULSE *(T1; retitled, was "The Second Pulse")*
The content is unchanged. **The title no longer counts pulses**, which B01 had already begun.

### E19 — GROUNDING HELPS LESS *(T4)*
**Replaces the last Turn line and beat:**
- **Was:** *"Arianna names the ethic: control was never the promise."*
- **Now:** **nobody names it.** B01 E35 already said it (B01 L976). Here it is **practised**:
  - Arianna closes the circle early, because it is not helping.
  - Seraphine stays with the young man as he is.
  - Mara sits with the two who could not come in.
- **B02's axis:** grounding helps **people** less, and they stay anyway.

### E30 — THE LANTERN *(T3)*
**Replaces the Turn:**
- **Encounter:** after the cluster, **Elisabet is shaken.** Her data underestimated M06. Caro has just
  finished the oxygen resident's placement (Leila helped; Caro kept the follow-up), and finds her in
  the ambulance bay.
- **Wants:** Elisabet wants to go back to the data. Caro wants her to stop for one hour.
- **Against:** Elisabet will not let herself be the one who is looked after.
- **Changes:** **Caro brings food and sits with her in the rig, and Elisabet lets her.** It reverses
  B01 E30, as B01's own note asks (*"Caro receives clarity → future reciprocal grounding"*, L1379).
  The attraction is plain to both. **Deepening, not declared** (M07).

### E40 — DRIFT *(T9)*
**Replaces the last Turn line:**
- **Was:** *"Lucien does not pass it on."*
- **Now:** **Nobody has asked him for it yet**, and he keeps it to himself for now. The decision moves
  to E47.

### E47 — NO RESET *(T9)*
**Adds a decision, B02's Act III climax:**
- After Baptiste announces the postponement, **Helena asks Lucien for the drift model**, so the
  Dominion can plan the zone's extension.
- **Lucien withholds it.** It is not the B03 refusal: he says it is unproven. **It is the ladder's
  next rung**, chosen, with a cost to his standing with Helena.
- **The rest of E47 stands:** the ensemble separated; distant receipts; the zone stands; uncertainty,
  not despair.

## 2. B03 amendments

### E07 — BY MOUTH *(T11)*
**Adds one report**, without Rex. Among the regional stories Mara collects is one from **Detroit**, by
way of the trucker: **a repairman who put a whole block's heat back on during a flicker night.** No
name. **It pays off in E43.**

### E13 — ONE READING AMONG MANY *(T2)*
**Replaces the Turn:**
- **Encounter:** **Seraphine goes with Baz** to the Warehouse District loading dock. The dock foreman
  is scared, and pretends not to be.
- **Wants:** Baz wants to fit the building into his standing map. Seraphine wants to see the people
  he reads as data.
- **Against:** the building does not stand. It comes and goes, a little larger each time.
- **Changes:**
  - **She reads the people, he reads the map**, and between them they get the foreman to talk.
  - **On the walk back they have their own conversation.** He asks what it is like to feel it. She
    asks why he stayed in New Orleans. **Each tells the other one thing they have not told Lucien.**
  - He marks the building as **a different kind of thing**.

**Guardrail:** no ominous framing. **It is the ordinary anchor B04's grief draws on.**

### E35 — ELSEWHERE *(T10)*
**Replaces Seraphine's line in the Turn.** This is **her parallel climax, in the same hour.**

- **Encounter:** at the off-registry household, **the father breaks down.**
- **Wants:** Seraphine wants to be where she is needed.
- **Against:** **the coordinates post (S03) lights up her phone.** She knows the district and feels
  the pull to go: the universal responder again.
- **Changes:** **she stays with the father**, calls the rota for his medication, and holds the room
  until he sleeps. **Her finite responsibility, chosen under pressure.** It is also the choice B04's
  *"the person she could not save"* will ask her to live with.
- **The rest of E35 stands:** Lucien's calls drop; Caro is routed round the perimeter; Elisabet
  watches the feeds.

**Guardrail:** her staying does not cause anything at the Warehouse, and is never framed as blame.
