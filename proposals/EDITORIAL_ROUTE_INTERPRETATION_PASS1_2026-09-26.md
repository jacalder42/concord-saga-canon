# Editorial Route Interpretation — Pass 1

Status: NON-CANONICAL PROPOSAL  
Date: 2026-09-26  
Purpose: editorial interpretation of the main-cast location forensics: where the principal characters should physically be for the strongest nine-book narrative.

This document follows `proposals/CHATGPT_EDITORIAL_ROUTE_INTERPRETATION_BRIEF_2026-09-26.md`, with the author's same-day clarifications: approval inherits the status/scope of the proposal; ~6,000 words is a target rather than a ceiling; the two Tahl VeilThread brushes are approved; and B06 Santa Fe rupture/death is distinct from the B08 return to Santa Fe as the first Loom wound-stabilisation stop.

**Authority key:** **FIXED** = restates an existing ruling/current constraint; **LEAN** = author direction/lean already on record; **DESIGN** = recommendation made by this pass; **RR** = information carried remotely rather than physical presence.

The governing reading is that the saga's geography should expand by **separating people into character-owned theaters**, not by making the principal cast tour the world together. The main-cast itinerary is not the world map; absence and delayed information are useful architecture. Source: `decisions/SAGA_CAST_SEPARATION_AND_GLOBAL_THEATERS_AUTHOR_RULING_2026-09-26.md` §§Accepted/Immediate consequence.

## 1. Executive recommendation

The cleanest saga route has four geographic phases:

| Phase | Geographic job | Recommended shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **B01** | Make home worth losing | NOLA/Louisiana is dense and lived. Global lives exist off-page or as bounded breadcrumbs. |
| **B02–B03** | Information outruns bodies | NOLA remains the principal lived center; Marrakesh becomes the first strong distant human case through Naima/local witnesses; Tahl moves independently; Vienna/Singapore/other cities enter mostly through people, institutions and reports. |
| **B04–B06** | Parallel theaters become plot | NOLA, Vienna, Chicago, Reykjavík/research, Detroit/Singapore/Technarc and Tahl's itinerant reporting all carry different conflicts. Santa Fe is Tahl's endpoint, not an ensemble destination. |
| **B07–B09** | Distance becomes cost, then selective convergence | B07 funeral re-gathers only enough cast to make later separation hurt. B08 follows the four-wound circuit selectively while other characters carry repair/care/institutional work. B09 escape → feint → Honey Island converges only the people needed for each final function. |

**READING:** this is the strongest correction to the flattening recorded in `recovery/MAIN_CAST_LOCATION_REGISTERS_PASS1_2026-09-26.md` §4. It preserves the current causal spine while restoring the environmental breadth that older NOLA-only and group-travel versions compressed.

# 16. Consequences if the recommended route is adopted

1. **Veil stays geographically coherent.** B02 expands through lived remote cases and Tahl's independent movement, not by exporting Caro/Seraphine.
2. **Neon becomes the geographic heart of the saga.** NOLA, Vienna, Chicago, Reykjavík, Detroit/Singapore and Tahl's reporting route each belong to a person rather than a montage.
3. **B06 becomes stronger by not converging.** Tahl dies at Santa Fe while Seraphine/Lucien/Caro/Lacuna remain tied to NOLA and Rex/Elisabet own other theaters.
4. **B07 funeral becomes a genuine re-gathering.** Some people are already in NOLA; others arrive through damaged travel systems; the reunion carries asymmetric knowledge.
5. **Loom's wound circuit stops being an ensemble road trip.** Seraphine/Lucien provide continuity; Caro/Elisabet/Rex can enter/exit by function; Kade/Lacuna/Elias carry separate human conflicts.
6. **The B09 feint finally has a natural owner.** Lacuna and NOLA locals can carry it while Seraphine is ruled absent.
7. **The Mending convergence becomes exceptional.** Seraphine/Lucien/Caro/Elisabet arrive because their routes require them; Rex/Kade/Elias remain in the human conflict outside it.
8. **Known cities can survive without principal visits.** Marrakesh, Vienna, Reykjavík, Singapore and Detroit can continue through recurring locals, institutions and information once established.
