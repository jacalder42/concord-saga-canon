**Counting rule:** one candidate per independently changed option, role, relationship, physical condition or information state, with a carrier into later choices. A learning beat counts only when it changes an actionable inference or capability. A boss battle spanning preparation and result counts once; each blow, notification or receipt does not become another event. A setup/ongoing condition alone does not count. A multi-kind event gets **one primary kind** plus optional secondary kinds. A/B/C are *analytic scope analogies to Concord*, not claims these published books use our event ontology.

### Rivers — source-observable chains, chapter positions withheld

The [author's own description](https://www.benaaronovitch.com/product/rivers-of-london-4/) confirms a murder/ghost witness, apprenticeship, river truce and malevolent spirit as independent story concerns. The [longer secondary synopsis](https://www.supersummary.com/rivers-of-london/summary/) shows their interaction: the witness changes Peter's assignment; the investigation develops evidence and a candidate explanation; a river-boundary dispute imposes a separate deadline; attacks, an ally's implication and Nightingale's injury change the investigation; a public riot and historical inquiry change the final explanation; both the possession and river dispute receive distinct resolutions.

**No event count is reported.** The whole-book synopsis supplies sequence but not chapter boundaries, time spans, the number of interim investigations, or the amount of normal life between crises. Its fourteen-chapter table-of-contents conflict with the earlier twelve-chapter estimate is unresolved. Assigning these moments to evenly spaced thirds would manufacture the clustering result we came to measure.

## Observations and limits

1. **Opening event type is broader than physical conflict.** DC-03/04/06/07 and AM-01/02/04/05/06/10 alter access, rules, status, relationships or choices without a fight. These counts are candidate rows from summaries, *not peer benchmarks*.
2. **One set piece can create several kinds of consequence while remaining one initiating event.** DC-08 changes resources and moral burden. AM-09 joins restriction, escape and decision; splitting every visible symptom would inflate the count.
3. **Coarse maps erase local density.** The previous five-book ten-window comparison remains useful for rhythm, but its ACOMAF opening shorthand cannot reveal the multiple chapter-anchored social/institutional turns above.
4. **Source resolution varies.** The two opening pilots cover different fractions by chapter count, not prose length. Their 9 and 10 candidate rows should **not** be compared as rates. Rivers cannot yet enter the quantitative matrix.
5. **Concord implication:** keep physical incidents, evidence/institutional decisions, location changes, relationships and aftermath distinct in B07–B08's unslotted pool. A summary that names only the final-site approach may suppress local events that exist in source drafts; equally, a long list of observations does not prove a book has turns.

## Measurement gate before numerical claims

- Secure the relevant authorized texts or detailed verified chapter notes; verify chapter/edition numbering for Rivers. Do not use an unlicensed full-text PDF or a compressed chapter recap as a substitute.
- Double-code at least the above opening sample with a second pass, settling event boundary disputes and attaching word/page anchors if available.
- Finish **all chapters** at the same source granularity per book. Only then calculate per-book events by kind and level, measured position/10k-word density, local clusters, longest gap, relationship to set pieces, and aftermath lag. Report denominator and uncertainty beside each metric.
- Keep the prior decile recode as a visual orientation, not as proof that these partial chapter ledgers are complete.

No Concord plot, rules, Book 1 sequence or milestone grid is amended here.
