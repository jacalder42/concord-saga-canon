## 8. Costs and risks

**Tone and warmth.** Veil gets less cosy. B02 Act II now holds five protagonist collisions in ten episodes (E21, E22,
E25, E30, E31). **Mitigations:** each has a cause in earlier material and a want against a want; none is played for
cruelty; the life basins are untouched (B02 E02, E04, E18, E32, **E45**; B03 E06, E08, E26); and warmth remains, only no
longer free.

**Drift into manufactured conflict.** The risks and the guards:
- **Withholding as a house style.** Lucien withholds, Seraphine and Lucien withhold from each other, Caro withholds a
  slice, Baz withholds logs. At packet level each must read differently: a boundary (Caro), isolation (Baz), mutual
  retreat (Seraphine and Lucien).
- **Ordinary people as instruments.** Rieux, Kesi, Leila, Mara, the neighbour and Denise are pressures by their own
  lights (XP2). None of them serves the Dominion or Technarc. Rieux's log is used by the review; he did not write it
  for them.
- **Everyone arguing about the same thing.** The list recurs (E21, E25, E31, B03 E22). That is the book's spine; the
  four scenes argue it from four different wants (his standing, her consent, Caro's patients, Mara's book).

**B04's grief shape.** It gets more complicated, as the audit asked:
- Lucien's grief is complicated: he was forgiven for deciding for Baz nine episodes before the death he will feel he
  decided.
- Seraphine's *"could not save"* now has Caro's voice against the rota. The narration still endorses no blame.
- Elisabet carries her refusal to co-sign as her own inference.
- Trip's backing was never given.

**The cost:** **a reader may feel the unfinished rifts as tragic irony.** No brief may point at it (the Baz test).

**Reader goodwill.**

  risks being unlikeable before Vienna. What holds him: his want is intelligible, the refusal (B03 E16) is his, and he

  risks looking petty. What holds him: his hurt is specific and right, his repair is joyful (E26), and E37–E38 are
