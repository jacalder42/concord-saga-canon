### A.2 Findings

**Santa Fe carries more than any other thin place.** It is where the B06 rupture happened, where
Tahl died, and, in the author's words, where *"Silence grieves and collects him."* The B08 search
therefore goes back to Neon's anchor. The cast return to the place where their truth-carrier died.
The B6 card's *"consequence in person"* now has a Loom sequel. **READING:** that makes Santa Fe the
book's emotional core, not only a stop on the route.

**Constraints the return must keep:**

- **Tahl's Echo must not be identifiable.** It appears identifiably only once, in B09's flare,
  known by Rex (ruled). Echo-like moments at the scar may occur, but *"may or may not be his."*
- **Seraphine's answer is reserved for the end.** The author (11-13): *"At the close, at the
  final moment, Seraphine truely understands the answer."* **Santa Fe can give doubt about the
  hard cap. It cannot give the answer.**
- **Formal Echo Nodes are post-Mending** (Mechanica §7.4). The scar is a scar, not a node.
- **The Rupture's causes stay under D5.** The finding is evidence and experience, not physics
  written on the page.

> **Superseded in part, 2026-09-26 (ruling, third follow-up):** *"the antagonist factions and

The crew is at Santa Fe. The storm wall is at Honey Island. **Three readings, not chosen:**

1. **The finding alone** (F2) points them back, and the book ends there. The fleeting opening is
   not needed.
2. **The finding says "it's not here"; the reported opening says "there."** The opening reaches
   them as **Elias's claim of a Kade miracle**. The crew reads the report differently from the
   factions. **The site secret holds:** the factions hear of a miracle, not a site.
3. **The opening alone** points them. That makes Santa Fe a dead end, which **wastes the ruled
   anchor**. Not recommended.

**Reading 2 uses everything ruled.** It leaves one question open (already on file): **where Elias
and Kade are when the opening happens.** If they are near the swamp in B08, the splinter is near
the site before B09. That does not break the secret but tests it. The claim can also be made
from a distance, since Elias *"claims it … whether it really was or not."*

### B.2 Findings

**Complicity, not violence by his hand.** The B09 near-kill of Rex is the first time Kade strikes.
B08 must not use that up. **READING, recommended:** in B08 Kade **authorises**. His words, a post
or a nod become the permission the splinter acts on, while he tells himself he is restraining
them. That:

- fits the author's *"he tells himself he's doing it to make sure no one gets hurt"*;
- is a small version of EliasEBCI's *"attempts to force irreversible authorization"*, which the
  B09 recovery makes the finale's shape (*"Elias engineers the frame in which Kade …
  authorizes"*). **B07's post, B08's permission and B09's attempted authorisation form a
  rising line**, in keeping with the ruling's "different in kind" steps;
- keeps him redeemable. He did not strike, but he cannot say he did not know.

**Who the violence hits: options, not chosen.**

1. **The Choirless**, or a Choirless-quieted community's enforcers. Violence against coercers
   makes the complicity morally murky, and it brings in POOL-017 as pressure without making it
   the anchor. *Risk:* it could read as justified.
2. **A Filament shelter or its people.** Direct harm to the crew's own world, so Lacuna's argument
   is proven; historical candidates are Ms. Leontine's refuge and Pivot's *"near-execution"*.
   *Risk:* it may make redemption too costly.
3. **Ordinary people in the wrong place.** Mirrors the B03 bystanders. *Risk:* it repeats the
   Warehouse pattern.

**The strands meet through MT.** The split means Kade is apart. **READING:** the crew, and
Lacuna, learn of his complicity the way the world does, through MT. His own channel is how the
people who love him find out. That is B08's emotional cost for the crew side, and it needs no
extra event.
