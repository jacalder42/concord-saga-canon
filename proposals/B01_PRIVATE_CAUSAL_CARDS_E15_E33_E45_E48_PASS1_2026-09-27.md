### 1.3 Four material registers, ordinary downstream physics

**Recommended design (PROPOSAL, Q-C4):** one OPEN source, and **each event expresses itself through a
different ordinary medium**. What happens between the medium and the observation is ordinary physics
that a skeptic can test. The only unexplained link is the source.

The working description of the disturbance is **a bounded, low-frequency mechanical pressure
disturbance in air, structure or ground**, at and below the bottom of hearing. No frequency, level or
signature is specified (the 09-23 ruling's *"avoid fabricating a frequency or coherence signature"*).
This description is a private working label, not a term for the page.

| Event | Register | Ordinary downstream coupling (testable) | Why this register here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **E15** | **Air / sound** | Audible thrum; a live PA picks up the low end and trips (feedback or over-excursion, protection mute) | An open Square with live amplified music |
| **E33** | **Glass / frames** | Single-glazed older windows and their frames respond to low-frequency pressure (the same way thunder or a heavy truck rattles them); microphones and sensors overload | Residential buildings inside the predicted zone |
| **E45** | **Ground / bodies** | Structure- and ground-borne vibration felt through feet and bodies; light loose objects shift; people stagger | A stronger event at a site where people have gathered |
| **E48** | **Still water** | A still water surface shows a fine standing ripple from sub-audible ground vibration | A quiet event, below hearing and below glass response; only the most sensitive ordinary indicator registers it |

**Why one source and four registers, not four couplings.** The 09-23 reviews warn against *"one
all-purpose law"* and against a shared window-and-amp law invented to link two episodes. This design
asserts **no** Resonance-to-matter law per medium: the downstream couplings are ordinary acoustics and
mechanics. It leaves **one** OPEN link (the source) instead of four (air, glass, circuit, water). The
alternative, a distinct Resonance coupling per event, is kept as fork A2 in each card.

**What would disconfirm the single-source design across the set:** E15's two original recordings show
no low-end content before the pop; E33's panes move on one façade only, in a sequence that follows a
passing vehicle; E45's shudder is recorded by an ordinary source (construction, a heavy vehicle, a
subsidence event); E48's ripple coincides with logged river or street traffic. Any one of these
downgrades that event to fork C without breaking the others.

**Mechanica §20–§21's conductivity ranking** (water highest, air low) is **consistent** with the
registers (the quietest event shows only in water) but is **not used as a law**: the systems audit
calls it underspecified, and R8 makes such tables tendencies only.

### 1.4 Cost is not one bucket

From the 09-23 coupling review: (1) **ordinary** consequences (lost work, repairs, injuries, fear);
(2) **deliberate-action** cost, only for a named person's actual Resonance-influenced act (Mechanica
§12–§17: immediate cost plus lingering consequence); (3) **ambient budget/residue** if fork A holds;
(4) **interpretive/social** cost. *"Ordinary repair/cleanup cost is not automatically UARS
expenditure"* (D5).

**Ambient budget (PROPOSAL, S1):** each event releases some stored pressure locally without repairing
the cap. The site stays thin. The interval to the next event is the private signature the trio's
model reads. Shortening intervals (E35–E36) mean accumulation is outrunning seepage. No quantities.

| Event | ActionState (§13) | What she does | Cost (§15) |

| E15 | **Baseline** | Ordinary care skill, one person at a time; attuned exposure, no deliberate boost | Ordinary exertion; attuned fatigue (R7: exposure is fallible and tiring) |
