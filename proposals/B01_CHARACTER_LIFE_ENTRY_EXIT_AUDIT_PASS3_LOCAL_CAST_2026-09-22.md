# B01 Character Life / Entry / Exit Audit — Pass 3: Local-Life Supporting Cast

Scope: Trip, Mara/M, Rootkeeper, Lacuna.
Purpose: decide which local supporting faces deserve B01 reader bandwidth and identify missing ordinary-life requirements before EBCI.

## Governing principle

A supporting character earns recurrence through **life + relationship + place**, not because an old outline assigned them a plot function.

Test:
If Resonance disappeared, would this person still have a reason to be here, know these people, and be doing something today?

# 1. TRIP

Classification:
**B01 RECURRING / PROTECT.**

## Recovered life

Trip already passes the no-plot test better than almost any supporting character.

Recovered Tier-1:
- New Orleans origin;
- Black American / New Orleans Creole-adjacent civic culture;
- working-class service economy;
- bartender/host at Velvet Vein;
- nightlife work deliberately chosen because it places her at information flow and safety;
- social/situational authority, not metaphysical authority;
- professional name Trip; legal name intentionally undisclosed.

Her job exists whether the anomaly exists.

## First appearance

Best entry:
Trip is already working.

Do not “introduce Trip” through lore.
Show:
- room reading;
- boundary setting;
- customer recognition;
- service work;
- ordinary conflict;
- hospitality decisions.

The reader learns her by watching her hold a room.

## Ordinary objective

Possible recovered-compatible objective:
**get through a good night without the room becoming someone else's problem.**

This is not a locked line or literal scene goal, but it captures the existing function.

B01 should give her at least one concern unrelated to Resonance:
- staffing;
- a difficult regular;
- a performer issue;
- supplies;
- closing;
- money;
- neighborhood nuisance;
- house rule;
- interpersonal friction.

Exact choice OPEN.

## Money/class

Recovered:
working-class service economy.

Protect the labor:
- late hours;
- feet/back;
- cleanup;
- stock;
- tips/cash;
- regulars;
- safety decisions;
- who gets cut off;
- who gets walked out;
- who gets fed/watered.

Do not romanticize the Vein into effortless magical hospitality.

## Social network

Recovered Tier-1 relationship lanes:
Seraphine, Lucien, Baz, Caro, Elisabet, Rex, Kade, Lacuna.

For B01, not all need equal familiarity.

Trip should know **locals as locals** before investigators become important.

If Caro/Seraphine are already NOLA friends, Vein can be part of their social ecology without making either a nightly regular.

## Humor/callback engine

High-value.

Best source is **house rules + customer memory**, not Trip delivering sitcom lines.

A Trip callback can change:

Do not invent a list of quirky rules yet. EBCI can select one or two after the cast map.

## B01 exit

Ambient continuity:

The city has changed; her work continues.

This is valuable because not every B01 exit should be “joins investigation.”

Reader expectation:
Trip/Vein will recur because the protagonists have a social place, not because Trip receives a mission.

## Open

Approved consolidation:

## Current weakness

Mara presently has a **function but insufficient civilian biography**.

We know what she does for the narrative.
We do not yet adequately know what she does for rent, family, pleasure or obligation.

That is exactly the kind of character-appliance risk this audit exists to catch.

## B01 use

E08 is a good first coordinate only if Mara is already doing something that belongs to her life.

Avoid:
“Mara appears because Seraphine needs a Filament elder.”

Prefer:
Seraphine encounters Mara while Mara is already:
- working;
- delivering/organizing something;
- caring for a place/person;
- keeping an ordinary appointment;
- handling neighborhood business.

Exact livelihood must be developed/recovered.

## Life packet required before EBCI

MUST RESOLVE:
1. livelihood / income;
2. home/neighborhood;
3. family or chosen-family obligation;
4. one pleasure/hobby;
5. one recurring place not created for anomaly work;
6. why Seraphine already knows or trusts her, if she does;
7. what the notebook is in human terms.

The “resonance-worn notebook” visual phrase should not be literalized automatically.

## Relationship to Seraphine

Recovered function supports familiarity/community trust, but exact relationship depth remains underdefined.

Best architecture:
Mara should not become another surrogate mother unless sources demand it.

She can be:
**a known community adult who notices when Seraphine is carrying too much because she notices everyone.**

That preserves difference from Caro's intimate friend role.

## B01 exit

Mara remains in the neighborhood/community network.

Her end-state change should be small:
ordinary care practices now have to operate under increasingly abnormal pressure.

She does not need to “join” anything.

## Recurrence

Strong if civilian life is solved.
Weak if she remains only “Filament community gravity.”

Therefore:
**do not promote Mara to full B01 recurring execution until Life Packet is approved.**

# 3. ROOTKEEPER

Classification:
**LOCAL / ONE-BOOK TEXTURE.**

## Important taxonomy correction

“Rootkeeper” is also a **Filament functional role** in faction structure.

The B01 Rootkeeper character is a role-named elder/local texture derived from older episode material.

Do not accidentally turn a functional title into:
- a formal rank;
- NOLA Filament commander;
- saga-wide named identity.

## Recovered B01 function

E09 / “Rootkeeper's Glance”:
- older male;
- shop/community-center texture;
- local elder;
- gives Seraphine care;
- reinforces “don't carry it alone”;
- place-bound;
- relational, not prophetic.

Editorial resolution explicitly says:
**Do not merge Rootkeeper into Mara.**

## Why keep him at all?

Because a lived city benefits from people who matter locally and do not become saga cast.

Rootkeeper can demonstrate:
- Seraphine has relationships beyond the recurring ensemble;
- Filament-like care is cultural/community practice rather than a superhero team;
- older people exist without becoming exposition elders;
- a place can contain history without requiring a character arc.

## Naming recommendation

Do not necessarily give him a conventional personal name in B01.

If “Rootkeeper” is only an old outline label and characters would not naturally call him that, prose may present him as:
- a local elder/shopkeeper/community figure;
- potentially unnamed;
- potentially given an ordinary name later if the scene earns it.

This should be decided at EBCI/prose level after faction-title collision is considered.

## Life packet

Enough for a local:
- what place he tends;
- what ordinary service/business/community activity happens there;
- why Seraphine knows the place;
- what he is doing before she arrives;
- one specific non-plot concern.

He does NOT need a saga biography.

## B01 exit

He stays where he belongs.

No recruitment.
No later “return” required.
No administrative ending.

If readers remember the place/person warmly, the character succeeded.

# 4. LACUNA

Classification:
**OPTIONAL NAMED ATMOSPHERIC SEED / CUT IS SAFE.**

## Recovered evidence

Strong historical evidence exists for B01:
- B01 Final Beat Bible explicitly puts Lacuna performing at Velvet Vein;
- current Tier-1 makes Veil peripheral;
- NOLA Black Creole;
- working-poor / gig-driven performance ecology;
- human-only;
- musician / rhythm-cadence social regulation;
- no mystical power;
- later saga importance is real.

Therefore:
Lacuna in B01 is not invented fan service.
It was genuinely developed.

But:
**historical provenance is not structural necessity.**

## Current author concern

B01 may be too early / too crowded.
Later Loom importance could be more effective if Lacuna's eventual emergence is surprising.

Both instincts are compatible with recovered canon.

## Three viable treatments

### A. Named atmospheric seed
She performs at the Vein.
Name appears naturally.
No portent.
No anomaly insight.
No “remember her.”
No relationship setup beyond ordinary familiarity.

Later payoff:
reader realizes this person has been part of the city's life all along.

### B. Unnamed performer
Preserve music/performance function.
Do not spend a name.
Later reveal that she was part of the scene only if useful; do not retcon aggressively.

### C. Remove from B01
Trip/Vein carry the hearth.
Lacuna enters later when she has a relationship/function worth reader attention.

All three preserve the important canon.

## Editorial preference for review

**A or C, not B unless prose naturally wants anonymity.**

Why:
B can feel like artificial mystery if later text says “that was Lacuna all along.”

A works only if she is truly background-normal.
C is cleanest if cast load remains high.

## If A is chosen

Her B01 life is simple and sufficient:
she is at the Vein because she has a gig.

That already passes the no-plot test.

Foreground:
- performance work;
- pay/set logistics;
- drinks;
- flirting;
- work complaints;
- musical opinions;
- local gossip.

Background:
- anomaly.

Forbidden:
- sensing destiny;
- recognizing Resonance;
- stabilizing metaphysical fields;
- mysterious warnings;
- Kade/Tahl foreshadowing;
- camera lingering because she will matter later.

## Trip × Lacuna

Recovered prior canon:
fluid adult intimacy/chemistry existed historically.

Current status:
OPEN for current re-lock.

Do not use B01 to force it.

If both are on-page, old familiarity can be expressed as shorthand without defining relationship status.

## B01 exit

If present:
gig ends; she leaves/goes to next ordinary obligation.

That mundane exit is preferable to tying her to the investigation.

Reader recurrence expectation:
LOW in B01.

This protects the later surprise.
