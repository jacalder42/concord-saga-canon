**Nothing else changes.** *"Drift plus No Reset"* as B02's close is **approved design**
(direction 2), and it stands. Only its *"recovered"* label, used in the saga-lock report, the Veil
brief and the pass-2 draft, is wrong. The Act I and Act II audit titles were spot-checked and match.

## 1. Options

### Option A (recommended as the spine): the Dominion clearance order on a recurring pocket

**The decision:**

- Baz's pocket model says one block is the likely next spike. It is not certain: the model has
  already failed once (pass 2, slot 12).
- **Helena's request** (A3) asks Lucien to report. He **partly obeys**: he passes a **bounded**
  version of the model.
- **Seraphine refuses to hand over the testimonies** people gave her in confidence, so the order is
  drawn **narrower** than it could have been.
- The Dominion issues a **public-safety clearance and restricted-access order** before the spike.
- **The credible alternative:** a Technarc offer of sensor data for Baz's map. It is **declined**.

### Option B: the Filament grounding circles, suspended at the Dominion's request

**The decision:** the liaison asks the Filaments to stop public grounding circles. Baz's
correlation, possibly confounded, links crowds at circles to spikes. Seraphine, with Arianna and
Mara, **suspends the public circles** and moves to private care that people opt into.

- **Beneficiary:** fewer people are exposed at gatherings.
- **Cost:** people who relied on the circles lose that care. It looks like capitulation, and the
  Dominion takes the credit.
- **Into B03:** the Filament restrictions formalize.
- **Strength:** the consent decision is very clean.
- **Weakness:** the institutional help is **only advice**, so it is weaker on B3. It also overlaps
  pass 2's slot 19 grounding-failure beat.
- **Sources:** A2 E08 *"The Quiet Warning"* (Tier D); the Act II audit; Arianna's *"control was
  never the promise"*.

### Option C: a public advisory from Baz's model

**The decision:** Baz, with Elisabet and Shirin, decides to release a **bounded timing and place
advisory** through civic channels: Kesi, Trip or the Vein. Anonymous MT **independently** reaches a
similar reading. People reroute, and businesses close early.

- **Beneficiary:** the people who avoid the block.
- **Cost:** rumor inflation, lost income, and "cried wolf" if the timing slips. The institutions
  brand it "unverified".
- **Into B03:** the institutions begin evidence control and *"information hygiene"*, and B03
  inherits *"a public capable of acting on uncertain information"*.
- **Strength:** the most direct *"act on uncertain evidence"*.
- **Weakness:** it **risks pre-spending B03's coordinates post** and its *"no blame, just
  inference"* chain. There is no real institutional help.
- **Sources:** pass 2, slots 13, 28, 39 and 41; review C02; the channels overlay.

### Option D: care set up in advance, with a registration condition

**The decision:** on the forecast, **Caro and Leila** set up triage and an overnight shelter before
official systems accept the risk. The Dominion offers staffing and supplies **if shelter users are
registered.** Seraphine refuses registration without consent.

- **Beneficiary:** people treated faster.
- **Cost:** diverted resources, and a fight over registration.
- **Into B03:** a registration and screening protocol.
- **Strength:** Caro's practical competence (pass 2, slot 20).
- **Weakness:** it **crowds M54**, Caro's first real handoff in B05, and B03's *"she cannot carry
  everyone"*.
- **Sources:** pass 2, slot 20; Leila's triage (overlay); the Act III *"systems miss"* material
  (Tier D synthesis, §0).

### Option E: a data trade with Technarc

**The decision:** Technarc offers sensor and timing data in exchange for Baz's map and reports.
Baz, Elisabet and Shirin decide whether to trade.

- **Weakness:** a deep B02 partnership **undoes A9**, the surprise of Technarc at the Warehouse.
- **Recommended only as Option A's declined alternative.**
- **Sources:** A1 E08 and E13 (Tier D); the Act I audit; pass 2, slots 08 and 22.

**Not recommended:**

- **Cancelling a civic gathering:** it repeats B01's E40 *"Civic Fractures"*.
- **The Notion *"Filament warning before a Warehouse District fracture"*:** it pre-spends the B03
  Warehouse and breaks *"no new catastrophe"*.

## 2. The recommendation, as one causal paragraph (the direction-2 test)

**Option A, with E as the declined alternative.** Option C's anonymous MT corroboration may stay
secondary and independent, if architecture wants it.

> In B01's wake, Helena Kael's request (A1) asks Lucien to report what he knows. Baz's pocket model
> predicts a spike in one block, a model that has already failed once. Lucien sends a bounded version
> of it to the Dominion. Seraphine refuses to hand over what people told her in confidence. Technarc
> offers better data for Baz's map, and they decline. The Dominion clears and restricts the block
> (A2). When the M06 cluster hits, the cleared residents are safe. One holdout, whose refusal
> Seraphine respected, is not coerced. **The capability is gained:** Baz's model is usefully right.
> **The commitment holds:** Seraphine's consent line. **The costs are real:** the Filaments lose
> access, the Dominion owns Baz's method, and Lucien's partial obedience strains the group. Drift
> and No Reset close the book. **B03 starts with a standing restricted-zone protocol, a Dominion
> used to acting on Baz's kind of evidence** (so its Warehouse perimeter is *expected*), **Lucien
> having to decide whether he will be used again** (his B03 refusal), **and Technarc outside the
> circle** (so its Warehouse presence is a surprise).

**The C02 pass test:** without the choice, B03 has no protocol, no expected perimeter, no refusal to
make, and no reason for Technarc to be surprising. **B03 would start differently. The test passes.**
