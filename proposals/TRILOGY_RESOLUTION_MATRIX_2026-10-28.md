## 2. The trilogy contract (proposed rules)
For every trilogy:
1. **Its own question.** Asked in its first book, escalated in the second, and answered in the third. The answer may be "no", but it must be an answer.
2. **An entry point.** A reader who has never read the earlier trilogy can start at its first book (B01, B04, B07). That book re-delivers on its own page everything its climax depends on.
3. **A partial payoff for every setup.** A thread may continue into the next trilogy, but each setup gets a payoff the reader can feel *inside* its trilogy. A setup with no payoff before a later trilogy is a **million-word dependency** and must be fixed or cut.
4. **Its own discovery ladder.** What characters learn about the phenomenon, the antagonists and themselves rises across the three books and never resets. Each trilogy's ceiling is the next one's floor.
5. **A trilogy ending that lands.** Book 3 resolves the lead's arc and the trilogy's antagonist face. Only the saga's reserved reveals (the cap, Mira, Silence and Hope, the cycle, the breathing Veil, the Wound chain and its site, Line 8) may stay unanswered without counting as a defect.

## 5. Million-word dependencies and the fix for each
Each fix is the smallest partial payoff inside the trilogy.

**Veil**
1. **The funder is Dominion** (§617: "a B03 payoff"). The split leans to B04, and M10 has the cast learn of Baz's death only in B04. *Fix:* the **reader** learns it in B03 (Baz discovers it, or the page shows it before he dies); the **cast** learns it in B04.
2. **Caldas has no Veil endpoint** (lethal intent ruled; no B03 beat). *Fix:* B03 gives Caldas's pursuit an outcome. He may survive into later books, but his Veil arc must close.
3. **Nyla as converter** (the B01 Epilogue light). *Fix:* build the ruled B02 capture and escape on the E44 pulse. That is Veil's partial payoff.
4. **The seated crowd changed the event** (§619). B02 and B03 never return to it. *Fix:* one follow-up in each book. It can be shown and argued, never confirmed (X1).

Also smaller: **Elisabet's Vienna outcome** (B02 E05). **The watchers' frame** stays non-interrogative, so Veil never promises to answer it.

**Neon**
1. **B06 E47: Seraphine recognises "the first one" as the swamp.** It depends on the B01 camp. *Fix:* Neon re-plants the camp and its ache on its own page (B04 or B05), not only as memory.
2. **The VT brush memory** (B05 E34, B06 E42) depends on B03. *Fix:* show the B03 brush again in Neon.
3. **The Concord co-option and the faction split** (ruled 10-18) are absent from all Neon architecture. *Fix:* stage the co-option in Neon, with the split as its cause, and give it a Neon-level turn.
4. **Hope–Caro, Lucien–Silence and the elders' corridor** pay off only at the B09 Mending. *Fix:* one Neon-level turn each (a choice, cost or discovery), not only presence.
5. **The Santa Fe Rupture stays open into B08.** This is acceptable **only if** Neon's dramatic question is answered "no" (§3); then the open wound *is* the answer.

**Loom**
1. **M53: Kade recognises Tahl's triangle** in the last scene. It is the only ruled payoff that is purely callback. *Fix:* show the triangle on MT, and Kade knowing it, at least once in B07–B08.
2. **Why the swamp matters:** the first tear, and Dré's death at the camp. Loom never says on the page what happened there. *Fix:* a Loom beat that gives the reader the camp's significance, within the FIRST_TEAR guard and "she never learns her part".
3. **The Choirless** are Loom's named pressure, with zero mentions in B07–B08. *Fix:* introduce them, and their pressure, in B07–B08.

Also: **Line 8** has no stage, and **Nyla has no Loom role** (see §7, D6). **The Dominion co-opter's collapse** and **Ren Bellande's Loom payoff** are placed nowhere.
