**Date:** 2026-09-23

**Production target:** [B01 v4.1b integrated architecture](B01_REVISED_BEAT_BIBLE_V4_1B_INTEGRATED_2026-09-22.md). No order, scene, cast or rule-file changes. No EBCI expansion.

## Packet architecture decision

Use **one shared world-systems baseline** for rules that remain true across eras, plus **three compact trilogy overlays** for changes in availability, institutions and behavior. This file is the **Veil/B01 pilot** for that structure. Its cross-era notes are navigation only; they are not Neon/Loom packets or approvals.

| Layer | Contents | Handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared baseline | Material cause and cost, human institutions, separate failures of grid/network/device, channels, ordinary care, access/class, provenance tags. | Distill after this pilot is reviewed; do not copy the same law into three era bibles. |
| Veil overlay | B01 normal operations and first local disturbances, low-amplitude anomaly evidence, class/work/venue/care continuity. | **This packet.** Only scene-facing facts already needed by B01. |
| Neon overlay | Patchy services, faction equipment, response and trade as breakdown spreads. | Later recovery/reconciliation; no premature B01 leakage. |
| Loom overlay | Rationing, transport/shelters, medicine, power and public legitimacy under collapse. | Later recovery/reconciliation. |

**Source labels:** CURRENT = later author/rule/production constraint; RECOVERED = historical source evidence; PROPOSAL = bounded implementation inference; OPEN = decision/source gap; SUPERSEDED = retired branch. A source can establish a **question** without providing an answer.

## 6. Trilogy overlay boundaries

The **common baseline** will keep: physical resource costs, evidence provenance, separate grid/network/device failure, human institutions and labor, limits of regulation, no tech-carried VT/LT, and non-equivalence of money, resonance load, power supply and reader pressure.

The **Veil overlay** states normal operations and the rare documented deviation. The future **Neon overlay** should inherit these services and specify which fail, who maintains alternatives, whose access worsens and what factions exploit. The future **Loom overlay** should inherit the same service categories and specify rationing, repair, shelter authority, travel and unequal recovery. Post-Mending deserves a separate later settlement note, not a fourth pre-EBCI trilogy overlay.

**Current gate:** this packet is ready for author/editor review as a B01 lived-world working surface. It does not resolve the objective mechanics of B01 pulses, approve a career detail left OPEN, authorize a global reveal or release EBCI.
