## 4. The prediction (acceptance tests 2 and 3)

- **What Lucien predicts (Ch 16–17, window Mon 6–Wed 15 Apr):** events fall inside a **band about the Square and along the river side** (H6: a source and
  decreasing distance), and the next will be **larger than the last**. The claim is about **physical effects** (cups, glass tickets, bob, loggers), **not witness
  reports**. Seraphine's warnings change reporting, so reporting is not the test. Baz's "watch, don't tell" block on Ursulines is a non-reporting control.
- **The miss (Ch 21, 24, 26–27):** Ch 21's repast ring falls outside the band yet inside the window, and Ch 24's Ursulines event falls outside the band too. H6 is dead:
  **distance from the Square is not the rule.** The surviving pattern: it returns to places it has already gathered, and it gathers where people carry something
  together. This yields the first forecast that works (Ch 34 and Ch 39), and the sitting intervention, without the Breathable Veil or the cap.
- **No unreported-equals-absent error:** a quiet block is quiet only if the detectors on it are quiet.

## 5. Baz: the risk, the wrong choice, the cost

- **Concrete risk:** Dominion already holds a file naming Baz; anyone who knows what he knows becomes a liability to the people who sent Lucien (BazID). Caldas
  has surveilled Ledet and Lucien's floor (Ch 19). A partial warning given to Seraphine or Lucien can be traced back to him.
- **Partial warnings he does give:** the sideways line to Seraphine (do not say who felt it first), the "write less" to Lucien. Both are misread.
- **The wrong choice (Ch 24):** he keeps the Ursulines block untold as the control, to protect his cover and the experiment's cleanliness. Mrs. Arceneaux breaks her hip.
- **What it costs him (Ch 24–30):** the one thing he wanted to keep, Lucien's trust and Lucien's distance from Dominion. Lucien learns Baz knew, and walks out at Ch 30.
