## 4. Candidate route geometry — not adopted

**REC: four strands, two convergences.** From the B07 break, the forward wound strand carries Seraphine/Lucien and selected investigators through SF and MC to SM. Caro participates at SF and MC, then can take a separate care/logistics task before rejoining for the final approach. Rex visits SF for Tahl, then pursues evidence/accountability; Elisabet owns the distributed repair/evidence network. Kade and Elias act through the public/splinter circuit, while Lacuna builds her independent NOLA capacity. These are *functions*, not mandatory passenger lists.

**REC: first convergence at SM is selective.** Only people required for the visit and escape need be there. Keep Lacuna/Kade split, local work and Rex/Elisabet's other obligations active. Do not put an antagonist at HI simply to witness the opening. Let a local observer or ordinary record reach Elias if a public claim is used. That keeps the B08 event order genuinely open and preserves the site secret.

**REC: second convergence begins with the B09 A2 feint and ends in A3.** The forward group approaches Louisiana through dispersed ordinary routes while Lacuna makes NOLA worth searching. Rex/Elisabet occupy a distinct rear/approach position; Kade/Elias and other factions can be in the wider Louisiana/NOLA search space without knowing HI. The A3 site reveal then sends some of them toward the true approach. Choose a plausible regional distance and enough elapsed time for the confrontation, Elisabet's crossing and the Mending. This does **not** choose the exact reveal, route, time of day or first perimeter breach.

**REC: Elisabet's final warning is an action, not a convenient transmission failure.** The current recollection says she leaves Rex to warn those at the Mending about Brightbreak and others closing in. A candidate is that a direct message would expose the concealed forward route or be too unreliable to confirm receipt under pursuit, while a physically passable protected route remains. Neither reason is established. The episode/event design must show the actual constraint and the short, paid-for journey. Her final moment with Caro is then earned before Caro joins the Mending.

**REC: the B08 close remains one of two honest clocks.** (A) SM, opening and Elias's claim occur in one interval, with the claim carried from a credible local observer; (B) the opening/claim is staggered around the SM visit. Both must preserve the SM-to-B09 escape continuity and B09 A3 site secrecy. Do not select A or B until book/act/episode milestones resolve the crowding. Neither clock makes Kade's physical location at the opening a fact.

Put direct witness and RR in different cells. Use the POV bands only after scene ownership is identified; a location does not earn a POV quota.
