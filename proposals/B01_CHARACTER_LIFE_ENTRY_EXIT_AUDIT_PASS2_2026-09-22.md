## B01 design consequence

Caro should appear early as evidence of Seraphine's already-existing life. She should not receive a conventional “new character entrance.”

Best entry principle:

Useful recurring comedy should derive from:
- Caro recognizing Seraphine's over-care before Seraphine does;
- established shorthand;
- Caro moving while Seraphine is still trying to emotionally hold everything;
- Caro's humor becoming diagnostic: when she stops joking, the scene is genuinely bad.

Do not make sarcasm her sole mode. Recovered grammar also requires competence, direct care and kinetic warmth.

Good friction domains:

- what constitutes enough evidence;
- whether anomalies are environmental/systemic vs isolated;
- how much uncertainty can be acted upon;
- professional territoriality;
- Lucien and Elisabet reaching similar questions through different analytic architectures.

- Lucien interests her because his rigor is recognizable but not identical;
- Caro first observes an independently compelling person before C/E becomes personally charged.

The fractured-friendship backstory gives Elisabet a mundane emotional vulnerability that predates Caro. Do not use Caro as the first person who teaches Elisabet she has feelings.
