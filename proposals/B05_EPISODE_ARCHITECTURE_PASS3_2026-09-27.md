## 0. Identity

**B05 = SEPARATE WORK: DIFFERENT SYSTEMS FAIL DIFFERENTLY.** The most deliberately polyphonic book.
The speed comes from changing city, institution, problem and competence, **not from keeping every
scene at crisis pitch.** People choose separate, necessary work, and the book tests whether
commitment survives distance.

**The peak is Act II** (Chicago, M54). **Act III is not a second set-piece**: it is convergence of
evidence, the reconnection, and Santa Fe becoming credible, by design (the 09-27 pressure
recalibration).

**The Colorstorm** runs through the book as NOLA's condition: **apparition (A1) → normalisation (A2)
→ changed meaning (A3)**. It causes nothing elsewhere and gates nothing (ruled). **D5 card owed.**

**Scale:** 45 episodes (15/15/15), 4 LR units, 3 supplements.
**POV:** Tahl 13 · Seraphine 10 · Caro 7 · Elisabet 5 · Rex 4 · Lucien 2 · Kade 2 · flex 2.
