## Proposed reconciliation architecture

A coherent human-only history can preserve the useful parts of both layers:

1. Baz grows up in Marseille in the current canon family/class ecology.
2. His education/documentation/translation/investigative path eventually takes him to Vienna.
3. Through a lower-status archival/research/documentation role in the Dominion ecosystem, he works with Lucien.
4. Their working relationship becomes closest-friend / near-brother intimacy.
5. Baz is the person who draws Lucien outside institutional Vienna into cafés, streetcars, food, sketching and ordinary city life.
6. Before B01, Baz leaves that immediate role for a different Dominion-aligned civic entity.
7. The new role is more public/civic-facing and better fits his truth-witness trajectory.
8. Lucien remains in the more formal structural/research apparatus.
9. They remain personally close and professionally useful to one another.
10. Lucien is therefore assigned to NOLA without Baz.
11. When Lucien needs help, calling Baz is a personal choice, not an automatic staffing escalation.

Items 1–5 are largely reconciliation of recovered/current material.
Items 6–11 are AUTHOR-DEVELOPMENT PROPOSAL unless separately recovered.

## Why Baz should probably have left the immediate Lucien/Dominion role

Narrative advantages:
- explains why he is not sent with Lucien from page one;
- makes “Baz, we need you” a character decision;
- preserves Baz's independent identity;
- lets Baz bring a different institutional/civic perspective;
- makes Lucien's trust visible through action;
- gives Virelli reason to view Baz as outside clean lines of authority;
- allows Baz to know Dominion without being owned by it;
- supports later truth-telling against institutional concealment;
- preserves class/status contrast.

Avoid:
Baz leaving because he already knows Dominion is evil. That would make Lucien slow rather than conflicted and flatten Baz's later discovery arc.

Better:
Baz prefers work closer to lived civic consequences, documentation, witnesses and public truth.
