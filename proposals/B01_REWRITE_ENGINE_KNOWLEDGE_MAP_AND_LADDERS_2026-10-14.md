## 1. The premise in one paragraph

Before page one, **Dominion has an undefined force and wants to harness it**; Lucien is its field scientist on the *events*;
Baz is its former hand who knows the *aim* and left. Seraphine's job is the dead boy and the paper; her cost is the folder
she did not carry in time. The solved mystery of B01 is **what the events are and what they are not**: a load that gathers
where many people carry a lot together, in places the Veil has thinned, that answers to *people being together* and not to
instruments or to any one person. Dominion's model (which Baz carries) is that the force can be harnessed through those who
feel it first, or through instruments that read it. **The crew shows both are wrong**, at a cost, and what is left is
Dominion's reason to hunt the one man who knows how much they know.

## 2. The central error (the mystery's spine)

| Dominion's model (Baz holds it) | What the ground shows (the crew finds it) | Where it is won |
|---|---|---|
| Some people are **receivers**; find the first who feel it and you find the source and the lever | The first to feel it are not special. Nobody is "first"; the camp is a place, not a person. Telling people changes nothing; sitting together lowers harm | disproof scenes (Ch 12, 21, 24, 27, 37, 39 equivalents) |
| **Instruments** can read it; quantify and you can control | Instruments read zero while bodies and glass and water show it; a plumb bob holds while eyes fail | Lucien's Ch 4 failure, the dish/coffee surfaces, the new-plate crack |
| It can be **harnessed** by owning the receivers or the data | Whoever holds the map holds less than they think; the thing is not a stock of power but a load shared among people | the Square, the sitting crowd, the gallery |
| Askers (people) and boxes (instruments) are one program | Two parties, different aims; the box side sees people as "human variance, a defect" | cream shoes vs Caldas, stakeout, records email |

This keeps the hard question *why it thins* reserved while giving each discovery a different relation to what Baz already
believes: **confirms** (he flinches), **contradicts** (he was acting on a bad map), or **goes beyond** (nobody expected it).

## 4. Five ladders

### 4a. Mystery (discoveries; each a different kind of scene)
Each rung teaches one new thing and is **won by action**. Type: W = won on the ground, C = confirms Baz's model, X = contradicts it, B = beyond it.

1. It is a place with an edge (the 40–60 steps), weight with no direction. *(W)*
2. It works on bodies, not meters (Lucien's failure). *(W, X)*
3. It travels and it is quiet; some people know and do not say. *(W)*
4. It is simultaneous and shared (two phones, the same minute). *(W, C)*
5. A hum comes before it. *(W, B)*
6. It is not the pumps, the ground, or old glass. *(W)*
7. The opposition's question is *who first*, and the answer is *nobody*. *(X)*
8. Telling does not change it; sitting together reduces harm. *(W, X)*
9. It follows people being together: repasts, laughter, a crowd. *(W, B)*
10. A second party sees it only as numbers; instruments cannot see it. *(W, X)*
11. Water and thin surfaces show it first; the camp holds. *(W, B)*
12. It can be forecast by the pattern, not the person. *(W)*
13. It is getting thinner, and faster. *(W, B)*
14. Why people matter: it answers to people together, not to one person. *(W, B)*

Replaces the stolen sheet as the answer key: the sheet becomes **what Baz carried out of Dominion**, and its lines are tested
by the crew rather than confirmed by them (Q-EN3).

### 4b. Opposition (what they do, not what they say)
Askers (Caldas) and boxes (instruments) are different parties and escalate on different ladders.
- **Askers:** observe → summon a witness → interview with a planted question → threaten a third party → buy a hold (oxygen) → hurt someone (Ledet's wrist) → seize (Baz at the Square) → fail in public → retreat to paper. Each step needs a **new kind** of act and leaves a cost.
- **Boxes:** a case on a mark → a series → steal a case and a phone → cream shoes at the gate → "human variance" → leave. Give the box side **one identifying fact earned by the crew** (a plate, a tag, an owner) and settle the zip-tie tail.
- Backstory is delivered by **Baz**: the Vienna table, the neighborhood that was not warned, why Caldas is at his door.

### 4c. Romance (each beat changes the terms)
Lucien's concealment is about **who gets the answers**, while his aim matches hers. His secret (what he knows, whom he writes to) is the pressure under every beat.

1. **First meeting:** a mutual lie at the coroner's (hers, his); neither exposed. *Terms: strangers who each hide something.*
2. **A shared night:** a corroboration scene; he keeps something back. *Terms: allies, one-sided.*
3. **The quarrel over her notebook:** she refuses to be "a post." *Terms: she sets the rules.*
4. **The waltz:** attraction acknowledged; he is told "next time I lead." *Terms: admitted.*
5. **The cover kiss:** real by accident; he is caught hiding. *Terms: trust broken at the moment of closeness.*
6. **The cost:** his name on a confession, her two steps below. *Terms: he pays.*
7. **The unsaid:** Lucien's C-3 and the promise ("Saturday") that is broken honestly, not by the plot. *Terms: truth, door closed.*
Static spans in braid 4 (Ch 23–29, 35–37) each get a beat that **changes who holds what**.

### 4d. Action (six distinct shapes)
1. CPR in the stilt house (hands, time, no machine). 2. The Square falls (a crowd that goes down, a mule that does not).
3. A night at the pumps (trespass, a person not yet trusted). 4. The Ursulines hip (a telephone line, a dispatcher, a call that fails
then works). 5. The gate crush (people as the hazard). 6. The Saturday Square (a crowd that sits when told, a man seized, a
gallery that stands). Add **the camp at night** before the climax (Q-KN8), the one scene where the ground is the antagonist.
Each action scene has a different driver, different weakness, a cost that carries over.

### 4e. Confessions (capital C; at most three)
- **C-1 (late, Seraphine to Renée):** the desk and the folder; made in person at the cost of being written into the file. *(kept from braid 4: Ch 41)*
- **C-2 (midpoint, Baz to the crew):** the aim and the sheet, forced by Caldas hurting someone, told badly, incomplete.
- **C-3 (Act III, Lucien to Seraphine):** what his notes do and what he wrote on the form; arrives after she finds it herself.
Everything else is partial, indirect, taken or deed (see the proposal of this date §5).
