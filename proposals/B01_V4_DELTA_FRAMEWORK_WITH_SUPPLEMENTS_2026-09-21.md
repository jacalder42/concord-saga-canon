## 1. Key correction: B1 has three narrative scales

The previous Life/Reward pass treated the choice too often as:
1. full narrative episode, or
2. texture embedded inside a narrative episode.

Recovered architecture establishes a third scale:

3. **supplemental interstitial — 150–600 words between episodes.**

Therefore B1 should be designed as:

### Layer A — causal narrative spine
36 v3 narrative episodes.

### Layer B — standalone narrative breathers
Long enough to need scene embodiment, POV continuity, relationship behavior, or meaningful dramatic change.

### Layer C — supplemental vehicles
Short artifacts/interstitials delivering FUN, SLICE, RELEASE, PING, TENSION or LINK without pretending every reward is a conventional scene.

This is closer to original Concord architecture.

## 2. What supplements are for

Recovered supplement types:
- LORE — historic/background;
- POL — political/off-page information;
- FUN — humor/enjoyable story during heavy stretch;
- SLICE — life progressing outside primary narrative.

Recovered functions:
- PING — reader recognition/connection;
- TENSION — world tightening;
- RELEASE — emotional payoff;
- LINK — off-page plot linkage.

Recovered B1 intensity:
**LIGHT.**

Hard constraints:
- 150–600 words;
- between episodes;
- never replace narrative;
- never break POV;
- never spoil future beats;
- never directly explain metaphysics;
- never introduce mechanics;
- never feel like optional bonus content.

## 3. Vehicle menu relevant to B1

### VEIN
Intimate, local, queer-coded, musical.
Role: NOLA social pulse, culture, flashbacks, breathers.

Trip-associated bridge rule:
conversations overheard at the bar; reader is another presence in room; Trip never directly addresses reader.

### VELVET
Velvet Vein Menu / Cocktail Book.
Role: culture, joy, sensory worldbuilding.

### RITUAL
Recipes / music / ritual inserts.
Role: multicultural grounding and identity.
Use carefully in B1: cultural practice, not magical instruction.

### CHRON
Chronicle.
Investigative/factual civic counterpoint.
B1 use should be light and must not create institutional exposition overload.

### FIELD
Field Notes.
Sparse/clinical/eerie.
B1 use should be rare because the primary narrative already contains substantial investigation.

### MT
Do **not** restore old B1 MT #1–3 merely because the historical Act-I supplement list contains them.

Current author chronology moved anonymous MT first visibility to approximately B2.
The old B1 MT deployment is superseded unless later author review reverses that ruling.

### VT
Not appropriate as ordinary B1 texture.
Hard-capped saga-wide and reserved for specific milestone windows.

## 8. Reader-reward architecture after supplement restoration

The full B1 reader experience now has three tempos:

### Long pulse — causal episodes
Mystery, escalation, major state changes.

### Medium pulse — Life/Reward narrative episodes
Core character, friendship, romance, wonder, recovery, pleasure.

### Short pulse — supplements
Culture, humor, social temperature, outside-life progress, artifact texture, public reaction.

This creates the desired serial feeling without bloating causal plot.

## 9. Cast protection through supplements

Supplements are especially useful for making the world feel populated without creating cast obligations.

Rules:
- unnamed speakers are preferred;
- a name appearing in an artifact does not require character promotion;
- Trip can be present as host without becoming POV exposition;
- Lacuna can be heard/seen/performance-listed without a biography package;
- civic voices can remain titles/roles or anonymous;
- do not use supplements to introduce future major cast early merely for breadcrumbing.
