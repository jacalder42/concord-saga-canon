Seraphine's own line is the people: the calls, the third column, the theory, the triage. **The physical evidence comes
from the men.** Her one theory is the wrong one, and she retracts it at E46. That is right for her arc. But it leaves her
without a single move of method that works.

**The approved Act II obligation** is that *"Seraphine initiates a consequential line of inquiry and retains ownership of
it"* (the revised Act II obligation in `decisions/REDRAFT_ACT_I_CHECKPOINT_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-29.md`). It is met by the calls. The editor's note
asks for one move of method beyond them.

## 2. Recommended: the glass tickets start with her (option A)

**The idea is a caseworker's.** People who will never call the city will still call the man who fixes the window, and they
will pay him. A repair bill is a record that nobody had to tell. It is how she already proves hardship for clients, from
their receipts.

**Draft 3 already sets it up.**
- At E34 Lanette catches her on the block. Lanette's landlord means to pull out the old glass, and Lanette wants to be
  told it won't come through her windows. Seraphine refuses the lie.
- At E36 the landlords' old-glass idea is what Baz tests, and what dies for him in the shop.

**What changes, chapter by chapter.**

| Ch. | Change | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **E34** (her POV) | After Lanette, or with Inez's pad, Seraphine has the idea: the glazier's spike is the record nobody reported. She cannot go herself: it is her caseload and her block, and Monday comes. **She asks Baz**, by a call that evening, and **she sets the terms**: the block and the time, copied by hand, no names and no addresses | about 150–250 words |
| **E36** (Baz's POV) | Baz is at Mr. Lacoste's because she sent him. The terms he offers the glazier are hers, and he knows it. **What is still his:** the morning at the counter, the crack in the new sheet, the death of the old-glass idea, taking the tickets to people nobody warned, and his bet that **it is coming faster**. One beat at the close: he sends her the copied sheets that night | about +60 words net |
| **E37** (her POV) | She reads Baz's sheets before work on Monday. One short paragraph. *Faster* is his bet, and the narrator does not confirm it. **For her it changes a decision:** she stops waiting for the next page. Whatever she does to bring people together, she will do on an ordinary day, this week | about 100–150 words |
| **E41** (her POV) | One clause. The Kerlerec night and Baz's *faster* together feed the decision she makes here (A3 join 3 already carries Kerlerec). No new argument | a clause |

**The cost is about 350–500 words**, before Q-D3-3 and Q-D3-4 take theirs.

**What it buys:**
- Her **one method that works** in the book, against a theory that fails. It is drawn from her job and from Lanette, not
  borrowed from the men.
- **Her decision changes because of what she learned.** This answers the Seraphine row of
  `decisions/REDRAFT_ACT_II_CHECKPOINT_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-29.md`: her discoveries must change a decision she makes. Here the change is the timing of the
  gathering, which E45 then stages from Caro's printout.
- **A quiet edge, if the reviser wants it (not required).** At Mr. Lacoste's, the man with the badge took only the times.
  Her terms also take only the times, and nobody's name. Baz may notice that the two requests are the same shape, and
  not like it. Nothing is concluded.

**Guards:**
- *Faster* stays Baz's bet. Nothing on the page confirms it, and no mechanism is named.
- The crack stays seen, not explained.
- She does not go to the shop and does not do Baz's legwork. **Her move is the idea and the terms.** The finding stays
  his.
- Baz's dissent and his E36 disagreement with Lucien about the false report are untouched.
- The man with the badge stays unattributed.
- No new character, location or event.

## 3. The alternatives

**B. She notices the hold sheet (E37).** At the depot, the hold sheet lists blocks in the same order as her own log. In
draft 3 nobody remarks on it. She could notice it and keep the suspicion to herself; it would then sharpen her refusal at
E48, when the woman offers the holds.
- **For:** it is in her own chapter, costs nobody else's turn, and pays off at E48.
- **Against:**
  - it falls in Act III, not mid–Act II;
  - with Q-D3-2's face at E20, it makes "upstairs" legible faster than the B-lite design intends (Q-EN);
  - the change list kept the connection for the reader, uncertified.

**Not recommended as the move.** It could be added later as one beat, if the author wants the E48 refusal to be informed.

**C. The ring is hers (E29).** She already walks the edges. She could be the one who says that it holds still.
- **Against:** this is a credit, not a move. The editor's note would stand.

**Not recommended.**
