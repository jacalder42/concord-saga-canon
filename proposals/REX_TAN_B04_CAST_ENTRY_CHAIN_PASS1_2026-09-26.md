## Unique ensemble function

Rex names material capacity: what carries the load, what fails first, who is in range, how much redundancy exists, and when a physical plan must stop. His safety halt forces a real choice inside the others' established roles.

| Character | Boundary with Rex |
| --- | --- |
| Seraphine | She owns emotional/coherence stakes and her own agency. Rex identifies a finite route, support system or body cost; he cannot stabilize her field. |
| Caro | She owns human care and triage. Rex gives a physical capacity or safe sequence; he does not direct care decisions. |
| Elisabet | She compares wider observations and uncertainty. Rex tests whether a particular local intervention is physically workable. |
| Lucien | His moral restraint and relationship with Seraphine remain distinct. Rex's protective action is a bounded safety act, never replacement romance. |
| Tahl | Rex supplies observations and limits; Tahl owns publication, correction and public trust. |

The old sheet's “Rational Thread” works as Rex's **method**, not a monopoly on reason. The author's earlier unrequited-tension material can be a slight undertone if useful; it cannot explain why he stays or displace the central relationships.

## Seeds without premature payoff

1. **Protocol 9:** Show the difference between a justified local protective limit and a broad classification that traps people or discounts their accounts. Han Wei and Protocol 9 are named, but jurisdiction, practice and Rex's access remain to be designed. Rex does not secretly invent or control the policy.
2. **Manufactured-meta ethics:** Specify who can consent, leave, testify and recover. B04 can contain a public meta incident without being the first public meta event or proving an operative-boost transfer. The battery/interface mechanism remains unresolved. Let the human testimony and bounded technical records become evidence, not a complete explanation.
3. **B06 Singapore:** An ordinary contact or contested record can connect Rex's NOLA witness chain with Kasumi's victim evidence, Shun's internal resistance, operational testimony and Han's control response. His earlier training gives him credible questions, not command access. By B06 he goes to Singapore for a concrete technical or witness obligation and carries M17's bounded local loss of Technarc authority. He learns of Tahl's death remotely.
4. **B05 remains open:** Continued NOLA work, an Atlanta leg, return to Detroit, and the timing of Singapore contacts require later act-level causality. None is locked here.
5. **Arc pressure:** Rex starts with the belief that a sufficient margin can prevent catastrophe. B04 shows a margin can guide protection even after a system has failed. Show exhaustion, an incomplete rescue, or a wrong human assumption; do not grant a miracle fix.
