**Date:** 2026-09-27 (UTC repository date)  
**Status:** EDITORIAL PROPOSAL / NON-CANONICAL. A qualitative re-reading of the 27-act rhythm against current cast routes and the B07 scene/POV answers. It changes no grid pressure value, book context, act overlay, rule, episode or status. It does not release the B02/B03 episode architecture hold.

## 1. What is being recalibrated

The 2026-09-26 rhythm reports already distinguish **W** (world/systemic pressure), **R** (reader/active experience) and **C** (capacity for ordinary life, humor, intimacy, wonder and reflection). Their 1–5 bands are **diagnostics, not canon or production targets**. This pass does not invent finer numbers. It asks which physical theater and character owns each increase, which places are recovering or waiting, how information crosses between them, and what pressure the reader actually experiences.

The prior 27-act matrix remains useful as a broad waveform. Its theaters need four current corrections:

1. **B05 A1–A2:** Rex and Tahl are in NOLA together at the B05 A1 meeting; Tahl posts the Last Clear Act there. Lucien is still in Vienna and joins the channel remotely; he returns as Rex leaves around A2. Caro moves to Chicago by A2. Singapore belongs to Rex's later transit and B06 intervention, not an assumed B05 A1 lived scene.
2. **B06:** the Fracture closes A1, the Rupture opens A2 and persists, and Tahl goes after it opens. His fatal contact comes **at the end of A3**. Seraphine/Lucien/Kade/Lacuna experience this from NOLA, Caro returns there late, Elisabet is in Reykjavík, Rex in Singapore, and Elias is not in NOLA during B06. Keep catastrophe, communication lag, and grief at different intensities.
3. **B07:** NOLA plus a westward Atchafalaya departure road are the planned lived settings (approved design), with a brief outside scene still possible if earned. Santa Fe, Singapore, Reykjavík, Chicago and a St. Louis Filament elder form a selective **receipt pool**, not a required five-city montage. Uptown is only a provisional lean for the civic break. No Elias POV; soft POV emphasis belongs to Kade, Seraphine, Lacuna and Caro.
4. **B08–B09:** the four-wound chain and selective travel are protected. Seraphine/Lucien attend the wounds; Caro joins Santa Fe and Mound City; Rex mourns at Santa Fe; Elisabet owns tasks in a US repair network; Lacuna develops NOLA/Gulf capacity; Kade/Elias work a separate public/splinter theater (city open). The B08 close and B09 final shared clock remain deferred. The factions infer Louisiana before B09 A3, **not** the Honey Island Mending site.

## 4. Four editorial tests before any score changes

1. **Asynchronous theater test.** For each major act, identify one active crisis/choice and at least one differently pressured theater. A remote receipt has an identifiable source, recipient, lag and consequence. It never stands in for a lived second plot by naming a city.
2. **Ownership and causal limits.** Dominion, Technarc, Choirless and Brightbreak apply different pressure. Do not import a manufactured-meta transfer mechanism, VT warning origin, global service collapse or MT compass from geography alone. D5 event physics and the relevant canon decisions govern.
3. **Human life test.** Each book earns ordinary continuity and at least one moment whose main job is why this world is worth saving. B07 funeral and B09 A2 are especially protected. A scene of care/competence can carry pressure and relief simultaneously; do not prescribe a humor quota.
4. **Act exit test.** B02 ends with credible distributed cause **[corrected 2026-09-27: drift plus No Reset; see Corrections below]**, B03 with Baz's loss, B04 with civic fracture, B05 with credible SF danger, B06 with Tahl loss and dispersed receipt, B07 with chosen departure, B08 with an incomplete escape and rival Louisiana interpretation, B09 with the hard-cap/breathing-veil choice. A later book must not spend an earlier book's protected uncertainty or a later book's reserved reveal.
