```text
WORLD ORDER / GOVERNANCE & LEAKS (Track-1)

ICEBERG GOVERNANCE:
- Layer 1 (on-page): local institutions + few named blocs touching plot.

- MG mixed credibility; clampdowns/curfews in some zones
- DG surfaces in pockets, fractures elsewhere
- TA visible in hotspots; legitimacy erodes on tech failure
- FO thickens into corridors/Knots/MT mutual aid
- CC emerges as route-truth & fairness federation
Loom:
- FO/Knots + convoys dominate local order
- CC stabilizer spine between nodes
- MG/DG/TA remnants patchy

MG RESPONSE MODES (behavior > ideology):
Denial-stability; Grid-control; Adaptive coalition; Fracture/fail states.

PERIMETER GOVERNANCE RHYTHM:
- Veil: 1 glimpse every 2–3 chapters
- Neon: 1 per chapter/act turn
- Loom: 1 per movement/travel segment
Forms: official bulletin, Chronicle insert, MT clip, secondary POV anecdote, checkpoint residue, background broadcast.
Rule: one scope signal per glimpse unless crescendo.

LEAK TAXONOMY:
1) Existential leaks (lived experience contradicts frame)
2) Data leaks (docs/footage/labs)
3) Narrative leaks (folk coherence/MT)
4) Structural leaks (undeniable Veil/VT phenomena)

SERAPHINE AS FIRST LEAK-THREAT:
She leaks by existing: perception + compassion contradict official frames.
Early leaks are tiny/deniable/personal, not public prophecy.
Consequences carried socially/medially by others.
Operating line: “Seraphine is the first leak because the Veil can’t keep her quiet.”

COMMS CRACKDOWN → MT EMERGENCE:
Veil late: soft censorship + framing.
Neon: hard blackouts/overrides.
MT forms as folk survival media; politics accrete later.
Dominions rule “the story the world tells itself,” not necessarily overt states.
```

## 6. Mundane-government response modes

From the block (*"behavior > ideology"*), with the recovery's reading (AS, G3). Mundane governments are in place,
competent and resonance-blind; their normal frameworks fail in public. **Two cities can be in different modes at
once, and a city can change mode.**

| Mode | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **Denial-stability** | The official frame holds: events are faults, weather, *"mass hysteria"* (`rules/Resonance-v1.md` §20). Services run; warnings are filed |
| **Grid-control** | Order by control of movement and information: orders, zones, checkpoints, curfews, communications throttling |
| **Adaptive coalition** | Officials work with whoever is competent (responders, Filaments, Concord-like networks) and say so |
| **Fracture / fail state** | Authority splits or withdraws; services stop; local order passes to folk order |

Per-theatre use for the Neon → Loom transition is recorded on M24 (Q-GV8; §8 below).

  Reykjavík late). It matches M24 (*"degrade unevenly across regions; some persist"*).

  epilogue and early B07**. Santa Fe does not physically cause worldwide degradation (M24's note stands); ruled M20,

- **In Loom** the remnants of mundane governments, the Dominions and Technarc are patchy; folk order and convoys
  carry local order (the block's Loom lines, read inside `Concord-Limits.md`).

## 13. The leak taxonomy

From the block (AS; G5):

1. **Existential leaks**: lived experience contradicts the official frame.
2. **Data leaks**: documents, footage, labs.
3. **Narrative leaks**: folk coherence, MT.
4. **Structural leaks**: undeniable Veil or VT phenomena.

- **Seraphine is the first leak-threat**: she leaks *by existing*; early leaks are tiny, deniable and personal, and
  others carry the public consequences. (B01's two unattributed hands collecting her record enact this; Q-VZ2's
  split by method stands, and the hands stay unnamed.)
- **The Veil playbook** (PRG 14332–14479, AS): contain, discredit, compartmentalize, recruit or neutralize.
