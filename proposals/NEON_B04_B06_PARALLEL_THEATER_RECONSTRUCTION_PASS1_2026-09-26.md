# Neon B04–B06 Parallel-Theater Reconstruction — Pass 1

**Status:** EDITORIAL PROPOSAL / NON-CANONICAL  
**Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Resolution:** act × theater × POV-function. Not episode architecture.

This reconstruction does not assign final episode counts, lock travel, alter the milestone grid, or authorize EBCI.

# 1. Governing architecture

Neon should change geographic grammar by book:

- **B04 — parallelism begins.** NOLA remains the emotional/public anchor while one or two secondary-protagonist theaters become directly lived.
- **B05 — parallel worlds.** The saga deliberately cross-cuts among differentiated systems and characters. Separation is a feature.
- **B06 — distributed failure → selective convergence.** Several theaters remain active until Santa Fe becomes the catastrophic center. Only people with a concrete reason converge.

This avoids both failures:
1. global events experienced only through NOLA reports;
2. an all-cast international road show.

# 2. POV framework

Use the author's soft target diagnostically:
- ~30% saga lead — Seraphine;
- ~30% trilogy lead — Tahl in Neon;
- ~30% secondary protagonists — Lucien/Caro/Elisabet/Rex/etc.;
- ~10% flexible/local/support.

Do **not** allocate episodes arithmetically.

A POV earns space when it owns:
- a decision;
- information unavailable elsewhere;
- a distinct system;
- a durable relationship change;
- or the human consequence of another plotline.

# 3. Book 4 — First Fracture

## Book identity
**Truth acquires public cost.**

B04 is not “B03 but bigger.”
It converts private/institutional uncertainty into public argument, grief, coercion and competing responses.

## Act I — aftermath before expansion

### NOLA — primary lived theater
Likely owners:
- Seraphine;
- Lucien;
- Caro;
- Tahl/public channel;
- local civic/social cast.

Functions:
- cast learns Baz is dead;
- grief changes routines rather than merely producing speeches;
- Seraphine's accumulated cost is visible;
- Lucien's grief destabilization begins;
- Tahl's public truth role becomes consequential;
- Caro's response competence expands under strain;
- competing explanations harden.

**Reader pressure should reset from B03.**

### Outside-city use
Prefer **receipts/contact/short direct cut**, not three full foreign plots.

Best candidate:
**Singapore/Rex** gets the first short direct non-NOLA institutional scene if B04 needs one:
- Kasumi's instability evidence;
- Shun Watanabe's warning/discrepancy;
- Han/control response;
- Rex forced to decide what counts as acceptable uncertainty.

Why Singapore first:
Technarc failure is explicitly a B04 macro pressure and the recovered local cast is unusually strong.

Vienna can pressure Lucien through records/people without requiring travel yet.

Elisabet can have field/research work without a Reykjavík return yet.

## Act II — public fracture of trust

### NOLA/public information
Tahl owns the difference between:
- observation;
- interpretation;
- publication;
- correction;
- public behavior.

**T1-A candidate fits here or early B05:** visible correction costs him authority with some audience while strengthening MT's epistemic ethic.

Do not force it into B04 if the act is already overloaded.

### Singapore — secondary lived theater candidate
Rex sees:
- evidence being discounted;
- control response tightening;
- workers/scientists disagreeing about what systems can actually guarantee.

B04 should **seed**, not complete, the Technarc legitimacy fracture.

### Caro
Keep primarily NOLA/civic.
Her competence still works.
The trap is forming: because she can help, more responsibility accumulates.

### Elisabet
Give her an independent research/field decision.
She should not exist only beside Caro.
Exact geography open.

### Lucien
His B04 plot is grief + institutional moral residue.
A physical Vienna return is not yet necessary.

## Act III — first Neon-scale fracture

Use **one principal civic/physical crisis**, not a climax in every city.

NOLA can remain the human/public anchor while Singapore and other sites provide bounded independent consequences.

Seraphine:
- attempts stabilization;
- failure/collapse function retained;
- exact physical mechanics still require D5 event work.

Tahl:
- MT becomes a lifeline because ordinary public information is failing, not because MT is supernatural.

Filaments:
- differing responses to risk become visible.

Technarc:
- secrecy/control worsens fear.

### B04 exit
The world has not merely learned “Resonance is global.”
It has learned:
**different institutions and communities will respond differently, and those responses themselves create consequences.**

## B04 milestone implications
Likely keep/strengthen:
- Tahl public-truth turn;
- Lucien/Baz consequence;
- first public Neon fracture.

Potential missing turn:
- **Tahl correction/retraction**, B04 late or B05 early.

Do not add a Rex milestone yet; his durable break belongs later.

# 4. Book 5 — Fracture Patterns

## Book identity
**Different systems fail differently.**

This should be the most deliberately polyphonic book so far.

The speed comes from changing:
- city;
- institution;
- sensory grammar;
- problem type;
- character competence.

Not from keeping every scene at crisis intensity.

## Act I — differentiated diagnosis

### NOLA — Seraphine / Tahl
Functions:
- brittle local conditions;
- Seraphine layered/coherence strain;
- Tahl synthesizes distributed evidence and carries public truth burden.

### Singapore — Rex
Direct lived theater recommended.

Human chain:
- Kasumi supplies harm/instability evidence;
- Shun Watanabe presses internal warning/resistance;
- Tamsin/other operators expose operational fragility;
- Han/hardliners prefer control/escalation;
- Rex cannot remain neutral observer.

This is diagnosis, not yet final institutional break.

### Reykjavík — Elisabet
**Recommended over using Vienna for the recovered “Reykjavík or Vienna” mixed-interference slot.**

Reason:
- B05 needs Elisabet's independent lane;
- Reykjavík has home/research/local continuity;
- Vienna can remain Lucien's later/parallel institutional lane;
- choosing Reykjavík prevents two male institutional plots while Caro/Elisabet remain attached.

Elisabet's job:
- compare observations;
- preserve uncertainty;
- discover where existing models fail;
- maintain human/home relationships while working.

No automatic Resonance expertise.

### Vienna — Lucien
Use selectively.

Recommended architecture:
Lucien does **not** need a full Vienna plot in A1 if B05 already has NOLA/Singapore/Reykjavík.
Vienna can enter through Marius/family/archive pressure.

A physical return can occur later only if it forces a choice that remote contact cannot.

### Caro
NOLA/care-system lane initially.
Her workload is becoming unsustainable.

## Act II — choices create divisions

### Singapore — Technarc
This is where internal disagreement becomes action.

Candidate chain:
Kasumi evidence → Shun refusal/resistance → hardliners proceed → intervention or controlled response fails / harms → Rex chooses responsibility.

Do not require a public manufactured-meta debut unless M17 chronology is resolved.

### NOLA / civic care — Caro
Caro is increasingly the person everyone calls because she succeeds.

Show:
- staff limits;
- transport;
- handoffs;
- community care;
- Filament/formal-service interface;
- people who cannot wait for cosmic answers.

### Tahl
If T1-A did not occur in B04, this is the strongest window:
he visibly corrects a meaningful interpretation.
The correction costs him with one audience and earns a different kind of trust.

### Seraphine/Lucien
Relationship strain/reconnection can happen across partial separation.
Do not require Lucien physically beside Seraphine throughout.

### Caro/Elisabet
This is an excellent window for the recovered relationship milestone:
**they choose the relationship while choosing not to abandon their separate work.**

Exact physical/intimacy beat remains romance-pass work.

## Act III — evacuation and threshold

### Evacuation city
**Leading test: Chicago.**
**Fallback/alternate: NOLA.**

Why Chicago now edges ahead editorially:
1. B05 is explicitly the distributed book.
2. NOLA already carries Seraphine/Tahl and much of B04.
3. Chicago is canonical Caro home/professional identity but underused as lived geography.
4. Family and professional responsibility can collide.
5. It lets the recovered evacuation perform character work rather than merely add scale.

But Chicago attendance remains **new placement**, so causal setup is required.

### Credible Chicago setup options
Prefer one:
- Caro travels home for a family/ordinary reason before crisis escalation;
- a professional/community connection draws her there for bounded work;
- she is already visiting after accumulated B04 strain.

Avoid:
- she flies to Chicago because maps predict the next disaster;
- fate places her there;
- Resonance draws her home.

### CARO-N1
At evacuation:
- demand exceeds personal capacity;
- Caro becomes a bottleneck by trying to cover too much;
- she must entrust a consequential task to someone else;
- she leaves before it is finished;
- scarcity remains real;
- the response works better because responsibility is distributed.

**Candidate new milestone: handoff is not abandonment.**

### Reykjavík — Elisabet
Her data/field line helps establish Santa Fe as a credible threshold **without making her omniscient**.

She may be physically separate from Caro during the evacuation.

### Tahl
Publishes the hardest bounded warning he can justify.
He cannot make institutions obey it.

### Santa Fe
At B05 it remains:
- warning;
- convergence hypothesis;
- threshold;
- threatened place.

Do not move the whole cast there yet.

## B05 exit
Santa Fe is credible enough that failure to act has moral weight.

# 5. Book 6 — The Breaking

## Book identity
**Knowledge does not guarantee prevention.**

The book begins with everyone applying what they learned in B05—and discovering that correct diagnosis still cannot make every system cooperate.

## Act I — distributed failure

Keep at least three live theaters long enough to preserve separation.

### Singapore — Rex
Manufactured-meta / AR / control failures begin before Santa Fe tear.

This is the strongest window for the durable Technarc turn:

**ignored evidence + internal dissent + failed intervention → loss of presumptive compliance in a consequential theater.**

Recommended M17 rewrite target.

Rex does not need to leave immediately for Santa Fe.

### Reykjavík / research network — Elisabet
Warning/model refinement.
She can identify risk while remaining unable to compel response.

Whether she later travels to Santa Fe remains open.

### Chicago or NOLA — Caro
If Chicago evacuation survives:
- Caro is dealing with aftermath/handoffs/displaced people.
- Her B05 lesson is tested under worsening conditions.

If NOLA evacuation wins instead:
- preserve equivalent care aftermath.

Either way, do not automatically send her to Santa Fe.

### NOLA / distributed — Seraphine/Lucien
Their instability worsens.
Exact co-location can vary.

### Tahl
Moves toward / is at Santa Fe by protected endpoint architecture.
His information work remains mortal.

## Act II — Santa Fe catastrophe

### Direct field group
**Required:** Tahl.

Other attendance should be selected by function, not importance.

Possible functions:
- local responders/Filaments;
- person carrying technical/environmental warning;
- extraction capability.

Do not assume Seraphine, Lucien, Caro, Elisabet and Rex all stand beside Tahl.

### Tahl sequence
Protected:
- helps people;
- late full VT slip with Intent spark;
- fatal systemic consequence;
- dies at Santa Fe;
- no farewell post;
- MT goes silent.

The death is not chosen martyrdom.

### Parallel theaters during catastrophe
Use sparingly but deliberately.

A cut away can show:
- MT stops updating;
- someone waits for a message;
- a local crisis continues even though the reader knows Tahl is dying elsewhere.

This makes distance hurt.

## Act III — asymmetric knowledge

Do **not** make this a second action climax.

Knowledge bands:

### Band 1 — direct witnesses
Small.
Know Tahl is dead or see immediate aftermath.

### Band 2 — operationally connected
Know something catastrophic happened; receive incomplete confirmation.

### Band 3 — MT/public audience
Experience silence first.
Rumor and misinformation fill the gap.

### Band 4 — emotionally close but geographically distant
Receive confirmation through another person/system after delay.

This is where character-specific grief becomes plot.

### Seraphine
Near-shatter function can occur after learning / amid wider collapse.
Do not make Tahl's death mechanically trigger her condition unless causal physics proves it.

### Lucien
Baz wound + Tahl loss rhyme.
Silence-precursor spiral remains his own trajectory, not emotional contagion.

### Caro
Strong recommendation:
**do not require direct witness.**
Her inability to leave people who need her can make delayed confirmation more painful and reinforce her independent arc.

### Elisabet
Could be operationally connected through warning/data.
Direct Santa Fe attendance remains a decision variable.

### Rex
Could remain in Singapore through the Technarc break and learn remotely.
If later extraction requires him, travel must be earned and timed.

### Kade
Succession begins through absence/public information.
He does not need to be beside Tahl.
Distance strengthens the idea that he inherits a public burden, not a magical baton.

## B06 exit
- Tahl dead;
- last message/pattern survives;
- MT silence creates vacuum;
- Kade begins to speak into it;
- different characters end Neon in different places;
- B07 funeral can become a genuine re-gathering rather than “everyone walks into the next room.”

# 9. Rhythm check

This architecture produces distinct act exits:

- **B04 A1:** grief becomes public interpretation.
- **B04 A2:** competing responses harden.
- **B04 A3:** plural failures become undeniable.
- **B05 A1:** systems are failing differently.
- **B05 A2:** people choose incompatible responses.
- **B05 A3:** evacuation + bounded warning makes Santa Fe actionable.
- **B06 A1:** even informed systems fail to prevent escalation.
- **B06 A2:** Tahl dies at the singular Neon catastrophe.
- **B06 A3:** information, grief and succession propagate unevenly.

No act needs a second climax merely to maintain scale.
