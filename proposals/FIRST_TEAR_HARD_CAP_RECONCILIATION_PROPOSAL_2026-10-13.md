## 3. Reconciled causal model

Five concepts, kept apart (the Oct 13 brief asked for this and it is right):

| # | Concept | Status | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Resonance source**: where resonance comes from | **OPEN** | What generates it. Mechanica treats it as what people feel pressing on the world (the folder's sheet, line 2). Do not decide more |
| 2 | **Blocking**: the cap's ceiling | **RULE** | §42A.2 |
| 3 | **Stored pressure** | **RULE** | §42A.2 |
| 4 | **Local boundary integrity**: how thin a point is | **RULE** (concentrates at the thinnest points); why a given point is thin is **OPEN** | |
| 5 | **Exposure through a fissure**: what people, animals and places receive | **RULE** in outline (wounds, §34); species and environment detail is **PROPOSED** | |

**What the model may say, using only ruled and approved pieces:**
- The cap blocks above a ceiling. What it blocks is stored. Storage concentrates where the boundary is thinnest. A break there releases the concentrated load and leaves a wound.
- A small trigger at a point already near failure can have an effect out of proportion to the trigger. That is a consequence of storage, not a new rule.

**What it must not say without a ruling:**
- A funnel radius, a collection area, a transfer speed, or any equation tying release size to trigger size.
- That ordinary grief breaks the Veil. The card's wording is care, urgency and helplessness exceeding buffering; the conversation's own caveat (an *exceptional* alignment of Will, Emotion and Intent, not strong feeling) is the safe reading.
- That resonance caused the child's illness or death.
- A reason the original wound must be repaired last.

**Source, trigger, outlet, victims (P2).** Useful as a drafting checklist, and compatible with §42A: source is the stored pressure, trigger is a disturbance at a point near threshold, outlet is the thinned point, victims are whoever is exposed. They need not coincide. This is a vocabulary for the proposal and for any later amendment draft, not a rule.

**Version B (P3).** Compatible with everything above. It is the only reading under which the cap's brittleness (a long accumulation) and Seraphine's role (the last disturbance) both hold, and it keeps her causally involved without being the cause of the cap's failure.

## 10. Adversarial check

Where the conversation's model is weak:

1. **Circular.** "Seraphine's response triggers the tear" and "the tear is traceable to Seraphine's response" are the same claim. The only independent evidence would be a physical trace (a timestamped instrument reading, an animal log, a water sample). Without one the trigger can't be shown. Keep it reserved.
2. **Unfalsifiable.** An undefined "coupling of Will, Emotion and Intent" explains any outcome. The conversation concedes it needs reconciling with her Loom role. Until it is, treat it as a character trait, not a mechanism.
3. **Too convenient.** The same woman who fails the child's paperwork also breaks the world at the moment she learns of the death. It couples moral guilt and cosmic cause, and the conversation's own caveat (causal responsibility is not moral guilt) is the right guard. The drafting risk is a reader who takes it as fate.
4. **Incompatible with ruled limits?** Not with §42A. It risks collision with "grief, feeling and Intent cause nothing" (now lifted for B01 but not for the reserved origin), and with "nobody reads minds, no powers, beams or blasts".
5. **Pressure before the trigger.** The page's pressure starts during the effort. The conversation handles this with "precursor leakage", which works but means the porch trigger is the second event, not the first, and the earlier effect needs its own explanation (stress at a thin point, not Seraphine).
6. **Conservation.** "A local release cannot create unlimited effect" is a good instinct and an unwritten rule. Writing it down is a canon decision, not a drafting aside.

### 12.1 What the full read shows about the first tear

1. **The prologue already stages the tear as pending.** Silence and Hope: *"Is it giving?" "Not yet."* and *"And when it gives?"* (unanswered). The screen door's tape holds while the wire beneath it is broken. So the page itself says the cap has not given when the boy is feverish. - **What the page already does.** The yellow dog is posted at the mouth of the shell road, leads her in, makes no sound all night, then goes off "like an alarm" at the siren; Dré says "There's a dog in the road" and the dog is in the road. The cat watches the dog from the freezer and sits against Seraphine in the night. That is already the oddity.
- **Why not add animal behavior.** Across the whole book no animal reacts to any event: not at the Square, the lot, Ursulines, Carrollton, Lacoste or the festival. The one near-exception is Mr. Ledet's cat dropping off the rail as a wrist breaks (Ch 30), which is a person's act, not the phenomenon. The crew's rules and the sheet name *still water and fine surfaces*, never animals ("Still water and fine surfaces show it first", sheet line 5). An animal that senses it at the camp would add a rule the book never states, and invite the question why no animal reacts later. Colors also collide: gray is the book's surveillance motif (the case, the suit, the hair), the camp cat is gray, Bijou is gray; yellow would pair with Ch 26's orange cat.
- **What fits the book.** Water is its own instrument. The coffee is already in Seraphine's hands at the give ("She held the coffee."), and the crew's later rule is *"ripples, then legs"* (coffee ripples before the Square drops, Ch 26 and Ch 34). A single line at the notification, such as the surface of the coffee shivering though her hands are still, would plant that rule in Ch 1, keep an ordinary explanation (her hands shook earlier in the same scene), and carry no resonance language. The dog's dish (filled in Ch 1) is a second candidate. Frogs falling silent after the generator is the conversation's idea; it is an animal reaction and falls under the objection above.
- **Recommendation:** no new animal behavior. If the author wants a trace at the give, use the coffee. This answers the author's lean (a subtle oddity, not resonance) without breaking the book's own rule. It is a manuscript change and waits for the author.
