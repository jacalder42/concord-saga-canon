# Wants lines: eleven characters

**What it does not change.** No card, registry row, pressure card, writer profile, grid, ruling, beat or packet. Per
Q-CE3's recommendation these lines would travel first through the non-canon pressure cards
(`proposals/CHARACTER_PRESSURE_CARDS_2026-09-29.md`); **a Wants line on a canon card is the author's edit.** Mara's
entry depends on her proposed lines (pressure cards §9), which await his approval.

**Method.**
- **(a)** is the protective want the character already has, from the cards and the approved pressure lines.
- **(b)** is **one** want for themselves that is not about protecting others. **[EVIDENCED]** means a card or an
  architecture row states it or shows the character acting on it. **[INFERRED]** gives its basis.
- **(c)** is where that want collides with **another protagonist's** want, by book and episode, and what it costs.
  Collisions are drawn from the end-state register (`proposals/RELATIONSHIP_END_STATE_REGISTER_2026-09-29.md`, entries R1–R18).
- Where the evidence is thin, two options are given, with a recommendation.

## Notes

- **Variety.** The eleven wants are: being needed (Seraphine), standing and an excluding loyalty (Lucien), a place among
  people (Baz), a place of her own (Caro), credit and being right (Elisabet), possession and a secret (Trip), keeping
  (Mara), vindication (Rex), a witness's pride (Tahl), attention (Kade), ground and being owed (Lacuna). No two are the
  same, and only Seraphine's is close to care.
- **Pleasure** is the one kind on the brief's list that no one is given as their main want. The Veil audit found
  pleasures that are *"small, granted, and never set against another protagonist's want"*. The obvious candidates (Caro
  and Elisabet's *"want each other for more than care"*, B03 E24; Kade's pride, B08 E15) are already on the page, and
  are better carried at prose level than as a single line.
- **Every (c) is placed before B09 E31.** Nothing here touches B09 E31–E45 or the ruled epilogue beats.
