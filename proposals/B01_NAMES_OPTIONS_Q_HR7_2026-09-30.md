# B01 names: options for Q-HR7

**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Status:** PROPOSAL, non-canonical. These are options for the author to choose from. **Nothing is named until he
chooses.**

**What it answers.** Q-HR7 in `decisions/B01_REDRAFT_HUMAN_READ_ANSWERS_Q_HR_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-30.md`, with the
direction the author gave there:
- Seraphine's workplace gets an institutional name, shortened in use to *the center*;
- Mara's first name changes (cast row A01);
- Lucien's Dominion-supplied cover is a **regional** review of unexpected costs and events across the parishes around
  the city (Q-HR6a, answered *regional*).

**What it does not change:** no card, cast row, packet or manuscript text.

**How every option was checked.** Each name below appears nowhere in:
- the cast registry, the held and retired lists;
- `canon/` and `ebci/`;
- the canon repository's proposals, decisions, reports, recovery and rules;
- the B01 redraft.

Four candidates were dropped because they are already in use: Althea, Delphine, Delacroix and Pellerin.

**Real-world check before adoption.** The organization names are invented. Before one is adopted, it should be checked
against real Louisiana agencies and firms, so that the book never names a real body.

## 1. Mara's new first name (A01)

**Who she is.** A community elder who has run a neighborhood food pantry for years, in a Catholic fraternal hall. She
keeps her own book of who needs what. She is Seraphine's ally.

**The name has to be:**
- **unlike *Mira* (L01) and *Mara Niht* (G08)**, so no *Ma-*, *Mi-* or *-ra* shape;
- **unlike the B01 names already on the page:** Marisol, Odile, Dolores, Denise, Vernice, Lorraine, Tavie and others.

| Option | Why it fits | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Inez** | Short and firm, like *Mara*, so her scenes keep their rhythm. Catholic, and at home in New Orleans. No sound-alike in the cast | None found |
| **Ernestine** | An elder's name. *Miss Ernestine* is how the neighborhood would say it. Distinct in sound from everyone near her | Long; people would shorten it (*Miss Ernie*) |
| **Winifred** (*Miss Winnie*) | Warm and old-fashioned. The nickname gives Seraphine and the regulars a familiar form | *W* is shared with Walt and Mrs. Whitlow, but the sound is distinct |
| **Geraldine** | Plain and sturdy, of a generation. *Miss Geraldine* | *G* is shared with Gail and Guidry |
| **Hortense** | Unusual and memorable, and very New Orleans for her age | May read as comic to some readers |

**Recommended: Inez.** It changes the least: two syllables, one strong vowel, the same weight on the page as *Mara*.
Nothing in the saga sounds like it.

## 2. Seraphine's workplace

**What it is.** A family and benefits services center:
- casework;
- recertifications and paperwork;
- home visits under rules printed on a card;
- a supervisor (Denise);
- numbers reported to the **parish**. See §4.

**It reads as a nonprofit under public contract.** Founder- or place-named nonprofits are common in the city, and an
invented founder's name is the least likely to collide with a real agency.

| Option | Why it fits | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Beaulieu Center for Family Services** | Founder-named, the most common shape for an old New Orleans social-service nonprofit. It shortens naturally to *the center*, or *Beaulieu* in a caseworker's mouth | Check that no real *Beaulieu* agency exists locally |
| **The Esplanade Family Services Center** | Named for a street, so it places her in the city | Street names are the likeliest to collide with a real organization |
| **The Tri-Parish Family Services Center** | Makes the parish structure visible, which helps the reader with the camp being *past the parish line* | It may echo Lucien's regional review too closely |

**Recommended: the Beaulieu Center for Family Services.** It is institutional, and it shortens to *the center* as the
author asked. It also keeps well clear of Lucien's cover.

## 3. Lucien's cover organization

**What it must do:**
- give him a credible reason to inspect a house for a neighboring parish's coroner, and to deal with the city;
- explain why a Viennese structural engineer is doing it;
- be a front that the Dominion supplies, and that he knows is one (Q-HR5a).

**It can have two layers:**
- **his employer**, which goes on the form's employer line;
- **the contract**, the regional review it is running.

The names can carry a faint institutional note of *order*, which fits the Dominion's *"Order as Mercy"*. That note
becomes legible only after the Dominion is named at B02 E08. **None of the options uses *Concord* or *Accord*,** which
are reserved (D11).

| Option | Employer (his line on the form) | The contract (what he says he is doing) | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | **The Hollen Institute for Civic Continuity**, Vienna | The Regional Review of Unexpected Public Costs | A European institute explains a Viennese engineer. *Continuity* is the faint Dominion note. The review's title is dull on purpose, a bureaucratic name nobody questions |
| **B** | **Meridian Public Risk**, a consultancy | The Gulf Parishes Contingent Cost Review | Reads as an ordinary consultancy, the kind parishes hire. Less European, so his accent needs its own explanation |
| **C** | **The Halvard Foundation for Urban Resilience** | Southeast Louisiana Incident and Cost Study | *Resilience* is how post-storm Louisiana talks, so it blends in. A foundation grant is a plausible reason for an outsider to be there |

**Recommended: A, the Hollen Institute for Civic Continuity, running the Regional Review of Unexpected Public Costs.**
- It fits the page's own phrase for his brief: *the relation between what was recorded and what was done*.
- It lets him give his employer's name *in full* (E14, E24): true to his papers, false to his allegiance.
- It leaves the Dominion unnamed until B02 E08.

## 4. A finding while checking: *the county*

**The redraft says *the county* in about ten places** (E02, E09, E12, E21, E37). Examples:
- Guidry's windbreaker;
- the county pulling Seraphine's numbers;
- Denise's county spreadsheet.

**Louisiana has parishes, not counties.** It is a verified factual slip. It goes into the correction list (Tier 1a) for
after the author's read.
