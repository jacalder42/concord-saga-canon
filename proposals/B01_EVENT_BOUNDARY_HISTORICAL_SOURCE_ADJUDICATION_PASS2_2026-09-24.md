## What the source test changes for B1 planning

The simple extrapolation `48 minus 12 = 36 plot episodes ≈ 36 distinct events` is **not a census**. The earlier packet's **22 clear-bearing / six boundary / twenty primarily support** is a map of episode functions at synopsis resolution, not an approved event number. This pass finds **one particularly credible within-episode split at E45**, several **conditional** splits at E23/E38/E40 and strong **merge pressure** at E35 and potentially E47. Hence the material is *not obviously empty*, but nothing supports claiming we already have forty independent changes.

The high-value recovery task is not to mint more Resonance incidents. Give recovered ordinary/civic candidates unique actor, location, constrained choice, changed access/role/resource, and later carry; then allow the selected B1 Life/Reward units to remain enjoyable without filing a “plot advancement” receipt. The eight Pass 1 placeholders remain **unfilled questions**. A historical “Visible Change” label does not fill them.
