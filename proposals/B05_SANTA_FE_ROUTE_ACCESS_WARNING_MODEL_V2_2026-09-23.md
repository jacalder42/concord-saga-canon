## Minimal event-and-evidence design (PROPOSAL, no invented measurements)

1. **Independent on-site observation:** An existing operational role (to be selected from the actual B5 cast/civic map) records a Santa Fe access change with local occurrence time and first reporting time. The observer's direct experience of material conditions is separate from a policy decision to close a route. Identify the physical change if the scene is to count as a Resonance event; a queue alone is a human systems consequence.
2. **Comparison:** Match one affected route segment to a nearby unaffected or differently affected segment with stated traffic, maintenance, weather and response differences. Preserve relevant *negative observations*: a busy route still operating, a network outage without physical damage, or a closure that predates the purported anomaly. A single comparative pair does not constitute “triangulation” of the whole Southwest.
3. **Source custody:** Originals of notices, inspection notes, ordinary footage and operator records must be attributed and time-stamped. Elisabet can compare copies and seek underlying records; access may be delayed or restricted. What she has is **source material**, not a calibrated Resonance instrument. If an institution changes its risk notice, keep version history; altered wording alone is not proof of deliberate concealment.
4. **Investigation:** Elisabet first reports a range of possible causes. Tahl corroborates the confirmed access hazard with local people and distinguishes confirmed closures from the inference that the Santa Fe corridor is destabilizing. Kade's editorial/MT role, if included, uses the current B5 route and cannot be assumed from stale Dec. 7 scene text.
5. **Immediate and lingering cost:** Bad route guidance wastes fuel, time and scarce care access; an early evacuation strains shelter, work and family capacity. Delayed warning increases exposure. Name affected people and decision authority during later beat construction. If someone intentionally exerts Resonance, record *that person's* separate capacity, Intent, immediate cost and aftermath. Civic injury cannot replace the Resonance cost ledger.
6. **B5 threshold vs B6 collapse:** B5 needs bounded, documentable instability plus uncertainty over the failure mode; B6 retains the major tear/collapse and Tahl's death. The same route may remain partially functional with graded communications/AR degradation. No mandatory citywide electronic shutdown or direct “density” reading.

## What would falsify or shrink the forecast

If the physical route changes track ordinary weather/congestion/maintenance and unaffected local controls show no anomalous material change, publish a safety alert without a Resonance conclusion. If a report's clock is only the upload time, do not call it synchronized with distant events. If selective reporting makes Santa Fe the apparent center, Elisabet must revise the map. If a Technarc classification improves predictive accuracy, characters can contest its ethics while acknowledging its useful observations; if it fails, they must correct the public claim. If no independent Santa Fe physical trace can be supplied, **Claim B remains a character hypothesis**, even if the author-pasted B5 macro later stages destabilization.
