## 1. The ruled frame (Mechanica §48, ruled 09-27)

- **Metas are altered humans**, their names erased at conversion. **Their personhood is never in
  question.** No POV; no Intent; no return to what they were.
- **Their load is stored and unintegrated; they cannot spend it.** A recipient who receives a drawn
  load spends it, and supplies the Intent.
- **Leakage:** a load that nothing spends **discharges uncontrolled**. That is *"fail
  catastrophically under pressure"*.
- **The draw crosses body to body, by contact only.** Apparatus positions, restrains, times and
  monitors; **it never conducts.** A distance machine can provoke leakage, nothing more.
- **It is charged by resonance harvested from followers and handlers.** Crowds amplify it but are
  not harvested.

## 3. What each person does

- **Rex** gets the people nearest him out and keeps notes of **what failed, where and when**. He does
  not diagnose the man. He cannot stop the handlers taking him.
- **Seraphine**, at the edge, **sees a frightened person**, not a hazard. She tries to reach him and
  is held back by the crowd. **Their first intersection**, with no trust yet (Rex entry, approved).
- **Technarc's retrieval team** takes him. **They are people doing a job**, not villains (A1).
- **Nobody names him on the page.** Nobody learns he is a *"manufactured meta"*: the term is
  institutional and hidden in B04.
