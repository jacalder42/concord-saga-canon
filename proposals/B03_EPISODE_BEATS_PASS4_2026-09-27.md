# Veil B03 — per-episode beats, Pass 4

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Status:** EDITORIAL PROPOSAL / PRE-EBCI — NON-CANONICAL.

- These are the per-episode beat sections for the 45 episodes and the three-episode epilogue of
  `proposals/B03_EPISODE_ARCHITECTURE_PASS3_2026-09-27.md`. The author approved pass 3 and asked for
  this pass (`decisions/B03_PASS3_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-27.md`, *"Yes for 1-9"*).
- **The format follows B02 pass 5.** Each episode has:
  - a provenance line;
  - a story job;
  - a **Turn**: the encounter, what someone wants, what stands against it, and what changes;
  - beats;
  - guardrail or exit notes where they are needed.
- **Architecture, not EBCI:** no ECID headers, no dialogue, no prose.
- **The B01 EBCI hold stands.** No episode is generated.

**Provenance tags:**

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pass 3 · E NN` | the pass-3 episode it expands |
| `pass 2 · slot NN` | the pass-2 slot behind it |
| `recovered · …` | a recovered source, Tier D |
| `approved · Xn` | an approved design or ruling it carries |
| `pass 4` | a choice made in this pass. §Q asks about the main ones |

**Place references are provisional.** Bywater is `NOLA-04`, the Marigny `NOLA-02`, the Tremé `NOLA-01`
and the Warehouse District `NOLA-06`.

**Unnamed on purpose:** the night-shift worker, the teenager, the child and the family that waits.
**Naming is left to EBCI.**

**Standing guardrails for every episode:**

- **Physics:** effects are **observed**, never explained (D5; Mechanica §60). Observation is kept
  distinct from inference. The Warehouse follows pass 3 §5's bounded event model.
- **Tahl:** no name and no named POV before the epilogue (A10), and no identity clue before E47. E46 is
  the anonymous author's **unnamed** POV; E47 names him. The MT voice is known by its work. *(Corrected
  09-27, ledger §189: this read "no name, no POV … before E47", stricter than A10 and contradicted by
  E46.)*
- **Kade: none.** **Rex:** only E43, alone. **Honey Island: none.** **Santa Fe:** a direction only.
- **Silence and Hope** do not act (M21). They are only the other side of the E47 brush.
- **Caro does not entrust consequential unfinished work to others and leave** (M54).
- **No Mira.** The seed was B02's.
- **The Warehouse:** *"no blame, just inference"*. The post → crowd → delay chain is **never
  narrator-certified**. **No one murders Baz.**
- **No second spectacle after the Warehouse. No Baz echo.** Baz is not made sentimental or prophetic.
- **Metas:** rumour only (A13).
- **Seraphine–Lucien:** necessity acknowledged, not resolved.

pass 3 · E01 | pass 2 · slot 01 | B02 exit

conditions.**
