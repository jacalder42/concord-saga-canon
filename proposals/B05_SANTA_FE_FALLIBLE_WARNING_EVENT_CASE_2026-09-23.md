## Evidence ladder and failure conditions

1. **Direct local fact:** inspectors can verify damage/motion and an exposure hazard. Two genuinely independent recording paths (for example a fixed site camera and a witness recorder) help check whether an audible/mechanical event occurred; shared compression, clock synchronization and handling still matter. Do not prescribe precise numbers before a building and instruments are chosen.
2. **Ordinary explanation test:** inspect maintenance/settlement, HVAC and vehicle/construction schedules, wind and weather, conventional seismic logs, altered fixtures and faulty equipment. A damaged fitting may be alarming even if it is completely mundane.
3. **Pattern inference:** compare separate local incidents, affected and unaffected assemblies, and a matched site if accessible. A pattern beyond one defect is stronger than one loud event but still does not read Emotion, Intent, or “Resonance density.”
4. **Regional forecast:** a qualified source can assert that local risk merits precaution, perhaps alongside other site-specific reports already in the B5 architecture. The *prediction of corridor threshold* is an inference with a stated observation window and explicit miss condition, not a calibrated percentage. A warning can be justified by plausible harm before its cause is proved.
5. **Institutional conflict:** preserve successive copies of a risk classification and their timestamps. A changed public message plus underlying data may expose a judgment or withheld uncertainty; it does not establish intentional concealment without evidence of knowledge, control and motive.

**Falsifiers or required revision:** If ordinary traffic/settlement completely accounts for readings, retain a material civic hazard but stop calling this Resonance evidence. If independent records do not match in time, withdraw simultaneity. If there is no credible access to originals, Tahl reports allegations rather than certifies a signal. If the only way to forecast Santa Fe is a magical “density” dial, reject this version. If risk does not recur or develop, keep the warning contested and let a later independent event carry the macro escalation.
