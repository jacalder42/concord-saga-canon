## 0. Governing identity

**B02 = PATTERN / PUBLIC LANGUAGE → THE FIRST CHOICE TO ACT.** Information globalizes before the
principal cast does, and New Orleans stays the lived centre. The book's engine is **one civic
decision on uncertain evidence** (A2): whether to let Baz's once-failed model move an institution.
The decision **helps, and costs.** The book closes on **drift**, a working directional model, and on
**No Reset**, the altered baseline that does not return.

**Scale:** **47 narrative episodes** (A1: 15, A2: 16, A3: 16), with **9 LR units** and **4–5
supplements**. That is comparable to B01's 48.

**POV:** a diagnostic only. **No numbers are set before scenes are assigned** (author, 09-27). The
count by owner is given in §5 as information.
