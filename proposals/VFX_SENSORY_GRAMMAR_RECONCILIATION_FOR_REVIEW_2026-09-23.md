## Proposed grammar

For a sensory sentence, first decide whether it reports an **objective effect**, a **fallible attuned perception**, a **subjective POV impression**, or a **metaphor/camera image**. Then identify the local source and carrier (light/electricity, glass, moisture, metal, structure, sound or people), the event's limit and any trace. If no literal effect is intended, keep it as image without granting the character a new ability. Mixed sentences may carry more than one label.

**Editorial sequence:** cause → medium/coupling → physical observation → POV interpretation → cost/residue. This sequence structures the private audit, not the prose order. The prose can reveal evidence through action and later correction.
