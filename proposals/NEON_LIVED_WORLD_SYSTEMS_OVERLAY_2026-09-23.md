## Three Neon falsification checks

1. A wealthy district has steady lights while an adjacent one loses power. Record the actual feed, protected loads, repair delay and who gets displaced. The disparity cannot be explained solely by a resonance “mood.”
2. A stored witness record survives but a live feed fails. Separate device charge, local storage, link availability, interference and what the record actually proves.
3. A shelter sells or allocates charge time. State who owns the fuel/equipment, queue rule, who is excluded and what an exhausted worker can realistically do. Do not import a complete barter or Knot constitution from an illustrative line.
