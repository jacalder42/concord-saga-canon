# Concord Saga — shared lived-world systems baseline

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Status:** PROPOSAL / reconciliation surface, not canon. Applies across Veil, Neon, Loom; post-Mending requires a separate settlement review. No EBCI release.

This baseline is the common interface for [Veil/B01](B01_VEIL_LIVED_WORLD_SYSTEMS_PACKET_2026-09-23.md), [Neon](NEON_LIVED_WORLD_SYSTEMS_OVERLAY_2026-09-23.md), and [Loom](LOOM_LIVED_WORLD_SYSTEMS_OVERLAY_2026-09-23.md). The overlays describe changes in availability and institutions; they cannot invent a new physical law. An era statement is a **trend**, never a universal rule for every block, class or scene.

## Shared causal ledger

For an event that matters, identify **site and time → physical resource/condition → person and authority → action → observation/record → immediate cost → later consequence**. Note which portions are witnessed, inferred or institutionally asserted. Ordinary scarcity and social pressure need no Resonance explanation.

## Era interface for one recurring service

For each recurring electricity, transit, communications, water, shelter, medicine or evidence service, an overlay answers: **where does it work, who supplies it, who can access it, what fails first, what alternative exists, who pays or waits, what survives afterward?** Give a specific place and an exception if the trend is uneven. A grid/network/device failure should be logged separately; a witness should be able to misattribute one to another.

**Minimal continuity row:** `book/scene | service or object | source/owner | access/charge or cash/time cost | failure/reliability | material residue | status/source | open decision`. Fill only fields that affect causality; no prices, inventory catalogue or agency chart by default.

## Decision gates

1. **B01 first:** resolve the on-page dependencies V1–V7 in the [Veil packet](B01_VEIL_LIVED_WORLD_SYSTEMS_PACKET_2026-09-23.md) only where a scene requires them. Correct the Trip pronoun in the integrated copy separately.
2. **Neon before Neon expansion:** decide device adoption and failure by district/class, faction versus public authority, first recipient/amplification mechanic for manufactured metas, and material cost of response. [Neon overlay](NEON_LIVED_WORLD_SYSTEMS_OVERLAY_2026-09-23.md) is a source-bounded scaffold.
3. **Loom before Loom expansion:** specify water, shelter, triage and ration authority in actual routes/settlements; audit the power/travel examples against canon before turning them into defaults. [Loom overlay](LOOM_LIVED_WORLD_SYSTEMS_OVERLAY_2026-09-23.md) is a source-bounded scaffold.
4. **Post-Mending separately:** LT and filtration do not rebuild utilities, settle property claims or erase inequality. [B09 endgame recovery](../recovery/B09_ENDGAME_MENDING_ECHO_RECOVERY_2026-09-19.md) sets the narrative constraints, not a full civil settlement.
