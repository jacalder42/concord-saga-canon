## 1. Finding

Recent structural work retained the causal convergence spine more reliably than the saga's distributed lived geography. The recovered environment program is materially broader: New Orleans, Vienna, Singapore, Reykjavík, Marrakesh, Honey Island and Atchafalaya have substantial development; Santa Fe, coastal Japan, Bristol, Mound City and Serpent Mound have narrower but distinct functions; B02 historical material also carries remote Chicago/London/Tokyo reports.

The corrective principle is **not more travel**. It is **parallel theaters with unequal narrative distance**.

A place can matter without the whole principal cast visiting it. Conversely, a city bible is not proof that a scene belongs in the final architecture.

## 5. Trilogy geography hypothesis for testing

### Veil — local certainty breaks
- B01 remains deliberately NOLA-dense.
- B02 should begin making other places **credible independent lives**, with Marrakesh the strongest recovered early case.
- Vienna/Singapore/Reykjavík can enter through the characters/institutions they own rather than one global montage.
- B03 should leave the reader with a world larger than NOLA before Neon opens.
- Remote Chicago/London/Tokyo material is useful only if it supplies bounded evidence or world texture; it is not a quota.

### Neon — distributed theaters become plot
Neon is the strongest candidate for intentional cast separation:
- Rex/Technarc/Singapore;
- Lucien/Dominion/Vienna;
- Elisabet research and field geography;
- NOLA political/social/MT life;
- Tahl's information network;
- Santa Fe escalation;
- manufactured-meta consequences.
The key audit question is **which plotlines genuinely require reunion and which improve when characters must act without one another**.

### Loom — distance becomes cost
Established theaters should now pay off:
- services fail unevenly;
- archives, repair networks, shelters and cultural nodes survive differently;
- MT carries information imperfectly;
- diaspora routes matter;
- wound teams need not equal the whole ensemble;
- final convergence at Honey Island should feel exceptional.
Reykjavík, Vienna, Singapore and Morocco/Marrakesh can remain alive through local anchors even when no principal is physically present.

## 6. Knowledge-asymmetry rule for later rhythm work

For each major turn, record:
1. who experiences it directly;
2. who hears about it later;
3. what information is lost/distorted/delayed;
4. who acts before learning the full truth;
5. what reunion changes because the characters lived different events.

This is a rhythm tool as much as a continuity tool. Separation creates anticipation, dramatic irony, delayed grief, mistrust, competence and earned reunion.

## 7. Anti-flattening guardrails

- Do not repair an information gap by teleporting a principal character to the site.
- Do not require one member of the main ensemble at every global event.
- Do not give every city a representative recurring character.
- Do not turn every city bible into mandatory plot.
- Do not treat MT/report reception as physical travel.
- Do not make all cities experience the same phenomenon at the same intensity or time.
- Do not let the wound chain become an all-cast sightseeing itinerary.
- Do not make NOLA the location of every faction confrontation merely because it has the richest cast.
- Do not interpret a separated relationship as stalled; off-site work can alter it.
- Do not converge the ensemble early merely to simplify EBCI.

10. **Global receipts:** Chicago/London/Tokyo and other one-off reports should survive only where they improve evidence, scale or contrast.
