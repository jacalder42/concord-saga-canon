## Why a person, and why now

Every judgement of B01 so far has been an assistant's: the checkpoints, the three blind reads, the tightening pass, the
outlines and the test's judges. Only a person can say whether a real reader feels the *"contest of consideration"*, or
whether that is what assistant readers see in assistant prose. This is also the cheapest point: before any new packet
or redraft exists.

## What to note

**As you go,** at each piece:
- **Where you would put the book down,** if anywhere. Give the page or paragraph.
- **Anywhere you skimmed.**

**Afterwards, a few lines on each:**
1. **Drama or consideration?** Does anyone want something against someone else, or does everyone mainly try to be the
   most considerate? Where is it most and least true?
2. **Appetites beyond care** (Q-IT5): do the characters want anything for themselves (ambition, pleasure, credit,
   something kept back, a loyalty that excludes someone)? Or are they six caretakers with different methods?
3. **Arguments and pace** (Q-IT5): where does a disagreement lengthen a scene without changing what happens next?
4. **Melodrama:** is there a place where bodily distress or feeling does the work a choice should do?
5. **What you would keep at any length:** the scenes, pleasures or people you would not lose in a ~150k version.
6. **Who you care about most and least,** and why.

**The second Q-IT5 question** (do the new choices make the book move faster?) can only be asked of redrafted text. It
goes to the first redrafted act's checkpoint.

## What happens with the answers

Claude records them in `decisions/` and reconciles them with the outlines and the obligations list. Then the redraft's
packets are derived under `ebci/prose/README.md` *Packets for the B01 redraft*. **If the read contradicts the
diagnosis** (for example, if the considerate contest doesn't show for a human reader), the redraft's scope comes back
to you before any packet is written.
