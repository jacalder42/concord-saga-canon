**Authority.** `decisions/CALDER_COMPANIONS_AND_B01_SHAPE_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-29.md`, Q3–Q5: a drafter receives a
short general instruction plus the relevant character's **established pressures** (what they want, what they protect,

not approved drafting instructions.

## 0. Two findings that apply to all six cards

**(a) The cards share one wound shape.** Each character's core fear is some version of *"if I don't do my method,
someone gets hurt"*. Each defensive loop absorbs responsibility and defers the character's own limits. Each failure
list includes some form of not receiving care. Laid side by side:

| Character | The method | The fear behind it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seraphine | regulation | *"If I falter, everything collapses"* |
| Lucien | containment | *"Emotional openness causes loss or collapse"* |
| Baz | truth | *"Letting people down when they need him most"* |
| Caro | motion | *"If she slows down, someone will be hurt"* |
| Elisabet | clarity | *"confusion or delay leads to irreversible harm"* |
| Trip | atmosphere | *"If social spaces fracture, people become exposed and unsafe"* |

If a drafter carries only the wound, the six become one caretaker. **What distinguishes them is the method, and the
methods are mutually incompatible.** That is the Compendium's *"legitimate goals but incompatible methods"*, and it is
already in the cards (§8 below).

**(b) The Veil-era lines tell the drafter the method works.** These are the cards' trilogy lines for Veil:

- Lucien: *"VEIL: restraint rewarded"* (LucienEBCI §IX).
- Caro: *"VEIL: action-driven care rewarded"* (CaroEBCI §IX).
- Elisabet: *"VEIL: clarity rewarded; distance functional"* (ElisabetEBCI §IX).
- Seraphine: *"This loop works — until it nearly breaks her"* (SeraphineIdentity §IX).

The costs are all placed in Neon: *"Neon-Specific Risk"* appears in the Lucien, Caro, Elisabet and Trip cards.

**Only Baz's card places a risk in Veil:** *"Veil-Specific Risk: Baz may delay necessary escalation in the hope calm
will be enough"* (BazEBCI §V). A B01 drafter reading these cards is told that the costs arrive later.

### Draft pressure card

- **Wants:** not to lose anyone else; to believe effort can still outrun loss. **[INFERRED]** from the §III wound and
  §VIII *"Believes effort can still outrun loss"*. The card gives her no want that is not about other people.
- **Protects:** her own steadiness, because she believes others survive on it (§IX, core wound and false belief;

- the title *"Emotional Regulator"* (Identity header and §I);
- *"Discovers she naturally de-escalates conflict"* (§VI.5);
- *"Conflict de-escalation"* as a job duty (§VII);
- *"Her body grounds others before her words do"* (Appearance §II);
- *"Children and distressed individuals calm near her"* (Appearance §V).

It is true of her. **Given to everyone else, it is the problem.** Her own card names the cost: the loop *"nearly breaks
her"*, and *"Calm imposed is violence"*.

### Mara

**Proposed 2026-09-29; awaiting the author's approval. Not used in any stack until approved.** **Update 2026-09-29: approved as provisional drafting guidance (Q-MO4,** `decisions/THIRD_PRESSURE_REVISED_SET_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-09-29.md`**).** The evidence (short
manuscript quotes, and an alternative reading for each line) is in the private manuscript repository,
`draft-notes/character-pressures/MARA_PRESSURES_PROPOSAL.md`. Drawn from what she does in B01 (E09, E40, E41,
E44–E48), deliberately not from her courtesy.

- **Wants:** every person accounted for by name, in her own book, and to be the one who keeps it. The keeping pleases
  her, and a blank or an unnamed person bothers her for her own sake (E09, E40, E44–E45). **[EVIDENCED]**
- **Protects:** her people's face, and her standing with them, through small managed fictions. The cost falls on plain
  truth, on the people she triages off the list, and on what Seraphine is told (E09, E40, E47, E48). **[EVIDENCED]**
  *Alternative:* she protects the timing of what she knows, keeping it back until she judges it is time.
- **Habitually misreads:** she trusts that a plain, sourced fact will settle people, and that she stands outside what
  everybody "hears". On the lot it doesn't carry, and she freezes with the rest (E40). **[EVIDENCED once, in E40;
  EXTRAPOLATED as a habit]** *Weaker alternative:* she reads distress as a shortage of food, heat or sleep.
- **When threatened:** she counts, triages and gives orders in her ordinary voice. Worse, she stops writing, goes
  clipped, decides for people alone and on paper, and pulls back to her own ground (E40, E41, E45, E48).
  **[EVIDENCED]**

**What distinguishes her:** a ledger, not a crouch. She rations and says no, waits rather than soothes, and keeps a
private satisfaction in the count. **She shows no appetite for public credit;** she arranges it for others.
