## 3. Global rules

- **Q-VZ2: the two hands are told apart by method.**
  - **Hand 1 (paper and permission):** holds, the caseload call, revoked access, the file going up, logs sent up.
  - **Hand 2 (instruments and collection):** the cases, the collector, the badge that asked for the minute, the turned
    lanyards, times taken.
  - Only Hand 2 *wants the times*. No names, no agenda.
- **Q-VZ1:** the packets' *no rule stated as a law* becomes ***no law stated by the narrator***. Characters may hold a
  local, provisional working rule.
- **Q-VZ3:** *it takes and never asks* is retired.
- **Q-SA2: a hint keeps its space only if it does something now.** *The people who sent him* comes down to its strongest
  few.
- **Q-SA1 and Q-D3-3: compression is selective, by function, with no quota.** Cut work already accomplished. Keep
  pleasures, bargains and consequences.
- **Q-D3-4: the contextual line pass runs last over the whole book:**
  - conspicuous clusters;
  - repeated endings;
  - explanations that duplicate an action;
  - successive closing beats.

  **The counts are recorded as description, not as a score.**
