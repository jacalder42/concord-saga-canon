- A3 is `THE WAREHOUSE INCIDENT`.
- Baz runs into the warehouse to rescue trapped civilians.

- Lucien arrives too late.
- Baz's final words to Lucien: `You... don't have to be alone.`
- Lucien's grief triggers a dark-spectrum implosion / Silence echo precursor.
- B3 end state marks Baz dead and foundational.

Other Notion pages reinforce this older version:
- Veil Trilogy Overview: `Baz dies (Book 3)`.
- Baz Character Sheet: endgame status `Deceased (Veil Book 3 warehouse incident)`.
- Lucien character material: Baz's death is Lucien's moral/emotional breaking point.
- B4 Notion Beat Bible opens with Lucien already spiraling after Baz's death.

## Later source conflict
Later recovered Final Canon Beat Bible / structural archive has Baz alive at B3 close and participating in the Veil->Neon truth-telling commitment with Tahl and the Filaments.

User recollection places Baz's killing in B4, followed by Lucien spiraling and Tahl speaking out.

### Reconciliation status
**NOTION B3 WAREHOUSE DEATH = SUPERSEDED PRIOR CANON, unless later evidence proves otherwise.**

However, the older scene may preserve valuable dramatic DNA for a later B4 death reconstruction:
- death while choosing to save civilians;
- Baz's truth/care ethic expressed through action;
- Lucien arrives too late;
- death directly fractures Lucien;
- final message addresses Lucien's isolation.

These elements should be treated as `SALVAGE CANDIDATES`, not current canon.

Important: do not simply move the warehouse scene from B3 to B4. Timing, antagonist causality, and later character development may have changed for a reason.

# 6. Antagonist Architecture Bible — recovered source

Notion contains the standalone `05.07 • Antagonist Architecture Bible` that GitHub faction files repeatedly reference.

It defines three antagonist layers:
1. Institutional — Dominion, Technarch, anti-resonance movements.
2. Emotional/Human — Kade spiral, extremist Filaments, civic panic, grief reactions.
3. Metaphysical/Environmental — Silence paradoxes, Hope overload, storms, Shards, saturation, diaspora.

Key older antagonist details:
- Han Wei greenlights manufactured metas and Protocol 9 progression.
- Virelli/Dominion oppose Filaments and target hybrids.
- Saeko is described here as a trauma-driven populist / anti-resonance civic face.
- Manufactured Metas are failures of control without empathy.
- extremist Filament splinters arise from fear and Kade's grief.
- Tahl and Kade can unintentionally generate antagonistic pressure.
- no single villain; saga antagonist thesis = `Resonance without empathy`.

Later GitHub faction canon develops this architecture further and should normally outrank older role assignments where they conflict.
Example:
- later GitHub assigns Ito the fear-amplifier role and Saeko the serenity/quiet-coercion role.

Therefore the Notion antagonist bible should be treated as lineage/provenance and as a source of missing causal mechanics, not as an automatic override.
