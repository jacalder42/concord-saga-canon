# Concord 2026 — Editorial Casting Resolution — Vienna / Lucien Ecosystem

Status: EDITORIAL RECOMMENDATION / PROPOSAL — NONCANONICAL UNTIL AUTHOR PROMOTION
Date: 2026-09-19
Scope: Vienna recurring/place cast, Lucien and Baz's pre-Concord human world, Dominion overlap, Filament/civic texture, and later Choirless recruitment.

## I. RECOVERED CITY FUNCTION

Vienna is historically well developed as:
- Lucien's origin and emotional architecture;
- Baz's home/social world;
- Dominion archival and ideological center;
- later Dominion collapse site;
- a city where cultural/intellectual warmth exists immediately outside institutional control;
- later Choirless intellectual recruitment territory;
- Filament/refugee corridors during collapse.

Unlike Reykjavík, Vienna already has several named characters with direct emotional claims on Lucien.

The task is therefore not to invent a Vienna cast. It is to distinguish:
1. Lucien's human past;
2. Dominion personnel;
3. civic/local texture;
4. later ideological/collapse populations.

## II. BAZ FOIX — KEEP / PRIMARY HUMAN VIENNA ANCHOR

Baz remains the indispensable pre-Concord relationship.

Recovered canon establishes:
- Vienna origin;
- low-level Dominion clerical lineage;
- junior archivist / Lucien's assistant;
- closest friend / near-brother;
- introduces Lucien to warmer, less institutional Vienna;
- specific shared routes, cafés, late-night food, streetcar memories, and sketch locations;
- dies in Book 3;
- no posthumous Echo.

Editorial principle:
**Vienna before Baz's death should feel partly like Baz's city, not merely Dominion's city.**

This matters because Lucien's grief is stronger if he loses not just a person but his guide to ordinary life.

Protect mundane memories:
- D-line/streetcar rides;
- Spittelberg pastry/warm place;
- late-night food;
- Café Europa/Jelinek-type conversations;
- Baz sketching;
- walking Vienna in winter.

Do not convert every memory into resonance foreshadowing.

## III. JANVIER “JAN” FOIX — KEEP / PROMOTE TO NARROW RECURRING FAMILY FACE

Recovered B7–B8 material explicitly establishes:
- Janvier “Jan” Arnaud;
- Baz's cousin;
- distrusts Lucien;
- confronts Lucien about Baz's death;
- eventual reconciliation seed.

Keep **Janvier “Jan” Arnaud**.

This character supplies a missing function:
**Baz belonged to people other than Lucien.**

Jan prevents Baz from becoming merely Lucien's grief object.

### Editorial trajectory
- Pre-Baz death: may exist offstage or appear lightly through Baz.
- After death: distrusts Dominion and Lucien's role in what happened.
- Loom: confrontation becomes unavoidable when Vienna/Dominion collapses.
- Reconciliation, if earned, should not require Jan to absolve Lucien.

Jan's value is moral specificity:
Lucien can be forgiven by himself without being entitled to forgiveness from Baz's family.

Do not make Jan another resonance specialist or faction leader.

## IV. HELENA KAEL — KEEP / FAMILY-DOMINION BRIDGE

Institutional pass already protected Helena as Lucien's aunt and family inheritance.

Vienna confirms this function.

Use Helena to show:
- Dominion ideology embedded in family expectations;
- Lucien's childhood/adolescent conditioning;
- affection expressed through discipline/control;
- the difference between institutional belonging and human belonging.

Do not make Helena a second Virelli.

Her strongest scenes are personal:
coded warnings, family pressure, moments where she almost chooses Lucien over Dominion, and the consequences of not doing so soon enough.

Historical B8 “dying messages” remain beat-level material, not automatically locked.

## V. MARIUS HOLT — KEEP / ARCHIVE FACE

Institutional pass protected Marius.

Vienna makes his geography concrete:
- archive redactions;
- Whisper Vault/log access;
- coded warnings;
- institutional memory;
- eventual leakage/whistleblowing.

Marius should be the recognizable archivist through whom readers experience Dominion records changing under political pressure.

### Consolidation rule
Do not add additional named archivists/genealogists unless they have a unique relationship.

Generic:
- registrar;
- genealogist;
- archive technician;
- subcommittee clerk;
can orbit Marius unnamed.

## VI. MARCELLUS VIRELLI / CALDAS REN — KEEP INSTITUTIONAL, NOT “VIENNA FRIENDS”

Vienna is their power base/context, but they do not belong to Lucien's human social ecosystem.

Maintain:
- Virelli = ideology/authority;
- Caldas = field consequence.

Do not use them to crowd out ordinary Vienna.

## VII. LUCIEN'S NON-DOMINION PRE-CONCORD WORLD — BAZ + PLACES, NOT NEW BEST FRIENDS

Recovered city material repeatedly frames Spittelberg, Neubau, cafés, bookstores, food stands, canal walks, and subculture as Lucien's rare exposure to warmth.

Baz is the bridge.

Do **not** invent another old friend who knew the “real Lucien.” That would dilute Baz's structural importance.

Instead, let recurring service/local faces recognize Baz and Lucien lightly:
- café server/owner;
- bookseller;
- food-stand worker;
- tram worker.

They need not become saga-recurring names.

Recognition is enough:
“Baz always ordered…”
“You haven't been here since…”
ordinary memory can wound Lucien more effectively than another exposition character.

## VIII. FILAMENT VIENNA — KEEP NETWORK, NO NEW SAGA LEADER

Recovered geography gives:
- Hushed Chapel contact point;
- student/artist Filament-inclined clusters;
- Prater refuge route;
- Danube Canal escape routes;
- later refugee operations.

No named Vienna Filament character recovered with enough unique narrative weight to justify promotion.

Use existing global Filament network where needed and local cells otherwise.

Do not create a Vienna counterpart to Mara/Arianna merely for symmetry.

## IX. CHOIRLESS INTELLECTUAL RECRUITMENT — USE PLACE + IDEAS BEFORE LIEUTENANTS

Recovered Vienna environment explicitly establishes Spittelberg/Neubau as later Choirless intellectual recruitment territory and Loom public-square occupation.

This should not require a new named Choirless philosopher.

Use:
- salons/cafés;
- academic debates;
- essays/supplement fragments;
- former Dominion scholars;
- frightened students/professionals;
- public meetings.

Ito Masayuki remains the recurring ideological face.

Vienna can demonstrate how Ito's doctrine appeals to people trained to value restraint/order without turning every recruit into a named antagonist.

## X. DONAUSTADT / TECHNARCH-DOMINION INTERFACE — ROUTE THROUGH EXISTING CAST

Historical city canon contains shared Dominion-Technarch research and prototype traffic.

Use existing:
- Rex;
- Han;
- Kasumi;
- Shun network;
- Marius for Dominion records.

Do not invent Vienna-specific Technarch scientists unless later recovery demands them.

## XI. CIVIC SURVIVAL FACE — DO NOT ADD YET

Vienna's Loom geography includes:
- Prater refugee camps;
- Danube Canal exodus;
- MQ evacuations;
- Judenplatz survivor circles.

At present, none requires a saga-recurring new civic coordinator.

Use Jan where a civilian personal lens matters.
Use Elisabet/Filaments where triage matters.
Use Marius where archive/institutional collapse matters.

A named local organizer can be created during beat construction only if repeated scenes prove the need.

## XIV. RELATIONSHIP GEOMETRY

Vienna gives Lucien four different claims on his past:

**Baz:** “You were capable of ordinary tenderness.”
**Jan:** “Baz's life did not belong to your grief.”
**Helena:** “You were made by this institution and family.”
**Marius:** “You know what the institution actually did.”

Virelli then supplies the antagonist claim:
**“You belong to what made you.”**

Lucien's arc can answer all five without adding more backstory characters.

## XV. PLACE / CHARACTER INTERSECTIONS

### Dominion Archives
Lucien + Baz + Marius + Helena/Virelli pressure.
Later: records, collapse, Whisper Vault.

### Spittelberg / Neubau
Baz's warm Vienna → Lucien's exposure to ordinary life → later Choirless intellectual recruitment.

This transformation is narratively useful:
the same district that once taught Lucien that life exists outside Dominion later becomes vulnerable to a doctrine promising relief from emotional chaos.

### Judenplatz
Keep primarily memory/ethical reflection rather than action spectacle.
Avoid treating real historical trauma as a magical prop. Any resonance treatment here requires sensitivity review during prose development.

### Prater
Refugee/Filament survival rather than Dominion intrigue.

### Danube Canal
Escape, protest, grief, ordinary city texture.

## XVI. EDITORIAL EFFECT

Vienna should feel more populated than Reykjavík but less ensemble-driven than NOLA.

Its human grammar becomes:

**Baz** = warmth and ordinary life.
**Jan** = grief that does not center Lucien.
**Helena** = family conditioning.
**Marius** = institutional truth.
**Virelli/Caldas** = institutional threat.
**local Vienna** = the life Lucien almost never allowed himself to have.

That makes the city emotionally useful instead of merely beautiful Dominion scenery.
