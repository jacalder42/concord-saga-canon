# B03 → B04 Handoff Reconciliation

**Status:** PROPOSAL / RECOVERY — NONCANONICAL UNTIL AUTHOR PROMOTION  
**Date:** 2026-09-19

## Governing current author rulings

1. Baz dies at the **end of B03**.
2. The rest of the primary cast learns of Baz's death at the **start of B04**.
3. Baz dies in the New Orleans **Warehouse Incident**, rescuing a child during a resonance rupture/collapse.
4. Lucien blames himself because **he asked Baz to come to New Orleans**.
5. **Tahl is not a primary character in B01–B03 and may not even be named before the B03 epilogue.**
6. The B03 epilogue is therefore Tahl's proper reveal/entry into the foreground.
7. Current channel chronology remains protected: B03 contains the first/only Veil VT brush; B06 contains the fatal full VT slip + Intent spark.

## Source conflict

November 2025 Final Beat Bibles make Tahl a visible primary POV from B01 onward and stage the whole ensemble at Baz's Warehouse death.

Later recovered structural material instead makes B03 increasingly Tahl-forward around drift/Santa Fe/VT, but the current author ruling supersedes that visibility.

Therefore:
- preserve **functions** assigned to Tahl where useful;
- move those functions off-axis, anonymous, indirect, or into the epilogue;
- do not preserve Tahl as a named B01–B03 co-lead;
- preserve Warehouse mechanics but remove primary-cast direct witnessing if necessary to maintain delayed B04 discovery.

# 1. Recovered dual-climax architecture

B03 has two different jobs that should not be forced into one event.

## Human/emotional climax — Warehouse Incident
The Warehouse Incident resolves the Veil trilogy's human stakes:
- institutional secrecy has consequences;
- resonance instability becomes lethally public;
- Baz's ordinary bravery becomes the trilogy's moral statement;
- a child survives because Baz chooses another person over himself;
- Baz dies;
- Lucien's future grief wound is created;
- the world can no longer treat resonance as harmless rumor.

## Saga-facing turn — Drift / Santa Fe / Tahl reveal
The Drift/Santa Fe material resolves the trilogy's larger mystery:
- anomalies have direction;
- instability is becoming systemic rather than local;
- maps/geometry increasingly bend southwest;
- Santa Fe emerges as a pressure/convergence zone;
- someone outside the primary cast has been independently collecting the pattern;
- that observer reaches the first/only Veil VT brush;
- the epilogue reveals him as Tahl and introduces the voice that will become Missing Thread.

**Recommended structural principle:**
**Warehouse answers “what does this cost people?”**
**Tahl/Santa Fe answers “where is this going?”**

These functions reinforce rather than compete.

# 3. Proposed late-B03 sequence

This is a **structural scaffold**, not an episode lock.

## Phase A — Direction
- Lucien/Baz/Seraphine investigation confirms anomalies are not random.
- geometry / civic distortions / node behavior increasingly indicate southwest drift.
- Santa Fe is named or strongly implied as an instability zone.
- institutions recognize more than they admit.
- Warehouse evidence becomes important because it may connect local NOLA instability to the larger pattern.

## Phase B — Warehouse investigation
- Baz goes to the Warehouse District to investigate the resonance event/evidence.
- exact reason, assignment, and antagonist-intervention mechanism remain unresolved.
- civilians become trapped as the event escalates.
- Baz shifts from investigator to rescuer.
- child rescue occurs.
- resonance shockwave / structural collapse kills Baz.
- exact witnesses remain unresolved.

### Important
Baz's death should not be framed as destiny, magical sacrifice, or heroic prophecy. It is ordinary courage inside systemic failure.

## Phase C — Information fracture
To preserve the current delayed-discovery ruling:
- the Warehouse zone becomes chaotic/contained;
- communications and official information are unreliable;
- institutional containment/evidence control can contribute without yet assigning exact antagonist causality;
- Baz is missing/unaccounted for from the primary cast's perspective rather than immediately confirmed dead;
- public footage shows catastrophe but not necessarily Baz's fate.

This gives B03 a powerful ending state:
**the reader may know more than Lucien, or may only know Baz has not come out.**

Exact dramatic-information choice remains OPEN.

## Phase D — B03 epilogue: Tahl reveal
The dedicated historical epilogue “The First Quiet” supplies a strong recoverable frame:
- New Orleans, late night, hours after Warehouse Rupture;
- rain/static/sirens;
- official silence and public rumor;
- Filament vigil begins;
- raw reporting turns the event from secret anomaly into public crisis;
- transition from Veil secrecy to Neon panic.

### Current reconstruction
The epilogue should pivot to a person the reader has not previously treated as a lead.

He has:
- collected anomalies;
- noticed the southwest directional pattern;
- seen/recorded enough of the Warehouse event to understand official explanations are inadequate;
- experienced the first/only Veil VT brush late in this process;
- survived it exhausted and shaken;
- begun writing because someone has to tell the truth.

Only here do we foreground/name him:
**Tahl.**

His first MT-style post can remain short, mortal, raw, and non-prophetic.

Historical line candidate:
> “This is not the last time we see something like this.”

Status: historical candidate, not current lock.

### Crucial revision
Tahl does **not** need to know Baz personally. Historical Baz canon even states that Tahl never knew Baz existed. Tahl can report the death/event as a human tragedy without being part of Baz's social circle.

# 4. Tahl's B03 VT brush

The later structural archive protects a first/only Veil VT brush.

Under the current visibility ruling, the cleanest placement is:
- during or immediately after Tahl's attempt to understand the Warehouse/Santa Fe pattern;
- not witnessed by primary cast;
- disclosed in epilogue POV;
- over-clarity + pressure collapse;
- no powers;
- no prophecy;
- no dialogue with VT entities;
- leaves Tahl exhausted and frightened rather than special.

Narrative effect:
the reader meets Tahl at the exact moment he realizes the story is larger than anyone's public explanation.

This preserves the VT breadcrumb without turning Veil into Tahl's trilogy.

# 5. B04 opening architecture

B04 should not begin as though everyone already processed Baz's death.

## Opening movement
1. Aftermath / missing-information state continues from B03.
2. Confirmation reaches the primary cast that Baz died in the Warehouse collapse.
3. Lucien receives the fact.
4. His first causal thought is not abstract grief but responsibility:
   **I asked him to come here.**
5. That guilt becomes the initiating wound of Lucien's Neon destabilization.
6. Seraphine becomes one of the few people with whom he cannot fully maintain containment.
7. Tahl, newly visible through MT, becomes a public truth-carrier rather than instantly becoming the protagonists' intimate friend.
8. Institutional explanations/containment collide with eyewitness footage and Tahl's reporting.

## Important separation
Two different relationships to Baz's death:
- **Lucien:** private guilt, friendship, responsibility.
- **Tahl:** public truth, witness/reporting, human cost.

Do not manufacture a prior Baz/Tahl friendship to connect these functions.
