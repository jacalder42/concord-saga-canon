## 2. Classification labels

- **PRESENT** — current authority preserves the decision with adequate specificity.
- **COMPRESSED** — current authority includes the broad event but drops resolved causal, constraint, or continuity detail.
- **MISSING** — resolved source decision is absent from current authority.
- **CONFLICT** — current authority or current rule language materially disagrees with stronger source evidence.
- **SUPERSEDED** — source decision was explicitly replaced later.
- **UNCERTAIN** — source material is suggestive but does not prove authorial adoption.
