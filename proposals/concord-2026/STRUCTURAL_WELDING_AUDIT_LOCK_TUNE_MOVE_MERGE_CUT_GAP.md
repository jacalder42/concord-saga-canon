# Concord 2026 — Structural Welding Audit

Status: EDITORIAL ANALYSIS / NON-CANONICAL
Purpose: audit the recovered 2025 Beat Bible against the 2026 Book Purpose Cards and antagonist recovery using the labels:

- **LOCK** — recovered structure already performs the intended function; preserve.
- **TUNE** — keep beat/function, but sharpen causality, consequence, POV, antagonist pressure, or differentiation.
- **MOVE** — function belongs elsewhere for pacing/causal clarity.
- **MERGE** — two beats appear to perform substantially the same job.
- **CUT** — beat appears redundant after welding; no current required use.
- **GAP** — needed function is missing from recovered beats and requires deliberate construction.

This document does not rewrite episodes. It identifies the minimum structural work required before Beat Bible 2.0.

- Baz's B04 death should occur early enough to causally precede Lucien's major spiral and Tahl's fully public MT role.

No other major recovered beat currently needs relocation at macro level.

## MERGE candidates
No mandatory merges yet. Candidate functions to inspect during episode rebuild:
1. B02 repeated `stronger pulse / global pulse cluster` events.

## CUT
No macro beat should be cut yet.
The audit found **redundant functions**, not obviously disposable story units.
Cuts should occur at episode/beat expansion after unique consequences are assigned.

# Highest-priority GAP list before Beat Bible 2.0

## GAP 1 — Baz B04 death
Must be resolved before B04 structural expansion.
Need:
- exact death circumstances;
- agency/choice Baz makes;
- relationship to truth/institutional pressure;
- immediate Lucien consequence;
- immediate Tahl consequence.

## GAP 2 — B02 containment feedback
Need one or more explicit beats proving containment/management creates secondary instability.

## GAP 3 — B05 engineered adaptation
Need explicit Manufactured Meta / designed intervention development and public failure lineage.

## GAP 4 — B05 Choirless coalescence
Need visible distinction between:
- Ito fear-amplification;
- Saeko serenity/quiet coercion.

## GAP 5 — B08 narrative capture
Need explicit beats showing Brightbreak appropriates Kade as proof and that Kade's public authority becomes contested.

## GAP 6 — Seraphine agency across nine books
Existing macro beats sometimes position Seraphine as stabilizer/collapse barometer.
Beat Bible 2.0 should ensure each book gives her at least one **moral decision** that changes the story, not merely a stabilization result.

## GAP 7 — Caro endgame preparation
Caro's stabilization arc exists but should be given enough agency in B05-B08 that her B09 Mending role feels earned as character resolution rather than functional necessity.

Recommended order is **not** B01 -> B09 straight through.

## Pass A — structural gaps
Resolve only the saga-level missing hinges:
1. Baz B04 death.

Across all nine books, verify:

- Lucien belief-state change per book;

## Pass C — antagonist pressure rail
Map Virelli/Dominions -> Han Wei/Technarc -> Metas -> Ito/Saeko/Choirless -> Elias/Brightbreak at book and act level.

## Pass D — Mechanica ceiling rail
Only after story architecture is stable, assign corridor/weather/FX ceilings and failure permissions.

# Final welding assessment

The recovered 2025 Beat Bible does **not** need a wholesale rewrite.

Approximate macro disposition:
- **LOCK:** ~72%
- **TUNE:** ~20%
- **GAP / additive work:** ~8%
- **MOVE:** very limited
- **MERGE/CUT:** defer to episode-level pass

The biggest opportunity is not replacing old structure. It is reconnecting story beats to antagonist causality and irreversible character choices that were already developed elsewhere in canon but never fully welded back into the Beat Bibles.
