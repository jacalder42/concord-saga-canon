# Concord 2026 — Editorial Casting Resolution — Filament & Adjacent Supporting Cast

Status: EDITORIAL RECOMMENDATION / PROPOSAL — NONCANONICAL UNTIL AUTHOR PROMOTION
Date: 2026-09-19
Builds on: SECONDARY_TERTIARY_CHARACTER_AUDIT_PASS1–PASS4
Scope: Resolve recovered character/name collisions for narrative quality rather than preserving historical development accidents.

## Editorial method

The established Concord Editorial Board is applied as an advisory composite:
- Gaiman lens — humanity, mythic/structural resonance, memorable recurrence
- Dinniman lens — propulsion, side-character energy, serialization payoff
- Hazelwood lens — relationship specificity and emotional legibility
- Deverell lens — world/faction coherence and long-form progression
- SenLinYu lens — interiority, vulnerability, earned emotional turns
- Regier lens — motivation, distress behavior, conflict psychology
- Jobs lens — clarity, differentiation, removal of redundant identities
- Fry consultant — systems clarity and cast-function load
- Questlove consultant — cultural texture, rhythm, community ensemble

Historical names are not protected merely because they appear in an old list. Useful narrative material may be reassigned.

# 1. Board-level resolution

The Filaments work best as a recurring civic ecology, not a hierarchy and not a parade of one-use helpers.

The board recommends protecting distinct recurring functions:
1. elder/community memory
2. care/non-militant ethic
3. routes/logistics
4. signals/wayfinding
5. triage/grounding craft
6. pattern perception and cost
7. material mutual aid
8. youth/messenger layer
9. fracture/radicalization counterexample

This permits consolidation without making New Orleans feel empty.

# 2. Mara / M — KEEP; PROMOTE AS RECURRING NOLA FILAMENT ANCHOR

Keep **Mara**, internal call sign **M**.

Retain recovered visual DNA:
- ~50s
- worn warmth; quiet authority; community gravity
- gray-salted twists, layered practical clothing
- resonance-worn notebook
- hands moving while comforting people
- marigold/umber visual language

Narrative assignment:
- recurring New Orleans Filament/community anchor;
- remembers people rather than doctrine;
- practical grounding and neighborhood continuity;
- one of the faces who makes the Filaments feel older than the protagonists' awareness of them;
- never “leader of the Filaments.”

### Early-scene resolution
**MERGE E08 unnamed Filament local into Mara/M.**

Reason:
E08's function — noticing Seraphine's exhaustion, reading civic patterns, offering ordinary human support — is exactly the sort of first impression that makes Mara worth recognizing later. Giving this beat to a recurring face creates retrospective depth without changing the scene's scale.

Mara may remain unnamed or only “M” in the earliest encounter if chronology benefits from delayed recognition.

**Do not merge Rootkeeper into Mara.**

Rootkeeper's older male presentation and shop/community-center texture is sufficiently distinct. Keep him as a small place-bound elder, likely one-book/local texture rather than saga-recurring cast.

# 3. Arianna Duplantier — KEEP

Keep name and long-form trajectory.

Narrative assignment:
- keeper of the compassionate/non-militant Filament ethic;
- evolves from ritualist/grounder to youth protector, safe-house organizer, remnant elder;
- ultimately helps carry survivors toward Honey Island.

Distinction from Mara:
- Mara = neighborhood memory, human recognition, practical gravity.
- Arianna = ethical continuity, teaching, organized care under pressure.

Do not merge.

# 4. Mira Tremeaux — SPLIT FUNCTION, RETIRE NAME

The historical “Mira” record is too contradictory to function as one coherent person:
- NOLA helper → organizer → mapper → radical → militant splinter leader;
- alternatively gentle recurring elder → diaspora guide → post-Mending teacher;
- alternatively Reykjavík/QZ Echo-guide.

Board resolution:
**RETIRE MIRA TREMEAUX as a current reader-facing identity.**

### 4A. Compassionate elder/teacher material
**MERGE into Mara + Arianna**, according to function:
- human-scale grounding/community recognition → Mara
- teaching/non-militant continuity/diaspora care → Arianna

Do not give Mara flashy “emotional cartography” VFX. Preserve the grounded tone.

### 4B. Reykjavík Echo-guide material
**DEMOTE to local Reykjavík Filament function**, not a major recurring named character unless later Icelandic development proves the slot needs a face.

Elisabet already gives Reykjavík emotional and narrative ownership. A second globally recurring elder risks satellite-character clutter.

### 4C. Radicalization trajectory
**TRANSFER to a new/renamed youth-generation character, not Mara or Arianna.**

The arc itself is valuable: a person readers knew as caring and useful becomes convinced that care without force is complicity. That makes the Filament fracture personal.

Use the existing historical **Ringo Marcel** concept as the seed and rename him for clarity:

## RENAMED: Ringo Marcel → **Ren Bellande**
- early 20s in Veil/early Neon;
- initially useful, anxious, earnest Filament youth;
- sees institutions repeatedly fail;
- moves from protection → direct action → coercion;
- becomes a recognizable face of the militant Filament splinter;
- not evil, not secretly controlled, and not equivalent to Choirless;
- his tragedy is that his original motive remains protection.

This absorbs the useful radicalization functions of:
- militant Mira
- Malik Price
- Domenico “Dome” Reyes
- old Ringo Marcel

Result:
**Mira Tremeaux CUT as identity; best material preserved elsewhere.**

# 20. Board rationale

### Clarity / Jobs
Readers can now identify each Filament face by a different action and emotional function.

### Humanity / Gaiman + Regier
The fracture is carried by someone readers first know as a helper. The stable elders remain people, not mystic exposition devices.

### Serialization / Dinniman + Deverell
Early faces can recur with escalating jobs. Recognition itself becomes payoff.

### Relationship / Hazelwood + SenLinYu
Recurring side characters can form actual bonds with Seraphine, Caro, Elisabet, Kade, and each other rather than resetting emotional trust with a new helper every book.

### Systems / Fry
The network now has complementary nodes rather than duplicate titles.

### Rhythm / Questlove
NOLA scenes can rotate among recognizable community voices — elder, runner, pantry, medic, route operator, youth — giving the city ensemble rhythm without crowding every scene.

# 21. Narrative rule going forward

Before adding a new named Filament/supporting character, ask:
1. Can this beat deepen an existing recurring face?
2. Does the new character provide a genuinely new relationship, place anchor, worldview, or recurring function?
3. Would recognition create more payoff than novelty?

If yes to #1 and no to #2, reuse the existing character.
