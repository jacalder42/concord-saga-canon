# Concord 2026 — Editorial Casting Resolution — Global / Regional Place Anchors

Status: EDITORIAL RECOMMENDATION / PROPOSAL — NONCANONICAL UNTIL AUTHOR PROMOTION
Date: 2026-09-19
Scope: Marrakesh/Morocco, Santa Fe/Kade geographic conflict, coastal Japan/Choirless overlap, and remaining regional character slots not already resolved by NOLA, Reykjavík, Vienna, Singapore, Filament, or institutional passes.

## I. PURPOSE

This pass asks a narrower question than the faction audits:

**Which named people make the global cities feel inhabited, and which names are merely development debris attached to functions?**

A city does not need a complete local ensemble simply because it appears in a city bible.

Global recurrence should be earned by:
- a relationship with a core character;
- a place the reader returns to;
- a worldview unavailable elsewhere;
- or a function that pays off across books.

## II. MARRAKESH — RECOVERED ARCHITECTURE

Historical environment material gives Marrakesh unusually strong saga importance:
- early global Filament root;
- Prism Convergence;
- emotional/resonance literacy model;
- humanitarian and diaspora corridor;
- Kade stabilization site;
- Caro/Elisabet relationship geography;
- Lacuna musical breakthrough;
- post-Mending cultural convergence.

Two separate environment bibles repeatedly require a **Marrakesh Filament elder**, including:
- Elisabet learning grounding in the Mellah;
- Kade being pulled from a grief spiral;
- Kade's later meltdown being halted;
- Filament mentorship during major convergence events.

The city bibles do not name this elder.

However, Core Visual Canon contains:
**Naima el-Faris — Filament diaspora coordinator at Marrakesh Prism Convergence.**

This is the strongest recovered named candidate for the repeated unnamed Marrakesh mentor/coordinator function.

## III. NAIMA EL-FARIS — KEEP / PROMOTE AS MARRAKESH PLACE ANCHOR

### Recovered visual/function DNA
- early 40s;
- rich golden-brown skin;
- black waves under bright scarf;
- soft hazel eyes with resonance sensitivity;
- lean/elegant/calm;
- flowing kaftans;
- copper bracelets;
- field satchel;
- prism-light visual language;
- role explicitly: Filament diaspora coordinator at Marrakesh Prism Convergence;
- atmosphere: “sunlight through patterned shade; watchful kindness.”

### Editorial resolution
**Naima becomes the named Marrakesh Filament anchor.**

She absorbs the unnamed “Marrakesh Filament elder/mentor” functions where age/title are not plot-critical.

Do not artificially age her into a generic wise elder. “Elder” can describe standing/experience within local Filament culture rather than advanced age.

### Relationship functions
**Elisabet:** early grounding teacher; offers a form of emotional literacy different from Reykjavík's restraint.

**Kade:** one of the few adults who can interrupt his spiral without treating him as a prophet, patient, or weapon.

**Caro:** recognizes that Caro's openness is not the same thing as regulation.

**Lacuna:** collaborator rather than disciple; local practices and his music can meet without one “discovering” the other.

**Seraphine:** sees Seraphine as a person before symbol.

### Guardrails
Naima is NOT:
- global leader of all Filaments;
- mystical oracle;
- “voice of Morocco”;
- sole keeper of local tradition;
- a convenient exposition machine.

She is one capable person embedded in a larger living network.

## IV. MARRAKESH LOCAL NETWORK — KEEP MOSTLY COLLECTIVE

The city already supplies:
- riad safe-house operators;
- merchants;
- storytellers;
- youth cells;
- diaspora coordinators;
- spiritual/community refuge;
- rooftop networks;
- aid workers.

Do not name a representative for every category.

Naima plus recognizable places is enough unless a specific relationship earns another face.

### Recommended recurring architecture
- **Naima el-Faris** — human continuity / diaspora / grounding.
- recurring riad or café staff can be recognized without full character arcs.
- local Filament cells remain plural.
- refugee families rotate unless one becomes relationally important.

## V. CULTURAL CARE NOTE — MARRAKESH

Historical development occasionally treats real cultural/religious sites as if their histories mechanically generate resonance.

During prose migration:
- avoid reducing the Mellah/Jewish history to supernatural “trauma energy”;
- avoid treating Koutoubia or Islamic practice as fantasy mechanics;
- distinguish fictional resonance phenomena from real religious meaning;
- use local human practices as culture, not exotic magic.

Marrakesh should feel like a real city inhabited by people, not an “emotional mystical city.”

## VI. SANTA FE — DO NOT BUILD A KADE HOME CAST

Recovery produced an important correction:

**Kade Harper's character sheet gives his origin as New Orleans, not Santa Fe.**

Santa Fe is repeatedly flagged in later structural memory as a **pressure zone/collapse location**, including the Book 6 rupture architecture, but it is not recovered as Kade's hometown or foundational personal ecosystem.

Therefore:
**Do not invent Santa Fe family/friends to explain Kade.**

Kade's personal place ecosystem remains primarily NOLA:
- Lacuna;
- Trip/Velvet Vein;
- Filament youth;
- later Ishaan;
- the wider NOLA civic ensemble.

Santa Fe should be treated as an event geography until stronger source recovery establishes named recurring locals.

## VII. SANTA FE — KEEP EVENT-BASED CASTING

When Santa Fe becomes narratively active:
- use civilians, responders, Filament contacts, evacuees, and local organizers as required;
- reuse global recurring characters only where travel/communications logic supports it;
- promote a local only if they form an actual relationship that survives the event.

Avoid a “city representative” invented merely because the city needs a face.

This also prevents geographic sprawl during the already-dense late Neon rupture.

## VIII. JAPAN / CHOIRLESS — KEEP PLACE CAST SUBORDINATE TO IDEOLOGICAL AUDIT

Recovered Filament canon identifies **coastal Japan** as an early Filament region.

Recovered antagonist architecture separately places Ito Masayuki / Saeko Morita / Choirless or anti-resonance development in the ideological layer.

Editorial rule:
**Do not create a generic Japanese supporting cast whose only job is to explain the Choirless.**

Japan is not synonymous with Choirless ideology.

When Japanese/coastal civic scenes appear, preserve:
- local Filament aid;
- civilians;
- ordinary civic resilience;
- disagreement with anti-resonance politics.

Ito Masayuki and Saeko Morita remain antagonist/ideological characters, not “Japan's representatives.”

Their exact geographic biographies should follow their dedicated recovered character sources, not be inferred from names.

## IX. GLOBAL FILAMENT MODEL

The city bibles occasionally drift toward a global organization with quasi-central leadership.

The current Filament resolution supersedes that tendency.

Regional rule:
- NOLA has its recurring ensemble.
- Marrakesh has Naima as a strong regional anchor.
- Reykjavík can retain local functions without a new global leader.
- Singapore has local cells and safe corridors.
- coastal Japan has local cells.
- other cities can have recurring local people if the story returns often enough.

No “Filament council of city representatives” should be invented.

## XI. THE GLOBAL CAST SHOULD NOT BECOME A PASSPORT COLLECTION

A recurring danger in the historical development is one named character per:
- city;
- profession;
- crisis;
- cultural function.

That produces breadth without memory.

The revised global architecture should instead create:
1. a small number of highly recognizable place anchors;
2. existing recurring characters crossing ecosystems;
3. unnamed/local populations who make each city feel larger than the cast.

Recognition matters more than cataloguing.

## XIII. EDITORIAL EFFECT

The saga's world becomes larger when some cities have many familiar people and others do not.

NOLA can feel like a neighborhood web.
Reykjavík can feel intimate.
Vienna can feel haunted by a few relationships.
Singapore can feel institutionally populated but personally lonely.
Marrakesh can feel communal through Naima plus crowds/networks.
Santa Fe can feel frightening precisely because the protagonists arrive amid people they do not know.

Uniform ensemble density would make the world feel designed rather than lived in.
