# B01 — NOTICE
Reader question: **Who keeps noticing the things everyone else dismisses?**

### MT-01 — First oddity
**Placement:** early B01 / after first civic anomaly  
**Vehicle:** short MissingThread post  
**Function:** establishes anonymous earnest voice. Threadnaut notices a small inconsistency between official explanation and mundane evidence.  
**Gear breadcrumb:** ordinary phone/recorder/notebook evidence only.  
**Reader learns:** this is careful curiosity, not conspiracy performance.  
**Tahl learns:** nothing metaphysical; only that the anomaly is real enough to document.  
**Do not:** name Tahl; invoke VT; sound prophetic.

### MT-02 — Correction
**Placement:** after second anomaly / cluster escalation  
**Vehicle:** MT update/correction  
**Function:** anonymous poster publicly corrects an earlier inference when new evidence contradicts it.  
**Character seed:** integrity over being right.  
**Reader learns:** Threadnaut is trustworthy because he will retract himself.  
**Long payoff:** foundational contrast with Elias/Brightbreak certainty.

### MT-03 — Pattern, not answer
**Placement:** late B01 Act I / before larger convergence  
**Vehicle:** MT thread with simple map or timestamp comparison  
**Function:** links multiple civic glitches without explaining them.  
**Gear breadcrumb:** basic field recordings + public data assembled manually.  
**Reader learns:** the anomalies correlate.  
**Tahl learns:** there is a pattern, but not what it means.

### Optional B01 tag — “Threadnaut”
**Placement:** late B01 or B01 end, only if source reconstruction supports it  
**Vehicle:** comment/reply/community nickname, not self-coronation  
**Function:** seed the label without identifying the person.  
**Rule:** MissingThread can exist before the label Threadnaut becomes stable.

**B01 exit state:** readers recognize the MT voice. The human remains invisible. No VT contact.

**Vehicle:** MT dispatch

**Placement:** first third B02

### MT-06 — The BRUSH
**Placement:** mid-B02, near historical “First Real Brush with VT” function  
**Vehicle:** MT artifact after an anomaly; the actual contact remains partly off-page.  
**Mechanics:** microscopic/ambiguous VT contact. No entry, communication, power, or proof.  
**Presentation possibility:** Threadnaut reports a few seconds that do not reconcile between subjective memory, timestamps, and recordings. Equipment contains ordinary corruption/noise rather than VT data.  
**Reader interpretation:** anomaly-induced equipment failure, stress, or something stranger.  
**Tahl interpretation:** unresolved. He refuses to overclaim.  
**Gear consequence:** begins adding redundancy specifically because his tools disagreed with his experience.

### MT-07 — Failed replication
**Placement:** later B02  
**Vehicle:** short field note / MT post  
**Function:** Threadnaut tries to reproduce the circumstances and fails.  
**Mechanica protection:** VT cannot be summoned through equipment or method.  
**Character function:** curiosity without certainty.  
**Reader learns:** he is testing himself rather than building mythology.

### MT-08 — Southwest discrepancy
**Placement:** B02 late  
**Vehicle:** map fragment / MT thread  
**Function:** faint directional bias appears in data but is too weak to name Santa Fe.  
**Breadcrumb:** supports later Drift architecture.  
**Rule:** phrase as statistical/observational weirdness, not prophecy.

**B02 exit state:** readers know Threadnaut is competent, connected, and personally affected by at least one anomaly. They still do not know Tahl Morgan.

# B03 EPILOGUE — THE FIRST QUIET / PUNCTURE

### EP-A — Consequence
Threadnaut reviews the information chain and realizes something he published exposed a real person / Warehouse participant. He may not know the name Baz Arnaud.

### EP-B — Identity reveal
For the first time the narrative identifies the human behind the voice:
**Tahl Morgan.**

The reveal should feel retrospective: reader recognizes habits, phrasing, evidence discipline, and gear already encountered through MT.

### EP-C — Disconnect
Tahl cannot find relief in feeds, comments, footage, maps, or official silence. He shuts the information stream down.

### EP-D — Sky
Historical “First Quiet” imagery already places strange Warehouse lights / faint shimmer in the sky. Tahl looks upward for quiet/scale, not metaphysical access.

### EP-E — PUNCTURE
He recognizes the impossible qualitative signature previously BRUSHED in B02. He follows/perceives it for an instant and momentarily punctures the VT boundary.

No entry. No dialogue. No powers. No proof. No digital capture.

### EP-F — Aftermath
Tahl returns exhausted/overloaded. His equipment is still only equipment. Whatever happened cannot be responsibly posted as fact.

### EP-G — First mature MT post
He reports only what he can substantiate, acknowledges uncertainty, and accepts that truth-telling carries responsibility.

This is the ethical birth of the Neon MT voice.
