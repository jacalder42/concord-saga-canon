This resolved the previously incomplete “Ito” reference to the historical name **Koro Ito**. **Superseded 2026-09-19:** James ruled that the names in `canon/characters/` are final, and `canon/characters/ItoID.md` reads **ITO MASAYUKI**. `Koro Ito` is the historical development-layer form; the canonical name is **Ito Masayuki**.

- red-white flashes;

These sources establish Ito as a deliberately developed antagonist identity, not a placeholder invented during the current audit.

Recovered antagonist architecture defines Choirless as:

## III. EDITORIAL RESOLUTION

### 1. Ito Masayuki — KEEP / PRIMARY HUMAN FACE OF CHOIRLESS

Keep the full historical name **Ito Masayuki**.

Function:
**the person who turns suppression from a policy preference into a moral doctrine of Erasure.**

Ito is not another Saeko.

- Saeko: “Resonance is dangerous; people deserve safety.”
- Tessa Vane: “If institutions will not protect us, neighbors will enforce safety.”
- Ito: “The source of suffering must be removed.”

Ito's argument is not merely anti-resonance fear. He concludes that emotional variance itself is the destabilizing substrate.

### 2. Preserve “emotion is entropy”

Keep **emotion is entropy** as the clean historical doctrinal core.

It distinguishes Choirless from:
- Dominion purity/legitimacy;
- Technarc prediction/control;
- Saeko safety/populism;
- Brightbreak certainty/influence.

Choirless believes peace requires subtraction.

### 3. “Order as Mercy / pruning as compassion” does NOT belong to Ito

Recovered later memory explicitly assigns this doctrine to **Marcellus Virelli**.

Do not migrate it to Ito.

This is an important forensic correction. Earlier search proximity could falsely suggest an Ito connection.

Virelli = pruning/containment as mercy.
Ito = emotion as entropy / peace through erasure.

### 4. PureTone — RETIRE AS SEPARATE MAJOR FACTION

Historical PureTone appears entangled with Ito/Saeko and anti-resonance weaponization.

Editorial resolution:
**PureTone becomes an earlier campaign/network/technology vocabulary in the genealogy of Choirless, not another full recurring faction.**

Possible uses:
- slogan;
- suppression-tech program;
- activist network;
- early extremist label.

Exact form can be settled during Neon beat reconciliation.

Do not maintain PureTone + Quiet Doctrine + Saeko movement + Choirless as four parallel anti-resonance factions.

### 5. Choirless is NOT Saeko's movement with masks

Saeko's movement creates permission structures and mass political legitimacy.

Choirless selects a harder conclusion from the same crisis.

Recruitment can flow from:
- disillusioned Saeko followers;
- Technarc personnel traumatized by failed control systems;
- Dominion intellectuals who abandon lineage doctrine but retain suppression logic;
- survivors of catastrophic resonance events;
- civilians who equate emotional amplitude with catastrophe.

This makes Choirless a synthesis of failed antagonist systems rather than Saeko's paramilitary wing.

### 6. Tessa Vane remains outside formal Choirless leadership

Do not make Tessa a Choirless lieutenant by default.

Her narrative value is ordinary civic radicalization.

A stronger Loom choice may be to force her to confront people who take her own suppression logic farther than she intended.

Whether she joins, resists, or is consumed by the network is a beat-level decision.

### 7. Choirless internal types — KEEP AS BEHAVIORAL STRATA, NOT THREE SUBFACTIONS

Historical appearance memory names:
- Quietists — pale/soft;
- Levelers — rigid white/red;
- Bereaved — hollow gray.

These are useful visual/social categories but should not become three organizations with separate leadership charts.

Editorial reinterpretation:
- **Bereaved** = trauma-driven recruits / entry population;
- **Quietists** = disciplined adherents practicing suppression/stillness;
- **Levelers** = hardline operational cells willing to enforce Erasure.

Names themselves remain provisional until language/tone review.

This gives Choirless internal evolution without cast bloat.

## IV. KORO ITO CHARACTER ENGINE

### Public presentation

Ito should initially be legible as a serious thinker rather than a screaming extremist.

He speaks in:
- controlled sentences;
- causal chains;
- moral arithmetic;
- low-affect certainty;
- language of relief, burden, noise, and preventable suffering.

Avoid mystical sermonizing despite the old “sermon posture” note.

### Core wound

Historical recovered material establishes trauma as the movement's substrate but does not cleanly recover Ito's personal biography.

Do **not** fabricate a detailed family tragedy as recovered canon.

Editorial permission:
during later character-bible development, give Ito a concrete personal encounter with catastrophic resonance if needed, but label it a new amendment.

### False belief

Human suffering is amplified by emotional variance; therefore reducing emotional amplitude is compassion.

### Truth he cannot accept

Pain suppressed is not pain resolved, and a humanity stripped of emotional variance is not safe humanity—it is diminished humanity.

### Relationship to systems

Ito can quote evidence from Technarc failures without being Technarc.
He can inherit suppression logic from Dominion without accepting bloodline legitimacy.
He can exploit Saeko-created fear without answering to Saeko.

He is the ideological scavenger who takes pieces of failed systems and assembles a cleaner, more frightening conclusion.
