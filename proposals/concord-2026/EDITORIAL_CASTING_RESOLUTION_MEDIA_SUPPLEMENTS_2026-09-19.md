# Concord 2026 — Editorial Casting Resolution — Media / Supplements / Public Voices

Status: EDITORIAL RECOMMENDATION / PROPOSAL — NONCANONICAL UNTIL AUTHOR PROMOTION
Date: 2026-09-19

## I. RECOVERED PARATEXT ARCHITECTURE

Historical Supplement Architecture:
- Veil: MT small/personal; Chronicle light; Vein cultural slices; VT private.
- Neon: MT influence explodes; Chronicle carries institutional narrative; Field Notes map shards.
- Loom: MT becomes global survival thread; Chronicle dies; VT echo-only.
- Post-Mending: MT evolves culturally toward Luminous Thread; Chronicle survives as archive.

Lean Tech & Comms Bible:
- MT is a mortal media channel created by Tahl.
- It cannot access or interpret VT.
- After Tahl's death, Kade inherits MT and changes its cultural role.
- Civilian networks progressively fail; MT remains narratively important through collapse.

This means media voices are not merely tertiary decoration. They are a chorus showing how the same events are interpreted, distorted, joked about, mapped, feared, and remembered.

The mistake in the historical character lists is not having recurring voices. It is giving too many voices overlapping jobs.

## II. NAOMI CLAIRBORNE — KEEP / PRIMARY CHRONICLE FACE

### Recovered nine-book trajectory
B1: Chronicle junior reporter.
B2: junior reporter; official-narrative veneer.
B3: amplifies misinformation.
B4: amplifies moral panic.
B5: develops/reports a “Bloom Severity Index.”
B6: breaking reports on Convergence Events.
B7: final pre-collapse global analysis.
B8: attempts to unify a global narrative and fails.
B9: writes “THE DAY THE SKY BREATHED,” later preserved culturally.

### Editorial resolution
**KEEP. Make Naomi the single primary human face of the Chronicle across the saga.**

Her arc becomes:
**junior institutional reporter → trusts the official frame → participates in distortion → recognizes the frame is failing → tries to report accurately inside a collapsing information system → loses the institution → becomes an imperfect witness → post-Mending archival voice.**

This is stronger than making her simply “good journalist” or “propagandist.”

She should make real mistakes, including early reliance on official/institutional sources. Her later credibility is earned through correction rather than retroactive purity.

### Function
Naomi answers:
**What does a basically serious person do when the institution that taught her how truth is verified can no longer keep up with reality?**

## VIII. MT CORE COMMENTER CHORUS

Historical lists repeatedly carry four handles from B1 onward:
- GlassHarbor
- Ribbon_9
- NthDaySurvivor
- tinfoilmage

That recurrence is valuable. They create the sensation that readers themselves have been watching the same online strangers for years.

### Editorial resolution
**KEEP THESE FOUR AS THE CORE MT CHORUS. Do not reveal their legal identities by default.**

They are characters through voice, not dossiers.

### Ribbon_9 — KEEP
Trajectory:
early “is this normal?” panic → bloom rumor mapping → trauma cataloguing → despair/break → defiant hope → first hopeful late MT material.

Function: **ordinary anxiety becoming practiced witness.**

Avoid diagnostic language such as “psychological break” unless story specifically supports it. Show behavior/posting changes instead.

### GlassHarbor — KEEP
Trajectory:
early recurring commenter → unverified emergency compilation → Technarc tracking/paranoia → Quietist purge tracking → final-hours documentation.

Function: **OSINT/crowdsourced verification under pressure.**

Critical rule: GlassHarbor is sometimes wrong. Corrections should be visible. Otherwise the handle becomes an author oracle.

### NthDaySurvivor — KEEP, CONTROL ACCURACY
Trajectory:
emotion-pattern evangelist → “glass-day” prophet → predicts Day of Glass correctly → later prediction only almost right → final echo-pattern attempt stops mid-line.

Function: **pattern hunger / apophenia living uncomfortably close to real pattern.**

Do not let every prediction become true. The “almost correct” beats are essential.

### tinfoilmage — KEEP
Trajectory:
joking/perceptive → humor buffer → humor erodes → last jokes from evacuation/collapse → dark humor returns.

Function: **community pressure valve.**

Do not make jokes literally prophetic as a supernatural gimmick. If a joke later looks eerie, coincidence/pattern recognition should remain plausible.

## XI. THE CHRONICLE SHOULD DIE AS AN INSTITUTION, NOT AS A VOICE

Historical supplement architecture explicitly says:
**Loom: Chronicle dead.**

Therefore do not continue producing a normal staffed Chronicle news ecosystem deep into Loom.

Better progression:
Veil — polished institutional publication.
Neon — stressed, reactive, contradictory.
Late Neon — fragmented desks, correspondents, emergency feeds.
Loom — publication/infrastructure effectively dead.
Naomi survives as a person/witness, not “the Chronicle.”
Post-Mending — old Chronicle material is archived and contested.

This makes institutional collapse visible.

## XV. WHY THE HANDLES SHOULD REMAIN HANDLES

Do not automatically reveal who Ribbon_9, GlassHarbor, NthDaySurvivor, or tinfoilmage are.

Their accumulated familiarity is the point.

Readers can know:
- posting rhythms;
- verbal tics;
- grudges;
- corrections;
- jokes;
- absences;
- changed tone.

That can create real character attachment without a face.

If one identity reveal later creates a major relationship payoff, it can be considered at beat level. Otherwise anonymity preserves the social scale of MT.
