# Concord 2026 — Editorial Casting Resolution — Reykjavík / Elisabet Ecosystem

Status: EDITORIAL RECOMMENDATION / PROPOSAL — NONCANONICAL UNTIL AUTHOR PROMOTION
Date: 2026-09-19
Scope: Reykjavík recurring/place cast, Elisabet's non-core social ecosystem, Filament/research texture, and Mara Niht boundary.

## I. RECOVERED CITY FUNCTION

Reykjavík has strong historical environment development but very little named local cast.

Recovered functions:
- Elisabet's childhood/home geography;
- independent resonance researchers;
- a university mentor slot connected to Elisabet;
- local priest who senses early anomaly;
- old fisherman at Harbor Shack #7;
- small Filament network;
- Technarc defectors;
- diaspora/evacuation support;
- Harpa becomes cultural site → triage site → post-Mending emotional arts center.

This is useful. It means the city does **not** need a NOLA-sized ensemble.

Reykjavík should feel intimate: a small number of recurring people encountered in different civic contexts.

## II. ELISABET'S SOCIAL PROBLEM

Historical Elisabet material gives her rich relationships with the main cast but almost no durable relationships from her pre-Concord life.

That creates a risk:
Reykjavík becomes a beautiful personality metaphor rather than **her home**.

Editorial goal:
give Elisabet 2–3 recognizable people who knew her before the saga's main circle and therefore see versions of her that Caro/Seraphine/Lucien cannot.

Do not create a full family tree merely to fill this gap.

- Harpa is a major city node;
- concerts and harmonic distortion matter in Neon;
- Harpa later becomes triage infrastructure;
- post-Mending it becomes a global emotional arts center.

Mara Niht is therefore an unusually natural candidate to humanize this location **if the recovered music-generation character is compatible**.

- Mara sees Elisabet's humor, habits, and history rather than “the Clarity Thread”;

Do not lock “Icelandic,” “singer,” “Harpa performer,” or any genre until the source body is recovered.
