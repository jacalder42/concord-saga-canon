Independent pages repeat:
- Baz Character Sheet: `Veil (alive) -> Neon (dies early) -> Loom (memory/legacy)` but also `Endgame Status: Deceased (Veil Book 3 warehouse incident)` — internally inconsistent wording, but its detailed arc and key scenes place death in B03.

- B04 Final Beat Bible opens with `Lucien: Grief Without Vent` after Baz's death.
- B03 Epilogue `The First Quiet` occurs hours after the Warehouse Rupture and is explicitly about fallout from Baz's death; B04 begins after a short time skip.
- Dec. 1 New Orleans City Bible calls Warehouse District `Baz's death site (canonical)`.

## Conflict with later recovered structural source
A different later recovered source — the Consolidated Trilogy Beats / structural archive used in the 2026 recovery — has B03 ending with Baz alive and Baz/Filaments/Tahl committing to truth-telling.

The user independently recalls Baz being killed in B04, followed by Lucien spiraling and Tahl speaking out.

Therefore the correct conclusion is **not** `Notion proves Baz dies B3` and not yet `B4 is settled`.

The correct conclusion is:
> **Baz's death timing was deliberately revised during late-2025 structural development, and Notion preserves the earlier B03 Warehouse-death version. The later structural archive appears to preserve a subsequent B03-survival version, but the exact revision artifact moving the death to B04 has not yet been recovered.**

This is a version-history problem, not a factual ambiguity inside one canon layer.

## High-value consequences

The earlier B03 version gives us potentially reusable *death design DNA* even if the death was later moved:
- Baz dies through ordinary bravery, not special power.
- He chooses to rescue civilians.
- Lucien arrives too late.
- Baz's last emotional function is to tell Lucien he does not have to be alone.
- death is linked to a public resonance catastrophe rather than a mastermind assassination.
- the death site becomes a persistent civic/psychological wound.

But NONE of those mechanics should be automatically transplanted into B04. They are superseded design material until the later revision is recovered or the user deliberately chooses to reuse them.
