## Tahl death mechanics — salvage boundary

The November Notion image of Tahl helping civilians during catastrophic collapse remains emotionally compatible, but the exact transit-platform death is **candidate detail**, not current lock.

A future reconciliation may preserve:

while rewriting the causal mechanics so:

- Tahl does not choose martyrdom;
- VT exposure/system collapse causes or makes his death unavoidable;
- MT remains distinct from VT;
- Echo behavior follows current no-resurrection / limited-agency rules.
