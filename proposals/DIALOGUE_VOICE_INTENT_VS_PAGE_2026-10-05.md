# Dialogue voice: original intent and design, side by side with the page

**Authority:** `decisions/B01_OPEN_QUESTIONS_ALL_AS_RECOMMENDED_AUTHOR_ANSWERS_2026-10-05.md` §2. The author: *"we need
to consider original intent and design side by side with the written prose, it is not certain that the written form wins
100%."* **Neither side wins by default.** For each trait this file weighs both and recommends one of four outcomes:
**the page updates the design**, **the design pulls the page back**, **keep both as modes**, or **leave it**.

**What it does not change:** no card, rule, packet, grid, profile line or manuscript text. It does not amend Q-WP3 (no
permanent per-character voice layer in drafting); Q-VI1 asks whether the direction changes Q-WP3's *preceding prose
governs* clause. It supersedes nothing in `proposals/DIALOGUE_VOICE_SHEETS_2026-10-05.md`; it is the side-by-side
review that answers that file's `[I]` inferences with evidence. Louisiana speech is weighed separately in
`proposals/WRITER_PROFILE_12B_LOUISIANA_SPEECH_SIDE_BY_SIDE_2026-10-05.md` (§9 here defers to it).

**No manuscript prose.** Page habits are described and counted. Short quotations supporting every page claim, with line
locators, are in the private manuscript repository: `draft-notes/voice/INTENT_VS_PAGE_EVIDENCE.md`.

### 11.2 Suppression (l.7720–7736)

Characters lose access to naming under resonance pressure, trauma, distortion (FX2/FX3), RES SHARD or RUPTURE, deep
corridors (U6/U7), metaphysical proximity or horror. Suppression shows as **stuttered, truncated or avoided names,
formal regression, or last-name fallback**. **Naming position** (l.7683): front-loaded names mean urgency; back-loaded
mean tenderness.

**B01 page:** no stuttered names. Avoidance and formal regression are present as character habits (Lucien answers his
name with a greeting), not as states. B01's events stay below the states that would trigger suppression.
