## 1. What was done

rulings, the freezes or the protected passages:

- **A, opposition and action:** 20 ideas;
- **B, the mystery as a driver:** 20 ideas, with a clue table;

- **D, structure and economy:** 19 ideas, a function map and a new running order.

A fifth agent graded every constraint on B01's plot by strength (about 115 rows). Its headline: **almost everything
that keeps the opposition passive is approved design, not ruled canon.** The hard limits are few: M51 (no one connects
the events to the swamp site), no certified cause or mechanism, imperfect prediction, Baz arriving in Act II.

## 4. Recommended package

**Tier 1 in full, plus R1(a), R2, R3, R4, R5(a), R6, R7 (D's skeleton) and R8.** Tier 3 is declined.

What the book becomes, in five lines:

1. **Act I ends** (about 26–36%, by Q-UI2) on a question with an object: somebody knew where it would come, and
   somebody wants the record of who felt it first. Seraphine's log is that record.
2. **The middle is contested.** Caldas speaks at about 47%; the crew signs the page; they choose whose households to
   tell; Ursulines lands on the untold woman, and the case went up before it. Seraphine sorts the two hands, strikes
   the vow, and refuses the oxygen-for-log offer to the woman's face, at a cost she pays.
3. **Discovery replaces confession.** She catches the sting from a hold list; Lorraine's plate puts the Hollen name on
   the car at the bereaved door; Baz tells Lucien at the end of Act II, and Lucien chooses silence.
4. **The climax is caused.** The crew builds a precursor rule from witnesses, uses it at the lot (where it half works
   and the crush still comes) and at the Square, where the warning works, St. Ann is sitting before it arrives, the
   collectors get no onset, and Isaiah films the case.
5. **The ending is a decision.** Lucien sends a true report with no people in it, with her yes, and stays inside
   knowingly. The word arrives through a document Baz recognises. What the phenomenon is, and who they are, stays open.

**Length:** Tier 1 and Tier 2 add about +10–14k and the cuts remove about 20–28k, so the book lands near
**150–160k**. D's full restructure (about 122k) is a separate decision (Q-UI2).

**What B02/B03 inherit, and what changes for them:**
- Lucien knows earlier (end of Act II, not Act III) that his reports feed the Dominion; it does not reach the camp
  clause (B02 E08) or confirmation (B02 E34). Those stay.
- Seraphine knows the Hollen name is on the car at Lorraine's door. She still does not know what the Dominion is.
- The precursor rule is a witness rule (imperfect), which B02's method arc can extend.
- The Dominion stays unnamed (OP-01); Technarc stays unnamed (OP-05); M51 untouched; *Veil* still at E49.
