## 1. Method

For each foreign or travelling cast member, **settle the facts first**: nationality, prior residence, work, funding,
sponsor. A category follows from the facts, never the reverse. Until the facts are set, the categories in §2 are
provisional, and the default reading is that the matter is handled offstage.

## 3. Later-book logistics (research and possible routes, not settled events)

From `SAGA_CAST.md`. Each is a constraint the reality imposes and a route that fits the existing design; none is placed.

- **Tahl's body (B06 → B07).** New Mexico's Office of the Medical Investigator (OMI) takes custody of a sudden,
  unexplained death and everything on or near the body. It may release the body **directly to family** (UNM OMI
  families FAQ) **or via a funeral home**. Interstate transport needs a burial-transit permit; in practice a funeral
  home's coach drives, and the locals and Filaments can follow as an escort. An exam and release take a few days,
  which fits the road and the parents' consent (D9).
- **Tahl's device.** The OMI holds it as an article on the body and releases it **to next of kin**. A route to Rex's
  B08 artefact: the parents give it to him. (A phone queues messages and sends them on signal, so a delayed fragment
  is accurate.)
- **The open rupture (B06 A2–A3).** A hazard open for weeks would get **a perimeter** (a closure order; state police
  or a national-forest closure). His live broadcast then shows him past it. The OMI does not cover pueblo land; keep
  the site off it or note tribal and federal jurisdiction.
- **"Platform".** After a death an account belongs to the estate. *Platform* is read as **audience, not account
  ownership**: Kade takes over the attention unless the parents hand him the account.
- **Baz's death (B04).** Within days a **missing-person report** to NOPD; the **coroner** identifies him once he is
  recovered; as a French national, **the French consulate** (New Orleans) is notified and handles repatriation papers.
  This sits in B04 and confirms nothing before it (M10).
- **Elisabet's warning (M55).** It reaches US responders **through a US agency**, such as the state
  emergency-management duty officer, or a US partner institution. Being slowed or ignored there is a possible cost.
