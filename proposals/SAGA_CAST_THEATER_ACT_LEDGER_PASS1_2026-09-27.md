## 1. Reading rules and authority

- **R** = ruled placement, sequence, or constraint in a decision or current ruled grid row.
- **D** = approved design (Tier B), including an accepted option, not promoted to ruled canon.
- **L** = author lean or direction; **H** = older historical candidate; **O** = genuinely open.
- **RR** = character receives or sends information remotely; it does not put their body at the source.
- A book-level route does not settle an act. A character's presence in a city does not make them a witness to every event there. An act row below may mix R, D, L, and O assertions; the codes attach to each assertion, not to the row.
- The 2026-09-26 physical-presence matrix and route ledger are forensic snapshots. The later author answers and 2026-09-27 decisions override their stale alternatives within the decisions' stated scope. The editorial route proposal's full tables are not approved wholesale; only its twelve selected options are D.

## 4. Editorial stress tests

1. **Theater ownership and page budget.** Veil has a NOLA core and a bounded MRK outside case. Neon can earn VIE through Lucien/Baz history, CHI through Caro, REK through Elisabet, SIN through Rex and NOLA through Seraphine/Tahl/public life. A city named in a report does not become a lived theater. Choose direct scenes where a character makes a decision there; use local anchors and mortal receipts for the rest. The approximately 30/30/30/10 POV bands are diagnostic, not quotas.
2. **Knowledge asymmetry.** Track direct witness, delayed recipient, distorted account, decision made before news, and reunion payoff. Baz's death, the Riot, Tahl's death and the B09 feint all gain force from meaningful absences; do not erase them with ensemble travel or omniscient MT.
3. **Different causal systems.** NOLA civic fracture, VIE institutional grief, CHI care delegation, REK bounded research, SIN technical trust failure and SF wound physics must each produce a measurable local consequence. Meta transfer mechanics and VT warning origin remain open; do not fill them by assertion in a location ledger.
4. **B08 book identity.** Tahl's B06 message gives the shape, including the private HI-last interpretation; the chain gives particulars. SF, MC and SM need different evidence, decisions, people and costs so B08 is not merely confirming an itinerary before B09. Seraphine's hard-cap doubt grows from becoming the Loom; the breathing-veil answer and the guides' price remain protected.
5. **Rex and Lacuna independence.** Rex's B04 independent entry and B06 SIN action need a B05 causal bridge, not a retroactive Technarc job. Lacuna's NOLA life should make her B09 feint credible; the B08 split should change her choices before her reunion with Kade.
