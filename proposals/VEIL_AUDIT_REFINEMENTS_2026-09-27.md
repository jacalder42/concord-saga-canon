## 2. ChatGPT's findings V01–V08: agreed, with concrete proposals

### V01 — B01 E48's human result *(high)*

**Agreed.** Arriving early must not vindicate *"arrive sooner / move faster / carry more"*.

**Proposal [P]:**

- **Why they are early:** **the trio's shared work predicted it** (E25, E27, E39). They are not there
  because Seraphine moved faster.
- **The beneficiary:** **one person** caught in the pulse's footprint, **a stranger whose distress the
  pulse amplifies.**
  - **Not a child.** A child would restage E01 as a rescue, and a rescue *reverses* E01 instead of
    answering it.
  - [Open] a street musician at the Square's edge, or an older man alone.
- **What she does:** she **stays with that one person and steadies them** until the pulse passes: a
  timed, constrained response (M03).
- **The overreach she declines:** pushing into the pulse to hold the whole Square, which is what
  saturated her in E33 and E45.
- **The visible cost:** her body pays: exhaustion, a nosebleed, the lag of E03. **She cannot also
  reach the others.**
- **How we know the narrow response helped:**
  - **The person walks out whole.**
  - Around them, **Baz, Lucien and Mara's people** take the others, which is E45's shared burden.
  - **The last time she tried to hold everything, she helped no one fully.**

**The book's answer to E01:** not *"arrive sooner"* but ***"arrive together, and carry one"***.

### V02 — B02 E47's immediate consequence *(high)*

**Agreed.**

**Proposal [P]: the institutional fallback.**

- **What Helena sought:** to draw the zone's extension **where it is needed.** That is a reasonable,
  protective use.
- **What happens without the drift model:** the Dominion **extends the Bywater zone by the crude
  rule**, a wider cordon than the lean requires, and **more households are displaced.**
- **Lucien sees the wider line the same week, and knows his withholding drew it.** He chose it
  because drift would give the Dominion **direction**, the next rung of the method, and he judged
  that worse.
- **The cost falls on people, not only on his standing with Helena.**
- **It sets up B03 E09** with a cause: the review's turn to *"advise and contain"* answers **the cost
  of that over-wide clearance.**

**The three commitments stay distinct:**

| B02 E26 | Partial obedience: he sends the grounds |
| B02 E47 | He withholds an **uncertain finding** |
