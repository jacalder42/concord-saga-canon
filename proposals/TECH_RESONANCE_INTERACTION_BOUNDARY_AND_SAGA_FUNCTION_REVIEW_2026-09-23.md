# Technology × Resonance boundary — saga-function review

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Status:** PROPOSAL FOR AUTHOR + EDITOR / CLAUDE REVIEW. Not a canon ruling or amendment to `rules/Mechanica-v4.md` or `rules/Resonance-v1.md`. Continues the [B02 evidence packet](../recovery/B02_MARRAKESH_GLOBAL_REPORT_EVIDENCE_PACKET_2026-09-23.md) and [distributed-event scale audit](../reports/SAGA_DISTRIBUTED_RESONANCE_SCALE_EVIDENCE_AUDIT_2026-09-23.md).

## Recommendation in one paragraph

**Yes: the absolute “technology is a casualty, not a carrier” wording is too narrow for the saga.** Keep the firm boundary that digital media cannot transmit a metaphysical payload or replace human Intent. Permit devices to record **ordinary physical effects**, infer local conditions from imperfect proxies, shape **ordinary material and social conditions** in which Resonance is expressed, and sometimes survive, degrade, or fail under pressure. Reserve intentional collection, containment, amplification or transfer of raw Resonance for separately approved and costly **special interfaces** after the manufactured-meta evidence is reconciled. A device does not acquire a human feeling, read Intent directly, transmit a person's inner state, or grant another person a free boost merely because it can interact with an environment.

This is a proposed **boundary correction**, not an assertion that all the listed interactions already work physically.

## The boundary to test: six different functions

| Function | Proposed permission | Physical / editorial gate | Saga value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **Record** | Microphones, cameras, ordinary sensors and people can preserve secondary sound, light, motion, physiology or service quality | Captured phenomenon must actually be audible/visible/physical at sensor; provenance and device processing matter. No recording of VT itself | Baz and Tahl investigate; contested evidence and misinformation remain meaningful |
| 2. **Infer** | Correlate proxies and forecast **risk**, sometimes wrong | Detection thresholds, calibration, baseline and false positives. An “Echo Badge” cannot directly read Emotion, Intent or a person's metaphysical signature by fiat | Rex/Elisabet/Technarch have substantive but fallible expertise |
| 3. **Condition** | Modify ordinary conditions: shelter geometry, airflow, sound and lighting cues, evacuation flow, grounding aids, shielding, redundancy and power | Specify which human or environmental variable changes and how. A machine cannot perform empathy, erase trauma, or automatically stabilize a Resonance field | Filament care and civic engineering can matter without becoming spells |
| 4. **Suffer / adapt** | Devices may remain normal, produce noisy readings, lose precision or intermittent service, recover, sustain damage, or fail | Device-specific exposure × vulnerability × engineering; cost includes repairs, access and unequal distribution. **Degradation does not equal failure** | Veil anomalies remain rare; Neon repair culture and Loom resource decisions have range |
| 5. **Communicate** | Broadcast testimony, warnings, measurements, rumor and instructions through ordinary channels | Receiver learns information, possibly changes behavior and thus the local human environment. No person's Resonance, Intent, emotion or VT event travels as digital content | MT can become a global lifeline without supernatural network robustness |
| 6. **Special local interface** | Possible research target: a physical device/biological meta captures, buffers, disturbs or releases local load | **OPEN / NOT APPROVED.** Requires source, material/biological coupling, capacity, control, depletion, loss, recipient limits, UARS costs, consent and failure. Digital packet cannot carry the payload | Gives Technarch experiments actual strategic stakes and ethical horror *if* source recovery and physics support it |

The word **carrier** should identify *what* is carried. Air carries an ordinary sound. A radio carries an encoded report of that sound. A wire carries electrical power. A harness might physically confine a meta under pressure. None of those statements alone implies that a person's directed Resonance travels through a phone or that a machine can choose an effect. A hypothetical engineered conduit for local raw load is a separate high-stakes decision, not implied by commonplace recordings or metal in the setting.

### Recommended replacement principle — draft text for review, not rule-file text

> Technology can observe and preserve the physical consequences of Resonance, change ordinary conditions that affect an event, and degrade or adapt under exposure. It cannot supply Intent or transmit a person's Resonance or VT as digital information. Any device intended to capture, buffer, redirect or augment Resonance directly requires a separately specified physical interface, finite budget, failure modes and human costs; no such general interface is approved by this statement.

Do not turn this into a blanket “tech may amplify Resonance” rule. There is a difference between making an existing crowd louder via a loudspeaker, worsening conditions through containment policy, or locally changing humidity/airflow, and creating more RP in a recipient. The latter is unproven. Likewise, a grounded sound cue may help a person regulate their own action without a speaker producing Resonance.

## Saga stress test

- **Veil:** Working phones, normal recording and occasional bounded artifacts make evidence and witness disagreement consequential. B01 E15's possible two audio recordings preserve an ordinary sound, never a Resonance sample. B02's Marrakesh call transmits testimony; remote emotion in NOLA is removed unless a separate non-network event is demonstrated. Credibility belongs to Baz's method, not to magical instruments.
- **Neon:** Correlates become more visible but sensor errors, hostile classifications and intentional misuse become stakes. Technarch can classify, confine, prototype and make costly mistakes. Manufactured metas can be dangerous pressure vessels; the author-recalled operative-boost loop is still OPEN. Better shielding, local caches and redundant power can succeed for a while. Public rescue and coercion both have engineering consequences.
- **Loom:** Reliability becomes uneven by location, energy supply, component complexity, maintenance, exposure and social access. A rugged digital recorder or cached burst can survive where a poorly protected analog link fails. Hand-carried paper and oral testimony matter because they solve custody and logistics, not because everything digital must die.
- **Post-Mending:** Less hostile conditions can make ordinary networks and instruments more reliable and allow reconstruction. Echo Nodes do not magically upgrade devices or grant flawless emotional diagnostics. Cultural literacy and engineering both matter; LT remains a separate post-Mending Thread identity.

**Narrative advantage:** This approach makes the conflict between control and care concrete. Institutions can know *some* real things and still classify badly or impose harm. Rex can fix a sensor without fixing a person. Tahl/Kade can distribute truthful testimony without distributing power. Resilient local systems can save lives while creating inequality in access. The meta program becomes an ethical and mechanical question, not merely a visual symbol of failure.

## Decisions for author + editor

1. **Wording:** Replace only the overbroad casualty/carrier shorthand, retaining the explicit no-digital-metaphysical-transmission and no-machine-Intent boundaries?
2. **Observation ceiling:** May approved instruments infer calibrated local risk from physical proxies, with measurable uncertainty? Recommendation: **yes**, without direct Emotion/Intent readout.
3. **Ordinary conditioning:** May conventional engineered changes to site and human behavior influence susceptibility or response? Recommendation: **yes where causal**, never automatic field dampening.
4. **Direct interfaces:** Does a meta harness or another finite physical system ever store/redirect raw load? **Hold pending source recovery and author ruling.** If restored, document ethics and energy/cost ledger per event.
5. **Tech VFX:** Which old “cameras recognize signatures,” emotional color glitches, or empathy-stabilized grids are objective effects? Recommend literal status only with a material link; otherwise POV, metaphor, propaganda or obsolete.
6. **Era envelope:** Should Loom failure be stated as a gradual service-quality and access crisis rather than “analog always wins / digital universally dies”? Recommendation: **yes**.

**Implementation sequence after review:** Decide 1–3 and 6; write three worked cases (B01 E15 ordinary recording, B02 multi-city report, B05 regional sensor/communications case). Then adjudicate 4 and edit Mechanica §5.5/§23 plus the stale `MT_RULES.md` through a separate rule-change review. This proposal makes no such edits.
